How much does it cost to rent a home in Villa Morra, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Villa Morra, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 1,660 per month, based on 218 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,200 to USD 2,700. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 10.1/m² and the median listed size is 164 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
