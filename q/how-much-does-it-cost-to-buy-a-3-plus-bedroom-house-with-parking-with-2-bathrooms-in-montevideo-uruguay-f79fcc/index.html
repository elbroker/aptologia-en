How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with parking with 2 bathrooms in Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with parking with 2 bathrooms for sale in Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 271,000, based on 558 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 179,925 to USD 390,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,515/m² and the median listed size is 185 m². That is 1% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 269,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
