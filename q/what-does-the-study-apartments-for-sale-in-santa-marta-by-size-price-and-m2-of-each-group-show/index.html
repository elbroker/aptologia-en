What does the study “Apartments for sale in Santa Marta by size: price and m² of each group” show?

In Santa Marta, an apartment for sale from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 402 million pesos in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 1,500 million pesos (+ 272.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "120 - 160 m²" (6.36 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (10.98 million pesos / m²).
