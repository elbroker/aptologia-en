How much does it cost to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in Buenos Aires?

1,100,000 Argentine pesos (USD 726) per month in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between 900,000 Argentine pesos (USD 594) and 1,400,000 Argentine pesos (USD 924).

In Buenos Aires, rent a 1 bedroom apartment costs in the median 750,000 Argentine pesos (USD 495) per month and one 2 bedroom, 1,100,000 Argentine pesos (USD 726) (+ 46.7%). One of 3 or more bedrooms is requested in 1,500,000 Argentine pesos (USD 990).
