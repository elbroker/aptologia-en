How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house in Barrios Unidos, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses in Barrios Unidos, Bogotá is COP 7,000,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 30% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 10,000,000).
