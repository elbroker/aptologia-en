How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more in Samborondón, Guayaquil, Ecuador in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more in Samborondón, Guayaquil, Ecuador is USD 3,200 per month, based on 117 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,500 to USD 4,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 7.25/m² and the median listed size is 400 m². That is 113% above the study's reference median for Guayaquil (USD 1,500). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
