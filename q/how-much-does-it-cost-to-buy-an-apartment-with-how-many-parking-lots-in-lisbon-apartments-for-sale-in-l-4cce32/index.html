How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many parking lots in Lisbon?

No parking: 500,000 EUR (USD 562,042); 1 parking: 750,000 EUR (USD 843,062); 2 or more parking: 1,250,000 EUR (USD 1,405,104) (medium of October of 2026).

In Lisbon, an apartment for sale from the "No parking" group is requested in 500,000 euros (USD 562,042) in the median and one from the group "2 or more parking" in 1,250,000 euros (USD 1,405,104) (+ 150.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking" (6,802 euros (USD 7,646) / m²) and the most expensive "1 parking" (8,194 euros (USD 9,210) / m²).
