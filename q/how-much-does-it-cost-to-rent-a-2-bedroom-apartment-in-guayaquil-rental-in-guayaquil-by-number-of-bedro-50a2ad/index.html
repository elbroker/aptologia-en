How much does it cost to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in Guayaquil?

USD 850 per month in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between USD 600 and USD 1,200.

In Guayaquil, rent a 1 bedroom apartment costs the median USD 500 per month and one 2 bedroom, USD 850 (+ 70.0%). One of 3 or more bedrooms is requested in USD 1,100.
