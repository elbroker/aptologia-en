How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 3 or more bedrooms in Montoya?

USD 15,980 per month in the median (October of 2026); the central half between USD 10,500 and USD 19,400.

In Punta del Este, an apartment of 3 or more bedrooms is rented for USD 15,980 per month in the middle of Montoya, the most expensive neighborhood, and USD 3,300 per month in Aidy Grill, the cheapest: 4.8 times less. We compare 8 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more bedrooms.
