How much does an apartment room in Bogotá cost?

1,953,167 pesos per month in the medium (rental).

Each room in an apartment for rent costs in the medium 383,333 pesos a month in Tocancipá and 2,200,000 pesos in El Retiro. In Bogotá they are 1,953,167 pesos per room. In the country, the room comes out more expensive in those of 1 alcoba (2,850,000 pesos) than in those of 3 or more (1,333,333 pesos).
