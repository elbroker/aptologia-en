How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 45–70 m² in Rosario, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in Rosario, Argentina is ARS 700,000 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from ARS 605,000 to ARS 845,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is ARS 13,333/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². The median number of bathrooms is 1.
