How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 45–70 m² 0–5 years old in Lagos de Torca, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² 0–5 years old for sale in Lagos de Torca, Bogotá is COP 355,000,000, based on 62 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 300,000,000 to COP 546,200,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,988,899/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 53% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 269,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 80% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
