What does the study “How much it costs to rent a department in Mexico's cities: Mexico City, Monterrey, Guadalajara and more” show?

To rent an apartment of 2 rooms costs in the medium 30,438 Mexican pesos (USD 1,648) a month in Mexico City, the most expensive of the sample, and 26,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,408) in Guadalajara, the cheapest.
