How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Arequipa, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Arequipa, Peru is PEN 553,766, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 485,500 to PEN 669,875. The median price per square metre is PEN 4,597/m² and the median listed size is 125 m². That is 10% above the study's reference median for Arequipa (PEN 501,350). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
