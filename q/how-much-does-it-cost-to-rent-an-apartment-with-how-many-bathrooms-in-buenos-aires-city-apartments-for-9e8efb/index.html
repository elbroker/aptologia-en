How much does it cost to rent an apartment with how many bathrooms in Buenos Aires City?

1 bath: 800,000 Argentine pesos (USD 528) per month; 2 bath: 1,500,000 Argentine pesos (USD 990) per month; 3 bath: 1,800,000 Argentine pesos (USD 1,188) per month (medium of October 2026).

In Buenos Aires City, a rental department of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 800,000 Argentine pesos (USD 528) per month in the medium and one of the group "3 bathrooms" in 1,800,000 Argentine pesos (USD 1,188) per month (+ 125.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (17,777.8 Argentine pesos (USD 11.7) / m² per month) and the most expensive "2 bathrooms" (19,666.7 Argentine pesos (USD 13.0) / m² per month).
