How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² in Península, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Península, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 3,000 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,638 to USD 3,688. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 32.5/m² and the median listed size is 77.5 m². That is 38% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 4,800). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
