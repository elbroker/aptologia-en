Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (Mexican pesos 235.3 (USD 12.7) / m² per month) and the most expensive "1 bath" (Mexican pesos 347.5 (USD 18.8) / m² per month).

In Guadalajara, a rental department of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 18,500 Mexican pesos (USD 1,002) per month in the median and one of the group "3 bathrooms" in 40,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,166) per month (+ 116.2%).
