How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Santa Fe?

The median is $2,100,000 per month, with 389 unique ads on 13 October of 2026.

The average rental of an apartment or studio apartment in Santa Fe (Bogotá) is $2,100,000 per month, about $60,000 per m². A 2 rooms costs in the median $2,700,000. The cheapest neighborhood with 5 ads or more is Las Cruces ($1,165,000) and the most expensive San Diego ($3,161,500).
