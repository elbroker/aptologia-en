How much does it cost to buy a 3-bedroom apartment in Tocancipa Occidente, Tocancipa in 2026?

The median asking price for 3-bedroom apartments for sale in Tocancipa Occidente, Tocancipa is COP 213,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 192,000,000 to COP 225,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,566,667/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 214,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 129,478. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 9.1% of listings include parking.
