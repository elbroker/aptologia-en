How much more does it cost to rent furnished in Colombia?

+ 31.8% in the rental in front of comparable apartments (October of 2026).

In front of comparable apartments in the same city, estrato, rooms and size, the "furnished" feature changes the rental + 31.8% on average in Colombia. The highest premium is in Rionegro (+ 75.9%) and the lowest in Bogotá (+ 19.2%).
