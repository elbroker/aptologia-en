How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with parking in Miramar, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking for sale in Miramar, Barranquilla is COP 315,000,000, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 270,000,000 to COP 352,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,924,225/m² and the median listed size is 63 m². That is 30% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 270,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
