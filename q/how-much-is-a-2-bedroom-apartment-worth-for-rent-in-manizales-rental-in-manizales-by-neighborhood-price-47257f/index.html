How much is a 2 bedroom apartment worth for rent in Manizales?

The median is $1,600,000 per month (542 ads).

The average rental of an apartment in Manizales is $1,800,000 per month; an apartment of 2 alcobas requests in the median $1,600,000. Among the neighborhoods with 30 ads or more, the cheapest is Cyprus ($1,400,000) and the most expensive La Camelia ($3,400,000): 2.4 times more.
