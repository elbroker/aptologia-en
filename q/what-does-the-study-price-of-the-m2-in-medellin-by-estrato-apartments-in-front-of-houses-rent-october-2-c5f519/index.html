What does the study “Price of the m² in Medellín by estrato: apartments in front of houses (rent, October 2026)” show?

In Medellín, the m² of an apartment for rent goes from 28,462 pesos per month in estrato 2 to 55,147 pesos per month in estrato 6. In estrato 5, the m² of a house is 30.8% cheaper than that of an apartment.
