How much does it cost to buy an apartment of what size in Lima?

Less than 50 m²: 332,429 soles (USD 96,762); 50 - 70 m²: 437,118 soles (USD 127,235; 70 - 90 m²: 573,000 soles (USD 166,787); 90 - 120 m²: 757,324 soles (USD 220,439; 120 - 160 m²: 959,252 soles (USD 279,216); More than 160 m²: 1,572,500 suns (USD 457,718) (mid-October of 2026).

In Lima, a department for sale of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 332,429 soles (USD 96,762) in the median and one of the group "More 160 m²" in 1,572,500 soles (USD 457,718) (+ 373.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (7,000 soles (USD 2,037) / m²) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (8,050 soles (USD 2,343) / m²).
