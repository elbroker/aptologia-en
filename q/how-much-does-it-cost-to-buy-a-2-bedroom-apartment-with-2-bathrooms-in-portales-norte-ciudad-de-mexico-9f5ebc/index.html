How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms in Portales Norte, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Portales Norte, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 4,169,224, based on 90 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 3,711,750 to MXN 4,574,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 56,292/m² and the median listed size is 72 m². That is 16% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 4,990,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
