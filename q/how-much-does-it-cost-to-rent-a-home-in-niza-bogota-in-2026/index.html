How much does it cost to rent a home in Niza, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Niza, Bogotá is COP 4,042,000 per month, based on 49 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,250,000 to COP 8,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 36,182/m² and the median listed size is 115 m². That is 16% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,497,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 500,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 73.5% of listings include parking.
