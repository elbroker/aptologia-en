How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 317,500, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 288,750 to USD 350,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,877/m² and the median listed size is 166 m². That is 32% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
