How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking in Vila Romana, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking in Vila Romana, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 7,050 per month, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 3,975 to BRL 10,125. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 74.3/m² and the median listed size is 88.5 m². That is 95% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,560. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
