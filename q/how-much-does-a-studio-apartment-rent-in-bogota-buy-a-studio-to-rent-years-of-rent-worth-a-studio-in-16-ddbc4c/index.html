How much does a studio apartment rent in Bogotá?

6.8% gross per year.

A study apartment in Barranquilla costs the same as 11.7 years of its rent (8.6% gross profitability), the lowest ratio of 16 Colombian cities; in Manizales are 19.2 years. In Bogotá, 14.7 years: 335 million pesos in price compared to 1.90 million pesos per month.
