How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Pereira?

2,600,000 pesos per month in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between 1,900,000 pesos and 3,100,000 pesos.

In Pereira, rent a 1 room apartment costs in the median 1,920,000 pesos per month and one of 2 rooms, 2,600,000 pesos (+ 35.4%). One of 3 or more rooms is requested in 3,100,000 pesos.
