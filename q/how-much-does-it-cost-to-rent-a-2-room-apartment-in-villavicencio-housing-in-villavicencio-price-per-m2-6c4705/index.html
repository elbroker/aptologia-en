How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Villavicencio?

1.20 million pesos per month in the medium (October of 2026).

An apartment of 2 rooms in Villavicencio is requested in 220 million pesos for sale and is rented for 1.20 million pesos a month in the medium. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.8% per year.
