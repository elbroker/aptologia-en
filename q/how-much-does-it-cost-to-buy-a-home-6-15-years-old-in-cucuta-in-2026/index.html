How much does it cost to buy a home 6–15 years old in Cúcuta in 2026?

The median asking price for homes 6–15 years old for sale in Cúcuta is COP 270,000,000, based on 155 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 177,500,000 to COP 420,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,332,540/m² and the median listed size is 68.4 m². That is 29% below the study's reference median (COP 380,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 221,500. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 71% of listings include parking.
