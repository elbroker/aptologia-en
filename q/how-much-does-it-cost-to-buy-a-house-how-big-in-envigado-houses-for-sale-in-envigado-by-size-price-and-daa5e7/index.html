How much does it cost to buy a house how big in Envigado?

90 - 120 m²: 800 million pesos; 120 - 160 m²: 870 million pesos; More than 160 m²: 2,400 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Envigado, a house for sale from the group "90 - 120 m²" is requested in 800 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 2,400 million pesos (+ 200.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "120 - 160 m²" (6.28 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "More than 160 m²" (7.09 million pesos / m²).
