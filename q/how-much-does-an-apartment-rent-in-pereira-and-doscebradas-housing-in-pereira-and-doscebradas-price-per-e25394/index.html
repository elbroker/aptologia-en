How much does an apartment rent in Pereira and Doscebradas?

The gross return is 7.5% per year (rent per m² × 12), before expenditure.

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter of Pereira and Doscebradas is Pinares Alto, with 8.00 million pesos in the median of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is Centro, with 3.63 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 2.60 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 382 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.5% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 15.1% of the rent (400,000 pesos per month in the median).
