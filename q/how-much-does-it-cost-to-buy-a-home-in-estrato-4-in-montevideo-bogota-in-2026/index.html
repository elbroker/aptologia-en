How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 4 in Montevideo, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 4 for sale in Montevideo, Bogotá is COP 559,500,000, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 356,250,000 to COP 795,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,225,983/m² and the median listed size is 58.3 m². That is 21% above the study's reference median (COP 462,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 291,000. 60.3% of listings include parking.
