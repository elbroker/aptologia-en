How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment 0–5 years old in El Recreo, Montería in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments 0–5 years old for sale in El Recreo, Montería is COP 647,015,643, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 500,000,000 to COP 850,000,000. The median listed size is 173 m². That is 66% above the study's reference median for Montería (COP 390,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
