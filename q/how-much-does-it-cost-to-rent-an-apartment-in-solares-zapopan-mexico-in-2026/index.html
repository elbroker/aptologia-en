How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Solares, Zapopan, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Solares, Zapopan, Mexico is MXN 21,250 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 15,250 to MXN 24,750. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 319/m² and the median listed size is 78.1 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Zapopan (MXN 28,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
