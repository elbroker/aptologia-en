How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in estrato 4 in La Estrella in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in estrato 4 in La Estrella is COP 2,600,000 per month, based on 152 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,400,000 to COP 2,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 40,323/m² and the median listed size is 62.5 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 2,600,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
