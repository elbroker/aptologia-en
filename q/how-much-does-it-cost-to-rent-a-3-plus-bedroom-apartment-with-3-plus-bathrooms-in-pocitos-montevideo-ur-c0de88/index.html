How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Pocitos, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Pocitos, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 2,500 per month, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,100 to USD 3,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 15.1/m² and the median listed size is 185 m². That is 26% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 1,990). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
