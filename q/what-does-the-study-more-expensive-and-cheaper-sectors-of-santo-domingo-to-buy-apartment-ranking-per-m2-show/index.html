What does the study “More expensive and cheaper sectors of Santo Domingo to buy apartment: ranking per m²” show?

In Santo Domingo, the sector with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is Piantini (USD 2,633 / m²) and the cheapest East Prado (USD 1,140 / m²), 2.3 times less. We compare 27 sectors with at least 30 ads with area.
