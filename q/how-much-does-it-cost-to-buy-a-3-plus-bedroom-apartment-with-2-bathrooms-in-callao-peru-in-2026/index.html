How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms in Callao, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Callao, Peru is PEN 327,890, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 252,816 to PEN 449,250. The median price per square metre is PEN 3,938/m² and the median listed size is 77 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median for Callao (PEN 304,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
