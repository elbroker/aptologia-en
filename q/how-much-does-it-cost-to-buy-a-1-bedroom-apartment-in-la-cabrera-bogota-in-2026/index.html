How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in La Cabrera, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in La Cabrera, Bogotá is COP 1,190,000,000, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 920,573,000 to COP 1,534,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 18,269,231/m² and the median listed size is 73 m². That is 183% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 420,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 880,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 97.1% of listings include parking.
