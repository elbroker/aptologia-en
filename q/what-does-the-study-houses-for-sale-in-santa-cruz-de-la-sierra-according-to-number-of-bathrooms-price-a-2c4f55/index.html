What does the study “Houses for sale in Santa Cruz de la Sierra according to number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In Santa Cruz de la Sierra, a house for sale from the "1 bathroom" group is ordered in USD 75,000 in the median and one from the "4 or more bathrooms" in USD 231,000 (+ 208.0%). per square metre, the cheapest is "1 bathroom" (USD 659 / m²) and the most expensive "3 bath" (USD 841 / m²).
