How much does it cost to rent an apartment under 45 m² in Campo Belo, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments under 45 m² in Campo Belo, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 3,400 per month, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 2,800 to BRL 4,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 96.8/m² and the median listed size is 37 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 717. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
