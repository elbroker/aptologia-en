How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² in Mirador Sur, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Mirador Sur, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 340,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 260,000 to USD 415,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,833/m² and the median listed size is 174 m². That is 42% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
