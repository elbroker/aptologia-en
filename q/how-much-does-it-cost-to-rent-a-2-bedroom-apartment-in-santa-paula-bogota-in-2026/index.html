How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in Santa Paula, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in Santa Paula, Bogotá is COP 4,500,000 per month, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,750,000 to COP 6,075,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 57,353/m² and the median listed size is 78 m². That is 73% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,600,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 718,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
