How much does it cost to buy a house in Mesa, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Mesa, Envigado is COP 1,770,000,000, based on 69 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 990,000,000 to COP 3,300,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,719,368/m² and the median listed size is 251 m². That is 23% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 2,300,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 962,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 98.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
