How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many bathrooms in Cuenca?

1 bathroom: USD 99,000; 2 bathrooms: USD 125,000; 3 bathrooms: USD 185,000; 4 or more bathrooms: USD 281,500 (mid-October of 2026).

In Cuenca, a department for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in USD 99,000 in the median and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in USD 281,500 (+ 184.3%). per square metre, the cheapest is "4 or more bathrooms" (USD 1,184 / m²) and the most expensive "1 bathroom" (USD 1,381 / m²).
