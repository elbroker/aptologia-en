How much do you rent to buy an apartment to rent in Cali?

The average gross return on 65 comparable neighbourhoods is 6.2% per year (12 months of rent requested on the order price), before HOA fee, predial, vacations and taxes.

The average rental of an apartment in Cali is $1,600,000 per month; a 2 bedroom apartment is requested in the median $1,400,000. Among the neighborhoods with 30 ads or more, the cheapest is Las Américas ($760,000) and the most expensive Arboledas ($8,000,000): 10.5 times more.
