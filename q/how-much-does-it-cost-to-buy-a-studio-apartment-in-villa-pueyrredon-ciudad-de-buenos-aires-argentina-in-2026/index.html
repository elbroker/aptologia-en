How much does it cost to buy a studio apartment in Villa Pueyrredón, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for studio apartments for sale in Villa Pueyrredón, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 86,400, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 74,375 to USD 103,200. The median price per square metre is USD 2,469/m² and the median listed size is 34 m². That is 1% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 87,150). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
