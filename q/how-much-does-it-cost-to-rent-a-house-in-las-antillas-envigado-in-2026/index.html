How much does it cost to rent a house in Las Antillas, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Las Antillas, Envigado is COP 6,825,000 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,687,500 to COP 10,125,000. That is 41% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 11,500,000).
