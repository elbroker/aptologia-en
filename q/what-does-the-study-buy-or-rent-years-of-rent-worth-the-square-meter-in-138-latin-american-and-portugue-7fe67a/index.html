What does the study “Buy or rent? Years of rent worth the square meter in 138 Latin American and Portuguese neighborhoods, districts and communes” show?

The area of Latin America (outside Colombia) where the square meter is paid with less years of rent is Sucre (Caracas, Venezuela): 7.9 years; in Lumiar (Lisbon, Portugal) it is necessary 31.5. The city with the lowest median areas is Caracas (Venezuela) (10.6 years) and the highest Lisbon (Portugal) (30.0).
