Where is it cheaper to buy a 3 apartment or more bedrooms in Asunción?

In Recoleta: USD 234,900 in the median.

In Asunción, an apartment of 3 or more bedrooms costs USD 585,000 in the middle in Trinidad, the most expensive neighborhood, and USD 234,900 in Recoleta, the cheapest: 2.5 times less. We compare 10 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more bedrooms.
