Where is it cheaper to buy an apartment of 3 or more rooms in Manizales?

In San Jorge: 341 million pesos in the median.

In Manizales, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs 850 million pesos in the median in Cerro de Oro, the most expensive neighborhood, and 341 million pesos in San Jorge, the cheapest: 2.5 times less. We compare 17 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
