What does the study “Duplex and penthouse for rent in Bogotá: how much more they cost than a normal apartment (2026)” show?

In Bogotá, a penthouse for rent costs 0.5% less per m² than a comparable apartment and a duplex 6.3% less. The medium rental is $14,000,000 for a penthouse, $7,500,000 for a duplex and $2,300,000 for the rest.
