How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Los Alpes, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Los Alpes, Barranquilla is COP 2,000,000 per month, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,750,000 to COP 2,775,000. That is 22% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,561,500).
