How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more 0–5 years old in Manga, Cartagena in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more 0–5 years old for sale in Manga, Cartagena is COP 2,173,741,824, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,575,000,000 to COP 3,725,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,000,000/m² and the median listed size is 401 m². That is 67% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 1,300,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 95.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
