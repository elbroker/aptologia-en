How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with 3+ bathrooms in Mariscal López, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Mariscal López, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 555,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 350,000 to USD 805,000. The median price per square metre is USD 967/m² and the median listed size is 480 m². That is 59% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 350,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
