How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with 2 bathrooms in Mataderos, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with 2 bathrooms for sale in Mataderos, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 220,000, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 174,000 to USD 310,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,468/m² and the median listed size is 161 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 290,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
