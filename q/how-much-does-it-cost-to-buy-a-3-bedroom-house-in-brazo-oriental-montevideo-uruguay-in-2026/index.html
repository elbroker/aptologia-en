How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house in Brazo Oriental, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses for sale in Brazo Oriental, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 248,000, based on 53 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 170,000 to USD 315,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,220/m² and the median listed size is 187 m². That is 29% below the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 350,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
