Where does he pay the least?

In Lumiar: 3.2% gross per year (31.5 years of rent).

In Lisbon, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical freguesia 3.3% gross per year: the price ordered is equivalent to 30.0 years of rent. The freguesia with the highest gross return is Arroios (4.1%) and that of minor, Lumir (3.2%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 3.7%.
