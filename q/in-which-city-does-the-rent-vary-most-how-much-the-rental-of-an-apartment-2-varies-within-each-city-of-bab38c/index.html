In which city does the rent vary most?

In Bogotá: P75 is 3.19 times P25.

In Bogotá the rental of a typical 2 expensive rooms (percentile 75) is 3.19 the typical cheap (percentile 25), the largest dispersion of 21 cities; in Sabaneta, only 1.32 times. In Bogotá, the central half of the 2 room offer goes from 1.60 million pesos to 5.09 million pesos a month, and the 10% cheaper is below 1.25 million pesos.
