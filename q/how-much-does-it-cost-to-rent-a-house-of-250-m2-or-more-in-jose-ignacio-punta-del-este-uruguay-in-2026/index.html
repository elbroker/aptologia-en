How much does it cost to rent a house of 250 m² or more in José Ignacio, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses of 250 m² or more in José Ignacio, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 18,575 per month, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 12,300 to USD 22,175. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 49.9/m² and the median listed size is 347 m². That is 154% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 7,300). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
