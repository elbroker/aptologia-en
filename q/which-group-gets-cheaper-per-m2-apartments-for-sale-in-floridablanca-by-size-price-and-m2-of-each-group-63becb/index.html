Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (4.09 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (5.26 million pesos / m²).

In Floridablanca, an apartment for sale from the group "50 - 70 m²" is requested in 300 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 765 million pesos (+ 155.4%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (4.09 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (5.26 million pesos / m²).
