What does the study “Price of the m² in Zipaquirá by estrato: apartments in front of houses (sale, October 2026)” show?

In Zipaquirá, the m² of an apartment for sale goes from 3.14 million pesos in estrato 2 to 4.68 million pesos in estrato 4. In estrato 4, the m² of a house is 9.9% cheaper than that of an apartment.
