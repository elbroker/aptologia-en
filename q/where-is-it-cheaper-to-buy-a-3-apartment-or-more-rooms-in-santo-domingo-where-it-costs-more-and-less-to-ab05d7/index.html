Where is it cheaper to buy a 3 apartment or more rooms in Santo Domingo?

In Eastern Prado: USD 137,000 in the median.

In Santo Domingo, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs USD 1,130,000 in the median in Av. Anacaona, the most expensive sector, and USD 137,000 in Eastern Prado, the cheapest: 8.2 times less. We compare 19 sectors with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
