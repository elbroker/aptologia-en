How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in San Isidro, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in San Isidro, Lima, Peru is PEN 6,430 per month, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 4,996 to PEN 8,156. The median monthly rent per square metre is PEN 32.3/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 111% above the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 3,050). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
