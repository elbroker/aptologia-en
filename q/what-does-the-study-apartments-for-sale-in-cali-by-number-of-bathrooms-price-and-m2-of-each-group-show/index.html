What does the study “Apartments for sale in Cali by number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In Cali, an apartment for sale from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 215 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 1,100 million pesos (+ 411.6%). For square meter, the cheapest is "1 bath" (3.90 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (5.95 million pesos / m²).
