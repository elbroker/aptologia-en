How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² with parking in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² with parking for sale in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 211,400, based on 64 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 190,162 to USD 239,250. The median price per square metre is USD 2,633/m² and the median listed size is 80.5 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
