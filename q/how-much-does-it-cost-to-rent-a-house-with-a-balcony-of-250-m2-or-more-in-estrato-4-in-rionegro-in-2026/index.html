How much does it cost to rent a house with a balcony of 250 m² or more in estrato 4 in Rionegro in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses with a balcony of 250 m² or more in estrato 4 in Rionegro is COP 10,100,000 per month, based on 74 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 6,925,000 to COP 14,375,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 24,000/m² and the median listed size is 400 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Rionegro (COP 10,500,000). 95.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
