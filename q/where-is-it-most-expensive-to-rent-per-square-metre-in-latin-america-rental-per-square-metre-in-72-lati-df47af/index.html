Where is it most expensive to rent per square metre in Latin America?

In Punta del Este (Uruguay), with a median of USD 40.6 per m² per month.

The highest square meter rental of the sample is in Punta del Este (Uruguay): USD 40.6 per m² per month; the lowest, in Cochabamba, Bolivia, with USD 4.0. In Bogotá, the average rent is USD 15.2 per m² per month (post 15 of 72).
