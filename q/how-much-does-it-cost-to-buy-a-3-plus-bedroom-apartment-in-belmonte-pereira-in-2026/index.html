How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Belmonte, Pereira in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Belmonte, Pereira is COP 450,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 4% below the study's reference median for Pereira (COP 470,000,000).
