How much does it cost to rent a house in Colombia?

Between 2,600,000 pesos (Bello) and 12,000,000 pesos (La Calera) in the monthly median.

Landing a house costs in the median 2,600,000 pesos a month in Bello, the cheapest city of 29, and 12,000,000 pesos in La Calera, the most expensive. In Bogotá the median is 10,000,000 pesos. In the whole country, the house for medium rental is 278 m².
