How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in El Refugio, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in El Refugio, Bogotá is COP 8,500,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,425,000 to COP 9,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 61,202/m² and the median listed size is 140 m². That is 227% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,600,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,471,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 93.3% of listings include parking.
