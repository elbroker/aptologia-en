What does the study “Price of houses for sale in Santa Cruz de la Sierra by number of bedrooms: how much each size costs” show?

In Santa Cruz de la Sierra, a 1 bedroom house costs USD 75,000 in the median, one 2 in the middle of the bedroom USD 79,000 (+ 5.3%) and one 3 or more USD 167,000 (+ 111.4%).
