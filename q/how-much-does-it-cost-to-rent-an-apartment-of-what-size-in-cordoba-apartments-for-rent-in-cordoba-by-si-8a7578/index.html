How much does it cost to rent an apartment of what size in Córdoba?

Less than 50 m²: 600,000 Argentine pesos (USD 396) per month; 50 - 70 m²: 795,000 Argentine pesos (USD 525) per month; 70 - 90 m²: 950,000 Argentine pesos (USD 627) per month (medium October of 2026.

In Córdoba, a rental department of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 600,000 Argentine pesos (USD 396) per month in the median and one of the group "70 - 90 m²" in 950,000 Argentine pesos (USD 627) per month (+ 58.3%). For each square metre, the cheapest is "50 - 70 m²" (13,492.1 Argentine pesos (USD 8.9) / m² per month) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (15,428.6 Argentine pesos (USD 10.2) / m² per month).
