How much does it cost to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in Zapopan?

28,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,516) per month in the medium (October 2026); the central half of the offer is between 21,550 Mexican pesos (USD 1,167) and 38,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,058).

In Zapopan, a 1 repurchase department is repaid for 19,475 Mexican pesos (USD 1,055) per month in the median, one 2 repurchase 28,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,516) (+ 43.8%) and one 3 or more 45,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,437) (+ 60.7%).
