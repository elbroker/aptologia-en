Which group gets cheaper per m²?

per square metre, the cheapest is "4 or more bathrooms" (281.7 Mexican pesos (USD 15.3) / m² per month) and the most expensive "1 bath" (400.0 Mexican pesos (USD 21.7) / m² per month).

In Mexico City, a rental department of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 22,500 Mexican pesos (USD 1,218) per month in the median and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 77,500 Mexican pesos (USD 4,197) per month (+ 244.4%). per square metre, the cheapest is "4 or more bathrooms" (281.7 Mexican pesos (USD 15.3) / m² per month) and the most expensive "1 bath" (400.0 Mexican pesos (USD 21.7) / m² per month).
