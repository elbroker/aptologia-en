How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² in Bellavista, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Bellavista, Cali is COP 1,196,617,446, based on 62 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 900,000,000 to COP 1,560,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,018,861/m² and the median listed size is 182 m². That is 178% above the study's reference median for Cali (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,410,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
