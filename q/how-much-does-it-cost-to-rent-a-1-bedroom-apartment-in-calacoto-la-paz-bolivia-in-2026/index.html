How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in Calacoto, La Paz, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in Calacoto, La Paz, Bolivia is USD 560 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 431 to USD 975. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 10/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 19% above the study's reference median for La Paz (USD 470). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 40. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
