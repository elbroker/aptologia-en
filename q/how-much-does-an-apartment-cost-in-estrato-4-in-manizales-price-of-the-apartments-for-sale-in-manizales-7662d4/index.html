How much does an apartment cost in estrato 4 in Manizales?

350 million pesos in the median.

In Manizales the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 4.66 million pesos in estrato 3 and 6.40 million pesos in estrato 6, 1.4 times more. The average total price ranges from 280 million pesos to 620 million pesos according to the estrato.
