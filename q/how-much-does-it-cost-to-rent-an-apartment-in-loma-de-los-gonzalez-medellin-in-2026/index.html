How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Loma de los González, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Loma de los González, Medellín is COP 6,300,000 per month, based on 116 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,000,000 to COP 8,050,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 60,145/m² and the median listed size is 106 m². That is 31% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 99.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
