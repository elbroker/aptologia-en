How much does it cost to rent a home with a terrace or balcony in the Suba locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes with a terrace or balcony in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 4,500,000 per month, based on 588 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,600,000 to COP 10,350,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 40,625/m² and the median listed size is 106 m². That is 46% above the study's reference median (COP 3,079,400). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 667,400. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 87.9% of listings include parking.
