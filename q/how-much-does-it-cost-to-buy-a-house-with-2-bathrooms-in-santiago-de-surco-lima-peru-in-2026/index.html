How much does it cost to buy a house with 2 bathrooms in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with 2 bathrooms for sale in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is PEN 1,800,000, based on 75 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 1,364,700 to PEN 2,033,950. The median price per square metre is PEN 6,879/m² and the median listed size is 268 m². That is 17% below the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 2,173,590). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
