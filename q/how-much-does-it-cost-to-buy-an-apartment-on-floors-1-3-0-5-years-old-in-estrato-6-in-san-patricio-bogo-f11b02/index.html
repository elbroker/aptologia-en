How much does it cost to buy an apartment on floors 1–3 0–5 years old in estrato 6 in San Patricio, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments on floors 1–3 0–5 years old in estrato 6 for sale in San Patricio, Bogotá is COP 1,000,000,000, based on 55 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 766,500,000 to COP 1,473,509,000. The median price per square metre is COP 11,340,206/m² and the median listed size is 116 m². That is 33% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 817,500. 96.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
