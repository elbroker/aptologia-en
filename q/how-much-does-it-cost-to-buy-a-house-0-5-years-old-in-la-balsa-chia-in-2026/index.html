How much does it cost to buy a house 0–5 years old in La Balsa, Chía in 2026?

The median asking price for houses 0–5 years old for sale in La Balsa, Chía is COP 1,225,000,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 782,500,000 to COP 1,941,907,500. The median price per square metre is COP 5,629,408/m² and the median listed size is 246 m². That is 11% above the study's reference median for Chía (COP 1,100,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 720,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
