Where is it cheaper to buy apartment in Jamundí?

Among the sectors with 30 ads or more, in Cr. los Naranjos: 3.14 million pesos per m².

The sector with the most expensive square meter of Jamundí is Alfaguara, with 3.82 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Cr. the Naranjos, with 3.14 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 1.22 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 205 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 6.9% per year.
