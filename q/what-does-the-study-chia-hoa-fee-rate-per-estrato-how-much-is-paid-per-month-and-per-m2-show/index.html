What does the study “Chia HOA fee rate per estrato: how much is paid per month and per m²” show?

In Chia, the median rate of HOA fee of an apartment goes from 189,050 pesos per month in estrato 2 to 1,644,000 pesos in estrato 6, 8.7 times more. per square meter, it goes from 3,875 pesos to 8,972 pesos per month.
