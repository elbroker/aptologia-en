How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Conocoto, Quito, Ecuador in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Conocoto, Quito, Ecuador is USD 135,000, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 115,000 to USD 159,000. The median price per square metre is USD 716/m² and the median listed size is 189 m². That is 27% below the study's reference median for Quito (USD 185,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
