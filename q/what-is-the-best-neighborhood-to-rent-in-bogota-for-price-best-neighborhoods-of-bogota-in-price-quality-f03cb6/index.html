What is the best neighborhood to rent in Bogotá for price?

Depends on the estrato. For space, parking and rooms by weight: Mirandela (estrato 3), Santa Teresa (estrato 4), La Calleja (estrato 5) and Santa Barbara Central (estrato 6), with cut to 12 October of 2026.

For what you pay, the most important areas in Bogotá are Mirandela in estrato 3 (33.6 m² for each million rent), Santa Teresa in estrato 4 (26.0 m² per million, 76% with parking lot), La Calleja in estrato 5 and Santa Barbara Central in estrato 6. The index compares each neighborhood only with neighborhoods of the same estrato: 50% square meters per weight, 25% parking lot and 25% rooms per weight.
