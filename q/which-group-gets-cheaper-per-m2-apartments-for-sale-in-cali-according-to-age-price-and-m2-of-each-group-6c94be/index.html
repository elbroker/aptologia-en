Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (4.08 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "1 to 5 years" (4.73 million pesos / m²).

In Cali, an apartment for sale from the group "To premiere (0 years)" is requested in 470 million pesos in the median and one from the group "21 to 30 years" in 500 million pesos (+ 6.4%). For square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (4.08 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "1 to 5 years" (4.73 million pesos / m²).
