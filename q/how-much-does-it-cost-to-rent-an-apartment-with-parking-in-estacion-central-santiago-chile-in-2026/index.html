How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking in Estación Central, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking in Estación Central, Santiago, Chile is CLP 350,000 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from CLP 287,500 to CLP 448,500. The median listed size is 50 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median for Santiago (CLP 450,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
