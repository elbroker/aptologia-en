How much does it cost to buy a house in San Joaquín, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in San Joaquín, Medellín is COP 1,400,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 925,000,000 to COP 2,200,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,191,950/m² and the median listed size is 230 m². That is 1% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 1,390,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
