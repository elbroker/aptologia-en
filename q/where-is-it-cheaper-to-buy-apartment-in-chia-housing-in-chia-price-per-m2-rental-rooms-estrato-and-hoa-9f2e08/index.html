Where is it cheaper to buy apartment in Chia?

Among the sectors with 30 ads or more, in Samaria: 4.98 million pesos per m².

The sector with the most expensive square meter of Chia is Vereda la Balsa, with 6.85 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is Samaria, with 4.98 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 1.55 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 290 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.2% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 16.2% of the rent (295,600 pesos per month in the median).
