How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in San Joaquín, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in San Joaquín, Medellín is COP 3,500,000 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,700,000 to COP 4,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 35,714/m² and the median listed size is 95 m². That is 36% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 5,500,000). The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
