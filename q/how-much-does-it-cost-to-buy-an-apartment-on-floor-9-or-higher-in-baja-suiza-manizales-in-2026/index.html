How much does it cost to buy an apartment on floor 9 or higher in Baja Suiza, Manizales in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments on floor 9 or higher for sale in Baja Suiza, Manizales is COP 477,000,000, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 450,000,000 to COP 550,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,319,133/m² and the median listed size is 63.5 m². That is 6% above the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 325,500. The most frequent estrato is 5, 91.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
