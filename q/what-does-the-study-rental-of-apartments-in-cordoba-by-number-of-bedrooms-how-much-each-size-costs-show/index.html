What does the study “Rental of apartments in Cordoba by number of bedrooms: how much each size costs” show?

In Córdoba, a 1 bedroom department is rented for 625,000 Argentine pesos (USD 413) per month in the median, one 2 Argentine 850,000 bedroom (USD 561) (+ 36.0%) and one 3 or more 1,300,000 Argentine pesos (USD 858) (+ 52.9%).
