How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 5 in Versalles, Floridablanca in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 5 for sale in Versalles, Floridablanca is COP 580,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 517,000,000 to COP 690,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,181,818/m² and the median listed size is 110 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 422,000. 90% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
