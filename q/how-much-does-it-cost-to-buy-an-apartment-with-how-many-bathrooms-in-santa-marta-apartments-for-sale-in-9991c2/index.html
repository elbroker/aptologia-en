How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many bathrooms in Santa Marta?

1 bathroom: 400 million pesos; 2 bathrooms: 600 million pesos; 3 bathrooms: 835 million pesos; 4 or more bathrooms: 1,200 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Santa Marta, an apartment for sale from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 400 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 1,200 million pesos (+ 200.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "4 or more bathrooms" (6.10 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "1 bath" (10.79 million pesos / m²).
