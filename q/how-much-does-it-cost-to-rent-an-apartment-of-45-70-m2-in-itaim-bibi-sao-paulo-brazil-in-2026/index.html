How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 45–70 m² in Itaim Bibi, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in Itaim Bibi, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 5,690 per month, based on 51 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 4,530 to BRL 8,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 110/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 57% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,504. 96% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
