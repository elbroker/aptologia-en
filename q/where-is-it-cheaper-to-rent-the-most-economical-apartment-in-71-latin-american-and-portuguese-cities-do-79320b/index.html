Where is it cheaper to rent?

In Cochabamba, Bolivia, from USD 236 per month.

The 10% cheapest of the apartments for sale starts in USD 47,128 in Soacha (Colombia) and in USD 365,208 in Lisbon (Portugal), the highest entry price of 64 cities. For rent, the entry goes from USD 236 per month in Cochabamba to USD 1,500 in Punta del Este. In Bogotá: USD 78,547 for sale and USD 424 per month for rent.
