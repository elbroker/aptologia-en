How many meters do you have an apartment of 2 rooms in Bogotá?

For sale, 67 m² in the medium; for rent, 62 m².

An apartment for sale in Bogotá has in the medium 98 m², and one for rent 70 m². A 2 rooms for sale measures 67 m² and a 3 rooms 124 m²; 16.9% of the apartments for sale have less than 50 m².
