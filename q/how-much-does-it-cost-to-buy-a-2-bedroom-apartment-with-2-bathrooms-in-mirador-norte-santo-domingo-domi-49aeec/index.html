How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms in Mirador Norte, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Mirador Norte, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 217,500, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 202,000 to USD 237,912. The median price per square metre is USD 1,965/m² and the median listed size is 116 m². That is 9% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
