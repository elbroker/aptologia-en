How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Valle del Lili, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Valle del Lili, Cali is COP 575,000,000, based on 72 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 507,000,000 to COP 692,071,151. The median price per square metre is COP 5,209,957/m² and the median listed size is 110 m². That is 34% above the study's reference median for Cali (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 500,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 98.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
