How much less is an old apartment worth in Colombia?

One over 20 years is asked for a 27.0% cheaper per m² than a new one in the same city and estrato.

An apartment over 20 years old is requested in Colombia a 27.0% cheaper per square metre than a new one from the same city and estrato. Unmatched, the median is 7.24 million pesos per m² in new to 6.02 million pesos in those over 20 years.
