What is the profitability of the rent in Colombia?

8.1% gross annual: USD 13.7 per m² per month compared to USD 2,027 per m² of sale.

Between 12 countries, the rent is more than the purchase price in Venezuela (10.1% gross per year) and less in Portugal (3.9%). Colombia is at 8.1%.
