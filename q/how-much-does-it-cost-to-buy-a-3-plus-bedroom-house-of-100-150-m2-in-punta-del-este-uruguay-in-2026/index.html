How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 100–150 m² in Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 100–150 m² for sale in Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 360,000, based on 388 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 295,000 to USD 452,500. The median price per square metre is USD 2,889/m² and the median listed size is 125 m². That is 49% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
