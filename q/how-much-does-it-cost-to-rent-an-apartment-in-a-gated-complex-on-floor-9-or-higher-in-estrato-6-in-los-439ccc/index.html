How much does it cost to rent an apartment in a gated complex on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 in Los Balsos, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in a gated complex on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 in Los Balsos, Medellín is COP 6,650,000 per month, based on 63 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,050,000 to COP 9,600,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 57,037/m² and the median listed size is 124 m². That is 39% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). 96.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
