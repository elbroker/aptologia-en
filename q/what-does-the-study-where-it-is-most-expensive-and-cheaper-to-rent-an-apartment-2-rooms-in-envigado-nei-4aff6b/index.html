What does the study “Where it is most expensive and cheaper to rent an apartment 2 rooms in Envigado: neighborhood ranking” show?

In Envigado, an apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 4,700,000 pesos per month in the medium in Zuñiga, the most expensive neighborhood, and 2,990,000 pesos per month in Las Interas, the cheapest: 1.6 times less. We compare 17 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
