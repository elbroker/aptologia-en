How much is HOA fee paid in Pereira?

The median rate is 398,000 pesos per month (4,794 pesos per m²), according to October 2026 announcements.

In Pereira, the most expensive HOA fee per square metre is paid in Alps: 6,193 pesos per m² per month in the median (637,000 fee pesos). The cheapest, in Los Corales, with 3,673 pesos per m². The median rate for the whole city is 398,000 pesos per month (4,794 pesos per m²).
