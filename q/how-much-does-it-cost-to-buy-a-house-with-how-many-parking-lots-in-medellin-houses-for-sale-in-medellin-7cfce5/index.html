How much does it cost to buy a house with how many parking lots in Medellín?

No parking lot: 525 million pesos; 1 parking lot: 920 million pesos; 2 or more parking lot: 2,200 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Medellín, a house for sale from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 525 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "2 or more parking lot" in 2,200 million pesos (+ 319.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (3.98 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (6.36 million pesos / m²).
