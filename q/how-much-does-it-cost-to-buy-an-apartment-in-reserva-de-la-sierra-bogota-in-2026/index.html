How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Reserva de la Sierra, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Reserva de la Sierra, Bogotá is COP 2,200,000,000, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,650,000,000 to COP 2,500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,322,581/m² and the median listed size is 182 m². That is 188% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,400,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
