How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with parking on floors 4–8 in Rodadero, Santa Marta in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking on floors 4–8 for sale in Rodadero, Santa Marta is COP 442,500,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 379,250,000 to COP 637,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,335,106/m² and the median listed size is 115 m². That is 26% below the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 600,500. The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
