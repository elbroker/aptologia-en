How much does it cost to buy an apartment in San Jose de Usaquen, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in San Jose de Usaquen, Bogotá is COP 275,000,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 258,000,000 to COP 620,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,700,000/m² and the median listed size is 48 m². That is 64% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 141,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 43.9% of listings include parking.
