How much does it cost to rent an apartment of what size in Santa Marta?

Less than 50 m²: 2,300,000 pesos per month; 50 - 70 m²: 2,500,000 pesos per month; 70 - 90 m²: 3,500,000 pesos per month; 90 - 120 m²: 4,500,000 pesos per month; 120 - 160 m²: 4,900,000 pesos per month; More than 160 m²: 8,000,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Santa Marta, an apartment for rent from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 2,300,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 8,000,000 pesos per month (+ 247.8%). For square meter, the cheapest is "120 - 160 m²" (36,765 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (57,609 pesos / m² per month).
