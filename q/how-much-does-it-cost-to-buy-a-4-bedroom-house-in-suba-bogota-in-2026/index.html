How much does it cost to buy a 4+ bedroom house in Suba, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in Suba, Bogotá is COP 1,280,000,000, based on 79 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 690,000,000 to COP 1,795,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,451,613/m² and the median listed size is 280 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,250,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 700,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 82.3% of listings include parking.
