How much is the rent of a 3 room house in Bogotá?

The median is $7,500,000 per month.

Landing a house in Bogotá costs at the median $10,000,000 per month, with 327 m²; per square meter ($30,799) comes out 38.4% cheaper than an apartment ($50,000). A house of 3 rooms asks for $7,500,000 and one of 4 or more, $11,500,000.
