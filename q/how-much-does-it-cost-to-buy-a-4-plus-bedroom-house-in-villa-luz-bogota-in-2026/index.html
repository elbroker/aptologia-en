How much does it cost to buy a 4+ bedroom house in Villa Luz, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in Villa Luz, Bogotá is COP 850,000,000, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 750,000,000 to COP 1,082,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,417,944/m² and the median listed size is 258 m². That is 32% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,250,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 100,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 90.7% of listings include parking.
