How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in Chicó Norte Iii, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in Chicó Norte Iii, Bogotá is COP 3,950,000 per month, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 88% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,100,000).
