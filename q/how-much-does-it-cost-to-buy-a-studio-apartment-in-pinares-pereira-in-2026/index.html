How much does it cost to buy a studio apartment in Pinares, Pereira in 2026?

The median asking price for studio apartments for sale in Pinares, Pereira is COP 349,065,000, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 280,000,000 to COP 395,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,852,425/m² and the median listed size is 51 m². That is 3% above the study's reference median for Pereira (COP 340,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 300,457. The most frequent estrato is 6, 95.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
