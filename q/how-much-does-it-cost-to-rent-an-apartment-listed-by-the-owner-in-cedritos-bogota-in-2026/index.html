How much does it cost to rent an apartment listed by the owner in Cedritos, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments listed by the owner in Cedritos, Bogotá is COP 3,041,950 per month, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,475,000 to COP 3,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 47,428/m² and the median listed size is 65 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 500,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 97.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
