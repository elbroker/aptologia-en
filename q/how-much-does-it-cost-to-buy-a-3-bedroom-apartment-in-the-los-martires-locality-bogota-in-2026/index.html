How much does it cost to buy a 3-bedroom apartment in the Los Mártires locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3-bedroom apartments for sale in the Los Mártires locality, Bogotá is COP 408,975,000, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 353,500,000 to COP 485,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,926,242/m² and the median listed size is 74 m². That is 41% above the study's reference median (COP 289,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 348,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 64.4% of listings include parking.
