What does the study “Apartments for rent in Barranquilla by number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In Barranquilla, an apartment for rent from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 1,750,000 pesos per month in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 7,000,000 pesos per month (+ 300.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (31,897 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (38,814 pesos / m² per month).
