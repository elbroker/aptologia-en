How much does it cost to rent a home in Vila das Mercês, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Vila das Mercês, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 2,385 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 1,875 to BRL 3,420. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 44.8/m² and the median listed size is 63.5 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 625. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
