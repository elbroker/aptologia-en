What does the study “What more does it give to rent: apartment 1, 2 or 3 rooms? Profitability by size in Colombia” show?

In Colombia, buying for rent yields more in apartments of 3 or more rooms (8.1% gross annual) and less in those of 1 room (7.4%). In 2 rooms the profitability is 7.7%. The table shows the same calculation in large cities.
