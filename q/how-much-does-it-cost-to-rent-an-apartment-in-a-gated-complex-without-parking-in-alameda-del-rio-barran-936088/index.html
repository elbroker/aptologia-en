How much does it cost to rent an apartment in a gated complex without parking in Alameda del Río, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in a gated complex without parking in Alameda del Río, Barranquilla is COP 1,291,500 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,120,050 to COP 1,637,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 25,319/m² and the median listed size is 51.7 m². That is 54% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 182,150. The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
