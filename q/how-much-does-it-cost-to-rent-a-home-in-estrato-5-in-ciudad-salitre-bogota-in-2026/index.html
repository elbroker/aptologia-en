How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 5 in Ciudad Salitre, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 5 in Ciudad Salitre, Bogotá is COP 5,350,000 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,149,500 to COP 6,850,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 63,485/m² and the median listed size is 86 m². That is 60% above the study's reference median (COP 3,339,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 630,000. 86.1% of listings include parking.
