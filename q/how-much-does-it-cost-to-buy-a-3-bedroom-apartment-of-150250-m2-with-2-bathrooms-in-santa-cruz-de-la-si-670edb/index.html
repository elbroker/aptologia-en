How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 170,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 145,000 to USD 212,000. The median price per square metre is USD 987/m² and the median listed size is 170 m². That is 70% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 80. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
