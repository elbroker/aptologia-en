How much does it cost to rent an apartment of what size in Cúcuta?

Less than 50 m²: 1,095,000 pesos per month; 50 - 70 m²: 1,300,000 pesos per month; 70 - 90 m²: 1,542,500 pesos per month; 90 - 120 m²: 1,800,000 pesos per month; 120 - 160 m²: 2,000,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Cúcuta, an apartment for rent from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 1,095,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "120 - 160 m²" in 2,000,000 pesos per month (+ 82.6%). For square meter, the cheapest is "70 - 90 m²" (21,341 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (23,634 pesos / m² per month).
