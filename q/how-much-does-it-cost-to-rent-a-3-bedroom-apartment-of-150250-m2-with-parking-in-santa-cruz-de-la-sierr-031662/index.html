How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² with parking in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with parking in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 992 per month, based on 90 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 750 to USD 1,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 5/m² and the median listed size is 186 m². That is 74% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 570). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 70. The median number of bathrooms is 3.
