How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom pet-friendly apartment with 2 bathrooms in Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom pet-friendly apartments with 2 bathrooms in Bogotá is COP 2,900,000 per month, based on 215 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,000,000 to COP 4,920,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 45,743/m² and the median listed size is 66 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 565,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 82.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
