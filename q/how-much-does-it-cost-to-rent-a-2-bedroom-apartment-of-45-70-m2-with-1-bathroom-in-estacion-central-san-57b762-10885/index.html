How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with 1 bathroom in Estación Central, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with 1 bathroom in Estación Central, Santiago, Chile is UF 9.11 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 8.5 to UF 9.9. The median monthly rent per square metre is UF 0.19/m² and the median listed size is 48 m². That is 34% below the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 13.8). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
