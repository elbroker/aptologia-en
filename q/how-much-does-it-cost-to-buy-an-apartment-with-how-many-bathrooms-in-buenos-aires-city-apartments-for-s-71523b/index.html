How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many bathrooms in Buenos Aires City?

1 bathroom: USD 115,450; 2 bathrooms: USD 249,000; 3 bathrooms: USD 430,000; 4 or more bathrooms: USD 760,000 (mid-October of 2026).

In Buenos Aires City, a department for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in USD 115,450 in the median and one in the group "4 or more bathrooms" in USD 760,000 (+ 558.3%). per square metre, the cheapest is "1 bathroom" (USD 2,340 / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (USD 3,436 / m²).
