How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 3,250 per month, based on 81 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,600 to USD 4,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 16.9/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 63% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 1,990). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
