How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in Rosario, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Rosario, Argentina is ARS 900,000 per month, based on 55 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from ARS 750,000 to ARS 1,325,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is ARS 12,727/m² and the median listed size is 85 m². That is 20% above the study's reference median for Rosario (ARS 750,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
