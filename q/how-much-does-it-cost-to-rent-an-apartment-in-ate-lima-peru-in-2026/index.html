How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Ate, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Ate, Lima, Peru is PEN 1,600 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 1,325 to PEN 2,175. The median listed size is 88.5 m². That is 48% below the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 3,050). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
