How much does it cost to rent a home with security or a doorman in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes with security or a doorman in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá is COP 5,600,000 per month, based on 1,631 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,600,000 to COP 12,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 64,000/m² and the median listed size is 85 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median (COP 7,327,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,285,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 80.1% of listings include parking.
