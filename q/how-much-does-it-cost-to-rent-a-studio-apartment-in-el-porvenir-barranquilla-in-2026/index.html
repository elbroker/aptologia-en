How much does it cost to rent a studio apartment in El Porvenir, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for studio apartments in El Porvenir, Barranquilla is COP 1,350,000 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,200,000 to COP 1,500,000. That is 31% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 1,967,000).
