Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (40,000 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (52,174 pesos / m² per month).

In Medellín, an apartment for rent from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 2,800,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "2 or more parking lot" in 7,000,000 pesos per month (+ 150.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (40,000 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (52,174 pesos / m² per month).
