How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house of 150–250 m² with parking in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² with parking in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 950 per month, based on 182 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 700 to USD 1,393. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 4.71/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 19% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 1,166). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
