What is the most expensive sector in Cajicá?

Calahorra, with 6.88 million pesos per m² in apartments for sale (October of 2026).

The sector with the most expensive square meter of Cajicá is Calahorra, with 6.88 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Capellania, with 4.79 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 1.90 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 330 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.2% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 14.3% of the rent (300,000 pesos per month in the median).
