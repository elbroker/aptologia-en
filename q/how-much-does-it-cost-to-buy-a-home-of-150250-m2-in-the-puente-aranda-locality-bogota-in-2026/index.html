How much does it cost to buy a home of 150–250 m² in the Puente Aranda locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes of 150–250 m² for sale in the Puente Aranda locality, Bogotá is COP 850,000,000, based on 170 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 680,000,000 to COP 1,237,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,415,185/m² and the median listed size is 190 m². That is 31% above the study's reference median (COP 650,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 935,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 83.5% of listings include parking.
