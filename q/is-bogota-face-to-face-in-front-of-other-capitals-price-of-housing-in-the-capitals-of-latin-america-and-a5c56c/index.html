Is Bogotá face to face in front of other capitals?

Bogotá costs USD 2,282 per m² for sale; see table for position in front of Lima, Mexico City, Santiago or Buenos Aires.

Among the capital cities of the region, the most expensive square meter for sale is that of Lisbon (USD 8,383 / m²) and the cheapest of Quito (USD 1,000 / m²). Bogotá marks USD 2,282 / m² for sale and USD 831 per month for an apartment with 2 rooms; the capital where that rental is cheapest is La Paz (USD 506).
