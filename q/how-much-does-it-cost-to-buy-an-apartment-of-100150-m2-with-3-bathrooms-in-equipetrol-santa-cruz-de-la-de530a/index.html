How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 186,750, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 148,750 to USD 245,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,617/m² and the median listed size is 116 m². That is 87% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 90. The median number of bathrooms is 3.
