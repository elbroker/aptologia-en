How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom in Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom for sale in Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 139,900, based on 87 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 101,450 to USD 170,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,628/m² and the median listed size is 84 m². That is 48% below the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 269,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
