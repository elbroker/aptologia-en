What does the study “Rental of apartments in Guadalajara by number of rooms: how much each size costs” show?

In Guadalajara, a department of 1 rechamber is rented by 19,900 Mexican pesos (USD 1,078) per month in the medium, one of 2 Mexican 26,000 rechambers (USD 1,408) (+ 30.7%) and one of 3 or more 36,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,950) (+ 38.5%).
