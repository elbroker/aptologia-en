How much does it cost to rent in Montevideo?

An apartment of 2 rooms, USD 826 per month in the middle.

The most expensive square meter of apartment in Uruguay is in Punta del Este, with USD 3,604 in the medium of ads for sale; the cheapest of the 3 measured cities, in Maldonado, with USD 2,497. In Montevideo, an apartment of 2 rooms is rented for USD 826 per month and sold for USD 203,500: 20.5 years of rent.
