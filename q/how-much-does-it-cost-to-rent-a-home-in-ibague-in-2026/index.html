How much does it cost to rent a home in Ibagué in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Ibagué is COP 999,000 per month, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 816,000 to COP 1,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 18,446/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 211,700. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 54.4% of listings include parking.
