How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house with a balcony with 3+ bathrooms in Laureles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses with a balcony with 3+ bathrooms in Laureles, Medellín is COP 8,000,000 per month, based on 155 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,800,000 to COP 12,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 31,746/m² and the median listed size is 250 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 8,500,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 88.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
