How much does it cost to buy a 4+ bedroom house in the Bosa locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in the Bosa locality, Bogotá is COP 500,000,000, based on 260 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 398,750,000 to COP 650,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 2,419,406/m² and the median listed size is 216 m². That is 4% above the study's reference median (COP 480,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 92,650. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 66.9% of listings include parking.
