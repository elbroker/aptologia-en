How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Buceo, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Buceo, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 177,431, based on 222 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 152,493 to USD 224,266. The median price per square metre is USD 3,044/m² and the median listed size is 61.5 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 185,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
