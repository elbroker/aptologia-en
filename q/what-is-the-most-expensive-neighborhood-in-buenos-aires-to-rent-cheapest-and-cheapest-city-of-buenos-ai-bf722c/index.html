What is the most expensive neighborhood in Buenos Aires to rent?

Núñez, with 22,967.0 Argentine pesos (USD 15.2) per m² per month in the median (October of 2026).

In Buenos Aires City, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to rent apartment is Núñez (22,967.0 Argentine pesos (USD 15.2) / m² per month) and the cheapest Villa Lugano (14,124.1 Argentine pesos (USD 9.3) / m² per month), 1.6 times less. We compare 35 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
