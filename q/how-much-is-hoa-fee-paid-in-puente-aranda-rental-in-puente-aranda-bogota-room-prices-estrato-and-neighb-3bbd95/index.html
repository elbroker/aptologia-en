How much is HOA fee paid in Puente Aranda?

The median is $217,800 per month, about 11.4% of the rent.

The average rental of an apartment or studio apartment in Puente Aranda (Bogotá) is $1,900,000 per month, about $42,472 per m². A 2 rooms costs in the median $1,800,000. The cheapest neighborhood with 5 ads or more is Santa Rita ($1,450,000) and the most expensive Industrial Center ($3,940,000).
