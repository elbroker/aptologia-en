How much does the rent of an apartment that mentions TransMilenio (near a station or trumcal) in Bogotá change?

- 4.4% per m² in front of comparable apartments of the same estrato and rooms, cut to 13 October of 2026.

In Bogotá, an apartment for rent that mentions TransMilenio (near a station or trump) costs 4.4% less per square meter than a comparable one of the same estratos and number of rooms. Thus 633 of 18,920 ads are announced (3.3%); the medium rental is $1,900,000 with the attribute and $2,500,000 without it.
