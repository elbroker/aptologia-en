How much does it cost to rent a 3-bedroom apartment in El Nogal, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3-bedroom apartments in El Nogal, Bogotá is COP 12,750,000 per month, based on 84 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 8,500,000 to COP 17,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 58,824/m² and the median listed size is 220 m². That is 96% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 6,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,000,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
