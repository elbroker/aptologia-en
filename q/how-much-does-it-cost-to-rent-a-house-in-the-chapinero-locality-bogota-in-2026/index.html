How much does it cost to rent a house in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá is COP 16,000,000 per month, based on 277 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 12,000,000 to COP 25,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 41,667/m² and the median listed size is 418 m². That is 118% above the study's reference median (COP 7,327,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,500,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 69.7% of listings include parking.
