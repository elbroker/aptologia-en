How much is the m² worth in estrato 6 in Chia?

9.93 million pesos for apartments.

In Chia, the m² of an apartment for sale goes from 5.00 million pesos in estrato 2 to 9.93 million pesos in estrato 6. In estrato 6, the m² of a house is 31.5% cheaper than that of an apartment.
