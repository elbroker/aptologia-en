How much is the square meter worth in Bogotá?

7,261,905 pesos in the medium of apartments for sale.

The most expensive square meter of apartment in Colombia is requested in El Retiro: 9.32 million pesos in the medium; the cheapest of the sample, in Soacha, with 3.39 million pesos. Bogotá holds the post 7 of 38 with 7.26 million pesos per m².
