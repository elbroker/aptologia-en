How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 1 in the Usme locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 1 for sale in the Usme locality, Bogotá is COP 152,500,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 128,250,000 to COP 217,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 2,785,788/m² and the median listed size is 51 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median (COP 200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 54,863. 16.7% of listings include parking.
