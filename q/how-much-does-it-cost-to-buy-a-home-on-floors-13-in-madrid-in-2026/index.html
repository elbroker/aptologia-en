How much does it cost to buy a home on floors 1–3 in Madrid in 2026?

The median asking price for homes on floors 1–3 for sale in Madrid is COP 210,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 190,000,000 to COP 223,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,706,897/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median (COP 205,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 150,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 9.7% of listings include parking.
