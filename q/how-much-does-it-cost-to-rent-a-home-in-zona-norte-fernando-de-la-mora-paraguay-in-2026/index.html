How much does it cost to rent a home in Zona Norte, Fernando de la Mora, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Zona Norte, Fernando de la Mora, Paraguay is PYG 4,500,000 per month, based on 51 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 3,450,000 to PYG 5,500,001.
