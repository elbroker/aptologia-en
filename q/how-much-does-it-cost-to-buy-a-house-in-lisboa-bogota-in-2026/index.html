How much does it cost to buy a house in Lisboa, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Lisboa, Bogotá is COP 1,025,000,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 900,000,000 to COP 1,750,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,931,650/m² and the median listed size is 249 m². That is 15% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 250,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 79.4% of listings include parking.
