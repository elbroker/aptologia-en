How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with parking with 2 bathrooms in Mesa, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms for sale in Mesa, Envigado is COP 651,377,722, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 500,000,000 to COP 790,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,291,667/m² and the median listed size is 94 m². That is 21% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 820,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 455,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
