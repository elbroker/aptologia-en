And the cheapest?

Central station, with 8,088.2 Chilean pesos (USD 8.2) per m² per month.

In Santiago, the most expensive m² for rent is Las Condes (16,333.3 Chilean pesos (USD 16.6) / m² per month) and the cheapest Central Station (8,088.2 Chilean pesos (USD 8.2) / m² per month), 2.0 times less. We compare 9 communes with at least 30 ads with area.
