How much does it cost to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in Montevideo?

33,200 Uruguayan pesos (USD 826) per month in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between 28,000 Uruguayan pesos (USD 696) and 39,000 Uruguayan pesos (USD 970).

In Montevideo, rent a 1 bedroom apartment costs in the median 27,900 Uruguayan pesos (USD 694) per month and one 2 bedroom, 33,200 Uruguayan pesos (USD 826) (+ 19.0%). One of 3 or more bedrooms is requested in 40,000 Uruguayan pesos (USD 995).
