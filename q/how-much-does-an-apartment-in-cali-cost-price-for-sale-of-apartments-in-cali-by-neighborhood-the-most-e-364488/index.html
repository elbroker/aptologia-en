How much does an apartment in Cali cost?

In the typical neighborhood (median between 114 neighborhoods with 30 ads or more) the price ordered is $358 million; a 2 bedroom, $280 million, with data from 5 October of 2026.

In Cali, the typical neighborhood asks for $358 million for an apartment for sale (medium between 114 neighborhoods with 30 ads or more); a 2 bedroom, $280 million. The most expensive neighborhood is Normandia ($1.49 billion) and the cheapest Ub Quintas de Bolívar ($172 million).
