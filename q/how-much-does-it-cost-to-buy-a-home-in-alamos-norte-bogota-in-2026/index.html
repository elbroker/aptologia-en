How much does it cost to buy a home in Alamos Norte, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Alamos Norte, Bogotá is COP 460,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 317,500,000 to COP 665,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,000,000/m² and the median listed size is 107 m². That is 47% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 175,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 77.4% of listings include parking.
