How much does it cost to rent an apartment of what size in Santa Cruz de la Sierra?

Less than 50 m²: USD 400 per month; 50 - 70 m²: USD 430 per month; 70 - 90 m²: USD 600 per month; 90 - 120 m²: USD 646 per month; 120 - 160 m²: USD 760 per month; More than 160 m²: USD 1,000 per month (mid-October of 2026).

In Santa Cruz de la Sierra, a rental department of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in USD 400 per month in the median and one of the group "More 160 m²" in USD 1,000 per month (+ 150.0%). per square metre, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (USD 5.1 / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (USD 9.5 / m² per month).
