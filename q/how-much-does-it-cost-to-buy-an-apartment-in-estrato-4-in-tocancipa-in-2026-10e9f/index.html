How much does it cost to buy an apartment in estrato 4 in Tocancipá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments in estrato 4 for sale in Tocancipá is COP 220,000,000, based on 91 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 196,500,000 to COP 250,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,770,492/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Tocancipá (COP 215,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 167,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
