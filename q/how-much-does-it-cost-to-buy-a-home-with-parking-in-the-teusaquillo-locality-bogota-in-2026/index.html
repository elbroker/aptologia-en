How much does it cost to buy a home with parking in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes with parking for sale in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá is COP 990,000,000, based on 1,796 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 680,000,000 to COP 1,480,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,576,353/m² and the median listed size is 142 m². That is 11% above the study's reference median (COP 892,550,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 500,000. The most frequent estrato is 4.
