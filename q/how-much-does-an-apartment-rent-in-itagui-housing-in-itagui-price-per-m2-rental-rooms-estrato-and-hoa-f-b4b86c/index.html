How much does an apartment rent in Itagüí?

The gross return is 7.1% per year (rent per m² × 12), before expenditure.

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter of Itagüí is Simón Bolívar, with 8.52 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is San Gabriel, with 5.00 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 2.50 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 465 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.1% per year.
