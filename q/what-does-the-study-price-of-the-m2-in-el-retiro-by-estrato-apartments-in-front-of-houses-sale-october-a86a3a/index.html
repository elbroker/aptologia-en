What does the study “Price of the m² in El Retiro by estrato: apartments in front of houses (sale, October 2026)” show?

In El Retiro, the m² of an apartment for sale goes from 6.50 million pesos in estrato 3 to 10.15 million pesos in estrato 6. In estrato 5, the m² of a house is 17.5% cheaper than that of an apartment.
