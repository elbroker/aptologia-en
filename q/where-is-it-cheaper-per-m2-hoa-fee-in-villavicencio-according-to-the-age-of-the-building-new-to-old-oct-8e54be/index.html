Where is it cheaper per m²?

In "1 to 5 years": 3,694 pesos per m² per month.

In Villavicencio, the highest square meter HOA fee is paid in the group buildings "11 to 20 years" (3,986 pesos per m² per month in the median) and the lowest in "1 to 5 years" (3,694 pesos). Average monthly rate per strip: 1 to 5 years: 235,000 pesos; 6 to 10 years: 247,500 pesos; 11 to 20 years: 257,000 pesos.
