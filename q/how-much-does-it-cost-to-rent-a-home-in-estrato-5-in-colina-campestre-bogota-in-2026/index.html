How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 5 in Colina Campestre, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 5 in Colina Campestre, Bogotá is COP 4,200,000 per month, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,900,000 to COP 5,250,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 39,474/m² and the median listed size is 102 m². That is 50% above the study's reference median (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 561,500. 96.6% of listings include parking.
