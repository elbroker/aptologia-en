How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 45–70 m² in Punta Carretas, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in Punta Carretas, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 1,348 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,280 to USD 1,400. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 25.2/m² and the median listed size is 51 m². That is 32% below the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 1,990). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
