What does the study “Houses for sale in El Retiro by number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In El Retiro, a house for sale from the group "2 bathrooms" is requested in 1,625 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 2,800 million pesos (+ 72.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "4 or more bathrooms" (7.75 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "2 bathrooms" (8.07 million pesos / m²).
