What city has the most offer of estrato 5 and 6?

Santa Marta: 79.9% of the ads for sale.

In Santa Marta the 79.9% of the apartments for sale are of estrato 5 or 6, the largest proportion of 30 cities; in Madrid it is only 0.2%. In the whole country, the estrato 4 brings together 27.8% of the offer for sale and the estrato 3 16.3%; in Bogotá the most offered estrato is 6.
