What percentage of apartments for rent in Barrios Unidas has a parking lot?

27.2% of the ads reporting the data include at least one parking lot.

The average rental of an apartment or studio apartment in Barrios Unidos (Bogotá) is $2,000,000 per month, about $60,000 per m². A 2 rooms costs in the median $2,375,000. The cheapest neighborhood with 5 ads or more is August Seven ($1,500,000) and the most expensive El Virrey ($15,000,000).
