How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 3 in Villas de Granada, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 3 for sale in Villas de Granada, Bogotá is COP 370,000,000, based on 83 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 320,000,000 to COP 422,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,115,253/m² and the median listed size is 94 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 219,000. 61.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
