What does the study “Profitability of the rent in Buenos Aires City by neighborhood: where you pay the most to rent” show?

In Buenos Aires City, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical neighborhood 6.0% gross per year: the price ordered is equivalent to 16.7 years of rent. The area with the highest gross profitability is Villa Lugano (9.2%) and the smaller district, Núñez (4.1%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 5.8%.
