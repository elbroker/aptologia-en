Where is it cheaper to buy apartment in La Estrella?

Among the sectors with 30 ads or more, in La Tablaza: 5.38 million pesos per m².

The sector with the most expensive square meter of La Estrella is Toledo, with 7.41 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is La Tablaza, with 5.38 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 2.40 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 400 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.1% per year.
