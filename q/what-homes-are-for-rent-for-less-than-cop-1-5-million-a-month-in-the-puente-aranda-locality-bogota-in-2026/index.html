What homes are for rent for less than COP 1.5 million a month in the Puente Aranda locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for rent for less than COP 1.5 million a month in the Puente Aranda locality, Bogotá have a median asking rent of COP 1,300,000 per month, based on 81 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,100,000 to COP 1,400,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 28,889/m² and the median listed size is 40 m². That is 28% below the study's reference median (COP 1,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 190,400. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 8.6% of listings include parking.
