How much does it cost to rent an apartment with how many parking lots in São Paulo?

Without vague: 2,100 actual (USD 413) per month; 1 vague: 3,500 actual (USD 688) per month; 2 or more vague: 9,380 actual (USD 1,843) per month (medium October of 2026).

In São Paulo, an apartment for rent from the group "Sin vaga" is requested in 2,100 reales (USD 413) per month in the median and one from the group "2 or more" in 9,380 reales (USD 1,843) per month (+ 346.7%). per square metre, the cheapest is "No vaga" (54.8 real (USD 10.8) / m² per month) and the most expensive "2 or more vaguely" (72.1 real (USD 14.2) / m² per month).
