How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² with parking in Renacimiento, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with parking for sale in Renacimiento, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 361,250, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 295,950 to USD 381,250. The median price per square metre is USD 1,819/m² and the median listed size is 203 m². That is 51% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
