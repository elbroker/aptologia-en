How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in Paraíso, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in Paraíso, Barranquilla is COP 1,842,900 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,600,000 to COP 2,600,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 32,143/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 20% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 277,812. The most frequent estrato is 4, 91.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
