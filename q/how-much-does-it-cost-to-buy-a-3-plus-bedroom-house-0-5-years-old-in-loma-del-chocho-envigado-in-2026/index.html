How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house 0–5 years old in Loma del Chocho, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses 0–5 years old for sale in Loma del Chocho, Envigado is COP 2,305,000,000, based on 174 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,500,000,000 to COP 3,700,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,420,309/m² and the median listed size is 283 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 2,300,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 955,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
