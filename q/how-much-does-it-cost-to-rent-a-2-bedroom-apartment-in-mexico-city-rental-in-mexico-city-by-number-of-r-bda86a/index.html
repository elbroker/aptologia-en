How much does it cost to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in Mexico City?

30,438 Mexican pesos (USD 1,648) per month in the medium (October 2026); the central half of the offer is between 22,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,191) and 45,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,437).

In Mexico City, renting a department of 1 rechamber costs in the median 25,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,354) per month and one of 2 rechambers, 30,438 Mexican pesos (USD 1,648) (+ 21.8%). One of 3 or more rectums is requested in 55,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,979).
