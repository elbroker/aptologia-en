How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² with parking in Miraflores, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with parking for sale in Miraflores, Lima, Peru is USD 254,053, based on 78 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 223,739 to USD 280,275. The median price per square metre is USD 2,906/m² and the median listed size is 85.5 m². That is 2% below the study's reference median for Lima (USD 258,023). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
