How much does it cost to rent an apartment in a gated complex with 3+ bathrooms in Llano Grande, Rionegro in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in a gated complex with 3+ bathrooms in Llano Grande, Rionegro is COP 7,400,000 per month, based on 49 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,200,000 to COP 9,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 61,151/m² and the median listed size is 115 m². That is 179% above the study's reference median for Rionegro (COP 2,650,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
