How many apartments of less than 350 million are there in Itagüí?

16.1% of the offer for sale (October of 2026); see the median area of each range in the table.

In Itagüí, the price range with the most offer of apartments for sale is 350 - 500 M of pesos (43.2% of the ads), with a median area of 65 m². 16.1% of the supply costs less than 350 million.
