How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 3 or more rooms in Marques Forest?

2,950 million pesos in the medium (October of 2026); the central half between 2,318 million pesos and 3,340 million pesos.

In Bogotá, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs 2,950 million pesos in the medium in the Marques Forest, the most expensive neighborhood, and 230 million pesos in El Tintal, the cheapest: 12.8 times less. We compare 119 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
