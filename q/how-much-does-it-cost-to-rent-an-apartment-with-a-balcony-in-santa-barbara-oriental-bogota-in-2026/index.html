How much does it cost to rent an apartment with a balcony in Santa Bárbara Oriental, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony in Santa Bárbara Oriental, Bogotá is COP 4,540,000 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,450,000 to COP 6,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 61,041/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 38% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 817,500. The most frequent estrato is 5, 91.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
