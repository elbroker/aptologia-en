How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 300,938, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 268,140 to USD 393,750. The median price per square metre is USD 3,587/m² and the median listed size is 86 m². That is 16% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
