How much does it cost to buy a 3-bedroom apartment in El Batán, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3-bedroom apartments for sale in El Batán, Bogotá is COP 750,000,000, based on 88 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 657,500,000 to COP 918,638,286. The median price per square metre is COP 6,215,992/m² and the median listed size is 114 m². That is 16% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 890,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 755,370. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 98.9% of listings include parking.
