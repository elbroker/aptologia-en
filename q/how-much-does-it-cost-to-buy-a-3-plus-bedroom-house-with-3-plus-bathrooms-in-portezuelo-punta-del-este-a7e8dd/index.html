How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with 3+ bathrooms in Portezuelo, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Portezuelo, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 650,000, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 397,500 to USD 1,495,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,415/m² and the median listed size is 312 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
