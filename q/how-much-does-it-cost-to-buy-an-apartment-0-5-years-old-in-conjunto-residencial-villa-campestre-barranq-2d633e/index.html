How much does it cost to buy an apartment 0–5 years old in Conjunto Residencial Villa Campestre, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments 0–5 years old for sale in Conjunto Residencial Villa Campestre, Barranquilla is COP 520,000,000, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 445,000,000 to COP 681,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,157,895/m² and the median listed size is 100 m². That is 16% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 525,900. The most frequent estrato is 4, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
