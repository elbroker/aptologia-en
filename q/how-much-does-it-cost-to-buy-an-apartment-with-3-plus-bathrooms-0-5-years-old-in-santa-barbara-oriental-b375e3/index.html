How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 3+ bathrooms 0–5 years old in Santa Bárbara Oriental, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms 0–5 years old for sale in Santa Bárbara Oriental, Bogotá is COP 1,360,000,000, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 860,000,000 to COP 1,673,008,688. The median price per square metre is COP 10,121,228/m² and the median listed size is 119 m². That is 81% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,130,500. The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
