How much does it cost to buy a home on floors 1–3 in the Antonio Nariño locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes on floors 1–3 for sale in the Antonio Nariño locality, Bogotá is COP 472,500,000, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 297,500,000 to COP 872,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,937,500/m² and the median listed size is 89 m². That is 31% below the study's reference median (COP 680,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 150,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 58.6% of listings include parking.
