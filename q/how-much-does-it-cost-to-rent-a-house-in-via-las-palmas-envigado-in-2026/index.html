How much does it cost to rent a house in Vía las Palmas, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Vía las Palmas, Envigado is COP 15,800,000 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 12,625,000 to COP 20,150,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 46,250/m² and the median listed size is 320 m². That is 34% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 11,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
