How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with parking 0–5 years old in Pan de Azúcar, Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking 0–5 years old for sale in Pan de Azúcar, Sabaneta is COP 619,500,000, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 547,500,000 to COP 676,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,779,487/m² and the median listed size is 76.5 m². That is 13% above the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 550,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 419,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
