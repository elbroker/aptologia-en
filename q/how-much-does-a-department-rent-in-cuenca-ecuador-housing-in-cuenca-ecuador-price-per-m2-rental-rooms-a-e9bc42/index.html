How much does a department rent in Cuenca (Ecuador)?

The gross return is 5.1% per year (rent per m² × 12), before expenditure.

A 2 room department in Cuenca (Ecuador) is ordered in USD 127,000 for sale and is rented for USD 600 per month in the medium. The gross implicit return on rent is 5.1% per year.
