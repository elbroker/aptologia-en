How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in estrato 3 in Alameda del Río, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in estrato 3 in Alameda del Río, Barranquilla is COP 1,350,000 per month, based on 67 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,200,000 to COP 1,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 23,077/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 52% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 217,000. 68.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
