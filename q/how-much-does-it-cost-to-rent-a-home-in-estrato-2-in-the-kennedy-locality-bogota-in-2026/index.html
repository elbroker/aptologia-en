How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 2 in the Kennedy locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 2 in the Kennedy locality, Bogotá is COP 1,100,000 per month, based on 72 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 917,950 to COP 1,300,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 22,581/m² and the median listed size is 48 m². That is 27% below the study's reference median (COP 1,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 105,400. 8.3% of listings include parking.
