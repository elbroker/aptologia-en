How much does it cost to buy a department of what size in Buenos Aires City?

Less than 50 m²: USD 95,700; 50 - 70 m²: USD 140,000; 70 - 90 m²: USD 180,000; 90 - 120 m²: USD 260,000; 120 - 160 m²: USD 395,000; More than 160 m²: USD 680,000 (mid-October of 2026).

In Buenos Aires City, a department for sale of the group "Less than 50 m²" is ordered in USD 95,700 in the median and one of the group "More than 160 m²" in USD 680,000 (+ 610.6%). For square meter, the cheapest is "70 - 90 m²" (USD 2,305 / m²) and the most expensive "More than 160 m²" (USD 3,136 / m²).
