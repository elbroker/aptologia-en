What homes are for sale for less than COP 250 million in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for less than COP 250 million in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 215,000,000, based on 69 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 199,000,000 to COP 230,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,312,500/m² and the median listed size is 25.4 m². That is 76% below the study's reference median (COP 892,550,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 220,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 17.4% of listings include parking.
