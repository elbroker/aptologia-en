How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 2 bathrooms 0–5 years old in estrato 3 in Puerto Espejo, Armenia in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms 0–5 years old in estrato 3 for sale in Puerto Espejo, Armenia is COP 260,000,000, based on 67 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 222,500,000 to COP 299,950,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,103,448/m² and the median listed size is 65 m². That is 32% below the study's reference median for Armenia (COP 380,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 209,100. 98.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
