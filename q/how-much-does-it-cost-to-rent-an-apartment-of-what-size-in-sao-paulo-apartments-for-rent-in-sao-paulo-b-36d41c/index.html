How much does it cost to rent an apartment of what size in São Paulo?

Less than 50 m²: 2,380 real (USD 468) per month; 50 - 70 m²: 3,195 real (USD 628) per month; 70 - 90 m²: 5,000 real (USD 983) per month; 90 - 120 m²: 7,000 real (USD 1,376) per month; 120 - 160 m²: 10,000 real (USD 1,965 per month; More than 160 m²: 13,600 real (USD 2,673) per month (medium October of 2026).

In São Paulo, an apartment for rent from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 2,380 reales (USD 468) per month in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 13,600 reales (USD 2,673) per month (+ 471.4%). The cheapest per square metre is "50 - 70 m²" (54.5 real (USD 10.7) / m² per month) and the most expensive "120 - 160 m²" (73.4 real (USD 14.4) / m² per month).
