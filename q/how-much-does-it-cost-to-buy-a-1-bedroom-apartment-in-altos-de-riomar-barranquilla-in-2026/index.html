How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in Altos de Riomar, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in Altos de Riomar, Barranquilla is COP 260,000,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 14% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 302,500,000).
