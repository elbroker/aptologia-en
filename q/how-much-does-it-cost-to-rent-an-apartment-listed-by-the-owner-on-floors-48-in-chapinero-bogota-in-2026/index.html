How much does it cost to rent an apartment listed by the owner on floors 4–8 in Chapinero, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments listed by the owner on floors 4–8 in Chapinero, Bogotá is COP 2,250,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,800,000 to COP 3,100,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,370/m² and the median listed size is 44.5 m². That is 32% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
