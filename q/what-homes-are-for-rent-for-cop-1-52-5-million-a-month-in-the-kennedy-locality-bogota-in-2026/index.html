What homes are for rent for COP 1.5–2.5 million a month in the Kennedy locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for rent for COP 1.5–2.5 million a month in the Kennedy locality, Bogotá have a median asking rent of COP 1,700,000 per month, based on 206 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,600,000 to COP 1,900,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 34,000/m² and the median listed size is 50 m². That is 13% above the study's reference median (COP 1,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 250,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 36.9% of listings include parking.
