What does the study “How much it costs to rent a single environment, study or studio in 27 Latin American cities” show?

A mono-environment or studio is rented by USD 277 per month in the median of Rosario (Argentina), the lowest of 27 cities, and by USD 1,005 per month in El Retiro (Colombia), the highest. In Bogotá, USD 597 per month. Among the capitals, from USD 429 in Buenos Aires City to USD 650 in Quito.
