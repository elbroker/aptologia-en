What does the study “Rental in Bosa, Bogotá: prices for rooms, estrato and neighborhood” show?

The average rental of an apartment or studio apartment in Bosa (Bogotá) is $1,000,000 per month, about $22,895 per m². A 2 rooms costs in the median $1,000,000. The cheapest neighborhood with 5 ads or more is Bosa ($900,000) and the most expensive Iceland ($1,300,000).
