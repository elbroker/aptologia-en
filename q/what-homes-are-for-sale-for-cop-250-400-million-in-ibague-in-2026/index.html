What homes are for sale for COP 250–400 million in Ibagué in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for COP 250–400 million in Ibagué have a median asking price of COP 320,000,000, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 270,000,000 to COP 360,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,164,557/m² and the median listed size is 92 m². That is 14% above the study's reference median (COP 280,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 336,200. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 60% of listings include parking.
