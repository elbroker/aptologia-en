How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking 6–20 years old in estrato 6 in Bello Horizonte, Santa Marta in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking 6–20 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Bello Horizonte, Santa Marta is COP 1,225,000,000, based on 72 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 800,000,000 to COP 1,700,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,531,915/m² and the median listed size is 108 m². That is 104% above the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 875,000. The median number of bathrooms is 3.
