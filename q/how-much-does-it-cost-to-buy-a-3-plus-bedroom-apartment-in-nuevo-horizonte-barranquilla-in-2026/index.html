How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Nuevo Horizonte, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Nuevo Horizonte, Barranquilla is COP 435,000,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 373,750,000 to COP 472,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,058,824/m² and the median listed size is 104 m². That is 15% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 510,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 484,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
