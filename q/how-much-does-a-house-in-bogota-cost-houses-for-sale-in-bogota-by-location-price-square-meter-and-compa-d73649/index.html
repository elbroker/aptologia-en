How much does a house in Bogotá cost?

The median price requested is $1.19 billion, with a median area of 262 m², data from 18 October of 2026.

A house for sale in Bogotá asks in the medium $1.19 billion, with 262 m²; its square meter ($4,782,609) is 35.0% cheaper than that of an apartment ($7,360,406). By location, the median house goes from $340 million in Usme to $2.70 billion in Chapinero.
