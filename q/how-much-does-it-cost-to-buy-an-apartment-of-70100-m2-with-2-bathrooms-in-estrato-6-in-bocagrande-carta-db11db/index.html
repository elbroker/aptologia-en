How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms in estrato 6 in Bocagrande, Cartagena in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms in estrato 6 for sale in Bocagrande, Cartagena is COP 1,100,000,000, based on 89 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 895,000,000 to COP 1,349,900,000. The median price per square metre is COP 12,547,468/m² and the median listed size is 90 m². That is 38% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 800,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 900,000. 96.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
