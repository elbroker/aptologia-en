How much does it cost to buy a 4+ bedroom house in La Calera in 2026?

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in La Calera is COP 2,725,000,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,125,000,000 to COP 3,225,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,809,091/m² and the median listed size is 485 m². That is 14% above the study's reference median (COP 2,400,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,808,450. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
