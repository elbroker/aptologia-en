How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 1 bathroom 0–5 years old in estrato 6 in Bocagrande, Cartagena in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 1 bathroom 0–5 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Bocagrande, Cartagena is COP 955,000,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 762,500,000 to COP 1,192,500,000. The median listed size is 68 m². That is 19% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 800,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 767,000. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
