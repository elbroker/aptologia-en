How much is a 2 bedroom apartment for rent in Cartagena?

The median is $3,300,000 per month (1,571 ads).

The average rental of an apartment in Cartagena is $3,600,000 per month; an apartment of 2 rooms requests in the median $3,300,000. Among the neighborhoods with 30 ads or more, the cheapest is Pie de la Popa ($2,500,000) and the most expensive Castillogrande ($7,500,000): 3.0 times more.
