How much does it cost to buy a house in the Suba locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 1,600,000,000, based on 5,434 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,100,000,000 to COP 2,250,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,000,000/m² and the median listed size is 310 m². That is 39% above the study's reference median (COP 1,150,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 900,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 92.7% of listings include parking.
