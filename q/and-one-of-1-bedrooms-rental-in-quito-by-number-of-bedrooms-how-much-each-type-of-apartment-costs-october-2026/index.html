And one of 1 bedrooms?

USD 550 per month in the median.

In Quito, rent a 1 bedroom apartment costs the median USD 550 per month and one 2 bedroom, USD 900 (+ 63.6%). One of 3 or more bedrooms is requested in USD 950.
