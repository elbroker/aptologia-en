How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in Miraflores, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Miraflores, Lima, Peru is USD 466,250, based on 74 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 388,750 to USD 554,125. The median price per square metre is USD 2,598/m² and the median listed size is 177 m². That is 81% above the study's reference median for Lima (USD 258,023). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
