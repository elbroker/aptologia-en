How much does it cost to rent a home in Villa Aurelia, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Villa Aurelia, Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 6,500,000 per month, based on 112 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 5,000,000 to PYG 9,512,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is PYG 45,227/m² and the median listed size is 190 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
