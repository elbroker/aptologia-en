What does the study “Price of the apartments for sale in Floridablanca by estrato: m², size and rooms” show?

In Floridablanca the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 4.17 million pesos in estrato 3 and 7.22 million pesos in estrato 6, 1.7 times more. The average total price ranges from 275 million pesos to 748 million pesos according to the estrato.
