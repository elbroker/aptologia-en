How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 2 bathrooms on floors 1–3 in El Ingenio, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms on floors 1–3 for sale in El Ingenio, Cali is COP 412,500,000, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 333,750,000 to COP 502,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,413,492/m² and the median listed size is 88 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Cali (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 520,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 97.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
