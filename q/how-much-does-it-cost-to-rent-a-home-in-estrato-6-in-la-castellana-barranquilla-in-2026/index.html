How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 6 in La Castellana, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 6 in La Castellana, Barranquilla is COP 5,246,000 per month, based on 55 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,800,000 to COP 6,446,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 44,801/m² and the median listed size is 94 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 751,000. 96.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
