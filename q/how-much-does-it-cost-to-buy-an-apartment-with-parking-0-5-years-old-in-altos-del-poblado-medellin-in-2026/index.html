How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking 0–5 years old in Altos del Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking 0–5 years old for sale in Altos del Poblado, Medellín is COP 1,250,000,000, based on 155 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 917,500,000 to COP 1,600,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 11,309,524/m² and the median listed size is 108 m². That is 49% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 700,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
