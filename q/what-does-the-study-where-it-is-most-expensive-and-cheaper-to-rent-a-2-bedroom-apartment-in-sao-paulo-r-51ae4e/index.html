What does the study “Where it is most expensive and cheaper to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in São Paulo: ranking of dances” show?

In São Paulo, an apartment of 2 bedrooms is rented for 17,860 real (USD 3,510) per month in the median in Vila Nova Conceição, the most expensive, and 1,533 real (USD 301) per month in Itaquera, the cheapest: 11.7 times less. We compare 35 bairros with at least 30 ads from 2 bedrooms.
