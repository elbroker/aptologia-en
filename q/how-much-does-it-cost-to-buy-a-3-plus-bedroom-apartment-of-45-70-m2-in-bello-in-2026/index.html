How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Bello in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Bello is COP 305,000,000, based on 940 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 270,000,000 to COP 360,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,301,369/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 10% below the study's reference median for Bello (COP 340,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 236,000. The most frequent estrato is 3, 68.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
