How much does it cost to buy an apartment of what size in Manizales?

Less than 50 m²: 261 million pesos; 50 - 70 m²: 330 million pesos; 70 - 90 m²: 480 million pesos; 90 - 120 m²: 600 million pesos; 120 - 160 m²: 645 million pesos; More than 160 m²: 780 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Manizales, an apartment for sale from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 261 million pesos in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 780 million pesos (+ 198.9%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (4.06 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (6.18 million pesos / m²).
