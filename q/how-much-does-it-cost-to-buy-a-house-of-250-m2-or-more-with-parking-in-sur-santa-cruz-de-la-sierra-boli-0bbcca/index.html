How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more with parking in Sur, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more with parking for sale in Sur, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 249,000, based on 171 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 155,000 to USD 377,000. The median price per square metre is USD 691/m² and the median listed size is 350 m². That is 56% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 160,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
