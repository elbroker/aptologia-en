How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 3+ bathrooms 6–20 years old in estrato 6 in Chicó Navarra, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms 6–20 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Chicó Navarra, Bogotá is COP 1,350,000,000, based on 157 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,050,000,000 to COP 1,700,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,447,583/m² and the median listed size is 158 m². That is 80% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,350,000. 99.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
