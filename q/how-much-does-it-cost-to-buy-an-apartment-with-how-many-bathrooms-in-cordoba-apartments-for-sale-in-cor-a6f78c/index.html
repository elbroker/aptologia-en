How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many bathrooms in Córdoba?

1 bathroom: USD 86,950; 2 bathrooms: USD 186,000; 3 bathrooms: USD 250,000 (mid-October of 2026).

In Córdoba, a department for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in USD 86,950 in the median and one of the group "3 bathrooms" in USD 250,000 (+ 187.5%). per square metre, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (USD 1,528 / m²) and the most expensive "2 bathrooms" (USD 1,978 / m²).
