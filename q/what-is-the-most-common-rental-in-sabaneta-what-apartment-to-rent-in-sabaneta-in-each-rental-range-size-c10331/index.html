What is the most common rental in Sabaneta?

2.5 - 4 M of pesos per month, with 69.8% of the offer.

In Sabaneta, the most available rental range of apartments for rent is 2.5 - 4 M of pesos per month (69.8% of the ads), with a median area of 75 m². 0.2% of the supply is requested in less than 1.5 million.
