Where is it cheaper to rent a 3 or more bedroom apartment in Mexico City?

In Naples: 32,809 Mexican pesos (USD 1,777) per month in the medium.

In Mexico City, a department of 3 or more rectums is rented by 90,000 Mexican pesos (USD 4,874) per month in the median in Polanco IV Section, the most expensive colony, and 32,809 Mexican pesos (USD 1,777) per month in Naples, the cheapest: 2.7 times less. We compare 9 colonies with at least 30 ads of 3 or more rectangles.
