How much is an apartment 2 for rent in Kennedy?

The median is $1,450,000 per month (167 ads).

The average rental of an apartment or studio apartment in Kennedy (Bogotá) is $1,500,000 per month, about $31,579 per m². A 2 rooms costs in the median $1,450,000. The cheapest neighborhood with 5 ads or more is Ub. Castile ($1,230,000) and the most expensive La Ejor ($2,800,000).
