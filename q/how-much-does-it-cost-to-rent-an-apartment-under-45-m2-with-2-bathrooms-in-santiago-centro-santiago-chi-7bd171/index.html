How much does it cost to rent an apartment under 45 m² with 2 bathrooms in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments under 45 m² with 2 bathrooms in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile is CLP 395,000 per month, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from CLP 359,035 to CLP 457,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is CLP 10,000/m² and the median listed size is 41 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Santiago (CLP 450,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
