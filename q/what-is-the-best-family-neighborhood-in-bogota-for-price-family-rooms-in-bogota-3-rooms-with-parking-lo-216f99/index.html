What is the best family neighborhood in Bogotá for price?

It depends on the estrato; in the table, the first of each estrato give more m² for each million rent.

In Bogotá, a family apartment (3 rooms or more, parking lot and 70 m² or more) costs the median $10,000,000 per month: $2,800,000 in estrato 3 and $3,661,000 in estrato 4. By estrato, the most-to-million-meter neighborhoods are Cedritos (estrato 4, 28.4 m²), La Calleja (estrato 5, 21.9 m²), Santa Barbara Occidental (estrato 6, 23.1 m²).
