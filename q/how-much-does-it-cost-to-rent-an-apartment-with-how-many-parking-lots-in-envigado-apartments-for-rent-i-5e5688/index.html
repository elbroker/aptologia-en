How much does it cost to rent an apartment with how many parking lots in Envigado?

No parking lot: 3,700,000 pesos per month; 1 parking lot: 4,000,000 pesos per month; 2 or more parking lot: 5,200,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Envigado, an apartment for rent from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 3,700,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "2 or more parking lot" in 5,200,000 pesos per month (+ 40.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (42,725 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (50,877 pesos / m² per month).
