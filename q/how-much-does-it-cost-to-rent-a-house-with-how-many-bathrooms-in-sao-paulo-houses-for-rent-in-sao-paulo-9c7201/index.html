How much does it cost to rent a house with how many bathrooms in São Paulo?

1 bath: 1,600 real (USD 314) per month; 2 bath: 3,600 real (USD 707) per month; 3 bath: 5,741 real (USD 1,128) per month; 4 or more bath: 10,000 real (USD 1,965) per month (medium October of 2026.

In São Paulo, a house for rent from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 1,600 reais (USD 314) per month in the median and one from the group "4 or more" in 10,000 reais (USD 1,965) per month (+ 525.0%). per square metre, the cheapest is "2 real bathrooms" (28.5 real (USD 5.6) / m² per month) and the most expensive "4 or more real bathrooms" (32.5 real (USD 6.4) / m² per month).
