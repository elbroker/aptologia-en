How much is HOA fee paid in Medellín?

The median rate is 648,402 pesos per month (5,577 pesos per m²), according to October 2026 announcements.

In Medellín, the most expensive HOA fee per square metre is paid in Lininde: 7,604 pesos per m² per month in the median (826,414 fee pesos). The cheapest, in Simón Bolívar, with 2,009 pesos per m². The median rate for the whole city is 648,402 pesos per month (5,577 pesos per m²).
