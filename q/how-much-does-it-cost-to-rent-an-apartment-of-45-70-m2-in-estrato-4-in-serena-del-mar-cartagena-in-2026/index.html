How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 45–70 m² in estrato 4 in Serena del Mar, Cartagena in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in estrato 4 in Serena del Mar, Cartagena is COP 2,600,000 per month, based on 67 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,275,000 to COP 3,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 44,286/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 30% below the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 3,700,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 362,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
