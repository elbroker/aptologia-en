How much does it cost to rent a home with security or a doorman in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes with security or a doorman in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá is COP 4,000,000 per month, based on 2,523 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,750,000 to COP 8,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 54,286/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². That is 13% below the study's reference median (COP 4,600,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 720,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 86.2% of listings include parking.
