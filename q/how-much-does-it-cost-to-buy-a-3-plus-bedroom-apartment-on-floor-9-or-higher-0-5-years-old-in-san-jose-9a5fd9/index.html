How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment on floor 9 or higher 0–5 years old in San José, Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments on floor 9 or higher 0–5 years old for sale in San José, Sabaneta is COP 620,000,000, based on 52 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 550,000,000 to COP 676,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,196,547/m² and the median listed size is 84 m². That is 13% above the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 550,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 400,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 94.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
