What does the study “Where it costs more and less to buy a apartment of 3 or more bedrooms in Lima: district ranking” show?

In Lima, a department of 3 or more bedrooms costs 1,535,375 soles (USD 446,911) in the middle in San Isidro, the most expensive district, and 331,500 soles (USD 96,492) in Los Olivos, the cheapest: 4.6 times less. We compare 19 districts with at least 30 ads from 3 or more bedrooms.
