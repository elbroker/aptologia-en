How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 3+ bathrooms more than 20 years old in estrato 6 in Alto Prado, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms more than 20 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Alto Prado, Barranquilla is COP 850,000,000, based on 133 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 670,000,000 to COP 1,350,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,627,064/m² and the median listed size is 260 m². That is 89% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,380,000. 97.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
