How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Bochica, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Bochica, Bogotá is COP 242,500,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 210,000,000 to COP 264,862,500. That is 63% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 660,000,000).
