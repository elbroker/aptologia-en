How much does it cost to rent a house in Trinidad, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Trinidad, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 2,614 per month, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,658 to USD 4,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 8.33/m² and the median listed size is 319 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 2,800). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
