How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more in Usaquén, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more for sale in Usaquén, Bogotá is COP 3,350,000,000, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,172,500,000 to COP 5,125,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,662,835/m² and the median listed size is 435 m². That is 179% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 650,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 94.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.5.
