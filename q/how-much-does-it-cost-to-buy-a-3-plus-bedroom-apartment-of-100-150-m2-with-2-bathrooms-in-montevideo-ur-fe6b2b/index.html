How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 369,550, based on 137 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 255,000 to USD 455,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,133/m² and the median listed size is 115 m². That is 100% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 185,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
