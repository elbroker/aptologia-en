How much does it cost to buy a home in Tocancipa in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Tocancipa is COP 213,000,000, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 190,000,000 to COP 234,906,544. The median price per square metre is COP 3,758,065/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 129,846. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 13.6% of listings include parking.
