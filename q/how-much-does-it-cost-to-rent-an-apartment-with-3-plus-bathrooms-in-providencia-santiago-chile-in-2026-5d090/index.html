How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Providencia, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Providencia, Santiago, Chile is UF 39 per month, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 32.8 to UF 45.1. The median monthly rent per square metre is UF 0.32/m² and the median listed size is 133 m². That is 181% above the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 13.8). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
