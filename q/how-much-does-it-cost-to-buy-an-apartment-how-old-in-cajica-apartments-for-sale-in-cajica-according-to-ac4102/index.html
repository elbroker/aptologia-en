How much does it cost to buy an apartment how old in Cajicá?

For starring (0 years): 419 million pesos; 1 to 5 years: 390 million pesos; 6 to 10 years: 410 million pesos; 11 to 20 years: 330 million pesos (medium of October of 2026).

In Cajicá, an apartment for sale from the group "To premiere (0 years)" is requested in 419 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "11 to 20 years" in 330 million pesos (- 21.3%). For each square meter, the cheapest is "11 to 20 years" (4.91 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "To premiere (0 years)" (5.87 million pesos / m²).
