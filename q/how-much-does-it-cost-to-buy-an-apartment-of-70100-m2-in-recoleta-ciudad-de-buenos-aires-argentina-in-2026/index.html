How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² in Recoleta, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Recoleta, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 209,000, based on 67 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 175,000 to USD 275,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,444/m² and the median listed size is 86 m². That is 27% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
