What does the study “Profitability of rent in Cartagena by estrato: which gives more to buy for rent” show?

In Cartagena, buying an apartment to rent it yields more in estrato 4 (9.1% gross per year, 11.0 years of rent) and less in estrato 6 (6.1%).
