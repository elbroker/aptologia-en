How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in Capital, Córdoba, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in Capital, Córdoba, Argentina is ARS 650,000 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from ARS 560,000 to ARS 775,000. That is 4% above the study's reference median for Córdoba (ARS 625,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
