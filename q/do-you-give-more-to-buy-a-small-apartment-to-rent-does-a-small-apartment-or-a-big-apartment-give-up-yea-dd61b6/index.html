Do you give more to buy a small apartment to rent?

In Colombia the section with less years of rent per m² is less than 50 m² (11.7 years).

In Colombia the square meter of an apartment of less than 50 m² is paid with 11.7 years of rent, the section of size with less years; the 120 m² or more needs 12.6. The rent per m² per low month of 45,678 pesos in those less than 50 m² to 45,143 pesos in those of 120 m² or more.
