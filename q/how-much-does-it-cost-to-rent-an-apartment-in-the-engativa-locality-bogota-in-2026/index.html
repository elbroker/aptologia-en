How much does it cost to rent an apartment in the Engativá locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in the Engativá locality, Bogotá is COP 1,700,000 per month, based on 407 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,350,000 to COP 2,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 30,645/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is 2% below the study's reference median (COP 1,734,500). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 227,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 29.5% of listings include parking.
