Where's the most expensive?

In Barranco: 5.08 soles (USD 1.5) per m² per month in the median.

In Lima, the maintenance of a department is in the median 280 soles (USD 81.5) per month (3.52 soles (USD 1.0) per m²). The most expensive district per m² is Barranco (5.08 soles (USD 1.5)) and the cheapest, Santiago de Surco (2.85 soles (USD 0.8)).
