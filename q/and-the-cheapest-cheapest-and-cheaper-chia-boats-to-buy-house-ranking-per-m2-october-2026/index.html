And the cheapest?

Santa Lucia, with 4.03 million pesos per m².

In Chia, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy house is Fontanar (8.85 million pesos / m²) and the cheapest Santa Lucia (4.03 million pesos / m²), 2.2 times less. We compare 23 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
