Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "90 - 120 m²" (4.51 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "120 - 160 m²" (4.94 million pesos / m²).

In Floridablanca, a house for sale from the group "90 - 120 m²" is requested in 500 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 1,400 million pesos (+ 180.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "90 - 120 m²" (4.51 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "120 - 160 m²" (4.94 million pesos / m²).
