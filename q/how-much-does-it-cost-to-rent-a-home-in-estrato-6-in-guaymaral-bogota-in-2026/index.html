How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 6 in Guaymaral, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 6 in Guaymaral, Bogotá is COP 13,070,000 per month, based on 109 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 11,200,000 to COP 15,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 29,006/m² and the median listed size is 496 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median (COP 12,100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,000,000. 96.3% of listings include parking.
