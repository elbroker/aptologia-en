How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 3+ bathrooms in El Refugio, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 3+ bathrooms in El Refugio, Bogotá is COP 12,500,000 per month, based on 73 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 9,500,000 to COP 17,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,000/m² and the median listed size is 238 m². That is 279% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,000,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
