How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 5 in La Riviera, Cúcuta in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 5 for sale in La Riviera, Cúcuta is COP 636,000,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 442,500,000 to COP 1,105,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,844,280/m² and the median listed size is 172 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 450,000. 95.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
