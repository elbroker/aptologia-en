How much does it cost to buy a 4+ bedroom house in Las Ferias, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in Las Ferias, Bogotá is COP 795,000,000, based on 51 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 750,000,000 to COP 985,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,070,175/m² and the median listed size is 276 m². That is 36% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,250,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 10,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 27.5% of listings include parking.
