What is the most expensive neighborhood in Asunción to buy?

Manorá, with USD 1,527 per m² in the median (October of 2026).

In Asunción, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy house is Manorá (USD 1,527 / m²) and the cheapest Saxony (USD 564 / m²), 2.7 times less. We compare 30 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
