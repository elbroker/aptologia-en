How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Cala Cala, Cochabamba, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Cala Cala, Cochabamba, Bolivia is USD 82,241, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 69,524 to USD 98,240. The median price per square metre is USD 989/m² and the median listed size is 88 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Cochabamba (USD 88,250). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 46.5. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
