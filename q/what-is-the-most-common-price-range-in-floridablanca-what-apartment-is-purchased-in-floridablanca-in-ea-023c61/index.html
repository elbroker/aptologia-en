What is the most common price range in Floridablanca?

350 - 500 M of pesos, with 48.2% of the ads.

In Floridablanca, the price range with the most offer of apartments for sale is 350 - 500 M of pesos (48.2% of the ads), with a median area of 83 m². 20.1% of the supply costs less than 350 million.
