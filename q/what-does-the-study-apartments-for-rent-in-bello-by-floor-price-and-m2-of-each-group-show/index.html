What does the study “Apartments for rent in Bello by floor: price and m² of each group” show?

In Bello, an apartment for rent from the "Floor 1 - 2" group is requested in 1,800,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the "Floor 21 group or more" in 2,100,000 pesos per month (+ 16.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 3 - 5" (30,679 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Floor 21 or more" (34,669 pesos / m² per month).
