How much does it cost to buy a home on floor 9 or higher in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes on floor 9 or higher for sale in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá is COP 850,000,000, based on 124 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 410,000,000 to COP 1,250,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,256,410/m² and the median listed size is 97.5 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median (COP 892,550,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 583,100. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 75% of listings include parking.
