How much does it cost to buy a home in Punta Hermosa, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Punta Hermosa, Lima, Peru is USD 342,500, based on 72 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 247,250 to USD 513,750. The median price per square metre is USD 2,089/m² and the median listed size is 164 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
