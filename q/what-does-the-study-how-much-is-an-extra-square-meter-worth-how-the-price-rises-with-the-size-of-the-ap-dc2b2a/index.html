What does the study “How much is an extra square meter worth? How the price rises with the size of the apartment in 33 cities of Colombia” show?

In Colombia, within the same estrato, an apartment for sale 10% larger is requested + 7.6% more expensive. In Bogotá an extra m² is worth about 6.07 million pesos, compared to 7.25 million pesos of the medium m². The effect of size is weaker in Soacha (+ 5.1% for 10% of area) and stronger in Rionegro (+ 10.1%).
