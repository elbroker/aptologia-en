How much does it cost to buy a home in Pueblo Libre, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Pueblo Libre, Lima, Peru is USD 156,716, based on 63 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 123,317 to USD 256,500. The median price per square metre is USD 1,938/m² and the median listed size is 79.5 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
