How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Parque Avellaneda, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Parque Avellaneda, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 88,850, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 72,000 to USD 106,225. The median price per square metre is USD 1,359/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 44% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 160,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
