What is the total cost of renting a 2 room apartment in São Paulo?

3,733 actual (USD 734) per month in the median.

In São Paulo, the condo and the IPTU amount in the median to 25.2% of the rental of an apartment: the medium rental is 3,621 real (USD 712) and the total average cost of 4,753 real (USD 934) per month. The medium condo is 950 real and the monthly IPTU is 259 real. The area where the extra cost is most heavy is Paradise (33.1%) and where the least, Butantã (19.2%).
