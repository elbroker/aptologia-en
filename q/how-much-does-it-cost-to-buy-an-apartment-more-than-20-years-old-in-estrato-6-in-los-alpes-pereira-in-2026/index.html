How much does it cost to buy an apartment more than 20 years old in estrato 6 in Los Alpes, Pereira in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments more than 20 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Los Alpes, Pereira is COP 460,500,000, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 367,500,000 to COP 552,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,186,047/m² and the median listed size is 108 m². That is 6% above the study's reference median for Pereira (COP 435,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 410,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
