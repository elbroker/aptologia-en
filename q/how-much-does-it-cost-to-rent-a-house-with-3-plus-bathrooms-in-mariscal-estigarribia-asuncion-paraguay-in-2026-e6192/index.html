How much does it cost to rent a house with 3+ bathrooms in Mariscal Estigarribia, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses with 3+ bathrooms in Mariscal Estigarribia, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 2,700 per month, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,200 to USD 4,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 6.67/m² and the median listed size is 400 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 2,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
