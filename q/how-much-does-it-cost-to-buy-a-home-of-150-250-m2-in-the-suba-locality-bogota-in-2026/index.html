How much does it cost to buy a home of 150–250 m² in the Suba locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes of 150–250 m² for sale in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 1,200,000,000, based on 1,927 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 950,000,000 to COP 1,430,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,153,846/m² and the median listed size is 196 m². That is 4% above the study's reference median (COP 1,150,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 956,500. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 96% of listings include parking.
