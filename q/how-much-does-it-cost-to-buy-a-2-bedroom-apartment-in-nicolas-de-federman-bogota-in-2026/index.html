How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Nicolás de Federmán, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Nicolás de Federmán, Bogotá is COP 640,000,000, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 29% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 495,000,000).
