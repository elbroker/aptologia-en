What does the study “Where it costs more and less to buy an apartment of 3 or more bedrooms in Lisbon: ranking of freguesias” show?

In Lisbon, an apartment of 3 or more bedrooms costs 1,867,500 euros (USD 2,099,225) in the median in Belém, the most expensive freguesia, and 395,000 euros (USD 444,013) in Olivais, the cheapest: 4.7 times less. We compare 10 freguesias with at least 30 ads from 3 or more bedrooms.
