New buildings pay for more HOA fee in Ibagué?

1 to 5 years: 3,958 pesos / m²; 6 to 10 years: 4,112 pesos / m²; 11 to 20 years: 4,313 pesos / m²; 21 to 30 years: 3,638 pesos / m² (medium per m² per month).

In Ibagué, the highest square meter HOA fee is paid in the group buildings "11 to 20 years" (4,313 pesos per m² per month in the median) and the lowest in "21 to 30 years" (3,638 pesos). Average monthly rate per strip: 1 to 5 years: 303,100 pesos; 6 to 10 years: 307,000 pesos; 11 to 20 years: 385,500 pesos; 21 to 30 years: 385,500 pesos.
