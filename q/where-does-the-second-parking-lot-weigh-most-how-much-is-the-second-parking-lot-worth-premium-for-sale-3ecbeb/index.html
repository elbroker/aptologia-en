Where does the second parking lot weigh most?

In Bucharest: + 50.9% for sale.

In Colombia an apartment for sale with 2 parking lot is ordered + 23.1% against one comparable to 1 (same city, estrato, rooms and area range); in rent the difference is + 15.6%. By city, the premium for sale goes from + 0.7% in Chia to + 50.9% in Bucaramanga.
