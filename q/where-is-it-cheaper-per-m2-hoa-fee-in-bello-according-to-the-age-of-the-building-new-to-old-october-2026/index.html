Where is it cheaper per m²?

In "21 to 30 years": 3,347 pesos per m² per month.

In Bello, the highest square meter HOA fee is paid in buildings of the group "To premiere" (4,590 pesos per m² per month in the median) and the lowest in "21 to 30 years" (3,347 pesos). Average monthly rate per strip: For starters: 274,000 pesos; 1 to 5 years: 252,000 pesos; 6 to 10 years: 255,700 pesos; 11 to 20 years: 261,000 pesos; 21 to 30 years: 217,000 pesos.
