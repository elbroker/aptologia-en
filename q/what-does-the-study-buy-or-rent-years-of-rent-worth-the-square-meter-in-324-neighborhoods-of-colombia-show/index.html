What does the study “Buy or rent? Years of rent worth the square meter in 324 neighborhoods of Colombia” show?

The district of Colombia where the square meter is paid with less years of rent is Quinta Camacho (Bogotá): 8.3 years; in Manzanillo (Cartagena) it takes 22.7 years. In Bogotá, from 8.3 years (Quinta Camacho) to 16.4 (Santa Barbara Alta). In Medellín, from 9.2 years (La Candelaria) to 16.7 (La Castellana). In Barranquilla, from 9.4 years (Barranquilla) to 14.2 (Alameda del Río). In Cali, from 10.4 years (Santa Teresita) to 14.3 (Bochalema). In Cartagena, from 11.0 years (Torices) to 22.7 (Manzanillo).
