How much does it cost to buy a home in Los Andes, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Los Andes, Bogotá is COP 890,000,000, based on 125 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 650,000,000 to COP 1,100,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,309,311/m² and the median listed size is 188 m². That is 3% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 457,740. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 89.6% of listings include parking.
