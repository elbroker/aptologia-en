What does the study “Where it costs more and less to buy an apartment of 3 or more rooms in Bucharest: neighborhood ranking” show?

In Bucharest, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs 800 million pesos in the medium in Cabecera del Llano, the most expensive neighborhood, and 320 million pesos in Central, the cheapest: 2.5 times less. We compare 17 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
