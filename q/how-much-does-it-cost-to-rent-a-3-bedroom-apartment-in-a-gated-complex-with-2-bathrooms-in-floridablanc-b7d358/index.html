How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in a gated complex with 2 bathrooms in Floridablanca in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in a gated complex with 2 bathrooms in Floridablanca is COP 2,400,000 per month, based on 118 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,012,500 to COP 2,700,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 29,393/m² and the median listed size is 83.6 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 2,400,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 418,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 85.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
