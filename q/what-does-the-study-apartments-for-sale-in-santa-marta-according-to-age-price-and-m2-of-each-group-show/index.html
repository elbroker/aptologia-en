What does the study “Apartments for sale in Santa Marta according to age: price and m² of each group” show?

In Santa Marta, an apartment for sale from the group "To premiere (0 years)" is requested in 580 million pesos in the median and one from the group "21 to 30 years" in 450 million pesos (- 22.4%). For each square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (5.35 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "To premiere (0 years)" (9.44 million pesos / m²).
