What is the most common price range in Cali?

200 - 350 M of pesos, with 33.4% of the ads.

In Cali, the price range with the most offer of apartments for sale is 200 - 350 M of pesos (33.4% of the ads), with a median area of 62 m². 40.0% of the supply costs less than 350 million.
