How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Chapinero Central, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Chapinero Central, Bogotá is COP 2,000,000 per month, based on 81 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,626,000 to COP 2,420,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 52,855/m² and the median listed size is 38.6 m². That is 43% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 350,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 32.1% of listings include parking.
