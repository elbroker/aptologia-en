And one of 1 bedrooms?

370,000 Chilean pesos (USD 377) per month in the median.

In Santiago de Chile, rent a 1 bedroom apartment costs in the median 370,000 Chilean pesos (USD 377) per month and one 2 bedroom, 480,000 Chilean pesos (USD 489) (+ 29.7%). One of 3 or more bedrooms is requested in 612,500 Chilean pesos (USD 623).
