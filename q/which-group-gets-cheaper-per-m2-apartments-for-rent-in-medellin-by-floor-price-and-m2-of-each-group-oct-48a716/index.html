Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (40,000 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Floor 21 or more" (57,692 pesos / m² per month).

In Medellín, an apartment for rent from the "Piso 1 - 2" group is requested in 3,300,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the "Piso 21 group or more" in 5,000,000 pesos per month (+ 51.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (40,000 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Floor 21 or more" (57,692 pesos / m² per month).
