Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "70 - 90 m²" (33,108 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (36,250 pesos / m² per month).

In Bello, an apartment for rent from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 1,600,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "90 - 120 m²" in 3,500,000 pesos per month (+ 118.8%).
