How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with parking 0–5 years old in La Doctora, Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking 0–5 years old for sale in La Doctora, Sabaneta is COP 568,000,000, based on 109 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 500,000,000 to COP 640,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,313,433/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². That is 3% above the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 550,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 391,681. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
