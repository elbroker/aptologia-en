How much does it cost to rent an apartment in El Nogal, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in El Nogal, Bogotá is COP 10,000,000 per month, based on 201 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 6,800,000 to COP 15,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 64,000/m² and the median listed size is 165 m². That is 186% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,800,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 91.5% of listings include parking.
