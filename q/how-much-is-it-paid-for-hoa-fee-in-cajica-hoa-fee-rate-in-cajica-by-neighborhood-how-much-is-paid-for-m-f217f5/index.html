How much is it paid for HOA fee in Cajicá?

The median rate is 330,000 pesos per month (4,689 pesos per m²), according to October 2026 announcements.

In Cajicá, the most expensive HOA fee per square metre is paid in Canelon: 5,405 pesos per m² per month in the median (390,000 fee pesos). The cheapest, in La Estacion, with 4,375 pesos per m². The median rate for the whole city is 330,000 pesos per month (4,689 pesos per m²).
