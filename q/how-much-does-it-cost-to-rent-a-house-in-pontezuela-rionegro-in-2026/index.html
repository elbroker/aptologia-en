How much does it cost to rent a house in Pontezuela, Rionegro in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Pontezuela, Rionegro is COP 7,900,000 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,375,000 to COP 13,625,000. That is 28% below the study's reference median for Rionegro (COP 11,000,000).
