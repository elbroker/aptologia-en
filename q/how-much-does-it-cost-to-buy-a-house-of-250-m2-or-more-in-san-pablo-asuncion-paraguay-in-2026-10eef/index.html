How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more in San Pablo, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more for sale in San Pablo, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 275,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 180,482 to USD 290,000. The median listed size is 360 m². That is 21% below the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 350,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
