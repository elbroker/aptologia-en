How much does it cost to rent a house in San Simón, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in San Simón, Bogotá is COP 13,900,000 per month, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 10,000,000 to COP 16,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 27,879/m² and the median listed size is 420 m². That is 32% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 10,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,555,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
