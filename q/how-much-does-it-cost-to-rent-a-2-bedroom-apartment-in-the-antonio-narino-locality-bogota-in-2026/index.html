How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in the Antonio Nariño locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in the Antonio Nariño locality, Bogotá is COP 1,425,000 per month, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,300,000 to COP 1,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 27,292/m² and the median listed size is 51.5 m². That is 2% below the study's reference median (COP 1,450,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 212,900. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 8.7% of listings include parking.
