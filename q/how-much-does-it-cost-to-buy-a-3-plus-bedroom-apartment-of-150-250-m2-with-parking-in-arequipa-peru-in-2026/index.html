How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² with parking in Arequipa, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with parking for sale in Arequipa, Peru is PEN 774,900, based on 85 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 627,250 to PEN 891,500. The median price per square metre is PEN 4,200/m² and the median listed size is 179 m². That is 55% above the study's reference median for Arequipa (PEN 501,350). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
