What percentage of apartments for rent in Bogotá is furnished?

13.7% of the ads with description mention it.

In Bogotá, an apartment for rent that is furnished costs on average 22.9% more per square meter than a comparable one of the same estrato and number of rooms. It is mentioned by 13.7% of the ads with description; the average rental of which is $4,100,000 compared to $2,200,000 of which is not.
