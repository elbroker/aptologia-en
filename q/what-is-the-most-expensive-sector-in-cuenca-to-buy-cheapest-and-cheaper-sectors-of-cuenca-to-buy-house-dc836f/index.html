What is the most expensive sector in Cuenca to buy?

Nulti, with USD 1,086 per m² in the median (October of 2026).

In Cuenca, the sector with the most expensive m² to buy home is Nulti (USD 1,086 / m²) and the cheapest San Blas (USD 798 / m²), 1.4 times less. We compare 7 sectors with at least 30 ads with area.
