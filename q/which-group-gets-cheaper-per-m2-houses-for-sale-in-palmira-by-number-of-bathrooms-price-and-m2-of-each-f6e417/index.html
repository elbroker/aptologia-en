Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (2.33 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "3 bathrooms" (2.70 million pesos / m²).

In Palmira, a house for sale from the group "2 bathrooms" is requested in 320 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 950 million pesos (+ 196.9%).
