How much does it cost to rent in estrato 3 in Pereira?

There are 30 ads from 2 rooms in estrato 3.

In Pereira, rent an apartment of 2 rooms costs in the median 1,700,000 pesos per month in estrato 4 and 2,900,000 pesos in estrato 6, 1.7 times more. per square meter, the rent goes from 30,421 pesos to 35,000 pesos per month according to the estrato.
