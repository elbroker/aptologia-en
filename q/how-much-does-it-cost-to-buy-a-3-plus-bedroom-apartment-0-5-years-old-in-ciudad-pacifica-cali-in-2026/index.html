How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment 0–5 years old in Ciudad Pacífica, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments 0–5 years old for sale in Ciudad Pacífica, Cali is COP 295,000,000, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 239,500,000 to COP 420,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,145,889/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². That is 31% below the study's reference median for Cali (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 270,500. The most frequent estrato is 4, 91.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
