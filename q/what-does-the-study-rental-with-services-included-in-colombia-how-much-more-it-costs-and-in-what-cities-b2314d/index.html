What does the study “Rental with services included in Colombia: how much more it costs and in what cities it is offered” show?

Only 1.1% of the apartments for rent in Colombia are announced with public services included; where the most offered is in Cali (9.4% of the ads). In apartment 1 the rental with services included is 15.8% lower than the one that does not include them (2,400,000 pesos against 2,850,000 pesos), because these ads are usually smaller units or in other areas.
