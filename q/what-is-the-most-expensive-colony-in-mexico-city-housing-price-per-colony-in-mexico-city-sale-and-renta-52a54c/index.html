What is the most expensive colony in Mexico City?

Florida, with 75,923 Mexican pesos (USD 4,112) per m² in apartments for sale (October of 2026).

The colony with the most expensive square meter in Mexico City is Florida, with 75,923 Mexican pesos (USD 4,112) in the median of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Villa Coyoacán, with 43,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,329). For rent, the highest medium rental is requested in Polanco III Section (83,000 Mexican pesos (USD 4,495) per month) and the lowest in Doctors (18,750 Mexican pesos (USD 1,015)).
