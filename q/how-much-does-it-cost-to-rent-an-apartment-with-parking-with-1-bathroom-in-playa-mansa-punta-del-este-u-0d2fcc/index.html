How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking with 1 bathroom in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking with 1 bathroom in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 2,850 per month, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,388 to USD 4,850. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 41.5/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 41% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 4,800). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
