How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many bathrooms in Floridablanca?

1 bathroom: 240 million pesos; 2 bathrooms: 425 million pesos; 3 bathrooms: 630 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Floridablanca, an apartment for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 240 million pesos in the medium and one of the group "3 bathrooms" in 630 million pesos (+ 162.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "1 bath" (4.22 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "2 bath" (5.21 million pesos / m²).
