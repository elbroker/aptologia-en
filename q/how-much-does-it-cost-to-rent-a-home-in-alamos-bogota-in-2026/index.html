How much does it cost to rent a home in Alamos, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Alamos, Bogotá is COP 1,616,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,421,250 to COP 1,824,825. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 37,019/m² and the median listed size is 41 m². That is 54% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,497,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 183,500. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 13.3% of listings include parking.
