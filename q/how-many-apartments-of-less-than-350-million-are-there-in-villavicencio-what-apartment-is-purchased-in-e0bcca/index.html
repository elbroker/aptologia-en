How many apartments of less than 350 million are there in Villavicencio?

61.8% of the offer for sale (October of 2026); see the median area of each range in the table.

In Villavicencio, the price range with the most offer of apartments for sale is 200 - 350 M of pesos (35.7% of the ads), with a median area of 60 m². 61.8% of the supply costs less than 350 million.
