And the cheapest?

Carcelén, with USD 642 per m².

In Quito, the sector with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is Cumbayá (USD 1,578 / m²) and the cheapest Carcelén (USD 642 / m²), 2.5 times less. We compare 22 sectors with at least 30 ads with area.
