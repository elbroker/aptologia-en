How much does it cost to buy a house in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 650,000, based on 234 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 495,750 to USD 895,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,400/m² and the median listed size is 475 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
