How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 2 bathrooms 6–20 years old in estrato 5 in Loma de los Bernal, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms 6–20 years old in estrato 5 for sale in Loma de los Bernal, Medellín is COP 657,500,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 502,500,000 to COP 760,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,007,299/m² and the median listed size is 81.5 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 497,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
