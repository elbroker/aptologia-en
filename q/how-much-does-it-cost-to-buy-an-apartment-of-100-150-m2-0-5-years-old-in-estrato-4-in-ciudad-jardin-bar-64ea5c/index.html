How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 100–150 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 4 in Ciudad Jardín, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 4 for sale in Ciudad Jardín, Barranquilla is COP 550,000,000, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 403,750,000 to COP 647,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,456,522/m² and the median listed size is 115 m². That is 22% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 511,000. 95.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
