What is the most common rental in Pereira?

2.5 - 4 M of pesos per month, with 40.4% of the offer.

In Pereira, the most available rental range of apartments for rent is 2.5 - 4 M of pesos per month (40.4% of the ads), with a median area of 87 m². 2.3% of the supply is requested in less than 1.5 million.
