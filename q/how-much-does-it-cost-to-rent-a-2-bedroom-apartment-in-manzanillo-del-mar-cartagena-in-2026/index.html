How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in Manzanillo del Mar, Cartagena in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in Manzanillo del Mar, Cartagena is COP 2,587,500 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,222,500 to COP 3,300,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 41,525/m² and the median listed size is 58.2 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 3,400,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 66.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
