How much does it cost to buy a home 16–30 years old in Los Patios in 2026?

The median asking price for homes 16–30 years old for sale in Los Patios is COP 500,000,000, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 300,000,000 to COP 935,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,132,512/m² and the median listed size is 160 m². That is 32% above the study's reference median (COP 380,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 600,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 79.1% of listings include parking.
