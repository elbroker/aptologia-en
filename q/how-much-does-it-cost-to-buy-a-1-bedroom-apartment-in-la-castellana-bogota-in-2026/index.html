How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in La Castellana, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in La Castellana, Bogotá is COP 320,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 255,250,000 to COP 393,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,805,153/m² and the median listed size is 28.2 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 420,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 323,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 50% of listings include parking.
