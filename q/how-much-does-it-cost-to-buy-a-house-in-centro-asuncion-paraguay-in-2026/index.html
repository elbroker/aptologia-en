How much does it cost to buy a house in Centro, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Centro, Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 850,000,000, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 650,000,000 to PYG 990,000,000. That is 13% below the study's reference median for Asunción (PYG 975,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
