How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 1 bathroom in Cercado de Lima, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 1 bathroom for sale in Cercado de Lima, Lima, Peru is USD 84,920, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 75,354 to USD 91,473. The median price per square metre is USD 2,179/m² and the median listed size is 40.5 m². That is 67% below the study's reference median for Lima (USD 258,023). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
