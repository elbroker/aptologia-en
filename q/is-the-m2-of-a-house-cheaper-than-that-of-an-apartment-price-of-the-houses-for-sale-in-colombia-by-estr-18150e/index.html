Is the m² of a house cheaper than that of an apartment?

It depends on the estrato: see the column "House vs. apartment per m²."

In Colombia a house for sale costs in the medium 2.76 million pesos per m² in estrato 2 and 5.98 million pesos in estrato 6; the total average price is from 450 million pesos to 2,200 million pesos. In estrato 6 the m² of house comes out - 24.9% against that of apartment; in estrato 3, - 30.1%.
