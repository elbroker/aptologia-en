How much does it cost to buy a home in Andes Norte, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Andes Norte, Bogotá is COP 890,000,000, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 659,338,574 to COP 1,300,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,408,602/m² and the median listed size is 141 m². That is 3% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 678,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 96.7% of listings include parking.
