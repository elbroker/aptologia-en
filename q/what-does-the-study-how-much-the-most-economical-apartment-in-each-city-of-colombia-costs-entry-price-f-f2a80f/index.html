What does the study “How much the most economical apartment in each city of Colombia costs: entry price for sale and rent” show?

The 10% cheapest of the apartments for sale starts in 150 million pesos in Soacha, the lowest entry price of 37 cities, and in 485 million pesos in Envigado, the highest. In Bogotá the entry price is 250 million pesos for sale and 1,350,000 pesos per month for rent; the lowest entry rental is in Soacha (850,000 pesos).
