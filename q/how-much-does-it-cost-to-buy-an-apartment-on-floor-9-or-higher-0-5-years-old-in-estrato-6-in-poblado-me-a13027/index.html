How much does it cost to buy an apartment on floor 9 or higher 0–5 years old in estrato 6 in Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments on floor 9 or higher 0–5 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Poblado, Medellín is COP 1,292,500,000, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 995,000,000 to COP 1,867,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,871,026/m² and the median listed size is 122 m². That is 54% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 850,000. 90% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
