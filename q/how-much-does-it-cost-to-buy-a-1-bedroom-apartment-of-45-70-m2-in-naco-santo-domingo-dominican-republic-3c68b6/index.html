How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 171,950, based on 136 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 159,000 to USD 190,666. The median price per square metre is USD 2,793/m² and the median listed size is 60.5 m². That is 28% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
