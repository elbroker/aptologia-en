And for rent?

Between + 25.5% and + 136.4%.

A 2 room apartment with 2 bathrooms is requested for sale + 136.8% against one with 1 bathroom in Chile, the largest difference of 11 countries, and + 13.1% in Paraguay. For rent, the second bath is added from + 25.5% (Paraguay) to + 136.4% (Brazil).
