What does the study “Houses for sale in Monteria by parking lot: price and m² of each group” show?

In Montería, a house for sale from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 475 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "2 or more parking lot" in 980 million pesos (+ 106.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (2.73 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (2.94 million pesos / m²).
