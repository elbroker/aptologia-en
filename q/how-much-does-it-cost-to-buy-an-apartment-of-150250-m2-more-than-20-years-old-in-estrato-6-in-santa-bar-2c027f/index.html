How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 150–250 m² more than 20 years old in estrato 6 in Santa Bárbara Central, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² more than 20 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Santa Bárbara Central, Bogotá is COP 1,100,000,000, based on 104 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 950,000,000 to COP 1,300,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,063,636/m² and the median listed size is 176 m². That is 47% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,184,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
