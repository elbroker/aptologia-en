How much does it cost to rent a home in Larrañaga, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Larrañaga, Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 28,000 per month, based on 49 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 25,000 to UYU 32,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 594/m² and the median listed size is 48 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 4,500. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
