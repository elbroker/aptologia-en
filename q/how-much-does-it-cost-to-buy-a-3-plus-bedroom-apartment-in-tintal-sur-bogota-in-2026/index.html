How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Tintal Sur, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Tintal Sur, Bogotá is COP 173,000,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 82% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 960,000,000).
