How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more in San Vicente, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more for sale in San Vicente, Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 1,200,000,000, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 853,750,000 to PYG 1,387,500,000. The median price per square metre is PYG 3,056,235/m² and the median listed size is 367 m². That is 23% above the study's reference median for Asunción (PYG 975,000,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
