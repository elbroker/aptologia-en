How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 100–150 m² with parking in Aidy Grill, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² with parking for sale in Aidy Grill, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 355,000, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 320,000 to USD 440,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,074/m² and the median listed size is 114 m². That is 1% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
