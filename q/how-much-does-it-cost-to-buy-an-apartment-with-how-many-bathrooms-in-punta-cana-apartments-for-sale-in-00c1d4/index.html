How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many bathrooms in Punta Cana?

1 bathroom: USD 165,000; 2 bathrooms: USD 250,000; 3 bathrooms: USD 395,250 (mid-October of 2026).

In Punta Cana, an apartment for sale from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in USD 165,000 in the median and one from the group "3 bathrooms" in USD 395,250 (+ 139.5%). per square metre, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (USD 2,399 / m²) and the most expensive "1 bathroom" (USD 2,622 / m²).
