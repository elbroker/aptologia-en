How much does it cost to buy a house in Rancho San Francisco Pueblo San Bartolo Ameyalco, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Rancho San Francisco Pueblo San Bartolo Ameyalco, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 32,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 22,490,000 to MXN 54,000,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 48,185/m² and the median listed size is 668 m². That is 117% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 14,750,000). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
