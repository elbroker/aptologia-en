How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 461,500, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 397,500 to USD 651,250. The median price per square metre is USD 4,667/m² and the median listed size is 112 m². That is 28% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
