How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 100–150 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 5 in Manga, Cartagena in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 5 for sale in Manga, Cartagena is COP 700,000,000, based on 103 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 545,000,000 to COP 950,300,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,762,712/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 800,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 700,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
