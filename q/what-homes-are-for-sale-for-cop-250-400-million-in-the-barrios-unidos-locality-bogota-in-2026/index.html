What homes are for sale for COP 250–400 million in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for COP 250–400 million in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 320,000,000, based on 204 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 290,000,000 to COP 350,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,416,667/m² and the median listed size is 30 m². That is 63% below the study's reference median (COP 860,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 248,280. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 34.8% of listings include parking.
