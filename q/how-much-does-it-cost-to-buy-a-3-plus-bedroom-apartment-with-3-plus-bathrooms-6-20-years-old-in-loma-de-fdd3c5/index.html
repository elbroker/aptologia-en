How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms 6–20 years old in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms 6–20 years old for sale in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado is COP 1,399,000,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,231,250,000 to COP 1,672,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,657,658/m² and the median listed size is 150 m². That is 71% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 820,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 710,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
