Where is it cheaper to buy a 2 room apartment in Bogotá?

In El Tintal: 220 million pesos in the median.

In Bogotá, an apartment of 2 rooms costs 1,930 million pesos in the medium in Cabrera, the most expensive neighborhood, and 220 million pesos in El Tintal, the cheapest: 8.8 times less. We compare 48 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
