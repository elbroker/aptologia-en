How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Ricaurte, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Ricaurte, Bogotá is COP 247,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 231,000,000 to COP 265,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,363,971/m² and the median listed size is 35 m². That is 54% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 535,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 167,500. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 15.2% of listings include parking.
