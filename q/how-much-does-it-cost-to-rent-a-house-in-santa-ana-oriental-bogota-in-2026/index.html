How much does it cost to rent a house in Santa Ana Oriental, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Santa Ana Oriental, Bogotá is COP 27,000,000 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 20,500,000 to COP 30,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 49,545/m² and the median listed size is 490 m². That is 157% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 10,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 600,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 97.2% of listings include parking.
