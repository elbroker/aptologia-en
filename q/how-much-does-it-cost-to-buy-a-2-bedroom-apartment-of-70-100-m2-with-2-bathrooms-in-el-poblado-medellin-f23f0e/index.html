How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms in El Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in El Poblado, Medellín is COP 810,000,000, based on 293 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 700,000,000 to COP 925,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,911,094/m² and the median listed size is 84 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 621,500. The most frequent estrato is 6, 99.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
