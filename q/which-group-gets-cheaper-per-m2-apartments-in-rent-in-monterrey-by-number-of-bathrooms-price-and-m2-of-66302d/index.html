Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (Mexican pesos 281.2 (USD 15.2) / m² per month) and the most expensive "1 bath" (Mexican pesos 395.5 (USD 21.4) / m² per month).

In Monterrey, a rental department of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 19,500 Mexican pesos (USD 1,056) per month in the median and one of the group "3 bathrooms" in 43,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,329) per month (+ 120.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (Mexican pesos 281.2 (USD 15.2) / m² per month) and the most expensive "1 bath" (Mexican pesos 395.5 (USD 21.4) / m² per month).
