How much does it cost to buy a house with how many bathrooms in Jamundí?

2 bathrooms: 385 million pesos; 3 bathrooms: 520 million pesos; 4 or more bathrooms: 980 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Jamundí, a house for sale from the group "2 bathrooms" is requested in 385 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 980 million pesos (+ 154.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "4 or more bathrooms" (3.39 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "3 bathrooms" (3.68 million pesos / m²).
