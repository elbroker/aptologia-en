How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 78,186, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 73,254 to USD 103,500. The median price per square metre is USD 1,500/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 50. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
