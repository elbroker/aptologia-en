How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Cayma, Arequipa, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Cayma, Arequipa, Peru is USD 176,500, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 129,250 to USD 264,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,213/m² and the median listed size is 131 m². That is 22% above the study's reference median for Arequipa (USD 145,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
