How much is estrato HOA fee 3?

The median is $204,000 per month (13.3% of the rent), with a typical range of $166,000 to $300,000.

In Bogotá, the rate of HOA fee of an apartment for rent amounts in the median to 15.8% of the rent: $700,000 per month on a medium rental of $4,000,000. It goes from $204,000 in estrato 3 to $1,528,500 in estrato 6, but as a proportion of the rent is almost equal in all estratos (13% to 17%). per square metre, the HOA fee costs $5,000 / m² in estrato 3 and $10,075 / m² in estrato 6.
