Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (3.25 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "120 - 160 m²" (4.07 million pesos / m²).

In Barranquilla, a house for sale from the group "70 - 90 m²" is requested in 288 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 1,074 million pesos (+ 273.6%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (3.25 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "120 - 160 m²" (4.07 million pesos / m²).
