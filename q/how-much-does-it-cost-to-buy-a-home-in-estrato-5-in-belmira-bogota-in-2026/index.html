How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 5 in Belmira, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 5 for sale in Belmira, Bogotá is COP 882,500,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 733,750,000 to COP 1,380,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,046,536/m² and the median listed size is 137 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median (COP 748,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 876,000. 100% of listings include parking.
