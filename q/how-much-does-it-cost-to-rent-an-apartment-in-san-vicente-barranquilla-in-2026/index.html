How much does it cost to rent an apartment in San Vicente, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in San Vicente, Barranquilla is COP 2,500,000 per month, based on 87 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,900,000 to COP 3,393,250. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 32,899/m² and the median listed size is 70 m². That is 11% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 405,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 96.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
