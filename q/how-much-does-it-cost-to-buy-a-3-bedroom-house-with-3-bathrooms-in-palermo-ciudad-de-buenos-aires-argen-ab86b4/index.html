How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with 3+ bathrooms in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 880,000, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 672,500 to USD 1,450,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,669/m² and the median listed size is 302 m². That is 203% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 290,000). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
