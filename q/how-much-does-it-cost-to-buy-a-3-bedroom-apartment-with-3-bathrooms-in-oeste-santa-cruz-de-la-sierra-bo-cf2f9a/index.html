How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Oeste, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Oeste, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 149,000, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 121,000 to USD 186,500. The median price per square metre is USD 913/m² and the median listed size is 153 m². That is 49% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 100. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
