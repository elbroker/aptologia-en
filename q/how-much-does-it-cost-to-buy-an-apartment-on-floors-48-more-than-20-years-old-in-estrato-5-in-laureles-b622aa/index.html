How much does it cost to buy an apartment on floors 4–8 more than 20 years old in estrato 5 in Laureles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments on floors 4–8 more than 20 years old in estrato 5 for sale in Laureles, Medellín is COP 850,000,000, based on 74 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 654,500,000 to COP 1,095,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,951,977/m² and the median listed size is 138 m². That is 1% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 503,500. 81.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
