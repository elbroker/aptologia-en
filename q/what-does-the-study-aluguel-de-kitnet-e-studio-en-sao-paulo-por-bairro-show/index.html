What does the study “Aluguel de kitnet e studio en São Paulo por bairro” show?

Rent a kitnet or studio in São Paulo costs the median 2,650 real (USD 521) per month; the most expensive bairro in the sample is Vila Nova Conceição (3,902 real (USD 767)) and the cheapest Moca (1,825 real (USD 359)). The median area is 27 m².
