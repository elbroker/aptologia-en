How much is the square meter worth in Jamundi for estrato?

From 3.20 million pesos (estrato 3) to 4.22 million pesos (estrato 5) in the median of October 2026.

In Jamundi the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 3.20 million pesos in estrato 3 and 4.22 million pesos in estrato 5, 1.3 times more. The average total price ranges from 198 million pesos to 430 million pesos according to the estrato.
