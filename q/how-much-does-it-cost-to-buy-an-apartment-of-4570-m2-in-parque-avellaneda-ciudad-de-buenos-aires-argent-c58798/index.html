How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 45–70 m² in Parque Avellaneda, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Parque Avellaneda, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 89,700, based on 55 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 72,000 to USD 99,950. The median price per square metre is USD 1,527/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 46% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
