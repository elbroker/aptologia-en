How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile is UF 9,900, based on 149 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 8,900 to UF 11,100. The median price per square metre is UF 85.8/m² and the median listed size is 118 m². That is 42% above the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 6,950). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
