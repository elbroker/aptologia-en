Is it cheaper to rent a house than an apartment?

per square meter, yes in 49 of 52 cities.

In 49 of 52 cities the square meter of a house is rented cheaper than that of an apartment; the biggest difference is in Monterrey (Mexico) (- 61.1%). In El Carmen de Viboral (Colombia), however, the house is rented + 14.3% per m² in front of the apartment.
