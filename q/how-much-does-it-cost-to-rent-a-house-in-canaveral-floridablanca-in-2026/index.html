How much does it cost to rent a house in Cañaveral, Floridablanca in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Cañaveral, Floridablanca is COP 5,000,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,550,000 to COP 5,950,000. That is 25% above the study's reference median for Floridablanca (COP 4,000,000).
