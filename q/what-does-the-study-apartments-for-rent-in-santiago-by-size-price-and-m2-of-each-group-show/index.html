What does the study “Apartments for rent in Santiago by size: price and m² of each group” show?

In Santiago, a rental department of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 370,000 Chilean pesos (USD 377) per month in the median and one of the group "More than 160 m²" in 1,700,000 Chilean pesos (USD 1,730) per month (+ 359.5%). The cheapest per square metre is "50 - 70 m²" (8,823.5 Chilean pesos (USD 9.0) / m² per month) and the most expensive "90 - 120 m²" (12,000.0 Chilean pesos (USD 12.2) / m² per month).
