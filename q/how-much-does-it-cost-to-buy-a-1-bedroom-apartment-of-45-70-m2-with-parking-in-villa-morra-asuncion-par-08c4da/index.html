How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with parking in Villa Morra, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking for sale in Villa Morra, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 109,500, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 101,746 to USD 116,500. The median price per square metre is USD 1,966/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 140,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
