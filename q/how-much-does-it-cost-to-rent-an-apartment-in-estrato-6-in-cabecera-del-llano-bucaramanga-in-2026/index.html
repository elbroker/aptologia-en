How much does it cost to rent an apartment in estrato 6 in Cabecera del Llano, Bucaramanga in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in estrato 6 in Cabecera del Llano, Bucaramanga is COP 3,500,000 per month, based on 55 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,950,000 to COP 4,675,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 32,895/m² and the median listed size is 100 m². That is 49% above the study's reference median for Bucaramanga (COP 2,350,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 701,000. 96% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
