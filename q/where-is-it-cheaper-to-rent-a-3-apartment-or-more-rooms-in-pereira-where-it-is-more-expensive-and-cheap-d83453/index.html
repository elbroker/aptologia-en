Where is it cheaper to rent a 3 apartment or more rooms in Pereira?

In Condina: 1,550,000 pesos per month in the median.

In Pereira, an apartment of 3 or more rooms is rented for 5,150,000 pesos per month in the median in Pinares Alto, the most expensive neighborhood, and 1,550,000 pesos per month in Condina, the cheapest: 3.3 times less. We compare 8 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
