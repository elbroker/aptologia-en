What does the study “Where it costs more and less to buy a apartment of 3 or more bedrooms in Quito: sector ranking” show?

In Quito, a department of 3 or more bedrooms costs USD 260,000 in the median in González Suárez, the most expensive sector, and USD 79,000 in Conocoto, the cheapest: 3.3 times less. We compare 18 sectors with at least 30 ads from 3 or more bedrooms.
