How much does it cost to rent an apartment on which floor in Itagüí?

Floor 1 - 2: 2,500,000 pesos per month; Floor 3 - 5: 2,200,000 pesos per month; Floor 6 - 10: 2,800,000 pesos per month; Floor 11 - 20: 2,850,000 pesos per month; Floor 21 or more: 2,950,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Itagüí, an apartment for rent from the "Piso 1 - 2" group is requested in 2,500,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the "Piso 21 group or more" in 2,950,000 pesos per month (+ 18.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (33,229 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Floor 11 - 20" (41,302 pesos / m² per month).
