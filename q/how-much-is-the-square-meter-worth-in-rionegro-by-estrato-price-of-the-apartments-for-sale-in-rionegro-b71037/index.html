How much is the square meter worth in Rionegro by estrato?

From 5.62 million pesos (estrato 3) to 10.44 million pesos (estrato 6) in the median of October 2026.

In Rionegro the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 5.62 million pesos in estrato 3 and 10.44 million pesos in estrato 6, 1.9 times more. The average total price ranges from 310 million pesos to 1,190 million pesos according to the estrato.
