And the cheapest?

Quinta Oriental, with 2.65 million pesos per m².

In Cúcuta, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy house is Caobos (3.73 million pesos / m²) and the cheapest Quinta Oriental (2.65 million pesos / m²), 1.4 times less. We compare 11 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
