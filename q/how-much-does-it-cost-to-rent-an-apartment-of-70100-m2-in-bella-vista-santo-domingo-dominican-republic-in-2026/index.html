How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 70–100 m² in Bella Vista, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in Bella Vista, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,300 per month, based on 65 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,100 to USD 1,400. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 14.4/m² and the median listed size is 85 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
