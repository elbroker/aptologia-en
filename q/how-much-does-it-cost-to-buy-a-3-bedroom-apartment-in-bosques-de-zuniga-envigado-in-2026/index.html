How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Bosques de Zúñiga, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Bosques de Zúñiga, Envigado is COP 1,200,000,000, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 900,000,000 to COP 1,441,868,563. The median price per square metre is COP 7,828,283/m² and the median listed size is 140 m². That is 41% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 850,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 743,908. The most frequent estrato is 5, 95.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
