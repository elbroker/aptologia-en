What homes are for sale for COP 400–700 million in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for COP 400–700 million in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 545,000,000, based on 100 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 449,750,000 to COP 600,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 2,760,000/m² and the median listed size is 208 m². That is 71% above the study's reference median (COP 319,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 240,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 69% of listings include parking.
