How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Jardín, Santa Marta in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Jardín, Santa Marta is COP 400,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 290,000,000 to COP 480,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,311,695/m² and the median listed size is 100 m². That is 33% below the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 505,500. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
