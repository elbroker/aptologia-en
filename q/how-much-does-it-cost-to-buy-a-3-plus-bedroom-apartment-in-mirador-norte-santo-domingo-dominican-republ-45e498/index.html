How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Mirador Norte, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Mirador Norte, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 285,000, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 248,750 to USD 315,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,475/m² and the median listed size is 178 m². That is 11% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 320,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
