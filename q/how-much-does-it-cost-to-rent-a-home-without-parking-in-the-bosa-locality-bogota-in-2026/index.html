How much does it cost to rent a home without parking in the Bosa locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes without parking in the Bosa locality, Bogotá is COP 1,100,000 per month, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 900,000 to COP 1,300,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 24,377/m² and the median listed size is 44 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 1,100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 103,000. The most frequent estrato is 2.
