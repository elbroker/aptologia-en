How much does it cost to buy an apartment without parking with 1 bathroom in Cedofeita, Santo Ildefonso, Sé, Miragaia, São Nicolau e Vitória, Oporto, Portugal in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments without parking with 1 bathroom for sale in Cedofeita, Santo Ildefonso, Sé, Miragaia, São Nicolau e Vitória, Oporto, Portugal is EUR 310,775, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 275,483 to EUR 380,450. The median price per square metre is EUR 5,572/m² and the median listed size is 58.5 m². That is 27% below the study's reference median for Oporto (EUR 425,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
