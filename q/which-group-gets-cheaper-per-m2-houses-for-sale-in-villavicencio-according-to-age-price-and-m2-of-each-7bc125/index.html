Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (2.90 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "11 to 20 years" (3.29 million pesos / m²).

In Villavicencio, a house for sale from the group "1 to 5 years" is requested in 650 million pesos in the median and one from the group "21 to 30 years" in 595 million pesos (- 8.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (2.90 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "11 to 20 years" (3.29 million pesos / m²).
