How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment on floor 9 or higher in Ciudad Mallorquín, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments on floor 9 or higher for sale in Ciudad Mallorquín, Barranquilla is COP 267,500,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 249,750,000 to COP 316,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,856,140/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 41% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 200,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
