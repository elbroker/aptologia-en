How much does it cost to buy a house with how many bathrooms in Luque?

1 bathroom: 500,000,000 Guarani (USD 87,637); 2 bathrooms: 550,000,000 Guarani (USD 96,400); 3 bathrooms: 850,000,000 Guarani (USD 148,982) (medium of October 2026).

In Luque, a house for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 500,000,000 Guaranis (USD 87,637) in the median and one of the group "3 bathrooms" in 850,000,000 Guaranis (USD 148,982) (+ 70.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (2,982,843 Guarani (USD 523) / m²) and the most expensive "1 bathroom" (3,000,000 Guarani (USD 526) / m²).
