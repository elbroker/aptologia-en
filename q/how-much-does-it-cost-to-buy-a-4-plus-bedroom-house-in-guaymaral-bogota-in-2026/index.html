How much does it cost to buy a 4+ bedroom house in Guaymaral, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in Guaymaral, Bogotá is COP 2,700,000,000, based on 325 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,300,000,000 to COP 3,150,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,222,925/m² and the median listed size is 440 m². That is 116% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,250,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,750,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 97.2% of listings include parking.
