How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Caribe Verde, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Caribe Verde, Barranquilla is COP 145,000,000, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 70% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 490,000,000).
