How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 560,000, based on 578 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 392,016 to USD 825,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,222/m² and the median listed size is 265 m². That is 133% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
