How much is a 2 bedroom apartment worth for rent in Barranquilla?

The median is $2,200,000 per month (2,715 ads).

The average rental of an apartment in Barranquilla is $2,600,000 per month; an apartment of 2 alcobas requests in the median $2,200,000. Among the neighborhoods with 30 ads or more, the cheapest is Alameda del Rio ($1,300,000) and the most expensive Buenavista ($5,000,000): 3.8 times more.
