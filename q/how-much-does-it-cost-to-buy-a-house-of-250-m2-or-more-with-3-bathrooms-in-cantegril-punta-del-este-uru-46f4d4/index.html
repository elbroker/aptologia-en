How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more with 3+ bathrooms in Cantegril, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Cantegril, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 785,000, based on 74 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 682,500 to USD 1,155,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,061/m² and the median listed size is 434 m². That is 12% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
