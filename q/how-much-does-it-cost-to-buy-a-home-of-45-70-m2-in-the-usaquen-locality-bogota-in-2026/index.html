How much does it cost to buy a home of 45–70 m² in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes of 45–70 m² for sale in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá is COP 460,000,000, based on 1,750 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 367,250,000 to COP 579,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,306,845/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 55% below the study's reference median (COP 1,030,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 469,303. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 86.7% of listings include parking.
