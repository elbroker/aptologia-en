How much is the m² worth in estrato 6 in Santa Marta?

9.53 million pesos for apartments.

In Santa Marta, the m² of an apartment for sale goes from 4.65 million pesos in estrato 3 to 9.53 million pesos in estrato 6. In estrato 6, the m² of a house is 57.4% cheaper than that of an apartment.
