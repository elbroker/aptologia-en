How much does it cost to rent a home under 45 m² in the Bosa locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes under 45 m² in the Bosa locality, Bogotá is COP 928,650 per month, based on 66 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 850,000 to COP 1,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 25,539/m² and the median listed size is 40 m². That is 16% below the study's reference median (COP 1,100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 100,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 9.1% of listings include parking.
