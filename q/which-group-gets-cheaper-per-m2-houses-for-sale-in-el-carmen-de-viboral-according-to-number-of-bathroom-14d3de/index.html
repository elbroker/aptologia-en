Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (4.28 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (6.31 million pesos / m²).

In El Carmen de Viboral, a house for sale from the group "2 bathrooms" is requested in 445 million pesos in the median and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 2,500 million pesos (+ 461.8%). For square meter, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (4.28 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (6.31 million pesos / m²).
