How much does it cost to rent a home in Indianópolis, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Indianópolis, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 5,300 per month, based on 185 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 3,500 to BRL 9,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 98.6/m² and the median listed size is 65 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,310. 70.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
