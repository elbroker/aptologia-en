How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house in La Encarnación, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses for sale in La Encarnación, Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 1,075,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 930,000,000 to PYG 1,375,000,000. That is 7% above the study's reference median for Asunción (PYG 1,000,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
