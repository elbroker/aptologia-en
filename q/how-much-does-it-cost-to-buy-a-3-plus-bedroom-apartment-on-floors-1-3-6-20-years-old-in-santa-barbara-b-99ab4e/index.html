How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment on floors 1–3 6–20 years old in Santa Bárbara, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments on floors 1–3 6–20 years old for sale in Santa Bárbara, Bogotá is COP 1,210,000,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 895,000,000 to COP 1,450,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,480,437/m² and the median listed size is 161 m². That is 61% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,301,550. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.5.
