What homes are for sale for COP 250–400 million in the Usme locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for COP 250–400 million in the Usme locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 330,000,000, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 255,300,000 to COP 350,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 2,133,333/m² and the median listed size is 144 m². That is 65% above the study's reference median (COP 200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 94,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 54.1% of listings include parking.
