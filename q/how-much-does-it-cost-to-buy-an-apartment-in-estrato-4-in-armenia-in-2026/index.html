How much does it cost to buy an apartment in estrato 4 in Armenia in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments in estrato 4 for sale in Armenia is COP 305,000,000, based on 452 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 250,000,000 to COP 390,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,076,923/m² and the median listed size is 64 m². That is 20% below the study's reference median for Armenia (COP 380,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 258,300. 84% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
