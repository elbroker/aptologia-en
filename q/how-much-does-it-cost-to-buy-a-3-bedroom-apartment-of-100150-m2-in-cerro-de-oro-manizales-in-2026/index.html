How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² in Cerro de Oro, Manizales in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² for sale in Cerro de Oro, Manizales is COP 975,930,000, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 857,500,000 to COP 1,045,803,348. The median price per square metre is COP 8,011,761/m² and the median listed size is 128 m². That is 117% above the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 450,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 81.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
