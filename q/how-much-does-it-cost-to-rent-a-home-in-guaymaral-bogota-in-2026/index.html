How much does it cost to rent a home in Guaymaral, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Guaymaral, Bogotá is COP 12,100,000 per month, based on 148 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 8,662,500 to COP 15,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 30,000/m² and the median listed size is 407 m². That is 246% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,497,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,800,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 85.1% of listings include parking.
