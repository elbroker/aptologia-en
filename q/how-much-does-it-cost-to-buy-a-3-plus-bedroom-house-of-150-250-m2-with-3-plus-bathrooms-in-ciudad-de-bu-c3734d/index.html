How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 349,000, based on 465 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 249,500 to USD 450,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,746/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 20% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 290,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
