How much does it cost to rent an apartment in La Francia, Manizales in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in La Francia, Manizales is COP 1,600,000 per month, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,421,128 to COP 2,125,000. That is 3% below the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 1,650,000).
