How many square meters do I buy with 100 million pesos in Bogotá?

Some 14 m² at average price per m² of October 2026.

With 100 million pesos, some 29 m² of apartment is bought in Soacha, the most surrendering city of 38, and 11 m² in El Retiro. In Bogotá they reach for 14 m² and in Medellín for 13 m².
