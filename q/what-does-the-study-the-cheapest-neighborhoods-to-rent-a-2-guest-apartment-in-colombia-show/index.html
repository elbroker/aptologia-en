What does the study “The cheapest neighborhoods to rent a 2 guest apartment in Colombia” show?

The cheapest neighborhood in Colombia to rent an apartment of 2 rooms, including 30 ads or more, is Madelena (Bogotá), with a medium rental of 1,300,000 pesos per month. In Bogotá, the cheapest is Madelena (1,300,000 pesos). In Medellín, the cheapest is San Antonio de Prado (1,500,000 pesos). In Cali, the cheapest is Bochalema (1,550,000 pesos). In Barranquilla, the cheapest is Alameda del Río (1,317,000 pesos). In Cartagena, the cheapest is Manzanillo (2,540,000 pesos).
