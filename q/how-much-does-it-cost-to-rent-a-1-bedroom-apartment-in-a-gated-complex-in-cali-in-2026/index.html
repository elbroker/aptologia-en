How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in a gated complex in Cali in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in a gated complex in Cali is COP 2,950,000 per month, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,800,000 to COP 3,900,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 57,895/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 55% above the study's reference median for Cali (COP 1,900,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 81% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
