How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 250 m² or more in Cabecera del Llano, Bucaramanga in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 250 m² or more for sale in Cabecera del Llano, Bucaramanga is COP 1,725,000,000, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,262,500,000 to COP 1,900,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,427,244/m² and the median listed size is 265 m². That is 301% above the study's reference median for Bucaramanga (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,550,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
