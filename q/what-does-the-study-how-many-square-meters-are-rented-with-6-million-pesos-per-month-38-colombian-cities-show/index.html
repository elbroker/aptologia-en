What does the study “How many square meters are rented with 6 million pesos per month? 38 Colombian cities” show?

With 6 million pesos a month, some 317 m² of apartment is rented in Tocancipá, the most surrendering city of 38, and 122 m² in El Retiro. In Bogotá they reach for 124 m² and in Medellín for 122 m².
