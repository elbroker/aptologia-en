How much does it cost to rent a home of 100–150 m² in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes of 100–150 m² in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá is COP 7,900,000 per month, based on 503 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 6,500,000 to COP 10,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 65,833/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median (COP 7,327,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,284,500. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 96.2% of listings include parking.
