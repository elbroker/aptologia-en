How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 4 in Tocancipa in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 4 for sale in Tocancipa is COP 214,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 193,125,000 to COP 231,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,803,571/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 213,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 129,478. 12.9% of listings include parking.
