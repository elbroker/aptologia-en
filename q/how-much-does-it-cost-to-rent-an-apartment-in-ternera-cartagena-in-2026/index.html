How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Ternera, Cartagena in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Ternera, Cartagena is COP 1,800,000 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,444,500 to COP 2,121,608. That is 50% below the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 3,600,000).
