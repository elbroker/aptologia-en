How much does it cost to buy a home in Sajonia, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Sajonia, Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 700,000,000, based on 163 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 600,000,000 to PYG 962,500,000. The median price per square metre is PYG 3,281,250/m² and the median listed size is 212 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
