What does the study “Apartments for sale in La Paz by size: price and m² of each group” show?

In La Paz, a department for sale of the group "70 - 90 m²" is requested in USD 95,000 in the median and one of the group "More than 160 m²" in USD 210,000 (+ 121.1%). For square meter, the cheapest is "120 - 160 m²" (USD 1,038 / m²) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (USD 1,159 / m²).
