How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many bathrooms in Lima?

1 bathroom: 371,473 suns (USD 108,127); 2 bathrooms: 585,000 suns (USD 170,280); 3 bathrooms: 1,193,425 suns (USD 347,378) (medium of October 2026).

In Lima, a department for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 371,473 soles (USD 108,127) in the median and one of the group "3 bathrooms" in 1,193,425 soles (USD 347,378) (+ 221.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (7,043 suns (USD 2,050) / m²) and the most expensive "1 bathroom" (7,800 suns (USD 2,270) / m²).
