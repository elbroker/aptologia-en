How much does it cost to buy a house without parking in estrato 3 in Pereira in 2026?

The median asking price for houses without parking in estrato 3 for sale in Pereira is COP 355,000,000, based on 128 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 300,000,000 to COP 510,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,166,667/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 68% below the study's reference median for Pereira (COP 1,100,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
