How much does it cost to rent a home with parking in Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes with parking in Bogotá is COP 5,527,000 per month, based on 13,362 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,100,000 to COP 11,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,273/m² and the median listed size is 110 m². That is 58% above the study's reference median (COP 3,495,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 966,500. The most frequent estrato is 6.
