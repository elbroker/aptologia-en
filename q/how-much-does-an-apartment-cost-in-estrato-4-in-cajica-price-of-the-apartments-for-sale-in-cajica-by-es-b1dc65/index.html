How much does an apartment cost in estrato 4 in Cajicá?

390 million pesos in the median.

In Cajicá the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 4.62 million pesos in estrato 2 and 6.72 million pesos in estrato 5, 1.5 times more. The average total price ranges from 260 million pesos to 550 million pesos according to the estrato.
