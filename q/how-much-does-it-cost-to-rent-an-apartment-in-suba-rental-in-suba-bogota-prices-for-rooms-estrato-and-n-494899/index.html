How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Suba?

The median is $2,307,000 per month, with 917 unique ads on 13 October of 2026.

The average rental of an apartment or studio apartment in Sub (Bogotá) is $2,307,000 per month, about $44,235 per m². A 2 rooms costs in the median $1,850,000. The cheapest neighborhood with 5 ads or more is Rincón De Santa Ines ($1,300,000) and the most expensive Naranjos ($11,100,000).
