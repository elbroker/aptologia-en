How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms in Polanco IV Sección, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms in Polanco IV Sección, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 76,000 per month, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 65,000 to MXN 92,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 356/m² and the median listed size is 210 m². That is 138% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 32,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
