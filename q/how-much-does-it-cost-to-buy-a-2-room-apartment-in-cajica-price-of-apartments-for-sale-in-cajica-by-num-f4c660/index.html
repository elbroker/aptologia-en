How much does it cost to buy a 2 room apartment in Cajicá?

330 million pesos in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the supply is between 295 million pesos and 400 million pesos.

In Cajicá, a 1 room apartment costs 292 million pesos in the medium, one 2 million rooms 330 million pesos (+ 12.8%) and one 3 or more 450 million pesos (+ 36.4%).
