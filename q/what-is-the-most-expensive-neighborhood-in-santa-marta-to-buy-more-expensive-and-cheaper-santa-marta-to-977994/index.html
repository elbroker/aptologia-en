What is the most expensive neighborhood in Santa Marta to buy?

Well Colorado, with 12.35 million pesos per m² in the median (October of 2026).

In Santa Marta, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is Pozos Colorado (12.35 million pesos / m²) and the cheapest Garden (4.31 million pesos / m²), 2.9 times less. We compare 18 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
