And the cheapest?

Moca, with 24.8 reales (USD 4.9) per m² per month.

In São Paulo, the most expensive bairro with the most expensive m² to rent home is Brooklin (45.5 real (USD 8.9) / m² per month) and the cheapest Mooca (24.8 real (USD 4.9) / m² per month), 1.8 times less. We compare 6 bairros with at least 30 ads with area.
