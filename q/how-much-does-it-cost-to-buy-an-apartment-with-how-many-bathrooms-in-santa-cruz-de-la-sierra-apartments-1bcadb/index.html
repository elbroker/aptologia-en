How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many bathrooms in Santa Cruz de la Sierra?

1 bathroom: USD 69,000; 2 bathrooms: USD 109,800; 3 bathrooms: USD 155,000 (mid-October of 2026).

In Santa Cruz de la Sierra, a department for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in USD 69,000 in the median and one of the group "3 bathrooms" in USD 155,000 (+ 124.6%). per square metre, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (USD 1,088 / m²) and the most expensive "1 bathroom" (USD 1,389 / m²).
