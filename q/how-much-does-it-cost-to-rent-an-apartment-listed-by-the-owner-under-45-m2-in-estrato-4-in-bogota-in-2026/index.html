How much does it cost to rent an apartment listed by the owner under 45 m² in estrato 4 in Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments listed by the owner under 45 m² in estrato 4 in Bogotá is COP 1,900,000 per month, based on 231 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,700,000 to COP 2,175,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 56,538/m² and the median listed size is 35 m². That is 42% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 287,000. 47.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
