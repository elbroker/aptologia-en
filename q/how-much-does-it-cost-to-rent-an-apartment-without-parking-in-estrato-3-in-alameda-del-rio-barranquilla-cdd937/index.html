How much does it cost to rent an apartment without parking in estrato 3 in Alameda del Río, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments without parking in estrato 3 in Alameda del Río, Barranquilla is COP 1,300,000 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,100,000 to COP 1,575,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 24,312/m² and the median listed size is 53 m². That is 54% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 182,150. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
