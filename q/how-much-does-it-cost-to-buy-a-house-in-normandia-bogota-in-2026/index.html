How much does it cost to buy a house in Normandia, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Normandia, Bogotá is COP 1,320,000,000, based on 103 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 992,850,000 to COP 1,705,776,220. The median price per square metre is COP 4,400,000/m² and the median listed size is 274 m². That is 10% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 20,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 93.2% of listings include parking.
