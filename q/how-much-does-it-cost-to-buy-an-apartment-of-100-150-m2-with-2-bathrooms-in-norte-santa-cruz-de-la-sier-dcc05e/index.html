How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 120,000, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 100,575 to USD 149,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,027/m² and the median listed size is 112 m². That is 20% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 60. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
