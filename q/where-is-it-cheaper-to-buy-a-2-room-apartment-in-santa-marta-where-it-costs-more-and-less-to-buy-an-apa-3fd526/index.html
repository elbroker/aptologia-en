Where is it cheaper to buy a 2 room apartment in Santa Marta?

In Rodadero: 442 million pesos in the medium.

In Santa Marta, an apartment of 2 rooms costs 850 million pesos in the medium in Bello Horizonte, the most expensive neighborhood, and 442 million pesos in Rodadero, the cheapest: 1.9 times less. We compare 7 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
