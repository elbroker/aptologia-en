What does the study “Profitability of the rental in Armenia by estrato: in which you give more to buy to rent” show?

In Armenia, buying an apartment to rent it yields more in estrato 3 (7.1% gross per year, 14.1 years of rent) and less in estrato 5 (6.8%).
