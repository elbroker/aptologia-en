How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 6 in Bellavista, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 6 for sale in Bellavista, Bogotá is COP 2,300,000,000, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,337,500,000 to COP 3,500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,977,324/m² and the median listed size is 257 m². That is 21% above the study's reference median (COP 1,900,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,067,000. 100% of listings include parking.
