How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking in Manorá, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking in Manorá, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 1,200 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,150 to USD 1,500. The median listed size is 80.5 m². That is 11% below the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 1,350). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
