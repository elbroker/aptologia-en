How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in Lima, Peru is PEN 380,000, based on 875 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 298,940 to PEN 501,792. The median price per square metre is PEN 8,200/m² and the median listed size is 44 m². That is 39% below the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 621,600). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PEN 1. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
