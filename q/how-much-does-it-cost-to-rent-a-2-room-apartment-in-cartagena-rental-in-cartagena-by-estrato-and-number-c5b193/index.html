How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Cartagena?

Between 2,000,000 pesos (estrato 3) and 5,000,000 pesos (estrato 6) per month in the median, according to October 2026 announcements.

In Cartagena, rent an apartment of 2 rooms costs in the median 2,000,000 pesos per month in estrato 3 and 5,000,000 pesos in estrato 6, 2.5 times more. per square meter, the rent goes from 33,621 pesos to 51,136 pesos per month according to the estrato.
