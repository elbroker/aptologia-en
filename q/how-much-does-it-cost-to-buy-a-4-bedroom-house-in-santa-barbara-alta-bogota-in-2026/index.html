How much does it cost to buy a 4+ bedroom house in Santa Barbara Alta, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in Santa Barbara Alta, Bogotá is COP 4,400,000,000, based on 63 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,600,000,000 to COP 5,500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,361,204/m² and the median listed size is 532 m². That is 252% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,250,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 500,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 96.8% of listings include parking.
