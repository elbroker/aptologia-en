How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in the Suba locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 360,000,000, based on 274 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 289,992,500 to COP 500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,846,154/m² and the median listed size is 40 m². That is 45% below the study's reference median (COP 650,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 321,850. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 56.2% of listings include parking.
