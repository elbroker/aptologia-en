How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² in Santa Elena, El Retiro in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Santa Elena, El Retiro is COP 765,000,000, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 698,000,000 to COP 810,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,528,571/m² and the median listed size is 78 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for El Retiro (COP 730,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 445,836. The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
