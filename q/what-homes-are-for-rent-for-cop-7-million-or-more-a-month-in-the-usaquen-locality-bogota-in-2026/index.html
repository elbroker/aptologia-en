What homes are for rent for COP 7 million or more a month in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for rent for COP 7 million or more a month in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá have a median asking rent of COP 12,500,000 per month, based on 1,929 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 9,000,000 to COP 18,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 52,239/m² and the median listed size is 251 m². That is 172% above the study's reference median (COP 4,600,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,800,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 96.8% of listings include parking.
