How much does an apartment cost 3 or more rooms in Bogotá?

980 million pesos in the medium of the offer for sale (October of 2026).

Buy an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs in the median 175 million pesos in Soacha, the cheapest city of the show, and 980 million pesos in Bogotá, the most expensive. In Bogotá, the rent is 6,500,000 pesos per month.
