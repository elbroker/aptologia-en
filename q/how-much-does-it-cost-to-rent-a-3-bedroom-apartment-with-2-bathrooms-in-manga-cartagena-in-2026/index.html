How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms in Manga, Cartagena in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms in Manga, Cartagena is COP 5,110,000 per month, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,000,000 to COP 6,015,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 42,870/m² and the median listed size is 111 m². That is 38% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 3,700,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 881,600. The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
