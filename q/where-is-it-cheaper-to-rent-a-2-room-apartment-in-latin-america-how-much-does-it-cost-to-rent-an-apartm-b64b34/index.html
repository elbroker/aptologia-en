Where is it cheaper to rent a 2 room apartment in Latin America?

In Soacha, Colombia: USD 298 per month in the median.

Renting an apartment of 2 rooms is cheaper in Soacha (Colombia), with a median of USD 298 per month, and more expensive in Punta del Este (Uruguay), with USD 3,700 per month. In Bogotá the median is USD 831 per month (2,646,500 pesos), placed 36 from 61 from minor to major.
