What does the study “How many times the neighborhood costs more than the cheapest: square meter gap in 33 Latin American and Portugal cities” show?

In Buenos Aires, Argentina, the square meter of Puerto Madero (USD 5,712) costs 4.8 times that of Nueva Pompeya (USD 1,189), the largest gap between areas of 33 cities. The smallest gap is in Ibagué (Colombia): 1.2 times between Warsaw and Picaleña and Mirolindo.
