How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 6 in Colina Campestre, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 6 for sale in Colina Campestre, Bogotá is COP 1,810,000,000, based on 62 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,399,250,000 to COP 2,287,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,638,889/m² and the median listed size is 256 m². That is 108% above the study's reference median (COP 870,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,521,500. 98.4% of listings include parking.
