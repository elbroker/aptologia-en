How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Las Brisas, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Las Brisas, Bogotá is COP 1,400,000 per month, based on 53 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,250,000 to COP 1,500,000. That is 49% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,750,000).
