How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 5 in El Prado, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 5 in El Prado, Barranquilla is COP 6,000,000 per month, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,200,000 to COP 12,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 25,833/m² and the median listed size is 242 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 484,500. 89.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
