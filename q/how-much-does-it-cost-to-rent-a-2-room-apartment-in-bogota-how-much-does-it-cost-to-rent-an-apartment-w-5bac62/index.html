How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Bogotá?

2,646,500 pesos per month in the median, without HOA fee.

To rent an apartment of 2 rooms costs in the median 950,000 pesos a month in Soacha, the cheapest of the sample, and 4,300,000 pesos in El Retiro, the most expensive. In Bogotá the median is 2,646,500 pesos and in Medellín 4,200,000 pesos, without HOA fee.
