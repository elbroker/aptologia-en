How much does it cost to rent a home in Jose Joaquin Vargas, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Jose Joaquin Vargas, Bogotá is COP 1,880,000 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,692,956 to COP 2,317,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 53,709/m² and the median listed size is 31 m². That is 46% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,497,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 162,862. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 28.9% of listings include parking.
