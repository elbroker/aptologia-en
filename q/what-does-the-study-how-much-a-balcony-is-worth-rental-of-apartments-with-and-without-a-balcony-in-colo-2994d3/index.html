What does the study “How much a balcony is worth: rental of apartments with and without a balcony in Colombia” show?

An apartment for rent with a balcony is requested in Colombia 16.1% more per square metre than a comparable one without that feature (same city and number of rooms). 70.5% of apartments for rent declare it. In Bogotá the difference is 29.5% (49.1% of the offer).
