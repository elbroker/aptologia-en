How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Calacoto, La Paz, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Calacoto, La Paz, Bolivia is USD 208,500, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 142,250 to USD 275,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,241/m² and the median listed size is 164 m². That is 60% above the study's reference median for La Paz (USD 130,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 60. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
