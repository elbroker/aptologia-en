How much does it cost to rent a house of what size in São Paulo?

Less than 50 m²: 1,200 real (USD 236) per month; 50 - 70 m²: 1,676 real (USD 329) per month; 70 - 90 m²: 2,300 real (USD 452) per month; 90 - 120 m²: 3,100 real (USD 609) per month; 120 - 160 m²: 4,080 real (USD 802 per month; More than 160 m²: 8,000 real (USD 1,572) per month (medium October of 2026).

In São Paulo, a rental house of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 1,200 reales (USD 236) per month in the median and one of the group "More 160 m²" in 8,000 reales (USD 1,572) per month (+ 566.7%). per square metre, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (28.9 real (USD 5.7) / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (34.3 real (USD 6.7) / m² per month).
