How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 139,250, based on 144 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 115,589 to USD 170,500. The median price per square metre is USD 1,618/m² and the median listed size is 85 m². That is 39% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 50. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
