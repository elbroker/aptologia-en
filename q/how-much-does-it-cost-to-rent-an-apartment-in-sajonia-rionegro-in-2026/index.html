How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Sajonia, Rionegro in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Sajonia, Rionegro is COP 6,000,000 per month, based on 79 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,500,000 to COP 6,550,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 56,336/m² and the median listed size is 106 m². That is 126% above the study's reference median for Rionegro (COP 2,650,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
