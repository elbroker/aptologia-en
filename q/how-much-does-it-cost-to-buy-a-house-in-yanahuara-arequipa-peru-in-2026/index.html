How much does it cost to buy a house in Yanahuara, Arequipa, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Yanahuara, Arequipa, Peru is PEN 1,237,500, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 1,026,525 to PEN 1,551,000. The median price per square metre is PEN 5,163/m² and the median listed size is 255 m². That is 7% above the study's reference median for Arequipa (PEN 1,153,200). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
