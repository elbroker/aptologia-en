How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many parking lots in Zipaquí?

No parking lot: 205 million pesos; 1 parking lot: 300 million pesos; 2 or more parking lot: 590 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Zipaquirá, an apartment for sale from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 205 million pesos in the median and one from the group "2 or more parking lot" in 590 million pesos (+ 187.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (3.36 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (5.59 million pesos / m²).
