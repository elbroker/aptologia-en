How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 3 or more rooms in Buenavista?

5,900,000 pesos per month in the median (October of 2026); the central half between 4,553,200 pesos and 8,500,000 pesos.

In Barranquilla, an apartment of 3 or more rooms is rented for 5,900,000 pesos per month in the medium in Buenavista, the most expensive neighborhood, and 1,425,000 pesos per month in Alameda del Río, the cheapest: 4.1 times less. We compare 16 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
