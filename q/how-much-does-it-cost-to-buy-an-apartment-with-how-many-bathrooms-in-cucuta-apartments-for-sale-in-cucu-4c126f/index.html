How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many bathrooms in Cúcuta?

2 bathrooms: 250 million pesos; 3 bathrooms: 350 million pesos; 4 or more bathrooms: 600 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Cúcuta, an apartment for sale from the group "2 bathrooms" is requested in 250 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 600 million pesos (+ 140.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (3.21 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "2 bathrooms" (3.68 million pesos / m²).
