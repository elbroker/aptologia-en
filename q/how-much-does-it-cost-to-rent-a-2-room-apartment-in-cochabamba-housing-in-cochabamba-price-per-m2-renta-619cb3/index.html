How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Cochabamba?

USD 350 per month in the median (October of 2026).

The area with the most expensive square meter of Cochabamba is Queru Queru, with USD 1,045 in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is Centro, with USD 787. A 2 room department is rented per USD 350 per month in the medium and sold per USD 79,545. The gross implicit return on rent is 4.9% per year.
