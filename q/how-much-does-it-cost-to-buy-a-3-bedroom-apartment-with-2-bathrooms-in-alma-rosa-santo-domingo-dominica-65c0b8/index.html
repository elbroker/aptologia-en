How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms in Alma Rosa, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Alma Rosa, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 225,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 172,000 to USD 285,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,573/m² and the median listed size is 140 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
