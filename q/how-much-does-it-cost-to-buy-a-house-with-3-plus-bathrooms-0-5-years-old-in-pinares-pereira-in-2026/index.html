How much does it cost to buy a house with 3+ bathrooms 0–5 years old in Pinares, Pereira in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with 3+ bathrooms 0–5 years old for sale in Pinares, Pereira is COP 1,200,000,000, based on 92 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 950,000,000 to COP 1,562,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,641,026/m² and the median listed size is 260 m². That is 9% above the study's reference median for Pereira (COP 1,100,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 955,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 96.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
