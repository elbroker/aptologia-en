How much does it cost to rent a house in San Isidro, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in San Isidro, Lima, Peru is USD 3,990 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 3,200 to USD 7,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 11.8/m² and the median listed size is 477 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Lima (USD 3,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
