How much does it cost to buy a department of what size in Cuenca?

70 - 90 m²: USD 103,251; 90 - 120 m²: USD 126,000; 120 - 160 m²: USD 157,300; More than 160 m²: USD 245,000 (mid-October of 2026).

In Cuenca, a department for sale of the group "70 - 90 m²" is requested in USD 103,251 in the median and one of the group "More than 160 m²" in USD 245,000 (+ 137.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "120 - 160 m²" (USD 1,094 / m²) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (USD 1,405 / m²).
