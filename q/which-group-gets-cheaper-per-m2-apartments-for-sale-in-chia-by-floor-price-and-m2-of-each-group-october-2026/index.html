Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 3 - 5" (5.49 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 6 - 10" (5.65 million pesos / m²).

In Chia, an apartment for sale from the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 380 million pesos in the median and one from the group "Piso 6 - 10" in 288 million pesos (- 24.2%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 3 - 5" (5.49 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 6 - 10" (5.65 million pesos / m²).
