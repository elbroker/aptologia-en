Where is it cheaper per m²?

In "11 to 20 years": 2,114 pesos per m² per month.

In Soacha, the highest square meter HOA fee is paid in the group buildings "6 to 10 years" (2,290 pesos per m² per month in the median) and the lowest in "11 to 20 years" (2,114 pesos). Average monthly rate per strip: 1 to 5 years: 120,000 pesos; 6 to 10 years: 115,500 pesos; 11 to 20 years: 109,750 pesos.
