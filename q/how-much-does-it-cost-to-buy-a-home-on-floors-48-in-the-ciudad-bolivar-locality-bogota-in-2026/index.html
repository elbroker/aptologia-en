How much does it cost to buy a home on floors 4–8 in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes on floors 4–8 for sale in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá is COP 235,000,000, based on 73 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 213,525,000 to COP 258,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,600,000/m² and the median listed size is 49 m². That is 2% below the study's reference median (COP 241,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 133,473. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 13.7% of listings include parking.
