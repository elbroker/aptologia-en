How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more with 3+ bathrooms in Jara, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Jara, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 450,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 282,500 to USD 687,500. The median price per square metre is USD 929/m² and the median listed size is 502 m². That is 29% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 350,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
