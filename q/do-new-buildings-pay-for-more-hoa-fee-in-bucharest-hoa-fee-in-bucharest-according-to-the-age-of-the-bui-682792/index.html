Do new buildings pay for more HOA fee in Bucharest?

For starters: 4,622 pesos / m²; 1 to 5 years: 4,718 pesos / m²; 6 to 10 years: 5,192 pesos / m²; 11 to 20 years: 5,079 pesos / m²; 21 to 30 years: 4,161 pesos / m² (median per m² per month).

In Bucaramanga, the highest square meter HOA fee is paid in the group buildings "6 to 10 years" (5,192 pesos per m² per month in the median) and the lowest in "21 to 30 years" (4,161 pesos). Average monthly rate per strip: For starters: 430,000 pesos; 1 to 5 years: 438,000 pesos; 6 to 10 years: 450,000 pesos; 11 to 20 years: 441,000 pesos; 21 to 30 years: 489,000 pesos.
