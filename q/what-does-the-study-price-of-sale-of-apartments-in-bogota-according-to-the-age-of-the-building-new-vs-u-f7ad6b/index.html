What does the study “Price of sale of apartments in Bogotá according to the age of the building: new vs. used” show?

In Bogotá, an apartment for sale from 0 to 2 years requests $8,378,378 per m² in the medium, and one of more than 30 years $6,322,651. Comparing apartments of the same estrato, town, size and rooms, the new costs 19.3% more per m² than one 11 to 20 years, and the one over 30 years 18.0% less.
