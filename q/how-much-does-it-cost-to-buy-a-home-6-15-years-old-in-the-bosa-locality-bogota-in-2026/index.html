How much does it cost to buy a home 6–15 years old in the Bosa locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes 6–15 years old for sale in the Bosa locality, Bogotá is COP 182,250,000, based on 172 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 164,870,000 to COP 210,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,828,556/m² and the median listed size is 47 m². That is 19% below the study's reference median (COP 224,750,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 90,692. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 11% of listings include parking.
