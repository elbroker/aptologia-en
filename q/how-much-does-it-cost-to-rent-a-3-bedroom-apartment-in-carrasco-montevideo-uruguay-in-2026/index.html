How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in Carrasco, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Carrasco, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 3,300 per month, based on 55 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,750 to USD 5,200. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 17.8/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 2,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
