How much does it cost to rent a home of 150–250 m² in the Suba locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes of 150–250 m² in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 7,200,000 per month, based on 181 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,900,000 to COP 8,655,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 37,864/m² and the median listed size is 190 m². That is 134% above the study's reference median (COP 3,079,400). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,160,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 97.2% of listings include parking.
