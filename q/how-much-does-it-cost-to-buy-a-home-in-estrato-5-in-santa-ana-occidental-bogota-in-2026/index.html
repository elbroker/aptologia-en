How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 5 in Santa Ana Occidental, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 5 for sale in Santa Ana Occidental, Bogotá is COP 1,390,000,000, based on 90 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 822,500,000 to COP 1,880,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,459,007/m² and the median listed size is 176 m². That is 2% below the study's reference median (COP 1,425,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 674,000. 92.2% of listings include parking.
