And the cheapest?

Union, with USD 1,864 per m².

In Montevideo, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is Puerto Buceo (USD 4,169 / m²) and the cheapest Union (USD 1,864 / m²), 2.2 times less. We compare 23 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
