How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment without parking with 3+ bathrooms in Robledo, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments without parking with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Robledo, Medellín is COP 272,500,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 222,500,000 to COP 350,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,362,214/m² and the median listed size is 65 m². That is 68% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 180,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
