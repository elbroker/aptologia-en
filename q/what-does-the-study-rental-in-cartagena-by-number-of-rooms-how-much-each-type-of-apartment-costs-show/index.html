What does the study “Rental in Cartagena by number of rooms: how much each type of apartment costs” show?

In Cartagena, rent a 1 room apartment costs in the median 2,669,000 pesos per month and one of 2 rooms, 3,000,000 pesos (+ 12.4%). One of 3 or more rooms is requested in 4,500,000 pesos.
