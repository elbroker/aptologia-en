How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² on floors 4–8 in Laureles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² on floors 4–8 for sale in Laureles, Medellín is COP 670,000,000, based on 62 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 550,000,000 to COP 758,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,703,392/m² and the median listed size is 85.5 m². That is 20% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 396,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 93.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
