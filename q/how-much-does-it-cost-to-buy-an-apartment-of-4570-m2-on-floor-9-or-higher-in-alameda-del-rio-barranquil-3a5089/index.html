How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 45–70 m² on floor 9 or higher in Alameda del Río, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² on floor 9 or higher for sale in Alameda del Río, Barranquilla is COP 217,500,000, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 196,000,000 to COP 258,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,172,571/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 52% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 200,000. The most frequent estrato is 3, 64.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
