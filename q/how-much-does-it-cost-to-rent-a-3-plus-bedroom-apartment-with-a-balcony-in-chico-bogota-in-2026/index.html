How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with a balcony in Chicó, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with a balcony in Chicó, Bogotá is COP 13,000,000 per month, based on 72 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 9,225,000 to COP 15,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 61,224/m² and the median listed size is 204 m². That is 294% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,807,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
