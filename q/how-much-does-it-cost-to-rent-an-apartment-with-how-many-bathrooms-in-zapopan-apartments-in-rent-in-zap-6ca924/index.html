How much does it cost to rent an apartment with how many bathrooms in Zapopan?

1 bath: 17,500 Mexican pesos (USD 948) per month; 2 bath: 28,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,516) per month; 3 bath: 50,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,708) per month; 4 or more bath: 65,000 Mexican pesos (USD 3,520) per month (medium October 2026).

In Zapopan, a rental department of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 17,500 Mexican pesos (USD 948) per month in the median and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 65,000 Mexican pesos (USD 3,520) per month (+ 271.4%). per square metre, the cheapest is "4 or more bathrooms" (267.4 Mexican pesos (USD 14.5) / m² per month) and the most expensive "1 bath" (335.0 Mexican pesos (USD 18.1) / m² per month).
