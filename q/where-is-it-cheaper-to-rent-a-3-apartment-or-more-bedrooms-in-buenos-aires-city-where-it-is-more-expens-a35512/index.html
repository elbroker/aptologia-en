Where is it cheaper to rent a 3 apartment or more bedrooms in Buenos Aires City?

In Recoleta: USD 2,500 per month in the median.

In Buenos Aires City, an apartment of 3 or more bedrooms is rented for USD 5,000 per month in the middle of Puerto Madero, the most expensive neighborhood, and USD 2,500 per month in Recoleta, the cheapest: 2.0 times less. We compare 6 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more bedrooms.
