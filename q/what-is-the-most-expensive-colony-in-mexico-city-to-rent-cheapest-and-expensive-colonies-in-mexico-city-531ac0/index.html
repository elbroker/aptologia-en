What is the most expensive colony in Mexico City to rent?

Juárez, with 505.7 Mexican pesos (USD 27.4) per m² per month in the medium (October of 2026).

In Mexico City, the colony with the most expensive m² to rent department is Juárez (505.7 Mexican pesos (USD 27.4) / m² per month) and the cheapest extension State of Hidalgo (243.0 Mexican pesos (USD 13.2) / m² per month), 2.1 times less. We compare 27 colonies with at least 30 ads with area.
