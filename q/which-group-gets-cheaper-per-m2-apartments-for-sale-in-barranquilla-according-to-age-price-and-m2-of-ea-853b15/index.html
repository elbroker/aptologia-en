Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (3.42 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "6 to 10 years" (4.89 million pesos / m²).

In Barranquilla, an apartment for sale from the group "To premiere (0 years)" is requested in 497 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "21 to 30 years" in 490 million pesos (- 1.4%). For square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (3.42 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "6 to 10 years" (4.89 million pesos / m²).
