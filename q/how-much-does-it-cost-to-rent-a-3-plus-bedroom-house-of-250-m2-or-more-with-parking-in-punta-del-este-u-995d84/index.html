How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more with parking in Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more with parking in Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 10,000 per month, based on 268 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 5,950 to USD 18,200. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 25/m² and the median listed size is 386 m². That is 37% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 7,300). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
