And the cheapest?

North zone, with 42,370 pesos per m² per month.

In Cartagena, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to rent apartment is Castillogrande (56,261 pesos / m² per month) and the cheapest North Zone (42,370 pesos / m² per month), 1.3 times less. We compare 15 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
