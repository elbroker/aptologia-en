How much does it cost to buy a home with security or a doorman in Ibagué in 2026?

The median asking price for homes with security or a doorman for sale in Ibagué is COP 230,000,000, based on 112 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 160,000,000 to COP 400,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,425,709/m² and the median listed size is 61.5 m². That is 18% below the study's reference median (COP 280,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 224,500. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 59.8% of listings include parking.
