How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Riomar, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Riomar, Barranquilla is COP 630,000,000, based on 350 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 450,000,000 to COP 900,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,103,987/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 40% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 683,500. The most frequent estrato is 6, 96.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
