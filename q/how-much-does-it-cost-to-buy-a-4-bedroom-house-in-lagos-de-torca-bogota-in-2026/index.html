How much does it cost to buy a 4+ bedroom house in Lagos de Torca, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in Lagos de Torca, Bogotá is COP 2,650,000,000, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,300,000,000 to COP 2,950,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,309,524/m² and the median listed size is 400 m². That is 112% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,250,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,500,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 88.9% of listings include parking.
