How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom house in a gated complex in El Retiro in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom houses in a gated complex in El Retiro is COP 7,500,000 per month, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,575,000 to COP 8,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 46,018/m² and the median listed size is 145 m². That is 35% below the study's reference median for El Retiro (COP 11,500,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
