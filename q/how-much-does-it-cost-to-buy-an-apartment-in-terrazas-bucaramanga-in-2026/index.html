How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Terrazas, Bucaramanga in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Terrazas, Bucaramanga is COP 565,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 450,000,000 to COP 1,150,000,000. That is 31% above the study's reference median for Bucaramanga (COP 430,000,000).
