How much does it cost to buy a home with parking in Chía in 2026?

The median asking price for homes with parking for sale in Chía is COP 1,000,000,000, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 427,000,000 to COP 2,490,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,888,889/m² and the median listed size is 250 m². That is 156% above the study's reference median (COP 390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 670,896. The most frequent estrato is 4.
