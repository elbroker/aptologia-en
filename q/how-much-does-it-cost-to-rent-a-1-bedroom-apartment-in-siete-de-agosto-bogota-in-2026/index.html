How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in Siete de Agosto, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in Siete de Agosto, Bogotá is COP 1,500,000 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 29% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,100,000).
