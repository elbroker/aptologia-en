How much does it cost to buy an apartment of what size in Arequipa?

70 - 90 m²: 329,300 soles (USD 95,851); 90 - 120 m²: 411,877 soles (USD 119,888); 120 - 160 m²: 560,000 soles (USD 163,003); More than 160 m²: 820,000 suns (USD 238,683) (mid-October of 2026).

In Arequipa, a department for sale of the group "70 - 90 m²" is requested in 329,300 soles (USD 95,851) in the median and one of the group "More than 160 m²" in 820,000 soles (USD 238,683) (+ 149.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "70 - 90 m²" (4,015 soles (USD 1,169) / m²) and the most expensive "90 - 120 m²" (4,241 soles (USD 1,235) / m²).
