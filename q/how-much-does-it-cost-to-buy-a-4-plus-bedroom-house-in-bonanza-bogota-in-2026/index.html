How much does it cost to buy a 4+ bedroom house in Bonanza, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in Bonanza, Bogotá is COP 787,500,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 737,500,000 to COP 950,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,090,909/m² and the median listed size is 266 m². That is 37% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,250,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 100,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 86.4% of listings include parking.
