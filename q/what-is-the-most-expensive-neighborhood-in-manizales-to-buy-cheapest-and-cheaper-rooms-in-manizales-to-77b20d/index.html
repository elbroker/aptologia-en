What is the most expensive neighborhood in Manizales to buy?

Golden Cerro, with 8.09 million pesos per m² in the median (October of 2026).

In Manizales, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is Cerro de Oro (8.09 million pesos / m²) and the cheapest Campobello (4.22 million pesos / m²), 1.9 times less. We compare 22 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
