How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms in Balvanera, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Balvanera, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 145,000, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 115,000 to USD 194,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,711/m² and the median listed size is 84 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
