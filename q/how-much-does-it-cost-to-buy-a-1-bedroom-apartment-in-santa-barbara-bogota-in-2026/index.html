How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in Santa Barbara, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in Santa Barbara, Bogotá is COP 518,000,000, based on 129 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 430,000,000 to COP 610,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,416,667/m² and the median listed size is 52 m². That is 23% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 420,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 500,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 85.3% of listings include parking.
