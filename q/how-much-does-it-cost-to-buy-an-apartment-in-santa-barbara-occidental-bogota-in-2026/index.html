How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Santa Barbara Occidental, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Santa Barbara Occidental, Bogotá is COP 1,050,000,000, based on 353 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 750,000,000 to COP 1,370,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,352,941/m² and the median listed size is 148 m². That is 37% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,080,100. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 98.9% of listings include parking.
