Where does a budget of 250 million pesos give more?

In Soacha: about 74 m².

With 250 million pesos, some 74 m² of apartment is bought in Soacha, the most surrendering city of 38, and 27 m² in El Retiro. In Bogotá they reach for 34 m² and in Medellín for 33 m².
