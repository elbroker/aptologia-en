How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment with 1 bathroom in Santana, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom in Santana, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 2,150 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 1,848 to BRL 2,525. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 45.8/m² and the median listed size is 50 m². That is 41% below the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 484. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
