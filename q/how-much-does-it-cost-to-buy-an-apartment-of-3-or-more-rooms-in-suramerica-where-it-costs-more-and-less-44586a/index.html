How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 3 or more rooms in Suramerica?

560 million pesos in the medium (October of 2026); the central half between 490 million pesos and 697 million pesos.

In Itagüí, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs 560 million pesos in the medium in Suramerica, the most expensive neighborhood, and 298 million pesos in San Gabriel, the cheapest: 1.9 times less. We compare 8 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
