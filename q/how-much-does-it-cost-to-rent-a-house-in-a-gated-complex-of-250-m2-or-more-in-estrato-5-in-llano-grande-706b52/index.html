How much does it cost to rent a house in a gated complex of 250 m² or more in estrato 5 in Llano Grande, Rionegro in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in a gated complex of 250 m² or more in estrato 5 in Llano Grande, Rionegro is COP 15,850,000 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 13,200,000 to COP 19,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 39,167/m² and the median listed size is 422 m². That is 51% above the study's reference median for Rionegro (COP 10,500,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.5.
