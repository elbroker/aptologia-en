What homes are for rent for less than COP 1.5 million a month in Cúcuta in 2026?

Homes listed for rent for less than COP 1.5 million a month in Cúcuta have a median asking rent of COP 950,000 per month, based on 122 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 762,500 to COP 1,187,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 15,505/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 27% below the study's reference median (COP 1,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 265,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 31.1% of listings include parking.
