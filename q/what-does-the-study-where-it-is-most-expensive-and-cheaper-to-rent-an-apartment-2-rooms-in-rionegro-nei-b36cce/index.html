What does the study “Where it is most expensive and cheaper to rent an apartment 2 rooms in Rionegro: neighborhood ranking” show?

In Rionegro, an apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 6,100,000 pesos per month in the medium in Saxony, the most expensive neighborhood, and 1,600,000 pesos per month in Cimarronas, the cheapest: 3.8 times less. We compare 11 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
