How much does it cost to buy a 3-bedroom apartment in Alhambra, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3-bedroom apartments for sale in Alhambra, Bogotá is COP 680,000,000, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 580,000,000 to COP 800,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,150,442/m² and the median listed size is 110 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 890,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 700,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 98.2% of listings include parking.
