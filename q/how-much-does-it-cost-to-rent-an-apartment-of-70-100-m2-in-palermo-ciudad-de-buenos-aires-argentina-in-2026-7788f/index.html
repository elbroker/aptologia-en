How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 70–100 m² in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 1,800 per month, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,250 to USD 2,200. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 22.1/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 11% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 1,625). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
