How many square meters do I buy with 1,200 million pesos in Bogotá?

Some 165 m² at average price per m² of October 2026.

With 1,200 million pesos, some 354 m² of apartment is bought in Soacha, the most surrendering city of 38, and 129 m² in El Retiro. In Bogotá they reach for 165 m² and in Medellín for 160 m².
