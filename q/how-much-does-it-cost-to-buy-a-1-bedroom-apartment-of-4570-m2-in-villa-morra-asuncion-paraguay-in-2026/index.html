How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Villa Morra, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Villa Morra, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 112,000, based on 93 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 100,000 to USD 120,303. The median price per square metre is USD 2,019/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is 20% below the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 140,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
