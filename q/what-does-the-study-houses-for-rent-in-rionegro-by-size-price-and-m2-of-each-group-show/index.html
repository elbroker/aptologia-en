What does the study “Houses for rent in Rionegro by size: price and m² of each group” show?

In Rionegro, a house for rent from the group "90 - 120 m²" is ordered in 4,500,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 12,000,000 pesos per month (+ 166.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (36,667 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "90 - 120 m²" (43,000 pesos / m² per month).
