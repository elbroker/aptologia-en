How much does it cost to buy a house with how many parking lots in Rionegro?

No parking lot: 950 million pesos; 1 parking lot: 1,000 million pesos; 2 or more parking lot: 2,600 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Rionegro, a house for sale from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 950 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "2 or more parking lot" in 2,600 million pesos (+ 173.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (4.88 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (7.18 million pesos / m²).
