How much does an apartment rent in Medellín?

The gross return is 7.9% per year (rent per m² × 12), before expenditure.

The municipality with the most expensive square meter of Medellín is Santa Elena, with 10.43 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Aranjuez, with 4.38 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 4.20 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 720 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.9% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 11.2% of the rent (700,000 pesos per month in the median).
