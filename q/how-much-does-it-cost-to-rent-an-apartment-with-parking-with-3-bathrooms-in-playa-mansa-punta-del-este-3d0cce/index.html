How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 6,500 per month, based on 261 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 4,300 to USD 13,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 42/m² and the median listed size is 164 m². That is 35% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 4,800). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 1,225. The median number of bathrooms is 3.
