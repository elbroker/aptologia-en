How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile is UF 10.4 per month, based on 985 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 8.26 to UF 14.3. The median monthly rent per square metre is UF 0.24/m² and the median listed size is 44 m². That is 25% below the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 13.8). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
