How much does it cost to buy a house of what size in Punta del Este?

50 - 70 m²: USD 181,000; 70 - 90 m²: USD 250,000; 90 - 120 m²: USD 305,000; 120 - 160 m²: USD 380,000; More than 160 m²: USD 880,000 (mid-October of 2026).

In Punta del Este, a house for sale from the group "50 - 70 m²" is requested in USD 181,000 in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in USD 880,000 (+ 386.2%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (USD 2,682 / m²) and the most expensive "50 - 70 m²" (USD 3,046 / m²).
