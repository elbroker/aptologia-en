How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with parking in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking for sale in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 73,005, based on 77 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 60,000 to USD 85,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,400/m² and the median listed size is 53 m². That is 27% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 50. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
