How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 3,500 per month, based on 79 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,500 to USD 6,300. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 41.2/m² and the median listed size is 85 m². That is 27% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 4,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
