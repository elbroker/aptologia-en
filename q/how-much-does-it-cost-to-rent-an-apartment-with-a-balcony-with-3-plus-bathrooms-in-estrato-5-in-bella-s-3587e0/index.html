How much does it cost to rent an apartment with a balcony with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 5 in Bella Suiza, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 5 in Bella Suiza, Bogotá is COP 6,566,000 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,700,000 to COP 8,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 53,560/m² and the median listed size is 126 m². That is 99% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,040,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
