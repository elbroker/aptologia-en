How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 45–70 m² in La Florida, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in La Florida, Santiago, Chile is UF 12.2 per month, based on 163 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 10.9 to UF 13.4. The median monthly rent per square metre is UF 0.23/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 13.8). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
