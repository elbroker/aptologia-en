How many apartments have a parking lot in Colombia?

89.2% of the apartments for sale announce at least one.

In Soacha the 79.9% of the apartments for sale are announced without a parking lot, the largest proportion of 24 cities; in Envigado is only 4.1%. In the whole country, 10.8% have no parking lot; in estrato 6 67.9% have 2 or more.
