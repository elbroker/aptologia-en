How much does it cost to rent a home of 250 m² or more in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes of 250 m² or more in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá is COP 19,000,000 per month, based on 885 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 15,000,000 to COP 25,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 54,118/m² and the median listed size is 330 m². That is 159% above the study's reference median (COP 7,327,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 3,000,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 91.3% of listings include parking.
