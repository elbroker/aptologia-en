How much does it cost to rent an apartment of what size in Chia?

Less than 50 m²: 1,500,000 pesos per month; 50 - 70 m²: 1,550,000 pesos per month; 70 - 90 m²: 2,600,000 pesos per month; 90 - 120 m²: 3,200,000 pesos per month (medium of October of 2026).

In Chia, an apartment for rent from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 1,500,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "90 - 120 m²" in 3,200,000 pesos per month (+ 113.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "50 - 70 m²" (29,412 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (40,278 pesos / m² per month).
