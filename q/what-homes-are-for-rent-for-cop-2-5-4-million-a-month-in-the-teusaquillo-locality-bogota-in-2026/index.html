What homes are for rent for COP 2.5–4 million a month in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for rent for COP 2.5–4 million a month in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá have a median asking rent of COP 3,000,000 per month, based on 289 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,800,000 to COP 3,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 54,000/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median (COP 2,769,500). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 429,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 66.4% of listings include parking.
