How much more expensive is the house in Madrid than in Bogotá?

per square meter, about 2.7 times: USD 6,091 (official valuation) versus USD 2,282 (offer).

The square meter of housing in Madrid is worth USD 6,091 according to the official value of the MIVAU (2026 -T2), 2.7 times the price ordered in Bogotá (USD 2,282). The most expensive Latin American city of comparison, Santiago (Chile), asks for USD 3,189 per m². For rent, Barcelona marks USD 18.7 per m² per month (Incasòl, 2025) compared to USD 15.2 in Bogotá.
