How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 3 in Usaquen, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 3 for sale in Usaquen, Bogotá is COP 315,000,000, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 250,000,000 to COP 590,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,097,561/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 74% below the study's reference median (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 252,000. 31.4% of listings include parking.
