What does the study “Houses for sale in Mosquera by number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In Mosquera, a house for sale from the group "2 bathrooms" is requested in 355 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 775 million pesos (+ 118.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (3.47 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "3 bathrooms" (3.95 million pesos / m²).
