How much does it cost to buy a house with parking more than 20 years old in estrato 4 in Quinta Camacho, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with parking more than 20 years old in estrato 4 for sale in Quinta Camacho, Bogotá is COP 2,000,000,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,700,000,000 to COP 3,500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,191,797/m² and the median listed size is 429 m². That is 67% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
