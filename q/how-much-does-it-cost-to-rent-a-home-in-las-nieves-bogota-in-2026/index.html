How much does it cost to rent a home in Las Nieves, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Las Nieves, Bogotá is COP 1,800,000 per month, based on 143 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,600,000 to COP 2,300,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 58,932/m² and the median listed size is 31.5 m². That is 49% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,497,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 300,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 28% of listings include parking.
