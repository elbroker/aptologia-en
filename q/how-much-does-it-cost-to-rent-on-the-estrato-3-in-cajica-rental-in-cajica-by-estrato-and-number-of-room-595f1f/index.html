How much does it cost to rent on the estrato 3 in Cajicá?

An apartment of 2 rooms in estrato 3 is requested in 1,550,000 pesos per month.

In Cajicá, rent an apartment of 2 rooms costs in the median 1,550,000 pesos per month in estrato 3 and 2,750,000 pesos in estrato 5, 1.8 times more. per square meter, the rent goes from 28,571 pesos to 39,872 pesos per month according to the estrato.
