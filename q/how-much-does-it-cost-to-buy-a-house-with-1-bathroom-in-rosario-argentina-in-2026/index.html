How much does it cost to buy a house with 1 bathroom in Rosario, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with 1 bathroom for sale in Rosario, Argentina is USD 77,500, based on 72 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 64,500 to USD 115,250. The median price per square metre is USD 1,014/m² and the median listed size is 90 m². The median number of bathrooms is 1.
