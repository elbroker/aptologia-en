How much does it cost to buy a home in San Antonio, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in San Antonio, Bogotá is COP 700,000,000, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 420,000,000 to COP 820,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,585,657/m² and the median listed size is 194 m². That is 19% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 231,500. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 64.9% of listings include parking.
