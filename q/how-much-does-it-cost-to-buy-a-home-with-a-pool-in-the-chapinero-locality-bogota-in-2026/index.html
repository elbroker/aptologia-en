How much does it cost to buy a home with a pool in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes with a pool for sale in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá is COP 2,886,913,500, based on 490 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,750,000,000 to COP 4,300,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 13,238,095/m² and the median listed size is 242 m². That is 80% above the study's reference median (COP 1,600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,132,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 98% of listings include parking.
