How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 3 in San José, Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 3 for sale in San José, Sabaneta is COP 335,000,000, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 320,000,000 to COP 372,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,983,607/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 222,500. 69.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
