How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 2 in the Suba locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 2 in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 1,300,000 per month, based on 55 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,200,000 to COP 1,615,251. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 26,531/m² and the median listed size is 49 m². That is 58% below the study's reference median (COP 3,079,400). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 165,000. 25.5% of listings include parking.
