How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in El Poblado, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in El Poblado, Barranquilla is COP 490,000,000, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 51% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 325,000,000).
