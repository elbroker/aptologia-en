How much does it cost to rent an apartment of what size in Itagüí?

Less than 50 m²: 2,100,000 pesos per month; 50 - 70 m²: 2,500,000 pesos per month; 70 - 90 m²: 3,000,000 pesos per month; 90 - 120 m²: 3,875,000 pesos per month (medium of October of 2026).

In Itagüí, an apartment for rent from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 2,100,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "90 - 120 m²" in 3,875,000 pesos per month (+ 84.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "90 - 120 m²" (38,095 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (46,429 pesos / m² per month).
