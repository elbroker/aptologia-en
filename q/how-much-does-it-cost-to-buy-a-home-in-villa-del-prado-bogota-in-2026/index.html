How much does it cost to buy a home in Villa del Prado, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Villa del Prado, Bogotá is COP 650,000,000, based on 83 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 520,000,000 to COP 785,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,875,000/m² and the median listed size is 138 m². That is 25% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 219,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 86.7% of listings include parking.
