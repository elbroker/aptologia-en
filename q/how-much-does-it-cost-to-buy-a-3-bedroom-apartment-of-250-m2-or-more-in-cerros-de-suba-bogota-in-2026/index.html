How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 250 m² or more in Cerros de Suba, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 250 m² or more for sale in Cerros de Suba, Bogotá is COP 2,500,000,000, based on 64 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,950,000,000 to COP 2,800,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,112,505/m² and the median listed size is 325 m². That is 233% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,000,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
