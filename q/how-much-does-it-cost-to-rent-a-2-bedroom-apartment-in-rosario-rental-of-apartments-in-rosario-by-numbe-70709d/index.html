How much does it cost to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in Rosario?

800,000 Argentine pesos (USD 528) per month in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between 750,000 Argentine pesos (USD 495) and 1,100,000 Argentine pesos (USD 726).

In Rosario, a 1 bedroom department is rented for 550,000 Argentine pesos (USD 363) per month in the median, one 2 Argentine 800,000 bedroom (USD 528) (+ 45.5%) and one 3 or more 900,000 Argentine pesos (USD 594) (+ 12.5%).
