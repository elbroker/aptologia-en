How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms for sale in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 180,000, based on 163 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 120,750 to USD 277,500. The median price per square metre is USD 618/m² and the median listed size is 320 m². That is 12% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 160,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
