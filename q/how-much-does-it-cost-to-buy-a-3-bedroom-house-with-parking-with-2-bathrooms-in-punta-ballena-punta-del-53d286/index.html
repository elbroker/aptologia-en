How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with parking with 2 bathrooms in Punta Ballena, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with parking with 2 bathrooms for sale in Punta Ballena, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 398,000, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 320,000 to USD 597,500. The median price per square metre is USD 2,182/m² and the median listed size is 185 m². That is 43% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
