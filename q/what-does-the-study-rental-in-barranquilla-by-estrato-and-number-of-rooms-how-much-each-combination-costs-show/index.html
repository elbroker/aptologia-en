What does the study “Rental in Barranquilla by estrato and number of rooms: how much each combination costs” show?

In Barranquilla, rent an apartment of 2 rooms costs in the median 1,500,000 pesos per month in estrato 3 and 3,600,000 pesos in estrato 6, 2.4 times more. per square meter, the rent goes from 18,739 pesos to 39,068 pesos per month according to the estrato.
