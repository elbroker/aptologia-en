How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² with parking in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with parking in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 2,550 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,800 to USD 2,960. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 13.7/m² and the median listed size is 186 m². That is 89% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
