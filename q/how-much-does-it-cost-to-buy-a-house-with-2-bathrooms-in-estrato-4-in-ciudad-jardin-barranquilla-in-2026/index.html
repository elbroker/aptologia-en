How much does it cost to buy a house with 2 bathrooms in estrato 4 in Ciudad Jardín, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with 2 bathrooms in estrato 4 for sale in Ciudad Jardín, Barranquilla is COP 677,545,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 580,000,000 to COP 880,000,000. That is 25% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 900,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
