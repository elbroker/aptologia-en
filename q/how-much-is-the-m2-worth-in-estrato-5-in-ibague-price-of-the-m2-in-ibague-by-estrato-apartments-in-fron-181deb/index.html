How much is the m² worth in estrato 5 in Ibagué?

5.00 million pesos for apartments.

In Ibagué, the m² of an apartment for sale goes from 3.49 million pesos in estrato 2 to 5.00 million pesos in estrato 5. In estrato 2, the m² of a house is 39.1% cheaper than that of an apartment.
