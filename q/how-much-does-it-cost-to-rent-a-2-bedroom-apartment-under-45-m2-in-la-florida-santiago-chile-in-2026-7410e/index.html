How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment under 45 m² in La Florida, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments under 45 m² in La Florida, Santiago, Chile is UF 10.4 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 9.47 to UF 12.2. The median monthly rent per square metre is UF 0.26/m² and the median listed size is 42 m². That is 25% below the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 13.8). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
