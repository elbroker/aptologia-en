How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking with 2 bathrooms in estrato 6 in Cerro de Oro, Manizales in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms in estrato 6 for sale in Cerro de Oro, Manizales is COP 817,000,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 645,000,000 to COP 994,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,062,500/m² and the median listed size is 99 m². That is 82% above the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 450,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
