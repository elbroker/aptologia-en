What does the study “How much the parking lot, balcony, closed set, furnished and pets go up in each city of Colombia” show?

Of five features of the apartment, the one that increases the rent in Colombia is "furnished" (+ 31.2% in front of a comparable apartment in the same city, estrato and rooms) and the one that least, "accepts pets" (+ 0.9%). The parking lot amounts to + 15.8% on average, from + 3.3% in Chia to + 32.0% in Barranquilla. The ads don't bring a pool, gym or elevator in a structured way, so don't measure here.
