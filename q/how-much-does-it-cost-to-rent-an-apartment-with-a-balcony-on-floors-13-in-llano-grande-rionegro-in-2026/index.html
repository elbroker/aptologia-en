How much does it cost to rent an apartment with a balcony on floors 1–3 in Llano Grande, Rionegro in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony on floors 1–3 in Llano Grande, Rionegro is COP 6,225,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,050,000 to COP 7,500,000. The median listed size is 97.5 m². That is 135% above the study's reference median for Rionegro (COP 2,650,000). The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.5.
