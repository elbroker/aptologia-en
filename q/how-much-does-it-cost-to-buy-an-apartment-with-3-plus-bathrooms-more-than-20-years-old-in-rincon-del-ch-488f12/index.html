How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 3+ bathrooms more than 20 years old in Rincon del Chicó, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms more than 20 years old for sale in Rincon del Chicó, Bogotá is COP 960,000,000, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 760,000,000 to COP 1,200,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,957,317/m² and the median listed size is 150 m². That is 28% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,240,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
