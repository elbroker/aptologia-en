Where is it cheaper to rent an apartment with 3 rooms or more?

In Soacha, Colombia: USD 298 per month in the median (October of 2026).

An apartment of 3 rooms or more is rented by USD 298 per month in the median of Soacha (Colombia), the lowest of 62 cities, and by USD 4,874 per month in San Pedro Garza Garcia (Mexico), the highest. In Bogotá, USD 2,042 per month. Among the capitals, from USD 730 in La Paz to USD 2,979 in Mexico City.
