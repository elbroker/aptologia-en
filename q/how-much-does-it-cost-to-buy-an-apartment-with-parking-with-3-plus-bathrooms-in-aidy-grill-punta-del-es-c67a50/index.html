How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Aidy Grill, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Aidy Grill, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 435,000, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 357,500 to USD 550,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,206/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 21% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
