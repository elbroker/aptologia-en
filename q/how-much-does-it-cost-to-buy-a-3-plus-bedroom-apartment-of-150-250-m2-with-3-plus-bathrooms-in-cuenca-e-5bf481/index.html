How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Cuenca, Ecuador in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Cuenca, Ecuador is USD 221,500, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 185,000 to USD 284,750. The median price per square metre is USD 1,171/m² and the median listed size is 190 m². That is 61% above the study's reference median for Cuenca (USD 137,250). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
