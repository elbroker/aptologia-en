How much does it cost to buy a house in Los Andes, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Los Andes, Bogotá is COP 990,000,000, based on 75 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 825,000,000 to COP 1,200,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,338,843/m² and the median listed size is 212 m². That is 18% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 100,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 92% of listings include parking.
