How much does it cost to buy a 2 bedroom apartment in Manantiales?

USD 620,000 in the median (October of 2026); the central half between USD 495,000 and USD 727,000.

In Punta del Este, a 2 bedroom apartment costs USD 620,000 in the middle of the area in Manantiales, the most expensive neighborhood, and USD 249,500 in Peninsula, the cheapest: 2.5 times less. We compare 10 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 bedrooms.
