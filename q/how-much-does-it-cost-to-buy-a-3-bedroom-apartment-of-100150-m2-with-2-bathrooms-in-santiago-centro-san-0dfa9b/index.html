How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile is UF 6,025, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 4,804 to UF 8,472. The median price per square metre is UF 55.4/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 13% below the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 6,950). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
