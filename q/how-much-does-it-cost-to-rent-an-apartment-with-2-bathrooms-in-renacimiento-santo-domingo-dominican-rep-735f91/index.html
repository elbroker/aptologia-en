How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 2 bathrooms in Renacimiento, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 2 bathrooms in Renacimiento, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,200 per month, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,000 to USD 1,362. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 8.38/m² and the median listed size is 136 m². That is 11% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
