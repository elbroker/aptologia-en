How much are the common expenses in Montevideo?

5,000 Uruguayan pesos (USD 124) per month in the median, 15.7% of the rent (October of 2026).

In Montevideo the common costs of an apartment for rent are in the median 5,000 Uruguayan pesos (USD 124) per month, 15.7% of the rent. The neighborhood with the highest common costs is Pocytes (6,122 Uruguayan pesos (USD 152)) and that of the lowest, Aguada (4,255 Uruguayan pesos (USD 106)).
