How much does it cost to buy a house in San Lucas, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in San Lucas, Medellín is COP 1,750,000,000, based on 101 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,490,000,000 to COP 2,790,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,744,186/m² and the median listed size is 271 m². That is 26% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 1,390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,260,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
