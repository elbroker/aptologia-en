How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 150–250 m² in Los Cacicazgos, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Los Cacicazgos, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 410,000, based on 53 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 350,000 to USD 441,750. The median price per square metre is USD 2,121/m² and the median listed size is 185 m². That is 71% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
