How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in San Diego, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in San Diego, Medellín is COP 400,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 46% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 740,500,000).
