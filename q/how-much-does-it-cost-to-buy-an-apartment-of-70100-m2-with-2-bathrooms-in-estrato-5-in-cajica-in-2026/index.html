How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms in estrato 5 in Cajicá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms in estrato 5 for sale in Cajicá is COP 502,500,000, based on 112 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 466,750,000 to COP 560,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,524,390/m² and the median listed size is 78 m². That is 29% above the study's reference median for Cajicá (COP 390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 360,000. 99.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
