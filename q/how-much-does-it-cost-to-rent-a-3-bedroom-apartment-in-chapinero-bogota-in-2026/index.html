How much does it cost to rent a 3-bedroom apartment in Chapinero, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3-bedroom apartments in Chapinero, Bogotá is COP 9,780,000 per month, based on 67 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,850,000 to COP 16,250,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 57,432/m² and the median listed size is 157 m². That is 50% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 6,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,219,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 94% of listings include parking.
