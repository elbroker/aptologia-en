How much does it cost to buy a 2 room apartment in Cartagena?

750 million pesos in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the supply is between 520 million pesos and 1,100 million pesos.

In Cartagena, a 1 room apartment costs 670 million pesos in the medium, one 2 million rooms 750 million pesos (+ 11.9%) and one 3 or more 898 million pesos (+ 19.7%).
