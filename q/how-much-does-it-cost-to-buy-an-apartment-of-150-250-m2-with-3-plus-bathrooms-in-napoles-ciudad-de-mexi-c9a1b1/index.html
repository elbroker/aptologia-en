How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Nápoles, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Nápoles, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 9,027,309, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 7,381,455 to MXN 9,780,501. The median price per square metre is MXN 46,749/m² and the median listed size is 194 m². That is 81% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 4,990,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
