How much does it cost to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in San Pedro Garza Garcia?

57,000 Mexican pesos (USD 3,087) per month in the medium (October 2026); the central half of the offer is between 45,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,437) and 65,000 Mexican pesos (USD 3,520).

In San Pedro Garza Garcia, a department of 1 rechamber is rented by 32,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,733) per month in the medium, one of 2 Mexican 57,000 recars (USD 3,087) (+ 78.1%) and one of 3 or more 90,000 Mexican pesos (USD 4,874) (+ 57.9%).
