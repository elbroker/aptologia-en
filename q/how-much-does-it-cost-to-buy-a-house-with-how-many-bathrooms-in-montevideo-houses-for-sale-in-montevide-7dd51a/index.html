How much does it cost to buy a house with how many bathrooms in Montevideo?

1 bathroom: USD 136,800; 2 bathrooms: USD 242,000; 3 bathrooms: USD 550,000; 4 or more bathrooms: USD 690,000 (mid-October of 2026).

In Montevideo, a house for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in USD 136,800 in the median and one in the group "4 or more bathrooms" in USD 690,000 (+ 404.4%). per square metre, the cheapest is "1 bathroom" (USD 1,373 / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (USD 2,277 / m²).
