How much does it cost to buy an apartment without parking in Valle de Lili, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments without parking for sale in Valle de Lili, Cali is COP 223,500,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 208,250,000 to COP 240,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,928,571/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 48% below the study's reference median for Cali (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 264,552. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
