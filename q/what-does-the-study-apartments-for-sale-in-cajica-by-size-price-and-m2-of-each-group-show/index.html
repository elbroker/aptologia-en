What does the study “Apartments for sale in Cajicá by size: price and m² of each group” show?

In Cajicá, an apartment for sale from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 290 million pesos in the median and one from the group "120 - 160 m²" in 796 million pesos (+ 174.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "50 - 70 m²" (5.48 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "120 - 160 m²" (6.52 million pesos / m²).
