Where is it most expensive to rent in Monterrey and its metropolitan area?

In Santa María: 37,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,004) per month in the medium.

To rent an apartment in Monterrey and its metropolitan area costs more in Santa María, with a medium rent of 37,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,004) per month, and less in Rome, with 18,980 Mexican pesos (USD 1,028).
