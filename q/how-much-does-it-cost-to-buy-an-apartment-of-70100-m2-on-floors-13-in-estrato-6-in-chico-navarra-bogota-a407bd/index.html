How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² on floors 1–3 in estrato 6 in Chicó Navarra, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² on floors 1–3 in estrato 6 for sale in Chicó Navarra, Bogotá is COP 835,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 752,500,000 to COP 949,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,939,175/m² and the median listed size is 88.5 m². That is 11% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 850,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
