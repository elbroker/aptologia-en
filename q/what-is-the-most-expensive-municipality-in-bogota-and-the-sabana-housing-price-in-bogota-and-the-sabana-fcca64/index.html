What is the most expensive municipality in Bogotá and the Sabana?

Bogotá, with 7,261,905 pesos per m² in apartments for sale.

In Bogotá and the Sabana, the most expensive square meter of apartment is requested in Bogotá (7.26 million pesos) and the cheapest in Soacha (3.39 million pesos). In Bogotá, an apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 2,646,500 pesos per month in the medium.
