How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms on floor 9 or higher in Laureles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms on floor 9 or higher for sale in Laureles, Medellín is COP 1,495,000,000, based on 104 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,137,500,000 to COP 1,850,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,819,293/m² and the median listed size is 186 m². That is 78% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 965,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 96.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
