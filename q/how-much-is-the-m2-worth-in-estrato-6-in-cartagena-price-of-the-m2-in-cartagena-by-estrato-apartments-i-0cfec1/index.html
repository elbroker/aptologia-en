How much is the m² worth in estrato 6 in Cartagena?

51,136 pesos per month for apartments.

In Cartagena, the m² of an apartment for rent goes from 33,621 pesos per month in estrato 3 to 51,136 pesos per month in estrato 6. In estrato 3, the m² of a house is 40.5% cheaper than that of an apartment.
