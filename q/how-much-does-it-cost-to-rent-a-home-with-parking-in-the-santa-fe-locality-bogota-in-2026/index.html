How much does it cost to rent a home with parking in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes with parking in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá is COP 2,900,000 per month, based on 245 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,300,000 to COP 3,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 56,635/m² and the median listed size is 52.9 m². That is 34% above the study's reference median (COP 2,162,225). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 456,500. The most frequent estrato is 4.
