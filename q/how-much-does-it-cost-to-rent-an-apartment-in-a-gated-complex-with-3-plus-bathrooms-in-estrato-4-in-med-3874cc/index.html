How much does it cost to rent an apartment in a gated complex with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 4 in Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in a gated complex with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 4 in Medellín is COP 3,900,000 per month, based on 253 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,000,000 to COP 5,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 41,667/m² and the median listed size is 90 m². That is 19% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). 51.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
