Where is it cheaper to buy a single environment or studio?

In Santa Cruz de la Sierra (Bolivia): USD 57,000 in the medium (October of 2026).

A mono-environment or studio is requested for USD 57,000 in the median of Santa Cruz de la Sierra (Bolivia), the lowest of 30 cities, and for USD 354,086 in Lisbon (Portugal), the highest. In Bogotá, USD 105,253. Among the capitals, from USD 68,000 in Asunción to USD 354,086 in Lisbon.
