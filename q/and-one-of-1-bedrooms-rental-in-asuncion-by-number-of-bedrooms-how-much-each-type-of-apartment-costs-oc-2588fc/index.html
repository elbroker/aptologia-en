And one of 1 bedrooms?

4,300,000 Guaraní (USD 754) per month in the median.

In Asunción, rent a 1 bedroom apartment costs in the median 4,300,000 Guaraní (USD 754) per month and one 2 bedroom, 5,650,000 Guaraní (USD 990) (+ 31.4%). One of 3 or more bedrooms is requested in 7,200,000 Guaraní (USD 1,262).
