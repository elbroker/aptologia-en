How much does it cost to rent an apartment of what size in Lima?

Less than 50 m²: 2,190 soles (USD 637) per month; 50 - 70 m²: 2,340 soles (USD 681) per month; 70 - 90 m²: 2,500 soles (USD 728) per month; 90 - 120 m²: 3,100 soles (USD 902) per month; 120 - 160 m²: 3,908 soles (USD 1,138; More than 160 m²: 7,020 suns (USD 2,043) per month (medium October of 2026).

In Lima, a rental department of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 2,190 soles (USD 637) per month in the median and one of the group "More 160 m²" in 7,020 soles (USD 2,043) per month (+ 220.5%). The cheapest per square metre is "120 - 160 m²" (29.8 soles (USD 8.7) / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (52.1 soles (USD 15.2) / m² per month).
