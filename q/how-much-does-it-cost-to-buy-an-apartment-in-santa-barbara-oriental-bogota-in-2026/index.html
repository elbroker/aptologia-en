How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Santa Barbara Oriental, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Santa Barbara Oriental, Bogotá is COP 890,000,000, based on 139 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 645,000,000 to COP 1,608,128,641. The median price per square metre is COP 9,600,000/m² and the median listed size is 114 m². That is 16% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 959,670. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 97.8% of listings include parking.
