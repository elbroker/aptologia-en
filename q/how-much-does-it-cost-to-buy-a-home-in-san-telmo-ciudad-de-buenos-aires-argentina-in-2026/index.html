How much does it cost to buy a home in San Telmo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in San Telmo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 129,500, based on 240 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 90,920 to USD 205,718. The median price per square metre is USD 2,162/m² and the median listed size is 64.5 m². The median number of bathrooms is 1.
