And the cheapest?

Ternera, with 3.80 million pesos per m².

In Cartagena, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is El Centro (13.39 million pesos / m²) and the cheapest Ternera (3.80 million pesos / m²), 3.5 times less. We compare 30 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
