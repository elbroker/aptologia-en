How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 429,000, based on 113 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 350,000 to USD 498,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,044/m² and the median listed size is 202 m². That is 79% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
