How much does it cost to buy a house with parking 6–20 years old in Villa Campestre, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with parking 6–20 years old for sale in Villa Campestre, Barranquilla is COP 1,525,000,000, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 705,000,000 to COP 2,200,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,543,590/m² and the median listed size is 295 m². That is 69% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 900,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 612,500. The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
