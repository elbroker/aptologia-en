How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² in San Antonio de Pereira, Rionegro in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in San Antonio de Pereira, Rionegro is COP 609,000,000, based on 111 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 537,000,000 to COP 690,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,800,000/m² and the median listed size is 77 m². That is 17% above the study's reference median for Rionegro (COP 520,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 350,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 86% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
