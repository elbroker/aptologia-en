How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms in Mayorca, Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms in Mayorca, Sabaneta is COP 2,950,000 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,400,000 to COP 3,312,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 46,967/m² and the median listed size is 62.5 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 3,100,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
