How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile is UF 12,800, based on 238 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 9,625 to UF 17,500. The median price per square metre is UF 69/m² and the median listed size is 168 m². That is 84% above the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 6,950). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
