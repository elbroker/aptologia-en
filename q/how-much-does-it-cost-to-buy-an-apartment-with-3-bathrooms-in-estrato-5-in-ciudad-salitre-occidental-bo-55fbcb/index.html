How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 5 in Ciudad Salitre Occidental, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 5 for sale in Ciudad Salitre Occidental, Bogotá is COP 1,875,000,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,537,500,000 to COP 2,300,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 11,139,481/m² and the median listed size is 167 m². That is 150% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 900,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
