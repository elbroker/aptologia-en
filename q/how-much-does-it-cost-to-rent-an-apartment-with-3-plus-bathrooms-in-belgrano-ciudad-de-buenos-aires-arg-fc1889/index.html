How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Belgrano, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Belgrano, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 4,000 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,900 to USD 5,250. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 21.2/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 146% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 1,625). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
