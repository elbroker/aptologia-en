How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 5 in La Castellana, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 5 in La Castellana, Bogotá is COP 2,950,000 per month, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,985,000 to COP 5,600,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 47,143/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 10% below the study's reference median (COP 3,275,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 421,000. 54.1% of listings include parking.
