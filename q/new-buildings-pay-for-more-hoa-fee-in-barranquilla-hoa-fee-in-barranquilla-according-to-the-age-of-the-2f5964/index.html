New buildings pay for more HOA fee in Barranquilla?

For starters: 4,524 pesos / m²; 1 to 5 years: 4,808 pesos / m²; 6 to 10 years: 5,105 pesos / m²; 11 to 20 years: 5,217 pesos / m²; 21 to 30 years: 4,667 pesos / m² (median per m² per month).

In Barranquilla, the highest square meter HOA fee is paid in the group buildings "11 to 20 years" (5,217 pesos per m² per month in the median) and the lowest in "To premiere" (4,524 pesos). Average monthly rate per strip: For starters: 550,000 pesos; 1 to 5 years: 496,000 pesos; 6 to 10 years: 523,500 pesos; 11 to 20 years: 600,000 pesos; 21 to 30 years: 710,000 pesos.
