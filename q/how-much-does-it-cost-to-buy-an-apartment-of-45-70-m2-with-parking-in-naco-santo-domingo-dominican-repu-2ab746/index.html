How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 45–70 m² with parking in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² with parking for sale in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 175,000, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 166,078 to USD 192,400. The median price per square metre is USD 2,788/m² and the median listed size is 61 m². That is 27% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
