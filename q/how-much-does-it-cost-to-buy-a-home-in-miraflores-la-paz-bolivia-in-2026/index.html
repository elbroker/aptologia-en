How much does it cost to buy a home in Miraflores, La Paz, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Miraflores, La Paz, Bolivia is USD 132,500, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 117,250 to USD 164,000. The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 45. The median number of bathrooms is 3.
