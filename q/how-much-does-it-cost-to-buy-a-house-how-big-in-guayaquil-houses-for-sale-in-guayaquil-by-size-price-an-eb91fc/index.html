How much does it cost to buy a house how big in Guayaquil?

70 - 90 m²: USD 76,000; 90 - 120 m²: USD 85,000; 120 - 160 m²: USD 155,000; More than 160 m²: USD 271,500 (mid-October of 2026).

In Guayaquil, a house for sale from the group "70 - 90 m²" is requested in USD 76,000 in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in USD 271,500 (+ 257.2%). For square meter, the cheapest is "90 - 120 m²" (USD 853 / m²) and the most expensive "120 - 160 m²" (USD 1,100 / m²).
