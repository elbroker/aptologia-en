How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Teusaquillo, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Teusaquillo, Bogotá is COP 534,500,000, based on 130 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 380,000,000 to COP 715,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,441,559/m² and the median listed size is 70 m². That is 30% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 372,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 59.2% of listings include parking.
