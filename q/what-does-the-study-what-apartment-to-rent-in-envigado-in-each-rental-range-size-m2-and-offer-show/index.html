What does the study “What apartment to rent in Envigado in each rental range: size, m² and offer” show?

In Envigado, the most available rental range of apartments for rent is 4 - 7 M of pesos per month (54.4% of the ads), with a median area of 95 m². 0.1% of the supply is requested in less than 1.5 million.
