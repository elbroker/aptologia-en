How much does it cost to buy a home on floors 4–8 in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes on floors 4–8 for sale in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá is COP 425,000,000, based on 307 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 269,500,000 to COP 635,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,576,379/m² and the median listed size is 64 m². That is 20% below the study's reference median (COP 530,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 298,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 64.2% of listings include parking.
