How much does it cost to buy a house in Unión, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Unión, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 169,900, based on 87 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 125,500 to USD 222,500. The median price per square metre is USD 1,338/m² and the median listed size is 128 m². That is 37% below the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 269,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
