How much does it cost to buy a home in Magdalena del Mar, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Magdalena del Mar, Lima, Peru is USD 188,714, based on 88 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 135,000 to USD 248,953. The median price per square metre is USD 2,306/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 250. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
