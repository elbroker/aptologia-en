How much does it cost to rent a house in San Lucas, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in San Lucas, Envigado is COP 12,000,000 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 10,200,000 to COP 16,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 43,472/m² and the median listed size is 280 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 11,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
