How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 4 in Cedritos, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 4 for sale in Cedritos, Bogotá is COP 580,000,001, based on 111 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 490,000,000 to COP 700,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,607,143/m² and the median listed size is 88 m². That is 23% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 615,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
