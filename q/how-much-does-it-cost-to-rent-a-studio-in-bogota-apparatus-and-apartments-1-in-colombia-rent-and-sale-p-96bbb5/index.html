How much does it cost to rent a studio in Bogotá?

2,180,000 pesos per month in the median (study and 1 room).

To rent a studio apartment or apartment 1 costs in the median 950,000 pesos a month in Cúcuta, the cheapest city of the sample, and 3,800,000 pesos in Envigado, the most expensive. In Bogotá the median is 2,180,000 pesos and in Medellín 3,350,000 pesos. These units are 20.3% of the apartment rental offer.
