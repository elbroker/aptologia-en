How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many bathrooms in Villavicencio?

1 bathroom: 180 million pesos; 2 bathrooms: 255 million pesos; 3 bathrooms: 550 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Villavicencio, an apartment for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 180 million pesos in the medium and one of the group "3 bathrooms" in 550 million pesos (+ 205.6%). For square meter, the cheapest is "1 bath" (3.71 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "3 bath" (4.69 million pesos / m²).
