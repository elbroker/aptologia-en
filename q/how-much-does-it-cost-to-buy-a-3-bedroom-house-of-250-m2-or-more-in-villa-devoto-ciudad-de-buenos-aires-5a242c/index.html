How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more in Villa Devoto, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more for sale in Villa Devoto, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 685,000, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 498,500 to USD 887,500. The median price per square metre is USD 1,966/m² and the median listed size is 335 m². That is 136% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 290,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
