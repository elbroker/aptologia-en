How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with 3+ bathrooms in Trinidad, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Trinidad, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 445,500, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 265,000 to USD 670,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,250/m² and the median listed size is 400 m². That is 27% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 350,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
