How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Tocancipá in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Tocancipá is COP 200,000,000, based on 71 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 185,000,000 to COP 230,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,700,000/m² and the median listed size is 55.5 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Tocancipá (COP 215,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 152,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
