What is the most expensive neighborhood in Envigado?

Las Palmas, with 10.32 million pesos per m² in apartments for sale (October of 2026).

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter of Envigado is Las Palmas, with 10.32 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is Alcalá, with 5.33 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 4.20 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 750 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 6.9% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 11.1% of the rent (513,000 pesos per month in the median).
