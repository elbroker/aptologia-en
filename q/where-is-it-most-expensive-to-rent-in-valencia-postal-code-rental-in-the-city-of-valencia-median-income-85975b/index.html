Where is it most expensive to rent in Valencia?

In the postal code 46004: 1,800 EUR per month of median income in 2025.

The postal code of the city of Valencia with the highest medium rental at 2025 was 46004, with 1,800 euros per month, and the lowest of 26 with 30 contracts or more was the 46035, with 790 euros. The central half of the 46004 contracts paid between 1,300 EUR and 2,500 EUR.
