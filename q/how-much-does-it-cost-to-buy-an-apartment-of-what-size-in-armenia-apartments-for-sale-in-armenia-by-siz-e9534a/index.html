How much does it cost to buy an apartment of what size in Armenia?

Less than 50 m²: 230 million pesos; 50 - 70 m²: 290 million pesos; 70 - 90 m²: 450 million pesos; 90 - 120 m²: 580 million pesos; 120 - 160 m²: 690 million pesos; More than 160 m²: 1,000 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Armenia, an apartment for sale from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 230 million pesos in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 1,000 million pesos (+ 334.8%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (4.25 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (6.00 million pesos / m²).
