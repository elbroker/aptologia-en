How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² in estrato 4 in Pan de Azúcar, Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² in estrato 4 for sale in Pan de Azúcar, Sabaneta is COP 595,000,000, based on 78 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 540,000,000 to COP 650,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,665,598/m² and the median listed size is 77 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 550,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 412,000. 98.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
