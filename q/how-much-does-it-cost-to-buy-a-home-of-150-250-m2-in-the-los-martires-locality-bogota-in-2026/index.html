How much does it cost to buy a home of 150–250 m² in the Los Mártires locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes of 150–250 m² for sale in the Los Mártires locality, Bogotá is COP 830,000,000, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 695,000,000 to COP 917,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,500,000/m² and the median listed size is 192 m². That is 137% above the study's reference median (COP 350,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 3 and 53.2% of listings include parking.
