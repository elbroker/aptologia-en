How many years of rent is an apartment in Bogotá?

An apartment of 2 rooms is equivalent to 17.0 years of your rent (October of 2026).

In Barranquilla an apartment of 2 rooms costs the same as 12.3 years of its rent, the lowest ratio of 31 Colombian cities; in Palmira it costs 18.6 years. In Bogotá they are 17.0 years (540 million pesos of price compared to 2.65 million pesos per month). The less years, the better to buy before rent, before costs and funding.
