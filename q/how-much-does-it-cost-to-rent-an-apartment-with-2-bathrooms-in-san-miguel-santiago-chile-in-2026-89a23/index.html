How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 2 bathrooms in San Miguel, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 2 bathrooms in San Miguel, Santiago, Chile is UF 12.9 per month, based on 125 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 11.6 to UF 14.6. The median monthly rent per square metre is UF 0.21/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 13.8). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
