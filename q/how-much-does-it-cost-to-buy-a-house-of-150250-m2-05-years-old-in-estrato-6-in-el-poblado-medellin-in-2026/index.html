How much does it cost to buy a house of 150–250 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 6 in El Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 150–250 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 6 for sale in El Poblado, Medellín is COP 1,400,000,000, based on 123 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,232,500,000 to COP 1,600,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,666,667/m² and the median listed size is 217 m². That is 1% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 1,390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 954,000. 97.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
