How much does it cost to rent an apartment listed by the owner with 2 bathrooms in estrato 4 in Cedritos, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments listed by the owner with 2 bathrooms in estrato 4 in Cedritos, Bogotá is COP 3,091,950 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,725,000 to COP 3,458,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 51,000/m² and the median listed size is 65 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
