How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 2 bathrooms in Delicias Norte, Chía in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Delicias Norte, Chía is COP 420,000,000, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 390,000,000 to COP 495,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,642,105/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². That is 25% above the study's reference median for Chía (COP 335,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 486,500. The most frequent estrato is 4, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
