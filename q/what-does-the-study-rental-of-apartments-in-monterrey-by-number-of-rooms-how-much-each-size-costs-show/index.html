What does the study “Rental of apartments in Monterrey by number of rooms: how much each size costs” show?

In Monterrey, a 1 repurchase department is repaid for 19,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,029) per month in the median, one 2 repurchase 29,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,571) (+ 52.6%) and one 3 or more 39,500 Mexican pesos (USD 2,139) (+ 36.2%).
