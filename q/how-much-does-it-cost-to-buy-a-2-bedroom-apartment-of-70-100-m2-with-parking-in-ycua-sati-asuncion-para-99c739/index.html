How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² with parking in Ycuá Satí, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with parking for sale in Ycuá Satí, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 190,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 145,000 to USD 215,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,135/m² and the median listed size is 84 m². That is 36% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 140,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
