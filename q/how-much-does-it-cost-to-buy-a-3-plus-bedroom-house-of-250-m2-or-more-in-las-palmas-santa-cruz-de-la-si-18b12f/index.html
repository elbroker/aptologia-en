How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more in Las Palmas, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more for sale in Las Palmas, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 557,500, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 297,500 to USD 807,871. The median price per square metre is USD 1,129/m² and the median listed size is 440 m². That is 248% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 160,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
