How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in Alejandria, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in Alejandria, Medellín is COP 5,940,382 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,925,000 to COP 6,575,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 61,765/m² and the median listed size is 90 m². That is 41% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,200,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
