How much does it cost to rent in Lima?

An apartment of 2 rooms, USD 838 per month in the middle.

The most expensive square meter of apartment in Peru is in Lima, with USD 2,137 in the medium of ads for sale; the cheapest of 3 measured cities, in Callao, with USD 1,156. In Lima, an apartment of 2 rooms is rented for USD 838 per month and sold for USD 156,324: 15.5 years of rent.
