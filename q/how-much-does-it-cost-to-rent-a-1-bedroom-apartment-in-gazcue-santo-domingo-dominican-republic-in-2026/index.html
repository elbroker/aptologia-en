How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in Gazcue, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in Gazcue, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 850 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 765 to USD 959. That is 23% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,100). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
