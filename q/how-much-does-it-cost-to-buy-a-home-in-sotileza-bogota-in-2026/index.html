How much does it cost to buy a home in Sotileza, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Sotileza, Bogotá is COP 1,870,000,000, based on 220 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,400,000,000 to COP 2,650,014,423. The median price per square metre is COP 6,755,674/m² and the median listed size is 294 m². That is 116% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,500,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 98.6% of listings include parking.
