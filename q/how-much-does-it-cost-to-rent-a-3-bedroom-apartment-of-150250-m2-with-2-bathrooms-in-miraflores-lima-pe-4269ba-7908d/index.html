How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in Miraflores, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in Miraflores, Lima, Peru is USD 2,000 per month, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,500 to USD 2,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 10.5/m² and the median listed size is 183 m². That is 60% above the study's reference median for Lima (USD 1,250). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
