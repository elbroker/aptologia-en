How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 3 or more rooms in Llano Grande?

720 million pesos in the medium (October of 2026); the central half between 620 million pesos and 1,800 million pesos.

In Rionegro, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs 720 million pesos in the medium in Llano Grande, the most expensive neighborhood, and 350 million pesos in Fontibon, the cheapest: 2.1 times less. We compare 10 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
