What does the study “Apartments for sale in Mexico City by number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In Mexico City, a department for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 3,010,190 Mexican pesos (USD 163,017) in the median and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 12,350,000 Mexican pesos (USD 668,817) (+ 310.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "1 Mexican pesos" (48,086 Mexican pesos (USD 2,604) / m²) and the most expensive "2 Mexican pesos" (54,437 Mexican pesos (USD 2,948) / m²).
