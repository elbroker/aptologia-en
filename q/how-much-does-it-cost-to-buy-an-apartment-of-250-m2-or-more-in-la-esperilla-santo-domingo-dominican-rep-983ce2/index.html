How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 250 m² or more in La Esperilla, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 250 m² or more for sale in La Esperilla, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 550,000, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 395,000 to USD 626,250. The median price per square metre is USD 1,566/m² and the median listed size is 327 m². That is 129% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
