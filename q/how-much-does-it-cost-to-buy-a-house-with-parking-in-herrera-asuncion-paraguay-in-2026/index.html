How much does it cost to buy a house with parking in Herrera, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with parking for sale in Herrera, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 350,000, based on 65 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 280,000 to USD 950,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,100/m² and the median listed size is 394 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (USD 350,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
