How much does it cost to buy an apartment in San Antonio de Pereira, Rionegro in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in San Antonio de Pereira, Rionegro is COP 575,000,000, based on 203 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 479,000,000 to COP 658,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,931,034/m² and the median listed size is 74 m². That is 11% above the study's reference median for Rionegro (COP 520,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 330,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 83.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
