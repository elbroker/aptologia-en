How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 100–150 m² in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 100–150 m² in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 2,499 per month, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,850 to USD 3,200. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 20.6/m² and the median listed size is 118 m². That is 54% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 1,625). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
