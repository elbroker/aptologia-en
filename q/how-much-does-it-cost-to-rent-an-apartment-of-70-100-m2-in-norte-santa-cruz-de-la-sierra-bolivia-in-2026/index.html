How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 70–100 m² in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 550 per month, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 464 to USD 644. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 6.34/m² and the median listed size is 85 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 570). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 50. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
