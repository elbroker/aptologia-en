What homes are for sale for COP 700–1,200 million in the Rafael Uribe Uribe locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for COP 700–1,200 million in the Rafael Uribe Uribe locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 823,000,000, based on 94 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 750,000,000 to COP 887,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 2,527,845/m² and the median listed size is 325 m². That is 83% above the study's reference median (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 299,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 57.4% of listings include parking.
