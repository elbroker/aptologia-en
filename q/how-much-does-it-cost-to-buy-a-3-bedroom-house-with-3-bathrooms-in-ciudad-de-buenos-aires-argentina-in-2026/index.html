How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with 3+ bathrooms in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 400,000, based on 1,334 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 289,250 to USD 648,750. The median price per square metre is USD 1,639/m² and the median listed size is 269 m². That is 38% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 290,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
