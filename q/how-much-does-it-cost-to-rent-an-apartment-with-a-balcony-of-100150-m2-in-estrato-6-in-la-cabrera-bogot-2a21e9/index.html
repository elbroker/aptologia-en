How much does it cost to rent an apartment with a balcony of 100–150 m² in estrato 6 in La Cabrera, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony of 100–150 m² in estrato 6 in La Cabrera, Bogotá is COP 11,850,000 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 8,610,000 to COP 12,930,000. The median listed size is 120 m². That is 259% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,570,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.5.
