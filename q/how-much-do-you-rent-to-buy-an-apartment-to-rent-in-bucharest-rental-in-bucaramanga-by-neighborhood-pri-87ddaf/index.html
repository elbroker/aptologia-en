How much do you rent to buy an apartment to rent in Bucharest?

The average gross return on 30 comparable neighbourhoods is 5.8% per year (12 months of rent requested on the order price), before HOA fee, predial, vacations and taxes.

The average rental of an apartment in Bucaramanga is $1,850,000 per month; an apartment of 2 alcobas requests in the median $1,566,150. Among the neighborhoods with 30 ads or more, the cheapest is Barro Blanco ($815,000) and the most expensive Puerta del Sol ($3,650,000): 4.5 times more.
