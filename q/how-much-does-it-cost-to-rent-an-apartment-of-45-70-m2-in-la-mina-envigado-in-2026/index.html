How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 45–70 m² in La Mina, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in La Mina, Envigado is COP 2,450,000 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,045,000 to COP 2,962,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 40,641/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 44% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 4,400,000). The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
