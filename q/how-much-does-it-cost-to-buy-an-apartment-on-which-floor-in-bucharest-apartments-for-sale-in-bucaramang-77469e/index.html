How much does it cost to buy an apartment on which floor in Bucharest?

Floor 1 - 2: 385 million pesos; Floor 3 - 5: 365 million pesos; Floor 6 - 10: 495 million pesos; Floor 11 - 20: 520 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Bucaramanga, an apartment for sale of the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 385 million pesos in the medium and one of the group "Piso 11 - 20" in 520 million pesos (+ 35.1%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 3 - 5" (3.98 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 11 - 20" (5.20 million pesos / m²).
