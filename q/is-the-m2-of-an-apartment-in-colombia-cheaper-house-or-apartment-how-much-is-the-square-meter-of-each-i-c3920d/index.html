Is the m² of an apartment in Colombia cheaper?

In 33 of 38 cities yes; in the country, 28.2% less per m².

In 33 of 38 Colombian cities the square meter of a house for sale is cheaper than that of an apartment; the biggest difference is in Santa Marta, where the house costs 60.0% less by m². In El Carmen de Viboral, however, the house leaves 20.7% more expensive per m². Throughout the country, the house is worth 28.2% less per m².
