How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with parking with 1 bathroom in Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with parking with 1 bathroom for sale in Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 800,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 350,000 to USD 950,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,500/m² and the median listed size is 262 m². That is 14% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
