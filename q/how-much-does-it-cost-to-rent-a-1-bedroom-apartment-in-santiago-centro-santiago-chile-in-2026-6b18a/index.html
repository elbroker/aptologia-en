How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile is UF 8.5 per month, based on 404 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 7.04 to UF 10.8. The median monthly rent per square metre is UF 0.26/m² and the median listed size is 34 m². That is 13% below the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 9.72). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
