How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms on floor 9 or higher in Camino Verde, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms on floor 9 or higher for sale in Camino Verde, Envigado is COP 644,000,000, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 580,000,000 to COP 697,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,314,394/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 21% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 820,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 492,500. The most frequent estrato is 4, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
