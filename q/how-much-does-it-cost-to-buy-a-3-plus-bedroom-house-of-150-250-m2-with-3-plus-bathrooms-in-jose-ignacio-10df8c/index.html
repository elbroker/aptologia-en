How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in José Ignacio, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in José Ignacio, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 890,000, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 550,000 to USD 1,375,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,917/m² and the median listed size is 205 m². That is 27% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
