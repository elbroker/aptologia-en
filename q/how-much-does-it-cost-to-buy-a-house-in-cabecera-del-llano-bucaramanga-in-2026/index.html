How much does it cost to buy a house in Cabecera del Llano, Bucaramanga in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Cabecera del Llano, Bucaramanga is COP 1,675,000,000, based on 80 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,337,500,000 to COP 2,025,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,208,333/m² and the median listed size is 342 m². That is 77% above the study's reference median for Bucaramanga (COP 945,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,500,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 91.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
