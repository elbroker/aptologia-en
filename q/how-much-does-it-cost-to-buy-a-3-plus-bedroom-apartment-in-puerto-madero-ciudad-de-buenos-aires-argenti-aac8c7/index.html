How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Puerto Madero, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Puerto Madero, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 1,450,000, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 970,000 to USD 1,713,358. The median price per square metre is USD 5,891/m² and the median listed size is 209 m². That is 409% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 285,000). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
