160,504 pesos a month.

In Barranquilla, the median rate of HOA fee of an apartment goes from 160,504 pesos per month in estrato 2 to 942,000 pesos in estrato 6, 5.9 times more. per square meter, it goes from 2,879 pesos to 6,000 pesos per month.
