How much does it cost to rent an apartment of what size in Bucharest?

50 - 70 m²: 1,500,000 pesos per month; 70 - 90 m²: 2,400,000 pesos per month; 90 - 120 m²: 3,000,000 pesos per month; 120 - 160 m²: 4,100,000 pesos per month; More than 160 m²: 5,880,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Bucaramanga, an apartment for rent from the group "50 - 70 m²" is requested in 1,500,000 pesos per month in the medium and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 5,880,000 pesos per month (+ 292.0%). For each square metre, the cheapest is "50 - 70 m²" (25,212 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "120 - 160 m²" (34,091 pesos / m² per month).
