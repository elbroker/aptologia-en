How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment with parking in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with parking for sale in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 390,000, based on 318 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 305,000 to USD 473,750. The median price per square metre is USD 4,031/m² and the median listed size is 93 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 700. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
