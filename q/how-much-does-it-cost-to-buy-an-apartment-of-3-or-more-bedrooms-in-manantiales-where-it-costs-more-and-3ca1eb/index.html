How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 3 or more bedrooms in Manantiales?

USD 1,370,000 in the median (October of 2026); the central half between USD 835,000 and USD 1,700,000.

In Punta del Este, an apartment of 3 or more bedrooms costs USD 1,370,000 in the middle in Manantiales, the most expensive neighborhood, and USD 322,500 in Roosevelt, the cheapest: 4.2 times less. We compare 10 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more bedrooms.
