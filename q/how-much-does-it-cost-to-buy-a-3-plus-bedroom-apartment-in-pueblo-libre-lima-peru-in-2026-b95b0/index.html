How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Pueblo Libre, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Pueblo Libre, Lima, Peru is USD 164,513, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 144,982 to USD 198,282. That is 48% below the study's reference median for Lima (USD 315,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
