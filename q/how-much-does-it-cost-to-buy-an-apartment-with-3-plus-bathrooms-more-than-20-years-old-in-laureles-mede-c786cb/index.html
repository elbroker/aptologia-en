How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 3+ bathrooms more than 20 years old in Laureles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms more than 20 years old for sale in Laureles, Medellín is COP 880,000,000, based on 209 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 700,000,000 to COP 1,100,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,917,750/m² and the median listed size is 146 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 560,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 76.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
