Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For each square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (3.71 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "To premiere (0 years)" (4.45 million pesos / m²).

In Bucharest, a house for sale from the group "To premiere (0 years)" is requested in 920 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "21 to 30 years" in 855 million pesos (- 7.1%).
