How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² in Santa Bárbara Central, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² for sale in Santa Bárbara Central, Bogotá is COP 770,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 672,500,000 to COP 945,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,772,727/m² and the median listed size is 114 m². That is 3% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,000,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
