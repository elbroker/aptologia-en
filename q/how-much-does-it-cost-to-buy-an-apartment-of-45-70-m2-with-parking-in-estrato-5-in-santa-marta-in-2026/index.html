How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 45–70 m² with parking in estrato 5 in Santa Marta in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² with parking in estrato 5 for sale in Santa Marta is COP 450,000,000, based on 115 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 360,000,000 to COP 591,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,187,085/m² and the median listed size is 61 m². That is 25% below the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 455,000. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
