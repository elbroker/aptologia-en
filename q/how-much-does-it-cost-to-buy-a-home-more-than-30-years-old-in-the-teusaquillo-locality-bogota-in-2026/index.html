How much does it cost to buy a home more than 30 years old in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes more than 30 years old for sale in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá is COP 1,250,000,000, based on 55 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 800,000,000 to COP 1,800,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,360,016/m² and the median listed size is 265 m². That is 40% above the study's reference median (COP 892,550,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 467,926. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 69.1% of listings include parking.
