How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Sabaneta?

2,850,000 pesos per month in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between 2,500,000 pesos and 3,244,000 pesos.

In Sabaneta, rent a 1 room apartment costs in the median 2,500,000 pesos per month and one of 2 rooms, 2,850,000 pesos (+ 14.0%). One of 3 or more rooms is requested in 3,300,000 pesos.
