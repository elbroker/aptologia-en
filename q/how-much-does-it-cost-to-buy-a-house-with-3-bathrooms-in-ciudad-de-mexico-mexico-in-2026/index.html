How much does it cost to buy a house with 3+ bathrooms in Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 18,500,000, based on 2,261 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 11,000,000 to MXN 31,500,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 41,885/m² and the median listed size is 438 m². The median number of bathrooms is 4.
