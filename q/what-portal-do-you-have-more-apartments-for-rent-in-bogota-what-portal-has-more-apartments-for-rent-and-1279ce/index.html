What portal do you have more apartments for rent in Bogotá?

Metromed, with 9,532 apartment ads (44.5% of the four portals offer), cut to 13 October of 2026.

Metrocuadrado has the largest offer of apartments for rent in Bogotá: 9,532 ads, 44.5% of the four large portals. The cheapest in front of comparable apartments is Rentola, with a price per m² 7.4% lower than the other portals in the same neighborhood and number of rooms.
