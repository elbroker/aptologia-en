How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 100–150 m² in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 100–150 m² in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is USD 980 per month, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 885 to USD 1,100. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 8.18/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median for Lima (USD 1,250). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
