How much does it cost to buy a house how big in Jamundi?

70 - 90 m²: 330 million pesos; 90 - 120 m²: 410 million pesos; 120 - 160 m²: 520 million pesos; More than 160 m²: 950 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Jamundí, a house for sale from the group "70 - 90 m²" is requested in 330 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 950 million pesos (+ 187.9%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (3.29 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (4.23 million pesos / m²).
