Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (26,580 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (46,969 pesos / m² per month).

In Sabaneta, an apartment for rent from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 2,100,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 6,675,000 pesos per month (+ 217.9%).
