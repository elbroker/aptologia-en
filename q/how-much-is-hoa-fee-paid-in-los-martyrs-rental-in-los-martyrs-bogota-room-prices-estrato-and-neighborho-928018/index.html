How much is HOA fee paid in Los Martyrs?

The median is $170,000 per month, about 10.0% of the rent.

The average rental of an apartment or studio apartment in Los Martyrs (Bogotá) is $1,600,000 per month, about $43,734 per m². A 2 rooms costs in the median $1,598,620. The cheapest neighborhood with 5 ads or more is Eduardo Santos ($1,400,001) and the most expensive Usatama ($1,800,000).
