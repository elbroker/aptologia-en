How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking 6–20 years old in estrato 6 in Playa Salguero, Santa Marta in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking 6–20 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Playa Salguero, Santa Marta is COP 842,500,000, based on 104 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 690,000,000 to COP 1,105,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,532,365/m² and the median listed size is 92.6 m². That is 40% above the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 676,208. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
