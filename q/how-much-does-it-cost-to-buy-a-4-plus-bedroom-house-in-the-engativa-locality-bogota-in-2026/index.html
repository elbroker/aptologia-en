How much does it cost to buy a 4+ bedroom house in the Engativá locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in the Engativá locality, Bogotá is COP 750,000,000, based on 1,165 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 569,000,000 to COP 960,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,464,567/m² and the median listed size is 230 m². That is 4% above the study's reference median (COP 720,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 100,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 69.8% of listings include parking.
