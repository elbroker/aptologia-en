How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 351,705, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 295,000 to USD 425,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,031/m² and the median listed size is 170 m². That is 47% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
