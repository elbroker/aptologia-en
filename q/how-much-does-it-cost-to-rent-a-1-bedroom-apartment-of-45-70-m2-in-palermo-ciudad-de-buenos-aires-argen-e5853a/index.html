How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is ARS 1,100,000 per month, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from ARS 879,250 to ARS 1,352,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is ARS 22,222/m² and the median listed size is 49.5 m². That is 29% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (ARS 850,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
