How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms 0–5 years old in Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms 0–5 years old for sale in Poblado, Medellín is COP 1,525,000,000, based on 150 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,173,750,000 to COP 2,190,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,563,002/m² and the median listed size is 176 m². That is 82% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,082,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 94.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
