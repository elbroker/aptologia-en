In which neighborhood does it cost more to rent furnished?

In Bocagrande (Cartagena), with + 54.5% in front of an unfurnished apartment of the same rooms.

The district of Colombia where the most heavy the amoblado is Bocagrande (Cartagena): an amoblado apartment is asked + 54.5% against one without amoblar of the same number of rooms; in La Prosperity (Madrid) the difference is - 15.0%. In the set of 17 neighborhoods with 30 ads or more on each side, the weighted average premium is + 36.6%.
