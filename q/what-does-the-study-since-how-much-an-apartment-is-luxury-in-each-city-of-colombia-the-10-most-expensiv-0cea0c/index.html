What does the study “Since how much an apartment is luxury in each city of Colombia: the 10% most expensive of the offer” show?

To be in the 10% most expensive of the offer, an apartment for sale has to order at least 2,300 million pesos in Bogotá, the highest threshold of 31 cities; in Soacha it is enough 230 million pesos. In Bogotá, 36.6% of the ads demand 1,000 million pesos or more; throughout the country, 22.5%.
