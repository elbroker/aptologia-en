How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 100–150 m² in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² for sale in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 230,000, based on 1,704 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 175,000 to USD 280,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,856/m² and the median listed size is 123 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
