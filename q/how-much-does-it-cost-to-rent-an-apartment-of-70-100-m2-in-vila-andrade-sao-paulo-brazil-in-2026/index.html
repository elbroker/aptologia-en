How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 70–100 m² in Vila Andrade, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in Vila Andrade, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 4,200 per month, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 2,999 to BRL 5,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 48.6/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 16% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,190. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
