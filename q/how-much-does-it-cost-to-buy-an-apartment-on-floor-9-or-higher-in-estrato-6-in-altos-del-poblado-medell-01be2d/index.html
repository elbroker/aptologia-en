How much does it cost to buy an apartment on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 in Altos del Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 for sale in Altos del Poblado, Medellín is COP 1,315,000,000, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,075,000,000 to COP 1,615,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 11,302,221/m² and the median listed size is 115 m². That is 57% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 760,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
