How much does it cost to rent an apartment of what size in La Ceja?

50 - 70 m²: 2,400,000 pesos per month; 70 - 90 m²: 2,800,000 pesos per month; 90 - 120 m²: 3,600,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In La Ceja, an apartment for rent from the group "50 - 70 m²" is requested in 2,400,000 pesos per month in the medium and one from the group "90 - 120 m²" in 3,600,000 pesos per month (+ 50.0%). For each square metre, the cheapest is "90 - 120 m²" (36,667 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (38,417 pesos / m² per month).
