What is the most common rental in Bogotá?

More than 7 M of pesos per month, with 25.5% of the offer.

In Bogotá, the most available rental range of apartments for rent is More than 7 M pesos per month (25.5% of the ads), with a median area of 190 m². 12.6% of the supply is requested in less than 1.5 million.
