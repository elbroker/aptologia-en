What does the study “Buy or rent according to the estrato? Years of rent worth the square meter by estrato and city in Colombia” show?

The combination of city and estrato where the square meter is paid with less years of rent in Colombia is Barranquilla, estrato 5: 10.3 years; the highest is Manizales, estrato 5, with 17.4 years. In the national average the estratos 3 to 6 are between 12.4 and 12.8 years, so within each city the neighborhood weighs more than the estrato. 19 cities have at least three measurable estratos.
