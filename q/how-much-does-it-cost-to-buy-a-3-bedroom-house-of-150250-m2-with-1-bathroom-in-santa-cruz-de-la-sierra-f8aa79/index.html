How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 150–250 m² with 1 bathroom in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² with 1 bathroom for sale in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 97,500, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 63,500 to USD 130,000. The median price per square metre is USD 567/m² and the median listed size is 180 m². That is 39% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 160,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
