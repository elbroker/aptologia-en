How much does it cost to rent a house in Florida Nueva, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Florida Nueva, Medellín is COP 5,000,000 per month, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,975,000 to COP 6,650,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 30,455/m² and the median listed size is 170 m². That is 41% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 8,500,000). The most frequent estrato is 5 and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
