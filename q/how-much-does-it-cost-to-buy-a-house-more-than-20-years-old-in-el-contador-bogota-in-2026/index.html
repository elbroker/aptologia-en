How much does it cost to buy a house more than 20 years old in El Contador, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses more than 20 years old for sale in El Contador, Bogotá is COP 1,200,000,000, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 985,000,000 to COP 1,467,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,181,347/m² and the median listed size is 235 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 230,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 97.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
