How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 2 bathrooms 6–20 years old in Suramérica, Itagüí in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms 6–20 years old for sale in Suramérica, Itagüí is COP 520,000,000, based on 91 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 460,000,000 to COP 581,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,712,538/m² and the median listed size is 77 m². That is 11% above the study's reference median for Itagüí (COP 470,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 435,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 93.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
