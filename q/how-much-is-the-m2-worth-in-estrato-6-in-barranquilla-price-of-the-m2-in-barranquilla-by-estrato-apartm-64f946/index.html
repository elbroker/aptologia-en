How much is the m² worth in estrato 6 in Barranquilla?

39,062 pesos per month for apartments.

In Barranquilla, the m² of an apartment for rent goes from 18,739 pesos per month in estrato 2 to 39,062 pesos per month in estrato 6. In estrato 3, the m² of a house is 42.2% cheaper than that of an apartment.
