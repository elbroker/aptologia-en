How much does it cost to rent an apartment in La Castellana, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in La Castellana, Barranquilla is COP 5,000,000 per month, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,775,000 to COP 6,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 47,680/m² and the median listed size is 85.5 m². That is 79% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 640,500. The most frequent estrato is 6, 96.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
