How much does it cost to rent an apartment of what size in Guayaquil?

Less than 50 m²: USD 350 per month; 50 - 70 m²: USD 525 per month; 70 - 90 m²: USD 800 per month; 90 - 120 m²: USD 850 per month; 120 - 160 m²: USD 1,000 per month; More than 160 m²: USD 1,600 per month (mid-October of 2026).

In Guayaquil, a rental department of the group "Less than 50 m²" is ordered in USD 350 per month in the median and one of the group "More than 160 m²" in USD 1,600 per month (+ 357.1%). per square metre, the cheapest is "120 - 160 m²" (USD 7.6 / m² per month) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (USD 10.0 / m² per month).
