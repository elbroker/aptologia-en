How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Aves María?

3,300,000 pesos per month in the median (October of 2026); the central half between 2,750,000 pesos and 3,800,000 pesos.

In Sabaneta, an apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 3,300,000 pesos per month in the medium in Aves María, the most expensive neighborhood, and 2,600,000 pesos per month in Calle Larga, the cheapest: 1.3 times less. We compare 11 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
