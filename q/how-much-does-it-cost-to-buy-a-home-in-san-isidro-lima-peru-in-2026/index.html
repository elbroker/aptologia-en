How much does it cost to buy a home in San Isidro, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in San Isidro, Lima, Peru is PEN 1,371,120, based on 929 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 745,000 to PEN 2,748,480. The median price per square metre is PEN 8,929/m² and the median listed size is 160 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PEN 1. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
