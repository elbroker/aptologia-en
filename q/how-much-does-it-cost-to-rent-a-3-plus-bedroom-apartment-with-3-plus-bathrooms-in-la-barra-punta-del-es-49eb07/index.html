How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 8,800 per month, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 4,625 to USD 16,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 69.2/m² and the median listed size is 150 m². That is 83% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 4,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
