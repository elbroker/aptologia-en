How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in Miraflores, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Miraflores, Lima, Peru is PEN 4,836 per month, based on 420 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 3,600 to PEN 7,202. The median monthly rent per square metre is PEN 33.6/m² and the median listed size is 150 m². That is 24% above the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 3,892). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PEN 455. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
