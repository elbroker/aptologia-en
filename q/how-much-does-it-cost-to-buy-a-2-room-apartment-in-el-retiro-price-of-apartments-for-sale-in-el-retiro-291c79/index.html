How much does it cost to buy a 2 room apartment in El Retiro?

750 million pesos in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the supply is between 600 million pesos and 980 million pesos.

In El Retiro, a 1 room apartment costs 560 million pesos in the medium, one 2 million rooms 750 million pesos (+ 33.9%) and one 3 or more 790 million pesos (+ 5.3%).
