How much is the m² worth in estrato 6 in Barranquilla?

5.09 million pesos for apartments.

In Barranquilla, the m² of an apartment for sale goes from 3.16 million pesos in estrato 2 to 5.09 million pesos in estrato 6. In estrato 3, the m² of a house is 37.7% cheaper than that of an apartment.
