How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile is UF 53.4 per month, based on 197 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 45 to UF 75. The median monthly rent per square metre is UF 0.34/m² and the median listed size is 152 m². That is 286% above the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 13.8). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
