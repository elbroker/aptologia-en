How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment 6–20 years old in Suramérica, Itagüí in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments 6–20 years old for sale in Suramérica, Itagüí is COP 534,068,000, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 479,250,000 to COP 600,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,620,512/m² and the median listed size is 79.7 m². That is 14% above the study's reference median for Itagüí (COP 470,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 450,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 91.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
