How much does it cost to buy a house with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Centro, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with parking with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Centro, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 340,000, based on 87 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 235,000 to USD 550,000. The median price per square metre is USD 892/m² and the median listed size is 351 m². That is 112% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 160,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
