What is the most expensive colony in Monterrey to rent?

Technological, with 394.4 Mexican pesos (USD 21.4) per m² per month in the medium (October of 2026).

In Monterrey, the colony with the most expensive m² to rent department is Technological (394.4 Mexican pesos (USD 21.4) / m² per month) and the cheapest San Jerónimo (315.8 Mexican pesos (USD 17.1) / m² per month), 1.2 times less. We compare 6 colonies with at least 30 ads with area.
