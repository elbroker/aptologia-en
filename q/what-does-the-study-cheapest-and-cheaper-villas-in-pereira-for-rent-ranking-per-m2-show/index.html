What does the study “Cheapest and cheaper villas in Pereira for rent: ranking per m²” show?

In Pereira, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to rent home is Cerritos (23,693 pesos / m² per month) and the cheapest Combia (19,286 pesos / m² per month), 1.2 times less. We compare 7 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
