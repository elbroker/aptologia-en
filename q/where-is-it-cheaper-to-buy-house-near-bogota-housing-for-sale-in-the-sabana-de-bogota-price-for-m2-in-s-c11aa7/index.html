Where is it cheaper to buy house near Bogotá?

Among the municipalities of the Sabana with 40 ads or more, in Soacha: $3,114,958 per m² (data of 18 October of 2026).

In the Sabana de Bogotá, the square meter of housing for sale goes from $3,114,958 in Soacha to $6,390,121 in La Calera, in front of $7,360,406 of an apartment in Bogotá. An apartment for sale in Soacha asks in the median $155 million.
