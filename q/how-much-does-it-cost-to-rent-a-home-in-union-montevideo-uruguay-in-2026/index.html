How much does it cost to rent a home in Unión, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Unión, Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 25,900 per month, based on 114 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 22,575 to UYU 35,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 482/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 4,000. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
