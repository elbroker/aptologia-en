How much does it cost to rent a house in Polo Club, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Polo Club, Bogotá is COP 10,000,000 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 7,225,000 to COP 11,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 35,714/m² and the median listed size is 280 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 10,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 400,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 68.8% of listings include parking.
