How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 100–150 m² without parking in estrato 5 in Laureles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 100–150 m² without parking in estrato 5 in Laureles, Medellín is COP 4,000,000 per month, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,100,000 to COP 5,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 32,800/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 17% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
