How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in San Isidro, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in San Isidro, Lima, Peru is PEN 1,140,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 926,718 to PEN 1,254,462. The median price per square metre is PEN 8,756/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 83% above the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 621,600). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
