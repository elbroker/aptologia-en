What does the study “Houses in rent in Monterrey by number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In Monterrey, a rental house of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 18,500 Mexican pesos (USD 1,002) per month in the median and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 47,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,545) per month (+ 154.1%). per square metre, the cheapest is "3 Mexican pesos" (133.5 Mexican pesos (USD 7.2) / m² per month) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (137.5 Mexican pesos (USD 7.4) / m² per month).
