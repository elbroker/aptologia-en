What amenity is more important for a rent?

Balcony, with 11.0% more per m².

In Bogotá, the most important amenity is a balcony: 11.0% more per m² than a comparable apartment of the same estrato and rooms. The pool (10.3%) and gym (9.3%) follow. On the other hand, visitor parking, fireplace, elevator does not raise the price per m² (between - 2.3% and - 1.4%).
