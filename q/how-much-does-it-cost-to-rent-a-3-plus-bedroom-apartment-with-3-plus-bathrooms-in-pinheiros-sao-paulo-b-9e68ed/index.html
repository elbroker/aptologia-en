How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Pinheiros, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Pinheiros, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 10,850 per month, based on 62 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 7,550 to BRL 16,785. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 77.7/m² and the median listed size is 144 m². That is 200% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 2,912. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
