How much does it cost to buy a home in Chorrillos, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Chorrillos, Lima, Peru is USD 335,000, based on 162 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 164,548 to USD 750,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,730/m² and the median listed size is 245 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
