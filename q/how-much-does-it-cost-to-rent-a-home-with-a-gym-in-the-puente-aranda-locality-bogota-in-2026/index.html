How much does it cost to rent a home with a gym in the Puente Aranda locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes with a gym in the Puente Aranda locality, Bogotá is COP 2,250,000 per month, based on 73 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,700,000 to COP 3,585,700. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,150/m² and the median listed size is 41 m². That is 25% above the study's reference median (COP 1,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 228,150. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 45.2% of listings include parking.
