How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 40,000 per month, based on 312 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 33,000 to UYU 50,700. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 494/m² and the median listed size is 83 m². That is 33% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 30,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 7,200. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
