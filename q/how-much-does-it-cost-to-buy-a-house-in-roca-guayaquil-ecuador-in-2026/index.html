How much does it cost to buy a house in Roca, Guayaquil, Ecuador in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Roca, Guayaquil, Ecuador is USD 234,500, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 139,889 to USD 433,250. That is 7% above the study's reference median for Guayaquil (USD 220,000). The median number of bathrooms is 4.5.
