What does the study “How much the price of square meter varies within each city of Colombia: dispersion in 37 cities” show?

In Santa Marta the square meter of the typical expensive apartment (percentile 75) costs 1.93 times that of the typical cheap (percentile 25), the largest dispersion of 37 Colombian cities; in Tocancipá the relationship is only 1.18. In Bogotá the range ranges from 5.81 million pesos to 9.19 million pesos per m² (1.58 times). A high dispersion indicates that the neighborhood and estrato weigh more on the price.
