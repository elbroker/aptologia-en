How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 2 bathrooms in Las Palmas, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Las Palmas, Medellín is COP 770,000,000, based on 193 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 660,000,000 to COP 940,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,183,673/m² and the median listed size is 79 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 500,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
