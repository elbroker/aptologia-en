What does the study “Profitability of estrato rental in Colombia: on which estrato you give more to buy to rent” show?

In Colombia, buying an apartment to rent it yields more in estrato 1 (9.5% gross annual) and less in estrato 2 (7.4%). In estrato 6 is 8.1% and in strate 3, 7.8%.
