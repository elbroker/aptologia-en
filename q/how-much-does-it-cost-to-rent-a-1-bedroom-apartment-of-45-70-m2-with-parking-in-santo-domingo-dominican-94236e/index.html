How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with parking in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,192 per month, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 950 to USD 1,300. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 19.5/m² and the median listed size is 62 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
