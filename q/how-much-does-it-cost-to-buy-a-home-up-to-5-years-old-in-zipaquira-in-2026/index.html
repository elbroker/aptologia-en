How much does it cost to buy a home up to 5 years old in Zipaquirá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes up to 5 years old for sale in Zipaquirá is COP 247,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 222,000,000 to COP 279,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,346,667/m² and the median listed size is 59 m². That is 10% above the study's reference median (COP 225,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 184,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 57.6% of listings include parking.
