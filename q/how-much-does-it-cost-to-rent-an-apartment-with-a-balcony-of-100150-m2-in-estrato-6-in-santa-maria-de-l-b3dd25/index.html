How much does it cost to rent an apartment with a balcony of 100–150 m² in estrato 6 in Santa María de los Ángeles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony of 100–150 m² in estrato 6 in Santa María de los Ángeles, Medellín is COP 6,400,000 per month, based on 123 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,500,000 to COP 7,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 53,391/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 33% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). 97.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
