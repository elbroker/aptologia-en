How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Las Vegas, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Las Vegas, Envigado is COP 695,000,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 670,327,500 to COP 747,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,625,000/m² and the median listed size is 67 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 750,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 97.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
