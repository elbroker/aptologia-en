How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with parking in Miraflores, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking in Miraflores, Lima, Peru is PEN 2,600 per month, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 2,400 to PEN 2,968. The median monthly rent per square metre is PEN 44.8/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 15% below the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 3,050). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
