How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² for sale in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 587,500, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 450,000 to USD 871,250. The median listed size is 128 m². That is 63% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
