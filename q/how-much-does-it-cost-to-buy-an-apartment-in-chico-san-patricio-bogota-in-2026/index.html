How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Chico San Patricio, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Chico San Patricio, Bogotá is COP 1,095,000,000, based on 73 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 710,000,000 to COP 1,448,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,383,838/m² and the median listed size is 137 m². That is 43% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,200,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 98.6% of listings include parking.
