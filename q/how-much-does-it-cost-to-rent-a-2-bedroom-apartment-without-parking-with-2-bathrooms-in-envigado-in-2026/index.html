How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment without parking with 2 bathrooms in Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments without parking with 2 bathrooms in Envigado is COP 4,000,000 per month, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,175,000 to COP 4,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 53,798/m² and the median listed size is 74 m². That is 9% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 4,400,000). The most frequent estrato is 5 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
