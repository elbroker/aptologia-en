How much does it cost to buy a department of what size in Mexico City?

Less than 50 m²: 2,713,884 Mexican pesos (USD 146,971); 50 - 70 m²: 3,295,000 Mexican pesos (USD 178,441); 70 - 90 m²: 4,345,000 Mexican pesos (USD 235,304); 90 - 120 m²: 5,584,000 Mexican pesos (USD 302,403; 120 - 160 m²: 7,350,000 Mexican pesos (USD 398,041); More than 160 m²: 10,700,000 Mexican pesos (USD 579,461) (mid-October of 2026).

In Mexico City, a department for sale of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 2,713,884 Mexican pesos (USD 146,971) in the median and one of the group "More than 160 m²" in 10,700,000 Mexican pesos (USD 579,461) (+ 294.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (48,611 Mexican pesos (USD 2,633) / m²) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (67,398 Mexican pesos (USD 3,650) / m²).
