How much does it cost to rent an apartment with how many bathrooms in Bucharest?

1 bath: 1,200,000 pesos per month; 2 bathrooms: 2,300,000 pesos per month; 3 bathrooms: 3,073,000 pesos per month; 4 or more bathrooms: 5,700,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Bucharest, an apartment for rent from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 1,200,000 pesos per month in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 5,700,000 pesos per month (+ 375.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "1 bath" (22,887 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (32,481 pesos / m² per month).
