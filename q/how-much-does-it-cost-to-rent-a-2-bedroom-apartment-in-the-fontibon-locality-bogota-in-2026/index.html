How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá is COP 1,650,000 per month, based on 281 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,400,000 to COP 2,100,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 38,462/m² and the median listed size is 45 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median (COP 1,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 201,100. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 24.6% of listings include parking.
