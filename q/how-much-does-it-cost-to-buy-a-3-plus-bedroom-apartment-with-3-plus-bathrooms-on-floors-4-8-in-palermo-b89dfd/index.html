How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms on floors 4–8 in Palermo, Manizales in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms on floors 4–8 for sale in Palermo, Manizales is COP 660,000,000, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 522,500,000 to COP 787,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,318,182/m² and the median listed size is 165 m². That is 47% above the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 435,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 98% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
