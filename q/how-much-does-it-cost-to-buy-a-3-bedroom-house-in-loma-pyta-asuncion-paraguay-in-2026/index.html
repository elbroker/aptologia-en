How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house in Loma Pytá, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses for sale in Loma Pytá, Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 1,000,000,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 876,500,000 to PYG 1,300,000,000. The median price per square metre is PYG 5,028,249/m² and the median listed size is 180 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (PYG 1,000,000,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
