How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment with parking in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments with parking in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,680 per month, based on 77 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,400 to USD 2,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 13.6/m² and the median listed size is 124 m². That is 24% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
