How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 45–70 m² in Villa Urquiza, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in Villa Urquiza, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is ARS 1,250,000 per month, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from ARS 995,000 to ARS 1,545,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is ARS 21,493/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 47% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (ARS 850,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
