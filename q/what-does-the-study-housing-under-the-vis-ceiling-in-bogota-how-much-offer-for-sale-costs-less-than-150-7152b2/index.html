What does the study “Housing under the VIS ceiling in Bogotá: how much offer for sale costs less than 150 minimum wages” show?

Only 9.6% of the used and new housing published for sale in Bogotá calls for a price equal to or less than the VIS ceiling of 2026 ($263 million, 150 minimum wages); under the VIP ceiling ($158 million) is 0.7%. The most available location at that price is Bosa, where 66.1% of the ads are below the top.
