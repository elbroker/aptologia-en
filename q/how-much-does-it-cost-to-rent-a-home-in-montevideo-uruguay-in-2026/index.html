How much does it cost to rent a home in Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 2,300 per month, based on 1,067 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,500 to USD 3,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 19.6/m² and the median listed size is 122 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 493. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
