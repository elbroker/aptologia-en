How much does it cost to rent a house with how many bathrooms in Montevideo?

1 bath: 27,850 Uruguayan pesos (USD 693) per month; 2 bath: 60,000 Uruguayan pesos (USD 1,492) per month; 3 bath: 90,000 Uruguayan pesos (USD 2,239) per month (medium October 2026).
