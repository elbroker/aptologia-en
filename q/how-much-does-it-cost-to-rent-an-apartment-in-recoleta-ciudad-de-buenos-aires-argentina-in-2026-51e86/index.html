How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Recoleta, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Recoleta, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 1,800 per month, based on 147 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,200 to USD 2,850. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 16.7/m² and the median listed size is 115 m². That is 11% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 1,625). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
