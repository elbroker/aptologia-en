What does the study “Price of apartments for sale in Quito per number of bedrooms: how much each size costs” show?

In Quito, a 1 bedroom department costs USD 122,500 in the median, one 2 USD 139,000 bedroom (+ 13.5%) and one 3 or more USD 155,000 (+ 11.5%).
