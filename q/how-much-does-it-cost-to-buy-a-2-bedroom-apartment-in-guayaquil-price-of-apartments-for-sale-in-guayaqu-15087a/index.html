How much does it cost to buy a 2 bedroom apartment in Guayaquil?

USD 155,250 in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between USD 120,000 and USD 190,000.

In Guayaquil, a 1 bedroom department costs USD 118,000 in the median, one 2 USD 155,250 bedroom (+ 31.6%) and one 3 or more USD 180,000 (+ 15.9%).
