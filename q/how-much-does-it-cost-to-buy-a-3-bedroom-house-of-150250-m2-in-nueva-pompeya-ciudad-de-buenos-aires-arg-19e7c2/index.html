How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 150–250 m² in Nueva Pompeya, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² for sale in Nueva Pompeya, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 140,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 120,000 to USD 198,000. The median price per square metre is USD 760/m² and the median listed size is 197 m². That is 52% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 290,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
