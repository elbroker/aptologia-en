How many years of rent does an apartment in Medellín cost?

In the middle of the neighborhoods, 15.4 years of rent ordered.

In Medellín, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical neighborhood 6.5% gross per year: the price ordered is equivalent to 15.4 years of rent. The area with the highest gross return is La Immaculate (15.3%) and the area of the minor St. Helena (2.8%), between 229 neighborhoods with 30 ads or more for rent and sale.
