Where is it cheaper to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in Buenos Aires City?

In Villa Lugano: 662,500 Argentine pesos (USD 437) per month in the medium.

In Buenos Aires City, a 2 bedroom apartment is rented for 1,550,000 Argentine pesos (USD 1,023) per month in the middle of Villa Devoto, the most expensive neighborhood, and 662,500 Argentine pesos (USD 437) per month in Villa Lugano, the cheapest: 2.3 times less. We compare 14 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 bedrooms.
