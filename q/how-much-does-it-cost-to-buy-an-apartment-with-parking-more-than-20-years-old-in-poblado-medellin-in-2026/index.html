How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking more than 20 years old in Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking more than 20 years old for sale in Poblado, Medellín is COP 1,100,000,000, based on 223 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 800,000,000 to COP 1,485,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,222,222/m² and the median listed size is 157 m². That is 31% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 942,400. The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
