How much does it cost to rent a house of 100–150 m² in Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses of 100–150 m² in Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 28,000 per month, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 20,750 to MXN 35,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 228/m² and the median listed size is 128 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
