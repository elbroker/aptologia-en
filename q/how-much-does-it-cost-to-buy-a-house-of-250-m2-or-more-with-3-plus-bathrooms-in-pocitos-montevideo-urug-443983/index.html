How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more with 3+ bathrooms in Pocitos, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Pocitos, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 850,000, based on 63 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 650,000 to USD 1,175,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,156/m² and the median listed size is 366 m². That is 216% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 269,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
