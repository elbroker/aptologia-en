How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 6,000 per month, based on 95 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 3,000 to USD 9,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 55/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 25% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 4,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
