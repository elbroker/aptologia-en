How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in Colina Campestre, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in Colina Campestre, Bogotá is COP 370,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 317,500,000 to COP 459,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,375,000/m² and the median listed size is 39 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 420,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 233,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 54.8% of listings include parking.
