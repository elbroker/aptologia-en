How much does it cost to buy a 4+ bedroom house in Kennedy Central, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in Kennedy Central, Bogotá is COP 700,000,000, based on 110 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 580,000,000 to COP 883,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 2,772,817/m² and the median listed size is 240 m². That is 44% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,250,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 120,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 62.7% of listings include parking.
