How much does it cost to rent an apartment with a balcony of 45–70 m² in Loma de los Bernal, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony of 45–70 m² in Loma de los Bernal, Medellín is COP 2,800,000 per month, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,700,000 to COP 3,100,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 46,429/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 42% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 88.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
