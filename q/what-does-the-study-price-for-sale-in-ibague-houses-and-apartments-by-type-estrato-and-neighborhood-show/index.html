What does the study “Price for sale in Ibagué: houses and apartments by type, estrato and neighborhood” show?

A house for sale in Ibagué asks in the median $270 million ($3,150,000 per m²); an apartment, $205 million, and a house, $550 million. Buy an apartment to rent yields in rough about 6.8% per year.
