How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms in Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms in Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 9,000,000 per month, based on 119 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 6,000,000 to PYG 12,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is PYG 25,487/m² and the median listed size is 360 m². That is 6% above the study's reference median for Asunción (PYG 8,500,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
