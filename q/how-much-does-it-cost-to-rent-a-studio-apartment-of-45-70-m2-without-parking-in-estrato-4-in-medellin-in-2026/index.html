How much does it cost to rent a studio apartment of 45–70 m² without parking in estrato 4 in Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for studio apartments of 45–70 m² without parking in estrato 4 in Medellín is COP 2,225,000 per month, based on 64 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,887,500 to COP 2,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 42,222/m² and the median listed size is 50 m². That is 21% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 2,800,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
