How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Barranquilla?

1,925,555 pesos per month in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between 1,500,000 pesos and 2,953,750 pesos.

In Barranquilla, a 1 room apartment is rented for 1,900,000 pesos per month in the medium, one of 2 rooms 1,925,555 pesos (+ 1.3%) and one of 3 or more 3,200,000 pesos (+ 66.2%).
