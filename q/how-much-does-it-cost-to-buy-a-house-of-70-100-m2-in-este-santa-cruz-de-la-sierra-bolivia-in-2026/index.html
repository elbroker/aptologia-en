How much does it cost to buy a house of 70–100 m² in Este, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 70–100 m² for sale in Este, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 55,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 49,000 to USD 64,775. The median price per square metre is USD 600/m² and the median listed size is 89.5 m². That is 66% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 160,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.5.
