How much is a parking lot worth in Bogotá?

An apartment with 1 parking lot requests in the median $40 million more than one comparable without it (same estrato, locality, rooms and size), with data from 18 October of 2026.

In Bogotá, an apartment for sale with a parking lot asks in the median $40 million more than a comparable one without a parking lot, and one with two parking lots asks for $105 million more than a comparable one. For rent, having a parking lot increases the rent by $200,000 a month in front of an equal apartment without it.
