How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² in Miramar, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Miramar, Barranquilla is COP 2,450,000 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,000,000 to COP 3,290,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 31,209/m² and the median listed size is 82.5 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 412,400. The most frequent estrato is 4, 95.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
