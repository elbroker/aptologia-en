How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 2 bathrooms in estrato 6 in Chicó Norte, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms in estrato 6 for sale in Chicó Norte, Bogotá is COP 840,000,000, based on 195 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 680,000,000 to COP 1,050,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,455,488/m² and the median listed size is 81.5 m². That is 12% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 883,000. 99.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
