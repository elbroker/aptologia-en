What is the most common price range in Envigado?

500 - 800 M of pesos, with 35.6% of the ads.

In Envigado, the price range with the most offer of apartments for sale is 500 - 800 M of pesos (35.6% of the ads), with a median area of 81 m². 1.7% of the supply costs less than 350 million.
