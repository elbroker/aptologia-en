How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house with 3+ bathrooms in Guadalajara, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms in Guadalajara, Mexico is MXN 55,000 per month, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 42,000 to MXN 77,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 142/m² and the median listed size is 394 m². That is 29% above the study's reference median for Guadalajara (MXN 42,500). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
