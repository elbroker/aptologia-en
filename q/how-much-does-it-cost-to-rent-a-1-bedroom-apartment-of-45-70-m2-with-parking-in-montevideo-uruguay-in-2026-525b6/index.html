How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with parking in Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking in Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 32,500 per month, based on 239 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 29,500 to UYU 39,250. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 650/m² and the median listed size is 50 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 30,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 5,000. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
