How much better is an apartment of 3 rooms than one of 2?

On average, + 20.9% in the same city and estrato (October of 2026).

In Colombia, moving from an apartment from 2 to one of 3 rooms for sale increases the price ordered + 20.9% within the same city and estrato; the greatest difference is in Bogotá (+ 33.4%) and the least in Piedecuesta (- 9.5%). From 1 to 2 rooms: + 27.8%. From 3 to 4 rooms: + 26.8%.
