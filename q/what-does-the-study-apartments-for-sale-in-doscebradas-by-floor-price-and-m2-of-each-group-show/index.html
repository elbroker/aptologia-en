What does the study “Apartments for sale in Doscebradas by floor: price and m² of each group” show?

In Doscebradas, an apartment for sale of the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 250 million pesos in the median and one of the group "Piso 11 - 20" in 275 million pesos (+ 10.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 3 - 5" (4.44 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 6 - 10" (4.81 million pesos / m²).
