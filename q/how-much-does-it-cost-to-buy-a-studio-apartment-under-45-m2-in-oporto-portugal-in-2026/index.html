How much does it cost to buy a studio apartment under 45 m² in Oporto, Portugal in 2026?

The median asking price for studio apartments under 45 m² for sale in Oporto, Portugal is EUR 218,990, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 199,500 to EUR 237,500. The median price per square metre is EUR 5,833/m² and the median listed size is 38 m². 21.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
