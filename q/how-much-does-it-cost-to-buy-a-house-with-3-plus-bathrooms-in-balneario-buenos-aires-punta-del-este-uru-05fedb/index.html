How much does it cost to buy a house with 3+ bathrooms in Balneario Buenos Aires, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Balneario Buenos Aires, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 300,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 270,000 to USD 377,500. That is 57% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
