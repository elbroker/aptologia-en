How much does it cost to buy an apartment of how old in Rionegro?

For starring (0 years): 540 million pesos; 1 to 5 years: 525 million pesos; 6 to 10 years: 495 million pesos; 11 to 20 years: 449 million pesos; 21 to 30 years: 355 million pesos (medium of October of 2026).

In Rionegro, an apartment for sale from the group "To premiere (0 years)" is requested in 540 million pesos in the median and one from the group "21 to 30 years" in 355 million pesos (- 34.2%). For each square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (5.53 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "To premiere (0 years)" (7.83 million pesos / m²).
