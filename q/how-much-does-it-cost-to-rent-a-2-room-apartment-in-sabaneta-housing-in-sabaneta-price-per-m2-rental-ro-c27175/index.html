How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Sabaneta?

2.90 million pesos per month in the medium (October of 2026).

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter of Sabaneta is Alto de las Flores, with 7.82 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is María Apoñadora, with 5.50 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 2.90 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 511 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 6.9% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 11.5% of the rent (380,000 pesos per month in the median).
