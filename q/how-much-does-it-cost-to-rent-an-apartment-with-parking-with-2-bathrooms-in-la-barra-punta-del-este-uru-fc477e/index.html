How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking with 2 bathrooms in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 6,700 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 3,638 to USD 9,300. That is 40% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 4,800). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
