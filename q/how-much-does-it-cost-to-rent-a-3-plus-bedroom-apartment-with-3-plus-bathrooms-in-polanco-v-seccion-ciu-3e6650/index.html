How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Polanco V Sección, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Polanco V Sección, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 85,000 per month, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 65,000 to MXN 96,250. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 321/m² and the median listed size is 249 m². That is 166% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 32,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
