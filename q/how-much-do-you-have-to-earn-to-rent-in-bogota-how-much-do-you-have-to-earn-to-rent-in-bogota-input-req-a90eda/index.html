How much do you have to earn to rent in Bogotá?

For a 2 medium rooms, $7,000,000 per month (4.0 minimum wages), if the rent with HOA fee does not exceed 30% of the income, cut to 13 October of 2026.

To rent a medium apartment of 2 rooms in Bogotá without spending more than 30% of the home income, a household needs to earn $7,000,000 per month: 4.0 minimum wages of 2026. With 2 minimum wages ($3,501,810) it only reaches 2.0% of the apartments for rent; in Usme it is enough 1.9 wages and in Chico Alto it is necessary 36.1.
