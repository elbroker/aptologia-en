Where does he pay the least?

In Extended State of Hidalgo: 5.3% gross per year (18.9 years of rent).

In Mexico City, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical colony 6.1% gross per year: the price requested is equivalent to 16.3 years of rent. The colony with the highest gross return is San Pedro de los Pinos (8.5%) and the lower one, the extension State of Hidalgo (5.3%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 7.7%.
