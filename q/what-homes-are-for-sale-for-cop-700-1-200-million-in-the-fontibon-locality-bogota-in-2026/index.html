What homes are for sale for COP 700–1,200 million in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for COP 700–1,200 million in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 870,000,000, based on 372 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 784,512,874 to COP 980,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,591,783/m² and the median listed size is 158 m². That is 64% above the study's reference median (COP 530,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 480,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 90.9% of listings include parking.
