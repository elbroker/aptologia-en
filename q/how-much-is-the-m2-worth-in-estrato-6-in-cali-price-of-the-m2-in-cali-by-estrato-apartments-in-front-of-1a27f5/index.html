How much is the m² worth in estrato 6 in Cali?

41,735 pesos per month for apartments.

In Cali, the m² of an apartment for rent goes from 21,743 pesos per month in estrato 3 to 41,735 pesos per month in estrato 6. In estrato 5, the m² of a house is 39.5% cheaper than that of an apartment.
