How much does it cost to rent a house of 250 m² or more in Carrasco, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses of 250 m² or more in Carrasco, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 6,000 per month, based on 75 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 4,500 to USD 7,900. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 14.9/m² and the median listed size is 360 m². That is 36% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 4,400). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
