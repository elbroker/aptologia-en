How much does an apartment in Medellín cost?

In the typical neighborhood (median between 268 neighborhoods with 30 ads or more) the price ordered is $562 million; a 2 bedroom, $530 million, with data from 5 October of 2026.

In Medellín, the typical neighborhood asks $562 million for an apartment for sale (medium between 268 neighborhoods with 30 ads or more); a 2 bedroom, $530 million. The most expensive neighborhood is La Calera ($2.25 billion) and the cheapest San Antonio ($190 million).
