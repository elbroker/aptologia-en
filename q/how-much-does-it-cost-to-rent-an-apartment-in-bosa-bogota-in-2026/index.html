How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Bosa, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Bosa, Bogotá is COP 1,000,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 900,000 to COP 1,175,000. That is 64% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,750,000).
