How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more in Altos de Riomar, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more for sale in Altos de Riomar, Barranquilla is COP 2,300,000,000, based on 101 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,500,000,000 to COP 3,500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,268,293/m² and the median listed size is 522 m². That is 156% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 900,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
