How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with parking in Mariscal Estigarribia, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with parking for sale in Mariscal Estigarribia, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 692,500, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 447,132 to USD 887,500. The median price per square metre is USD 1,288/m² and the median listed size is 438 m². That is 98% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 350,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
