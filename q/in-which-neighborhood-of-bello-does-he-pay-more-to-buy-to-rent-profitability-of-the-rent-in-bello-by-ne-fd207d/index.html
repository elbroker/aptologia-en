In which neighborhood of Bello does he pay more to buy to rent?

In La Gabriela, with 8.3% gross per year (12.1 years of rent), according to October announcements of 2026.

In Bello, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical neighborhood 7.1% gross per year: the price requested is equivalent to 14.1 years of rent. The area with the highest gross return is La Gabriela (8.3%) and the smaller district, Santa Ana (6.7%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 7.3%.
