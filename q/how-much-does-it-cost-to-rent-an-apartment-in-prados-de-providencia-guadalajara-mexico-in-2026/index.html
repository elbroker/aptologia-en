How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Prados de Providencia, Guadalajara, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Prados de Providencia, Guadalajara, Mexico is MXN 26,900 per month, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 22,000 to MXN 32,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 261/m² and the median listed size is 100 m². That is 17% above the study's reference median for Guadalajara (MXN 23,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
