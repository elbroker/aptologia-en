Where is it cheaper to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in Asunción?

In Jara: 5,000,000 Guaraní (USD 876) per month in the median.

In Asunción, a 2 bedroom apartment is rented for 6,600,000 Guaraní (USD 1,157) per month in the middle of Villa Morra, the most expensive neighborhood, and 5,000,000 Guaraní (USD 876) per month in Jara, the cheapest: 1.3 times less. We compare 11 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 bedrooms.
