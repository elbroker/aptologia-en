How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 220,000, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 200,000 to USD 280,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,159/m² and the median listed size is 81.5 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
