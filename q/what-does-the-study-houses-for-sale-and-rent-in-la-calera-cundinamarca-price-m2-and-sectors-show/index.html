What does the study “Houses for sale and rent in La Calera (Cundinamarca): price, m² and sectors” show?

In La Calera the offer is mainly of houses: a house for sale is requested in the median 2,200 million pesos, with 360 m² of area and 6.05 million pesos per m²; rent a house costs 12,000,000 pesos per month. The most expensive sector of the sample is Conjunta Casa de Campo, with 2,900 million pesos per house. Of 1,834 ads, 88.7% are houses.
