How much does it cost to buy a home in Tintala, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Tintala, Bogotá is COP 236,500,000, based on 72 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 205,000,000 to COP 350,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,823,864/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 73% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 135,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 38.9% of listings include parking.
