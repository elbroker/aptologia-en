How much does it cost to rent an apartment on floors 1–3 in Mesa, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments on floors 1–3 in Mesa, Envigado is COP 3,600,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,650,000 to COP 4,275,000. The median listed size is 110 m². That is 18% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 4,400,000). The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.5.
