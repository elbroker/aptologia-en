How much does it cost to buy a home in La Carolina, Quito, Ecuador in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in La Carolina, Quito, Ecuador is USD 179,500, based on 225 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 130,000 to USD 239,800. The median price per square metre is USD 1,711/m² and the median listed size is 105 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
