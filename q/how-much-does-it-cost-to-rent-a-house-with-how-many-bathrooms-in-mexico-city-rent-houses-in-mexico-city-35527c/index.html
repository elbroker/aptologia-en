How much does it cost to rent a house with how many bathrooms in Mexico City?

1 bath: 22,700 Mexican pesos (USD 1,229) per month; 2 bath: 40,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,166) per month; 3 bath: 75,000 Mexican pesos (USD 4,062) per month; 4 or more bath: 120,000 Mexican pesos (USD 6,499) per month (medium October 2026).

In Mexico City, a rental house of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 22,700 Mexican pesos (USD 1,229) per month in the median and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 120,000 Mexican pesos (USD 6,499) per month (+ 428.6%). per square metre, the cheapest is "2 Mexican pesos" (193.7 Mexican pesos (USD 10.5) / m² per month) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (211.8 Mexican pesos (USD 11.5) / m² per month).
