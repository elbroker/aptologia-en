How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is PEN 5,000 per month, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 4,090 to PEN 6,256. The median monthly rent per square metre is PEN 24.4/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 64% above the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 3,050). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
