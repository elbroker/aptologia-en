How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in Alameda, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Alameda, Bogotá is COP 3,399,500 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 54% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 7,400,000).
