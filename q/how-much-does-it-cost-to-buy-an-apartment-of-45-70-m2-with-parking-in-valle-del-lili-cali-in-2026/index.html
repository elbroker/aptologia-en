How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 45–70 m² with parking in Valle del Lili, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² with parking for sale in Valle del Lili, Cali is COP 270,000,000, based on 196 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 235,000,000 to COP 300,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,461,538/m² and the median listed size is 62 m². That is 37% below the study's reference median for Cali (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 296,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
