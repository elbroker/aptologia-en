In which district of Bogotá is the HOA fee more expensive?

In El Virrey: 10,897 pesos per m² per month.

In Bogotá, the most expensive HOA fee per square metre is paid in El Virrey: 10,897 pesos per m² per month in the median (1,433,000 fee pesos). The cheapest, in Tierra Buena, with 2,562 pesos per m². The median rate for the whole city is 750,000 pesos per month (7,026 pesos per m²).
