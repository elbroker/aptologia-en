How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Monte Castro, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Monte Castro, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 149,500, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 130,175 to USD 173,500. The median price per square metre is USD 2,564/m² and the median listed size is 61 m². That is 9% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
