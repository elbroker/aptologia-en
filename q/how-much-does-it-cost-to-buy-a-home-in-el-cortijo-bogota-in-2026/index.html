How much does it cost to buy a home in El Cortijo, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in El Cortijo, Bogotá is COP 320,000,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 301,250,000 to COP 367,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,242,347/m² and the median listed size is 65 m². That is 63% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 260,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 78.9% of listings include parking.
