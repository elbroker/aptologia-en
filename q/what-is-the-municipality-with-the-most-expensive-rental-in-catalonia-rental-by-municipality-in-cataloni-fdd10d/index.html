What is the municipality with the most expensive rental in Catalonia?

Sant Vicenç de Montalt, with 1,637 euros per month of average income in 2025.

The highest average rental income in Catalonia in 2025 was paid in Sant Vicenç de Montalt (Barcelona), with 1,637 euros per month, and the lowest of 289 municipalities with 30 contracts or more in La Senia (Tarragona), with 345 euros. In Barcelona, the municipality with more contracts (30,789), the average income was 1,135 euros per month.
