What is Pereira's most expensive neighborhood to buy?

Villa Verde, with 5.17 million pesos per m² in the medium (October of 2026).

In Pereira, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy house is Villa Verde (5.17 million pesos / m²) and the cheapest Centre (3.42 million pesos / m²), 1.5 times less. We compare 23 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
