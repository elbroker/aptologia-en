How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,185 per month, based on 468 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 950 to USD 1,350. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 14.5/m² and the median listed size is 78 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
