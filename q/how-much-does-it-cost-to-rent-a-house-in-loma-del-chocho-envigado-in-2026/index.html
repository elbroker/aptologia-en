How much does it cost to rent a house in Loma del Chocho, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Loma del Chocho, Envigado is COP 12,500,000 per month, based on 146 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 10,050,000 to COP 16,950,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 44,865/m² and the median listed size is 295 m². That is 6% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 11,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 99.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
