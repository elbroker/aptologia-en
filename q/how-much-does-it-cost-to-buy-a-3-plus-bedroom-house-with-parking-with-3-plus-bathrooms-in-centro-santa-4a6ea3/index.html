How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Centro, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with parking with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Centro, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 348,500, based on 84 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 240,000 to USD 550,000. The median price per square metre is USD 857/m² and the median listed size is 356 m². That is 118% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 160,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
