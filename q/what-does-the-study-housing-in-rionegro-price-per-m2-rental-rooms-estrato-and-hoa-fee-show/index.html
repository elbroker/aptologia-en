What does the study “Housing in Rionegro: price per m², rental, rooms, estrato and HOA fee” show?

The sector with the most expensive square meter of Rionegro is Llanogrande, with 11.11 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Cimarronas, with 5.73 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 2.60 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 500 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 6.1% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 12.0% of the rent (335,000 pesos per month in the median).
