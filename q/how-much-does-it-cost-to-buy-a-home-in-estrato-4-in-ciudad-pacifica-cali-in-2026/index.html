How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 4 in Ciudad Pacífica, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 4 for sale in Ciudad Pacífica, Cali is COP 235,000,000, based on 186 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 215,750,000 to COP 275,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,980,162/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 240,000. 61.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
