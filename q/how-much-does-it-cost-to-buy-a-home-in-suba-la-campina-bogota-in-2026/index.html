How much does it cost to buy a home in Suba la Campiña, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Suba la Campiña, Bogotá is COP 345,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 280,000,000 to COP 482,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,355,130/m² and the median listed size is 68 m². That is 60% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 310,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 67.7% of listings include parking.
