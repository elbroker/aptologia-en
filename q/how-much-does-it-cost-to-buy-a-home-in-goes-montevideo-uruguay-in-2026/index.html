How much does it cost to buy a home in Goes, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Goes, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 149,250, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 124,175 to USD 197,250. The median price per square metre is USD 1,390/m² and the median listed size is 107 m². The median number of bathrooms is 1.
