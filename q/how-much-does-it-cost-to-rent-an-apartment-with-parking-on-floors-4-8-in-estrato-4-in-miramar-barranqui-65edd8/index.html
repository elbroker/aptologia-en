How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking on floors 4–8 in estrato 4 in Miramar, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking on floors 4–8 in estrato 4 in Miramar, Barranquilla is COP 2,354,000 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,950,000 to COP 3,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 32,895/m² and the median listed size is 64 m². That is 16% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
