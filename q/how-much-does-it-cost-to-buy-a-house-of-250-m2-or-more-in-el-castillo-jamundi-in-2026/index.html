How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more in El Castillo, Jamundí in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more for sale in El Castillo, Jamundí is COP 750,000,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 650,000,000 to COP 912,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 2,376,944/m² and the median listed size is 308 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 548,500. The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
