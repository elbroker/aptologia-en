How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 2 in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 2 for sale in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá is COP 285,000,000, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 205,000,000 to COP 427,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,000,000/m² and the median listed size is 71.1 m². That is 37% below the study's reference median (COP 454,982,760). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 170,000. 22.2% of listings include parking.
