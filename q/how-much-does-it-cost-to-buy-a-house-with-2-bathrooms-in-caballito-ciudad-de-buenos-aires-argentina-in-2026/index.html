How much does it cost to buy a house with 2 bathrooms in Caballito, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with 2 bathrooms for sale in Caballito, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 327,500, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 268,500 to USD 406,250. The median price per square metre is USD 1,778/m² and the median listed size is 170 m². That is 13% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 290,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
