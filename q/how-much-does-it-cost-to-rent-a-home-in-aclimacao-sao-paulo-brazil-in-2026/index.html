How much does it cost to rent a home in Aclimação, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Aclimação, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 3,000 per month, based on 79 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 2,465 to BRL 4,600. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 61.8/m² and the median listed size is 47 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 850. 81.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
