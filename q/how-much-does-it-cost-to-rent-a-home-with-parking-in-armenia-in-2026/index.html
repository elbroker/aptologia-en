How much does it cost to rent a home with parking in Armenia in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes with parking in Armenia is COP 2,500,000 per month, based on 55 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,875,000 to COP 3,422,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 30,909/m² and the median listed size is 72.6 m². That is 25% above the study's reference median (COP 2,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 288,000. The most frequent estrato is 5.
