How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Chico Virrey, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Chico Virrey, Bogotá is COP 8,000,000 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 6,400,000 to COP 10,750,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 71,667/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 129% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,450,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
