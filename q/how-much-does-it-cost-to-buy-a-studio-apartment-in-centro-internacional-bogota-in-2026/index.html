How much does it cost to buy a studio apartment in Centro Internacional, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for studio apartments for sale in Centro Internacional, Bogotá is COP 338,000,000, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 303,750,000 to COP 420,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,984,831/m² and the median listed size is 32.8 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 338,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 306,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 22.9% of listings include parking.
