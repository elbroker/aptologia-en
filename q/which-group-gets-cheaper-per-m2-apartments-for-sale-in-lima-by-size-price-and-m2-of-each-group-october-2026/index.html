Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (7,000 soles (USD 2,037) / m²) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (8,050 soles (USD 2,343) / m²).

In Lima, a department for sale of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 332,429 soles (USD 96,762) in the median and one of the group "More 160 m²" in 1,572,500 soles (USD 457,718) (+ 373.0%).
