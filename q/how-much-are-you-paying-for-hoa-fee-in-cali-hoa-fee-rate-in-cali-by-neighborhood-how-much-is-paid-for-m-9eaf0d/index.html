How much are you paying for HOA fee in Cali?

The median rate is 480,000 pesos per month (5,311 pesos per m²), according to October 2026 announcements.

In Cali, the most expensive HOA fee per square metre is paid in La Arboleda: 8,824 pesos per m² per month in the median (1,600,500 fee pesos). The cheapest, in Ciudad Guabinas, with 2,543 pesos per m². The median rate for the whole city is 480,000 pesos per month (5,311 pesos per m²).
