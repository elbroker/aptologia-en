How much does it cost to rent a house with a balcony of 250 m² or more in El Porvenir, Rionegro in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses with a balcony of 250 m² or more in El Porvenir, Rionegro is COP 14,600,000 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 11,750,000 to COP 19,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 38,091/m² and the median listed size is 370 m². That is 39% above the study's reference median for Rionegro (COP 10,500,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
