How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Ciudad Montes, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Ciudad Montes, Bogotá is COP 270,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 248,650,000 to COP 363,500,000. That is 59% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 660,000,000).
