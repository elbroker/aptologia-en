How much does it cost to buy a home on floors 1–3 in the La Candelaria locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes on floors 1–3 for sale in the La Candelaria locality, Bogotá is COP 570,000,000, based on 63 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 410,000,000 to COP 1,650,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,926,829/m² and the median listed size is 70 m². That is 1% below the study's reference median (COP 575,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 446,600. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 49.2% of listings include parking.
