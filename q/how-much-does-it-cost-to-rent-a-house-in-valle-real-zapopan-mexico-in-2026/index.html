How much does it cost to rent a house in Valle Real, Zapopan, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Valle Real, Zapopan, Mexico is MXN 63,500 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 32,068 to MXN 75,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 176/m² and the median listed size is 355 m². That is 112% above the study's reference median for Zapopan (MXN 30,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
