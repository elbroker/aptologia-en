And one of 3 or more bedrooms?

In Santa Cruz de la Sierra, a 1 bedroom department costs USD 70,000 in the median, one 2 in the middle of the room 105,000 (+ 50.0%) and one 3 or more USD 145,000 (+ 38.1%).
