How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 2 bathrooms in Santa María de los Ángeles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 2 bathrooms in Santa María de los Ángeles, Medellín is COP 4,800,000 per month, based on 158 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,700,000 to COP 5,650,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 52,899/m² and the median listed size is 85 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
