How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom house of 100–150 m² with 1 bathroom in São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom houses of 100–150 m² with 1 bathroom in São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 2,800 per month, based on 71 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 2,350 to BRL 3,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 24.5/m² and the median listed size is 110 m². That is 32% below the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 4,140). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 105. 84.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
