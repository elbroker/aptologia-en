How much does it cost to rent an apartment under 45 m² in Juárez, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments under 45 m² in Juárez, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 22,920 per month, based on 53 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 20,200 to MXN 28,577. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 627/m² and the median listed size is 36.6 m². That is 28% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 32,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
