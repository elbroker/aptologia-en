How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking with 1 bathroom in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking with 1 bathroom in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 440 per month, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 350 to USD 615. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 8.89/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is 23% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 570). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
