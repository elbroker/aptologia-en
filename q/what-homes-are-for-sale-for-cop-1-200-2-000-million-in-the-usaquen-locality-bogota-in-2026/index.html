What homes are for sale for COP 1,200–2,000 million in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for COP 1,200–2,000 million in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 1,490,000,000, based on 3,355 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,300,000,000 to COP 1,700,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,352,941/m² and the median listed size is 202 m². That is 45% above the study's reference median (COP 1,030,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,396,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 97.9% of listings include parking.
