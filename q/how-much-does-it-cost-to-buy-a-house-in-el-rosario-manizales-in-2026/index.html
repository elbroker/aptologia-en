How much does it cost to buy a house in El Rosario, Manizales in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in El Rosario, Manizales is COP 1,850,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,500,000,000 to COP 2,280,000,000. That is 147% above the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 750,000,000).
