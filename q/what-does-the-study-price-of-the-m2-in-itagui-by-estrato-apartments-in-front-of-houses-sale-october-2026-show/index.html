What does the study “Price of the m² in Itagüí by estrato: apartments in front of houses (sale, October 2026)” show?

In Itagüí, the m² of an apartment for sale goes from 4.82 million pesos in estrato 2 to 8.08 million pesos in estrato 5. In estrato 2, the m² of a house is 25.2% cheaper than that of an apartment.
