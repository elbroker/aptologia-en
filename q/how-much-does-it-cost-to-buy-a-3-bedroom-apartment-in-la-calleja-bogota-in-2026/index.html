How much does it cost to buy a 3-bedroom apartment in La Calleja, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3-bedroom apartments for sale in La Calleja, Bogotá is COP 1,100,000,000, based on 181 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 820,000,000 to COP 1,350,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,272,727/m² and the median listed size is 140 m². That is 24% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 890,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,100,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 100% of listings include parking.
