Is the amoblate worth the same in all estratos?

No: it goes from + 24.6% to + 38.8% according to the estrato.

The parking lot increases the rental + 30.4% in the estrato 2 and + 14.8% in the estrato 6, in front of comparable apartments without parking lot in the same city and rooms. The amoblation ranges from + 24.6% (estrato 6) to + 38.8% (strate 5).
