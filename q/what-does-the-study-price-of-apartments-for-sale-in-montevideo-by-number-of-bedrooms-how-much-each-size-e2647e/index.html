What does the study “Price of apartments for sale in Montevideo by number of bedrooms: how much each size costs” show?

In Montevideo, a 1 bedroom apartment costs USD 156,900 in the median, one 2 USD 203,500 bedroom (+ 29.7%) and one 3 or more USD 398,000 (+ 95.6%).
