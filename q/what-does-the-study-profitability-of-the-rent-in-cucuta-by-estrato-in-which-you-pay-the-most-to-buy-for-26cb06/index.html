What does the study “Profitability of the rent in Cucuta by estrato: in which you pay the most to buy for rent” show?

In Cúcuta, buying an apartment to rent it yields more in estrato 4 (8.1% gross per year, 12.4 years of rent) and less in estrato 5 (7.1%).
