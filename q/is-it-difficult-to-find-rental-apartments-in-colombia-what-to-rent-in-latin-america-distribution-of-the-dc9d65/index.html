Is it difficult to find rental apartments in Colombia?

1 room studios and apartments account for 20.3% of the offer of rent in Colombia.

In Argentina 53.8% of the apartments for rent are studios or 1 room; in Ecuador only 19.0%. In Colombia, 3 or more rooms are 47.4% of the offer.
