How much does it cost to buy a home in Santa Fe, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Santa Fe, Bogotá is COP 330,000,000, based on 53 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 270,000,000 to COP 550,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,718,954/m² and the median listed size is 66.5 m². That is 62% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 268,300. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 28.3% of listings include parking.
