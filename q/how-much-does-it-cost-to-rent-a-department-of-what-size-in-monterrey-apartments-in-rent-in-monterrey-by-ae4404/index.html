How much does it cost to rent a department of what size in Monterrey?

Less than 50 m²: 17,000 Mexican pesos (USD 921) per month; 50 - 70 m²: 23,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,246) per month; 70 - 90 m²: 27,700 Mexican pesos (USD 1,500) per month; 90 - 120 m²: 33,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,787) per month; 120 - 160 m²: 35,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,895 per month; More than 160 m²: 50,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,708) per month (medium October of 2026).

In Monterrey, an income department of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 17,000 Mexican pesos (USD 921) per month in the median and one of the group "More than 160 m²" in 50,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,708) per month (+ 194.1%). per square metre, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (268.5 Mexican pesos (USD 14.5) / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (457.9 Mexican pesos (USD 24.8) / m² per month).
