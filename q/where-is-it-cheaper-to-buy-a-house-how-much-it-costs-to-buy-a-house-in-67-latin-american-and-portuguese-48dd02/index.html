Where is it cheaper to buy a house?

In Soacha, Colombia: USD 109,966 in the medium (October of 2026).

A house is requested for USD 109,966 in the median of Soacha (Colombia), the lowest of 67 cities, and for USD 1,461,308 in Lisbon (Portugal), the highest. In Bogotá, USD 377,026. Among the capitals, from USD 185,000 in Quito to USD 1,461,308 in Lisbon.
