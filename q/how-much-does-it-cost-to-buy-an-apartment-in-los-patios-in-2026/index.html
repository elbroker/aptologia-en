How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Los Patios in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Los Patios is COP 330,000,000, based on 49 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 200,000,000 to COP 380,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,848,691/m² and the median listed size is 74.8 m². That is 13% below the study's reference median (COP 380,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 355,500. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 77.6% of listings include parking.
