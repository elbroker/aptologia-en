What homes are for rent for COP 1.5–2.5 million a month in Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for rent for COP 1.5–2.5 million a month in Bogotá have a median asking rent of COP 1,850,000 per month, based on 4,766 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,650,000 to COP 2,100,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 47,619/m² and the median listed size is 39 m². That is 47% below the study's reference median (COP 3,495,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 300,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 34.9% of listings include parking.
