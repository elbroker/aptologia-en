How much does it cost to rent an apartment on floor 9 or higher in Santa Ana, Bello in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments on floor 9 or higher in Santa Ana, Bello is COP 2,350,000 per month, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,975,000 to COP 2,600,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 36,250/m² and the median listed size is 64 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Bello (COP 2,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 69.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
