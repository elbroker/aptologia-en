How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Recoleta, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Recoleta, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 3,000 per month, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,000 to USD 5,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 15.4/m² and the median listed size is 180 m². That is 85% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 1,625). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
