How much does it cost to rent a home on floors 1–3 in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes on floors 1–3 in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá is COP 2,200,000 per month, based on 296 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,600,000 to COP 3,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 45,714/m² and the median listed size is 45 m². That is 21% below the study's reference median (COP 2,769,500). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 300,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 50% of listings include parking.
