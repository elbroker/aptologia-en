What does the study “Where it is more expensive and cheaper to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in Mexico City: colony ranking” show?

In Mexico City, a department of 2 recamares is rented for 70,600 Mexican pesos (USD 3,823) per month in the median in Polanco III Section, the most expensive colony, and 26,001 Mexican pesos (USD 1,408) per month in Expand State of Hidalgo, the cheapest: 2.7 times less. We compare 17 colonies with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
