How much does it cost to rent in Asunción?

An apartment of 2 rooms, USD 990 per month in the middle.

The most expensive square meter of apartment in Paraguay is in Asunción, with USD 1,869 in the medium of ads for sale; the cheapest of 2 measured cities, in Luque, with USD 1,438. In Asunción, an apartment of 2 rooms is rented for USD 990 per month and sold for USD 156,200: 13.1 years of rent.
