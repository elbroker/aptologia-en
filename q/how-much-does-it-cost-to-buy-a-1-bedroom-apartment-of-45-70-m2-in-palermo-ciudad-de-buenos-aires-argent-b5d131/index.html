How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 223,177, based on 122 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 180,000 to USD 269,875. The median price per square metre is USD 4,165/m² and the median listed size is 52 m². That is 35% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
