Where is it cheaper to buy apartment in Lisbon?

Among the freguesias with 30 ads or more, in Olivais: 4,973 euros (USD 5,590) per m².

The freguesia with the most expensive square meter in Lisbon is Santo António, with 9,398 euros (USD 10,565) in the median of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Olivais, with 4,973 euros (USD 5,590). For rent, the highest medium rental is requested in Santo António (3,188 euros (USD 3,583) per month) and the lowest in Lumir (1,700 euros (USD 1,911)).
