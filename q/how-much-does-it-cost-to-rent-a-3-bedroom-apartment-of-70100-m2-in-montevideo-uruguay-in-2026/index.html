How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² in Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 39,800 per month, based on 158 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 33,250 to UYU 48,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 477/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 33% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 30,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 6,900. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
