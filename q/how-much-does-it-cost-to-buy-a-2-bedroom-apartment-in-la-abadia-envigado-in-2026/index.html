How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in La Abadía, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in La Abadía, Envigado is COP 650,000,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 12% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 740,000,000).
