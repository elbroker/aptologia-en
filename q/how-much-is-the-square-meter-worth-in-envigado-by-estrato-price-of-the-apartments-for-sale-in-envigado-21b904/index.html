How much is the square meter worth in Envigado by estrato?

From 5.61 million pesos (estrato 2) to 8.86 million pesos (estrato 5) in the median of October 2026.

In Envigado the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 5.61 million pesos in estrato 2 and 8.86 million pesos in estrato 5, 1.6 times more. The average total price ranges from 450 million pesos to 1,150 million pesos according to the estrato.
