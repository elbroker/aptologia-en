How much does it cost to rent an apartment on which floor in Armenia?

Floor 1 - 2: 2,600,000 pesos per month; Floor 3 - 5: 2,200,000 pesos per month; Floor 6 - 10: 2,500,000 pesos per month; Floor 11 - 20: 2,500,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Armenia, an apartment for rent from the "Floor 1 - 2" group is requested in 2,600,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the "Floor 11 - 20 group in 2,500,000 pesos per month (- 3.8%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 3 - 5" (28,895 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Floor 1 - 2" (34,579 pesos / m² per month).
