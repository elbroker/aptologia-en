How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 4 in Chapinero Central, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 4 in Chapinero Central, Bogotá is COP 1,993,970 per month, based on 63 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,626,500 to COP 2,655,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 52,273/m² and the median listed size is 39 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median (COP 1,900,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 356,329. 38.1% of listings include parking.
