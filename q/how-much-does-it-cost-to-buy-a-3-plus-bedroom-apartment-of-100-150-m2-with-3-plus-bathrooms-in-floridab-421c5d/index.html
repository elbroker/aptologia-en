How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Floridablanca in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Floridablanca is COP 650,000,000, based on 95 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 435,500,000 to COP 850,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,720,436/m² and the median listed size is 119 m². That is 48% above the study's reference median for Floridablanca (COP 440,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 550,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
