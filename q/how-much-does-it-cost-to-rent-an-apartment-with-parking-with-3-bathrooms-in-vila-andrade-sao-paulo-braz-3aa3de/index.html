How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Vila Andrade, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Vila Andrade, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 8,252 per month, based on 86 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 5,102 to BRL 13,098. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 52.2/m² and the median listed size is 162 m². That is 128% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 2,825. The median number of bathrooms is 4.
