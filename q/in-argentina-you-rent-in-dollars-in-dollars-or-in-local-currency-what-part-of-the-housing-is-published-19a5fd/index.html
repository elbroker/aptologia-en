In Argentina, you rent in dollars?

Little: 30.1% of the rental ads are in dollars, compared to 100.0% for sale.

In Argentina, 100.0% of the houses for sale are published in dollars, but only 30.1% in rent; in Uruguay, 100.0% and 52.7%; in Peru, 33.9% and 28.2%. When comparing apartments from the same operation and country, the dollar ads ask between + 24.7% and + 369.0% compared to those published in local currency, because they are concentrated in different segments.
