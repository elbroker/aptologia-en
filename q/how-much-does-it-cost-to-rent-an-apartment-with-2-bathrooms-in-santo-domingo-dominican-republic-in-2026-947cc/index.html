How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 2 bathrooms in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 2 bathrooms in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,350 per month, based on 1,486 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,100 to USD 1,700. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 11.5/m² and the median listed size is 121 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
