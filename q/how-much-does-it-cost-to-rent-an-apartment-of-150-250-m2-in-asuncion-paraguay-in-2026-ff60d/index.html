How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 150–250 m² in Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 150–250 m² in Asunción, Paraguay is USD 2,000 per month, based on 208 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,600 to USD 2,300. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 10.2/m² and the median listed size is 181 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
