How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Península, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Península, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 450,000, based on 140 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 333,750 to USD 882,500. The median price per square metre is USD 3,365/m² and the median listed size is 140 m². That is 25% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 1,000. The median number of bathrooms is 3.
