How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 3+ bathrooms 0–5 years old in estrato 6 in Villa Country, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms 0–5 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Villa Country, Barranquilla is COP 1,055,000,000, based on 64 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 500,000,000 to COP 2,300,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,888,889/m² and the median listed size is 183 m². That is 134% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,137,146. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.5.
