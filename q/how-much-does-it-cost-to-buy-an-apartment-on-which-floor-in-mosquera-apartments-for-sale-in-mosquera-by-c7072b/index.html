How much does it cost to buy an apartment on which floor in Mosquera?

Floor 1 - 2: 280 million pesos; Floor 3 - 5: 284 million pesos; Floor 6 - 10: 340 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Mosquera, an apartment for sale of the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 280 million pesos in the median and one of the group "Piso 6 - 10" in 340 million pesos (+ 21.4%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 3 - 5" (4.48 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 6 - 10" (5.22 million pesos / m²).
