How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Los Laureles, Armenia in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Los Laureles, Armenia is COP 420,000,000, based on 52 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 367,500,000 to COP 555,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,562,500/m² and the median listed size is 87.5 m². That is 11% above the study's reference median for Armenia (COP 380,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 435,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 96.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
