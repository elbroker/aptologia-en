How much does it cost to rent a home in Santa Paula, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Santa Paula, Bogotá is COP 5,200,000 per month, based on 182 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,425,000 to COP 10,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 56,307/m² and the median listed size is 101 m². That is 49% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,497,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 850,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 93.4% of listings include parking.
