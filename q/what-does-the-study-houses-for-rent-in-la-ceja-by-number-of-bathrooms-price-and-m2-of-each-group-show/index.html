What does the study “Houses for rent in La Ceja by number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In La Ceja, a house for rent from the group "2 bathrooms" is ordered in 3,900,000 pesos per month in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 10,900,000 pesos per month (+ 179.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (26,667 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (33,913 pesos / m² per month).
