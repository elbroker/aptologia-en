How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá is COP 2,100,000 per month, based on 278 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,650,000 to COP 2,670,250. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 61,565/m² and the median listed size is 35 m². That is 11% below the study's reference median (COP 2,350,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 300,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 33.8% of listings include parking.
