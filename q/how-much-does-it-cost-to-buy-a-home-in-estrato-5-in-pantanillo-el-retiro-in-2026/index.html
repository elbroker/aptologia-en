How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 5 in Pantanillo, El Retiro in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 5 for sale in Pantanillo, El Retiro is COP 2,600,000,000, based on 220 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
