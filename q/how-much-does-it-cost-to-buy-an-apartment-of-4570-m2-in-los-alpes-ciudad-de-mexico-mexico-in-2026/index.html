How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 45–70 m² in Los Alpes, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Los Alpes, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 3,900,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 3,543,950 to MXN 4,460,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 67,308/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 4,990,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
