What is the most expensive city to buy housing in Portugal?

Lisbon: 7,458 EUR (USD 8,383) per m² in the medium (October of 2026).

The most expensive square meter of apartment is requested in Lisbon, with 7,458 euros (USD 8,383) in the medium, and the cheapest in Faro, with 5,101 euros (USD 5,735).
