What is the most expensive colony in Mexico City to buy?

Santa Fe la Loma, with 80,056 Mexican pesos (USD 4,335) per m² in the medium (October of 2026).

In Mexico City, the colony with the most expensive m² to buy house is Santa Fe la Loma (80,056 Mexican pesos (USD 4,335) / m²) and the cheapest South Hill (23,677 Mexican pesos (USD 1,282) / m²), 3.4 times less. We compare 26 colonies with at least 30 ads with area.
