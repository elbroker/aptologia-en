How much does it cost to rent an apartment with a balcony in Chapinero Alto, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony in Chapinero Alto, Bogotá is COP 3,600,000 per month, based on 98 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,800,000 to COP 5,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 56,231/m² and the median listed size is 63 m². That is 9% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 650,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 87.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
