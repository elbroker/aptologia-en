What does the study “Apartments for sale in Bello according to age: price and m² of each group” show?

In Bello, an apartment for sale from the group "To premiere (0 years)" is requested in 360 million pesos in the median and one from the group "21 to 30 years" in 300 million pesos (- 16.7%). For each square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (4.50 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "To premiere (0 years)" (5.66 million pesos / m²).
