How much does it cost to buy a 2 room apartment in Santa Marta?

700 million pesos in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the supply is between 450 million pesos and 877 million pesos.

In Santa Marta, a 1 room apartment costs 450 million pesos in the medium, one 2 million rooms 700 million pesos (+ 55.6%) and one 3 or more 760 million pesos (+ 8.6%).
