How much do you rent to buy an apartment to rent in Medellín?

The average gross return on 229 comparable neighbourhoods is 6.5% per year (12 months of rent requested on the order price), before HOA fee, predial, vacations and taxes.

The average rental of an apartment in Medellín is $4,100,000 per month; an apartment of 2 alcobas requests in the median $3,850,000. Among the neighborhoods with 30 ads or more, the cheapest is Florence ($1,100,000) and the most expensive La Calera ($12,000,000): 10.9 times more.
