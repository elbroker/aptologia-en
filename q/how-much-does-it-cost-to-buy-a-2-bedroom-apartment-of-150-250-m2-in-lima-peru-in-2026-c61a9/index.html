How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² in Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Lima, Peru is USD 480,351, based on 71 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 398,000 to USD 620,348. The median price per square metre is USD 2,690/m² and the median listed size is 164 m². That is 86% above the study's reference median for Lima (USD 258,023). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
