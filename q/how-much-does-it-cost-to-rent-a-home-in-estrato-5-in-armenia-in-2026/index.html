How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 5 in Armenia in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 5 in Armenia is COP 2,300,000 per month, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,775,000 to COP 3,411,250. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 33,694/m² and the median listed size is 64.5 m². That is 15% above the study's reference median (COP 2,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 311,000. 75% of listings include parking.
