What does the study “What apartment is purchased in Bello in each price range: size, m² and offer” show?

In Bello, the price range with the most offer of apartments for sale is 200 - 350 M of pesos (48.2% of the ads), with a median area of 56 m². 51.0% of the supply costs less than 350 million.
