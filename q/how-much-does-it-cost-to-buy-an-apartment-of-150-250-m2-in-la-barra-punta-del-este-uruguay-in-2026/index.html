How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 150–250 m² in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 632,700, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 485,000 to USD 730,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,610/m² and the median listed size is 174 m². That is 76% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
