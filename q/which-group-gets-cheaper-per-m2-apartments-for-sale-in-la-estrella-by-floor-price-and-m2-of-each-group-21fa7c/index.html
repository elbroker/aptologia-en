Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (5.71 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 11 - 20" (6.52 million pesos / m²).

In La Estrella, an apartment for sale from the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 418 million pesos in the median and one from the group "Piso 11 - 20" in 450 million pesos (+ 7.8%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (5.71 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 11 - 20" (6.52 million pesos / m²).
