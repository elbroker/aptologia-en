How much does it cost to rent an apartment in La Calleja, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in La Calleja, Bogotá is COP 4,804,250 per month, based on 72 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,200,000 to COP 8,058,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 52,365/m² and the median listed size is 86 m². That is 37% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 652,500. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 97.2% of listings include parking.
