What does the study “What apartment to rent in Bello in each rental range: size, m² and offer” show?

In Bello, the most available rental range of apartments for rent is 1.5 - 2.5 M of pesos per month (69.7% of the ads), with a median area of 58 m². 4.0% of the supply is requested in less than 1.5 million.
