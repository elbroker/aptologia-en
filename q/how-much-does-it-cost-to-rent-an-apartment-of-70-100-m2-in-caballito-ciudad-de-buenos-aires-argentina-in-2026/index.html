How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 70–100 m² in Caballito, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in Caballito, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is ARS 1,400,000 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from ARS 1,300,000 to ARS 1,787,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is ARS 17,673/m² and the median listed size is 84 m². That is 65% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (ARS 850,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
