What does the study “More expensive and cheaper villas in Medellín to buy house: ranking per m²” show?

In Medellín, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy house is Las Palmas (8.71 million pesos / m²) and the cheapest San Javier (2.98 million pesos / m²), 2.9 times less. We compare 37 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
