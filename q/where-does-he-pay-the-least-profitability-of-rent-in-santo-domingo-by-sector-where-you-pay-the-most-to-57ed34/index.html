Where does he pay the least?

In Renaissance: 5.7% gross per year (17.6 years of rent).

In Santo Domingo, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical sector 6.9% gross per year: the price requested is equivalent to 14.6 years of rent. The sector with the highest gross return is El Vergel (9.6%) and that of the minor, Renaissance (5.7%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 7.4%.
