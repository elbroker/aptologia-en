What does the study “Rental in Santa Cruz de la Sierra by number of bedrooms: how much each type of apartment costs” show?

In Santa Cruz de la Sierra, rent a 1 bedroom apartment costs the median USD 425 per month and one 2 bedroom, USD 630 (+ 48.2%). One of 3 or more bedrooms is requested in USD 785.
