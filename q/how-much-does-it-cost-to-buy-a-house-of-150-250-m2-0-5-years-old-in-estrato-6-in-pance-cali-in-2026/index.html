How much does it cost to buy a house of 150–250 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 6 in Pance, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 150–250 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Pance, Cali is COP 1,485,000,000, based on 74 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,205,000,000 to COP 1,600,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,013,575/m² and the median listed size is 211 m². That is 60% above the study's reference median for Cali (COP 930,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,166,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
