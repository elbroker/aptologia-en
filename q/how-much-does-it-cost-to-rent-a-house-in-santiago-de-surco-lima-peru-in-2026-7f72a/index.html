How much does it cost to rent a house in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is USD 3,800 per month, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,550 to USD 6,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 10.6/m² and the median listed size is 400 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (USD 3,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
