How much does it cost to rent an apartment with how many bathrooms in Asunción?

1 bathroom: 4,500,000 Guarani (USD 789) per month; 2 bathrooms: 6,000,000 Guarani (USD 1,052) per month; 3 bathrooms: 7,200,000 Guarani (USD 1,262) per month (medium October of 2026).

In Asunción, a rental department of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 4,500,000 Guaranis (USD 789) per month in the median and one of the group "3 bathrooms" in 7,200,000 Guaranis (USD 1,262) per month (+ 60.0%). per square metre, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (60,000.0 Guarani (USD 10.5) / m² per month) and the most expensive "1 bathroom" (78,125.0 Guarani (USD 13.7) / m² per month).
