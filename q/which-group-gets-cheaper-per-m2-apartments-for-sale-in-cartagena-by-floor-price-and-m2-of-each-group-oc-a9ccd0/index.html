Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 3 - 5" (7.57 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 21 or more" (11.08 million pesos / m²).

In Cartagena, an apartment for sale of the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 750 million pesos in the median and one of the group "Piso 21 or more" in 1,250 million pesos (+ 66.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 3 - 5" (7.57 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 21 or more" (11.08 million pesos / m²).
