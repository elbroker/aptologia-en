What does the study “Houses for rent in Santa Cruz de la Sierra by size: price and m² of each group” show?

In Santa Cruz de la Sierra, a house for rent from the group "90 - 120 m²" is requested in USD 478 per month in the medium and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in USD 1,468 per month (+ 207.5%). per square metre, the cheapest is "120 - 160 m²" (USD 4.4 / m² per month) and the most expensive "More than 160 m²" (USD 4.9 / m² per month).
