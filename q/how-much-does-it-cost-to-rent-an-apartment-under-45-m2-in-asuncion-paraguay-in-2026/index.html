How much does it cost to rent an apartment under 45 m² in Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments under 45 m² in Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 4,000,000 per month, based on 205 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 3,300,000 to PYG 4,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is PYG 97,500/m² and the median listed size is 40 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
