How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with parking with 2 bathrooms in Carrasco Norte, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with parking with 2 bathrooms for sale in Carrasco Norte, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 432,450, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 358,388 to USD 565,250. The median price per square metre is USD 2,831/m² and the median listed size is 168 m². That is 61% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 269,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
