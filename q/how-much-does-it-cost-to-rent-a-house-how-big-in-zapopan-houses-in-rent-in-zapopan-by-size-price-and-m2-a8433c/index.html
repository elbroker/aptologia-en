How much does it cost to rent a house how big in Zapopan?

90 - 120 m²: 14,150 Mexican pesos (USD 766) per month; 120 - 160 m²: 19,350 Mexican pesos (USD 1,048) per month; More than 160 m²: 42,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,275) per month (medium October of 2026).

In Zapopan, a rental house of the group "90 - 120 m²" is requested in 14,150 Mexican pesos (USD 766) per month in the median and one of the group "More than 160 m²" in 42,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,275) per month (+ 196.8%). For each square metre, the cheapest is "90 - 120 m²" (137.1 Mexican pesos (USD 7.4) / m² per month) and the most expensive "120 - 160 m²" (151.9 Mexican pesos (USD 8.2) / m² per month).
