How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 45–70 m² in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile is UF 6,440, based on 216 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 5,500 to UF 7,200. The median price per square metre is UF 112/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 6,950). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
