How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Punta del Este?

USD 3,800 per month in the median (October of 2026).

The area with the most expensive square meter of Punta del Este is Manantiales, with USD 4,880 in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is Cantegril, with USD 2,833. A 2 room department is rented per USD 3,800 per month in the medium and sold per USD 310,000. The gross implicit return on rent is 13.7% per year.
