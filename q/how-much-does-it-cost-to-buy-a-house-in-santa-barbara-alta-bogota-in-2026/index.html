How much does it cost to buy a house in Santa Barbara Alta, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Santa Barbara Alta, Bogotá is COP 4,000,000,000, based on 126 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,125,000,000 to COP 5,000,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,941,898/m² and the median listed size is 419 m². That is 233% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 847,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 98.4% of listings include parking.
