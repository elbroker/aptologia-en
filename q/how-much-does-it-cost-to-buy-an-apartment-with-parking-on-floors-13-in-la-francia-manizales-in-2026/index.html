How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking on floors 1–3 in La Francia, Manizales in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking on floors 1–3 for sale in La Francia, Manizales is COP 360,000,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 346,163,400 to COP 490,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,555,556/m² and the median listed size is 71.9 m². That is 20% below the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 212,600. The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
