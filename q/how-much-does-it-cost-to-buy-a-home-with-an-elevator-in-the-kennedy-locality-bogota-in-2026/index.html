How much does it cost to buy a home with an elevator in the Kennedy locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes with an elevator for sale in the Kennedy locality, Bogotá is COP 376,500,000, based on 486 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 250,000,000 to COP 532,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,119,869/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 16% below the study's reference median (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 228,750. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 62.8% of listings include parking.
