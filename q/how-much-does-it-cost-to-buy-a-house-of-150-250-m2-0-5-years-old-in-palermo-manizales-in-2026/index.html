How much does it cost to buy a house of 150–250 m² 0–5 years old in Palermo, Manizales in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 150–250 m² 0–5 years old for sale in Palermo, Manizales is COP 965,000,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 835,000,000 to COP 1,197,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,927,395/m² and the median listed size is 211 m². That is 25% above the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 770,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
