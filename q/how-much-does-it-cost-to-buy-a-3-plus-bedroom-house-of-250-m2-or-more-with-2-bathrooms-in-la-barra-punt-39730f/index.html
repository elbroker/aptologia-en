How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms for sale in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 649,500, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 505,000 to USD 950,000. The median listed size is 300 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
