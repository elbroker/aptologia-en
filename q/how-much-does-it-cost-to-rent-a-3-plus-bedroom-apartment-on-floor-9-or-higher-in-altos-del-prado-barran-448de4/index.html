How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment on floor 9 or higher in Altos del Prado, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments on floor 9 or higher in Altos del Prado, Barranquilla is COP 6,200,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,000,000 to COP 9,090,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 35,000/m² and the median listed size is 162 m². That is 121% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
