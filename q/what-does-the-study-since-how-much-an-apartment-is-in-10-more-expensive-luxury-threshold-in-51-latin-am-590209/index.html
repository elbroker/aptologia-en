What does the study “Since how much an apartment is in 10% more expensive: luxury threshold in 51 Latin American and Portugal cities” show?

To be in the 10% most expensive of the offer, an apartment for sale has to order at least USD 1,673,535 in Lisbon (Portugal), the highest threshold of 51 cities; in Soacha (Colombia) it is sufficient to have USD 72,263. Where the most luxury is separated from the typical market is Buenos Aires (Argentina): the threshold is 3.3 times the median.
