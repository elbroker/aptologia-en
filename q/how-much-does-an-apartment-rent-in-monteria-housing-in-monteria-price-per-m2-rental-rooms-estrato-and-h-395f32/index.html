How much does an apartment rent in Montería?

The gross return is 8.2% per year (rent per m² × 12), before expenditure.

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter of Monteverde is Monteverde, with 3.90 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is La Castellana, with 3.68 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 8.2% per year.
