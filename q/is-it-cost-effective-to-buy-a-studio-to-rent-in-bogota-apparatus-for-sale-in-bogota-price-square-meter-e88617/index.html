Is it cost-effective to buy a studio to rent in Bogotá?

The typical gross return is 7.4% per year before expenditure: average rental of $1,900,000 compared to $10,068,085 per m² of sale.

A studio for sale in Bogotá asks in the median $326 million, with 32 m² ($10,068,085 per m²). Tired, a typical one asks $1,900,000 per month: a gross return of 7.4% per year, comparing square meter of rent and sale.
