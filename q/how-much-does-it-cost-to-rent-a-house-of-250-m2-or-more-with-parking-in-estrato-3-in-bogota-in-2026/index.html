How much does it cost to rent a house of 250 m² or more with parking in estrato 3 in Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses of 250 m² or more with parking in estrato 3 in Bogotá is COP 9,000,000 per month, based on 119 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 6,900,000 to COP 15,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 22,500/m² and the median listed size is 392 m². That is 10% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 10,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
