How much does it cost to rent an apartment under 45 m² in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments under 45 m² in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile is UF 19 per month, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 17 to UF 21.5. The median monthly rent per square metre is UF 0.51/m² and the median listed size is 38 m². That is 37% above the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 13.8). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
