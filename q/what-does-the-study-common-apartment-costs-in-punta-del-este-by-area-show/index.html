What does the study “Common apartment costs in Punta del Este by area” show?

In Punta del Este, the common costs of a department are in the median USD 800 per month (USD 7.07 per m²). The most expensive area per m² is Roosevelt (USD 7.49) and the cheapest, Aidy Grill (USD 6.05).
