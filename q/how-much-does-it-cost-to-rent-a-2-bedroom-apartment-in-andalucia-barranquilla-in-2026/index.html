How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in Andalucía, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in Andalucía, Barranquilla is COP 2,700,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 23% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,200,000).
