How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 4 in Pradera Norte, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 4 for sale in Pradera Norte, Bogotá is COP 499,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 425,000,000 to COP 598,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,852,632/m² and the median listed size is 90.2 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 499,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 437,000. 93.5% of listings include parking.
