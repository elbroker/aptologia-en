How much does it cost to rent a home in Villa del Prado, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Villa del Prado, Bogotá is COP 2,200,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,750,000 to COP 3,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 31,812/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 37% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,497,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 250,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 46.7% of listings include parking.
