Where does a budget of 2.5 million pesos a month give more?

In Tocancipá: about 132 m².

With 2.5 million pesos a month, some 132 m² of apartment is rented in Tocancipá, the most surrendering city of 38, and 51 m² in El Retiro. In Bogotá they reach for 52 m² and in Medellín for 51 m².
