What does the study “Housing in La Paz: price per m², rental, rooms and profitability” show?

The area with the most expensive square meter of La Paz is Calacoto, with USD 1,241 in the median of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is Irpavi, with USD 981. A 2 room department is rented per USD 506 per month in the medium and sold per USD 110,000. The gross implicit return on rent is 6.1% per year.
