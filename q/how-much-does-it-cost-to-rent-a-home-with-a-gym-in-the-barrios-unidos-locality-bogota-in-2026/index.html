How much does it cost to rent a home with a gym in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes with a gym in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá is COP 2,600,000 per month, based on 253 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,000,000 to COP 6,300,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 70,680/m² and the median listed size is 39 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median (COP 2,700,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 380,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 54.2% of listings include parking.
