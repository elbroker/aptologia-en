What does the study “Price of apartments for sale in Armenia by number of rooms: how much each size costs” show?

In Armenia, a 1 room apartment costs 329 million pesos in the medium, one 2 million rooms 350 million pesos (+ 6.4%) and one 3 or more 430 million pesos (+ 22.9%).
