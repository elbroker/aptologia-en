How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² in Polanco V Sección, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² in Polanco V Sección, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 67,000 per month, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 59,200 to MXN 90,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 361/m² and the median listed size is 197 m². That is 109% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 32,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
