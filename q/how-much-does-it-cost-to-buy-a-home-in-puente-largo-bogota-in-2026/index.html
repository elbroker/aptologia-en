How much does it cost to buy a home in Puente Largo, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Puente Largo, Bogotá is COP 850,000,000, based on 177 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 610,000,000 to COP 1,200,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,978,261/m² and the median listed size is 132 m². That is 2% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 600,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 94.4% of listings include parking.
