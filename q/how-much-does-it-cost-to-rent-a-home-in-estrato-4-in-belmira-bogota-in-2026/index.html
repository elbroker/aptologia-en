How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 4 in Belmira, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 4 in Belmira, Bogotá is COP 2,500,000 per month, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,100,000 to COP 4,450,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 47,244/m² and the median listed size is 50 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median (COP 3,200,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 451,500. 97.1% of listings include parking.
