How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Bosa San Bernardino, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Bosa San Bernardino, Bogotá is COP 172,000,000, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 163,240,000 to COP 177,000,000. That is 74% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 660,000,000).
