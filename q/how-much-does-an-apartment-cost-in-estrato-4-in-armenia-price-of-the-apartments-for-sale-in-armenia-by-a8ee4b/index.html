How much does an apartment cost in estrato 4 in Armenia?

305 million pesos in the median.

In Armenia the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 3.73 million pesos in estrato 3 and 6.65 million pesos in estrato 6, 1.8 times more. The average total price ranges from 220 million pesos to 720 million pesos according to the estrato.
