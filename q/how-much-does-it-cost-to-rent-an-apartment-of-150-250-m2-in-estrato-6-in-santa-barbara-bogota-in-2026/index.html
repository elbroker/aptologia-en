How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 150–250 m² in estrato 6 in Santa Bárbara, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 150–250 m² in estrato 6 in Santa Bárbara, Bogotá is COP 7,700,000 per month, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,985,750 to COP 8,775,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 42,500/m² and the median listed size is 174 m². That is 133% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,500,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
