How much does it cost to rent a pet-friendly house in estrato 6 in Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for pet-friendly houses in estrato 6 in Medellín is COP 15,000,000 per month, based on 51 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 11,340,000 to COP 21,852,095. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 43,366/m² and the median listed size is 350 m². That is 76% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 8,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,160,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
