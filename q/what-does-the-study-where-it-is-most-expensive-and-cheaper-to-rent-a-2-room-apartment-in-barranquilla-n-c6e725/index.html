What does the study “Where it is most expensive and cheaper to rent a 2 room apartment in Barranquilla: neighborhood ranking” show?

In Barranquilla, an apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 3,120,000 pesos per month in the medium in Altos del Prado, the most expensive neighborhood, and 1,299,500 pesos per month in Alameda del Río, the cheapest: 2.4 times less. We compare 7 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
