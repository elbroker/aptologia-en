How much does it cost to buy a home with a terrace or balcony in the Usme locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes with a terrace or balcony for sale in the Usme locality, Bogotá is COP 350,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 250,000,000 to COP 695,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,448,485/m² and the median listed size is 216 m². That is 75% above the study's reference median (COP 200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 170,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 45.2% of listings include parking.
