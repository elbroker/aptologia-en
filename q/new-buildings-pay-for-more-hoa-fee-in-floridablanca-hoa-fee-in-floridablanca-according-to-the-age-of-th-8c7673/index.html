New buildings pay for more HOA fee in Floridablanca?

1 to 5 years: 4,681 pesos / m²; 6 to 10 years: 5,140 pesos / m²; 11 to 20 years: 5,233 pesos / m²; 21 to 30 years: 4,438 pesos / m² (medium per m² per month).

In Floridablanca, the highest square meter HOA fee is paid in the group buildings "11 to 20 years" (5,233 pesos per m² per month in the median) and the lowest in "21 to 30 years" (4,438 pesos). Average monthly rate per strip: 1 to 5 years: 412,500 pesos; 6 to 10 years: 416,000 pesos; 11 to 20 years: 440,000 pesos; 21 to 30 years: 418,000 pesos.
