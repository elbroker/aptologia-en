How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in La Molina, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in La Molina, Lima, Peru is PEN 2,900 per month, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 2,400 to PEN 3,652. The median monthly rent per square metre is PEN 21.2/m² and the median listed size is 128 m². That is 25% below the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 3,892). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
