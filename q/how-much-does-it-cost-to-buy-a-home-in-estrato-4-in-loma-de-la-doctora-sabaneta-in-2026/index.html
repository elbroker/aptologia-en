How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 4 in Loma de la Doctora, Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 4 for sale in Loma de la Doctora, Sabaneta is COP 580,000,000, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
