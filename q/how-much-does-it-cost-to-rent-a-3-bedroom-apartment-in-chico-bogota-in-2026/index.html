How much does it cost to rent a 3-bedroom apartment in Chico, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3-bedroom apartments in Chico, Bogotá is COP 11,000,000 per month, based on 163 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 7,500,000 to COP 16,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 60,000/m² and the median listed size is 190 m². That is 69% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 6,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,000,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 99.4% of listings include parking.
