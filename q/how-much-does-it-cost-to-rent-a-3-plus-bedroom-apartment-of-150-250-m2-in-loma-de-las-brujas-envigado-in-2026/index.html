How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado is COP 9,100,000 per month, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 8,000,000 to COP 10,875,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 51,251/m² and the median listed size is 178 m². That is 107% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 4,400,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 98.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
