What does the study “HOA fee in Rionegro according to the age of the building: new to old” show?

In Rionegro, the highest square meter HOA fee is paid in the group buildings "6 to 10 years" (4,875 pesos per m² per month in the median) and the lowest in "To premiere" (4,625 pesos). Average monthly rate per strip: For starters: 312,000 pesos; 1 to 5 years: 300,000 pesos; 6 to 10 years: 311,500 pesos.
