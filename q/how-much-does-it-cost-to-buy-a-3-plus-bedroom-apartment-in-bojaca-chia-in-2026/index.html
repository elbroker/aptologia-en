How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Bojacá, Chía in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Bojacá, Chía is COP 275,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 39% below the study's reference median for Chía (COP 450,000,000).
