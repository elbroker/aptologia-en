How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 1 bathroom in San Nicolás, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom for sale in San Nicolás, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 145,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 114,500 to USD 179,750. The median price per square metre is USD 1,440/m² and the median listed size is 102 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
