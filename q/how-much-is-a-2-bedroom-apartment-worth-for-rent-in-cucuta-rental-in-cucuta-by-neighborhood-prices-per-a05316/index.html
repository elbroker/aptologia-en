How much is a 2 bedroom apartment worth for rent in Cúcuta?

The median is $1,050,000 per month (1,027 ads).

The average rental of an apartment in Cúcuta is $1,300,000 per month; an apartment of 2 alcobas requests in the median $1,050,000. Among the neighborhoods with 30 ads or more, the cheapest is La Cabrera ($850,000) and the most expensive San Mateo ($2,500,000): 2.9 times more.
