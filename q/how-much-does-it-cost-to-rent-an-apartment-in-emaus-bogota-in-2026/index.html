How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Emaus, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Emaus, Bogotá is COP 4,000,000 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,277,500 to COP 7,450,000. That is 45% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,750,000).
