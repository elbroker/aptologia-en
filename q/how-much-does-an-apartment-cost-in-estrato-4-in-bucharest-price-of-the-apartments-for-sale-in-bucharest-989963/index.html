How much does an apartment cost in estrato 4 in Bucharest?

385 million pesos in the median.

In Bucharest the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 4.17 million pesos in estrato 3 and 5.48 million pesos in estrato 6, 1.3 times more. The average total price ranges from 265 million pesos to 760 million pesos according to the estrato.
