How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more in Santa Marta in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more in Santa Marta is COP 9,250,000 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,625,000 to COP 12,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 22,222/m² and the median listed size is 404 m². That is 85% above the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 5,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 86.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
