How much does it cost to buy a house with how many bathrooms in Envigado?

2 bathrooms: 1,050 million pesos; 3 bathrooms: 1,400 million pesos; 4 or more bathrooms: 2,590 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Envigado, a house for sale from the group "2 bathrooms" is requested in 1,050 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 2,590 million pesos (+ 146.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (6.56 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (7.11 million pesos / m²).
