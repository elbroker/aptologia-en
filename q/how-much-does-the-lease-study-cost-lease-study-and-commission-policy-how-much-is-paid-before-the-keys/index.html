How much does the lease study cost?

In the revised rates, between 5% + VAT and 7% of the rent. In a $2,500,000 rent, about $150,000. If the study comes out negative, ALL RIGHT warns that the money is not returned.

Renting an apartment with real estate requires paying about 1.3 rentals before receiving the keys. With the rates published by a real estate in Bogotá ([Master Group, 2026] (https://www.grupomaster.co/tarifas-de-nuestros-servicios/)), a $2 bedroom 2,500,000 (the median ordered in Bogotá according to Aptology)) costs $3,243,750 more to enter: $743,750 than the first month.
