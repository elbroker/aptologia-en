How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in Montevideo, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in Montevideo, Bogotá is COP 1,900,000 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,750,000 to COP 2,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 47,368/m² and the median listed size is 42 m². That is 27% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,600,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 206,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 20.5% of listings include parking.
