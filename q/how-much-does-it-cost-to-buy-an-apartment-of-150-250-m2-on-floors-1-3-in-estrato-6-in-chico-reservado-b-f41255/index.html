How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 150–250 m² on floors 1–3 in estrato 6 in Chicó Reservado, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² on floors 1–3 in estrato 6 for sale in Chicó Reservado, Bogotá is COP 1,750,000,000, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,480,000,000 to COP 2,287,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,510,870/m² and the median listed size is 198 m². That is 133% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,000,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
