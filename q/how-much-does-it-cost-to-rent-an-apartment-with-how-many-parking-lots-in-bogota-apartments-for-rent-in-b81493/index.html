How much does it cost to rent an apartment with how many parking lots in Bogotá?

No parking lot: 1,650,000 pesos per month; 1 parking lot: 3,000,000 pesos per month; 2 or more parking lot: 8,554,500 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Bogotá, an apartment for rent from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 1,650,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "2 or more parking lot" in 8,554,500 pesos per month (+ 418.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (40,625 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (54,605 pesos / m² per month).
