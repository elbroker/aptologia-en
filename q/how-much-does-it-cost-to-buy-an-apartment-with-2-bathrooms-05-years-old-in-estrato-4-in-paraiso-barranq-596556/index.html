How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 2 bathrooms 0–5 years old in estrato 4 in Paraíso, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms 0–5 years old in estrato 4 for sale in Paraíso, Barranquilla is COP 310,000,000, based on 83 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 285,000,000 to COP 357,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,075,758/m² and the median listed size is 64 m². That is 31% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 308,000. 90.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
