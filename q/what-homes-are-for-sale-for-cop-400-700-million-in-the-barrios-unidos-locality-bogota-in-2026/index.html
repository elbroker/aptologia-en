What homes are for sale for COP 400–700 million in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for COP 400–700 million in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 565,000,000, based on 349 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 480,000,000 to COP 640,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,953,528/m² and the median listed size is 79 m². That is 34% below the study's reference median (COP 860,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 469,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 74.5% of listings include parking.
