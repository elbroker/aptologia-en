Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "70 - 90 m²" (21,341 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (23,634 pesos / m² per month).

In Cúcuta, an apartment for rent from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 1,095,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "120 - 160 m²" in 2,000,000 pesos per month (+ 82.6%).
