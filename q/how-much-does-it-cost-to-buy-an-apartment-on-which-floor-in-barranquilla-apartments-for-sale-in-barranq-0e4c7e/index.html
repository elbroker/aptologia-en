How much does it cost to buy an apartment on which floor in Barranquilla?

Floor 1 - 2: 420 million pesos; Floor 3 - 5: 400 million pesos; Floor 6 - 10: 500 million pesos; Floor 11 - 20: 600 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Barranquilla, an apartment for sale of the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 420 million pesos in the median and one of the group "Piso 11 - 20" in 600 million pesos (+ 42.9%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (4.01 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 11 - 20" (5.43 million pesos / m²).
