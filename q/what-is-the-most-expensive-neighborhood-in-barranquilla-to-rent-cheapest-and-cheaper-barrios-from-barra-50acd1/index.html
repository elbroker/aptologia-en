What is the most expensive neighborhood in Barranquilla to rent?

La Castellana, with 47,680 pesos per m² per month in the median (October of 2026).

In Barranquilla, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to rent apartment is La Castellana (47,680 pesos / m² per month) and the cheapest El Prado (23,345 pesos / m² per month), 2.0 times less. We compare 31 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
