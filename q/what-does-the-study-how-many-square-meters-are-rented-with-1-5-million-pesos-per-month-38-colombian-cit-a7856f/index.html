What does the study “How many square meters are rented with 1.5 million pesos per month? 38 Colombian cities” show?

With 1.5 million pesos a month, some 79 m² of apartment is rented in Tocancipá, the most surrendering city of 38, and 30 m² in El Retiro. In Bogotá they reach for 31 m² and in Medellín for 30 m².
