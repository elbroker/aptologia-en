How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Ñuñoa, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Ñuñoa, Santiago, Chile is CLP 560,000 per month, based on 520 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from CLP 470,000 to CLP 700,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is CLP 11,778/m² and the median listed size is 50 m². That is 24% above the study's reference median for Santiago (CLP 450,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
