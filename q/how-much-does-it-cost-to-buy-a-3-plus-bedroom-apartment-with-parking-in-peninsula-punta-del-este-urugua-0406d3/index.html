How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with parking in Península, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking for sale in Península, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 440,000, based on 222 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 321,250 to USD 657,500. The median price per square metre is USD 3,454/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 22% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 830. The median number of bathrooms is 3.
