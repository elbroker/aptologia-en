200,000 pesos a month.

In Sabaneta, the median rate of HOA fee of an apartment goes from 200,000 pesos per month in estrato 2 to 450,000 pesos in estrato 5, 2.2 times more. per square meter, it goes from 3,630 pesos to 4,962 pesos per month.
