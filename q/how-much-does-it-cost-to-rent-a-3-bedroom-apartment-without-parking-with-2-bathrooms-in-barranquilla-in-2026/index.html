How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment without parking with 2 bathrooms in Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments without parking with 2 bathrooms in Barranquilla is COP 1,600,000 per month, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,239,750 to COP 2,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 23,377/m² and the median listed size is 64.9 m². That is 43% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 233,926. The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
