How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Santa Ana Oriental, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Santa Ana Oriental, Bogotá is COP 14,000,000 per month, based on 63 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 11,250,000 to COP 18,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 59,259/m² and the median listed size is 243 m². That is 300% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,000,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
