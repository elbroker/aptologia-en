How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in Santiago, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Santiago, Dominican Republic is USD 253,700, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 193,893 to USD 300,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,561/m² and the median listed size is 160 m². That is 43% above the study's reference median for Santiago (USD 178,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
