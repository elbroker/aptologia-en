How much does it cost to rent a home with parking in Ibagué in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes with parking in Ibagué is COP 1,351,300 per month, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,038,700 to COP 1,906,950. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 20,085/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 35% above the study's reference median (COP 999,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 241,000. The most frequent estrato is 4.
