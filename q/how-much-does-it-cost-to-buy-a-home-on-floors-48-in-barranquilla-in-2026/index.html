How much does it cost to buy a home on floors 4–8 in Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for homes on floors 4–8 for sale in Barranquilla is COP 239,500,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 177,000,000 to COP 407,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,628,827/m² and the median listed size is 62 m². That is 9% below the study's reference median (COP 262,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 209,615. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 45.7% of listings include parking.
