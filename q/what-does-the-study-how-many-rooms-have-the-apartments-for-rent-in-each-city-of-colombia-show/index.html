What does the study “How many rooms have the apartments for rent in each city of Colombia” show?

In Bogotá the 39.6% of the apartments for rent is apartment or 1 room, the largest proportion of 24 cities; in Madrid is just 0.4%. Where more than 3 rooms are weighed or more is Floridablanca (81.3%). All over the country: 20.3% of 1, 32.3% of 2 and 47.4% of 3 or more.
