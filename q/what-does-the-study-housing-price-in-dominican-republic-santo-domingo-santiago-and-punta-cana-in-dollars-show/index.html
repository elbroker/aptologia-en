What does the study “Housing price in Dominican Republic: Santo Domingo, Santiago and Punta Cana in dollars” show?

The most expensive square meter of apartment is requested in Punta Cana, with USD 2,527 in the medium, and the cheapest in Santiago, with USD 1,496. To rent one of 2 rooms costs USD 1,600 a month in Punta Cana and USD 1,400 in Santo Domingo.
