Where is it cheaper to rent a 3 apartment or more rooms in Santo Domingo?

In The Million: USD 1,295 per month in the median.

In Santo Domingo, an apartment of 3 or more rooms is rented per USD 4,200 per month in the medium in Av. Anacaona, the most expensive sector, and USD 1,295 a month in El Millón, the cheapest: 3.2 times less. We compare 9 sectors with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
