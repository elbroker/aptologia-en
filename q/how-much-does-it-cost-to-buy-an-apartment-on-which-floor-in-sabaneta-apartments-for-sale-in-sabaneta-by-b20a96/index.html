How much does it cost to buy an apartment on which floor in Sabaneta?

Floor 1 - 2: 497 million pesos; Floor 3 - 5: 500 million pesos; Floor 6 - 10: 530 million pesos; Floor 11 - 20: 550 million pesos; Floor 21 or more: 540 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Sabaneta, an apartment for sale of the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 497 million pesos in the median and one of the group "Piso 21 or more" in 540 million pesos (+ 8.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (6.02 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 21 or more" (7.56 million pesos / m²).
