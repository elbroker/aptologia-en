How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 250 m² or more in Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 250 m² or more in Asunción, Paraguay is USD 2,800 per month, based on 107 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,500 to USD 3,550. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 8.57/m² and the median listed size is 308 m². That is 107% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 1,350). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
