How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 3 or more bedrooms in Equipetrol?

USD 238,112 in the median (October of 2026); the central half between USD 175,000 and USD 365,000.

In Santa Cruz de la Sierra, a department of 3 or more bedrooms costs USD 238,112 in the median in Equimetrol, the most expensive area, and USD 125,000 in West, the cheapest: 1.9 times less. We compare 6 areas with at least 30 ads from 3 or more bedrooms.
