How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment with 1 bathroom in Caballito, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom in Caballito, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is ARS 1,100,000 per month, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from ARS 900,000 to ARS 1,260,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is ARS 18,542/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 29% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (ARS 850,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
