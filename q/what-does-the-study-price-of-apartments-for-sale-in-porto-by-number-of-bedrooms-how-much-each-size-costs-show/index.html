What does the study “Price of apartments for sale in Porto by number of bedrooms: how much each size costs” show?

In Porto, a 1 bedroom apartment costs 295,000 euros (USD 331,604) in the medium, one 2 euro 430,875 bedroom (USD 484,339) (+ 46.1%) and one 3 or more 749,850 euros (USD 842,894) (+ 74.0%).
