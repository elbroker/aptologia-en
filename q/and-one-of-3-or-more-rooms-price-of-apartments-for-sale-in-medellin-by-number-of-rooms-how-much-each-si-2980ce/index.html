And one of 3 or more rooms?

890 million pesos in the median.

In Medellín, a 1 room apartment costs 630 million pesos in the medium, one 2 million rooms 720 million pesos (+ 14.3%) and one 3 or more 890 million pesos (+ 23.6%).
