How much does an apartment cost 2 rooms in Bogotá?

540 million pesos in the medium of the offer for sale (October of 2026).

Buying an apartment of 2 rooms costs in the medium 165 million pesos in Soacha, the cheapest city of the show, and 750 million pesos in El Retiro, the most expensive. In Bogotá the median is 540 million pesos and the rent is 2,646,500 pesos per month.
