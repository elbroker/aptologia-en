How much does it cost to buy a house how big in Lima?

90 - 120 m²: 583,125 soles (USD 169,734); 120 - 160 m²: 770,000 soles (USD 224,129); More than 160 m²: 2,324,000 suns (USD 676,461) (mid-October of 2026).

In Lima, a house for sale from the group "90 - 120 m²" is requested in 583,125 soles (USD 169,734) in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 2,324,000 soles (USD 676,461) (+ 298.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "90 - 120 m²" (5,519 soles (USD 1,606) / m²) and the most expensive "More than 160 m²" (5,903 soles (USD 1,718) / m²).
