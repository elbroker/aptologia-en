How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 3+ bathrooms in El Porvenir, Rionegro in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 3+ bathrooms in El Porvenir, Rionegro is COP 3,700,000 per month, based on 81 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,000,000 to COP 6,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 42,308/m² and the median listed size is 90 m². That is 40% above the study's reference median for Rionegro (COP 2,650,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 90% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
