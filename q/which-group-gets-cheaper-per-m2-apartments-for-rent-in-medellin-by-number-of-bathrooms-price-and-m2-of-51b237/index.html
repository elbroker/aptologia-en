Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "1 bath" (43,750 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "2 bath" (50,000 pesos / m² per month).

In Medellín, an apartment for rent from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 2,200,000 pesos per month in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 8,500,000 pesos per month (+ 286.4%). For square meter, the cheapest is "1 bath" (43,750 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "2 bath" (50,000 pesos / m² per month).
