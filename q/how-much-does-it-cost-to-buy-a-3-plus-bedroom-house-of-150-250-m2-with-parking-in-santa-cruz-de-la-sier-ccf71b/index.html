How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 150–250 m² with parking in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² with parking for sale in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 146,650, based on 942 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 115,000 to USD 190,000. The median price per square metre is USD 775/m² and the median listed size is 192 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 160,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
