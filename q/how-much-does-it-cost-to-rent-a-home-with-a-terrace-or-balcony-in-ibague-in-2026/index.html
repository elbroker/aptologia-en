How much does it cost to rent a home with a terrace or balcony in Ibagué in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes with a terrace or balcony in Ibagué is COP 1,128,000 per month, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 927,900 to COP 1,777,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 20,000/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 13% above the study's reference median (COP 999,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 244,850. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 74.3% of listings include parking.
