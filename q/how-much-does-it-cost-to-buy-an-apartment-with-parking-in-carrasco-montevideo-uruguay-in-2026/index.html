How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking in Carrasco, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking for sale in Carrasco, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 491,200, based on 171 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 360,000 to USD 744,500. The median price per square metre is USD 3,655/m² and the median listed size is 140 m². That is 166% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 185,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
