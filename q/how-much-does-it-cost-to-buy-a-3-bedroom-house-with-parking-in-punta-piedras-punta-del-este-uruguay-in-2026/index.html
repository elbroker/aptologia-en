How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with parking in Punta Piedras, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with parking for sale in Punta Piedras, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 1,025,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 587,500 to USD 1,200,000. That is 46% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
