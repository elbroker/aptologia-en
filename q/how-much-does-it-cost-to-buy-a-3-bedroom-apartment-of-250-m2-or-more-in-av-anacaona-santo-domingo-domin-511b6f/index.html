How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 250 m² or more in Av. Anacaona, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 250 m² or more for sale in Av. Anacaona, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,245,500, based on 62 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 692,500 to USD 1,800,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,463/m² and the median listed size is 416 m². That is 419% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
