How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in El Redil, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in El Redil, Bogotá is COP 1,190,000 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,025,000 to COP 1,461,250. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 31,660/m² and the median listed size is 38.5 m². That is 51% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,450,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 250,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 2.9% of listings include parking.
