How much does an apartment rent in Barranquilla?

The gross return is 8.9% per year (rent per m² × 12), before expenditure.

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter of Barranquilla is Rio Alto, with 7.04 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is Puerta Dorada, with 3.22 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 2.30 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 340 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 8.9% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 16.7% of the rent (457,750 pesos per month in the median).
