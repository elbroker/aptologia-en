How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² in Bella Vista, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Bella Vista, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 325,000, based on 102 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 275,000 to USD 408,880. The median price per square metre is USD 1,614/m² and the median listed size is 196 m². That is 35% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
