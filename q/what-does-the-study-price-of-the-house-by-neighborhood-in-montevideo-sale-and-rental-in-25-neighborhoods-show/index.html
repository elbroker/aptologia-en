What does the study “Price of the house by neighborhood in Montevideo: sale and rental in 25 neighborhoods” show?

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter in Montevideo is Puerto Buceo, with USD 4,169 in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Union, with USD 1,864. For rent, the highest medium rental is requested in Punta Carretas (38,500 Uruguayan pesos (USD 958) per month) and the lowest in Union (25,000 Uruguayan pesos (USD 622)).
