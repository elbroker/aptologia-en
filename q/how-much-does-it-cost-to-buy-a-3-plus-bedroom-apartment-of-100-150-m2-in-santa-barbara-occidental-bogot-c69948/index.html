How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² in Santa Bárbara Occidental, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² for sale in Santa Bárbara Occidental, Bogotá is COP 1,010,000,000, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 810,000,000 to COP 1,212,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,117,816/m² and the median listed size is 125 m². That is 35% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,102,450. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
