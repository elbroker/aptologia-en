How much does it cost to rent a house with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 8,850 per month, based on 62 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 5,250 to USD 15,375. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 24.5/m² and the median listed size is 285 m². That is 21% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 7,300). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
