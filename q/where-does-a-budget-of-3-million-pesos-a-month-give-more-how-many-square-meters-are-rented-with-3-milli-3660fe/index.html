Where does a budget of 3 million pesos a month give more?

In Tocancipá: about 159 m².

With 3 million pesos a month, some 159 m² of apartment is rented in Tocancipá, the most surrendering city of 38, and 61 m² in El Retiro. In Bogotá they reach for 62 m² and in Medellín for 61 m².
