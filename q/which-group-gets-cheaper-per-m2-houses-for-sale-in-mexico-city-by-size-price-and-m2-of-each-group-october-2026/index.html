Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "90 - 120 m²" (40,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,166) / m²) and the most expensive "120 - 160 m²" (40,992 Mexican pesos (USD 2,220) / m²).

In Mexico City, a house for sale from the group "90 - 120 m²" is requested in 4,200,000 Mexican pesos (USD 227,452) in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 15,950,000 Mexican pesos (USD 863,775) (+ 279.8%).
