How much does it cost to rent a house in a gated complex of 150–250 m² in estrato 6 in Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in a gated complex of 150–250 m² in estrato 6 in Medellín is COP 9,500,000 per month, based on 230 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 7,800,000 to COP 11,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 47,222/m² and the median listed size is 203 m². That is 12% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 8,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,000,000. 98.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
