What does the study “Does it cost more than a pet-accepting rent? Estrato and city premium in Colombia” show?

An apartment that accepts pets is rented in Colombia + 0.9% against a comparable one that does not accept them (same city, estrato and rooms). The share of the offer that supports pets ranges from 7.4% on the estrato 2 to 18.3% on the estrato 1. By city, the premium goes from - 9.0% in Pereira to + 6.5% in Medellín.
