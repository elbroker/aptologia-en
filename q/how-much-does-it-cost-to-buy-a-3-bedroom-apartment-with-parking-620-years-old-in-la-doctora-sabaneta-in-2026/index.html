How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with parking 6–20 years old in La Doctora, Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking 6–20 years old for sale in La Doctora, Sabaneta is COP 510,000,000, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 446,500,000 to COP 644,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,716,418/m² and the median listed size is 76 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 550,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 403,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
