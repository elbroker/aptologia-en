How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 3 in Las Villas, Zipaquirá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 3 for sale in Las Villas, Zipaquirá is COP 299,750,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 256,500,000 to COP 335,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,332,906/m² and the median listed size is 65.5 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 267,500. 90.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
