How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Brooklin Paulista, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Brooklin Paulista, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 8,000 per month, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 4,150 to BRL 10,800. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 84.6/m² and the median listed size is 84 m². That is 121% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,584. 97.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
