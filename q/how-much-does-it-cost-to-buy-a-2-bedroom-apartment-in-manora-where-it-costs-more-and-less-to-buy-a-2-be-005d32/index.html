How much does it cost to buy a 2 bedroom apartment in Manorá?

USD 205,000 in the median (October of 2026); the central half between USD 162,500 and USD 230,000.

In Asunción, a 2 bedroom apartment costs USD 205,000 in the middle in Manorá, the most expensive neighborhood, and USD 117,155 in Ytay, the cheapest: 1.7 times less. We compare 14 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 bedrooms.
