How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Cedofeita, Santo Ildefonso, Sé, Miragaia, São Nicolau e Vitória, Oporto, Portugal in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Cedofeita, Santo Ildefonso, Sé, Miragaia, São Nicolau e Vitória, Oporto, Portugal is EUR 299,000, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 282,000 to EUR 355,000. The median price per square metre is EUR 5,588/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 30% below the study's reference median for Oporto (EUR 425,000). 25.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
