What's the most rent-making on Rionegro?

In estrato 3: 6.6% gross annual (October of 2026).

In Rionegro, buying an apartment to rent it yields more in estrato 3 (6.6% gross per year, 15.2 years of rent) and less in estrato 5 (5.8%).
