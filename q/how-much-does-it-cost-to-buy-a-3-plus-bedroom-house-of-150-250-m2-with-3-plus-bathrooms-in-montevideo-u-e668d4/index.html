How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 420,000, based on 340 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 298,000 to USD 639,250. The median price per square metre is USD 2,236/m² and the median listed size is 198 m². That is 56% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 269,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
