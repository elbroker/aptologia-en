How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom pet-friendly house of 250 m² or more in Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom pet-friendly houses of 250 m² or more in Medellín is COP 16,000,000 per month, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 12,000,000 to COP 20,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 40,000/m² and the median listed size is 365 m². That is 88% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 8,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 980,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 96.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
