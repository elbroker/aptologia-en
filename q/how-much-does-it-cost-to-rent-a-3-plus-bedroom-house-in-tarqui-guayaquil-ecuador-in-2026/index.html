How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house in Tarqui, Guayaquil, Ecuador in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses in Tarqui, Guayaquil, Ecuador is USD 1,150 per month, based on 80 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 650 to USD 2,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 5/m² and the median listed size is 193 m². That is 23% below the study's reference median for Guayaquil (USD 1,500). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
