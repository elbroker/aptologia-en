Where is it cheaper to buy a 2 room apartment in Medellín?

In San Antonio de Prado: 230 million pesos in the median.

In Medellín, an apartment of 2 rooms costs 1,500 million pesos in the medium in San Lucas, the most expensive neighborhood, and 230 million pesos in San Antonio de Prado, the cheapest: 6.5 times less. We compare 29 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
