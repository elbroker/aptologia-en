How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment on floors 4–8 0–5 years old in Conquistadores, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments on floors 4–8 0–5 years old for sale in Conquistadores, Medellín is COP 989,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 869,000,000 to COP 1,200,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,500,000/m² and the median listed size is 127 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 610,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
