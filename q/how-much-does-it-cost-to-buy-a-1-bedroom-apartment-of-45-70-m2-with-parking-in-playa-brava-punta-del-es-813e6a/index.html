How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with parking in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking for sale in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 210,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 193,752 to USD 242,500. The median price per square metre is USD 4,021/m² and the median listed size is 51 m². That is 42% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
