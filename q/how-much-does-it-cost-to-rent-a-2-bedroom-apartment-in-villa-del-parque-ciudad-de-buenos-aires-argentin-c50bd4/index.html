How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in Villa del Parque, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in Villa del Parque, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is ARS 1,250,000 per month, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from ARS 1,100,000 to ARS 1,575,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is ARS 21,212/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 14% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (ARS 1,100,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
