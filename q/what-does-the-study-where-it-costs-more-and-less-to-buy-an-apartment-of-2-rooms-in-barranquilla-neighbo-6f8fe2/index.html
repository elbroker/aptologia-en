What does the study “Where it costs more and less to buy an apartment of 2 rooms in Barranquilla: neighborhood ranking” show?

In Barranquilla, an apartment of 2 rooms costs 550 million pesos in the medium in Buenavista, the most expensive neighborhood, and 203 million pesos in Alameda, the cheapest: 2.7 times less. We compare 17 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
