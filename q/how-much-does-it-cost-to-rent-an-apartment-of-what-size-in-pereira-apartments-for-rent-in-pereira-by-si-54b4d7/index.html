How much does it cost to rent an apartment of what size in Pereira?

Less than 50 m²: 1,800,000 pesos per month; 50 - 70 m²: 1,950,000 pesos per month; 70 - 90 m²: 2,675,000 pesos per month; 90 - 120 m²: 3,500,000 pesos per month; 120 - 160 m²: 4,200,000 pesos per month; More than 160 m²: 5,100,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Pereira, an apartment for rent from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 1,800,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 5,100,000 pesos per month (+ 183.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (26,944 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (44,000 pesos / m² per month).
