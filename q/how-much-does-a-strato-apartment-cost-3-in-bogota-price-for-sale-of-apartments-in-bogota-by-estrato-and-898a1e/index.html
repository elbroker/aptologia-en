How much does a strato apartment cost 3 in Bogotá?

The median price requested is $285 million; a 2 room, $260 million.

In Bogotá, an apartment for sale of estrato 3 requests in the median $285 million ($5,625,000 per m²), one of estrato 4 $550 million and one of estrato 6 $1.53 billion. The square meter of estrato 6 costs 1.6 times that of strate 3.
