How much does it cost to buy a 2 room apartment in Santa Teresita?

815 million pesos in the medium (October of 2026); the central half between 538 million pesos and 1,058 million pesos.

In Cali, an apartment of 2 rooms costs 815 million pesos in the medium in Santa Teresita, the most expensive neighborhood, and 215 million pesos in Caney, the cheapest: 3.8 times less. We compare 16 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
