How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Loma de los González, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Loma de los González, Medellín is COP 8,200,000 per month, based on 53 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 6,500,000 to COP 11,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 59,420/m² and the median listed size is 138 m². That is 71% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
