And the cheapest?

Center, with 3.63 million pesos per m².

In Pereira, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is Pinares Alto (8.00 million pesos / m²) and the cheapest Centre (3.63 million pesos / m²), 2.2 times less. We compare 26 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
