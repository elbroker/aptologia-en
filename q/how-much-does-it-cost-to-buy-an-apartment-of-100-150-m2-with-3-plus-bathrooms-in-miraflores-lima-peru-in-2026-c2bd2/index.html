How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Miraflores, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Miraflores, Lima, Peru is USD 301,935, based on 79 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 279,000 to USD 390,144. The median price per square metre is USD 2,539/m² and the median listed size is 125 m². That is 17% above the study's reference median for Lima (USD 258,023). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
