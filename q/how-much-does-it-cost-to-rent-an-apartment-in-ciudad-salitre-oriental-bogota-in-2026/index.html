How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Ciudad Salitre Oriental, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Ciudad Salitre Oriental, Bogotá is COP 3,600,000 per month, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,800,000 to COP 4,500,000. That is 31% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,750,000).
