What homes are for sale for less than COP 250 million in Los Patios in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for less than COP 250 million in Los Patios have a median asking price of COP 180,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 155,000,000 to COP 200,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 2,852,128/m² and the median listed size is 62 m². That is 53% below the study's reference median (COP 380,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 190,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 48.4% of listings include parking.
