What does the study “Apartments for rent in Bucaramanga by parking lot: price and m² of each group” show?

In Bucharest, an apartment for rent from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 1,350,000 pesos per month in the medium and one from the group "2 or more parking lot" in 4,600,000 pesos per month (+ 240.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (24,000 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (34,524 pesos / m² per month).
