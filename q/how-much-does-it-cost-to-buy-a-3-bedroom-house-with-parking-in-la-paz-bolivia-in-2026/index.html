How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with parking in La Paz, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with parking for sale in La Paz, Bolivia is USD 390,000, based on 215 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 250,000 to USD 630,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,011/m² and the median listed size is 362 m². That is 14% above the study's reference median for La Paz (USD 343,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
