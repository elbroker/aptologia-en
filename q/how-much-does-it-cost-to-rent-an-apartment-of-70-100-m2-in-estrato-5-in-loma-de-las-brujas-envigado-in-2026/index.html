How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 70–100 m² in estrato 5 in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in estrato 5 in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado is COP 4,400,000 per month, based on 106 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,925,000 to COP 4,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 52,500/m² and the median listed size is 85.5 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 4,400,000). 97.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
