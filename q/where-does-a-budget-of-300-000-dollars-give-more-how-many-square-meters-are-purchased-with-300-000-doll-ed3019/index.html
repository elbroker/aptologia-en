Where does a budget of 300,000 dollars give more?

In Maracaibo (Venezuela): about 396 m².

With 300,000 dollars are bought about 396 m² of apartment in Maracaibo (Venezuela), the most surrendering city of 30, and only 36 m² in Lisbon (Portugal).
