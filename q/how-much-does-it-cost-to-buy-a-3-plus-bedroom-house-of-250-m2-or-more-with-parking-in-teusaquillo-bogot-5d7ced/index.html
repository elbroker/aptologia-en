How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more with parking in Teusaquillo, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more with parking for sale in Teusaquillo, Bogotá is COP 1,775,000,000, based on 100 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,450,000,000 to COP 2,500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,449,153/m² and the median listed size is 400 m². That is 48% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
