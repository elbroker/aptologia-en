What does the study “Cheapest and cheaper city of Buenos Aires to buy apartment: ranking per m²” show?

In Buenos Aires City, the most expensive district for buying apartment is Puerto Madero (USD 5,712 / m²) and the cheapest New Pompeya (USD 1,189 / m²), 4.8 times less. We compare 46 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
