How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms in Providencia, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Providencia, Santiago, Chile is UF 6,000, based on 125 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 5,450 to UF 6,850. The median price per square metre is UF 100/m² and the median listed size is 62 m². That is 14% below the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 6,950). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
