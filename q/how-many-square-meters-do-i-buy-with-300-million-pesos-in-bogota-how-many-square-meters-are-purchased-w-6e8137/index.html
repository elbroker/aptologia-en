How many square meters do I buy with 300 million pesos in Bogotá?

Some 41 m² at average price per m² of October 2026.

With 300 million pesos, some 88 m² of apartment is bought in Soacha, the most surrendering city of 38, and 32 m² in El Retiro. In Bogotá they reach for 41 m² and in Medellín for 40 m².
