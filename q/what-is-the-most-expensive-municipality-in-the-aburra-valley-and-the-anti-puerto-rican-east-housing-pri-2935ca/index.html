What is the most expensive municipality in the Aburrá Valley and the Anti-Puerto Rican East?

El Retiro, with 9,321,839 pesos per m² in apartments for sale.

In the Valley of Aburrá and the Anti-Puerto Rican East, the most expensive square meter of apartment is requested in El Retiro (9.32 million pesos) and the cheapest in El Carmen de Viboral (4.48 million pesos). Medellin marks 7.50 million pesos per m² and an apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 4,200,000 pesos per month in the medium.
