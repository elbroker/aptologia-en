How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more in estrato 6 in Pance, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more in estrato 6 for sale in Pance, Cali is COP 2,500,000,000, based on 739 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,800,000,000 to COP 3,500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,319,624/m² and the median listed size is 469 m². That is 169% above the study's reference median for Cali (COP 930,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,500,000. 99.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
