How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 6,450 per month, based on 62 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 4,050 to USD 12,450. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 53.4/m² and the median listed size is 129 m². That is 34% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 4,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
