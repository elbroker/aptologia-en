How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² for sale in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 325,000, based on 87 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 285,000 to USD 380,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,586/m² and the median listed size is 125 m². That is 35% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
