How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Piantini?

USD 1,950 per month in the median (October of 2026); the central half between USD 1,600 and USD 2,500.

In Santo Domingo, an apartment of 2 rooms is rented for USD 1,950 per month in the medium in Piantini, the most expensive sector, and USD 1,100 per month in Gazcue, the cheapest: 1.8 times less. We compare 7 sectors with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
