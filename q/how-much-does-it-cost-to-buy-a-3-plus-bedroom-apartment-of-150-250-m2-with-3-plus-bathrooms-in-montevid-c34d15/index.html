How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 650,000, based on 157 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 510,000 to USD 807,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,465/m² and the median listed size is 193 m². That is 251% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 185,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
