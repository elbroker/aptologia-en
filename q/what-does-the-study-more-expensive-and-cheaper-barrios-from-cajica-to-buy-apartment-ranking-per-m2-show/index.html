What does the study “More expensive and cheaper Barrios from Cajicá to buy apartment: ranking per m²” show?

In Cajicá, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is Calahorra (6.88 million pesos / m²) and the cheapest Capellania (4.79 million pesos / m²), 1.4 times less. We compare 10 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
