How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Manizales in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Manizales is COP 550,000,000, based on 77 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 485,000,000 to COP 750,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,626,506/m² and the median listed size is 90 m². That is 22% above the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 419,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 94.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
