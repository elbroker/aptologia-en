How much does it cost to rent a house in estrato 6 in Altos del Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in estrato 6 in Altos del Poblado, Medellín is COP 13,400,000 per month, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 10,000,000 to COP 22,250,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 35,890/m² and the median listed size is 350 m². That is 58% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 8,500,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
