How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many parking lots in Manizales?

No parking lot: 300 million pesos; 1 parking lot: 460 million pesos; 2 or more parking lot: 750 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Manizales, an apartment for sale from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 300 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "2 or more parking lot" in 750 million pesos (+ 150.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (4.96 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "1 parking lot" (6.00 million pesos / m²).
