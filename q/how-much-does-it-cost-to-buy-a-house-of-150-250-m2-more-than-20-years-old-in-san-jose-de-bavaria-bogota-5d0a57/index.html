How much does it cost to buy a house of 150–250 m² more than 20 years old in San José de Bavaria, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 150–250 m² more than 20 years old for sale in San José de Bavaria, Bogotá is COP 1,599,500,000, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,276,250,000 to COP 1,800,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,767,394/m² and the median listed size is 201 m². That is 33% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 810,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 97.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
