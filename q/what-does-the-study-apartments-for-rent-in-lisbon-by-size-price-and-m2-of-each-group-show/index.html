What does the study “Apartments for rent in Lisbon by size: price and m² of each group” show?

In Lisbon, an apartment for rent from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 1,300 EUR (USD 1,461) per month in the medium and one from the group "120 - 160 m²" in 2,500 EUR (USD 2,810) per month (+ 92.3%). The cheapest per square metre is "120 - 160 m²" (19.0 EUR (USD 21.4) / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (33.1 EUR (USD 37.2) / m² per month).
