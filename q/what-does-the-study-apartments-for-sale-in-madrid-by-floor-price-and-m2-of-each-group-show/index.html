What does the study “Apartments for sale in Madrid by floor: price and m² of each group” show?

In Madrid, an apartment for sale of the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 220 million pesos in the median and one of the group "Piso 11 - 20" in 204 million pesos (- 7.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 6 - 10" (3.50 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 1 - 2" (3.79 million pesos / m²).
