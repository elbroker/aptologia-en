How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many parking lots in Medellín?

No parking lot: 380 million pesos; 1 parking lot: 690 million pesos; 2 or more parking lot: 1,270 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Medellín, an apartment for sale from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 380 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "2 or more parking lot" in 1,270 million pesos (+ 234.2%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (5.32 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (8.18 million pesos / m²).
