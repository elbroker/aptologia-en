How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in San Cristóbal, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in San Cristóbal, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 105,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 89,475 to USD 137,500. The median price per square metre is USD 1,645/m² and the median listed size is 59.5 m². That is 34% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 160,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
