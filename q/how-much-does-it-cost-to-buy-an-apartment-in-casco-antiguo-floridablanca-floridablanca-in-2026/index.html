How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Casco Antiguo Floridablanca, Floridablanca in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Casco Antiguo Floridablanca, Floridablanca is COP 390,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 275,000,000 to COP 463,500,000. That is 11% below the study's reference median for Floridablanca (COP 440,000,000).
