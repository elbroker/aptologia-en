How much does it cost to rent a home in Mburicaó, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Mburicaó, Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 6,800,000 per month, based on 67 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 5,500,000 to PYG 12,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is PYG 32,491/m² and the median listed size is 272 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
