How much does it cost to rent an apartment in San Isidro, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in San Isidro, Argentina is USD 1,800 per month, based on 65 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,500 to USD 2,700. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 18.6/m² and the median listed size is 104 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
