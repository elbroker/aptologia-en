What is the most common price range in Medellín?

More than 1,200 M of pesos, with 28.9% of the ads.

In Medellín, the price range with the most offer of apartments for sale is over 1,200 M of pesos (28.9% of the ads), with a median area of 192 m². 8.7% of the supply costs less than 350 million.
