How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Renacimiento, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Renacimiento, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 385,000, based on 65 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 338,400 to USD 559,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,651/m² and the median listed size is 248 m². That is 60% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
