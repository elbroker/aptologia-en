How much does it cost to buy a home in Barrio Blanco, Cúcuta in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Barrio Blanco, Cúcuta is COP 425,000,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 310,000,000 to COP 696,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 2,919,838/m² and the median listed size is 144 m². That is 12% above the study's reference median for Cúcuta (COP 380,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 492,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 96.9% of listings include parking.
