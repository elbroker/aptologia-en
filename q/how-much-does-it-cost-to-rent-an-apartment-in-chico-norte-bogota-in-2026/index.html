How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Chico Norte, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Chico Norte, Bogotá is COP 6,600,000 per month, based on 649 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,292,500 to COP 10,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 72,033/m² and the median listed size is 92.5 m². That is 89% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,100,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 90.3% of listings include parking.
