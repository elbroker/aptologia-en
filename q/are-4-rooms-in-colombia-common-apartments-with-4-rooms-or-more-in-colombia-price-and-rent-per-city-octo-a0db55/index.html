Are 4 rooms in Colombia common?

No: they are 10.0% of the apartments for sale.

An apartment of 4 rooms or more is requested in the median in 400 million pesos in Cúcuta, the cheapest city of 20, and in 1,650 million pesos in Bogotá, the most expensive. In Medellín it costs 1,100 million pesos. These units are only 10.0% of the apartments for sale in the country.
