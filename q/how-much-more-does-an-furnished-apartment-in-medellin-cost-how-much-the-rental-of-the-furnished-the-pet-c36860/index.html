How much more does an furnished apartment in Medellín cost?

+ 40.9% in the rent in front of comparable apartments without amoblar.

In Medellín, the most important feature of the rental in front of comparable apartments in the same neighborhood, rooms and size is "furnished": + 40.9% in the rental. The least heavy is "closed set" (+ 3.2%).
