How much does it cost to rent an apartment with how many bathrooms in Santo Domingo?

1 bathroom: USD 1,100 per month; 2 bathrooms: USD 1,350 per month; 3 bathrooms: USD 2,100 per month; 4 or more bathrooms: USD 2,500 per month (mid-October of 2026).

In Santo Domingo, an apartment for rent from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in USD 1,100 per month in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in USD 2,500 per month (+ 127.3%). per square metre, the cheapest is "4 or more bathrooms" (USD 10.1 / m² per month) and the most expensive "1 bathroom" (USD 15.0 / m² per month).
