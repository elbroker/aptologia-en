What does the study “Renovated apartment for rent in Bogotá: the more you pay (2026)” show?

In Bogotá, an apartment for rent announced as remodeled costs 5.4% less per square meter than a comparable one of the same estrato and number of rooms. Thus 1,246 of 18,920 ads are announced (6.6%); the medium rental is $6,400,000 with the attribute and $2,305,500 without it.
