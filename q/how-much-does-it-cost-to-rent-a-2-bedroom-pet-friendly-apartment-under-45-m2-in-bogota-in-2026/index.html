How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom pet-friendly apartment under 45 m² in Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom pet-friendly apartments under 45 m² in Bogotá is COP 1,590,000 per month, based on 95 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,325,000 to COP 1,900,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 40,000/m² and the median listed size is 40 m². That is 52% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 206,000. The most frequent estrato is 3, 28.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
