How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms in Vila Olímpia, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms in Vila Olímpia, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 8,025 per month, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 6,859 to BRL 10,165. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 100/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 122% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,536. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
