How much does it cost to rent an apartment on floors 4–8 in El Tesoro, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments on floors 4–8 in El Tesoro, Medellín is COP 8,200,000 per month, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,750,000 to COP 11,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 49,167/m² and the median listed size is 153 m². That is 71% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 89.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
