How much does it cost to buy an apartment 0–5 years old in estrato 5 in Belén Rosales, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments 0–5 years old in estrato 5 for sale in Belén Rosales, Medellín is COP 862,500,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 627,500,000 to COP 1,067,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,000,000/m² and the median listed size is 128 m². That is 3% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 225,000. The median number of bathrooms is 2.5.
