How much does it cost to buy a house with how many parking lots in Envigado?

No parking lot: 990 million pesos; 1 parking lot: 1,100 million pesos; 2 or more parking lot: 2,450 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Envigado, a house for sale from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 990 million pesos in the medium and one from the "2 or more parking lot" in 2,450 million pesos (+ 147.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (5.32 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (7.17 million pesos / m²).
