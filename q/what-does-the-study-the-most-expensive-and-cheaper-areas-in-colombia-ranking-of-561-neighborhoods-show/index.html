What does the study “The most expensive and cheaper areas in Colombia: ranking of 561 neighborhoods” show?

The district of Colombia with the highest rate of HOA fee per square metre is Centro Histórico (Santa Marta): 11,268 pesos per m² per month in the medium (625,000 pesos of monthly rate). The lowest is in Simón Bolívar (Medellín), with 2,009 pesos per m². Of the 40 neighborhoods with the most expensive HOA fee per m², 32 are in Bogotá.
