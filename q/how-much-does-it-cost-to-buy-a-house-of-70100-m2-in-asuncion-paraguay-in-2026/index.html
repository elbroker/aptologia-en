How much does it cost to buy a house of 70–100 m² in Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 70–100 m² for sale in Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 700,000,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 550,000,000 to PYG 921,375,000. The median price per square metre is PYG 8,416,667/m² and the median listed size is 81 m². The median number of bathrooms is 1.
