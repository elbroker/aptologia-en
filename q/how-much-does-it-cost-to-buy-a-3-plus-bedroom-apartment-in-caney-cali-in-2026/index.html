How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Caney, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Caney, Cali is COP 300,000,000, based on 127 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 39% below the study's reference median for Cali (COP 490,000,000).
