How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in estrato 4 in Aves María, Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments in estrato 4 for sale in Aves María, Sabaneta is COP 650,000,000, based on 208 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 550,000,000 to COP 802,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,470,588/m² and the median listed size is 99 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 550,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 460,500. 90.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
