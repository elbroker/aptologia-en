How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 2 bathrooms in Centro, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 2 bathrooms in Centro, Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 37,000 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 31,250 to UYU 45,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 442/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 23% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 30,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 6,200. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
