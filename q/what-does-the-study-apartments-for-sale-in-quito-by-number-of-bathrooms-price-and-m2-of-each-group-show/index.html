What does the study “Apartments for sale in Quito by number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In Quito, a department for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in USD 75,000 in the median and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in USD 267,500 (+ 256.7%). per square metre, the cheapest is "4 or more bathrooms" (USD 853 / m²) and the most expensive "2 bathrooms" (USD 1,030 / m²).
