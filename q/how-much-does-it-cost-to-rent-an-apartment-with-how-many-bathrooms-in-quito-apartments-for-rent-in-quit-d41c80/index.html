How much does it cost to rent an apartment with how many bathrooms in Quito?

1 bathroom: USD 460 per month; 2 bathrooms: USD 800 per month; 3 bathrooms: USD 1,250 per month; 4 or more bathrooms: USD 2,000 per month (mid-October of 2026).

In Quito, a rental department of the group "1 bathroom" is ordered in USD 460 per month in the median and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in USD 2,000 per month (+ 334.8%). per square metre, the cheapest is "4 or more bathrooms" (USD 6.8 / m² per month) and the most expensive "1 bathroom" (USD 8.0 / m² per month).
