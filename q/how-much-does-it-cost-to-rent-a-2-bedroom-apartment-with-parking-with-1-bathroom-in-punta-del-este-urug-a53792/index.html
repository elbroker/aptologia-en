How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment with parking with 1 bathroom in Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments with parking with 1 bathroom in Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 2,400 per month, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,700 to USD 4,075. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 34.2/m² and the median listed size is 62.5 m². That is 50% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 4,800). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
