How much does it cost to buy an apartment on which floor in Villavicencio?

Floor 1 - 2: 280 million pesos; Floor 3 - 5: 240 million pesos; Floor 6 - 10: 360 million pesos; Floor 11 - 20: 321 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Villavicencio, an apartment for sale of the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 280 million pesos in the median and one of the group "Piso 11 - 20" in 321 million pesos (+ 14.6%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 3 - 5" (3.93 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 6 - 10" (5.08 million pesos / m²).
