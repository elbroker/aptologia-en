How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Laureles, Manizales in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Laureles, Manizales is COP 545,000,000, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 450,000,000 to COP 712,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,993,675/m² and the median listed size is 88.5 m². That is 21% above the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 440,250. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
