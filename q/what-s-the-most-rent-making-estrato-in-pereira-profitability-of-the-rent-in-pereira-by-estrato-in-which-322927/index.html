What's the most rent-making estrato in Pereira?

In estrato 4: 7.4% gross annual (October of 2026).

In Pereira, buying an apartment to rent it yields more in estrato 4 (7.4% gross per year, 13.5 years of rent) and less in estrato 5 (6.6%).
