Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For each square metre, the cheapest is "50 - 70 m²" (30,832 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (37,179 pesos / m² per month).

In Cajicá, an apartment for rent from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 1,566,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "90 - 120 m²" in 3,500,000 pesos per month (+ 123.5%).
