What does the study “Rate of HOA fee in Floridablanca by estrato: how much is paid per month and per m²” show?

In Floridablanca, the median rate of HOA fee of an apartment goes from 225,000 pesos per month in estrato 3 to 475,000 pesos in estrato 5, 2.1 times more. per square meter, it goes from 3,704 pesos to 4,986 pesos per month.
