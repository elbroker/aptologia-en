How much does it cost to buy a home in General Paz, Córdoba, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in General Paz, Córdoba, Argentina is USD 122,500, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 90,000 to USD 169,774. The median price per square metre is USD 1,787/m² and the median listed size is 69.5 m². The median number of bathrooms is 1.
