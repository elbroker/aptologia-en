How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in Loma de San Julian, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Loma de San Julian, Medellín is COP 4,000,000 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 27% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 5,500,000).
