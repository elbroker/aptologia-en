How much does it cost to buy an apartment of what size in Punta del Este?

Less than 50 m²: USD 160,000; 50 - 70 m²: USD 210,000; 70 - 90 m²: USD 270,000; 90 - 120 m²: USD 357,000; 120 - 160 m²: USD 485,000; More than 160 m²: USD 870,000 (mid-October of 2026).

In Punta del Este, an apartment for sale from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in USD 160,000 in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in USD 870,000 (+ 443.8%). For square meter, the cheapest is "70 - 90 m²" (USD 3,412 / m²) and the most expensive "More than 160 m²" (USD 4,000 / m²).
