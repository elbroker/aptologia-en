How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Puerto Espejo, Armenia in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Puerto Espejo, Armenia is COP 225,000,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 34% below the study's reference median for Armenia (COP 340,000,000).
