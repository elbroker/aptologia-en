How much does it cost to buy a home with a fireplace in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes with a fireplace for sale in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá is COP 1,145,000,000, based on 64 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 807,500,000 to COP 2,900,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,969,697/m² and the median listed size is 238 m². That is 152% above the study's reference median (COP 454,982,760). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 658,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 82.8% of listings include parking.
