How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom for sale in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 229,000, based on 75 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 187,000 to USD 265,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,789/m² and the median listed size is 78 m². That is 39% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
