How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment with parking with 1 bathroom in Brooklin, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments with parking with 1 bathroom in Brooklin, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 4,350 per month, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 3,600 to BRL 5,100. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 103/m² and the median listed size is 45 m². That is 20% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 830. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
