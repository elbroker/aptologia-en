How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Chico Santa Barbara, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Chico Santa Barbara, Bogotá is COP 900,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 685,000,000 to COP 1,275,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,515,924/m² and the median listed size is 140 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 969,500. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 96.8% of listings include parking.
