How much does it cost to buy a 2 room apartment in Itagüí?

465 million pesos in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the supply is between 370 million pesos and 530 million pesos.

In Itagüí, a 1 room apartment costs 378 million pesos in the medium, one 2 million rooms 465 million pesos (+ 23.2%) and one 3 or more 479 million pesos (+ 3.0%).
