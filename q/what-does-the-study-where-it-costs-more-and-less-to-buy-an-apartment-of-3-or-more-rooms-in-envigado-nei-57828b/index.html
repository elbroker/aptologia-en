What does the study “Where it costs more and less to buy an apartment of 3 or more rooms in Envigado: neighborhood ranking” show?

In Envigado, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs 1,290 million pesos in the medium in Loma Benedictinos, the most expensive neighborhood, and 350 million pesos in La Mina, the cheapest: 3.7 times less. We compare 33 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
