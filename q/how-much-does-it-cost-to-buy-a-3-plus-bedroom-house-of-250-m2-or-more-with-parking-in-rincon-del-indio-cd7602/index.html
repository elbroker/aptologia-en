How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more with parking in Rincón del Indio, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more with parking for sale in Rincón del Indio, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 967,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 730,000 to USD 1,600,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,609/m² and the median listed size is 365 m². That is 38% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
