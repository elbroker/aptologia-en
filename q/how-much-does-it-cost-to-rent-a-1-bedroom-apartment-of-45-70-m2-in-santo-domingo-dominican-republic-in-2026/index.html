How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is DOP 39,950 per month, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from DOP 24,500 to DOP 50,542. The median monthly rent per square metre is DOP 660/m² and the median listed size is 56.5 m². That is 4% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (DOP 38,500). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
