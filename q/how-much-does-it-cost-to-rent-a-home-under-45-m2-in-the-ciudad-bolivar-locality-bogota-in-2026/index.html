How much does it cost to rent a home under 45 m² in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes under 45 m² in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá is COP 1,200,000 per month, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,075,000 to COP 1,350,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 31,250/m² and the median listed size is 41 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median (COP 1,250,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 133,700. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 6.6% of listings include parking.
