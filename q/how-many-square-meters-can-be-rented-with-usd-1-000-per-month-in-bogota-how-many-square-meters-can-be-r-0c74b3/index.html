How many square meters can be rented with USD 1,000 per month in Bogotá?

Some 66 m² of apartment, according to the median of October of 2026.

With USD 1,000 per month you can rent 250 m² of apartment in Cochabamba (Bolivia), the most successful city, and only 25 m² in Punta del Este (Uruguay). In Bogotá they reach for 66 m².
