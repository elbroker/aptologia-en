How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² in Los Colores, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Los Colores, Medellín is COP 700,000,000, based on 111 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 583,000,000 to COP 790,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,732,709/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 17% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 410,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 88% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
