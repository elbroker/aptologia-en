Where is it cheaper to buy apartment in Caracas?

Among the areas with 30 ads or more, in Sucre: USD 1,000 per m².

The area with the most expensive square meter of Caracas is Chacao, with USD 2,472 in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Sucre, with USD 1,000. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented per USD 950 per month in the medium and sold per USD 160,000. The gross implicit return on rent is 10.4% per year.
