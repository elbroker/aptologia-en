How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with parking in Zipaquirá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking for sale in Zipaquirá is COP 268,000,000, based on 243 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 238,500,000 to COP 295,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,649,123/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 3% below the study's reference median for Zipaquirá (COP 275,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 220,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
