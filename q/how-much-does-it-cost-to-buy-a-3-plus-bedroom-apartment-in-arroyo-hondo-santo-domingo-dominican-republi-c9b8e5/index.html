How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Arroyo Hondo, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Arroyo Hondo, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is DOP 8,200,000, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from DOP 7,369,000 to DOP 8,700,000. The median price per square metre is DOP 62,143/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (DOP 8,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
