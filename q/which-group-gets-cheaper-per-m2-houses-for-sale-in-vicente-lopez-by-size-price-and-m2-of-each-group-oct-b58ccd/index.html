Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (USD 1,709 / m²) and the most expensive "90 - 120 m²" (USD 2,084 / m²).

In Vicente López, a house for sale from the group "90 - 120 m²" is requested in USD 212,500 in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in USD 390,000 (+ 83.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (USD 1,709 / m²) and the most expensive "90 - 120 m²" (USD 2,084 / m²).
