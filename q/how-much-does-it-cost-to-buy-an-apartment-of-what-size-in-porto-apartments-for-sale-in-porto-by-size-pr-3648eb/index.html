How much does it cost to buy an apartment of what size in Porto?

Less than 50 m²: 255,000 EUR (USD 286,641); 50 - 70 m²: 299,000 EUR (USD 336,101; 70 - 90 m²: 385,000 EUR (USD 432,772); 90 - 120 m²: 479,250 EUR (USD 538,717; 120 - 160 m²: 650,000 EUR (USD 730,654); More than 160 m²: 1,184,000 EUR (USD 1,330,914) (mid-October of 2026).

In Porto, an apartment for sale from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 255,000 euros (USD 286,641) in the median and one from the group "More 160 m²" in 1,184,000 euros (USD 1,330,914) (+ 364.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "90 - 120 m²" (4,698 EUR (USD 5,281) / m²) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (6,146 EUR (USD 6,908) / m²).
