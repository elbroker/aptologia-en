What does the study “Where it is most expensive and cheaper to rent an apartment 2 rooms in Bogotá: neighborhood ranking” show?

In Bogotá, an apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 12,250,000 pesos a month in the middle of La Cabrera, the most expensive neighborhood, and 1,300,000 pesos a month in Madelena, the cheapest: 9.4 times less. We compare 24 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
