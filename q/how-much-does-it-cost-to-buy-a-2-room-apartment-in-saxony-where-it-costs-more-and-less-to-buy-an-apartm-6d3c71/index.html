How much does it cost to buy a 2 room apartment in Saxony?

1,250 million pesos in the medium (October of 2026); the central half between 1,150 million pesos and 1,358 million pesos.

In Rionegro, an apartment of 2 rooms costs 1,250 million pesos in the medium in Saxony, the most expensive neighborhood, and 288 million pesos in Four Esquinas, the cheapest: 4.3 times less. We compare 12 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
