How much does it cost to rent a furnished studio apartment in Santa Bárbara, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for furnished studio apartments in Santa Bárbara, Bogotá is COP 2,575,000 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,000,000 to COP 3,325,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 81,875/m² and the median listed size is 29 m². That is 36% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,900,000). The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
