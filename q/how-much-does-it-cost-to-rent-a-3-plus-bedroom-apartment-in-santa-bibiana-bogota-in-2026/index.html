How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in Santa Bibiana, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Santa Bibiana, Bogotá is COP 7,000,000 per month, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 5% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 7,400,000).
