How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with 3+ bathrooms in Villa Devoto, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Villa Devoto, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 510,000, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 386,250 to USD 722,425. The median price per square metre is USD 2,165/m² and the median listed size is 246 m². That is 76% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 290,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
