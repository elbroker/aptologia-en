How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 6 in Chicó Navarra, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 6 for sale in Chicó Navarra, Bogotá is COP 870,000,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 782,500,000 to COP 991,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,119,048/m² and the median listed size is 92 m². That is 16% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 895,800. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
