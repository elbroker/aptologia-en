How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 3 in Las Aguas, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 3 for sale in Las Aguas, Bogotá is COP 479,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 275,000,000 to COP 780,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,833,333/m² and the median listed size is 82 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median (COP 514,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 190,000. 21.2% of listings include parking.
