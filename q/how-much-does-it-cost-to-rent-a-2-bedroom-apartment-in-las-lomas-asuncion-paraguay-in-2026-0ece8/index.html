How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in Las Lomas, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in Las Lomas, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 1,500 per month, based on 99 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,215 to USD 1,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 15.8/m² and the median listed size is 85.5 m². That is 11% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 1,350). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
