How much does it cost to buy a house in Sur, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Sur, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 130,000, based on 541 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 90,000 to USD 220,316. The median price per square metre is USD 667/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 19% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 160,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
