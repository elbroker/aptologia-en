How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Bogotá?

2,300,000 pesos per month in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between 1,500,000 pesos and 4,200,000 pesos.

In Bogotá, a 1 room apartment is rented for 2,211,500 pesos per month in the medium, one of 2 rooms 2,300,000 pesos (+ 4.0%) and one of 3 or more 6,300,000 pesos (+ 173.9%).
