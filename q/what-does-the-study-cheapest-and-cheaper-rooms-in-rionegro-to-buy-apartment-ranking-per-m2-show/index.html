What does the study “Cheapest and cheaper rooms in Rionegro to buy apartment: ranking per m²” show?

In Rionegro, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is Llanogrande (11.11 million pesos / m²) and the cheapest Cimarronas (5.73 million pesos / m²), 1.9 times less. We compare 18 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
