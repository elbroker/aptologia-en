How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment with 1 bathroom in Fontibón, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom in Fontibón, Bogotá is COP 1,300,000 per month, based on 49 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,200,000 to COP 1,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 31,111/m² and the median listed size is 42 m². That is 61% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 200,200. The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
