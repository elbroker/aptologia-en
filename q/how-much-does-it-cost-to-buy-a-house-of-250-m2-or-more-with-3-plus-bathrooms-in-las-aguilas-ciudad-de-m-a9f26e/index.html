How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more with 3+ bathrooms in Las Águilas, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Las Águilas, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 16,500,000, based on 75 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 10,950,000 to MXN 19,650,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 36,413/m² and the median listed size is 405 m². That is 12% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 14,750,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
