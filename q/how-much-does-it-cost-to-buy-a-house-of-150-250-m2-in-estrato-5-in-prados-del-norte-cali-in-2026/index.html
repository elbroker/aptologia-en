How much does it cost to buy a house of 150–250 m² in estrato 5 in Prados del Norte, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 150–250 m² in estrato 5 for sale in Prados del Norte, Cali is COP 695,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 620,000,000 to COP 790,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,263,158/m² and the median listed size is 220 m². That is 25% below the study's reference median for Cali (COP 930,000,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
