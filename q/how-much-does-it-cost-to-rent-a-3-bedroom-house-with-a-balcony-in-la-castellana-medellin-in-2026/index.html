How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house with a balcony in La Castellana, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses with a balcony in La Castellana, Medellín is COP 6,650,000 per month, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,000,000 to COP 8,900,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 29,667/m² and the median listed size is 216 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 8,500,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 76.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
