How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² on floors 4–8 in El Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² on floors 4–8 for sale in El Poblado, Medellín is COP 807,500,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 672,500,000 to COP 975,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,398,356/m² and the median listed size is 83 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 681,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 94.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
