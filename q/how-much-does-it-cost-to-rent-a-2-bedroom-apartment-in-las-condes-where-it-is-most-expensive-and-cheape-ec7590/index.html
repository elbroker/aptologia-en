How much does it cost to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in Las Condes?

1,365,000 Chilean pesos (USD 1,389) per month in the median (October of 2026); the central half between 1,100,000 Chilean pesos (USD 1,120) and 1,712,500 Chilean pesos (USD 1,743).

In Santiago, a 2 bedroom department is rented for 1,365,000 Chilean pesos (USD 1,389) per month in the median in Las Condes, the most expensive commune, and 360,000 Chilean pesos (USD 366) per month in Central Station, the cheapest: 3.8 times less. We compare 9 communes with at least 30 ads from 2 bedrooms.
