How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Santa Paula, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Santa Paula, Bogotá is COP 5,050,000 per month, based on 134 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,500,000 to COP 8,442,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 57,080/m² and the median listed size is 95.5 m². That is 44% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 965,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 96.3% of listings include parking.
