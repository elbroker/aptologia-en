How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more in San Bartolo Ameyalco, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more for sale in San Bartolo Ameyalco, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 30,250,000, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 17,275,000 to MXN 52,500,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 50,052/m² and the median listed size is 520 m². That is 105% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 14,750,000). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
