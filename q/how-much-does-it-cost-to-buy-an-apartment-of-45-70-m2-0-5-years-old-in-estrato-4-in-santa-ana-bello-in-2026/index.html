How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 45–70 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 4 in Santa Ana, Bello in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 4 for sale in Santa Ana, Bello is COP 399,500,000, based on 66 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 360,000,000 to COP 430,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,711,851/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Bello (COP 340,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 261,000. 95.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
