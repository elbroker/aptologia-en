How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 3 or more bedrooms in Carrasco?

USD 595,000 in the median (October of 2026); the central half between USD 485,975 and USD 990,000.

In Montevideo, an apartment of 3 or more bedrooms costs USD 595,000 in the middle in Carrasco, the most expensive neighborhood, and USD 188,000 in Central, the cheapest: 3.2 times less. We compare 7 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more bedrooms.
