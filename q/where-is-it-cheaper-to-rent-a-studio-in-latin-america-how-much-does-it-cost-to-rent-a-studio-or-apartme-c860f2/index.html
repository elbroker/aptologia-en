Where is it cheaper to rent a studio in Latin America?

In Cúcuta, Colombia: USD 298 per month in the median.

To rent a studio or apartment 1 is cheaper in Cúcuta (Colombia), with a median of USD 298 per month, and more expensive in Punta del Este (Uruguay), with USD 2,500. In Bogotá the median is USD 685 per month.
