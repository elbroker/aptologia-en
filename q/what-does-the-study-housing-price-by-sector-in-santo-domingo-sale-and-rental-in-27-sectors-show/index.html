What does the study “Housing price by sector in Santo Domingo: sale and rental in 27 sectors” show?

The sector with the most expensive square meter of Santo Domingo is Piantini, with USD 2,633 in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Prado Oriental, with USD 1,140. For rent, the highest medium rental is requested in Av. Anacaona (USD 4,000 per month) and the lowest in El Vergel (USD 950).
