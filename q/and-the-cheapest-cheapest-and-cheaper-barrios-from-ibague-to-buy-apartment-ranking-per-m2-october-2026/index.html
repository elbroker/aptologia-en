And the cheapest?

Picaleña and Mirolindo, with 4.07 million pesos per m².

In Ibagué, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is Warsaw (4.91 million pesos / m²) and the cheapest Picaleña and Mirolindo (4.07 million pesos / m²), 1.2 times less. We compare 7 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
