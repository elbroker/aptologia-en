How much does it cost to buy an apartment on floors 1–3 0–5 years old in estrato 6 in Chicó Norte, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments on floors 1–3 0–5 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Chicó Norte, Bogotá is COP 920,000,000, based on 118 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 680,000,000 to COP 1,300,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,158,087/m² and the median listed size is 112 m². That is 23% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,078,500. 99.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
