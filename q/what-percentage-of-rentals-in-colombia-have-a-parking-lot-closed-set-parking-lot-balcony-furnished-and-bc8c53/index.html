What percentage of rentals in Colombia have a parking lot?

87.6% of the apartment ads.

In Pereira the 84.4% of the apartments for rent is in close set, the largest percentage of the country; in Cucuta only 29.3%. All over Colombia, 87.6% of the ads have a parking lot, 70.3% balcony, 14.0% are furnished and 10.6% accept pets. The city with more balconies is Envigado (89.6%).
