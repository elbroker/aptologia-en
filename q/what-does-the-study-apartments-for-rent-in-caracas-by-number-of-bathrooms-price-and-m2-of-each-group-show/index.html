What does the study “Apartments for rent in Caracas by number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In Caracas, an apartment for rent from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in USD 880 per month in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in USD 3,000 per month (+ 240.9%). per square metre, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (USD 10.8 / m² per month) and the most expensive "1 bathroom" (USD 14.7 / m² per month).
