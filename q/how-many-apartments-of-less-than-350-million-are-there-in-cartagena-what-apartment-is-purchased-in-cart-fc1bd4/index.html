How many apartments of less than 350 million are there in Cartagena?

10.6% of the offer for sale (October of 2026); see the median area of each range in the table.

In Cartagena, the price range with the most offer of apartments for sale is over 1,200 M of pesos (27.0% of the ads), with a median area of 153 m². 10.6% of the supply costs less than 350 million.
