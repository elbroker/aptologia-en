How much does it cost to buy a house in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 767,500, based on 102 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 497,500 to USD 1,150,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,706/m² and the median listed size is 279 m². That is 165% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 290,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
