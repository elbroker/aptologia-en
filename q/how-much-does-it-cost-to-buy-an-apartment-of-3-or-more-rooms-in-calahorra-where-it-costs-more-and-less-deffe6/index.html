How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 3 or more rooms in Calahorra?

680 million pesos in the medium (October of 2026); the central half between 530 million pesos and 791 million pesos.

In Cajicá, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs 680 million pesos in the medium in Calahorra, the most expensive neighborhood, and 388 million pesos in Chuntame, the cheapest: 1.8 times less. We compare 6 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
