How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 3+ bathrooms 6–20 years old in Altos de Riomar, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms 6–20 years old for sale in Altos de Riomar, Barranquilla is COP 890,000,000, based on 66 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 627,500,000 to COP 1,265,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,110,805/m² and the median listed size is 178 m². That is 98% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,182,500. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
