How much does it cost to buy a home in Calatrava, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Calatrava, Bogotá is COP 1,375,000,000, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,050,000,000 to COP 1,837,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,518,337/m² and the median listed size is 246 m². That is 59% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,200,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 92.9% of listings include parking.
