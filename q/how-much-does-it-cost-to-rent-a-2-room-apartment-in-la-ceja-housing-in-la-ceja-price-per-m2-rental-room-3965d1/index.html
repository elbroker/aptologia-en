How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in La Ceja?

2.50 million pesos per month in the medium (October of 2026).

An apartment of 2 rooms in La Ceja is requested in 385 million pesos for sale and is rented for 2.50 million pesos per month in the medium. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.6% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 9.7% of the rent (342,224 pesos per month in the median).
