How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² in La Esperilla, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² for sale in La Esperilla, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 260,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 211,250 to USD 291,500. The median price per square metre is USD 2,152/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
