Is the rent per m² of a small apartment more expensive?

Yes in 9 of 11 countries: the m² of the large apartments is rented cheaper.

In Paraguay the rent per m² of an apartment of 120 m² or more is 52.4% lower than that of one of less than 50 m², the largest difference of 11 countries; in Colombia, - 1.2%. In 9 of 11 countries the tenant of a small apartment pays more for each square meter.
