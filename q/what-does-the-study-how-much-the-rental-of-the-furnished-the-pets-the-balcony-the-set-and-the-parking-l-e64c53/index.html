What does the study “How much the rental of the furnished, the pets, the balcony, the set and the parking lot in Sabaneta” show?

In Sabaneta, the most important feature of the rental in front of comparable apartments of the same estrato, rooms and size is "furnished": + 45.4% in the rental. The least heavy is "accept pets" (+ 0.4%).
