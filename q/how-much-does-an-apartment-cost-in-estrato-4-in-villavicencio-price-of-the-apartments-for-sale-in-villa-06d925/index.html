How much does an apartment cost in estrato 4 in Villavicencio?

345 million pesos in the median.

In Villavicencio the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 4.00 million pesos in estrato 3 and 5.96 million pesos in estrato 5, 1.5 times more. The average total price ranges from 230 million pesos to 750 million pesos according to the estrato.
