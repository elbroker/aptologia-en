How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 3+ bathrooms on floors 4–8 in Álamos, Pereira in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms on floors 4–8 for sale in Álamos, Pereira is COP 665,000,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 530,000,000 to COP 821,477,500. The median price per square metre is COP 5,011,480/m² and the median listed size is 127 m². That is 53% above the study's reference median for Pereira (COP 435,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 535,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 96.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
