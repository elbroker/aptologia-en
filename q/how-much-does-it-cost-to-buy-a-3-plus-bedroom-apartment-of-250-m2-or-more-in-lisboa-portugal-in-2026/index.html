How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 250 m² or more in Lisboa, Portugal in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 250 m² or more for sale in Lisboa, Portugal is EUR 2,157,500, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 1,575,000 to EUR 2,425,000. The median price per square metre is EUR 7,614/m² and the median listed size is 268 m². That is 227% above the study's reference median for Lisboa (EUR 660,000). 77.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
