What's the most rent-making estrato in Bucharest?

In estrato 5: 8.0% gross annual (October of 2026).

In Bucharest, buying an apartment to rent it yields more in estrato 5 (8.0% gross per year, 12.5 years of rent) and less in estrato 3 (6.9%).
