What does the study “Leasing with real estate or direct with the owner in Bogotá: how many there are and how much the price changes (2026)” show?

In Bogotá, only 14.7% of the apartments for rent are published by a private person (possible direct owner); 59.2% is published by a real estate or company. In front of a comparable real estate apartment, the private apartment costs 2.4% less per m².
