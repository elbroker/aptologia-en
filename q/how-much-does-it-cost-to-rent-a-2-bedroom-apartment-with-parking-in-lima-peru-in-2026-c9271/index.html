How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment with parking in Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments with parking in Lima, Peru is USD 1,198 per month, based on 330 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 950 to USD 1,450. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 11.5/m² and the median listed size is 100 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Lima (USD 1,250). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
