How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more in Lomas de Tarango, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more for sale in Lomas de Tarango, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 12,800,000, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 8,550,000 to MXN 18,450,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 33,666/m² and the median listed size is 442 m². That is 13% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 14,750,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
