How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 2 bathrooms in Urbanización Real, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Urbanización Real, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 229,500, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 216,387 to USD 283,205. The median price per square metre is USD 2,013/m² and the median listed size is 123 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
