How much does it cost to buy a house how old in Cajicá?

For starring (0 years): 1,300 million pesos; 1 to 5 years: 1,285 million pesos; 6 to 10 years: 1,250 million pesos; 11 to 20 years: 1,555 million pesos; 21 to 30 years: 1,800 million pesos (medium of October of 2026).

In Cajicá, a house for sale from the group "To premiere (0 years)" is requested in 1,300 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "21 to 30 years" in 1,800 million pesos (+ 38.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (5.70 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "1 to 5 years" (6.48 million pesos / m²).
