How much does it cost to buy a home in San Angel, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in San Angel, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 19,400,000, based on 101 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 8,000,000 to MXN 39,500,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 53,817/m² and the median listed size is 375 m². The median number of bathrooms is 3.
