How much does it cost to buy a house with how many bathrooms in Mexico City?

1 bath: 5,550,000 Mexican pesos (USD 300,561); 2 bathrooms: 7,650,000 Mexican pesos (USD 414,287); 3 bathrooms: 13,990,000 Mexican pesos (USD 757,631); 4 or more bathrooms: 24,900,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,348,464) (medium October 2026).

In Mexico City, a house for sale from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 5,550,000 Mexican pesos (USD 300,561) in the median and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 24,900,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,348,464) (+ 348.6%). For square meter, the cheapest is "1 Mexican pesos" (32,610 Mexican pesos (USD 1,766) / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (43,409 Mexican pesos (USD 2,351) / m²).
