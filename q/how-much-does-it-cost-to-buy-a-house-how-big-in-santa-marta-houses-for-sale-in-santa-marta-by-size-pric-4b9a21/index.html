How much does it cost to buy a house how big in Santa Marta?

70 - 90 m²: 320 million pesos; 90 - 120 m²: 390 million pesos; 120 - 160 m²: 500 million pesos; More than 160 m²: 900 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Santa Marta, a house for sale from the group "70 - 90 m²" is requested in 320 million pesos in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 900 million pesos (+ 181.2%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (3.17 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (4.11 million pesos / m²).
