How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 4 in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 4 in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá is COP 2,400,000 per month, based on 395 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,800,000 to COP 5,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 52,632/m² and the median listed size is 39.5 m². That is 11% below the study's reference median (COP 2,700,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 300,000. 45.8% of listings include parking.
