How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² 0–5 years old in Laureles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² 0–5 years old for sale in Laureles, Medellín is COP 1,050,000,000, based on 387 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 887,500,000 to COP 1,330,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,939,394/m² and the median listed size is 170 m². That is 25% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 720,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 99.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.5.
