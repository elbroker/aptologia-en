How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 150–250 m² in Serrallés, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Serrallés, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 300,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 280,000 to USD 391,350. The median price per square metre is USD 1,563/m² and the median listed size is 192 m². That is 25% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
