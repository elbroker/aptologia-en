Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "4 or more bathrooms" (4.80 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "3 bathrooms" (5.74 million pesos / m²).

In Itagüí, a house for sale of the group "2 bathrooms" is requested in 500 million pesos in the medium and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 990 million pesos (+ 98.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "4 or more bathrooms" (4.80 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "3 bathrooms" (5.74 million pesos / m²).
