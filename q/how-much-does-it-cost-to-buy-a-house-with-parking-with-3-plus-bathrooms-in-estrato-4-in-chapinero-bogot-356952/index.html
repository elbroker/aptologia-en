How much does it cost to buy a house with parking with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 4 in Chapinero, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with parking with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 4 for sale in Chapinero, Bogotá is COP 1,820,000,000, based on 67 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,350,000,000 to COP 2,550,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,937,630/m² and the median listed size is 380 m². That is 52% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 5.
