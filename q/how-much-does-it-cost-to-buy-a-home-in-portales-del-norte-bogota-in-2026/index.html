How much does it cost to buy a home in Portales del Norte, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Portales del Norte, Bogotá is COP 487,500,000, based on 64 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 400,000,000 to COP 577,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,805,193/m² and the median listed size is 80.5 m². That is 44% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 420,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 90.6% of listings include parking.
