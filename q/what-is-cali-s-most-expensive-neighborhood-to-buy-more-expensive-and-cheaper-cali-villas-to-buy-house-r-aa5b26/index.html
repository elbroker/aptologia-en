What is Cali's most expensive neighborhood to buy?

Pance Alto, with 5.90 million pesos per m² in the median (October of 2026).

In Cali, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to buy house is Pance Alto (5.90 million pesos / m²) and the cheapest Cordoba City (1.92 million pesos / m²), 3.1 times less. We compare 31 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
