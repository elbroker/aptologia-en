Where does a budget of 5 million pesos a month give more?

In Tocancipá: about 264 m².

With 5 million pesos a month, some 264 m² of apartment is rented in Tocancipá, the most surrendering city of 38, and 102 m² in El Retiro. In Bogotá they reach for 104 m² and in Medellín for 102 m².
