How much does it cost to buy an apartment of what size in Rionegro?

Less than 50 m²: 308 million pesos; 50 - 70 m²: 380 million pesos; 70 - 90 m²: 609 million pesos; 90 - 120 m²: 845 million pesos; 120 - 160 m²: 1,268 million pesos; More than 160 m²: 1,600 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Rionegro, an apartment for sale from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 308 million pesos in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 1,600 million pesos (+ 419.9%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Less than 50 m²" (6.33 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "120 - 160 m²" (9.43 million pesos / m²).
