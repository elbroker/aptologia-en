How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,500 per month, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,250 to USD 2,375. That is 11% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
