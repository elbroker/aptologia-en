What is the most expensive sector of Tocancipa?

Verganzo, with 3.67 million pesos per m² in apartments for sale (October of 2026).

The sector with the most expensive square meter of Tocancipá is Verganzo, with 3.67 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Vergancia, with 3.51 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 6.2% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 15.6% of the rent (171,283 pesos per month in the median).
