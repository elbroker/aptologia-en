How big are the apartments in Bogotá?

The median for sale is 99 m²; 16.1% has less than 50 m².

The medium apartment for sale measures 53 m² in Soacha, the smallest of 38 Colombian cities, and 114 m² in Montería, the largest. In Bogotá it measures 99 m² and 16.1% of the offer has less than 50 m². The median in the country is 87 m² for sale and 80 m² for rent.
