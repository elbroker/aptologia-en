How much does it cost to buy a home of 45–70 m² in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes of 45–70 m² for sale in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá is COP 230,000,000, based on 123 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 196,100,000 to COP 268,375,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,433,962/m² and the median listed size is 51 m². That is 28% below the study's reference median (COP 319,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 160,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 30.9% of listings include parking.
