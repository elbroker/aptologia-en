New buildings pay for more HOA fee in Envigado?

For starters: 5,667 pesos / m²; 1 to 5 years: 5,671 pesos / m²; 6 to 10 years: 5,795 pesos / m²; 11 to 20 years: 5,906 pesos / m²; 21 to 30 years: 5,231 pesos / m² (median per m² per month).

In Envigado, the highest square meter HOA fee is paid in group buildings "11 to 20 years" (5,906 pesos per m² per month in the median) and the lowest in "21 to 30 years" (5,231 pesos). Average monthly rate per strip: For starters: 537,500 pesos; 1 to 5 years: 550,000 pesos; 6 to 10 years: 600,000 pesos; 11 to 20 years: 636,000 pesos; 21 to 30 years: 495,000 pesos.
