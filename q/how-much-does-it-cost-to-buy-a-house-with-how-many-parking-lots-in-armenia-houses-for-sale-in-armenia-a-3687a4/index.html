How much does it cost to buy a house with how many parking lots in Armenia?

No parking lot: 450 million pesos; 1 parking lot: 620 million pesos; 2 or more parking lot: 1,430 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Armenia, a house for sale from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 450 million pesos in the medium and one from the "2 or more parking lot" in 1,430 million pesos (+ 217.8%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (3.64 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (4.96 million pesos / m²).
