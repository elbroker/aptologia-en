How much does an apartment rent in Floridablanca and Piedecuesta?

The gross return is 7.1% per year (rent per m² × 12), before expenditure.

The sector with the most expensive square meter of Floridablanca and Piedechaesta is Cañaveral, with 5.57 million pesos in the median of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is Piedechaesta, with 3.66 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 1.90 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 320 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.1% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 17.9% of the rent (390,750 pesos per month in the median).
