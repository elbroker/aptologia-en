How much does it cost to buy a house of what size in Villavicencio?

70 - 90 m²: 295 million pesos; 90 - 120 m²: 350 million pesos; 120 - 160 m²: 430 million pesos; More than 160 m²: 960 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Villavicencio, a house for sale from the group "70 - 90 m²" is requested in 295 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 960 million pesos (+ 225.4%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (2.64 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (3.54 million pesos / m²).
