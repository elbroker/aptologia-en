How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Zuñiga, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Zuñiga, Medellín is COP 1,170,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 30% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 900,000,000).
