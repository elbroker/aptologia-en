How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Granadillo, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Granadillo, Barranquilla is COP 2,200,000 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,860,000 to COP 2,600,000. That is 14% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,561,500).
