How much does it cost to rent a room in Bogotá?

The median is $900,000 per month; half of the ads are between $700,000 and $1,200,000 (data from 18 October of 2026).

Landing a room in Bogotá costs at the median $900,000 per month (half of the ads are between $700,000 and $1,200,000), compared to $1,900,000 of a studio. The 43.5% of the rooms demand half a minimum wage ($875,452) or less.
