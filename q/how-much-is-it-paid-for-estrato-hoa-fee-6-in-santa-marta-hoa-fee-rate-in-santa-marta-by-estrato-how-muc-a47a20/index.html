How much is it paid for estrato HOA fee 6 in Santa Marta?

645,000 pesos per month in the median (7,377 pesos per m²), October 2026.

In Santa Marta, the median rate of HOA fee of an apartment goes from 300,000 pesos per month in estrato 3 to 645,000 pesos in estrato 6, 2.1 times more. per square meter, it goes from 4,648 pesos to 7,377 pesos per month.
