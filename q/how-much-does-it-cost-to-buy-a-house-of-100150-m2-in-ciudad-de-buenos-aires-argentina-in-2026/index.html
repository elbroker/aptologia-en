How much does it cost to buy a house of 100–150 m² in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 100–150 m² for sale in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 215,000, based on 716 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 160,000 to USD 275,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,714/m² and the median listed size is 125 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
