How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² on floors 4–8 in Bochalema, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² on floors 4–8 for sale in Bochalema, Cali is COP 379,000,000, based on 78 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 350,000,000 to COP 438,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,702,241/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Cali (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 374,500. The most frequent estrato is 4, 98.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
