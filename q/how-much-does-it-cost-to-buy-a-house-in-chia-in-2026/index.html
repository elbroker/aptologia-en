How much does it cost to buy a house in Chía in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Chía is COP 1,500,000,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 845,000,000 to COP 2,500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,172,414/m² and the median listed size is 290 m². That is 285% above the study's reference median (COP 390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 925,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 86.8% of listings include parking.
