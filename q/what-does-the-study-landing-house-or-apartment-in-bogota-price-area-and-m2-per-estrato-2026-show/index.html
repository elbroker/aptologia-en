What does the study “Landing house or apartment in Bogotá: price, area and m² per estrato (2026)” show?

In Bogotá, a house for rent costs 26.1% less per square metre than a comparable apartment of the same estrato and number of rooms. But it is much larger: 300 m² in the medium versus 68 m², so the medium rental is $10,000,000 against $3,400,000.
