How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² in Evaristo Morales, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Evaristo Morales, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 220,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 197,250 to USD 242,872. The median price per square metre is USD 2,580/m² and the median listed size is 85.5 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
