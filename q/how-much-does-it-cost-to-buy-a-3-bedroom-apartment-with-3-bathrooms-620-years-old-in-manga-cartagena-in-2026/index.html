How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms 6–20 years old in Manga, Cartagena in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms 6–20 years old for sale in Manga, Cartagena is COP 1,135,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 775,000,000 to COP 1,712,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,594,643/m² and the median listed size is 154 m². That is 42% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 800,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 864,500. The most frequent estrato is 5, 90% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
