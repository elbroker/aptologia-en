How much does it cost to rent in estrato 6?

5,600,000 pesos per month in the median.

To rent an apartment of 2 rooms in estrato 6 costs in Colombia 5,600,000 pesos a month in the median, 3.5 times what is requested in estrato 3 (1,600,000 pesos). In estrato 4 the median is 2,600,000 pesos and in strate 5, 3,800,000 pesos.
