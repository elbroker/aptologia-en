How much does it cost to rent an apartment on floor 9 or higher in estrato 5 in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments on floor 9 or higher in estrato 5 in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado is COP 6,400,000 per month, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,325,000 to COP 9,225,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 53,704/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 45% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 4,400,000). 98.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
