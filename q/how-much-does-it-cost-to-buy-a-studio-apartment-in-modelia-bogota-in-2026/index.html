How much does it cost to buy a studio apartment in Modelia, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for studio apartments for sale in Modelia, Bogotá is COP 301,915,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 278,000,000 to COP 315,000,000. That is 9% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 330,000,000).
