How much does it cost to rent an apartment under 45 m² in Macul, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments under 45 m² in Macul, Santiago, Chile is UF 9.23 per month, based on 103 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 8.26 to UF 10.4. The median monthly rent per square metre is UF 0.26/m² and the median listed size is 35 m². That is 33% below the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 13.8). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
