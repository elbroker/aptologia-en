How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 70–100 m² in Ciudad Salitre, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in Ciudad Salitre, Bogotá is COP 4,275,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,685,000 to COP 4,900,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 49,490/m² and the median listed size is 88.5 m². That is 30% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
