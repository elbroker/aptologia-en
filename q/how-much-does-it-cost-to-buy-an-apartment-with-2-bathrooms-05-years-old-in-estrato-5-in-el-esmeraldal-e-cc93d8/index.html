How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 2 bathrooms 0–5 years old in estrato 5 in El Esmeraldal, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms 0–5 years old in estrato 5 for sale in El Esmeraldal, Envigado is COP 812,500,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 772,500,000 to COP 907,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,289,787/m² and the median listed size is 88 m². That is 1% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 820,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
