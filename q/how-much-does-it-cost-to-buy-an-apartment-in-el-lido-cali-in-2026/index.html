How much does it cost to buy an apartment in El Lido, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in El Lido, Cali is COP 279,500,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 225,000,000 to COP 295,000,000. That is 30% below the study's reference median for Cali (COP 400,000,000).
