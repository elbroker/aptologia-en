How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 1,700 per month, based on 150 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,212 to USD 2,100. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 21.1/m² and the median listed size is 79.5 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 1,625). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
