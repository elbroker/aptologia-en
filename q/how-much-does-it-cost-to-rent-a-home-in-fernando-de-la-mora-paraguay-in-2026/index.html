How much does it cost to rent a home in Fernando de la Mora, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Fernando de la Mora, Paraguay is PYG 4,225,000 per month, based on 168 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 3,200,000 to PYG 5,724,750. The median monthly rent per square metre is PYG 46,948/m² and the median listed size is 87 m². The median number of bathrooms is 1.
