What is the cheapest neighborhood to rent in Usaquén?

Among the neighborhoods with 5 ads or more, Hacienda La Estancia Navarra, with a medium rental of $1,550,000. The most expensive is Santa Barbara Cerros, with $21,718,000.

The average rental of an apartment or studio in Usaquén (Bogotá) is $3,800,000 per month, about $51,163 per m². A 2 rooms costs in the median $3,400,000. The cheapest neighborhood with 5 ads or more is Hacienda La Estancia Navarra ($1,550,000) and the most expensive Santa Barbara Cerros ($21,718,000).
