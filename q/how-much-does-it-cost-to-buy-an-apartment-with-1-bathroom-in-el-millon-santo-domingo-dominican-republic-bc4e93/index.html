How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 1 bathroom in El Millón, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 1 bathroom for sale in El Millón, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 135,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 118,000 to USD 152,500. The median price per square metre is USD 2,296/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 44% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
