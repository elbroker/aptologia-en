How much does it cost to rent an apartment with a balcony with 3+ bathrooms in Loma del Escobero, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony with 3+ bathrooms in Loma del Escobero, Envigado is COP 5,675,000 per month, based on 116 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,600,000 to COP 7,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,476/m² and the median listed size is 116 m². That is 29% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 4,400,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
