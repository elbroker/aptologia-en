How much does it cost to buy a department of 3 or more rooms in San José Insurgentes?

15,500,000 Mexican pesos (USD 839,405) in the medium (October 2026); the central half between 9,650,000 Mexican pesos (USD 522,598) and 18,000,000 Mexican pesos (USD 974,793).

In Mexico City, a department of 3 or more rectums costs 15,500,000 Mexican pesos (USD 839,405) in the median in San José Insurgentes, the most expensive colony, and 4,100,000 Mexican pesos (USD 222,036) in San Pedro de los Pinos, the cheapest: 3.8 times less. We compare 20 colonies with at least 30 ads of 3 or more rectangles.
