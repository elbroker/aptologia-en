How much does it cost to buy a house in El Retiro, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in El Retiro, Medellín is COP 3,390,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,261,750,000 to COP 3,850,000,000. That is 144% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 1,390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 594,740. The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
