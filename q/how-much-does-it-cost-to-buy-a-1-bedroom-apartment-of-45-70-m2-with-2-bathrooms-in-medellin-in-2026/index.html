How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms in Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Medellín is COP 650,000,000, based on 257 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 565,000,000 to COP 740,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 11,187,806/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 23% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 480,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 94.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
