How much does an apartment rent in Guayaquil and Samborondón?

The gross return is 7.5% per year (rent per m² × 12), before expenditure.

A 2 room apartment in Guayaquil and Samborondón is ordered in USD 155,250 for sale and is rented for USD 850 per month in the medium. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.5% per year.
