How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 379,000, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 320,000 to USD 450,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,201/m² and the median listed size is 161 m². That is 58% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
