How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment with 1 bathroom in Monserrat, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom for sale in Monserrat, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 95,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 85,500 to USD 110,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,536/m² and the median listed size is 62.5 m². That is 42% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
