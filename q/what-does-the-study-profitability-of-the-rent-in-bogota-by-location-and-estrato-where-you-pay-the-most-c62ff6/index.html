What does the study “Profitability of the rent in Bogotá by location and estrato: where you pay the most to rent” show?

Buy an apartment to rent in Bogotá in gross 8.2% per year (the price is equivalent to 12.3 years of rent). By location, the gross profitability ranges from 5.7% in Antonio Nariño to 8.4% in Chapinero; by estrato, from 6.7% (estrato 2) to 8.6% (estrato 6).
