How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment on floor 9 or higher 0–5 years old in Suramérica, Itagüí in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments on floor 9 or higher 0–5 years old for sale in Suramérica, Itagüí is COP 617,500,000, based on 90 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 550,000,000 to COP 750,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,271,780/m² and the median listed size is 89.5 m². That is 31% above the study's reference median for Itagüí (COP 470,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 461,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 97.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
