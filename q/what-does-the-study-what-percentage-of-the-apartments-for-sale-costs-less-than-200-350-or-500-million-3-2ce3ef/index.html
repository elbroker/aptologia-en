What does the study “What percentage of the apartments for sale costs less than 200, 350 or 500 million? 31 Colombian cities” show?

In Soacha the 79.2% of the apartments for sale are requested in less than 200 million pesos, the largest proportion of 31 cities; in Bogotá only 3.1%. The city with the most offer above 1,200 million is Bogotá, with 31.6% of its ads; in Bogotá, 19.9% costs less than 350 million.
