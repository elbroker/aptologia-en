Where is it cheaper to buy apartment in Cartagena?

Among the neighborhoods with 30 ads or more, in Ternera: 3.80 million pesos per m².

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter of Cartagena is El Centro, with 13.39 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is Ternera, with 3.80 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 3.40 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 750 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 6.7% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 16.2% of the rent (647,000 pesos per month in the median).
