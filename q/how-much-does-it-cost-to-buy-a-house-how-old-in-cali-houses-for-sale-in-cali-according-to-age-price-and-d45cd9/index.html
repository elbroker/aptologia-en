How much does it cost to buy a house how old in Cali?

For starring (0 years): 950 million pesos; 1 to 5 years: 950 million pesos; 6 to 10 years: 870 million pesos; 11 to 20 years: 1,200 million pesos; 21 to 30 years: 850 million pesos (medium of October of 2026).

In Cali, a house for sale from the group "To premiere (0 years)" is requested in 950 million pesos in the median and one from the group "21 to 30 years" in 850 million pesos (- 10.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (3.08 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "11 to 20 years" (3.84 million pesos / m²).
