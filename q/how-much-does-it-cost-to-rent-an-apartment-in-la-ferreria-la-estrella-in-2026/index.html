How much does it cost to rent an apartment in La Ferreria, La Estrella in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in La Ferreria, La Estrella is COP 2,400,000 per month, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,200,000 to COP 2,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 36,066/m² and the median listed size is 68 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median for La Estrella (COP 2,600,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
