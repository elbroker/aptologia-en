How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Altos de Riomar, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Altos de Riomar, Barranquilla is COP 5,200,000 per month, based on 84 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,555,000 to COP 7,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 36,471/m² and the median listed size is 150 m². That is 86% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 809,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
