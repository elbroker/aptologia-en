How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Miraflores, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Miraflores, Lima, Peru is USD 299,500, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 267,375 to USD 371,500. The median price per square metre is USD 2,717/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 16% above the study's reference median for Lima (USD 258,023). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
