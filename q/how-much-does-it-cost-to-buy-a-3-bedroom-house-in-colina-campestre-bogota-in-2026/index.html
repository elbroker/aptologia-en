How much does it cost to buy a 3-bedroom house in Colina Campestre, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3-bedroom houses for sale in Colina Campestre, Bogotá is COP 917,500,000, based on 72 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 693,750,000 to COP 1,291,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,380,117/m² and the median listed size is 168 m². That is 17% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,100,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 635,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 100% of listings include parking.
