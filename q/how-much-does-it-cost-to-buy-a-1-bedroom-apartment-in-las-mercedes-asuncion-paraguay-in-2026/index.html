How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in Las Mercedes, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in Las Mercedes, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 88,021, based on 90 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 77,438 to USD 111,500. The median price per square metre is USD 1,705/m² and the median listed size is 52 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 95,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
