Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (2.88 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "6 to 10 years" (3.75 million pesos / m²).

In Cúcuta, an apartment for sale from the group "1 to 5 years" is requested in 280 million pesos in the median and one from the group "21 to 30 years" in 312 million pesos (+ 11.6%). For square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (2.88 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "6 to 10 years" (3.75 million pesos / m²).
