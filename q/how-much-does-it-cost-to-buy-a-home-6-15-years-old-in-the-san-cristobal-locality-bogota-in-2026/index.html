How much does it cost to buy a home 6–15 years old in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes 6–15 years old for sale in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá is COP 227,500,000, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 190,000,000 to COP 313,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,669,331/m² and the median listed size is 50 m². That is 29% below the study's reference median (COP 319,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 128,621. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 20.7% of listings include parking.
