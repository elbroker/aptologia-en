How much does it cost to rent a home in Santa Isabel, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Santa Isabel, Bogotá is COP 1,700,000 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,400,000 to COP 2,600,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 35,000/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 51% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,497,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 53,150. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 30.3% of listings include parking.
