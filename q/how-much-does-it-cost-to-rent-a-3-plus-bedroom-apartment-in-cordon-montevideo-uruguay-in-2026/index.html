How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in Cordón, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Cordón, Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 36,750 per month, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 32,250 to UYU 42,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 443/m² and the median listed size is 90 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 40,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 6,000. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
