What does the study “Apartments for sale in Cajicá by number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In Cajicá, an apartment for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 280 million pesos in the medium and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 790 million pesos (+ 182.1%). For square meter, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (5.57 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (6.59 million pesos / m²).
