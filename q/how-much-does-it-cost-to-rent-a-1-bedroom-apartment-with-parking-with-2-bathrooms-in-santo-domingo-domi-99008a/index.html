How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment with parking with 2 bathrooms in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,250 per month, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,100 to USD 1,369. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 15.9/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
