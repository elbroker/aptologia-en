How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Puerto Madero, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Puerto Madero, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 1,600,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,300,000 to USD 1,828,500. The median listed size is 214 m². That is 870% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
