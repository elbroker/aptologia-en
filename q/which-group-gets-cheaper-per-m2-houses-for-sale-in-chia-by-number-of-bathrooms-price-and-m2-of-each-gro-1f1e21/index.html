Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (4.66 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (5.12 million pesos / m²).

In Chia, a house for sale from the group "2 bathrooms" is requested in 650 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 1,450 million pesos (+ 123.1%). For square meter, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (4.66 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (5.12 million pesos / m²).
