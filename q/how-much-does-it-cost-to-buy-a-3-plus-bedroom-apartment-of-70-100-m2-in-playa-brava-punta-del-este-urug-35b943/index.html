How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 340,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 300,000 to USD 392,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,879/m² and the median listed size is 87 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
