How much are you paying for HOA fee in Sabaneta?

The median rate is 380,000 pesos per month (4,890 pesos per m²), according to October 2026 announcements.

In Sabaneta, the most expensive HOA fee per square metre is paid in Prados de Sabaneta: 5,494 pesos per m² per month in the median (390,000 fee pesos). The cheapest, in Maria Auxiliadora, with 3,927 pesos per m². The median rate for the whole city is 380,000 pesos per month (4,890 pesos per m²).
