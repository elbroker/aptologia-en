How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms 0–5 years old in Bella Suiza, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms 0–5 years old for sale in Bella Suiza, Bogotá is COP 727,077,154, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 567,750,000 to COP 757,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,397,110/m² and the median listed size is 78.5 m². That is 3% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,005,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
