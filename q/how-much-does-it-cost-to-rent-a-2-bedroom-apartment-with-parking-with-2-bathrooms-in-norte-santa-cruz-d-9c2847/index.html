How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment with parking with 2 bathrooms in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 570 per month, based on 51 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 502 to USD 614. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 6.67/m² and the median listed size is 88.5 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (USD 570). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 70. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
