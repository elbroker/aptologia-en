How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Escobero, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Escobero, Envigado is COP 950,000,000, based on 158 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 779,702,250 to COP 1,293,750,000. That is 19% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 800,000,000).
