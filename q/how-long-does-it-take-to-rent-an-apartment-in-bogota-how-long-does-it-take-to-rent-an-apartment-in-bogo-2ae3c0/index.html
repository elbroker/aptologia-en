How long does it take to rent an apartment in Bogotá?

The active apartments have been published 32 days in the medium (cut to 12 October of 2026); 29% has been published for more than 60 days. It's the age of active ads, not the exact time until the contract.

An apartment for rent in Bogotá has been published in the medium 32 days, and 3 of each 10 have been more than 60. The price is heavy: below $1.5 million the median is 25 days and above $7 million, 62 days (half takes more than two months). The 2 rooms are the ones that last the least published (27 days).
