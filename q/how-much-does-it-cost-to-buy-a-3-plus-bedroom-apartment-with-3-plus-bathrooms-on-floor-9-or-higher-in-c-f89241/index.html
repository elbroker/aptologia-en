How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms on floor 9 or higher in Castillogrande, Cartagena in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms on floor 9 or higher for sale in Castillogrande, Cartagena is COP 2,400,000,000, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,850,000,000 to COP 3,300,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,810,811/m² and the median listed size is 195 m². That is 200% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 800,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,625,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
