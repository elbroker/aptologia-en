How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Samaria, Chía in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Samaria, Chía is COP 255,000,000, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 240,000,000 to COP 280,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,977,273/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Chía (COP 335,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 190,400. The most frequent estrato is 3, 82.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
