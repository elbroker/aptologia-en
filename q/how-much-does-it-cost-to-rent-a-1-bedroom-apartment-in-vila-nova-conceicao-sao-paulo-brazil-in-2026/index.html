How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in Vila Nova Conceição, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in Vila Nova Conceição, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 5,000 per month, based on 91 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 3,776 to BRL 8,875. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 132/m² and the median listed size is 44 m². That is 67% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,300. 74.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
