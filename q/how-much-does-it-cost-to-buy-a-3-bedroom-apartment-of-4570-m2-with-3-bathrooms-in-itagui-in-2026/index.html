How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Itagüí in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Itagüí is COP 318,000,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 303,750,000 to COP 385,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,272,727/m² and the median listed size is 61 m². That is 32% below the study's reference median for Itagüí (COP 470,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 240,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
