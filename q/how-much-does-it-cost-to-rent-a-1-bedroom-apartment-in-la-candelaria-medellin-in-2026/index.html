How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in La Candelaria, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in La Candelaria, Medellín is COP 1,850,000 per month, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 47% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 3,500,000).
