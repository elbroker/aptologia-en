How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in Los Alpes, Pereira in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in Los Alpes, Pereira is COP 342,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 2% below the study's reference median for Pereira (COP 350,000,000).
