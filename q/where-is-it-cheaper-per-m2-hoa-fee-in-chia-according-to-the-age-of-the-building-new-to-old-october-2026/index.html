Where is it cheaper per m²?

In "1 to 5 years": 4,559 pesos per m² per month.

In Chia, the highest square meter HOA fee is paid in group buildings "11 to 20 years" (6,019 pesos per m² per month in the median) and the lowest in "1 to 5 years" (4,559 pesos). Average monthly rate per strip: 1 to 5 years: 275,484 pesos; 6 to 10 years: 450,000 pesos; 11 to 20 years: 487,500 pesos.
