How much does it cost to rent an apartment in a gated complex in Ditaires, Itagüí in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in a gated complex in Ditaires, Itagüí is COP 2,600,000 per month, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,400,000 to COP 2,837,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 38,194/m² and the median listed size is 67.5 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median for Itagüí (COP 2,725,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 90% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
