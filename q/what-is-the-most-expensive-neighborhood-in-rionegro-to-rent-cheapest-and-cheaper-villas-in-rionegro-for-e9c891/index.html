What is the most expensive neighborhood in Rionegro to rent?

Saxony, with 48,193 pesos per m² per month in the median (October of 2026).

In Rionegro, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to rent house is Saxony (48,193 pesos / m² per month) and the cheapest San Antonio de Pereira (30,171 pesos / m² per month), 1.6 times less. We compare 9 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
