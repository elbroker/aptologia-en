What does the study “Apartments for rent in Buenos Aires City by size: price and m² of each group” show?

In Buenos Aires City, a rental department of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 720,000 Argentine pesos (USD 475) per month in the median and one of the group "120 - 160 m²" in 1,925,000 Argentine pesos (USD 1,271) per month (+ 167.4%). The cheapest per square metre is "120 - 160 m²" (15,264.8 Argentine pesos (USD 10.1) / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (19,697.0 Argentine pesos (USD 13.0) / m² per month).
