How much more does a new apartment cost than one used in Colombia?

In the national median, + 13.8% per m²; in Barranquilla, + 28.2%.

A new apartment (0 to 1 year) is requested in Barranquilla 28.2% more per m² than one used, the greatest difference between 32 cities; in Montería, the difference is - 8.5%. In Bogotá the new costs + 14.8% per m². In Colombia, + 13.8%.
