How much does it cost to buy a house in Quinta Paredes, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Quinta Paredes, Bogotá is COP 1,490,000,000, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 930,000,000 to COP 2,100,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,861,803/m² and the median listed size is 247 m². That is 24% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 690,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 83.6% of listings include parking.
