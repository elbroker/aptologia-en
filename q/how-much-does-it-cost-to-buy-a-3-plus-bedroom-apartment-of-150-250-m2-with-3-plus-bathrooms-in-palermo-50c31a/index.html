How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 890,000, based on 118 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 596,000 to USD 1,175,100. The median price per square metre is USD 4,636/m² and the median listed size is 197 m². That is 439% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
