How much does it cost to buy a home in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is USD 360,000, based on 840 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 240,000 to USD 605,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,019/m² and the median listed size is 182 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 400. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
