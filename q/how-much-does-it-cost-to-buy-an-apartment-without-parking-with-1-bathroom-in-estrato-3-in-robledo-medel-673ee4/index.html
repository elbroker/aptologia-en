How much does it cost to buy an apartment without parking with 1 bathroom in estrato 3 in Robledo, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments without parking with 1 bathroom in estrato 3 for sale in Robledo, Medellín is COP 225,000,000, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 200,000,000 to COP 254,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,000,000/m² and the median listed size is 45 m². That is 73% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 185,500. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
