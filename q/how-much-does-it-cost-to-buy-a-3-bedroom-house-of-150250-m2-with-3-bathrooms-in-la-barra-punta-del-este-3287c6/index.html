How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 640,000, based on 113 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 490,000 to USD 830,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,235/m² and the median listed size is 198 m². That is 9% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
