How much more does an furnished apartment cost in Envigado?

+ 38.8% in the rent in front of comparable apartments without amoblar.

In Envigado, the most important feature of the rental in front of comparable apartments of the same estrato, rooms and size is "furnished": + 38.8% in the rental. The least heavy is "accept pets" (+ 1.6%).
