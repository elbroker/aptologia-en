How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 3+ bathrooms on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 in Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 3+ bathrooms on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 in Poblado, Medellín is COP 8,500,000 per month, based on 86 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 6,500,000 to COP 13,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 59,188/m² and the median listed size is 140 m². That is 77% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). 89.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
