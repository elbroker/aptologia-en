Where is it cheaper to buy apartment in Latin America?

Among the cities with 30 ads or more, in Maracaibo (Venezuela), with USD 758 per m².

The square meter of a most expensive apartment for sale in Latin America is in Punta del Este (Uruguay, spa), with a median of USD 3,604 / m²; the cheapest of the sample, in Maracaibo (Venezuela), with USD 758 / m². Bogotá is located 20 of 68 with USD 2,282 / m², and the most expensive Colombian city is El Retiro (USD 2,929 / m²). Lisbon, outside the region, marks USD 8,383 / m².
