Where does a budget of 450 million pesos give more?

In Soacha: about 133 m².

With 450 million pesos, some 133 m² of apartment is bought in Soacha, the most surrendering city of 38, and 48 m² in El Retiro. In Bogotá they reach for 62 m² and in Medellín for 60 m².
