How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² in Lima, Peru is USD 750 per month, based on 105 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 650 to USD 850. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 13.3/m² and the median listed size is 53 m². That is 40% below the study's reference median for Lima (USD 1,250). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
