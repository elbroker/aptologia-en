How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² more than 20 years old in Santa Paula, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² more than 20 years old for sale in Santa Paula, Bogotá is COP 1,380,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,150,000,000 to COP 1,590,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,862,745/m² and the median listed size is 199 m². That is 84% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,400,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
