How much does an apartment rent in Lima?

In the typical district, 5.2% gross per year.

In Lima, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical district 5.2% gross per year: the price requested is equivalent to 19.1 years of rent. The district with the highest gross return is Surquillo (6.0%) and the district with the lowest return is Chorrillo (4.7%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 5.4%.
