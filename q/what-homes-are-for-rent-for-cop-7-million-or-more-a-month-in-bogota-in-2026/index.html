What homes are for rent for COP 7 million or more a month in Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for rent for COP 7 million or more a month in Bogotá have a median asking rent of COP 12,500,000 per month, based on 6,058 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 9,017,500 to COP 18,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 53,333/m² and the median listed size is 255 m². That is 258% above the study's reference median (COP 3,495,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,861,602. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 95.4% of listings include parking.
