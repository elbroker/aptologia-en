How much does it cost to rent a home in Parque Rodó, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Parque Rodó, Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 30,900 per month, based on 107 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 25,750 to UYU 39,900. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 629/m² and the median listed size is 48 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 4,500. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
