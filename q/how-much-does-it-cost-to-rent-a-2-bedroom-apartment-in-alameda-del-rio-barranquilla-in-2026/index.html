How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in Alameda del Río, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in Alameda del Río, Barranquilla is COP 1,317,000 per month, based on 156 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,141,350 to COP 1,550,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 25,081/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 43% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 180,000. The most frequent estrato is 3, 52.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
