What does the study “Price of the m² in Cajicá by estrato: apartments in front of houses (rent, October 2026)” show?

In Cajicá, the m² of an apartment for rent goes from 28,571 pesos per month in estrato 3 to 39,872 pesos per month in estrato 5. In estrato 5, the m² of a house is 15.0% cheaper than that of an apartment.
