How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Hato Viejo, Bello in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Hato Viejo, Bello is COP 301,000,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 263,750,000 to COP 321,250,000. That is 11% below the study's reference median for Bello (COP 340,000,000).
