How much does it cost to rent an apartment with how many bathrooms in Manizales?

1 bath: 1,400,000 pesos per month; 2 bathrooms: 2,170,000 pesos per month; 3 bathrooms: 4,947,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Manizales, an apartment for rent from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 1,400,000 pesos per month in the medium and one from the group "3 bathrooms" in 4,947,000 pesos per month (+ 253.4%). For square meter, the cheapest is "1 bath" (29,671 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "2 bath" (32,892 pesos / m² per month).
