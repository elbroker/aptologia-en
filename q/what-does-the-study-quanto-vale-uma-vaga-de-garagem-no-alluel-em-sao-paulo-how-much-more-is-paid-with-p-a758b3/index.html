What does the study “Quanto vale uma vaga de garagem no alluel em São Paulo: how much more is paid with parking” show?

In São Paulo, an apartment for rent with garagem vaga is ordered + 38.4% against one without vaga of the same bairro, number of quarts and area range; a second vaga sum + 28.0% against one (same quarts and area range). The 14.6% of the apartments for rent that report vagas have none.
