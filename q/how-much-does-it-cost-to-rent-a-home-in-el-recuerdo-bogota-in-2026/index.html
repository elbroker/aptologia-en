How much does it cost to rent a home in El Recuerdo, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in El Recuerdo, Bogotá is COP 2,000,000 per month, based on 55 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,700,000 to COP 2,850,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 64,516/m² and the median listed size is 31 m². That is 43% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,497,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 288,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 29.1% of listings include parking.
