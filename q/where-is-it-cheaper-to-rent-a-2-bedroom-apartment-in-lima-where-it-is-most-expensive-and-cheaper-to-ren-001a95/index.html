Where is it cheaper to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in Lima?

In San Miguel: 1,892 soles (USD 551) per month in the median.

In Lima, a 2 bedroom apartment is rented for 3,784 soles (USD 1,102) per month in the middle of San Isidro, the most expensive district, and 1,892 soles (USD 551) per month in San Miguel, the cheapest: 2.0 times less. We compare 9 districts with at least 30 ads from 2 bedrooms.
