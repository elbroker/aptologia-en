How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Sabaneta?

Between 2,100,000 pesos (estrato 2) and 3,400,000 pesos (estrato 5) per month in the median, according to October 2026 announcements.

In Sabaneta, rent an apartment of 2 rooms costs in the median 2,100,000 pesos per month in estrato 2 and 3,400,000 pesos in estrato 5, 1.6 times more. per square meter, the rent goes from 36,000 pesos to 43,590 pesos per month according to the estrato.
