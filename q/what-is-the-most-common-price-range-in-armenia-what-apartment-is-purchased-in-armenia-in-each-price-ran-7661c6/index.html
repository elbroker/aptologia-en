What is the most common price range in Armenia?

200 - 350 M of pesos, with 32.0% of the ads.

In Armenia, the price range with the most available apartments for sale is 200 - 350 M of pesos (32.0% of the ads), with a median area of 58 m². 42.2% of the supply costs less than 350 million.
