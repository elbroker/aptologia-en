How much does it cost to buy an apartment on which floor in Bogotá?

Floor 1 - 2: 730 million pesos; Floor 3 - 5: 750 million pesos; Floor 6 - 10: 950 million pesos; Floor 11 - 20: 562 million pesos; Floor 21 or more: 380 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Bogotá, an apartment for sale of the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 730 million pesos in the median and one of the group "Piso 21 or more" in 380 million pesos (- 47.9%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (6.86 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 6 - 10" (8.00 million pesos / m²).
