How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² with parking in Niquia, Bello in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with parking for sale in Niquia, Bello is COP 435,000,000, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 416,250,000 to COP 461,825,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,890,707/m² and the median listed size is 73 m². That is 28% above the study's reference median for Bello (COP 340,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 300,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
