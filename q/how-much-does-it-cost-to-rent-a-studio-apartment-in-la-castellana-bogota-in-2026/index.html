How much does it cost to rent a studio apartment in La Castellana, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for studio apartments in La Castellana, Bogotá is COP 1,985,000 per month, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,600,000 to COP 2,150,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 79,167/m² and the median listed size is 22 m². That is 4% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,900,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 300,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 14.3% of listings include parking.
