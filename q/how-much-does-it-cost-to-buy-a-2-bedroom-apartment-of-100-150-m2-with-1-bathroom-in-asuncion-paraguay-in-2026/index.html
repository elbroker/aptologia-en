How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² with 1 bathroom in Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 1 bathroom for sale in Asunción, Paraguay is USD 191,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 166,612 to USD 238,150. The median price per square metre is USD 1,745/m² and the median listed size is 116 m². That is 36% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 140,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
