How much does an apartment in Medellín cost in 2026?

The median requested for a 2 bedroom in the Aptology ads is $550 million, about 26 years of minimum wage.

In Bogotá, an apartment of 2 alcobas costs 30.2 years of full minimum wage: the median price requested is $634 million, according to the sales announcements of 5 October of 2026, and the minimum wage of 2026 is $1,750,905 ([Decree 1469 of 2025] (https://www.cancilleria.gov.co/normograma/compilacion/docs/decreto14692025.htm)). In Cartagena they are 37.1 years old and in Cali, the cheapest of the eight cities measured, 12.6.
