How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more in estrato 4 in El Prado, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more in estrato 4 for sale in El Prado, Barranquilla is COP 1,396,000,000, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 993,000,000 to COP 2,925,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,230,769/m² and the median listed size is 538 m². That is 55% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 900,000,000). 93.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
