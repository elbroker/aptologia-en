Where does he pay the least?

In Pantanillo: 6.0% gross per year (16.8 years of rent).

In El Retiro, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical sector 6.4% gross per year: the price requested is equivalent to 15.6 years of rent. The sector with the highest gross return is Los Salados (6.7%) and that of the minor, Pantanillo (6.0%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 6.5%.
