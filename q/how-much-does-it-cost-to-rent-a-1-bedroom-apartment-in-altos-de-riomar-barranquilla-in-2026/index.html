How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in Altos de Riomar, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in Altos de Riomar, Barranquilla is COP 2,500,000 per month, based on 53 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 23% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,030,000).
