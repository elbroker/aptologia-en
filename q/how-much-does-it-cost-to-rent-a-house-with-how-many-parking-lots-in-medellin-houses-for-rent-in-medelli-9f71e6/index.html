How much does it cost to rent a house with how many parking lots in Medellín?

No parking lot: 4,500,000 pesos per month; 1 parking lot: 6,000,000 pesos per month; 2 or more parking lot: 13,000,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Medellín, a house for rent from the "No parking lot" group is ordered in 4,500,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "2 or more parking lot" in 13,000,000 pesos per month (+ 188.9%). For square meter, the cheapest is "1 parking lot" (30,357 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (39,279 pesos / m² per month).
