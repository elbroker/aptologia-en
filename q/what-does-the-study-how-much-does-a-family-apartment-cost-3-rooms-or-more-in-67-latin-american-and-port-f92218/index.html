What does the study “How much does a family apartment cost 3 rooms or more in 67 Latin American and Portugal cities” show?

An apartment of 3 rooms or more is requested for USD 54,983 in the median of Soacha (Colombia), the lowest of 67 cities, and for USD 1,011,675 in Lisbon (Portugal), the highest. In Bogotá, USD 307,905. Among the capitals, from USD 150,000 in La Paz to USD 1,011,675 in Lisbon.
