How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in El Millón, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in El Millón, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 235,000, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 208,500 to USD 257,500. The median price per square metre is USD 1,348/m² and the median listed size is 167 m². That is 2% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
