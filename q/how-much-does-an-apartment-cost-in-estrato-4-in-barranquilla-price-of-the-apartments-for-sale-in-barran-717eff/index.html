How much does an apartment cost in estrato 4 in Barranquilla?

385 million pesos in the median.

In Barranquilla the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 3.16 million pesos in estrato 2 and 5.09 million pesos in estrato 6, 1.6 times more. The average total price ranges from 180 million pesos to 795 million pesos according to the estrato.
