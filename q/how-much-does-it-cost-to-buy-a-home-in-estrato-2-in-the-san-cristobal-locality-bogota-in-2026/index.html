How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 2 in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 2 for sale in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá is COP 270,000,000, based on 222 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 196,575,000 to COP 450,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,072,626/m² and the median listed size is 67 m². That is 15% below the study's reference median (COP 319,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 145,150. 32.9% of listings include parking.
