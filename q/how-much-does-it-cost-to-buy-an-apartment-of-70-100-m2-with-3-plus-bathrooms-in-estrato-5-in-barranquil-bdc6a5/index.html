How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 5 in Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 5 for sale in Barranquilla is COP 460,000,000, based on 199 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 380,000,000 to COP 572,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,191,649/m² and the median listed size is 90 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 530,000. 99% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
