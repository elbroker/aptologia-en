How much does it cost to buy a home in Niza Norte, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Niza Norte, Bogotá is COP 1,150,000,000, based on 89 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 660,000,000 to COP 1,400,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,168,539/m² and the median listed size is 210 m². That is 33% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 490,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 89.9% of listings include parking.
