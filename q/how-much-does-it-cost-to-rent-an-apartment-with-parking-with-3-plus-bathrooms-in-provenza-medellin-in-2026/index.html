How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Provenza, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Provenza, Medellín is COP 9,750,000 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 7,000,000 to COP 11,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 60,287/m² and the median listed size is 154 m². That is 103% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
