How much does it cost to buy an apartment on floors 4–8 more than 20 years old in estrato 6 in Santa Bárbara, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments on floors 4–8 more than 20 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Santa Bárbara, Bogotá is COP 1,060,000,000, based on 71 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 840,000,000 to COP 1,450,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,279,262/m² and the median listed size is 174 m². That is 41% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,068,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
