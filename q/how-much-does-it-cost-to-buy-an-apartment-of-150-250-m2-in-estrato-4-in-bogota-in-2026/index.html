How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 150–250 m² in estrato 4 in Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² in estrato 4 for sale in Bogotá is COP 1,070,000,000, based on 387 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 839,500,000 to COP 1,350,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,789,474/m² and the median listed size is 177 m². That is 43% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 929,800. 97.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
