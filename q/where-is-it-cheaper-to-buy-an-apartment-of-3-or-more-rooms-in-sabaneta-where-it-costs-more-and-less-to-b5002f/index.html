Where is it cheaper to buy an apartment of 3 or more rooms in Sabaneta?

In Maria Auxiliadora: 290 million pesos in the median.

In Sabaneta, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs 682 million pesos in the medium in El Carmelo, the most expensive neighborhood, and 290 million pesos in María Auxiliadora, the cheapest: 2.4 times less. We compare 23 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
