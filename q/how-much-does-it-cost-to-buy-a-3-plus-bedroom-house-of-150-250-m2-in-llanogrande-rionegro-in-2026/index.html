How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 150–250 m² in Llanogrande, Rionegro in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² for sale in Llanogrande, Rionegro is COP 1,280,000,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,135,000,000 to COP 1,667,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,272,727/m² and the median listed size is 179 m². That is 42% below the study's reference median for Rionegro (COP 2,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 347,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 97.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
