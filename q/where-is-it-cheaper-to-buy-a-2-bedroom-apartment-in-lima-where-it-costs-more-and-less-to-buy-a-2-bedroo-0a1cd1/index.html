Where is it cheaper to buy a 2 bedroom apartment in Lima?

In Chorrillo: 371,000 soles (USD 107,989) in the median.

In Lima, a 2 bedroom apartment costs 828,900 soles (USD 241,273) in the middle in Miraflores, the most expensive district, and 371,000 soles (USD 107,989) in Chorrillo, the cheapest: 2.2 times less. We compare 15 districts with at least 30 ads from 2 bedrooms.
