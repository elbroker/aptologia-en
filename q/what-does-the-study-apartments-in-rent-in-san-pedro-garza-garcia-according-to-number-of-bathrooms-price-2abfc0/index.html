What does the study “Apartments in rent in San Pedro Garza Garcia according to number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In San Pedro Garza Garcia, a rental department of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 30,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,625) per month in the medium and one of the group "3 bathrooms" in 88,000 Mexican pesos (USD 4,766) per month (+ 193.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (Mexican pesos 397.1 (USD 21.5) / m² per month) and the most expensive "1 bath" (Mexican pesos 514.5 (USD 27.9) / m² per month).
