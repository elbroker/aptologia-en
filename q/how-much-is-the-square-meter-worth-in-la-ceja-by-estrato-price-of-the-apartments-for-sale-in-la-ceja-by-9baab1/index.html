How much is the square meter worth in La Ceja by estrato?

From 5.10 million pesos (estrato 3) to 7.72 million pesos (estrato 5) in the median of October 2026.

In La Ceja the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 5.10 million pesos in estrato 3 and 7.72 million pesos in estrato 5, 1.5 times more. The average total price ranges from 310 million pesos to 525 million pesos according to the estrato.
