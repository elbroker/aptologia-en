How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many bathrooms in Montevideo?

1 bathroom: USD 165,000; 2 bathrooms: USD 299,000; 3 bathrooms: USD 560,000; 4 or more bathrooms: USD 862,500 (mid-October of 2026).

In Montevideo, an apartment for sale from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in USD 165,000 in the median and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in USD 862,500 (+ 422.7%). per square metre, the cheapest is "1 bathroom" (USD 2,929 / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (USD 4,304 / m²).
