How much does it cost to buy an apartment on floors 1–3 in estrato 6 in Bello Horizonte, Santa Marta in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments on floors 1–3 in estrato 6 for sale in Bello Horizonte, Santa Marta is COP 790,000,000, based on 55 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 500,000,000 to COP 1,085,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,523,810/m² and the median listed size is 82 m². That is 32% above the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 756,500. 79.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
