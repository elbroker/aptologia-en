How many years of rent is a flat in Lisbon?

27.1 years per square metre (October announcements of 2026).

In Lisbon the square meter of an apartment is equivalent to 27.1 years of rent and in Barcelona to 23.0 years, compared to 12.5 years in Bogotá. In comparison, the lowest ratio is in Caracas, Venezuela (9.6 years) and the highest in Santiago, Chile (27.5).
