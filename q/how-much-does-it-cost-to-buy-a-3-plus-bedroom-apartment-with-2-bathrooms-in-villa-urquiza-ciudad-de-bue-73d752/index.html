How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms in Villa Urquiza, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Villa Urquiza, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 352,500, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 261,250 to USD 445,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,069/m² and the median listed size is 102 m². That is 114% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
