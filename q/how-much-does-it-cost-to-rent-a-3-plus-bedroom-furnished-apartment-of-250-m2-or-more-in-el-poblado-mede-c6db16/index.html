How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom furnished apartment of 250 m² or more in El Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom furnished apartments of 250 m² or more in El Poblado, Medellín is COP 15,100,000 per month, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 12,700,000 to COP 17,950,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 46,098/m² and the median listed size is 300 m². That is 215% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 95.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
