And the cheapest?

Olivais, with 4,973 euros (USD 5,590) per m².

In Lisbon, the freguesia with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is Santo António (9,398 euros (USD 10,565) / m²) and the cheapest Olivais (4,973 euros (USD 5,590) / m²), 1.9 times less. We compare 20 freguesias with at least 30 ads with area.
