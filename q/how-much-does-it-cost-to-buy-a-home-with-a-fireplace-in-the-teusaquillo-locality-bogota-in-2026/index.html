How much does it cost to buy a home with a fireplace in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes with a fireplace for sale in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá is COP 1,250,000,000, based on 411 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 900,000,000 to COP 1,900,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,516,588/m² and the median listed size is 218 m². That is 40% above the study's reference median (COP 892,550,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 672,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 93.2% of listings include parking.
