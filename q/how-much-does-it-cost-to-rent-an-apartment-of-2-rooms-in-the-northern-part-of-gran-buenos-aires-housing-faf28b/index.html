How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 2 rooms in the northern part of Gran Buenos Aires?

USD 1,500 per month in the median (October of 2026).

An apartment of 2 rooms in the northern part of Gran Buenos Aires is ordered in USD 280,000 for sale and is rented for USD 1,500 per month in the medium. The gross implicit return on rent is 6.9% per year.
