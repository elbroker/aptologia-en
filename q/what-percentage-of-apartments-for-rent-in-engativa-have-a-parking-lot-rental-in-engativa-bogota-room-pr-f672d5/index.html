What percentage of apartments for rent in Engativá have a parking lot?

18.5% of the ads reporting the data include at least one parking lot.

The average rental of an apartment or studio apartment in Engativá (Bogotá) is $1,600,900 per month, about $36,000 per m². A 2 rooms costs in the median $1,500,000. The cheapest neighborhood with 5 ads or more is La Estrada ($1,225,000) and the most expensive Western Normandy ($2,931,000).
