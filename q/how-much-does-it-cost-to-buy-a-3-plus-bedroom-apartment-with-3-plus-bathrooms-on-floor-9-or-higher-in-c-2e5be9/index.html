How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms on floor 9 or higher in Cerritos, Pereira in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms on floor 9 or higher for sale in Cerritos, Pereira is COP 940,000,000, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 880,000,000 to COP 1,250,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,572,816/m² and the median listed size is 126 m². That is 116% above the study's reference median for Pereira (COP 435,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 682,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
