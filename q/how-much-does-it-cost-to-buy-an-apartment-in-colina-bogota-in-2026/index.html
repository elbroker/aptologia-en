How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Colina, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Colina, Bogotá is COP 660,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 515,000,000 to COP 840,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,513,158/m² and the median listed size is 112 m². That is 14% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 538,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 93.9% of listings include parking.
