How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in El Salado, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in El Salado, Envigado is COP 750,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 567,000,000 to COP 845,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,225,617/m² and the median listed size is 83 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 850,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 526,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 93.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
