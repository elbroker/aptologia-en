How much does it cost to buy a house in Bogotá?

1,200 million pesos in the median.

Buying a house costs in the median 350 million pesos in Soacha, the cheapest city of 39, and 2,500 million pesos in El Retiro, the most expensive. In Bogotá the median is 1,200 million pesos. Throughout the country, the house for medium sale measures 256 m².
