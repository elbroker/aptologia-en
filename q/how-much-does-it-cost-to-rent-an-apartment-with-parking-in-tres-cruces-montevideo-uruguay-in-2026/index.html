How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking in Tres Cruces, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking in Tres Cruces, Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 35,050 per month, based on 72 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 29,375 to UYU 38,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 617/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 17% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 30,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 5,500. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
