What homes are for sale for less than COP 250 million in Barranquilla in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for less than COP 250 million in Barranquilla have a median asking price of COP 184,500,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 160,750,000 to COP 206,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,551,332/m² and the median listed size is 53 m². That is 30% below the study's reference median (COP 262,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 151,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 6.5% of listings include parking.
