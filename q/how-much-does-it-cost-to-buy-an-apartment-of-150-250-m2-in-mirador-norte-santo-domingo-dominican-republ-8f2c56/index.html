How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 150–250 m² in Mirador Norte, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Mirador Norte, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 282,500, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 259,250 to USD 315,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,594/m² and the median listed size is 178 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
