What homes are for sale for COP 2,000 million or more in the Engativá locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for COP 2,000 million or more in the Engativá locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 2,400,000,000, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,000,000,000 to COP 2,776,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,446,623/m² and the median listed size is 450 m². That is 340% above the study's reference median (COP 545,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,300,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 70% of listings include parking.
