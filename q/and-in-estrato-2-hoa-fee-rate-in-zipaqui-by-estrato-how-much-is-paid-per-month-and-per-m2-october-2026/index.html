120,000 pesos a month.

In Zipaquirá, the median rate of HOA fee of an apartment goes from 120,000 pesos per month in estrato 2 to 280,000 pesos in estrato 4, 2.3 times more. per square meter, it goes from 2,128 pesos to 4,204 pesos per month.
