How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Antiguo Country, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Antiguo Country, Bogotá is COP 970,000,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 721,250,000 to COP 1,195,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 11,874,531/m² and the median listed size is 77.5 m². That is 81% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 535,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,200,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 97.1% of listings include parking.
