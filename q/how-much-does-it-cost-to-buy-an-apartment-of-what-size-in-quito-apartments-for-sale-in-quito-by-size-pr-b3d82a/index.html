How much does it cost to buy an apartment of what size in Quito?

50 - 70 m²: USD 72,750; 70 - 90 m²: USD 89,000; 90 - 120 m²: USD 119,500; 120 - 160 m²: USD 143,800; More than 160 m²: USD 218,000 (mid-October of 2026).

In Quito, a department for sale of the group "50 - 70 m²" is requested in USD 72,750 in the median and one of the group "More than 160 m²" in USD 218,000 (+ 199.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (USD 887 / m²) and the most expensive "50 - 70 m²" (USD 1,122 / m²).
