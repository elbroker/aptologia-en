How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Córdoba, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Córdoba, Argentina is USD 120,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 95,600 to USD 175,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,923/m² and the median listed size is 68 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median for Córdoba (USD 130,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
