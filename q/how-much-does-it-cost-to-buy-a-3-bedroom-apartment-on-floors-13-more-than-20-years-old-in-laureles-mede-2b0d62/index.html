How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment on floors 1–3 more than 20 years old in Laureles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments on floors 1–3 more than 20 years old for sale in Laureles, Medellín is COP 795,000,000, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 632,000,000 to COP 950,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,636,364/m² and the median listed size is 140 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 454,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 62.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
