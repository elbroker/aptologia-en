How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 1 bathroom in Villa Ortúzar, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 1 bathroom for sale in Villa Ortúzar, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 137,000, based on 73 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 115,000 to USD 171,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,953/m² and the median listed size is 51 m². That is 17% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
