How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking on floor 9 or higher in Gran Granada, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking on floor 9 or higher for sale in Gran Granada, Bogotá is COP 420,500,000, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 365,000,000 to COP 527,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,058,015/m² and the median listed size is 70.9 m². That is 44% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 413,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
