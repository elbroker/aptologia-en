How much does it cost to rent in estrato 3 in Bogotá?

An apartment of 2 rooms in estrato 3 is requested in 1,500,000 pesos per month.

In Bogotá, rent an apartment of 2 rooms costs in the median 1,156,454 pesos per month in estrato 2 and 7,000,000 pesos in estrato 6, 6.1 times more. per square meter, the rent goes from 25,000 pesos to 59,259 pesos per month according to the estrato.
