What does the study “Cheapest and expensive Colonies of Guadalajara to rent apartment: ranking per m²” show?

In Guadalajara, the colony with the most expensive m² to rent department is Country Club (371.1 Mexican pesos (USD 20.1) / m² per month) and the cheapest Prados de Providencia (261.2 Mexican pesos (USD 14.1) / m² per month), 1.4 times less. We compare 7 colonies with at least 30 ads with area.
