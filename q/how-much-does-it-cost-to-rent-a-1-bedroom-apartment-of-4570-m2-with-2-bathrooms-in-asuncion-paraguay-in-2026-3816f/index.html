How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms in Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms in Asunción, Paraguay is USD 1,200 per month, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 975 to USD 1,200. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 18.5/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 11% below the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 1,350). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
