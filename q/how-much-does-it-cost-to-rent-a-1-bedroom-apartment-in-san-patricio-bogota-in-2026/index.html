How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in San Patricio, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in San Patricio, Bogotá is COP 3,400,000 per month, based on 52 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,950,000 to COP 3,912,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 65,385/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is 39% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,450,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 622,500. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 96.2% of listings include parking.
