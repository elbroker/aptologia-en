What does the study “Price of houses for sale in Asunción by number of bedrooms: how much each size costs” show?

In Asunción, a 1 bedroom house costs USD 250,000 in the median, one 2 USD 255,000 bedroom (+ 2.0%) and one 3 or more USD 360,000 (+ 41.2%).
