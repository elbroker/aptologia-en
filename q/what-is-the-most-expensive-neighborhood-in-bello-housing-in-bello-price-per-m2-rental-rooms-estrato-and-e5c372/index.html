What is the most expensive neighborhood in Bello?

Ciudad Fabricato, with 7.38 million pesos per m² in apartments for sale (October of 2026).

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter of Bello is Ciudad Fabricato, with 7.38 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Cabañitas, with 4.32 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 1.80 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 320 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.3% per year.
