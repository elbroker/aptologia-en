How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 385,500, based on 150 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 330,000 to USD 453,750. The median price per square metre is USD 3,285/m² and the median listed size is 117 m². That is 7% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 700. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
