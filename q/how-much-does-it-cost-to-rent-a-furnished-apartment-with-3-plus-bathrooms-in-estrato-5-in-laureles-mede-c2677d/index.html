How much does it cost to rent a furnished apartment with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 5 in Laureles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for furnished apartments with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 5 in Laureles, Medellín is COP 6,500,000 per month, based on 55 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,500,000 to COP 7,850,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 51,909/m² and the median listed size is 124 m². That is 35% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). 74.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
