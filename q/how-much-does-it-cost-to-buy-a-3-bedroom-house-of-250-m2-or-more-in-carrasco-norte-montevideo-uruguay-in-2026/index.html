How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more in Carrasco Norte, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more for sale in Carrasco Norte, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 499,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 398,750 to USD 805,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,282/m² and the median listed size is 476 m². That is 86% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 269,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
