How much does it cost to buy a 4+ bedroom house in the Puente Aranda locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in the Puente Aranda locality, Bogotá is COP 850,000,000, based on 391 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 690,000,000 to COP 1,047,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,457,277/m² and the median listed size is 252 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 850,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 165,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 79% of listings include parking.
