Where is it cheaper to buy a 2 room apartment in Latin America?

In Soacha, Colombia: USD 51,841 in the median.

Buy a 2 room apartment is cheaper in Soacha (Colombia), with a median of USD 51,841, and more expensive in Lisbon (Portugal), with USD 685,691. In Bogotá the median is USD 169,662 (540 million pesos), placed 46 of 62 from minor to major.
