What does the study “Housing in Palmira: price per m², rental, rooms, estrato and HOA fee” show?

An apartment of 2 rooms in Palmira is requested in 245 million pesos for sale and is rented for 1.10 million pesos per month in the medium. The gross implicit return on rent is 6.3% per year.
