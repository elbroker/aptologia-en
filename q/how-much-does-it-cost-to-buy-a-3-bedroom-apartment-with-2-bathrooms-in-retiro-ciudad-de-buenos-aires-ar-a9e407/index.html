How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms in Retiro, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Retiro, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 362,500, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 285,250 to USD 496,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,528/m² and the median listed size is 144 m². That is 120% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
