How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking with 2 bathrooms in San Bartolo, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms for sale in San Bartolo, Lima, Peru is USD 166,800, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 125,000 to USD 190,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,821/m² and the median listed size is 88 m². That is 35% below the study's reference median for Lima (USD 258,023). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
