How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 45–70 m² in San Miguel, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in San Miguel, Lima, Peru is PEN 377,000, based on 167 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 335,000 to PEN 429,411. The median price per square metre is PEN 6,354/m² and the median listed size is 61 m². That is 39% below the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 621,600). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PEN 1. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
