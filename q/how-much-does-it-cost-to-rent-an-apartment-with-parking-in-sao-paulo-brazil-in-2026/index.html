How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking in São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking in São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 5,150 per month, based on 7,771 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 3,000 to BRL 9,100. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 65.7/m² and the median listed size is 73 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,310. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
