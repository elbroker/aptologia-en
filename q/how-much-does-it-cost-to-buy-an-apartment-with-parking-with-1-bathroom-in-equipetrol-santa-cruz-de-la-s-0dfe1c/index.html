How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking with 1 bathroom in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking with 1 bathroom for sale in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 85,152, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 72,500 to USD 99,500. The median price per square metre is USD 1,596/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 15% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 60. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
