How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Medellín?

Between 1,400,000 pesos (estrato 2) and 5,880,764 pesos (estrato 6) per month in the median, according to October 2026 announcements.

In Medellín, rent an apartment of 2 rooms costs in the median 1,400,000 pesos per month in estrato 2 and 5,880,764 pesos in estrato 6, 4.2 times more. per square meter, the rent goes from 28,179 pesos to 55,147 pesos per month according to the estrato.
