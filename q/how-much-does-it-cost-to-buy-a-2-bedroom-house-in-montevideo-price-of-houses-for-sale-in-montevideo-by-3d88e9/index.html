How much does it cost to buy a 2 bedroom house in Montevideo?

USD 140,000 in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between USD 99,000 and USD 215,000.

In Montevideo, a 1 bedroom house costs USD 115,000 in the median, one 2 USD 140,000 bedroom (+ 21.7%) and one 3 or more USD 350,000 (+ 150.0%).
