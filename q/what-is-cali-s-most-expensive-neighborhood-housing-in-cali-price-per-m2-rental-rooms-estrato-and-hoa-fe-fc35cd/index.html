What is Cali's most expensive neighborhood?

Pance, with 7.00 million pesos per m² in apartments for sale (October of 2026).

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter of Cali is Pance, with 7.00 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is Torres de Comfandi, with 3.10 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 1.55 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 280 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.7% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 17.3% of the rent (469,500 pesos per month in the median).
