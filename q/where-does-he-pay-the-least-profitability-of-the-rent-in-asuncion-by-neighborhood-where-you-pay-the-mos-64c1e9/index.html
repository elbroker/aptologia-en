Where does he pay the least?

In Trinidad: 7.5% gross per year (13.4 years of rent).

In Asunción, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical neighborhood 8.4% gross per year: the price requested is equivalent to 11.9 years of rent. The area with the highest gross return is Las Mercedes (9.6%) and the smaller district, Trinidad (7.5%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 8.0%.
