How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more in Sucre, Caracas, Venezuela in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more for sale in Sucre, Caracas, Venezuela is USD 320,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 223,750 to USD 402,500. The median price per square metre is USD 666/m² and the median listed size is 459 m². That is 29% below the study's reference median for Caracas (USD 450,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 6.
