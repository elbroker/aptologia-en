What does the study “How many square meters has an apartment in Latin America: medium area by number of rooms and country” show?

An apartment 2 for sale has in the medium 108 m² in the Dominican Republic and only 61 m² in Chile. In Colombia, one of 2 rooms has 68 m² and one of 3 or more, 106 m².
