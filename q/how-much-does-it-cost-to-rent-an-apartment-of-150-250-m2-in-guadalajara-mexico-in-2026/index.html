How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 150–250 m² in Guadalajara, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 150–250 m² in Guadalajara, Mexico is MXN 36,250 per month, based on 80 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 31,000 to MXN 44,625. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 207/m² and the median listed size is 174 m². The median number of bathrooms is 3.
