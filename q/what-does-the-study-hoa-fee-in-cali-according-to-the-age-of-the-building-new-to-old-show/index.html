What does the study “HOA fee in Cali according to the age of the building: new to old” show?

In Cali, the highest square meter HOA fee is paid in the group buildings "21 to 30 years" (5,913 pesos per m² per month in the median) and the lowest in "1 to 5 years" (5,155 pesos). Average monthly rate per strip: For starters: 543,000 pesos; 1 to 5 years: 450,000 pesos; 6 to 10 years: 429,000 pesos; 11 to 20 years: 593,000 pesos; 21 to 30 years: 760,000 pesos.
