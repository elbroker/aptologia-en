How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Belgrano, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Belgrano, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 243,000, based on 101 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 205,000 to USD 277,088. The median price per square metre is USD 4,500/m² and the median listed size is 53 m². That is 47% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
