How much does it cost to rent a home in Sacomã, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Sacomã, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 1,920 per month, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 1,631 to BRL 2,575. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 45.8/m² and the median listed size is 46.5 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 474. 64.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
