How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in El Retiro?

4,175,000 pesos per month in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between 3,500,000 pesos and 5,500,000 pesos.

In El Retiro, rent a 1 room apartment costs in the median 3,950,000 pesos per month and one of 2 rooms, 4,175,000 pesos (+ 5.7%). One of 3 or more rooms is requested in 4,200,000 pesos.
