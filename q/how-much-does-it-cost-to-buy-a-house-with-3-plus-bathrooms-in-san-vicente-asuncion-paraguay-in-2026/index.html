How much does it cost to buy a house with 3+ bathrooms in San Vicente, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in San Vicente, Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 1,040,000,000, based on 76 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 720,000,000 to PYG 1,200,000,000. The median price per square metre is PYG 3,426,573/m² and the median listed size is 300 m². That is 7% above the study's reference median for Asunción (PYG 975,000,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
