How much does it cost to buy a home in a gated complex in Los Patios in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in a gated complex for sale in Los Patios is COP 400,000,000, based on 63 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 272,500,000 to COP 895,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,729,706/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median (COP 380,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 327,500. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 85.7% of listings include parking.
