How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Península, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Península, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 249,500, based on 226 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 200,000 to USD 313,750. The median price per square metre is USD 3,300/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². That is 20% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 310,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 592. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
