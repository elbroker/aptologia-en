How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Punta Carretas, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Punta Carretas, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 211,200, based on 83 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 187,000 to USD 243,040. The median price per square metre is USD 3,942/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is 14% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 185,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
