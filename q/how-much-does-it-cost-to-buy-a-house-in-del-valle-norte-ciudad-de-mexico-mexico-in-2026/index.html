How much does it cost to buy a house in Del Valle Norte, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Del Valle Norte, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 13,700,000, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 9,550,000 to MXN 15,870,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 45,707/m² and the median listed size is 298 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 14,750,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
