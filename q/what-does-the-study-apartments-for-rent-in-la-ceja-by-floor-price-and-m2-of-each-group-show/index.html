What does the study “Apartments for rent in La Ceja by floor: price and m² of each group” show?

In La Ceja, an apartment for rent from the "Piso 1 - 2" group is requested in 2,750,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the "Piso 11 - 20 group in 3,300,000 pesos per month (+ 20.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 3 - 5" (37,679 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Floor 11 - 20" (41,667 pesos / m² per month).
