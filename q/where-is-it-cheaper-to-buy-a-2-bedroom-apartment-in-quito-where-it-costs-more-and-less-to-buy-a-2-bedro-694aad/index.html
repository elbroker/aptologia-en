Where is it cheaper to buy a 2 bedroom apartment in Quito?

In Ponceano: USD 89,500 in the median.

In Quito, a 2 bedroom department costs USD 187,500 in the middle in Cumbayá, the most expensive sector, and USD 89,500 in Ponceano, the cheapest: 2.1 times less. We compare 7 sectors with at least 30 ads from 2 bedrooms.
