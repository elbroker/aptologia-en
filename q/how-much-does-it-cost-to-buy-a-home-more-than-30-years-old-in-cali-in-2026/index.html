How much does it cost to buy a home more than 30 years old in Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for homes more than 30 years old for sale in Cali is COP 770,000,000, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 511,250,000 to COP 1,300,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,420,139/m² and the median listed size is 246 m². That is 133% above the study's reference median (COP 330,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 742,763. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 87% of listings include parking.
