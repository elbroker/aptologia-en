How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 150–250 m² in Recoleta, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 150–250 m² in Recoleta, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 2,800 per month, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,450 to USD 3,625. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 15.9/m² and the median listed size is 180 m². That is 72% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 1,625). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
