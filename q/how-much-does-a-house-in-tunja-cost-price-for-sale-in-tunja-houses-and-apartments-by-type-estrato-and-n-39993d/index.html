How much does a house in Tunja cost?

The median price requested is $388 million ($3,994,223 per m²), with data from 18 October of 2026.

A house for sale in Tunja asks in the median $388 million ($3,994,223 per m²); an apartment, $340 million, and a house, $550 million.
