What does the study “Buy or rent in Latin America? How many years of rent is worth an apartment of 2 rooms in 48 cities” show?

Luque (Paraguay) is the 48 city of Latin America and Portugal where an apartment 2 costs less years of its own rent: 10.7; in Santiago (Chile) it costs 38.6 years. Among the capitals, the relationship goes from 12.6 years in Mexico City to 38.6 in Santiago; Bogotá is in 17.0.
