How much does it cost to rent a home up to 5 years old in the Kennedy locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes up to 5 years old in the Kennedy locality, Bogotá is COP 1,500,000 per month, based on 367 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,300,000 to COP 1,850,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 32,843/m² and the median listed size is 47 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 1,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 165,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 31.3% of listings include parking.
