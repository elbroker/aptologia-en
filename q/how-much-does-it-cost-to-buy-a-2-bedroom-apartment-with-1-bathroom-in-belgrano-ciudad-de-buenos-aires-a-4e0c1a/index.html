How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment with 1 bathroom in Belgrano, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom for sale in Belgrano, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 187,000, based on 74 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 150,000 to USD 256,750. The median price per square metre is USD 2,907/m² and the median listed size is 63.5 m². That is 13% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
