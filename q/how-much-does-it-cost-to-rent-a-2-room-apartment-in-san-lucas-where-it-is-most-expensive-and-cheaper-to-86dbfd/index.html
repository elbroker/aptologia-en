How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in San Lucas?

7,400,000 pesos per month in the median (October of 2026); the central half between 5,800,000 pesos and 8,525,000 pesos.

In Medellín, an apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 7,400,000 pesos a month in the middle of San Lucas, the most expensive neighborhood, and 1,475,000 pesos a month in San Antonio de Prado, the cheapest: 5.0 times less. We compare 30 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
