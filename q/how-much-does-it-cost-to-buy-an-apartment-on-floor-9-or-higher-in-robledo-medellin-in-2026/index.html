How much does it cost to buy an apartment on floor 9 or higher in Robledo, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments on floor 9 or higher for sale in Robledo, Medellín is COP 272,500,000, based on 72 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 240,000,000 to COP 362,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,567,829/m² and the median listed size is 49.2 m². That is 68% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 205,782. The most frequent estrato is 3, 31.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
