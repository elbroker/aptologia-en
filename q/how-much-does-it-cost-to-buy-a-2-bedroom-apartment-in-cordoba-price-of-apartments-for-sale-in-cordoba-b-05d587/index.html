How much does it cost to buy a 2 bedroom apartment in Córdoba?

USD 120,000 in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between USD 95,600 and USD 175,000.

In Córdoba, a 1 bedroom department costs USD 79,500 in the median, one 2 USD 120,000 bedroom (+ 50.9%) and one 3 or more USD 210,000 (+ 75.0%).
