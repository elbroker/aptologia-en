How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment 0–5 years old in San Remo, Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments 0–5 years old for sale in San Remo, Sabaneta is COP 585,000,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 491,250,000 to COP 675,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,375,855/m² and the median listed size is 81.5 m². That is 6% above the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 550,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 409,500. The most frequent estrato is 4, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
