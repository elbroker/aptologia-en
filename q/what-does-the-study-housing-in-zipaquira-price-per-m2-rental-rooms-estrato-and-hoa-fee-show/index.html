What does the study “Housing in Zipaquirá: price per m², rental, rooms, estrato and HOA fee” show?

The sector with the most expensive square meter of Zipaquirá is Algarra Iii, with 4.80 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Villa María, with 3.14 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 6.0% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 18.2% of the rent (224,149 pesos per month in the median).
