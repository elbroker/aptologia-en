What does the study “Housing price per bairro in São Paulo: rent in 74 bairros” show?

Renting an apartment in São Paulo costs more in Vila Nova Conceição, with a medium rental of 10,065 reais (USD 1,978) per month, and less in Itaquera, with 1,500 reales (USD 295). The median of the city is 3,621 real (USD 712) per month; per square meter, the most expensive bairro is Vila Nova Conceição (131.6 real (USD 25.9) per m² per month).
