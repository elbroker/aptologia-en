How much does it cost to rent a studio apartment with parking on floor 9 or higher in El Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for studio apartments with parking on floor 9 or higher in El Poblado, Medellín is COP 4,000,000 per month, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,500,000 to COP 4,600,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 67,582/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 43% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 2,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
