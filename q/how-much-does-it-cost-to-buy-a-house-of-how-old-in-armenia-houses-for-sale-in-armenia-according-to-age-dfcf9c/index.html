How much does it cost to buy a house of how old in Armenia?

For starring (0 years): 1,225 million pesos; 1 to 5 years: 1,050 million pesos; 6 to 10 years: 1,100 million pesos; 11 to 20 years: 1,100 million pesos; 21 to 30 years: 780 million pesos (medium of October of 2026).

In Armenia, a house for sale of the group "To premiere (0 years)" is requested in 1,225 million pesos in the median and one of the group "21 to 30 years" in 780 million pesos (- 36.3%). For each square meter, the cheapest is "To premiere (0 years)" (3.82 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "6 to 10 years" (4.63 million pesos / m²).
