Is the m² of a house cheaper than that of an apartment in Chia?

estrato 3: house - 13.1%; estrato 4: house - 17.6%; estrato 5: house - 23.2% (medium per m², October of 2026).

In Chia, the m² of an apartment for rent goes from 30,000 pesos per month in estrato 3 to 38,503 pesos per month in estrato 5. In estrato 5, the m² of a house is 23.2% cheaper than that of an apartment.
