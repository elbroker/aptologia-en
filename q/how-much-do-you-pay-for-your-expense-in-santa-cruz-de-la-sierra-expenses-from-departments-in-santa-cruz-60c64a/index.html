How much do you pay for your expense in Santa Cruz de la Sierra?

USD 70 per month in the median (USD 0.75 per m²), according to October 2026 announcements.

In Santa Cruz de la Sierra, the costs of a department are in the median USD 70 per month (USD 0.75 per m²). The most expensive area per m² is Equipetrol (USD 0.88) and the cheapest, Hammocks (USD 0.65).
