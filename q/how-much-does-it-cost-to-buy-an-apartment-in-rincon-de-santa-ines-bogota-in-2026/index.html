How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Rincón de Santa Inés, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Rincón de Santa Inés, Bogotá is COP 233,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 216,000,000 to COP 237,000,000. That is 65% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 660,000,000).
