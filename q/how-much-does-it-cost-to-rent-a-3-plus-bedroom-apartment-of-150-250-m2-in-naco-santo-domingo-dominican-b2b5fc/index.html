How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 150–250 m² in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 2,000 per month, based on 71 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,675 to USD 2,800. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 11/m² and the median listed size is 186 m². That is 48% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
