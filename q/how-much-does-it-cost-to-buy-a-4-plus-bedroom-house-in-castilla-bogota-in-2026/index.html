How much does it cost to buy a 4+ bedroom house in Castilla, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in Castilla, Bogotá is COP 797,000,000, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 692,500,000 to COP 980,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,576,783/m² and the median listed size is 198 m². That is 36% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,250,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 143,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 71.4% of listings include parking.
