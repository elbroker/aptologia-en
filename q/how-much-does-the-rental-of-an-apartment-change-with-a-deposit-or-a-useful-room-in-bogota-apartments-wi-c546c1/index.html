How much does the rental of an apartment change with a deposit or a useful room in Bogotá?

- 3.5% per m² in front of comparable apartments of the same estrato and rooms, cut to 13 October of 2026.

In Bogotá, an apartment for rent with a deposit or a useful room costs 3.5% less per square meter than a comparable one of the same estrato and number of rooms. Thus 3,008 of 18,920 ads are announced (15.9%); the medium rental is $6,000,000 with the attribute and $2,100,000 without it.
