How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment with parking in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments with parking in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 900 per month, based on 113 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 700 to USD 1,185. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 9.53/m² and the median listed size is 97 m². That is 58% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 570). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 15. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
