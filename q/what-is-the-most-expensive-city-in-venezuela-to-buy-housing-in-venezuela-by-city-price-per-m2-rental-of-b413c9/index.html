What is the most expensive city in Venezuela to buy?

Caracas: USD 1,400 per m² in the median (October of 2026).

The most expensive square meter of apartment in Venezuela is in Caracas, with USD 1,400 in the medium of ads for sale; the cheapest of the 2 measured cities, in Maracaibo, with USD 758. In Caracas, an apartment of 2 rooms is rented for USD 950 per month and sold for USD 160,000: 14.0 years of rent.
