What does the study “Aluguel em São Paulo by number of quarts: kitnet, 1, 2, 3 and 4 quartos” show?

In São Paulo an apartment of 2 quartos is rented in the median by 3,000 reales (USD 590) per month, as is one of 1 quarto, and one of 3 quartos per 7,250 reales (USD 1,425). A kitnet or studio is requested in 2,650 real (USD 521), and per m² costs 96 real (USD 18.8) against 56 real (USD 11.1) in 2 quartos.
