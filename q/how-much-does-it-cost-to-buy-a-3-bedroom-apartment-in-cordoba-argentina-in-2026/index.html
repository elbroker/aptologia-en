How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Córdoba, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Córdoba, Argentina is USD 210,000, based on 117 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 125,000 to USD 314,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,700/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 62% above the study's reference median for Córdoba (USD 130,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
