How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Antiguo Country, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Antiguo Country, Bogotá is COP 5,000,000 per month, based on 92 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,595,000 to COP 7,125,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 64,575/m² and the median listed size is 84 m². That is 43% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 860,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 92.4% of listings include parking.
