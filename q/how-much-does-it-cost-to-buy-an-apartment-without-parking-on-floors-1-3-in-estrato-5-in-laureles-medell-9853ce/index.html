How much does it cost to buy an apartment without parking on floors 1–3 in estrato 5 in Laureles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments without parking on floors 1–3 in estrato 5 for sale in Laureles, Medellín is COP 638,000,000, based on 49 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 470,000,000 to COP 800,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,932,203/m² and the median listed size is 113 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 375,000. The median number of bathrooms is 3.
