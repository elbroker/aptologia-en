How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many parking lots in Porto?

No parking: 314,950 EUR (USD 354,030); 1 parking: 414,000 EUR (USD 465,370); 2 or more parking: 920,000 EUR (USD 1,034,156) (medium of October of 2026).

In Porto, an apartment for sale from the "No parking" group is requested in 314,950 euros (USD 354,030) in the median and one from the group "2 or more parking" in 920,000 euros (USD 1,034,156) (+ 192.1%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking" (4,874 euros (USD 5,479) / m²) and the most expensive "2 or more parking" (6,036 euros (USD 6,785) / m²).
