How much does an apartment rent in Bogotá?

The gross return is 8.0% per year, before HOA fee, taxes, vacations and maintenance.

Buying an apartment to rent it yields more in Punta del Este (Uruguay): a gross return of 13.5% annually, compared to 3.7% in Lisbon (Portugal), the lowest. In Bogotá, the gross profit implied is 8.0%; the median of 66 cities, 6.9%.
