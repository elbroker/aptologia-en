How much does it cost to buy an apartment in estrato 4 in Madrid in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments in estrato 4 for sale in Madrid is COP 205,500,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 190,250,000 to COP 222,825,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,763,709/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 205,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 152,600. 4.3% of listings include parking.
