How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 45–70 m² on floor 9 or higher in estrato 4 in Las Lomitas, Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² on floor 9 or higher in estrato 4 for sale in Las Lomitas, Sabaneta is COP 422,000,000, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 374,500,000 to COP 465,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,876,228/m² and the median listed size is 61 m². That is 23% below the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 550,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 330,000. 72.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
