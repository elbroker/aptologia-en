How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² in Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² in Sabaneta is COP 4,150,000 per month, based on 411 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,850,000 to COP 4,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 36,364/m² and the median listed size is 110 m². That is 34% above the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 3,100,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 97% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
