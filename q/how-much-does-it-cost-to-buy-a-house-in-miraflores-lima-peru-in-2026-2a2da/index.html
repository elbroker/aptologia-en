How much does it cost to buy a house in Miraflores, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Miraflores, Lima, Peru is USD 755,000, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 612,500 to USD 986,500. The median price per square metre is USD 2,353/m² and the median listed size is 352 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Lima (USD 720,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
