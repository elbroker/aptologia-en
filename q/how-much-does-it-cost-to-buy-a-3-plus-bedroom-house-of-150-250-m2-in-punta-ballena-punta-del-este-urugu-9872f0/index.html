How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 150–250 m² in Punta Ballena, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² for sale in Punta Ballena, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 490,000, based on 106 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 392,750 to USD 650,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,500/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 30% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
