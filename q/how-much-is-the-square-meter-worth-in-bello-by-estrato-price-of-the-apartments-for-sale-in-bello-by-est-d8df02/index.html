How much is the square meter worth in Bello by estrato?

From 4.38 million pesos (estrato 2) to 6.21 million pesos (estrato 4) in the median of October 2026.

In Bello the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 4.38 million pesos in estrato 2 and 6.21 million pesos in estrato 4, 1.4 times more. The average total price ranges from 248 million pesos to 415 million pesos according to the estrato.
