How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment with 1 bathroom in Jara, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom for sale in Jara, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 87,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 76,750 to USD 94,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,772/m² and the median listed size is 48 m². That is 38% below the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 140,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
