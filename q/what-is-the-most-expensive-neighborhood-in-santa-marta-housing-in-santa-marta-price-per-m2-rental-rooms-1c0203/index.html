What is the most expensive neighborhood in Santa Marta?

Well Colorado, with 12.35 million pesos per m² in apartments for sale (October of 2026).

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter of Santa Marta is Pozos Colorado, with 12.35 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is Garden, with 4.31 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 3.50 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 700 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 6.2% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 17.0% of the rent (553,000 pesos per month in the median).
