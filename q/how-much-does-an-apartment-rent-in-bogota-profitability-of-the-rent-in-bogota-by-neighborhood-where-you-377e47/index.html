How much does an apartment rent in Bogotá?

In the typical neighborhood, 6.1% gross per year: 12 months of rent requested on the price of sale ordered, before expenses and taxes, with data of 5 October of 2026.

In Bogotá, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical neighborhood 6.1% gross per year: the price requested is equivalent to 16.4 years of rent. The area with the highest gross return is Chico Santa Barbara (12.0%) and the smaller El Centro (0.9%), between 197 neighborhoods with 30 ads or more for rent and sale.
