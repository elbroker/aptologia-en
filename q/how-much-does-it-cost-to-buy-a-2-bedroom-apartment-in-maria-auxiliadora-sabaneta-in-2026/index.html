How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in María Auxiliadora, Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in María Auxiliadora, Sabaneta is COP 320,000,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 37% below the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 510,000,000).
