How much does it cost to rent a studio apartment under 45 m² in Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for studio apartments under 45 m² in Asunción, Paraguay is USD 800 per month, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 700 to USD 900. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 22.2/m² and the median listed size is 38 m². The median number of bathrooms is 1.
