How much does it cost to rent a 3-bedroom apartment in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3-bedroom apartments in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá is COP 7,900,000 per month, based on 1,479 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,500,000 to COP 12,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 48,680/m² and the median listed size is 158 m². That is 68% above the study's reference median (COP 4,700,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,422,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 97.4% of listings include parking.
