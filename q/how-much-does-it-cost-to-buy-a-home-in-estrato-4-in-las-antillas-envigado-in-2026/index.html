How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 4 in Las Antillas, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 4 for sale in Las Antillas, Envigado is COP 560,000,000, based on 135 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
