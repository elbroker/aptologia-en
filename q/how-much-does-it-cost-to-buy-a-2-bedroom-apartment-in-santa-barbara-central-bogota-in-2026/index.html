How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Santa Barbara Central, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Santa Barbara Central, Bogotá is COP 650,000,000, based on 104 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 548,000,000 to COP 796,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,024,725/m² and the median listed size is 89 m². That is 21% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 535,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 811,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 99% of listings include parking.
