How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms for sale in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 170,000, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 116,000 to USD 228,750. The median price per square metre is USD 614/m² and the median listed size is 300 m². That is 6% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 160,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
