What does the study “Houses for rent in Santa Cruz de la Sierra according to number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In Santa Cruz de la Sierra, a house for rent from the "1 bathroom" group is ordered in USD 433 per month in the median and one from the "3 bath" group in USD 1,500 per month (+ 246.4%). per square metre, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (USD 3.9 / m² per month) and the most expensive "3 bathrooms" (USD 5.1 / m² per month).
