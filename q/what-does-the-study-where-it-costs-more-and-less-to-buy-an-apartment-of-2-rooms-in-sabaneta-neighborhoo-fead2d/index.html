What does the study “Where it costs more and less to buy an apartment of 2 rooms in Sabaneta: neighborhood ranking” show?

In Sabaneta, an apartment of 2 rooms costs 569 million pesos in the medium in Las Lomites, the most expensive neighborhood, and 440 million pesos in Calle Larga, the cheapest: 1.3 times less. We compare 12 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
