How much does it cost to rent a house with how many bathrooms in Quito?

2 bathrooms: USD 850 per month; 3 bathrooms: USD 1,300 per month; 4 or more bathrooms: USD 2,300 per month (mid-October of 2026).

In Quito, a house for rent from the group "2 bathrooms" is requested in USD 850 per month in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in USD 2,300 per month (+ 170.6%). per square metre, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (USD 5.0 / m² per month) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (USD 5.3 / m² per month).
