How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with parking in Cartagena in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking for sale in Cartagena is COP 670,000,000, based on 362 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 450,000,000 to COP 860,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,662,526/m² and the median listed size is 60.8 m². That is 16% below the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 800,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 555,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
