How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 6 in Santa Mónica, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 6 for sale in Santa Mónica, Barranquilla is COP 950,000,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 670,000,000 to COP 1,098,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,375,000/m² and the median listed size is 199 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 719,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
