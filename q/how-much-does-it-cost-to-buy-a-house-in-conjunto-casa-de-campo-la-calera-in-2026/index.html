How much does it cost to buy a house in Conjunto Casa de Campo, La Calera in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Conjunto Casa de Campo, La Calera is COP 2,900,000,000, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,380,000,000 to COP 3,100,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,273,148/m² and the median listed size is 440 m². That is 32% above the study's reference median for La Calera (COP 2,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,570,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
