How much does it cost to rent a house in a gated complex in estrato 6 in Cali in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in a gated complex in estrato 6 in Cali is COP 13,000,000 per month, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 10,000,000 to COP 18,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 33,948/m² and the median listed size is 400 m². That is 30% above the study's reference median for Cali (COP 10,000,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
