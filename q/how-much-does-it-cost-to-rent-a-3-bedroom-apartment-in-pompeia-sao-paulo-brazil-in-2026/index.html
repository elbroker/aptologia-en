How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in Pompéia, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Pompéia, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 6,780 per month, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 5,000 to BRL 9,700. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 57.6/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 15% below the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 8,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,800. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
