How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Ciudad Pacífica, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Ciudad Pacífica, Cali is COP 230,000,000, based on 153 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 210,000,000 to COP 270,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,962,264/m² and the median listed size is 59 m². That is 18% below the study's reference median for Cali (COP 280,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 240,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 59.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
