How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 3 in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 3 for sale in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá is COP 420,000,000, based on 361 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 298,000,000 to COP 950,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,008,772/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 74% below the study's reference median (COP 1,600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 330,000. 51% of listings include parking.
