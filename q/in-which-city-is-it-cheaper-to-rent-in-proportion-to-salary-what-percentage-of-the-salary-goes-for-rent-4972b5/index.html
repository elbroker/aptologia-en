In which city is it cheaper to rent in proportion to salary?

Of the 9 measures, in Cali (44.2%) and Manizales (46.8%).

In 6 of the 9 measured cities, rent an apartment of 2 alcobas costs more than half of the income of a two-person home with the average income of its city. In Bogotá it is 54.4%: average order rental of $2,500,000 (Aptology, 5 October of 2026) compared to a $4,594,160 per month income (2 × $2,297,080, per capita income of [DANE, GEIH 2025] (https://www.dane.gov.co/files/operaciones/PM/anex-PM-TotalNacional-2025.xlsx)).
