What does the study “HOA fee rate in Bucharest by estrato: how much is paid per month and per m²” show?

In Bucaramanga, the median rate of HOA fee of an apartment goes from 263,000 pesos per month in estrato 3 to 782,000 pesos in estrato 6, 3.0 times more. per square meter, it goes from 3,900 pesos to 5,694 pesos per month.
