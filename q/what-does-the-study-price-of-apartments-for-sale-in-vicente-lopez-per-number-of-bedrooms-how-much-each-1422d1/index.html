What does the study “Price of apartments for sale in Vicente López per number of bedrooms: how much each size costs” show?

In Vicente López, a 1 bedroom department costs USD 175,700 in the median, one 2 USD 272,000 bedroom (+ 54.8%) and one 3 or more USD 646,960 (+ 137.9%).
