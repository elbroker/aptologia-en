How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 445,000, based on 81 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 369,000 to USD 580,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,700/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 24% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
