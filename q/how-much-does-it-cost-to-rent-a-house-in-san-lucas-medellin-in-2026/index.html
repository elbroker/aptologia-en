How much does it cost to rent a house in San Lucas, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in San Lucas, Medellín is COP 12,500,000 per month, based on 95 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 9,500,000 to COP 17,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 45,000/m² and the median listed size is 272 m². That is 47% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 8,500,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 96.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
