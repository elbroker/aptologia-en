How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment on floors 1–3 in Engativá, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments on floors 1–3 for sale in Engativá, Bogotá is COP 369,800,000, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 292,475,000 to COP 493,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,000,000/m² and the median listed size is 72 m². That is 51% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 247,500. The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
