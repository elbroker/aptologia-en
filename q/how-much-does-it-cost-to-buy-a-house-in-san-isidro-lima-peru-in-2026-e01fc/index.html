How much does it cost to buy a house in San Isidro, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in San Isidro, Lima, Peru is USD 1,450,000, based on 133 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,000,000 to USD 1,980,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,066/m² and the median listed size is 454 m². That is 101% above the study's reference median for Lima (USD 720,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
