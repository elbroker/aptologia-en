How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment in Altos del Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in Altos del Poblado, Medellín is COP 5,500,000 per month, based on 114 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,425,000 to COP 7,150,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 63,889/m² and the median listed size is 85 m². That is 31% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,200,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
