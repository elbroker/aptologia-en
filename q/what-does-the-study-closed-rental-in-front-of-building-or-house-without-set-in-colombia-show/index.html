What does the study “Closed rental in front of building or house without set in Colombia” show?

An apartment for rent in a closed set is requested in Colombia 11.0% more per square meter than one comparable without that feature (same city and number of rooms). 57.8% of apartments for rent declare it. In Bogotá the difference is 3.8% (38.0% of the offer).
