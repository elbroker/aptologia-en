How much does it cost to rent an apartment with how many bathrooms in São Paulo?

1 bath: 2,500 real (USD 491) per month; 2 bath: 5,300 real (USD 1,042) per month; 3 bath: 8,505 real (USD 1,671) per month; 4 or more bath: 12,500 real (USD 2,457) per month (medium October of 2026.

In São Paulo, an apartment for rent from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 2,500 reales (USD 491) per month in the median and one from the group "4 or more reals" in 12,500 reales (USD 2,457) per month (+ 400.0%). per square metre, the cheapest is "1 bathroom" (58.9 real (USD 11.6) / m² per month) and the most expensive "3 bath" (72.2 real (USD 14.2) / m² per month).
