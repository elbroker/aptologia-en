How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms in Estación Central, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms in Estación Central, Santiago, Chile is UF 9.35 per month, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 7.77 to UF 10.6. The median listed size is 50 m². That is 32% below the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 13.8). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
