How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Santa Marta?

Between 2,400,000 pesos (estrato 4) and 4,000,000 pesos (estrato 6) per month in the median, according to October 2026 announcements.

In Santa Marta, rent an apartment of 2 rooms costs in the median 2,400,000 pesos per month in estrato 4 and 4,000,000 pesos in estrato 6, 1.7 times more. per square meter, the rent goes from 31,946 pesos to 48,649 pesos per month according to the estrato.
