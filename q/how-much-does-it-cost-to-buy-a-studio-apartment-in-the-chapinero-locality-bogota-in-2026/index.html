How much does it cost to buy a studio apartment in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for studio apartments for sale in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá is COP 343,850,000, based on 416 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 280,000,000 to COP 490,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,377,293/m² and the median listed size is 34 m². That is 79% below the study's reference median (COP 1,600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 390,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 49.5% of listings include parking.
