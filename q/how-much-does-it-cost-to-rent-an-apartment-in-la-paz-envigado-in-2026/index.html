How much does it cost to rent an apartment in La Paz, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in La Paz, Envigado is COP 3,350,000 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,900,000 to COP 3,900,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 40,805/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 4,400,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 88.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
