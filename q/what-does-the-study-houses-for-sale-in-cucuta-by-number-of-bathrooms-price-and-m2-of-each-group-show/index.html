What does the study “Houses for sale in Cúcuta by number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In Cúcuta, a house for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 320 million pesos in the medium and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 680 million pesos (+ 112.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "1 bath" (2.50 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (3.14 million pesos / m²).
