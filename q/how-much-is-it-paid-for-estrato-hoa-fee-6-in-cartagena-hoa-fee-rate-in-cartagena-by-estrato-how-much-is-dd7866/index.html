How much is it paid for estrato HOA fee 6 in Cartagena?

985,000 pesos per month in the median (8,620 pesos per m²), October 2026.

In Cartagena, the median rate of HOA fee of an apartment goes from 250,000 pesos per month in estrato 3 to 985,000 pesos in estrato 6, 3.9 times more. per square meter, it goes from 4,155 pesos to 8,620 pesos per month.
