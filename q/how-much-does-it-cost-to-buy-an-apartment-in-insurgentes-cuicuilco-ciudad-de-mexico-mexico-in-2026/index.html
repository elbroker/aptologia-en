How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Insurgentes Cuicuilco, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Insurgentes Cuicuilco, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 5,400,000, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 3,800,000 to MXN 6,500,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 46,465/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 4,990,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
