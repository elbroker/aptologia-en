How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 2 bathrooms in Mburucuyá, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 2 bathrooms in Mburucuyá, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 1,340 per month, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,112 to USD 1,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 14.1/m² and the median listed size is 93 m². That is 1% below the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 1,350). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
