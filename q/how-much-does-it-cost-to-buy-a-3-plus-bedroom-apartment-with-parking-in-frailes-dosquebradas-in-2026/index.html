How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with parking in Frailes, Dosquebradas in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking for sale in Frailes, Dosquebradas is COP 247,500,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 220,000,000 to COP 280,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,629,121/m² and the median listed size is 53.5 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median for Dosquebradas (COP 260,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 204,796. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
