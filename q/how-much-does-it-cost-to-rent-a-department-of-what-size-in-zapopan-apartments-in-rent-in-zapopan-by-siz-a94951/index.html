How much does it cost to rent a department of what size in Zapopan?

Less than 50 m²: 17,000 Mexican pesos (USD 921) per month; 50 - 70 m²: 18,000 Mexican pesos (USD 975) per month; 70 - 90 m²: 21,500 Mexican pesos (USD 1,164) per month; 90 - 120 m²: 28,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,516) per month; 120 - 160 m²: 36,600 Mexican pesos (USD 1,982 per month; More than 160 m²: 59,450 Mexican pesos (USD 3,220) per month (medium October of 2026).

In Zapopan, an income department of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 17,000 Mexican pesos (USD 921) per month in the median and one of the group "More than 160 m²" in 59,450 Mexican pesos (USD 3,220) per month (+ 249.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "70 - 90 m²" (273.3 Mexican pesos (USD 14.8) / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (418.3 Mexican pesos (USD 22.7) / m² per month).
