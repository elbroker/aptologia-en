How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 45–70 m² in Viviendas del Sur, Itagüí in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Viviendas del Sur, Itagüí is COP 390,000,000, based on 95 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 350,000,000 to COP 470,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,607,143/m² and the median listed size is 62 m². That is 17% below the study's reference median for Itagüí (COP 470,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 300,000. The most frequent estrato is 3, 86.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
