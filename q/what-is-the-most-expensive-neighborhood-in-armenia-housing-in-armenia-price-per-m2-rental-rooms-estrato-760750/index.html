What is the most expensive neighborhood in Armenia?

El Caimo, with 7.85 million pesos per m² in apartments for sale (October of 2026).

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter in Armenia is El Caimo, with 7.85 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is Centro, with 3.54 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 2.25 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 350 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.3% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 14.0% of the rent (350,000 pesos per month in the median).
