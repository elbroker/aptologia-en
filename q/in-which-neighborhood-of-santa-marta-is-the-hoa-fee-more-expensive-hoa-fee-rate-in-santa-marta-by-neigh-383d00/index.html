In which neighborhood of Santa Marta is the HOA fee more expensive?

In Historical Center: 11,268 pesos per m² per month.

In Santa Marta, the most expensive HOA fee per square meter is paid in Historical Center: 11,268 pesos per m² per month in the median (625,000 fee pesos). The cheapest, in Bavaria, with 5,556 pesos per m². The median rate for the whole city is 572,000 pesos per month (7,060 pesos per m²).
