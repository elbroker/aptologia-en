How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment with 1 bathroom in Tarqui, Guayaquil, Ecuador in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom in Tarqui, Guayaquil, Ecuador is USD 350 per month, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 280 to USD 450. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 5.41/m² and the median listed size is 70 m². That is 59% below the study's reference median for Guayaquil (USD 850). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
