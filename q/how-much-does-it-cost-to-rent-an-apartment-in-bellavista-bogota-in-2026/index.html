How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Bellavista, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Bellavista, Bogotá is COP 8,900,000 per month, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,950,000 to COP 14,250,000. That is 224% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,750,000).
