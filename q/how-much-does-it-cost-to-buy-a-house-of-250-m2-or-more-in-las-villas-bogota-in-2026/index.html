How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more in Las Villas, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more for sale in Las Villas, Bogotá is COP 1,300,000,000, based on 91 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,137,500,000 to COP 1,500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,312,500/m² and the median listed size is 300 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 275,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 95.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
