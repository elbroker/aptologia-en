How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 5 in Laureles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 5 for sale in Laureles, Medellín is COP 860,000,000, based on 427 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 744,950,000 to COP 980,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,884,058/m² and the median listed size is 125 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 600,000. 93.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
