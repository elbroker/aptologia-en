How much does it cost to rent an apartment in a gated complex in San Diego, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in a gated complex in San Diego, Medellín is COP 3,800,000 per month, based on 75 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,275,000 to COP 4,300,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,000/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². That is 21% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 88.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
