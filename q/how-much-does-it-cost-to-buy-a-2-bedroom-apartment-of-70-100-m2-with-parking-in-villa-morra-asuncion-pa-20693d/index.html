How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² with parking in Villa Morra, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with parking for sale in Villa Morra, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 160,000, based on 52 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 143,352 to USD 180,050. The median price per square metre is USD 1,946/m² and the median listed size is 82 m². That is 14% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 140,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
