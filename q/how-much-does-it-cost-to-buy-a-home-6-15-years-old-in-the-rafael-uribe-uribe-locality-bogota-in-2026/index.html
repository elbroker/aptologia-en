How much does it cost to buy a home 6–15 years old in the Rafael Uribe Uribe locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes 6–15 years old for sale in the Rafael Uribe Uribe locality, Bogotá is COP 270,000,000, based on 76 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 216,250,000 to COP 375,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,790,156/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 40% below the study's reference median (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 158,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 46.1% of listings include parking.
