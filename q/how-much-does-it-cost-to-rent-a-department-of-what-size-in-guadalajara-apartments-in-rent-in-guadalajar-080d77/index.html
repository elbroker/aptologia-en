How much does it cost to rent a department of what size in Guadalajara?

Less than 50 m²: 16,700 Mexican pesos (USD 904) per month; 50 - 70 m²: 18,250 Mexican pesos (USD 988) per month; 70 - 90 m²: 23,500 Mexican pesos (USD 1,273) per month; 90 - 120 m²: 28,000 Mexican pesos (USD 1,516) per month; 120 - 160 m²: 33,100 Mexican pesos (USD 1,793 per month; More than 160 m²: 39,900 Mexican pesos (USD 2,161) per month (medium October of 2026).

In Guadalajara, an income department of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 16,700 Mexican pesos (USD 904) per month in the median and one of the group "More than 160 m²" in 39,900 Mexican pesos (USD 2,161) per month (+ 138.9%). per square metre, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (200.8 Mexican pesos (USD 10.9) / m² per month) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (424.5 Mexican pesos (USD 23.0) / m² per month).
