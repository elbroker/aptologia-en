How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Fontibon, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Fontibon, Bogotá is COP 1,500,000 per month, based on 154 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,283,525 to COP 1,987,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 33,794/m² and the median listed size is 46 m². That is 57% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 204,950. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 31.2% of listings include parking.
