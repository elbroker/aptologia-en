What homes are for sale for less than COP 250 million in Cali in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for less than COP 250 million in Cali have a median asking price of COP 220,000,000, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 193,000,000 to COP 240,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,736,572/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 33% below the study's reference median (COP 330,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 218,026. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 26.3% of listings include parking.
