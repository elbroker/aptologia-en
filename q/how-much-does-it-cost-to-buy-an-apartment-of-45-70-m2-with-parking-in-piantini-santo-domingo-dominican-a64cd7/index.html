How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 45–70 m² with parking in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² with parking for sale in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 201,170, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 188,750 to USD 220,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,188/m² and the median listed size is 63 m². That is 16% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
