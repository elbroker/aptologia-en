What does the study “Price of the house by neighborhood in Asunción: sale and rental in 23 neighborhoods” show?

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter of Asunción is Manorá, with USD 2,314 in the median of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Ciudad Nueva, with USD 1,383. For rent, the highest medium rental is requested in Ycuá Satí (6,500,000 Guaraní (USD 1,139) per month) and the lowest in Saxony (3,500,000 Guaraní (USD 613)).
