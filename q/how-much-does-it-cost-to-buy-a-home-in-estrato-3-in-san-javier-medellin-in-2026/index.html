How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 3 in San Javier, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 3 for sale in San Javier, Medellín is COP 360,000,000, based on 51 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 310,000,000 to COP 407,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,558,728/m² and the median listed size is 84 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 212,500. 17.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
