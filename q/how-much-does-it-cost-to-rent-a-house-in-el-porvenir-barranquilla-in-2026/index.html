How much does it cost to rent a house in El Porvenir, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in El Porvenir, Barranquilla is COP 10,000,000 per month, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 6,800,000 to COP 12,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 29,570/m² and the median listed size is 372 m². That is 22% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 8,200,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 86.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
