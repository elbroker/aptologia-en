What does the study “What percentage of the departments for sale costs less than 75,000 dollars? 20 Latin American and Portuguese cities” show?

In Cochabamba, Bolivia, 32.9% of the departments for sale are requested in less than 75,000 dollars, the largest proportion of 20 cities; in Lisbon, Portugal, 93.5% exceed 350,000 dollars.
