How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house with 2 bathrooms in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses with 2 bathrooms in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 2,800 per month, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,250 to USD 3,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 14.3/m² and the median listed size is 224 m². That is 26% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 3,800). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
