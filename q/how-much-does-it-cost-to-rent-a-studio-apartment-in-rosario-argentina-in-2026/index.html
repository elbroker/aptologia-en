How much does it cost to rent a studio apartment in Rosario, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for studio apartments in Rosario, Argentina is ARS 420,000 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from ARS 380,000 to ARS 453,750. The median monthly rent per square metre is ARS 14,425/m² and the median listed size is 30 m². The median number of bathrooms is 1.
