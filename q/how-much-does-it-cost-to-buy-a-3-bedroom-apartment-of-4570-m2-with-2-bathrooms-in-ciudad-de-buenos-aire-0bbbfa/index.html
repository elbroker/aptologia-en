How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 132,500, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 114,250 to USD 170,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,985/m² and the median listed size is 66 m². That is 20% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
