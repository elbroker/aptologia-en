What is the most common price range in Bucharest?

350 - 500 M of pesos, with 28.8% of the ads.

In Bucharest, the price range with the most available apartments for sale is 350 - 500 M of pesos (28.8% of the ads), with a median area of 90 m². 31.3% of the supply costs less than 350 million.
