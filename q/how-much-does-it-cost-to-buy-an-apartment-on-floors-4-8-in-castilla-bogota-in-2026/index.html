How much does it cost to buy an apartment on floors 4–8 in Castilla, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments on floors 4–8 for sale in Castilla, Bogotá is COP 310,000,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 257,500,000 to COP 360,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,480,769/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 59% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 215,000. The most frequent estrato is 3, 67.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
