How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment with parking in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with parking for sale in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 284,500, based on 102 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 246,913 to USD 341,250. The median price per square metre is USD 2,498/m² and the median listed size is 119 m². That is 19% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
