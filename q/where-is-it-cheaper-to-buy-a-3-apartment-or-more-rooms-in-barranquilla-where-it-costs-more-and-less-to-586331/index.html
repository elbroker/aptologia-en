Where is it cheaper to buy a 3 apartment or more rooms in Barranquilla?

In Golden Gate: 190 million pesos in the median.

In Barranquilla, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs 1,465 million pesos in the medium in El Golf, the most expensive neighborhood, and 190 million pesos in Puerta Dorada, the cheapest: 7.7 times less. We compare 40 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
