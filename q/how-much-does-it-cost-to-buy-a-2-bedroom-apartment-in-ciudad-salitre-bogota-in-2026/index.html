How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Ciudad Salitre, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Ciudad Salitre, Bogotá is COP 708,397,268, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 43% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 495,000,000).
