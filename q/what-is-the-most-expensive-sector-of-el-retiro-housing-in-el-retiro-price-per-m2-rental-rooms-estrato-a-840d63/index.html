What is the most expensive sector of El Retiro?

Los Salados, with 10.08 million pesos per m² in apartments for sale (October of 2026).

The sector with the most expensive square meter of El Retiro is Los Salados, with 10.08 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Pantanillo, with 8.73 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 4.30 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 750 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 6.3% per year.
