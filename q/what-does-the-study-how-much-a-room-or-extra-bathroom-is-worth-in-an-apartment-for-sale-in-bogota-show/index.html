What does the study “How much a room or extra bathroom is worth in an apartment for sale in Bogotá” show?

In Bogotá, just like size, estrato and locality, an apartment for sale with a second bathroom asks 11.7% more than one with a single bathroom, and from 2 to 3 rooms changes the price in - 9.2%. With the fixed area, the additional bathroom adds more value than the additional room, which only distributes the same meters.
