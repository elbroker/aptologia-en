How much does it cost to buy an apartment on floors 4–8 0–5 years old in estrato 6 in El Refugio, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments on floors 4–8 0–5 years old in estrato 6 for sale in El Refugio, Bogotá is COP 2,070,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,360,000,000 to COP 2,850,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,055,556/m² and the median listed size is 237 m². That is 176% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,125,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
