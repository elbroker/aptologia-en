How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Boston, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Boston, Barranquilla is COP 264,000,000, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
