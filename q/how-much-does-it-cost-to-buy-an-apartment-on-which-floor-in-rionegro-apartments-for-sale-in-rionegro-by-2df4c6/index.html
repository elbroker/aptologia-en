How much does it cost to buy an apartment on which floor in Rionegro?

Floor 1 - 2: 520 million pesos; Floor 3 - 5: 490 million pesos; Floor 6 - 10: 435 million pesos; Floor 11 - 20: 557 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Rionegro, an apartment for sale from the "Piso 1 - 2" group is requested in 520 million pesos in the median and one from the "Piso 11 - 20 group" in 557 million pesos (+ 7.1%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (6.94 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 11 - 20" (7.70 million pesos / m²).
