How much does it cost to rent an apartment listed by the owner of 45–70 m² in estrato 4 in Cajicá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments listed by the owner of 45–70 m² in estrato 4 in Cajicá is COP 1,775,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,700,000 to COP 2,175,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 33,176/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 15% below the study's reference median for Cajicá (COP 2,100,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
