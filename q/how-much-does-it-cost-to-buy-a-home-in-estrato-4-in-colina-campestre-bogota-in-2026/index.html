How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 4 in Colina Campestre, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 4 for sale in Colina Campestre, Bogotá is COP 680,000,000, based on 317 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 478,000,000 to COP 880,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,991,150/m² and the median listed size is 98 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median (COP 870,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 523,000. 88.6% of listings include parking.
