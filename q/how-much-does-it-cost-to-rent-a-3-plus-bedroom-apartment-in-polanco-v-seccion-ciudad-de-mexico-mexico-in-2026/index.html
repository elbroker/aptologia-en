How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in Polanco V Sección, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Polanco V Sección, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 80,000 per month, based on 65 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 62,000 to MXN 92,465. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 324/m² and the median listed size is 247 m². That is 45% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 55,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
