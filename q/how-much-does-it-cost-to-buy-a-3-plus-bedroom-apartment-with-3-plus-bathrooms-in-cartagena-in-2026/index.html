How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Cartagena in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Cartagena is COP 1,300,000,000, based on 1,609 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 845,000,000 to COP 2,000,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,716,535/m² and the median listed size is 159 m². That is 62% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 800,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,194,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 97.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
