Where is it cheaper to rent an apartment of 3 or more rooms in Bogotá?

In Fontibon: 1,600,000 pesos per month in the median.

In Bogotá, an apartment of 3 or more rooms is rented for 15,000,000 pesos a month in the middle of the quarter in Cabrera, the most expensive, and 1,600,000 pesos a month in Fontibon, the cheapest: 9.4 times less. We compare 29 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
