How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² in Monte Castro, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Monte Castro, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 154,500, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 137,500 to USD 236,850. The median price per square metre is USD 1,909/m² and the median listed size is 82.5 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
