How much does it cost to buy a studio apartment under 45 m² in Cordón, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for studio apartments under 45 m² for sale in Cordón, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 103,250, based on 110 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 95,770 to USD 116,854. The median price per square metre is USD 3,062/m² and the median listed size is 34 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 118,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
