What is the most expensive city to buy housing in Bolivia?

Santa Cruz de la Sierra: USD 1,241 per m² in the median (October of 2026).

The most expensive square meter of apartment is requested in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, with USD 1,241 in the medium, and the cheapest in Cochabamba, with USD 971. To rent one of 2 rooms costs USD 630 a month in Santa Cruz de la Sierra and USD 350 in Cochabamba.
