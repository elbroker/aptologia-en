How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in Providencia, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Providencia, Santiago, Chile is UF 7,430, based on 695 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 5,950 to UF 8,900. The median price per square metre is UF 93.9/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². That is 38% above the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 5,400). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
