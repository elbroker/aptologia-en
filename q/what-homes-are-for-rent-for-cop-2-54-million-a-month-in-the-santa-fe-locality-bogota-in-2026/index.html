What homes are for rent for COP 2.5–4 million a month in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for rent for COP 2.5–4 million a month in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá have a median asking rent of COP 2,900,000 per month, based on 192 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,646,500 to COP 3,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 59,762/m² and the median listed size is 48 m². That is 34% above the study's reference median (COP 2,162,225). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 401,550. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 59.4% of listings include parking.
