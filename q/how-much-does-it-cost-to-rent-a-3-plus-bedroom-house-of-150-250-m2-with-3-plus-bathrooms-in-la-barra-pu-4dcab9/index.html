How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² with 3+ bathrooms in La Barra, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 8,000 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 4,450 to USD 14,250. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 33.6/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 10% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 7,300). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
