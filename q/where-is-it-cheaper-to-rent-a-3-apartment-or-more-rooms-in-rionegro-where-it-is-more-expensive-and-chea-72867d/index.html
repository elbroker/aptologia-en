Where is it cheaper to rent a 3 apartment or more rooms in Rionegro?

In Cimarronas: 1,600,000 pesos per month in the median.

In Rionegro, an apartment of 3 or more rooms is rented for 3,275,000 pesos per month in the medium in Llano Grande, the most expensive neighborhood, and 1,600,000 pesos per month in Cimarronas, the cheapest: 2.0 times less. We compare 11 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
