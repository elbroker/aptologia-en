How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in El Caney, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in El Caney, Cali is COP 330,000,000, based on 87 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 255,000,000 to COP 397,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,078,704/m² and the median listed size is 79 m². That is 34% below the study's reference median for Cali (COP 500,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 434,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 98.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
