How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 77,758, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 65,725 to USD 87,100. The median price per square metre is USD 1,274/m² and the median listed size is 63 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 50. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
