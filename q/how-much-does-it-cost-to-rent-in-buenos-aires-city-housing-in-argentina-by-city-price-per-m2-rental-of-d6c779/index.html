How much does it cost to rent in Buenos Aires City?

An apartment of 2 rooms, USD 726 per month in the middle.

The most expensive square meter of apartment in Argentina is in Vicente López, with USD 3,600 in the medium of ads for sale; the cheapest of the 6 measured cities, in Córdoba, with USD 1,813. In Buenos Aires City, an apartment with 2 rooms is rented for USD 726 per month and sold for USD 160,000: 18.4 years of rent.
