How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Altos del Prado, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Altos del Prado, Barranquilla is COP 790,000,000, based on 351 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 650,000,000 to COP 1,100,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,852,691/m² and the median listed size is 224 m². That is 76% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,200,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 99.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
