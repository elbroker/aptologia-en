What does the study “Students' rooms in Bogotá: apartment and 1 room close to the universities (2026)” show?

Close to the universities of Bogotá, the 1 cheapest apartment is in Seven of August (Barrios Unidos), with a medium rental of $1,500,000. By location, the median goes from $1,500,000 in Los Martyrs to $2,400,000 in Chapinero.
