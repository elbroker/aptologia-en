How much does it cost to buy a home in Cercado de Lima, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Cercado de Lima, Lima, Peru is USD 130,882, based on 167 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 100,441 to USD 166,265. The median price per square metre is USD 1,881/m² and the median listed size is 68.5 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 190. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
