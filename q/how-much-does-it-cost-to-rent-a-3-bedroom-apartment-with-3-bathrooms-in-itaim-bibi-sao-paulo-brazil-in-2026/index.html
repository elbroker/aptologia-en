How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Itaim Bibi, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Itaim Bibi, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 14,840 per month, based on 82 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 9,912 to BRL 20,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 76.1/m² and the median listed size is 158 m². That is 310% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 3,136. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
