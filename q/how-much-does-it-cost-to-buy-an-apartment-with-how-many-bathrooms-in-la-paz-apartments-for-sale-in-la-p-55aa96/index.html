How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many bathrooms in La Paz?

1 bathroom: USD 72,800; 2 bathrooms: USD 110,250; 3 bathrooms: USD 157,000 (mid-October of 2026).

In La Paz, a department for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in USD 72,800 in the median and one of the group "3 bathrooms" in USD 157,000 (+ 115.7%). per square metre, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (USD 1,063 / m²) and the most expensive "1 bathroom" (USD 1,237 / m²).
