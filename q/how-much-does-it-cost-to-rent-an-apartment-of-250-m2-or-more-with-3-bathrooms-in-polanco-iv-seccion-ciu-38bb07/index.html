How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 250 m² or more with 3+ bathrooms in Polanco IV Sección, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 250 m² or more with 3+ bathrooms in Polanco IV Sección, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 110,000 per month, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 89,625 to MXN 130,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 349/m² and the median listed size is 300 m². That is 244% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 32,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
