How much does it cost to rent a studio apartment in Engativá, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for studio apartments in Engativá, Bogotá is COP 1,417,277 per month, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,300,000 to COP 1,850,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 45,714/m² and the median listed size is 30 m². That is 25% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,900,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 252,100. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 24.3% of listings include parking.
