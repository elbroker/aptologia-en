How much does it cost to buy a home with security or a doorman in the Antonio Nariño locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes with security or a doorman for sale in the Antonio Nariño locality, Bogotá is COP 320,000,000, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 271,250,000 to COP 450,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,192,707/m² and the median listed size is 54.5 m². That is 53% below the study's reference median (COP 680,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 218,500. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 55.2% of listings include parking.
