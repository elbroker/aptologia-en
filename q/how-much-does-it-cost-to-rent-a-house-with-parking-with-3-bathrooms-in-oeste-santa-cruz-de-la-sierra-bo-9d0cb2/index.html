How much does it cost to rent a house with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Oeste, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Oeste, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 1,250 per month, based on 67 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 900 to USD 2,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 4.57/m² and the median listed size is 290 m². That is 7% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 1,166). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
