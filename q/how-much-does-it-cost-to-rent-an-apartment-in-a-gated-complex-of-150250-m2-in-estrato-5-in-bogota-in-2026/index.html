How much does it cost to rent an apartment in a gated complex of 150–250 m² in estrato 5 in Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in a gated complex of 150–250 m² in estrato 5 in Bogotá is COP 8,500,000 per month, based on 81 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 7,369,000 to COP 10,900,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 48,750/m² and the median listed size is 180 m². That is 158% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,370,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
