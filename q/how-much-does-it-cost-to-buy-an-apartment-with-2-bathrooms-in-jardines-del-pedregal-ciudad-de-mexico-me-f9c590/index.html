How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 2 bathrooms in Jardines del Pedregal, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Jardines del Pedregal, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 7,400,000, based on 53 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 6,100,000 to MXN 8,900,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 62,319/m² and the median listed size is 124 m². That is 48% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 4,990,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
