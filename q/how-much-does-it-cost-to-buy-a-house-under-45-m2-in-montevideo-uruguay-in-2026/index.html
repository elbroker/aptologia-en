How much does it cost to buy a house under 45 m² in Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for houses under 45 m² for sale in Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 94,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 79,925 to USD 139,095. The median listed size is 38 m². The median number of bathrooms is 1.
