How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Evaristo Morales, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Evaristo Morales, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 155,000, based on 49 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 140,500 to USD 173,900. The median price per square metre is USD 2,810/m² and the median listed size is 59 m². That is 35% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
