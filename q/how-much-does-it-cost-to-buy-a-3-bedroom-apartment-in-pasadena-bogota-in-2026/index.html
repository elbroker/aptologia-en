How much does it cost to buy a 3-bedroom apartment in Pasadena, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3-bedroom apartments for sale in Pasadena, Bogotá is COP 899,732,500, based on 110 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 699,250,000 to COP 1,040,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,501,226/m² and the median listed size is 108 m². That is 1% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 890,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 850,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 99.1% of listings include parking.
