How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment without parking on floors 1–3 in El Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments without parking on floors 1–3 in El Poblado, Medellín is COP 7,050,000 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,100,000 to COP 9,450,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 44,444/m² and the median listed size is 138 m². That is 47% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
