What does the study “The most expensive neighborhoods in Colombia: ranking of the square meter of apartments for sale” show?

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter of apartment in Colombia is El Centro (Cartagena), with 13.39 million pesos in the medium. Of the 40 most expensive neighborhoods, 13 are in Bogotá and 11 in Medellín and its area (Envigado, Sabaneta, Rionegro, El Retiro).
