How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Teusaquillo?

The median is $2,393,900 per month, with 415 unique ads on 13 October of 2026.

The average rental of an apartment or studio apartment in Teusaquillo (Bogotá) is $2,393,900 per month, about $54,286 per m². A 2 rooms costs in the median $3,100,000. The cheapest neighborhood with 5 ads or more is Santa Teresita ($1,675,000) and the most expensive Salitre City ($3,736,000).
