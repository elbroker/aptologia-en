How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in Fontibón, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in Fontibón, Bogotá is COP 245,000,000, based on 66 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 34% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 369,000,000).
