How much does it cost to buy an apartment of what size in Bogotá?

Less than 50 m²: 260 million pesos; 50 - 70 m²: 390 million pesos; 70 - 90 m²: 575 million pesos; 90 - 120 m²: 750 million pesos; 120 - 160 m²: 1,000 million pesos; More than 160 m²: 1,800 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Bogotá, an apartment for sale from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 260 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 1,800 million pesos (+ 592.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "50 - 70 m²" (6.46 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "More than 160 m²" (7.78 million pesos / m²).
