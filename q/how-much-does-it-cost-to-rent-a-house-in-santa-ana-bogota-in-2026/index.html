How much does it cost to rent a house in Santa Ana, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Santa Ana, Bogotá is COP 22,610,000 per month, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 17,725,000 to COP 35,750,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 48,155/m² and the median listed size is 500 m². That is 115% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 10,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 660,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
