How much does it cost to buy a house how big in Bello?

70 - 90 m²: 360 million pesos; 90 - 120 m²: 391 million pesos; 120 - 160 m²: 502 million pesos; More than 160 m²: 965 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Bello, a house for sale from the group "70 - 90 m²" is requested in 360 million pesos in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 965 million pesos (+ 168.1%). For square meter, the cheapest is "120 - 160 m²" (3.81 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "More than 160 m²" (4.44 million pesos / m²).
