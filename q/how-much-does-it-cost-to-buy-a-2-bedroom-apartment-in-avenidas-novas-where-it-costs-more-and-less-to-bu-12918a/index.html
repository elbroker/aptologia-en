How much does it cost to buy a 2 bedroom apartment in Avenidas Novas?

965,000 EUR (USD 1,084,740) in the medium (October of 2026); the central half between EUR 833,750 (USD 937,204) and EUR 1,273,750 (USD 1,431,801).

In Lisbon, a 2 bedroom apartment costs 965,000 euros (USD 1,084,740) in the median in Avenidas Novas, the most expensive freguesia, and 575,000 euros (USD 646,348) in Santa María Maio, the cheapest: 1.7 times less. We compare 7 freguesias with at least 30 ads from 2 bedrooms.
