What does the study “How much each room costs: rental per bedroom in dollars in 75 Latin American and Portuguese cities” show?

Each room in an apartment for rent costs at the median USD 120 per month in Tocancipá (Colombia), the cheapest city, and USD 2,050 in Punta del Este (Uruguay), the most expensive. In Bogotá they are USD 614 per room.
