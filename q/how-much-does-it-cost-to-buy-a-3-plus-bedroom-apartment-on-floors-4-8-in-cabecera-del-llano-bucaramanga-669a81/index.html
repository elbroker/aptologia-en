How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment on floors 4–8 in Cabecera del Llano, Bucaramanga in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments on floors 4–8 for sale in Cabecera del Llano, Bucaramanga is COP 750,000,000, based on 63 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 600,000,000 to COP 885,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,884,045/m² and the median listed size is 157 m². That is 74% above the study's reference median for Bucaramanga (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 884,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
