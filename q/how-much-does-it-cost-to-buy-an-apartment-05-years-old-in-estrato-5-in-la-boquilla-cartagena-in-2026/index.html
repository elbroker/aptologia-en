How much does it cost to buy an apartment 0–5 years old in estrato 5 in La Boquilla, Cartagena in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments 0–5 years old in estrato 5 for sale in La Boquilla, Cartagena is COP 875,000,000, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 750,000,000 to COP 1,290,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,731,211/m² and the median listed size is 103 m². That is 9% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 800,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 800,000. 98.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
