How much does it cost to buy a 3-bedroom apartment in Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for 3-bedroom apartments for sale in Barranquilla is COP 375,000,000, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 196,500,000 to COP 470,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,666,667/m² and the median listed size is 88 m². That is 44% above the study's reference median (COP 260,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 417,506. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 55.3% of listings include parking.
