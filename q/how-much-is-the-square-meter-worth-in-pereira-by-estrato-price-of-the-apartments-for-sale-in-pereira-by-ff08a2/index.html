How much is the square meter worth in Pereira by estrato?

From 3.90 million pesos (estrato 2) to 6.12 million pesos (estrato 6) in the median of October 2026.

In Pereira the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 3.90 million pesos in estrato 2 and 6.12 million pesos in estrato 6, 1.6 times more. The average total price ranges from 188 million pesos to 600 million pesos according to the estrato.
