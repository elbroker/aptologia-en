How much does it cost to buy an apartment 0–5 years old in La Aguacatala, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments 0–5 years old for sale in La Aguacatala, Medellín is COP 902,000,000, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 650,000,000 to COP 1,360,000,000. The median listed size is 129 m². That is 7% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 676,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 94.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
