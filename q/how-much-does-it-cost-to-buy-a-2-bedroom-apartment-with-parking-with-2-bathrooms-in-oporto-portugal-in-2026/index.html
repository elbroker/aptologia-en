How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment with parking with 2 bathrooms in Oporto, Portugal in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms for sale in Oporto, Portugal is EUR 450,000, based on 90 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 395,000 to EUR 578,125. The median price per square metre is EUR 5,108/m² and the median listed size is 88 m². That is 6% above the study's reference median for Oporto (EUR 425,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
