How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 3 or more rooms in Fabricato?

510 million pesos in the medium (October of 2026); the central half between 431 million pesos and 602 million pesos.

In Bello, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs 510 million pesos in the medium in Fabricato, the most expensive neighborhood, and 270 million pesos in San Gabriel, the cheapest: 1.9 times less. We compare 14 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
