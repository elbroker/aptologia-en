What does the study “Apartments for rent in Floridablanca by floor: price and m² of each group” show?

In Floridablanca, an apartment for rent of the group "Piso 3 - 5" is requested in 2,000,000 pesos per month in the medium and one of the group "Piso 11 - 20" in 2,400,000 pesos per month (+ 20.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 3 - 5" (23,846 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Floor 11 - 20" (30,159 pesos / m² per month).
