How much does it cost to buy a home in Ciudad Alsacia, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Ciudad Alsacia, Bogotá is COP 500,000,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 325,000,000 to COP 590,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,066,667/m² and the median listed size is 67 m². That is 42% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 314,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 58.5% of listings include parking.
