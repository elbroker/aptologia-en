How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 4 in Pan de Azúcar, Sabaneta in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 4 in Pan de Azúcar, Sabaneta is COP 3,100,000 per month, based on 106 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
