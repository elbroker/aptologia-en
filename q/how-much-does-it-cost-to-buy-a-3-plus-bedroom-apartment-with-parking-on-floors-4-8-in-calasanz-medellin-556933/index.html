How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with parking on floors 4–8 in Calasanz, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking on floors 4–8 for sale in Calasanz, Medellín is COP 558,000,000, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 470,000,000 to COP 760,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,388,889/m² and the median listed size is 97 m². That is 34% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 363,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
