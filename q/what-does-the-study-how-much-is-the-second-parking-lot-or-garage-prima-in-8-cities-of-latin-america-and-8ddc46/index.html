What does the study “How much is the second parking lot or garage? Prima in 8 cities of Latin America and Portugal” show?

An apartment for sale with 2 parking is requested + 44.1% against one with 1 the same number of rooms and area range in Caracas (Venezuela), the largest premium of 8 cities; in Lisbon (Portugal) the difference is + 9.5%. For rent, the premium goes from + 24.6% to + 52.3%.
