How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms in San Pedro Garza Garcia, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms in San Pedro Garza Garcia, Mexico is MXN 64,000 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 45,750 to MXN 80,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 374/m² and the median listed size is 150 m². That is 28% above the study's reference median for San Pedro Garza Garcia (MXN 50,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
