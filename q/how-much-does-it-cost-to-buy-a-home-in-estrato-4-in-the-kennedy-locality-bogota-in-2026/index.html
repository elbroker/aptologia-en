How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 4 in the Kennedy locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 4 for sale in the Kennedy locality, Bogotá is COP 620,000,000, based on 240 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 498,750,000 to COP 800,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,000,000/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 38% above the study's reference median (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 343,150. 82.1% of listings include parking.
