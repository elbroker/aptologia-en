How much does it cost to rent an apartment on which floor in Pereira?

Floor 1 - 2: 2,700,000 pesos per month; Floor 3 - 5: 2,650,000 pesos per month; Floor 6 - 10: 2,875,000 pesos per month; Floor 11 - 20: 3,150,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Pereira, an apartment for rent from the "Piso 1 - 2" group is requested in 2,700,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the "Piso 11 - 20 group in 3,150,000 pesos per month (+ 16.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (31,183 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Floor 11 - 20" (36,716 pesos / m² per month).
