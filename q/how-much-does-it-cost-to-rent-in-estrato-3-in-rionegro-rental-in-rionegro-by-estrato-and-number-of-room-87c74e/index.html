How much does it cost to rent in estrato 3 in Rionegro?

An apartment of 2 rooms in estrato 3 is requested in 1,600,000 pesos per month.

In Rionegro, rent an apartment of 2 rooms costs in the median 1,600,000 pesos per month in estrato 3 and 6,200,000 pesos in estrato 6, 3.9 times more. per square meter, the rent goes from 30,769 pesos to 54,545 pesos per month according to the estrato.
