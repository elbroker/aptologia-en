How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 250 m² or more on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 in El Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 250 m² or more on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 for sale in El Poblado, Medellín is COP 2,375,000,000, based on 104 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,750,000,000 to COP 2,950,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,971,355/m² and the median listed size is 309 m². That is 183% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,529,000. 97.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
