How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in a gated complex in Villa Country, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in a gated complex in Villa Country, Barranquilla is COP 4,080,000 per month, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,900,000 to COP 4,975,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 29,979/m² and the median listed size is 135 m². That is 46% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
