What homes are for sale for COP 250–400 million in the Bosa locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for COP 250–400 million in the Bosa locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 300,000,000, based on 125 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 270,000,000 to COP 350,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,852,339/m² and the median listed size is 76 m². That is 33% above the study's reference median (COP 224,750,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 139,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 35.2% of listings include parking.
