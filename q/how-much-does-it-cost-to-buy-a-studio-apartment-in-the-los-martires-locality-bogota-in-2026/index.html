How much does it cost to buy a studio apartment in the Los Mártires locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for studio apartments for sale in the Los Mártires locality, Bogotá is COP 240,000,000, based on 74 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 211,250,000 to COP 309,060,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,877,252/m² and the median listed size is 32 m². That is 31% below the study's reference median (COP 350,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 150,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 14.9% of listings include parking.
