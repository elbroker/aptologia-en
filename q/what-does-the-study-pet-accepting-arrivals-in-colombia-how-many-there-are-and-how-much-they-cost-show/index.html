What does the study “Pet-accepting arrivals in Colombia: how many there are and how much they cost” show?

An apartment for rent that accepts pets is requested in Colombia 3.1% more per square meter than one comparable without that feature (same city and number of rooms). 10.6% of apartments for rent declare it. In Bogotá the difference is 4.2% (12.2% of the offer).
