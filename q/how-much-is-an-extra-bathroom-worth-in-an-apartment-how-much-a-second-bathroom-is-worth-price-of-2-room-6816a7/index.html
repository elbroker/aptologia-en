How much is an extra bathroom worth in an apartment?

In Colombia, an apartment of 2 rooms with 2 bathrooms is requested 37.4% more expensive than one with 1.

A 2 bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms is requested in Colombia 37.4% more expensive than one with 1 bathroom of the same estrato and city; the biggest difference is in Santa Marta (114.3%). In Bogotá it is 42.9%. The minor in Sabaneta (4.4%). Without matching by estrato the difference would be 91.8%.
