What does the study “Houses for rent in Asunción by number of bathrooms: price and m² of each group” show?

In Asunción, a house for rent from the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 5,000,000 Guaranis (USD 876) per month in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 11,750,000 Guaranis (USD 2,059) per month (+ 135.0%). per square metre, the cheapest is "3 bathrooms" (33,333.3 Guarani (USD 5.8) / m² per month) and the most expensive "1 bathroom" (44,444.4 Guarani (USD 7.8) / m² per month).
