How much does a house in Cúcuta and its metropolitan area cost?

The median is $450 million.

A house for sale in Cúcuta and its metropolitan area requests in the median $360 million ($3,066,667 per m²); an apartment, $270 million, and a house, $450 million. Buy an apartment to rent yields in rough about 7.3% per year.
