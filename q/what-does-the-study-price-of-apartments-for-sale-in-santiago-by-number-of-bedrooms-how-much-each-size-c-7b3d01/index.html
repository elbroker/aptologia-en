What does the study “Price of apartments for sale in Santiago by number of bedrooms: how much each size costs” show?

In Santiago, a 1 bedroom department costs 3,234 UF (USD 135,553) in the medium, one 2 5,400 UF (USD 226,340) (+ 67.0%) and one 3 or more 10,490 UF (USD 439,686) (+ 94.3%).
