How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more 0–5 years old in estrato 4 in La Soledad, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more 0–5 years old in estrato 4 for sale in La Soledad, Bogotá is COP 1,481,170,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,183,750,000 to COP 1,862,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,142,530/m² and the median listed size is 350 m². That is 23% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
