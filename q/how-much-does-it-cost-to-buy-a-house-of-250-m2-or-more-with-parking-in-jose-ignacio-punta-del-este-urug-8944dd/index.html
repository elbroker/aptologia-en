How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more with parking in José Ignacio, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more with parking for sale in José Ignacio, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 1,500,000, based on 97 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 970,000 to USD 2,190,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,828/m² and the median listed size is 360 m². That is 114% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
