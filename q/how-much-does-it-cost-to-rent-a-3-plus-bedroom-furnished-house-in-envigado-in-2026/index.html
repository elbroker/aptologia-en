How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom furnished house in Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom furnished houses in Envigado is COP 18,000,000 per month, based on 187 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 13,000,000 to COP 23,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 52,000/m² and the median listed size is 327 m². That is 53% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 11,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 750,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
