How much does an apartment rent in Porto?

The gross return is 3.9% per year (rent per m² × 12), before expenditure.

The freguesia with the most expensive square meter of Porto is Aldoar, Foz do Douro and Nevogilde, with 7,240 euros (USD 8,138) in the median of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Ramalde, with 4,499 euros (USD 5,057). The gross implicit return on rent is 3.9% per year.
