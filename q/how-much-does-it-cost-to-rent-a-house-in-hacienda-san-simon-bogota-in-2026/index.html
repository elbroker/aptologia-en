How much does it cost to rent a house in Hacienda San Simon, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Hacienda San Simon, Bogotá is COP 13,500,000 per month, based on 76 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 12,447,750 to COP 15,125,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 31,250/m² and the median listed size is 450 m². That is 29% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 10,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,044,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
