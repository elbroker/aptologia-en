New buildings pay for more management in Sabaneta?

For starters: 4,760 pesos / m²; 1 to 5 years: 4,972 pesos / m²; 6 to 10 years: 4,848 pesos / m²; 11 to 20 years: 5,000 pesos / m²; 21 to 30 years: 4,088 pesos / m² (median per m² per month).

In Sabaneta, the highest square meter HOA fee is paid in the group buildings "11 to 20 years" (5,000 pesos per m² per month in the median) and the lowest in "21 to 30 years" (4,088 pesos). Average monthly rate per strip: For starters: 380,000 pesos; 1 to 5 years: 380,000 pesos; 6 to 10 years: 379,000 pesos; 11 to 20 years: 450,000 pesos; 21 to 30 years: 270,000 pesos.
