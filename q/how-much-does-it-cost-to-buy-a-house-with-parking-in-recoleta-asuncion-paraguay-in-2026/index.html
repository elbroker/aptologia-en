How much does it cost to buy a house with parking in Recoleta, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with parking for sale in Recoleta, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 450,000, based on 118 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 330,000 to USD 665,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,061/m² and the median listed size is 390 m². That is 29% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 350,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
