How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in Ampliación Estado de Hidalgo, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in Ampliación Estado de Hidalgo, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 3,115,730, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 2,800,009 to MXN 4,051,238. The median price per square metre is MXN 62,440/m² and the median listed size is 54.5 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 3,542,328). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
