How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² in Bella Vista, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Bella Vista, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,370 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,200 to USD 1,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 15.6/m² and the median listed size is 90 m². That is 1% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
