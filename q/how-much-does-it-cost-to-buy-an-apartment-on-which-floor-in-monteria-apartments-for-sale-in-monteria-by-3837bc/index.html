How much does it cost to buy an apartment on which floor in Montería?

Floor 1 - 2: 390 million pesos; Floor 3 - 5: 400 million pesos; Floor 6 - 10: 420 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Montería, an apartment for sale of the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 390 million pesos in the median and one of the group "Piso 6 - 10" in 420 million pesos (+ 7.8%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (3.41 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 6 - 10" (3.85 million pesos / m²).
