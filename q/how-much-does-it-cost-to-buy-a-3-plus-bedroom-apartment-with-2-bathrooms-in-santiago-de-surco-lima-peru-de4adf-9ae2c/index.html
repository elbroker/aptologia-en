How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is USD 250,000, based on 187 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 198,364 to USD 320,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,927/m² and the median listed size is 121 m². That is 3% below the study's reference median for Lima (USD 258,023). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 392. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
