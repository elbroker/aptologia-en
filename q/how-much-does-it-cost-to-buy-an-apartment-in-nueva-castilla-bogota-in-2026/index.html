How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Nueva Castilla, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Nueva Castilla, Bogotá is COP 320,000,000, based on 53 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 230,000,000 to COP 390,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,978,723/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is 58% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 220,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 60.4% of listings include parking.
