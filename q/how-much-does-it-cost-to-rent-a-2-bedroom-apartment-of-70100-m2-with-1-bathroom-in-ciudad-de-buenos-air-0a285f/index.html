How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is ARS 1,200,000 per month, based on 122 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from ARS 999,250 to ARS 1,400,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is ARS 15,714/m² and the median listed size is 74 m². That is 41% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (ARS 850,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
