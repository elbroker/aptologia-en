Where is every extra meter worth more?

Where the elasticity is greater, like Buenos Aires City (Argentina).

An apartment for sale 10% larger is requested + 6.2% more expensive in Guayaquil (Ecuador), the weakest effect of 20 cities, and + 10.7% in Buenos Aires (Argentina), the strongest. In the medium of the cities, 10% more area increases the price + 8.6% for sale and + 7.3% for rent.
