How much does it cost to rent a home in El Tintal, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in El Tintal, Bogotá is COP 1,365,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,250,000 to COP 1,537,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 30,000/m² and the median listed size is 45 m². That is 61% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,497,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 157,900. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 23.3% of listings include parking.
