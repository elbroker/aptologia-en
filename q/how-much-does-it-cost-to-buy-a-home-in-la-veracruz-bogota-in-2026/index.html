How much does it cost to buy a home in La Veracruz, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in La Veracruz, Bogotá is COP 305,000,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 242,500,000 to COP 540,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,731,997/m² and the median listed size is 28 m². That is 65% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 181,500. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 14.7% of listings include parking.
