How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Manantiales, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Manantiales, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 1,550,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,192,500 to USD 1,750,000. The median price per square metre is USD 5,010/m² and the median listed size is 267 m². That is 331% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
