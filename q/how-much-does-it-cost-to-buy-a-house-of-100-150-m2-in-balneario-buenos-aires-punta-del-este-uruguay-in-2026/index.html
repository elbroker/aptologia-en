How much does it cost to buy a house of 100–150 m² in Balneario Buenos Aires, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 100–150 m² for sale in Balneario Buenos Aires, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 265,000, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 190,000 to USD 320,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,071/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 62% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 700,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
