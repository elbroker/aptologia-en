How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in Independencia, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in Independencia, Santiago, Chile is CLP 287,683 per month, based on 153 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from CLP 256,000 to CLP 323,600. The median monthly rent per square metre is CLP 9,541/m² and the median listed size is 31 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median for Santiago (CLP 370,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
