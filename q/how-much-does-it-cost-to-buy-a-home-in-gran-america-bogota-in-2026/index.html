How much does it cost to buy a home in Gran America, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Gran America, Bogotá is COP 550,000,000, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 290,000,000 to COP 897,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,620,956/m² and the median listed size is 69.5 m². That is 36% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 324,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 52.9% of listings include parking.
