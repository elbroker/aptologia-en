How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 250 m² or more on floors 4–8 in estrato 6 in Rósales, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 250 m² or more on floors 4–8 in estrato 6 in Rósales, Bogotá is COP 16,500,000 per month, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 13,700,000 to COP 18,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,000/m² and the median listed size is 300 m². That is 400% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,700,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
