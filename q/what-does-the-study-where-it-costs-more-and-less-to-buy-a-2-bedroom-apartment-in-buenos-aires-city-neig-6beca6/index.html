What does the study “Where it costs more and less to buy a 2 bedroom apartment in Buenos Aires City: neighborhood ranking” show?

In Buenos Aires City, a 2 bedroom apartment costs USD 639,900 in the middle in Puerto Madero, the most expensive neighborhood, and USD 62,500 in Villa Lugano, the cheapest: 10.2 times less. We compare 39 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 bedrooms.
