How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in Bolívar, Bucaramanga in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Bolívar, Bucaramanga is COP 350,000,000, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 26% below the study's reference median for Bucaramanga (COP 470,000,000).
