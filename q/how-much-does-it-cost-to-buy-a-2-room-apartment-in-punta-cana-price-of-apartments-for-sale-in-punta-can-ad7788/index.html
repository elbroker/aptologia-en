How much does it cost to buy a 2 room apartment in Punta Cana?

USD 253,500 in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between USD 169,000 and USD 356,250.

In Punta Cana, a 1 room apartment costs USD 173,000 in the median, one 2 room USD 253,500 (+ 46.5%) and one 3 or more USD 389,000 (+ 53.5%).
