How much does it cost to rent an apartment with how many bathrooms in Guayaquil?

1 bathroom: USD 380 per month; 2 bathrooms: USD 810 per month; 3 bathrooms: USD 1,100 per month; 4 or more bathrooms: USD 1,400 per month (mid-October of 2026).

In Guayaquil, a rental department of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in USD 380 per month in the median and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in USD 1,400 per month (+ 268.4%). per square metre, the cheapest is "1 bathroom" (USD 7.1 / m² per month) and the most expensive "2 bath" (USD 8.5 / m² per month).
