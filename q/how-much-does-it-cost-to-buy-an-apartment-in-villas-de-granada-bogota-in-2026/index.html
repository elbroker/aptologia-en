How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Villas de Granada, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Villas de Granada, Bogotá is COP 307,500,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 260,000,000 to COP 342,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,692,308/m² and the median listed size is 65 m². That is 60% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 248,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 65.6% of listings include parking.
