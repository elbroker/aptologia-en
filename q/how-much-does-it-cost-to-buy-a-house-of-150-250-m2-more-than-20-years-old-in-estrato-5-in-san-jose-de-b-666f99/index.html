How much does it cost to buy a house of 150–250 m² more than 20 years old in estrato 5 in San José de Bavaria, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 150–250 m² more than 20 years old in estrato 5 for sale in San José de Bavaria, Bogotá is COP 1,599,000,000, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,267,500,000 to COP 1,800,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,777,778/m² and the median listed size is 202 m². That is 33% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 820,000. 97.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
