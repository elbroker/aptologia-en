How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with 3+ bathrooms in Caballito, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Caballito, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 438,000, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 370,000 to USD 650,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,660/m² and the median listed size is 275 m². That is 51% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 290,000). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
