How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in La Castellana, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in La Castellana, Medellín is COP 894,500,000, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 675,000,000 to COP 980,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,200,000/m² and the median listed size is 118 m². That is 6% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 445,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 95.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
