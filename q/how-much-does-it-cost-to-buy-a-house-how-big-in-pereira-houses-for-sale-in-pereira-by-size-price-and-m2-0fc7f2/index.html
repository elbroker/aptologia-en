How much does it cost to buy a house how big in Pereira?

50 - 70 m²: 300 million pesos; 70 - 90 m²: 380 million pesos; 90 - 120 m²: 460 million pesos; 120 - 160 m²: 580 million pesos; More than 160 m²: 1,400 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Pereira, a house for sale from the group "50 - 70 m²" is requested in 300 million pesos in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 1,400 million pesos (+ 366.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (4.00 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "50 - 70 m²" (5.04 million pesos / m²).
