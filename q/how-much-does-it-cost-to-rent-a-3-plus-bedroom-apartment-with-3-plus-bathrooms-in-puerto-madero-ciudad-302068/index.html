How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Puerto Madero, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Puerto Madero, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 6,500 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 4,612 to USD 7,950. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 28.3/m² and the median listed size is 190 m². That is 300% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 1,625). The median number of bathrooms is 3.5.
