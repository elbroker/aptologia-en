How much does it cost to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in Lisbon?

1,650 EUR (USD 1,855) per month in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between EUR 1,400 (USD 1,574) and EUR 2,100 (USD 2,361).

In Lisbon, rent a 1 bedroom apartment costs in the median 1,450 euro (USD 1,630) per month and one 2 bedroom, 1,650 euro (USD 1,855) (+ 13.8%). One of 3 or more bedrooms is requested in 2,300 euros (USD 2,585).
