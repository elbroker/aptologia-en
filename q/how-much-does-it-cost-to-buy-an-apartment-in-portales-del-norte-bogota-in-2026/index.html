How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Portales del Norte, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Portales del Norte, Bogotá is COP 470,000,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 380,000,000 to COP 615,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,758,242/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 39% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 455,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 87.8% of listings include parking.
