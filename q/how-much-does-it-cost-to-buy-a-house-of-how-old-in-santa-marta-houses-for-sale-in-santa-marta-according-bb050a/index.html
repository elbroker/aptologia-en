How much does it cost to buy a house of how old in Santa Marta?

For starring (0 years): 757 million pesos; 1 to 5 years: 670 million pesos; 6 to 10 years: 740 million pesos; 11 to 20 years: 620 million pesos; 21 to 30 years: 665 million pesos (medium of October of 2026).

In Santa Marta, a house for sale from the group "To premiere (0 years)" is requested in 757 million pesos in the median and one from the group "21 to 30 years" in 665 million pesos (- 12.2%). For each square meter, the cheapest is "21 to 30 years" (3.08 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "To premiere (0 years)" (3.67 million pesos / m²).
