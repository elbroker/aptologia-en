How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Colina Campestre, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Colina Campestre, Bogotá is COP 2,900,000 per month, based on 189 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,000,000 to COP 4,100,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 43,622/m² and the median listed size is 72 m². That is 17% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 400,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 73.5% of listings include parking.
