How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² with parking in Providencia, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with parking for sale in Providencia, Santiago, Chile is UF 9,750, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 8,395 to UF 10,500. The median price per square metre is UF 79/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 40% above the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 6,950). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
