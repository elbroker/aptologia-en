In Santa Marta, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to rent apartment is Bello Horizonte (54,861 pesos / m² per month) and the cheapest Rodadero (36,364 pesos / m² per month), 1.5 times less. We compare 6 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
