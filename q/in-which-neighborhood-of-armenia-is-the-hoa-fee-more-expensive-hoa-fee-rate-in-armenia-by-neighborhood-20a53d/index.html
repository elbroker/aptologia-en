In which neighborhood of Armenia is the HOA fee more expensive?

In La Castellana: 5,476 pesos per m² per month.

In Armenia, the most expensive HOA fee per square metre is paid in La Castellana: 5,476 pesos per m² per month in the median (525,000 fee pesos). The cheapest, in Puerto Espejo, with 3,456 pesos per m². The median rate for the whole city is 320,500 pesos per month (4,296 pesos per m²).
