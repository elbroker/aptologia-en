How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 7,200,000 per month, based on 409 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 5,500,000 to PYG 9,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is PYG 54,545/m² and the median listed size is 136 m². That is 38% above the study's reference median for Asunción (PYG 5,200,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
