What homes are for sale for COP 250–400 million in the Los Mártires locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for COP 250–400 million in the Los Mártires locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 305,000,000, based on 173 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 270,000,000 to COP 349,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,325,581/m² and the median listed size is 41 m². That is 13% below the study's reference median (COP 350,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 210,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 22.5% of listings include parking.
