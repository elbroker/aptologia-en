What homes are for sale for less than COP 250 million in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá in 2026?

Homes listed for sale for less than COP 250 million in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 219,000,000, based on 272 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 196,750,000 to COP 235,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,086,932/m² and the median listed size is 43 m². That is 59% below the study's reference median (COP 530,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 147,130. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 11.8% of listings include parking.
