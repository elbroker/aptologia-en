How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house with 2 bathrooms in José Ignacio, Punta del Este, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses with 2 bathrooms in José Ignacio, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 12,450 per month, based on 51 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 6,500 to USD 18,250. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 54.9/m² and the median listed size is 162 m². That is 71% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 7,300). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
