What does the study “How many square meters are purchased with 1,000 million pesos? 38 Colombian cities” show?

With 1,000 million pesos, some 295 m² of apartment is bought in Soacha, the most surrendering city of 38, and 107 m² in El Retiro. In Bogotá they reach for 138 m² and in Medellín for 133 m².
