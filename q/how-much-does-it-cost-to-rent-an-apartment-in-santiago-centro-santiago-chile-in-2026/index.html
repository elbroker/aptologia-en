How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile is CLP 403,207 per month, based on 1,476 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from CLP 320,000 to CLP 500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is CLP 9,091/m² and the median listed size is 45 m². That is 10% below the study's reference median for Santiago (CLP 450,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
