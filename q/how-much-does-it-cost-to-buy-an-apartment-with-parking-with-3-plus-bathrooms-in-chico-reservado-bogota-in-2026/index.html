How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Chicó Reservado, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Chicó Reservado, Bogotá is COP 1,950,000,000, based on 331 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,450,000,000 to COP 2,600,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,699,231/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 160% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,883,450. The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
