How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms on floors 1–3 in Parque Natura, Jamundí in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms on floors 1–3 for sale in Parque Natura, Jamundí is COP 214,000,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 194,500,000 to COP 276,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,851,393/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Jamundí (COP 210,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 231,500. The most frequent estrato is 4, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
