How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms in estrato 4 in Laureles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms in estrato 4 for sale in Laureles, Medellín is COP 602,500,000, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 503,000,000 to COP 750,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,307,883/m² and the median listed size is 82 m². That is 28% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 400,000. 95.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
