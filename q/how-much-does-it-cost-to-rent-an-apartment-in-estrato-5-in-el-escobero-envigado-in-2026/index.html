How much does it cost to rent an apartment in estrato 5 in El Escobero, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in estrato 5 in El Escobero, Envigado is COP 4,500,000 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,900,000 to COP 5,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,403/m² and the median listed size is 87 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 4,400,000). 75.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
