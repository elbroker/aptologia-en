Where is it cheaper to buy a 3 apartment or more rooms in Pereira?

In Condina: 280 million pesos in the median.

In Pereira, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs 1,100 million pesos in the median in Pinares Alto, the most expensive neighborhood, and 280 million pesos in Condina, the cheapest: 3.9 times less. We compare 18 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
