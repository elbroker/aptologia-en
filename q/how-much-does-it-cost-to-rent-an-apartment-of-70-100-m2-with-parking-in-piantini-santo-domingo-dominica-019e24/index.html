How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 70–100 m² with parking in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² with parking in Piantini, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,375 per month, based on 81 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,250 to USD 1,599. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 16.7/m² and the median listed size is 81 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
