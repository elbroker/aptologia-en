How much does it cost to buy a home of 45–70 m² in Chía in 2026?

The median asking price for homes of 45–70 m² for sale in Chía is COP 260,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 241,000,000 to COP 289,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,910,588/m² and the median listed size is 50.5 m². That is 33% below the study's reference median (COP 390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 190,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 30% of listings include parking.
