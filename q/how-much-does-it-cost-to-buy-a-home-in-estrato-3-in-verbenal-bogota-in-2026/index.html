How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 3 in Verbenal, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 3 for sale in Verbenal, Bogotá is COP 311,000,000, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 265,000,000 to COP 379,267,875. The median price per square metre is COP 5,370,411/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 9% below the study's reference median (COP 343,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 234,000. 46.2% of listings include parking.
