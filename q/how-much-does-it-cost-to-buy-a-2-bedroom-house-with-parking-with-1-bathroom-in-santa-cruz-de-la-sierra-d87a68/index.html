How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom house with parking with 1 bathroom in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom houses with parking with 1 bathroom for sale in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 74,450, based on 62 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 52,750 to USD 110,000. The median price per square metre is USD 664/m² and the median listed size is 106 m². That is 53% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 160,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
