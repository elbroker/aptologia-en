How much does it cost to buy a home in Los Laureles, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Los Laureles, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 227,500, based on 242 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 115,000 to USD 370,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,491/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
