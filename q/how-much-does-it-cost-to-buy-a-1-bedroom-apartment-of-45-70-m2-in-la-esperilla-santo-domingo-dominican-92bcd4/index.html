How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in La Esperilla, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in La Esperilla, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 169,600, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 161,000 to USD 178,712. The median price per square metre is USD 3,004/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 29% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
