How much does it cost to rent a home in Alto de Pinheiros, São Paulo, Brazil in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Alto de Pinheiros, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 11,950 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 7,554 to BRL 16,750. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 54.1/m² and the median listed size is 184 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 2,125. 94.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
