How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Suba?

The median is $2,450,000 per month.

The average rental of an apartment in Bogotá goes from $1,000,000 in Usme to $4,495,000 in Chapinero: 4.5 times more. A 2 rooms cost at the median $1,443,500 in Kennedy, $1,850,000 in Suba and $4,610,000 in Chapinero.
