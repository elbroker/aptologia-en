How much does it cost to rent a home in a gated complex in the Los Mártires locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in a gated complex in the Los Mártires locality, Bogotá is COP 1,600,000 per month, based on 53 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,400,000 to COP 2,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,000/m² and the median listed size is 34 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 1,600,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 161,300. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 43.4% of listings include parking.
