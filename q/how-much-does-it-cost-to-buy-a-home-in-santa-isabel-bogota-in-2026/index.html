How much does it cost to buy a home in Santa Isabel, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Santa Isabel, Bogotá is COP 830,000,000, based on 88 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 527,500,000 to COP 900,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,700,985/m² and the median listed size is 222 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 100,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 72.7% of listings include parking.
