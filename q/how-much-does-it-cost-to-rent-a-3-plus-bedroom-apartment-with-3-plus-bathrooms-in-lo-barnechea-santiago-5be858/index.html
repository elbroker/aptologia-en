How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Lo Barnechea, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Lo Barnechea, Santiago, Chile is UF 80 per month, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 58.2 to UF 93.8. The median monthly rent per square metre is UF 0.36/m² and the median listed size is 209 m². That is 478% above the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 13.8). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
