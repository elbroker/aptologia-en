In which quarter of Cartagena is the HOA fee more expensive?

In Bahia Kristal: 10,354 pesos per m² per month.

In Cartagena, the most expensive HOA fee per square metre is paid in Bahia Kristal: 10,354 pesos per m² per month in the median (720,000 fee pesos). The cheapest, in Ternera, with 3,299 pesos per m². The median rate for the whole city is 740,000 pesos per month (7,303 pesos per m²).
