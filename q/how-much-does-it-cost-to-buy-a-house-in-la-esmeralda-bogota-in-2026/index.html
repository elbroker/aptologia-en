How much does it cost to buy a house in La Esmeralda, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in La Esmeralda, Bogotá is COP 1,300,000,000, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,193,250,000 to COP 1,499,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,185,985/m² and the median listed size is 250 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 130,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 94% of listings include parking.
