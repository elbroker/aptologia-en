How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms 6–20 years old in Ciudad Jardín, Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms 6–20 years old for sale in Ciudad Jardín, Cali is COP 810,000,000, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 703,750,000 to COP 990,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,000,000/m² and the median listed size is 140 m². That is 88% above the study's reference median for Cali (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 850,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
