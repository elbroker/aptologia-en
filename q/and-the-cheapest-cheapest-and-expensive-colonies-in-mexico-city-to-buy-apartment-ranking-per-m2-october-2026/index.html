And the cheapest?

Villa Coyoacán, with 43,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,329) per m².

In Mexico City, the colony with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is Florida (75,923 Mexican pesos (USD 4,112) / m²) and the cheapest Villa Coyoacán (43,000 Mexican pesos (USD 2,329) / m²), 1.8 times less. We compare 50 colonies with at least 30 ads with area.
