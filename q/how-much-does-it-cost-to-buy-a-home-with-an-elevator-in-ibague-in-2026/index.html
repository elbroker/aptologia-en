How much does it cost to buy a home with an elevator in Ibagué in 2026?

The median asking price for homes with an elevator for sale in Ibagué is COP 265,000,000, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 198,750,000 to COP 422,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,001,991/m² and the median listed size is 66 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median (COP 280,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 308,400. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 78.6% of listings include parking.
