How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with parking in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking for sale in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 469,000, based on 778 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 325,000 to USD 704,252. The median price per square metre is USD 2,063/m² and the median listed size is 240 m². That is 95% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
