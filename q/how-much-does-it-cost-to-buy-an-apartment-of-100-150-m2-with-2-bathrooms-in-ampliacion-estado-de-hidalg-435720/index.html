How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Ampliación Estado de Hidalgo, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Ampliación Estado de Hidalgo, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 6,632,084, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 6,320,334 to MXN 6,865,654. The median price per square metre is MXN 59,672/m² and the median listed size is 111 m². That is 33% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 4,990,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
