How much does it cost to buy an apartment how old in Chia?

For starring (0 years): 310 million pesos; 1 to 5 years: 325 million pesos; 6 to 10 years: 420 million pesos; 11 to 20 years: 395 million pesos (medium of October of 2026).

In Chia, an apartment for sale from the group "To premiere (0 years)" is requested in 310 million pesos in the median and one from the group "11 to 20 years" in 395 million pesos (+ 27.4%). For each square meter, the cheapest is "6 to 10 years" (5.40 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "To premiere (0 years)" (5.79 million pesos / m²).
