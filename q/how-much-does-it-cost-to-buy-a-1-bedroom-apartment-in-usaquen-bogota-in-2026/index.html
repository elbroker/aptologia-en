How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in Usaquén, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in Usaquén, Bogotá is COP 280,000,000, based on 79 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 24% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 369,000,000).
