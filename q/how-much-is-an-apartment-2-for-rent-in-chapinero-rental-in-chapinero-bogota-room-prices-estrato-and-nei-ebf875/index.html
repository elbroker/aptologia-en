How much is an apartment 2 for rent in Chapinero?

The median is $4,620,000 per month (245 ads).

The average rental of an apartment or studio apartment in Chapinero (Bogotá) is $3,500,000 per month, about $63,326 per m². A 2 rooms costs in the median $4,620,000. The cheapest neighborhood with 5 ads or more is Galerias ($1,425,000) and the most expensive Las Acacias ($12,207,500).
