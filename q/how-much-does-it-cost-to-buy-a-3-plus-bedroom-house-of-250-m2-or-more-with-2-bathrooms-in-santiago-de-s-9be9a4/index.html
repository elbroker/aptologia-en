How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms for sale in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is PEN 1,970,600, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 1,774,000 to PEN 2,250,675. The median price per square metre is PEN 6,556/m² and the median listed size is 304 m². That is 9% below the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 2,173,590). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
