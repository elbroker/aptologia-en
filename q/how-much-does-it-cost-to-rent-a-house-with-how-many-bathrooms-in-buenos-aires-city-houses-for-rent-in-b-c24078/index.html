How much does it cost to rent a house with how many bathrooms in Buenos Aires City?

2 bathrooms: USD 2,800 per month; 3 bathrooms: USD 3,950 per month; 4 or more bathrooms: USD 6,000 per month (mid-October of 2026).

In Buenos Aires City, a house for rent from the group "2 bathrooms" is requested in USD 2,800 per month in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in USD 6,000 per month (+ 114.3%). per square metre, the cheapest is "4 or more bathrooms" (USD 13.3 / m² per month) and the most expensive "2 bathrooms" (USD 14.3 / m² per month).
