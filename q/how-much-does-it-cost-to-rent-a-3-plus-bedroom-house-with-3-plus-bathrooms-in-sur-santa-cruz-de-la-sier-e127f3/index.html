How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house with 3+ bathrooms in Sur, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms in Sur, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 830 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 658 to USD 1,208. That is 29% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 1,166). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
