How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Jara, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Jara, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 1,450 per month, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,000 to USD 2,200. That is 7% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 1,350). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
