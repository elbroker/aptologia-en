How much does it cost to rent a house in Ciudad Jardín, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Ciudad Jardín, Barranquilla is COP 7,000,000 per month, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,425,000 to COP 9,000,000. That is 12% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 8,000,000).
