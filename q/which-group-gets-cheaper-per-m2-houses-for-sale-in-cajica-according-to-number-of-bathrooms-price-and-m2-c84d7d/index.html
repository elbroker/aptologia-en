Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (5.81 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "3 bathrooms" (6.54 million pesos / m²).

In Cajicá, a house for sale of the group "2 bathrooms" is requested in 1,076 million pesos in the medium and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 1,400 million pesos (+ 30.1%). For square meter, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (5.81 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "3 bathrooms" (6.54 million pesos / m²).
