How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 3 in Puerto Espejo, Armenia in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 3 for sale in Puerto Espejo, Armenia is COP 245,000,000, based on 130 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 211,250,000 to COP 295,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,062,500/m² and the median listed size is 61.7 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 204,500. 80.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
