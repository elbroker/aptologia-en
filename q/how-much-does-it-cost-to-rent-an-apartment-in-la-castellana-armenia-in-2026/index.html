How much does it cost to rent an apartment in La Castellana, Armenia in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in La Castellana, Armenia is COP 2,556,514 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,090,485 to COP 3,950,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 34,762/m² and the median listed size is 80.7 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Armenia (COP 2,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 569,122. The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
