How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 100–150 m² with parking in Calasanz, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² with parking for sale in Calasanz, Medellín is COP 685,000,000, based on 52 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 600,000,000 to COP 850,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,040,866/m² and the median listed size is 113 m². That is 18% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 400,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
