How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom in Pocitos, Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom for sale in Pocitos, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 256,850, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 240,875 to USD 295,725. The median price per square metre is USD 3,280/m² and the median listed size is 82.5 m². That is 39% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 185,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
