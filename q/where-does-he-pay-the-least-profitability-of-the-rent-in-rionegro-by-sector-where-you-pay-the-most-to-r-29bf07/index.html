Where does he pay the least?

In Water Eye: 5.5% gross per year (18.1 years of rent).

In Rionegro, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical sector 6.0% gross per year: the price ordered is equivalent to 16.6 years of rent. The sector with the highest gross return is Llanogrande (7.6%) and that of minor, Water Eye (5.5%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 6.0%.
