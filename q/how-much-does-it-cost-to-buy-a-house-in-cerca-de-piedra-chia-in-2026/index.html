How much does it cost to buy a house in Cerca de Piedra, Chía in 2026?

The median asking price for houses for sale in Cerca de Piedra, Chía is COP 1,150,000,000, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 780,000,000 to COP 1,600,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,500,000/m² and the median listed size is 296 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Chía (COP 1,100,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 870,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 97.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
