How much do you pay for HOA fee in Rionegro?

The median rate is 300,000 pesos per month (4,706 pesos per m²), according to October 2026 announcements.

In Rionegro, the most expensive HOA fee per square metre is paid in Saxony: 7,583 pesos per m² per month in the median (845,000 fee pesos). The cheapest, in Central, with 4,154 pesos per m². The median rate for the whole city is 300,000 pesos per month (4,706 pesos per m²).
