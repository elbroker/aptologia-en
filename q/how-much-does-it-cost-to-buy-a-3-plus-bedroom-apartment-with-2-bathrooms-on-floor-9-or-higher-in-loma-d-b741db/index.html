How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms on floor 9 or higher in Loma del Indio, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms on floor 9 or higher for sale in Loma del Indio, Medellín is COP 665,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 570,000,000 to COP 690,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,770,270/m² and the median listed size is 78 m². That is 21% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 460,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
