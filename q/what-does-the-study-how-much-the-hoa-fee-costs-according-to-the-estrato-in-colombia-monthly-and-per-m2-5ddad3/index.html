What does the study “How much the HOA fee costs according to the estrato in Colombia: monthly and per m² rate” show?

The rate of HOA fee of an apartment in Colombia is in the median 1,040,000 pesos per month in estrato 6 and 210,000 in estrato 3, 5.0 times more. per square meter goes from 2,953 pesos to 7,231 pesos per month according to the estrato.
