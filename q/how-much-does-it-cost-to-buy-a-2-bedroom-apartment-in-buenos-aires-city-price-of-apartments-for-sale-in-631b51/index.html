How much does it cost to buy a 2 bedroom apartment in Buenos Aires City?

USD 160,000 in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between USD 108,000 and USD 233,390.

In Buenos Aires City, a 1 bedroom department costs USD 115,000 in the median, one 2 in the middle of the city 160,000 (+ 39.1%) and one 3 or more USD 285,000 (+ 78.1%).
