How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Parque del Virrey, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Parque del Virrey, Bogotá is COP 1,075,000,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 767,500,000 to COP 1,800,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 11,063,830/m² and the median listed size is 115 m². That is 41% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,090,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 93.2% of listings include parking.
