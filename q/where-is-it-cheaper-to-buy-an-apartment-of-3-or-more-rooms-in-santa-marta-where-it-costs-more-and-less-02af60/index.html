Where is it cheaper to buy an apartment of 3 or more rooms in Santa Marta?

In Bavaria: 550 million pesos in the median.

In Santa Marta, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs 1,442 million pesos in the medium in Pozos Colorados, the most expensive neighborhood, and 550 million pesos in Bavaria, the cheapest: 2.6 times less. We compare 8 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
