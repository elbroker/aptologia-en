What does the study “Rental in Fontibón, Bogotá: room prices, estrato and neighborhood” show?

The average rental of an apartment or studio apartment in Fontibón (Bogotá) is $1,780,000 per month, about $43,662 per m². A 2 rooms costs in the median $1,600,000. The cheapest neighborhood with 5 ads or more is Morabia ($1,200,000) and the most expensive Western City Salt ($4,100,000).
