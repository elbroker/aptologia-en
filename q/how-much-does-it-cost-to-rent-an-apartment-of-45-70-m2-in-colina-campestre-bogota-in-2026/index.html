How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 45–70 m² in Colina Campestre, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in Colina Campestre, Bogotá is COP 2,495,000 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,975,000 to COP 2,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 42,061/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 84.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
