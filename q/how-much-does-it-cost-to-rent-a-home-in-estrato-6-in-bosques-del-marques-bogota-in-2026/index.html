How much does it cost to rent a home in estrato 6 in Bosques del Marqués, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 6 in Bosques del Marqués, Bogotá is COP 16,000,000 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 13,500,000 to COP 18,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 51,354/m² and the median listed size is 317 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,500,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
