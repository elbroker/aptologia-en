How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² on floors 1–3 in Colina Campestre, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² on floors 1–3 for sale in Colina Campestre, Bogotá is COP 882,500,000, based on 52 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 790,000,000 to COP 1,000,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,872,485/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 705,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
