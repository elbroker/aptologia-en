How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking in Arequipa, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking in Arequipa, Peru is PEN 2,948 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 2,525 to PEN 3,575. The median monthly rent per square metre is PEN 20.7/m² and the median listed size is 138 m². The median number of bathrooms is 3.
