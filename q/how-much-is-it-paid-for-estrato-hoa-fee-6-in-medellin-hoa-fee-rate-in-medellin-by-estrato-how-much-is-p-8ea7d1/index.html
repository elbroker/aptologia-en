How much is it paid for estrato HOA fee 6 in Medellín?

919,000 pesos per month in the median (6,236 pesos per m²), October 2026.

In Medellín, the median rate of HOA fee of an apartment goes from 172,000 pesos per month in estrato 2 to 919,000 pesos in estrato 6, 5.3 times more. per square meter, it goes from 3,340 pesos to 6,236 pesos per month.
