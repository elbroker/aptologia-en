How much does it cost to rent a home in La Molina, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in La Molina, Lima, Peru is USD 3,800 per month, based on 51 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,525 to USD 5,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 7.75/m² and the median listed size is 455 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
