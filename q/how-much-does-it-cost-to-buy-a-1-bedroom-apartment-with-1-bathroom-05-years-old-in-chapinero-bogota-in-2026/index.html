How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment with 1 bathroom 0–5 years old in Chapinero, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom 0–5 years old for sale in Chapinero, Bogotá is COP 310,000,000, based on 74 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 265,000,000 to COP 350,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,000,000/m² and the median listed size is 33.2 m². That is 59% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 305,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 47.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
