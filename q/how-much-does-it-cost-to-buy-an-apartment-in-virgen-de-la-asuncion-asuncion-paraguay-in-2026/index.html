How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Virgen de la Asunción, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Virgen de la Asunción, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 232,637, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 192,758 to USD 354,500. The median price per square metre is USD 2,059/m² and the median listed size is 113 m². That is 66% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 140,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
