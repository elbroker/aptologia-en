How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment in Alto Prado, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Alto Prado, Barranquilla is COP 4,850,000 per month, based on 104 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,344,250 to COP 6,925,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 26,053/m² and the median listed size is 174 m². That is 42% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 3,426,700). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 957,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 99% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
