How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,700 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,425 to USD 2,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 9.75/m² and the median listed size is 177 m². That is 26% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
