New buildings pay for more HOA fee in Mosquera?

1 to 5 years: 4,310 pesos / m²; 6 to 10 years: 4,333 pesos / m²; 11 to 20 years: 3,065 pesos / m² (medium per m² per month).

In Mosquera, the highest square meter HOA fee is paid in the group buildings "6 to 10 years" (4,333 pesos per m² per month in the median) and the lowest in "11 to 20 years" (3,065 pesos). Average monthly rate per strip: 1 to 5 years: 280,000 pesos; 6 to 10 years: 300,000 pesos; 11 to 20 years: 181,000 pesos.
