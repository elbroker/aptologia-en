How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 1 bathroom in Lince, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 1 bathroom for sale in Lince, Lima, Peru is PEN 343,400, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 301,309 to PEN 400,297. The median price per square metre is PEN 7,916/m² and the median listed size is 43 m². That is 45% below the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 621,600). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
