How much does it cost to rent an apartment on which floor in Bogotá?

Floor 1 - 2: 3,150,000 pesos per month; Floor 3 - 5: 4,000,000 pesos per month; Floor 6 - 10: 5,500,000 pesos per month; Floor 11 - 20: 2,300,000 pesos per month; Floor 21 or more: 2,475,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Bogotá, an apartment for rent from the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 3,150,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "Piso 21 or more" in 2,475,000 pesos per month (- 21.4%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (45,572 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "Floor 6 - 10" (55,000 pesos / m² per month).
