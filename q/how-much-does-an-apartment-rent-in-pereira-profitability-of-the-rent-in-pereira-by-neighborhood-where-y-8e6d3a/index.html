How much does an apartment rent in Pereira?

In the typical neighborhood, 6.7% gross per year.

In Pereira, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical neighborhood 6.7% gross per year: the price requested is equivalent to 14.8 years of rent. The district with the highest gross return is San José Sur (8.9%) and the smaller district Galicia (6.1%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 7.3%.
