How much does it cost to rent an apartment of what size in Medellín?

Less than 50 m²: 2,025,000 pesos per month; 50 - 70 m²: 3,000,000 pesos per month; 70 - 90 m²: 4,000,000 pesos per month; 90 - 120 m²: 5,200,000 pesos per month; 120 - 160 m²: 6,300,000 pesos per month; More than 160 m²: 9,700,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Medellín, an apartment for rent from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 2,025,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 9,700,000 pesos per month (+ 379.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "More than 160 m²" (44,492 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (51,333 pesos / m² per month).
