And one of 3 or more bedrooms?

55,000 Uruguayan pesos (USD 1,368) per month in the median.

In Montevideo, a 1 bedroom house is rented by 22,000 Uruguayan pesos (USD 547) per month in the median, one 2 Uruguayan 30,000 Uruguayan pesos (USD 746) (+ 36.4%) and one 3 or more 55,000 Uruguayan pesos (USD 1,368) (+ 83.3%).
