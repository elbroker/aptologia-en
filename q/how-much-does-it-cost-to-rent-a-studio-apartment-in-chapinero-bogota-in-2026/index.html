How much does it cost to rent a studio apartment in Chapinero, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for studio apartments in Chapinero, Bogotá is COP 1,800,000 per month, based on 175 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,550,000 to COP 2,300,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 61,667/m² and the median listed size is 30 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,900,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 254,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 25.7% of listings include parking.
