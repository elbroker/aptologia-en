How much does it cost to rent a 2 bedroom apartment in Punta del Este?

USD 3,800 per month in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between USD 2,200 and USD 7,000.

In Punta del Este, a 1 bedroom apartment is rented per USD 2,550 per month in the median, one 2 USD 3,800 bedroom (+ 49.0%) and one 3 or more USD 6,820 (+ 79.5%).
