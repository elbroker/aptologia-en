How much does it cost to buy a 2 room apartment in Manzanillo del Mar?

1,219 million pesos in the medium (October of 2026); the central half between 738 million pesos and 1,355 million pesos.

In Cartagena, an apartment of 2 rooms costs 1,219 million pesos in the medium in Manzanillo del Mar, the most expensive neighborhood, and 320 million pesos in Torices, the cheapest: 3.8 times less. We compare 17 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
