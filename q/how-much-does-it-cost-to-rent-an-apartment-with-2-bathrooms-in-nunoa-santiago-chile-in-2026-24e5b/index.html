How much does it cost to rent an apartment with 2 bathrooms in Ñuñoa, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with 2 bathrooms in Ñuñoa, Santiago, Chile is UF 18.2 per month, based on 220 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 14.9 to UF 21.7. The median monthly rent per square metre is UF 0.29/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 32% above the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 13.8). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
