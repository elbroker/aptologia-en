What does the study “Where it is more expensive and cheaper to rent a apartment of 3 or more bedrooms in Santiago: ranking of communes” show?

In Santiago, a department of 3 or more bedrooms is rented for 71 UF (USD 2,964) per month in the median in Vitacura, the most expensive commune, and 12 UF (USD 509) per month in San Miguel, the cheapest: 5.8 times less. We compare 8 communes with at least 30 ads from 3 or more bedrooms.
