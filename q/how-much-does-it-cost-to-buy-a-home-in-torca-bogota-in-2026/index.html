How much does it cost to buy a home in Torca, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes for sale in Torca, Bogotá is COP 1,925,000,000, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 530,000,000 to COP 3,200,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,847,619/m² and the median listed size is 138 m². That is 123% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 580,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 80% of listings include parking.
