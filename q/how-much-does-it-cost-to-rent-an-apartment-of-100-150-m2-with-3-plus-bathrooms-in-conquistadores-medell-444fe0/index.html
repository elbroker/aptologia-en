How much does it cost to rent an apartment of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Conquistadores, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Conquistadores, Medellín is COP 4,500,000 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,000,000 to COP 4,900,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 38,462/m² and the median listed size is 109 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 80% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
