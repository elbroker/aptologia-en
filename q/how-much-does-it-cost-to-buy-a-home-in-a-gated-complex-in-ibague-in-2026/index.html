How much does it cost to buy a home in a gated complex in Ibagué in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in a gated complex for sale in Ibagué is COP 185,000,000, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 150,000,000 to COP 282,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,282,201/m² and the median listed size is 59.5 m². That is 34% below the study's reference median (COP 280,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 197,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 58.8% of listings include parking.
