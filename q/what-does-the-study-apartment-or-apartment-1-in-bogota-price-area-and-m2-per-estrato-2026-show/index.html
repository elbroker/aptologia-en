What does the study “Apartment or apartment 1 in Bogotá: price, area and m² per estrato (2026)” show?

In Bogotá, a 1 room apartment costs the median $2,400,000 per month and a $1,950,000 studio apartment: $450,000 more. The 1 room has 40 m² in front of 31 m² in the study area, and per m² the study area is $63,462 against $62,500.
