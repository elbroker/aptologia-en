How much does it cost to rent a 1-bedroom apartment in Montevideo, Uruguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 27,900 per month, based on 1,678 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 24,000 to UYU 32,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 625/m² and the median listed size is 45 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 30,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 4,500. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
