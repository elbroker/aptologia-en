How much does it cost to rent a 3 apartment or more rooms in La Calera?

11,500,000 pesos per month in the median (October of 2026); the central half between 7,900,000 pesos and 14,500,000 pesos.

In Medellín, an apartment of 3 or more rooms is rented for 11,500,000 pesos a month in the middle of La Calera, the most expensive neighborhood, and 1,750,000 pesos a month in Pajarito, the cheapest: 6.6 times less. We compare 46 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
