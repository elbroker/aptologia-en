How much does it cost to buy a house with parking 0–5 years old in El Contador, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for houses with parking 0–5 years old for sale in El Contador, Bogotá is COP 1,315,000,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,000,000,000 to COP 1,537,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,074,563/m² and the median listed size is 240 m². That is 10% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 5 and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
