How much does it cost to buy an apartment without parking with 2 bathrooms in Santa Maria Maior, Lisboa, Portugal in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments without parking with 2 bathrooms for sale in Santa Maria Maior, Lisboa, Portugal is EUR 650,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 535,500 to EUR 890,000. The median price per square metre is EUR 6,559/m² and the median listed size is 92 m². That is 2% below the study's reference median for Lisboa (EUR 660,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
