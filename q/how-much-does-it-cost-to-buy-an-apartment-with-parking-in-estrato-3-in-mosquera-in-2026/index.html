How much does it cost to buy an apartment with parking in estrato 3 in Mosquera in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with parking in estrato 3 for sale in Mosquera is COP 343,000,000, based on 108 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 250,000,000 to COP 370,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,843,775/m² and the median listed size is 67 m². That is 7% above the study's reference median for Mosquera (COP 320,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 284,594. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
