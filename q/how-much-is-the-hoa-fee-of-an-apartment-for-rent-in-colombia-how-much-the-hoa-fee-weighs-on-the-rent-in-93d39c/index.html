How much is the HOA fee of an apartment for rent in Colombia?

In the median, 15.8% of the monthly rental.

The rate of HOA fee in the median is 15.8% of the rental of an apartment in Colombia; it weighs more in Cúcuta (22.0%) and less in La Ceja (9.7%). In Bogotá it is 16.1%. By estrato, it ranges from 13.9% in strate 1 to 16.1% in strate 6.
