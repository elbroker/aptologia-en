How much does it cost to buy a home with a gym in Ibagué in 2026?

The median asking price for homes with a gym for sale in Ibagué is COP 295,000,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 212,500,000 to COP 536,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,356,520/m² and the median listed size is 72 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median (COP 280,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 351,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 65.8% of listings include parking.
