How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking with 2 bathrooms in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile is UF 34.5 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 29.8 to UF 42. The median monthly rent per square metre is UF 0.38/m² and the median listed size is 97 m². That is 149% above the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 13.8). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
