What does the study “Where it is more expensive and cheaper to rent a apartment of 3 or more bedrooms in Lima: district ranking” show?

In Lima, a department of 3 or more bedrooms is rented for 6,769 soles (USD 1,970) per month in the middle of Barranco, the most expensive district, and 2,200 soles (USD 640) per month in San Miguel, the cheapest: 3.1 times less. We compare 10 districts with at least 30 ads from 3 or more bedrooms.
