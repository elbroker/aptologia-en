What does the study “House or apartment to rent: which gives more in the cities of Colombia” show?

In Colombia, a house purchased for rent yields 8.1% gross per year and an apartment 8.1%; the house yields more in 8 of 28 cities with sufficient data. The main advantage of the house is in Santa Marta (8.0% against 6.2%).
