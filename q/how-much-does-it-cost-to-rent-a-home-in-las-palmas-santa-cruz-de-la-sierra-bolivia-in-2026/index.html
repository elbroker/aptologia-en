How much does it cost to rent a home in Las Palmas, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes in Las Palmas, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 2,142 per month, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 750 to USD 3,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 7.95/m² and the median listed size is 300 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
