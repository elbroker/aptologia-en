How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in Chicó Navarra, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in Chicó Navarra, Bogotá is COP 550,000,000, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 436,250,000 to COP 620,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,000,000/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is 31% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 420,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 582,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 91.3% of listings include parking.
