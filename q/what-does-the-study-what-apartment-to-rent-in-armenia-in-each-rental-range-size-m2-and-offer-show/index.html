What does the study “What apartment to rent in Armenia in each rental range: size, m² and offer” show?

In Armenia, the most available rental range of apartments for rent is 2.5 - 4 M of pesos per month (40.1% of the ads), with a median area of 80 m². 14.2% of the supply is requested in less than 1.5 million.
