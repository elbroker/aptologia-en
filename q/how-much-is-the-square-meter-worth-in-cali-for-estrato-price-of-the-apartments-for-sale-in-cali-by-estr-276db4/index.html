How much is the square meter worth in Cali for estrato?

From 3.49 million pesos (estrato 3) to 6.22 million pesos (estrato 6) in the median of October 2026.

In Cali the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 3.49 million pesos in estrato 3 and 6.22 million pesos in estrato 6, 1.8 times more. The average total price ranges from 212 million pesos to 920 million pesos according to the estrato.
