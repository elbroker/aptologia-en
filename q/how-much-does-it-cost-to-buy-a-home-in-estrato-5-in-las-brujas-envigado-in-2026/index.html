How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 5 in Las Brujas, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 5 for sale in Las Brujas, Envigado is COP 1,055,000,000, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 851,250,000 to COP 1,407,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,556,627/m² and the median listed size is 129 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 596,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
