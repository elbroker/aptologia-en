How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment in San Isidro, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in San Isidro, Lima, Peru is USD 184,000, based on 93 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 162,000 to USD 238,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,432/m² and the median listed size is 52 m². That is 15% above the study's reference median for Lima (USD 160,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
