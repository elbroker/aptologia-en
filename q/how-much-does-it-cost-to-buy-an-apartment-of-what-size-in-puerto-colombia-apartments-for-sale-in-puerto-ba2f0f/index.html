How much does it cost to buy an apartment of what size in Puerto Colombia?

50 - 70 m²: 295 million pesos; 70 - 90 m²: 510 million pesos; 90 - 120 m²: 560 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Puerto Colombia, an apartment for sale from the group "50 - 70 m²" is requested in 295 million pesos in the median and one from the group "90 - 120 m²" in 560 million pesos (+ 89.8%). For square meter, the cheapest is "50 - 70 m²" (5.18 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "70 - 90 m²" (6.43 million pesos / m²).
