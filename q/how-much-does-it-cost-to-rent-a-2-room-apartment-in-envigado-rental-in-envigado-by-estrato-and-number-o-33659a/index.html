How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Envigado?

Between 2,600,000 pesos (estrato 3) and 4,800,000 pesos (estrato 6) per month in the median, according to October 2026 announcements.

In Envigado, rent an apartment of 2 rooms costs in the median 2,600,000 pesos per month in estrato 3 and 4,800,000 pesos in estrato 6, 1.8 times more. per square meter, the rent goes from 32,667 pesos to 51,765 pesos per month according to the estrato.
