How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 3+ bathrooms 0–5 years old in estrato 6 in Santa María de los Ángeles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms 0–5 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Santa María de los Ángeles, Medellín is COP 1,400,000,000, based on 80 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,137,500,000 to COP 1,785,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,845,853/m² and the median listed size is 163 m². That is 67% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,052,300. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
