Where is it cheaper to rent an apartment of 3 or more bedrooms in São Paulo?

In Morumbi: 5,750 real (USD 1,130) per month in the median.

In São Paulo, an apartment of 3 or more bedrooms is rented for 17,000 reais (USD 3,341) per month in the median in Vila Nova Conceição, the most expensive, and 5,750 reals (USD 1,130) per month in Morumbi, the cheapest: 3.0 times less. We compare 25 bairros with at least 30 ads from 3 or more bedrooms.
