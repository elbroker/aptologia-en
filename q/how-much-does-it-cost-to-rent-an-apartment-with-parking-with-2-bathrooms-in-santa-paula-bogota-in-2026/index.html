How much does it cost to rent an apartment with parking with 2 bathrooms in Santa Paula, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms in Santa Paula, Bogotá is COP 3,900,000 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,381,250 to COP 4,830,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 57,143/m² and the median listed size is 68 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 662,500. The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
