How much does it cost to buy an apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Calahorra, Cajicá in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Calahorra, Cajicá is COP 775,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 703,750,000 to COP 820,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,984,661/m² and the median listed size is 106 m². That is 99% above the study's reference median for Cajicá (COP 390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 500,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
