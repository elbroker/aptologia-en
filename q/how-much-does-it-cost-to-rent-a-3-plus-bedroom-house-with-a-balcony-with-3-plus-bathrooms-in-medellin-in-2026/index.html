How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom house with a balcony with 3+ bathrooms in Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses with a balcony with 3+ bathrooms in Medellín is COP 10,700,000 per month, based on 1,880 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 7,300,000 to COP 16,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 35,897/m² and the median listed size is 288 m². That is 26% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 8,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 900,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 91.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
