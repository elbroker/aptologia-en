Does the pool go up the HOA fee?

Yes: gym + 3.6% per m²; swimming pool + 3.0% per m² in front of comparable buildings without it.

Within the same estrato, new buildings in Bogotá charge more HOA fee per square metre: in estrato 6, $10,181 per m² per month in buildings 0 to 2 years compared to $8,299 in those 21 to 30 years. The most important amenity of the rate is a gym: 3.6% more per m² than in comparable buildings without it. In the whole city, the simple median is $6,462 per m² in new buildings and $7,438 in those over 30 years, because new ones are concentrated in lower estratos.
