How much does it cost to rent an apartment with a balcony on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 in Santa María de los Ángeles, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 in Santa María de los Ángeles, Medellín is COP 6,400,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,350,000 to COP 8,775,000. The median listed size is 116 m². That is 33% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). 93.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
