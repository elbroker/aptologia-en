What is Mosquera's most expensive sector?

San José, with 5.50 million pesos per m² in apartments for sale (October of 2026).

The sector with the most expensive square meter of Mosquera is San José, with 5.50 million pesos in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Mosquera, with 4.88 million pesos. An apartment of 2 rooms is rented for 1.25 million pesos a month in the medium and sold for 265 million pesos. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.0% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 17.6% of the rent (259,085 pesos per month in the median).
