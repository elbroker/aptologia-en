How much does it cost to buy an apartment of what size in Santa Cruz de la Sierra?

Less than 50 m²: USD 60,000; 50 - 70 m²: USD 78,372; 70 - 90 m²: USD 97,221; 90 - 120 m²: USD 116,880; 120 - 160 m²: USD 135,000; More than 160 m²: USD 218,000 (mid-October of 2026).

In Santa Cruz de la Sierra, a department for sale of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in USD 60,000 in the median and one of the group "More than 160 m²" in USD 218,000 (+ 263.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "120 - 160 m²" (USD 1,000 / m²) and the most expensive "Less than 50 m²" (USD 1,454 / m²).
