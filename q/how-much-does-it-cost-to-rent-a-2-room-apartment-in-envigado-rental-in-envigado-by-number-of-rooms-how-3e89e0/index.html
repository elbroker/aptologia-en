How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Envigado?

4,150,000 pesos per month in the medium (October of 2026); the central half of the offer is between 3,600,000 pesos and 4,600,000 pesos.

In Envigado, rent a 1 room apartment costs in the median 3,800,000 pesos per month and one of 2 rooms, 4,150,000 pesos (+ 9.2%). One of 3 or more rooms is requested in 4,500,000 pesos.
