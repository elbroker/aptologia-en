In which neighborhood of Itagüí is the HOA fee more expensive?

In Suramerica: 5,572 pesos per m² per month.

In Itagüí, the most expensive HOA fee per square metre is paid in Suramerica: 5,572 pesos per m² per month in the median (430,000 fee pesos). The cheapest, in Santa Maria, with 4,255 pesos per m². The median rate for the whole city is 335,000 pesos per month (4,762 pesos per m²).
