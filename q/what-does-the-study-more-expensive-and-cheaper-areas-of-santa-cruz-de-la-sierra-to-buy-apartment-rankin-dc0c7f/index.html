What does the study “More expensive and cheaper areas of Santa Cruz de la Sierra to buy apartment: ranking per m²” show?

In Santa Cruz de la Sierra, the area with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is Equipetrol (USD 1,553 / m²) and the cheapest East (USD 1,000 / m²), 1.6 times less. We compare 9 areas with at least 30 ads with area.
