How much does it cost to buy an apartment of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 6 in Cabecera del Llano, Bucaramanga in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 6 for sale in Cabecera del Llano, Bucaramanga is COP 672,500,000, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 567,500,000 to COP 892,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,280,733/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 56% above the study's reference median for Bucaramanga (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 804,500. 98.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
