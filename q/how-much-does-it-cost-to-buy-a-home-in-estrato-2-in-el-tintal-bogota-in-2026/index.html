How much does it cost to buy a home in estrato 2 in El Tintal, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes in estrato 2 for sale in El Tintal, Bogotá is COP 200,000,000, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 181,250,000 to COP 225,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,030,352/m² and the median listed size is 48 m². That is 11% below the study's reference median (COP 225,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 125,200. 15.5% of listings include parking.
