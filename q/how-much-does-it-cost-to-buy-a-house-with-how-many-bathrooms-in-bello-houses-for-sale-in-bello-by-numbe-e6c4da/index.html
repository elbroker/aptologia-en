How much does it cost to buy a house with how many bathrooms in Bello?

1 bathroom: 310 million pesos; 2 bathrooms: 400 million pesos; 3 bathrooms: 538 million pesos; 4 or more bathrooms: 1,800 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Bello, a house for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 310 million pesos in the medium and one of the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 1,800 million pesos (+ 480.6%). For square meter, the cheapest is "1 bath" (3.83 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (5.40 million pesos / m²).
