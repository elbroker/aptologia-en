What does the study “The more it costs to rent an apartment with a balcony in Colombia, city by city and by estrato” show?

In front of comparable apartments in the same city, estrato, rooms and size, the feature "with balcony" changes the rental + 11.0% on average in Colombia. The highest premium is in Barranquilla (+ 25.5%) and the lowest in El Retiro (- 8.3%).
