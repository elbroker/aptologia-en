What does the study “House or apartment: how much the price changes per square metre in each Latin American country” show?

In Paraguay the square meter of a house for sale costs 50.1% less than that of an apartment; in Colombia, 28.2% less. The lowest difference is in Peru, where the house leaves 20.0% cheaper per m².
