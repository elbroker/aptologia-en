How much does it cost to rent a house in estrato 4 in La Castellana, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in estrato 4 in La Castellana, Bogotá is COP 10,000,000 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 7,600,000 to COP 12,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 30,303/m² and the median listed size is 323 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 10,000,000). 94.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
