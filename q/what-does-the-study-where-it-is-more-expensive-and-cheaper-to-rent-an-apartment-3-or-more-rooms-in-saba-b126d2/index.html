What does the study “Where it is more expensive and cheaper to rent an apartment 3 or more rooms in Sabaneta: neighborhood ranking” show?

In Sabaneta, an apartment of 3 or more rooms is rented for 3,650,000 pesos a month in the middle of Vegas of the Doctor, the most expensive neighborhood, and 1,900,000 pesos a month in María Auxiliadora, the cheapest: 1.9 times less. We compare 21 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
