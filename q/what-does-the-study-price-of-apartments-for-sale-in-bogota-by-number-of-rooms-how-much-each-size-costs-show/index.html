What does the study “Price of apartments for sale in Bogotá by number of rooms: how much each size costs” show?

In Bogotá, a 1 room apartment costs 420 million pesos in the medium, one 2 million rooms 540 million pesos (+ 28.6%) and one 3 or more 980 million pesos (+ 81.5%).
