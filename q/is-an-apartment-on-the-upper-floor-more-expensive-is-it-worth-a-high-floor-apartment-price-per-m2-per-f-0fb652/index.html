Is an apartment on the upper floor more expensive?

Yes: from the floor 11 onwards a 20.8% more per m² is requested than on floors 1 - 2, in the same city and estrato.

A flat apartment 11 is requested in Colombia 20.8% more expensive per square metre than one in floors 1 and 2 of the same city and estrato. The comparison uses 56 city and estrato combinations with 30 ads or more in each group.
