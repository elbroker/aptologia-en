And the cheapest?

The America, with 25,263 pesos per m² per month.

In Medellín, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to rent house is Manila (47,225 pesos / m² per month) and the cheapest La America (25,263 pesos / m² per month), 1.9 times less. We compare 23 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
