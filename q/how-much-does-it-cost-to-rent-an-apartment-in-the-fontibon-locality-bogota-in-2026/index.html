How much does it cost to rent an apartment in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá is COP 1,800,000 per month, based on 566 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,450,000 to COP 2,660,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 39,845/m² and the median listed size is 47 m². That is 2% below the study's reference median (COP 1,833,850). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 222,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 39.8% of listings include parking.
