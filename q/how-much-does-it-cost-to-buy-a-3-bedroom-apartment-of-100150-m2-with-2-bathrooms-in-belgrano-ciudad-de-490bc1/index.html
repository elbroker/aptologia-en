How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Belgrano, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Belgrano, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 515,799, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 390,000 to USD 775,000. The median price per square metre is USD 4,235/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 213% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
