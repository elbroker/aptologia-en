What does the study “Price of apartments for sale in San Isidro by number of bedrooms: how much each size costs” show?

In San Isidro, a 1 bedroom department costs USD 171,000 in the median, one 2 USD 292,500 bedroom (+ 71.1%) and one 3 or more USD 330,000 (+ 12.8%).
