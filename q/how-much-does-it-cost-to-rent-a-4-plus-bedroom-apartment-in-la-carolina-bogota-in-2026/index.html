How much does it cost to rent a 4+ bedroom apartment in La Carolina, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 4+ bedroom apartments in La Carolina, Bogotá is COP 15,000,000 per month, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 11,750,000 to COP 19,150,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,000/m² and the median listed size is 322 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 14,700,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,963,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 93.5% of listings include parking.
