How much does it cost to buy an apartment with how many parking lots in Sabaneta?

No parking lot: 390 million pesos; 1 parking lot: 550 million pesos; 2 or more parking lot: 680 million pesos (medium October of 2026).

In Sabaneta, an apartment for sale from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 390 million pesos in the medium and one from the group "2 or more parking lot" in 680 million pesos (+ 74.4%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (5.83 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "1 parking lot" (7.39 million pesos / m²).
