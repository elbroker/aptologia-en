What is the most expensive neighborhood in Punta del Este to rent?

Montoya, with USD 95.5 per m² per month in the median (October of 2026).

In Punta del Este, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to rent apartment is Montoya (USD 95.5 / m² per month) and the cheapest San Rafael (USD 24.4 / m² per month), 3.9 times less. We compare 11 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
