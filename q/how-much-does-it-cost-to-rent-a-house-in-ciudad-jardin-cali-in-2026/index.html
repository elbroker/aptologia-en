How much does it cost to rent a house in Ciudad Jardín, Cali in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Ciudad Jardín, Cali is COP 13,000,000 per month, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 8,875,000 to COP 17,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 25,403/m² and the median listed size is 500 m². That is 30% above the study's reference median for Cali (COP 10,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
