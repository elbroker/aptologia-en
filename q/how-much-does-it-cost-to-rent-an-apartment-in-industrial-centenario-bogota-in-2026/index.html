How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Industrial Centenario, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Industrial Centenario, Bogotá is COP 1,600,000 per month, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,500,000 to COP 1,800,000. That is 42% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,750,000).
