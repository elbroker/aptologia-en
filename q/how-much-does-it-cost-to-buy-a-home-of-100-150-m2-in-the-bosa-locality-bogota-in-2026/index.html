How much does it cost to buy a home of 100–150 m² in the Bosa locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes of 100–150 m² for sale in the Bosa locality, Bogotá is COP 440,000,000, based on 53 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 340,000,000 to COP 500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,333,333/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 96% above the study's reference median (COP 224,750,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 110,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 56.6% of listings include parking.
