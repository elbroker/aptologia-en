Where is it cheaper to buy apartment in Lima?

Among the districts with 30 ads or more, in Los Olivos: 3,702 soles (USD 1,078) per m².

The district with the most expensive square meter in Lima is Barranco, with 9,016 suns (USD 2,624) in the median of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Los Olivos, with 3,702 suns (USD 1,078). For rent, the highest medium rental is requested in San Isidro (4,810 soles (USD 1,400) per month) and the lowest in San Martín de Porres (1,175 soles (USD 342)).
