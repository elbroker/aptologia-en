How much do you rent to buy an apartment to rent in Rionegro?

The average gross return on 24 comparable neighbourhoods is 6.2% per year (12 months of rent requested on the order price), before HOA fee, predial, vacations and taxes.

The average rental of an apartment in Rionegro is $2,500,000 per month; an apartment of 2 rooms asks in the median $2,500,000. Among the neighborhoods with 30 ads or more, the cheapest is Alto Bonito ($1,525,000) and the most expensive Saxony ($6,000,000): 3.9 times more.
