How much does it cost to rent a 2 room apartment in Santa Cruz de la Sierra?

USD 630 per month in the median (October of 2026).

The area with the most expensive square meter of Santa Cruz de la Sierra is Equipetrol, with USD 1,553 in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest of the sample is East, with USD 1,000. A 2 room department is rented per USD 630 per month in the medium and sold per USD 105,000. The gross implicit return on rent is 7.0% per year.
