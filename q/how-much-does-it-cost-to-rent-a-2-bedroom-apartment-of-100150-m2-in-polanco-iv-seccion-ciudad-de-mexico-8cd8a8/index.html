How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² in Polanco IV Sección, Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² in Polanco IV Sección, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 55,000 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 45,000 to MXN 65,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 469/m² and the median listed size is 119 m². That is 72% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 32,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
