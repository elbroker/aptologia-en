What does the study “Where it costs more and less to buy an apartment of 3 or more rooms in Medellín: neighborhood ranking” show?

In Medellín, an apartment of 3 or more rooms costs 2,500 million pesos in the medium in La Calera, the most expensive neighborhood, and 260 million pesos in San Antonio de Prado, the cheapest: 9.6 times less. We compare 60 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 3 or more rooms.
