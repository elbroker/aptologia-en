How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is USD 2,000 per month, based on 95 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,300 to USD 2,850. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 8.37/m² and the median listed size is 248 m². That is 60% above the study's reference median for Lima (USD 1,250). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
