How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom house with 3+ bathrooms in Floresta, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Floresta, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 294,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 249,975 to USD 380,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,294/m² and the median listed size is 238 m². That is 1% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 290,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
