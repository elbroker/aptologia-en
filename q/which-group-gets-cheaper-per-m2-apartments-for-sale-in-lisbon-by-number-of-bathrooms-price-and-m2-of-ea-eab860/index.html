Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "1 bathroom" (7,104 euro (USD 7,986) / m²) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (8,500 euro (USD 9,555) / m²).

In Lisbon, an apartment for sale of the group "1 bathroom" is requested in 430,000 euros (USD 483,356) in the median and one in the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 1,651,000 euros (USD 1,855,861) (+ 284.0%).
