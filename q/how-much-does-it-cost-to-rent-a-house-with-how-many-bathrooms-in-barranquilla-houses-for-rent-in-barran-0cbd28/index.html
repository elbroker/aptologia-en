How much does it cost to rent a house with how many bathrooms in Barranquilla?

1 bath: 3,500,000 pesos per month; 2 bathrooms: 5,000,000 pesos per month; 3 bathrooms: 6,000,000 pesos per month; 4 or more bathrooms: 11,000,000 pesos per month (medium October of 2026).

In Barranquilla, a house for rent from the group "1 bathroom" is ordered in 3,500,000 pesos per month in the medium and one from the group "4 or more bathrooms" in 11,000,000 pesos per month (+ 214.3%). For square meter, the cheapest is "2 bathrooms" (20,426 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "4 or more bathrooms" (27,650 pesos / m² per month).
