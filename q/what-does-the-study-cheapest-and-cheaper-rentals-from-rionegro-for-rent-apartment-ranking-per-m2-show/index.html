What does the study “Cheapest and cheaper rentals from Rionegro for rent apartment: ranking per m²” show?

In Rionegro, the neighborhood with the most expensive m² to rent apartment is Llanogrande (70,482 pesos / m² per month) and the cheapest Fontibon (29,630 pesos / m² per month), 2.4 times less. We compare 18 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area.
