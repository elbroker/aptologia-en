How much does it cost to buy a 1-bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² in Pereira in 2026?

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Pereira is COP 380,000,000, based on 86 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 340,000,000 to COP 512,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,008,065/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 13% below the study's reference median for Pereira (COP 435,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 366,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 96.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
