How much does an apartment rent in Puerto Colombia?

The gross return is 6.5% per year (rent per m² × 12), before expenditure.

An apartment of 2 rooms in Puerto Colombia is requested in 427 million pesos for sale and is rented for 2.00 million pesos per month in the medium. The gross implicit return on rent is 6.5% per year. HOA fee is equivalent to 15.2% of the rent (380,000 pesos per month in the median).
