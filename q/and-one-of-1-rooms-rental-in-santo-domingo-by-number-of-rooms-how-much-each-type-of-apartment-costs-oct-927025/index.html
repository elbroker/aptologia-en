And one of 1 rooms?

USD 1,100 per month in the median.

In Santo Domingo, rent a 1 room apartment costs in the median USD 1,100 per month and one of 2 rooms, USD 1,400 (+ 27.3%). One of 3 or more rooms is requested in USD 1,800.
