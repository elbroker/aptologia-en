How much does it cost to rent a house in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado is COP 9,000,000 per month, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 7,750,000 to COP 11,475,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 41,407/m² and the median listed size is 230 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 11,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 97.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
