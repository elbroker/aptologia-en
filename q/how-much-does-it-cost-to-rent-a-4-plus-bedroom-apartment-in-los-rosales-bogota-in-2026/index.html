How much does it cost to rent a 4+ bedroom apartment in Los Rosales, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for 4+ bedroom apartments in Los Rosales, Bogotá is COP 18,000,000 per month, based on 89 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 14,000,000 to COP 20,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 51,724/m² and the median listed size is 340 m². That is 22% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 14,700,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,500,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 97.8% of listings include parking.
