How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² on floors 1–3 in El Poblado, Medellín in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² on floors 1–3 in El Poblado, Medellín is COP 5,950,000 per month, based on 69 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,800,000 to COP 7,300,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 47,024/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 24% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 83.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
