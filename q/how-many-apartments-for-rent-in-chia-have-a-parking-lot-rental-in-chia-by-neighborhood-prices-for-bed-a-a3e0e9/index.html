How many apartments for rent in Chia have a parking lot?

67.7% of the ads in the purified neighborhoods include a parking lot.

The average rental of an apartment in Chia is $1,700,000 per month; a 2 bedroom apartment asks for $1,550,000. Among the neighborhoods with 30 ads or more, the cheapest is 20 from July Sector II ($1,300,000) and the most expensive Calahorra ($2,675,000): 2.1 times more.
