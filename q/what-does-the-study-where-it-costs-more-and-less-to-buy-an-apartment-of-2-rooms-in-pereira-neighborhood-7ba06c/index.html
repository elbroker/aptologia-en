What does the study “Where it costs more and less to buy an apartment of 2 rooms in Pereira: neighborhood ranking” show?

In Pereira, an apartment of 2 rooms costs 667 million pesos in the median in Cerritos, the most expensive neighborhood, and 295 million pesos in Condina, the cheapest: 2.3 times less. We compare 9 neighborhoods with at least 30 ads from 2 rooms.
