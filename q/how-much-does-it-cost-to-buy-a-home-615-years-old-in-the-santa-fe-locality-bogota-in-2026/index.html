How much does it cost to buy a home 6–15 years old in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes 6–15 years old for sale in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá is COP 470,000,000, based on 137 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 330,000,000 to COP 665,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,685,714/m² and the median listed size is 52 m². That is 3% above the study's reference median (COP 454,982,760). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 461,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 72.3% of listings include parking.
