Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For each square metre, the cheapest is "50 - 70 m²" (43,396 pesos / m² per month) and the most expensive "120 - 160 m²" (54,971 pesos / m² per month).

In Bogotá, an apartment for rent from the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 1,700,000 pesos per month in the median and one from the group "More than 160 m²" in 12,500,000 pesos per month (+ 635.3%).
