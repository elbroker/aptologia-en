How much does it cost to buy a 2-bedroom apartment in the Bosa locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in the Bosa locality, Bogotá is COP 178,000,000, based on 209 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 160,000,000 to COP 211,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,063,830/m² and the median listed size is 44 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median (COP 189,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 95,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 7.7% of listings include parking.
