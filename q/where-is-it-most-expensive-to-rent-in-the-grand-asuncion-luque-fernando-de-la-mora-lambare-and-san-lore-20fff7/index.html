Where is it most expensive to rent in the Grand Asunción (Luque, Fernando de la Mora, Lambaré and San Lorenzo)?

In Fernando de la Mora: 3,500,000 Guarani (USD 613) per month in the medium.

To rent a department in the Grand Asunción (Luque, Fernando de la Mora, Lambaré and San Lorenzo) costs more in Fernando de la Mora, with a medium rent of 3,500,000 Guaranis (USD 613) per month, and less in San Lorenzo, with 3,200,000 Guaranis (USD 561).
