How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more 0–5 years old in estrato 6 in Riomar, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more 0–5 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Riomar, Barranquilla is COP 3,050,000,000, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,200,000,000 to COP 3,900,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,736,322/m² and the median listed size is 555 m². That is 239% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 900,000,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
