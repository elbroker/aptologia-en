How much does it cost to rent a 2-bedroom apartment of 100–150 m² in Ciudad de México, Mexico in 2026?

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² in Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 35,500 per month, based on 650 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 28,001 to MXN 48,375. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 306/m² and the median listed size is 116 m². That is 11% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 32,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
