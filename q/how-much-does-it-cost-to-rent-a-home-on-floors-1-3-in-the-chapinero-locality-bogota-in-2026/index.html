How much does it cost to rent a home on floors 1–3 in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá in 2026?

The median asking rent for homes on floors 1–3 in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá is COP 6,800,000 per month, based on 1,001 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,100,000 to COP 12,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 60,731/m² and the median listed size is 105 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median (COP 7,327,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,403,500. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 79.3% of listings include parking.
