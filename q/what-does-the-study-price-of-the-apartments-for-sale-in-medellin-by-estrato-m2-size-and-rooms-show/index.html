What does the study “Price of the apartments for sale in Medellín by estrato: m², size and rooms” show?

In Medellín the square meter of an apartment for sale costs in the median 3.81 million pesos in estrato 1 and 8.65 million pesos in estrato 6, 2.3 times more. The average total price ranges from 235 million pesos to 1,250 million pesos according to the estrato.
