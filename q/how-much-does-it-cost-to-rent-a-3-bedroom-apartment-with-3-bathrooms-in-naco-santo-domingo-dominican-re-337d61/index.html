How much does it cost to rent a 3+ bedroom apartment with 3+ bathrooms in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic in 2026?

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 2,500 per month, based on 80 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,888 to USD 3,125. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 12.4/m² and the median listed size is 207 m². That is 85% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
