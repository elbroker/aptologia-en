Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (3.28 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 6 - 10" (3.80 million pesos / m²).

In Cúcuta, an apartment for sale of the group "Piso 1 - 2" is requested in 300 million pesos in the median and one of the group "Piso 6 - 10" in 315 million pesos (+ 5.0%). For square meter, the cheapest is "Floor 1 - 2" (3.28 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "Floor 6 - 10" (3.80 million pesos / m²).
