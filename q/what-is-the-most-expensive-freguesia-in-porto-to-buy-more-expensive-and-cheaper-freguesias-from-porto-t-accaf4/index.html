What is the most expensive freguesia in Porto to buy?

Aldoar, Foz do Douro e Nevogilde, with 7,240 euros (USD 8,138) per m² in the median (October of 2026).

In Porto, the freguesia with the most expensive m² to buy apartment is Aldoar, Foz do Douro and Nevogilde (7,240 euros (USD 8,138) / m²) and the cheapest Ramalde (4,499 euros (USD 5,057) / m²), 1.6 times less. We compare 7 freguesias with at least 30 ads with area.
