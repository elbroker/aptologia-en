How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Palermo, Manizales in 2026?

The median asking rent for apartments in Palermo, Manizales is COP 3,180,000 per month, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,900,000 to COP 4,100,000. That is 93% above the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 1,650,000).
