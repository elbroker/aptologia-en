Which group gets cheaper per m²?

For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (3.40 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (4.47 million pesos / m²).

In Pereira, a house for sale from the "No parking lot" group is requested in 520 million pesos in the medium and one from the "2 or more parking lot" in 1,500 million pesos (+ 188.5%). For square meter, the cheapest is "No parking lot" (3.40 million pesos / m²) and the most expensive "2 or more parking lot" (4.47 million pesos / m²).
