How much does it cost to buy an apartment in Comuneros, Bucaramanga in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Comuneros, Bucaramanga is COP 240,000,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 180,000,000 to COP 265,000,000. That is 44% below the study's reference median for Bucaramanga (COP 430,000,000).
