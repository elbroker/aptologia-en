In which neighborhood of Cali do you pay more to buy to rent?

In Santa Teresita, with 9.6% gross per year (10.4 years of rent), according to October announcements of 2026.

In Cali, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical neighborhood 7.7% gross per year: the price requested is equivalent to 12.9 years of rent. The area with the highest gross return is Santa Teresita (9.6%) and the smaller district Bochalema (7.0%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 7.7%.
