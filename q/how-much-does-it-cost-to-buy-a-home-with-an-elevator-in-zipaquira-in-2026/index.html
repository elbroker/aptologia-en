How much does it cost to buy a home with an elevator in Zipaquirá in 2026?

The median asking price for homes with an elevator for sale in Zipaquirá is COP 232,500,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 214,375,000 to COP 261,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,302,963/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is 3% above the study's reference median (COP 225,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 176,337. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 50% of listings include parking.
