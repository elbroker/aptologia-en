What does the study “Profitability of the rent in Santa Marta by estrato: in which you pay the most to buy for rent” show?

In Santa Marta, buying an apartment to rent it yields more in estrato 3 (8.2% gross per year, 12.1 years of rent) and less in estrato 6 (6.2%).
