How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Colombia on average?

The October Aptology Index of 2026 provides a medium rental of 3,420,362 pesos per month.

The October 2026 rental Aptology Index marks a national medium rental of 3,420,362 pesos per month for an apartment in Colombia (average of 25 ad-weighted cities). The most expensive city is Medellín (4,600,000 pesos) and the cheapest Soacha (850,000 pesos); Bogotá marks 2,750,000 pesos.
