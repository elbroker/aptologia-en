What does the study “Price of the house by neighborhood in Buenos Aires City: sale and rental in 46 neighborhoods” show?

The neighborhood with the most expensive square meter in Buenos Aires is Puerto Madero, with USD 5,712 in the medium of apartments for sale; the cheapest in the sample is Nueva Pompeya, with USD 1,189. For rent, the highest medium rental is requested in Caballito (1,200,000 Argentine pesos (USD 792) per month) and the lowest in Villa Lugano (680,000 Argentine pesos (USD 449)).
