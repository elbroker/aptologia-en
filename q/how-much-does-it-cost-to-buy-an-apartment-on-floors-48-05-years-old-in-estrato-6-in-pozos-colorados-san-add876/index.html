How much does it cost to buy an apartment on floors 4–8 0–5 years old in estrato 6 in Pozos Colorados, Santa Marta in 2026?

The median asking price for apartments on floors 4–8 0–5 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Pozos Colorados, Santa Marta is COP 630,000,000, based on 87 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 450,000,000 to COP 857,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,525,227/m² and the median listed size is 67 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 573,500. 79.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
