How much does an apartment rent in Envigado?

In the typical neighborhood, 6.9% gross per year.

In Envigado, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical neighborhood 6.9% gross per year: the price requested is equivalent to 14.5 years of rent. The area with the highest gross return is Las Palmas (8.5%) and the smaller area (5.2%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 6.9%.
