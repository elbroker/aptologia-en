How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment in San Pedro, Madrid in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in San Pedro, Madrid is COP 190,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 10% below the study's reference median for Madrid (COP 210,000,000).
