How much does it cost to rent a house of 250 m² or more in Las Lomas, Asunción, Paraguay in 2026?

The median asking rent for houses of 250 m² or more in Las Lomas, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 3,750 per month, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,275 to USD 4,925. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 7.32/m² and the median listed size is 426 m². That is 34% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 2,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
