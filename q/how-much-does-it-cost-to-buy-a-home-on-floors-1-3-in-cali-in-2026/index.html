How much does it cost to buy a home on floors 1–3 in Cali in 2026?

The median asking price for homes on floors 1–3 for sale in Cali is COP 476,000,000, based on 64 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 273,750,000 to COP 812,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,191,228/m² and the median listed size is 98.5 m². That is 44% above the study's reference median (COP 330,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 274,710. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 73.4% of listings include parking.
