How much does an apartment rent in Cartagena?

In the typical neighborhood, 6.6% gross per year.

In Cartagena, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical neighborhood 6.6% gross per year: the price requested is equivalent to 15.1 years of rent. The district with the highest gross return is Torices (9.1%) and the smaller district, Manzanillo (4.4%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 6.8%.
