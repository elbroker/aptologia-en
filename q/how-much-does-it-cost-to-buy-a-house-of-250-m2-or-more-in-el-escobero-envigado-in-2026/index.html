How much does it cost to buy a house of 250 m² or more in El Escobero, Envigado in 2026?

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more for sale in El Escobero, Envigado is COP 2,550,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,950,000,000 to COP 3,500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,083,333/m² and the median listed size is 450 m². That is 11% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 2,300,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 890,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 93.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
