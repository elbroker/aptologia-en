How much does it cost to buy an apartment of what size in Santiago?

Less than 50 m²: 2,680 UF (USD 112,332); 50 - 70 m²: 3,841 UF (USD 160,987); 70 - 90 m²: 6,700 UF (USD 280,829); 90 - 120 m²: 8,800 UF (USD 368,850; 120 - 160 m²: 11,000 UF (USD 461,063); More than 160 m²: 16,500 UF (USD 691,594) (mid-October of 2026).

In Santiago, a department for sale of the group "Less than 50 m²" is requested in 2,680 UF (USD 112,332) in the median and one of the group "More 160 m²" in 16,500 UF (USD 691,594) (+ 515.7%). For square meter, the cheapest is "50 - 70 m²" (67 UF (USD 2,821) / m²) and the most expensive "90 - 120 m²" (85 UF (USD 3,576) / m²).
