How much does it cost to buy a 3+ bedroom apartment of 45–70 m² 0–5 years old in Alameda del Río, Barranquilla in 2026?

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² 0–5 years old for sale in Alameda del Río, Barranquilla is COP 250,000,000, based on 80 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 211,500,000 to COP 310,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,409,722/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 44% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 233,300. The most frequent estrato is 3, 86.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
