Sale prices of 1-bedroom apartments in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá is COP 475,000,000, based on 1,078 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 380,000,000 to COP 608,031,250. The median price per square metre is COP 10,000,000/m² and the median listed size is 49 m². That is 50% below the study's reference median (COP 950,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 500,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 79.2% of listings include parking.
