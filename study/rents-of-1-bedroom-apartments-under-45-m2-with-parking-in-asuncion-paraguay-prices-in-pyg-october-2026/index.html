Rents of 1-bedroom apartments under 45 m² with parking in Asunción, Paraguay (prices in PYG) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments under 45 m² with parking in Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 4,200,000 per month, based on 105 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
