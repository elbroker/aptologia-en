Frequently asked questions

How much is the square meter rent in Bogotá? The median is $47,500 per m² per month (20,638 ads, cut to 12 October of 2026). Half of the apartments are between $34,524 and $63,333 per m².

How much is the m² rent in estrato 3? $36,111 per m² per month in the median; in estrato 4, $47,956.

What's the most expensive neighborhood per square meter? Between neighborhoods with at least 30 ads, Chico Norte Et. Ii with $88,106 / m², followed by Chico La Cabrera ($79,479 / m²).

What's the cheapest neighborhood per square meter? Look at it with $29,787 / m² and Las Brisas with $31,143 / m².

Why are small apartments more expensive per m²? Because kitchen, bathroom and common areas weigh the same in a small apartment. A 1 room apartment costs $60,000 / m², compared to $41,026 / m² one of 2.

Methodology

  • Source: daily intake base of Aptology, with public ads of Metroocuadrado, Fincaraíz, Cienalquís, Rentola and alchem.com. Court as at 12 October of 2026.
  • Filters: live ads that pass quality control, only apartments in Bogotá, rent between $400,000 and $30,000,000. TikTok and Facebook Market are excluded.
  • Duplicate: one ad is left per group of duplicates (the same building on several portals). There are 21,042 unique apartments.
  • Price per m²: rent divided by the published area; areas from 20 to 400 m² and values from $8,000 to $200,000 per m².
  • Limitations: are prices ordered, not closed. The neighborhoods come as each portal writes, so the same sector can appear with two names. Only groups with at least 20 ads are published.
  • Open data: datos.csv, CC BY license 4. 0.
