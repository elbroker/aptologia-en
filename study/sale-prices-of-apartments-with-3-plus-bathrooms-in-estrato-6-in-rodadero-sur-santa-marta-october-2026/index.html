Sale prices of apartments with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 6 in Rodadero Sur, Santa Marta — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 6 for sale in Rodadero Sur, Santa Marta is COP 1,500,000,000, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
