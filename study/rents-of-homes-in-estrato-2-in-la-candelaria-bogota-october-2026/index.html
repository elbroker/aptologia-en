Rents of homes in estrato 2 in La Candelaria, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 2 in La Candelaria, Bogotá is COP 1,800,000 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
