Homes for sale for less than COP 250 million in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá — October 2026

Homes listed for sale for less than COP 250 million in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 220,000,000, based on 266 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 200,000,000 to COP 230,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,597,297/m² and the median listed size is 45 m². That is 9% below the study's reference median (COP 241,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 137,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 8.6% of listings include parking.
