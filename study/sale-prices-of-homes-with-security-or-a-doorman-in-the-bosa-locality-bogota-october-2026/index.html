Sale prices of homes with security or a doorman in the Bosa locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with security or a doorman for sale in the Bosa locality, Bogotá is COP 185,000,000, based on 261 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 165,000,000 to COP 210,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,950,000/m² and the median listed size is 46 m². That is 18% below the study's reference median (COP 224,750,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 95,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 13.4% of listings include parking.
