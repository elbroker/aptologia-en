Sale prices of homes of 150–250 m² in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes of 150–250 m² for sale in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá is COP 600,000,000, based on 73 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 400,000,000 to COP 840,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,045,455/m² and the median listed size is 208 m². That is 88% above the study's reference median (COP 319,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 10,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 61.6% of listings include parking.
