Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments in Calle Larga, Sabaneta — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Calle Larga, Sabaneta is COP 2,900,000 per month, based on 53 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,600,000 to COP 3,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 38,462/m² and the median listed size is 74 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 3,300,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 72.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
