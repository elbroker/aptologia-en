Rents of homes in estrato 5 in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 5 in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá is COP 4,915,000 per month, based on 431 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,277,000 to COP 9,350,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 63,256/m² and the median listed size is 82 m². That is 33% below the study's reference median (COP 7,327,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 800,000. 78.9% of listings include parking.
