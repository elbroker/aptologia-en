Sale prices of homes with security or a doorman in the Los Mártires locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with security or a doorman for sale in the Los Mártires locality, Bogotá is COP 295,000,000, based on 164 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 240,000,000 to COP 400,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,103,448/m² and the median listed size is 38.5 m². That is 16% below the study's reference median (COP 350,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 209,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 34.1% of listings include parking.
