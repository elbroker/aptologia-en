Rents of houses of 150–250 m² in estrato 6 in El Poblado, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses of 150–250 m² in estrato 6 in El Poblado, Medellín is COP 9,400,000 per month, based on 141 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 7,800,000 to COP 11,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 45,882/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 11% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 8,500,000). 98.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
