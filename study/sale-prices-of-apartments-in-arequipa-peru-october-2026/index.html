Sale prices of apartments in Arequipa, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Arequipa, Peru is PEN 501,350, based on 524 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 383,375 to PEN 666,062. The median price per square metre is PEN 4,215/m² and the median listed size is 119 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PEN 70. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
