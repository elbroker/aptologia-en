Rents of apartments with 1 bathroom in estrato 6 in Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with 1 bathroom in estrato 6 in Medellín is COP 3,800,000 per month, based on 307 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
