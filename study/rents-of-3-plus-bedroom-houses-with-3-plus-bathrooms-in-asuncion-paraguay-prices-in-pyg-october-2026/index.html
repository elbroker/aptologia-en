Rents of 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms in Asunción, Paraguay (prices in PYG) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms in Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 10,000,000 per month, based on 983 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
