Sale prices of homes with security or a doorman in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with security or a doorman for sale in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá is COP 650,000,000, based on 405 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 405,000,000 to COP 990,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,250,000/m² and the median listed size is 79 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median (COP 860,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 520,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 74.6% of listings include parking.
