Sale prices of homes in estrato 3 in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes in estrato 3 for sale in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá is COP 320,000,000, based on 617 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 236,500,000 to COP 600,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,108,696/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 40% below the study's reference median (COP 530,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 170,000. 44.1% of listings include parking.
