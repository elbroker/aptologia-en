Sale prices of homes in Fontibon, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in Fontibon, Bogotá is COP 380,000,000, based on 550 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 235,875,000 to COP 699,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,270,270/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 56% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 180,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 52.5% of listings include parking.
