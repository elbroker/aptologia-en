Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in ARS) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is ARS 1,500,000 per month, based on 369 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from ARS 1,200,000 to ARS 1,850,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is ARS 16,854/m² and the median listed size is 86 m². That is 76% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (ARS 850,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
