Sale prices of houses with parking 0–5 years old in Manga, Cartagena — October 2026

The median asking price for houses with parking 0–5 years old for sale in Manga, Cartagena is COP 1,500,000,000, based on 88 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 980,000,000 to COP 2,525,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,592,105/m² and the median listed size is 288 m². That is 15% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 1,300,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 645,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
