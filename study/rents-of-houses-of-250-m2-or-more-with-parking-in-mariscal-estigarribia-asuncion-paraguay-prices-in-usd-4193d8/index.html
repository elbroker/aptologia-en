Rents of houses of 250 m² or more with parking in Mariscal Estigarribia, Asunción, Paraguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses of 250 m² or more with parking in Mariscal Estigarribia, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 2,900 per month, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,200 to USD 4,575. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 6.55/m² and the median listed size is 425 m². That is 4% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 2,800). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
