Sale prices of apartments with parking 0–5 years old in Cañaveralejo, Sabaneta — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with parking 0–5 years old for sale in Cañaveralejo, Sabaneta is COP 492,500,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 453,099,480 to COP 580,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,182,540/m² and the median listed size is 67 m². That is 10% below the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 550,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 370,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
