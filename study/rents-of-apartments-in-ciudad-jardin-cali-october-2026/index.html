Rents of apartments in Ciudad Jardín, Cali — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Ciudad Jardín, Cali is COP 4,500,000 per month, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,200,000 to COP 6,050,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 40,283/m² and the median listed size is 111 m². That is 137% above the study's reference median for Cali (COP 1,900,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 90.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
