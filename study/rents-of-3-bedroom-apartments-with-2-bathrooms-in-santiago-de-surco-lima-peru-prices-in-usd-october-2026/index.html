Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is USD 1,300 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 950 to USD 1,400. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 8.01/m² and the median listed size is 150 m². That is 4% above the study's reference median for Lima (USD 1,250). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
