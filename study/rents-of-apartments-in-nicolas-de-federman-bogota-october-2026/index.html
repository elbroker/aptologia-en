Rents of apartments in Nicolas de Federman, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Nicolas de Federman, Bogotá is COP 3,200,000 per month, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,500,000 to COP 4,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 47,170/m² and the median listed size is 77 m². That is 9% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 525,250. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 68.4% of listings include parking.
