Rents of apartments with 1 bathroom in Vila Clementino, São Paulo, Brazil — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with 1 bathroom in Vila Clementino, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 3,290 per month, based on 63 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 2,870 to BRL 3,800. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 89.1/m² and the median listed size is 40 m². That is 9% below the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 730. 70.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
