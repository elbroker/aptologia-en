Rents of apartments of 100–150 m² in Providencia, Santiago, Chile — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 100–150 m² in Providencia, Santiago, Chile is CLP 1,400,000 per month, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from CLP 1,200,000 to CLP 1,665,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is CLP 11,700/m² and the median listed size is 114 m². That is 211% above the study's reference median for Santiago (CLP 450,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
