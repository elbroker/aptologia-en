Rents of apartments of 70–100 m² in Roma Norte, Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in Roma Norte, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 40,000 per month, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 32,750 to MXN 51,276. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 497/m² and the median listed size is 79 m². That is 25% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 32,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
