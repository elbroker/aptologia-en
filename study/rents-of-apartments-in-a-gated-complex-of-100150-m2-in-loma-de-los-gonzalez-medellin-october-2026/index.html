Rents of apartments in a gated complex of 100–150 m² in Loma de los González, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in a gated complex of 100–150 m² in Loma de los González, Medellín is COP 6,550,000 per month, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 6,000,000 to COP 7,625,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 60,000/m² and the median listed size is 111 m². That is 36% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 97.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
