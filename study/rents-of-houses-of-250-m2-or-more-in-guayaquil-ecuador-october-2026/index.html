Rents of houses of 250 m² or more in Guayaquil, Ecuador — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses of 250 m² or more in Guayaquil, Ecuador is USD 2,500 per month, based on 227 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,950 to USD 3,800. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 6.21/m² and the median listed size is 400 m². The median number of bathrooms is 4.
