Rents of homes in estrato 4 in Chapinero Alto, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 4 in Chapinero Alto, Bogotá is COP 2,800,000 per month, based on 194 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
