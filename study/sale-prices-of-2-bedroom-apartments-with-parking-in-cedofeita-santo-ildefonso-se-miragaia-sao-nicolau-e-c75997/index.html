Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments with parking in Cedofeita, Santo Ildefonso, Sé, Miragaia, São Nicolau e Vitória, Oporto, Portugal — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with parking for sale in Cedofeita, Santo Ildefonso, Sé, Miragaia, São Nicolau e Vitória, Oporto, Portugal is EUR 495,500, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 413,950 to EUR 581,000. The median price per square metre is EUR 5,647/m² and the median listed size is 85 m². That is 17% above the study's reference median for Oporto (EUR 425,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
