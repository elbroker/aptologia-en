Sale prices of apartments in Santa Barbara Central, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Santa Barbara Central, Bogotá is COP 900,000,000, based on 489 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 670,000,000 to COP 1,250,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,809,315/m² and the median listed size is 140 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,000,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 98.2% of listings include parking.
