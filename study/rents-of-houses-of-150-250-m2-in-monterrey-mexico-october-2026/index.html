Rents of houses of 150–250 m² in Monterrey, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses of 150–250 m² in Monterrey, Mexico is MXN 26,900 per month, based on 204 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 21,875 to MXN 32,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 134/m² and the median listed size is 196 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
