Rents of 3+ bedroom houses of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 5,500 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 3,200 to USD 10,600. The median listed size is 137 m². That is 25% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 7,300). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
