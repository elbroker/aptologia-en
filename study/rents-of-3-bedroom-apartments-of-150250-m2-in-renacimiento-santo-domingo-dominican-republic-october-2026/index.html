Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² in Renacimiento, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² in Renacimiento, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,550 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,362 to USD 1,688. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 8/m² and the median listed size is 188 m². That is 15% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
