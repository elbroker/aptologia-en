Sale prices of 1-bedroom apartments with parking in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments with parking for sale in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 85,000, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 74,500 to USD 111,250. The median price per square metre is USD 1,606/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 15% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 60. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
