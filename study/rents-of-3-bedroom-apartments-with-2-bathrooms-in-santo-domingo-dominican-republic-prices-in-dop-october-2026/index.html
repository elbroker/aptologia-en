Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic (prices in DOP) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is DOP 36,000 per month, based on 219 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from DOP 25,000 to DOP 50,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is DOP 312/m² and the median listed size is 117 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (DOP 38,500). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
