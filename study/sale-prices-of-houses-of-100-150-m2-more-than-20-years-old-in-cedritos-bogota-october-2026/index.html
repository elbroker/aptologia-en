Sale prices of houses of 100–150 m² more than 20 years old in Cedritos, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for houses of 100–150 m² more than 20 years old for sale in Cedritos, Bogotá is COP 740,000,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 687,500,000 to COP 799,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,550,505/m² and the median listed size is 134 m². That is 38% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 655,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 97.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
