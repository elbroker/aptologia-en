Rents of 2-bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² in Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² in Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 65,000 per month, based on 296 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 46,800 to MXN 85,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 360/m² and the median listed size is 180 m². That is 103% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 32,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
