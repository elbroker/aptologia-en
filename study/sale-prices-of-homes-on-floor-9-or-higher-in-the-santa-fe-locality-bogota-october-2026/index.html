Sale prices of homes on floor 9 or higher in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes on floor 9 or higher for sale in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá is COP 447,000,000, based on 174 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 330,000,000 to COP 672,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,144,651/m² and the median listed size is 44.6 m². That is 2% below the study's reference median (COP 454,982,760). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 325,750. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 56.3% of listings include parking.
