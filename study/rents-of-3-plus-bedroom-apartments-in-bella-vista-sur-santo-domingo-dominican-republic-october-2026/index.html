Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments in Bella Vista Sur, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Bella Vista Sur, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 2,450 per month, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
