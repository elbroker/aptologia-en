Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments in Santa Cruz Atoyac, Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Santa Cruz Atoyac, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 5,396,437, based on 76 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 4,597,500 to MXN 6,200,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 67,420/m² and the median listed size is 76 m². That is 17% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 4,606,874). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
