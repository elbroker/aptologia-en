Rents of houses with 3+ bathrooms in Las Carmelitas, Asunción, Paraguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses with 3+ bathrooms in Las Carmelitas, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 3,100 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,200 to USD 3,400. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 8.07/m² and the median listed size is 380 m². That is 11% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 2,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
