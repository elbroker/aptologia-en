Sale prices of apartments of 70–100 m² in Jesús María, Lima, Peru (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Jesús María, Lima, Peru is USD 167,903, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
