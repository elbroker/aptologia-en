Rents of apartments of 45–70 m² in Las Mercedes, Asunción, Paraguay (prices in PYG) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in Las Mercedes, Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 4,250,000 per month, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 18% below the study's reference median for Asunción (PYG 5,200,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
