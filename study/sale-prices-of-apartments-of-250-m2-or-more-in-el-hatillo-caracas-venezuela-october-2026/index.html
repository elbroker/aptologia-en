Sale prices of apartments of 250 m² or more in El Hatillo, Caracas, Venezuela — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 250 m² or more for sale in El Hatillo, Caracas, Venezuela is USD 422,500, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 301,250 to USD 498,750. The median price per square metre is USD 1,106/m² and the median listed size is 362 m². That is 76% above the study's reference median for Caracas (USD 240,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
