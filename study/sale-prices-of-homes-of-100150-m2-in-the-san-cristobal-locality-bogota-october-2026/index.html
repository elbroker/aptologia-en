Sale prices of homes of 100–150 m² in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes of 100–150 m² for sale in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá is COP 407,500,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 320,000,000 to COP 550,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,163,874/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 28% above the study's reference median (COP 319,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 352,650. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 38.2% of listings include parking.
