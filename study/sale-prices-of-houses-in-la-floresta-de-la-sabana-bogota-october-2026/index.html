Sale prices of houses in La Floresta de la Sabana, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for houses for sale in La Floresta de la Sabana, Bogotá is COP 3,000,000,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,425,000,000 to COP 3,500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,123,661/m² and the median listed size is 516 m². That is 150% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 690,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
