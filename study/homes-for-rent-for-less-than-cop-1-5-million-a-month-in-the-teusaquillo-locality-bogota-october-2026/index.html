Homes for rent for less than COP 1.5 million a month in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá — October 2026

Homes listed for rent for less than COP 1.5 million a month in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá have a median asking rent of COP 1,300,000 per month, based on 110 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,105,000 to COP 1,400,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,000/m² and the median listed size is 25 m². That is 53% below the study's reference median (COP 2,769,500). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 305,100. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 10% of listings include parking.
