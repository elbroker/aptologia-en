Sale prices of apartments with parking in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with parking for sale in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 275,000, based on 1,751 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
