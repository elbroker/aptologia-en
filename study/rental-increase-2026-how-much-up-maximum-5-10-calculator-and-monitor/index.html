Rental increase 2026: How much up? Maximum 5.10% (calculator and monitor)

Machine-assisted English version of the Spanish original by Aptología. All figures are locked to the original (numbers are copied, not re-translated); if in doubt, the Spanish original prevails.

In 2026 the urban housing rental in Colombia may rise by a maximum of 5.10%, which is inflation (CPI) of 2025 published by DANE on 8 January of 2026 (DANE), This is set out in article 20 of the 820 Act of 2003: a $1,000,000 rental may be up to $1,051,000, and only when 12 months from the last readjustment are fulfilled.

Summary: The top of the rental adjustment is the previous year's PCI, not the increase in the minimum wage (23% by 2026). Since 2016, a rent that always increased the legal ceiling grew 85%; if it had increased as the minimum wage, it would have grown 172%.

How is it calculated (formula)

  1. Take your current rental (e.g. $1,500,000).
  2. Multiplicate it by the previous year's CPI in decimal: 1,500,000 × 0.051 = $76,500 maximum increase.
  3. New maximum rental: 1,500,000 × 1.051 = $1,576,500,

Rules of Law 820 of 2003 (text): - Article 20 each 12 month of contract performance at the same price, the lessor may raise the rent to 100% of the previous calendar year's CPI. It is not automatic in January: it applies on the anniversary of the contract or the last readjustment, and should be notified to the tenant. - Article 18 the monthly rental cannot exceed 1% of the commercial value of the property. - The increase can be less or zero; never greater. The rule is for urban housing; commercial premises are governed by the terms of the contract.

Finding: rental of a tenant with a contract in force increases less than the minimum wage

In the 16 years of the series (2011 - 2026), the increase in the minimum wage was higher than the legal ceiling of the rent each year. 2016 was the year more equal (7.00% compared to 6.77%). In 2026 the gap is the largest in the series: 23% versus 5.10%.

hypothetical scenario with a $1,000,000 rent in 2015: - If each year the legal ceiling went up: $1,846,344 in 2026. - If it had risen as the minimum wage: $2,717,385 in 2026, i.e. $871,041 plus a month,

Eye: this applies within a contract. The price you ask for a new apartment on the market has no legal ceiling, so this scenario does not say how much market rental went up.

Methodology

  • Public data (DANE): annual change of the total CPI at December of each year (Annex of variations 2003 - 2025). Annual change in the subclass 04130100 "Actual rental" of the December 2024 and 2025 Annexes and September 2026 Annex for the monitor.
  • Public data (decrees): Minimum monthly wage for each year according to the minimum wage decrees. The 2026 leaves the Decrees 1469 of 2025 and 159 of 2026.
  • Legal rule: year A = annual CPI to December of year A- 1 (Law 820, art. 20).
  • Aptology data: average price per city (live apartment ads, photo of 2026 - 10 - 05). It's only used on the weight table by city.
  • hypothetical scenario: $1,000,000 in 2015, to which it is applied each year, from 2016, the ceiling or increase of the minimum wage.
  • Update: update download the latest DANE annex and regenerate datos.csv. calculator / ipc _ per _ anio.json and the charts.

Limitations

  • The cap is legal, not a market data. It doesn't measure how much new contract rentals go up.
  • DANE's "actual rental" is only included here for 2024 and 2025 (verified annexes). The previous years are pending.
  • The minimum wage of 2026 was fixed on a temporary basis by Decree 159 of 2026, while the Council of State decides.
  • The medium Aptology are prices ordered in ads, not closed prices, and Medellín includes a wide range of high value ads.
  • This is not legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the rent increase by 2026? Maximum 5.10%, which was the PCI of 2025 according to DANE. In a $1,000,000 rental is $51,000 more.

Does the rent go up with the minimum wage? No. The cap is the previous year's CPI (Law 820, art. 20). The minimum wage increased 23% by 2026, but that does not change the housing rental ceiling.

Since when can I get my rent up? Since 12 months of the contract or last readjustment, not automatically in January, and with announcement to the tenant.

How much does a commercial place rent up in 2026? The CPC ceiling of the Act 820 applies to urban housing. In a commercial place he sends what was agreed in the contract, which often also uses the IPC as a reference.

How much will the rent go up in 2027? It shall be known on 8 January 2027 when DANE publishes the December 2026 IPC. As at September 2026, annual inflation was 6.29%.

Can I get more of the IPC if the owner made improvements? Article 20 of the 820 Act of 2003 sets the adjustment ceiling at 100% of the previous year's CPI for urban housing and provides no exception for improvements. In a particular case, a legal consultation should be made.
