Sale prices of apartments 6–20 years old in Cerros de Suba, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments 6–20 years old for sale in Cerros de Suba, Bogotá is COP 2,100,000,000, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,525,000,000 to COP 2,600,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,812,500/m² and the median listed size is 245 m². That is 180% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,700,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
