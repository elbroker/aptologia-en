Sale prices of homes with security or a doorman in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with security or a doorman for sale in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá is COP 450,000,000, based on 714 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 280,000,000 to COP 680,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,835,687/m² and the median listed size is 64 m². That is 15% below the study's reference median (COP 530,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 300,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 67.4% of listings include parking.
