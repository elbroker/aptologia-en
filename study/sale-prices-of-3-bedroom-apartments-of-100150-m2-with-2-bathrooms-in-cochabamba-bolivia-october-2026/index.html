Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Cochabamba, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Cochabamba, Bolivia is USD 99,000, based on 52 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 88,877 to USD 126,997. The median price per square metre is USD 848/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 12% above the study's reference median for Cochabamba (USD 88,250). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 40. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
