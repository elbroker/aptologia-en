Rents of apartments of 45–70 m² in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 1,100 per month, based on 291 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 850 to USD 1,400. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 20.7/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². The median number of bathrooms is 1.
