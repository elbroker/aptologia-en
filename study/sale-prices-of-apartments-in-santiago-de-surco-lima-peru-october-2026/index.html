Sale prices of apartments in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is PEN 823,150, based on 1,044 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 605,000 to PEN 1,162,500. The median price per square metre is PEN 6,776/m² and the median listed size is 118 m². That is 32% above the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 621,600). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PEN 1. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
