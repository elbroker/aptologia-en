Sale prices of homes up to 5 years old in the Rafael Uribe Uribe locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes up to 5 years old for sale in the Rafael Uribe Uribe locality, Bogotá is COP 405,000,000, based on 350 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 257,750,000 to COP 620,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,954,522/m² and the median listed size is 110 m². That is 10% below the study's reference median (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 170,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 48.6% of listings include parking.
