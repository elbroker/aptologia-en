Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments in estrato 4 in Bellavista, Barranquilla — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments in estrato 4 for sale in Bellavista, Barranquilla is COP 445,000,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 399,500,000 to COP 499,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,800,363/m² and the median listed size is 92 m². That is 1% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 470,000. 97.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
