Gross rental profitability in 28 municipalities of Catalonia with official data (2026)

Machine-assisted English version of the Spanish original by Aptología. All figures are locked to the original (numbers are copied, not re-translated); if in doubt, the Spanish original prevails.

With official data, the gross return on the highest rental of 28 Catalan municipalities is in Lleida: 6.7% per year (7.59 €per m² per month compared to 1,369 €per m² of value assessed); the lowest, in Sant Cugat del Vallès, with 3.8%. In Barcelona it is 4.3%: the square meter is worth 23.0 years of rent.

Abstract: we cross the average rent per m² of the bonds of Incasòl with the value measured by m² of the MIVAU (2026 -T2) in 28 municipalities with 30 assessments or more.

Updated: 18 October 2026 (official data of Incasòl and MIVAU included in the exports of 18 October 2026) Next update: monthly.

Methodology

  • Sources: average rent per m² of the bonds deposited in Incasòl (Generalitat de Catalunya) and average value measured per m² of free housing of the MIVAU (2026 -T2), as they appear in the Exports of Aptology of 18 October of 2026.
  • Calculation: Gross return = rental €/ m² × 12 - value valued €/ m²; years of rent = 100 - profitability. Before taxes, community, vacations and expenses.
  • Sample: municipalities with rent per m² published and 30 assessments or more.
  • Limitations: the years of rent (2024 or 2025) and valuation (2026) do not match; the value assessed may differ from the market price.
  • Open data: datos.csv, CC BY license 4. 0.
