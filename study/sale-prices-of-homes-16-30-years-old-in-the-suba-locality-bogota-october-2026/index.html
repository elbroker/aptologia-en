Sale prices of homes 16–30 years old in the Suba locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes 16–30 years old for sale in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 1,150,000,000, based on 2,693 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 650,000,000 to COP 1,600,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,416,667/m² and the median listed size is 216 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 1,150,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 690,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 92.5% of listings include parking.
