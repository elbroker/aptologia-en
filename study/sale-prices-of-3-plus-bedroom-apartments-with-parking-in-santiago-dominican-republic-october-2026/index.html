Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments with parking in Santiago, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking for sale in Santiago, Dominican Republic is USD 203,893, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 158,750 to USD 290,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,459/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 15% above the study's reference median for Santiago (USD 178,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
