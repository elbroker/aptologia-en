Rents of 1-bedroom apartments with a balcony in Rósales, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments with a balcony in Rósales, Bogotá is COP 5,400,000 per month, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,695,000 to COP 7,540,000. The median listed size is 77 m². That is 64% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 899,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
