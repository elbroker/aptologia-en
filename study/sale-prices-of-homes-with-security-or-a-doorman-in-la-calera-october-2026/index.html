Sale prices of homes with security or a doorman in La Calera — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with security or a doorman for sale in La Calera is COP 2,500,000,000, based on 49 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,900,000,000 to COP 2,900,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,765,385/m² and the median listed size is 400 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median (COP 2,325,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,850,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
