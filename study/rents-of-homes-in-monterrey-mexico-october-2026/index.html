Rents of homes in Monterrey, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in Monterrey, Mexico is MXN 28,000 per month, based on 2,565 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 21,000 to MXN 37,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 309/m² and the median listed size is 98 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
