Homes for sale for less than COP 250 million in Tunja — October 2026

Homes listed for sale for less than COP 250 million in Tunja have a median asking price of COP 192,500,000, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 156,218,750 to COP 225,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,324,736/m² and the median listed size is 47.2 m². That is 51% below the study's reference median (COP 390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 113,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 25% of listings include parking.
