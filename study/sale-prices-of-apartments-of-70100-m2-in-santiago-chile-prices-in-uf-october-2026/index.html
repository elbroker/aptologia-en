Sale prices of apartments of 70–100 m² in Santiago, Chile (prices in UF) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Santiago, Chile is UF 6,990, based on 1,210 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 5,492 to UF 8,395. The median price per square metre is UF 84.5/m² and the median listed size is 82.3 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
