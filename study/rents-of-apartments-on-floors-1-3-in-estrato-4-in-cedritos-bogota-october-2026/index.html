Rents of apartments on floors 1–3 in estrato 4 in Cedritos, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments on floors 1–3 in estrato 4 in Cedritos, Bogotá is COP 2,500,000 per month, based on 85 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,000,000 to COP 3,184,700. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 40,870/m² and the median listed size is 67 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 400,000. 95.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
