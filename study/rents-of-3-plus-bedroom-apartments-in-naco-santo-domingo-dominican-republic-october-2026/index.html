Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 2,325 per month, based on 106 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,700 to USD 2,995. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 11.9/m² and the median listed size is 198 m². That is 29% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
