Rents of 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking in Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking in Medellín is COP 3,300,000 per month, based on 1,042 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,700,000 to COP 3,900,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 54,065/m² and the median listed size is 62 m². That is 31% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 406,476. The most frequent estrato is 5 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
