Sale prices of apartments in Los Cedros, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Los Cedros, Bogotá is COP 512,749,500, based on 52 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 427,500,000 to COP 630,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,857,143/m² and the median listed size is 82 m². That is 33% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 480,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 96.2% of listings include parking.
