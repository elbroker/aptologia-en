Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 134,000, based on 1,365 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 94,000 to USD 178,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,321/m² and the median listed size is 59 m². That is 19% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 165,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
