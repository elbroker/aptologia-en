Homes for sale for COP 2,000 million or more in the La Candelaria locality, Bogotá — October 2026

Homes listed for sale for COP 2,000 million or more in the La Candelaria locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 3,600,000,000, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,700,000,000 to COP 4,480,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,128,205/m² and the median listed size is 600 m². That is 526% above the study's reference median (COP 575,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 10,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 10.6% of listings include parking.
