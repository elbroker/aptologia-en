Sale prices of 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more in San Isidro, Argentina (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more for sale in San Isidro, Argentina is USD 670,000, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
