Rents of apartments with a balcony on floors 4–8 in Santa Bárbara, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony on floors 4–8 in Santa Bárbara, Bogotá is COP 5,524,800 per month, based on 88 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,871,850 to COP 9,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 52,659/m² and the median listed size is 98.5 m². That is 67% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 950,400. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
