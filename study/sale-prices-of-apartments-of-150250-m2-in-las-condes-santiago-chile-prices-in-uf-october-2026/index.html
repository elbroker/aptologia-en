Sale prices of apartments of 150–250 m² in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile (prices in UF) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile is UF 14,495, based on 416 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 12,500 to UF 16,900. The median price per square metre is UF 80.3/m² and the median listed size is 174 m². That is 109% above the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 6,950). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
