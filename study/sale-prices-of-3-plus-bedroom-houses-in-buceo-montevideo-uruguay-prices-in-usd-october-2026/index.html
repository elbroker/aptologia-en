Sale prices of 3+ bedroom houses in Buceo, Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses for sale in Buceo, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 358,000, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 285,000 to USD 440,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,622/m² and the median listed size is 238 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 350,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
