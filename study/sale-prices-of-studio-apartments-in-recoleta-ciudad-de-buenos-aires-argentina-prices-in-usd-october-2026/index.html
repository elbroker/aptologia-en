Sale prices of studio apartments in Recoleta, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for studio apartments for sale in Recoleta, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 106,000, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
