Sale prices of 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more in Ciudad Jardín, Cali — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more for sale in Ciudad Jardín, Cali is COP 2,100,000,000, based on 350 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,482,500,000 to COP 2,987,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,301,075/m² and the median listed size is 460 m². That is 126% above the study's reference median for Cali (COP 930,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,560,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
