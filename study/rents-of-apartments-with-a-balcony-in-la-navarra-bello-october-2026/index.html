Rents of apartments with a balcony in La Navarra, Bello — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony in La Navarra, Bello is COP 1,900,000 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,712,500 to COP 2,175,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 32,643/m² and the median listed size is 56.5 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median for Bello (COP 2,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
