Rents of apartments with parking in Chácara Santo Antônio, São Paulo, Brazil — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with parking in Chácara Santo Antônio, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 6,500 per month, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 4,448 to BRL 8,118. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 86.9/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². That is 80% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,195. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
