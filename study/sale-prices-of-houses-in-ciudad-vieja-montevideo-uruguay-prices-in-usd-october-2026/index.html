Sale prices of houses in Ciudad Vieja, Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for houses for sale in Ciudad Vieja, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 290,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 196,250 to USD 441,250. The median price per square metre is USD 833/m² and the median listed size is 405 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 269,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
