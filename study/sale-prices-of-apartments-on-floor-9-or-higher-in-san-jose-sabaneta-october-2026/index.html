Sale prices of apartments on floor 9 or higher in San José, Sabaneta — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments on floor 9 or higher for sale in San José, Sabaneta is COP 580,000,000, based on 142 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 470,000,000 to COP 660,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,133,919/m² and the median listed size is 76 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 550,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 380,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 79.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
