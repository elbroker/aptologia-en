Sale prices of houses more than 20 years old in estrato 5 in Belén, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking price for houses more than 20 years old in estrato 5 for sale in Belén, Medellín is COP 867,500,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 791,250,000 to COP 1,075,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,811,680/m² and the median listed size is 208 m². That is 38% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 1,390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 403,500. 92.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
