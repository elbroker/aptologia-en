Rents of apartments with a balcony in estrato 3 in Torices, Cartagena — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony in estrato 3 in Torices, Cartagena is COP 2,688,000 per month, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
