Sale prices of homes in a gated complex in the Suba locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes in a gated complex for sale in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 1,230,000,000, based on 4,426 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 595,000,000 to COP 1,950,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,984,043/m² and the median listed size is 183 m². That is 7% above the study's reference median (COP 1,150,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 790,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 91.7% of listings include parking.
