Sale prices of homes with a gym in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with a gym for sale in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá is COP 1,000,000,000, based on 5,076 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 620,000,000 to COP 1,800,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,443,466/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 3% below the study's reference median (COP 1,030,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 995,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 94.2% of listings include parking.
