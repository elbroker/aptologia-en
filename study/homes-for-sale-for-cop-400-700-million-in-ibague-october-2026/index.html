Homes for sale for COP 400–700 million in Ibagué — October 2026

Homes listed for sale for COP 400–700 million in Ibagué have a median asking price of COP 465,000,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 420,000,000 to COP 553,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,065,693/m² and the median listed size is 147 m². That is 66% above the study's reference median (COP 280,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 397,700. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 73.5% of listings include parking.
