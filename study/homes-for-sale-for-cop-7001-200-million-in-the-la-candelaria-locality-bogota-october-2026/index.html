Homes for sale for COP 700–1,200 million in the La Candelaria locality, Bogotá — October 2026

Homes listed for sale for COP 700–1,200 million in the La Candelaria locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 900,000,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 762,500,000 to COP 1,100,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,285,714/m² and the median listed size is 175 m². That is 57% above the study's reference median (COP 575,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 890,809. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 56.2% of listings include parking.
