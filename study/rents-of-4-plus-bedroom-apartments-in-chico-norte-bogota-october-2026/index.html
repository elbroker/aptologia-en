Rents of 4+ bedroom apartments in Chico Norte, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 4+ bedroom apartments in Chico Norte, Bogotá is COP 15,500,000 per month, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 8,750,000 to COP 20,625,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 52,356/m² and the median listed size is 300 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 14,700,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,505,500. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
