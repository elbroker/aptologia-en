How much is an extra bathroom worth in a rental in Bogotá? Room and estrato difference (2026)

Machine-assisted English version of the Spanish original by Aptología. All figures are locked to the original (numbers are copied, not re-translated); if in doubt, the Spanish original prevails.

In Bogotá, an additional bathroom increases the rent 38.9% on average, in front of an apartment of the same estrato and number of rooms. In a 2 room, from 1 to 2 bath the rent 41.7% is increased within the same estrato; per m² the difference is 6.7%, because much of the premium is extra area.

Abstract: We compare 16,147 unique apartments for rent in Bogotá according to their number of bathrooms, within the same estrato and number of rooms.

Updated: 13 October of 2026 · Next update: monthly.

How to use it to decide: If you share the apartment or live in a couple, the second bathroom is usually the most noticeable cost in the day to day. If you live alone, an apartment with a bathroom of the same estrato saves you that difference every month. The difference per m² shows that much of the premium is extra area.

Methodology

  • Source: daily intake base of Aptology, with public ads of Metroocuadrado, Fincaraíz, Cienalquís, Rentola and alchem.com. Court as at 13 October of 2026.
  • Filters: live ads that pass quality control, in Bogotá, rent between $400,000 and $30,000,000. TikTok and Facebook Market are excluded.
  • Duplicate: one ad is left per group of duplicates (the same building on several portals).
  • Comparison: within each estrato × room cell the medium rental is compared with n + 1 bathrooms in front of n bathrooms (minimum 8 ads per side) and averages by weight of the smaller group size. It is reported only when there are 30 ads or more compared.
  • Limitations: are prices ordered, not closed. The neighborhoods and localities come as each portal writes.
  • Open data: datos.csv, CC BY license 4. 0.
