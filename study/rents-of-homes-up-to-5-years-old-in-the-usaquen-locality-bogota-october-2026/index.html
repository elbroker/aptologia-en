Rents of homes up to 5 years old in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes up to 5 years old in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá is COP 3,800,000 per month, based on 2,521 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,550,000 to COP 7,755,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 56,364/m² and the median listed size is 68 m². That is 17% below the study's reference median (COP 4,600,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 602,150. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 79.1% of listings include parking.
