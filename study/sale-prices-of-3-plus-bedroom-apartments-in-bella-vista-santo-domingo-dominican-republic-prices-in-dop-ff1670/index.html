Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments in Bella Vista, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic (prices in DOP) — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Bella Vista, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is DOP 10,800,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from DOP 9,500,000 to DOP 14,447,000. The median price per square metre is DOP 73,333/m² and the median listed size is 150 m². That is 35% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (DOP 8,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
