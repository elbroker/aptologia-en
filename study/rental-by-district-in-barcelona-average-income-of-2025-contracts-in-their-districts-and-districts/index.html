Rental by district in Barcelona: average income of 2025 contracts in their districts and districts

Machine-assisted English version of the Spanish original by Aptología. All figures are locked to the original (numbers are copied, not re-translated); if in doubt, the Spanish original prevails.

The Barcelona neighborhood with the highest average rent in 2025 was the Tres Torres, with 2,014 euros per month, and the lowest of the 68 neighborhoods with 30 contracts or more was Ciutat Meridiana, with 601 euros. By district, Sarrià-Sant Gervasi heads 1,604 euros (19.06 euros per m²) and Nou Barris closes with 796 euros.

Abstract: We order 68 neighborhoods and 10 districts of Barcelona according to the rental contracts registered in 2025.

Methodology

  • Source: Incasòl, Generalitat de Catalunya (bonds of rental contracts, 2025). The official tables were taken from the night exportes of Aptology of 18 October of 2026 (is _ rent _ municipality, is _ rent _ neighborhood, is _ purchase _ municipality), which collect them without changing them.
  • Statistical: average monthly and per m² income; year 2025.
  • What is measured: income from registered contracts (bonds) or official valuation values, not prices ordered in portals.
  • Minimum sample: only rows with 30 contracts or valuation or more are published.
  • Limitations: Each source uses its own methodology (mean in Catalonia, median in the Valencia Community), so they should not compare with each other without caution.
  • Open data: datos.csv, CC BY license 4. 0.
