Rents of apartments in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile is CLP 1,215,000 per month, based on 309 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from CLP 850,000 to CLP 1,700,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is CLP 16,333/m² and the median listed size is 70 m². That is 170% above the study's reference median for Santiago (CLP 450,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
