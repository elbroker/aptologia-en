Sale prices of homes in Tierra Buena, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in Tierra Buena, Bogotá is COP 190,000,000, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 170,000,000 to COP 220,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,808,511/m² and the median listed size is 51 m². That is 78% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 125,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 10% of listings include parking.
