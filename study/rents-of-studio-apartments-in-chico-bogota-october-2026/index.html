Rents of studio apartments in Chico, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for studio apartments in Chico, Bogotá is COP 3,550,000 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,787,500 to COP 4,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 94,028/m² and the median listed size is 37 m². That is 87% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,900,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 368,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 62% of listings include parking.
