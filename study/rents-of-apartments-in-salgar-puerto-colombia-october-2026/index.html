Rents of apartments in Salgar, Puerto Colombia — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Salgar, Puerto Colombia is COP 2,775,000 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,937,742 to COP 3,575,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 31,853/m² and the median listed size is 78 m². That is 10% above the study's reference median for Puerto Colombia (COP 2,529,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 393,900. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
