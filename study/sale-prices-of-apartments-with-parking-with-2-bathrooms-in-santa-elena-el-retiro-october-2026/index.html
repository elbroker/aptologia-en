Sale prices of apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms in Santa Elena, El Retiro — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms for sale in Santa Elena, El Retiro is COP 710,000,000, based on 74 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 626,868,750 to COP 790,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,468,182/m² and the median listed size is 76 m². That is 3% below the study's reference median for El Retiro (COP 730,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 400,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
