Sale prices of apartments in Cali — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Cali is COP 270,000,000, based on 155 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 239,500,000 to COP 494,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,230,769/m² and the median listed size is 64 m². That is 18% below the study's reference median (COP 330,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 259,220. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 52.3% of listings include parking.
