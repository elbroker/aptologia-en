Rents of 2-bedroom houses of 70–100 m² with parking in Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in UYU) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom houses of 70–100 m² with parking in Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 33,750 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 25,000 to UYU 44,998. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 400/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 38,500). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
