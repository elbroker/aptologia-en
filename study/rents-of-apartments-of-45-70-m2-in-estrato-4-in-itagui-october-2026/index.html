Rents of apartments of 45–70 m² in estrato 4 in Itagüí — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in estrato 4 in Itagüí is COP 2,700,000 per month, based on 179 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,450,000 to COP 3,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 42,727/m² and the median listed size is 64 m². That is 1% below the study's reference median for Itagüí (COP 2,725,000). 88% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
