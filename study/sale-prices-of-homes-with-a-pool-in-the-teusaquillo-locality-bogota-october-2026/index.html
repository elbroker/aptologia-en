Sale prices of homes with a pool in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with a pool for sale in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá is COP 1,140,000,000, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 715,000,000 to COP 2,100,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,812,558/m² and the median listed size is 102 m². That is 28% above the study's reference median (COP 892,550,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 694,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 88.2% of listings include parking.
