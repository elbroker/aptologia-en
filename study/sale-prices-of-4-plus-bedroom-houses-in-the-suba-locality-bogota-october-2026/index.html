Sale prices of 4+ bedroom houses in the Suba locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 1,700,000,000, based on 4,006 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,200,000,000 to COP 2,400,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,905,660/m² and the median listed size is 339 m². That is 6% above the study's reference median (COP 1,600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 980,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 93.6% of listings include parking.
