Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic (prices in DOP) — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom for sale in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is DOP 3,900,000, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from DOP 3,500,000 to DOP 5,767,500. The median price per square metre is DOP 53,846/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². That is 51% below the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (DOP 8,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
