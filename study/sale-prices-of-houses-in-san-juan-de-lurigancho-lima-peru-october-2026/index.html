Sale prices of houses in San Juan de Lurigancho, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for houses for sale in San Juan de Lurigancho, Lima, Peru is PEN 703,000, based on 51 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 488,500 to PEN 870,825. The median price per square metre is PEN 3,692/m² and the median listed size is 175 m². That is 68% below the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 2,173,590). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
