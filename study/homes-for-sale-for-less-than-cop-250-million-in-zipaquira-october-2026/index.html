Homes for sale for less than COP 250 million in Zipaquirá — October 2026

Homes listed for sale for less than COP 250 million in Zipaquirá have a median asking price of COP 210,000,000, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 188,000,000 to COP 222,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,542,373/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median (COP 225,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 151,118. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 17.9% of listings include parking.
