Rents of 2-bedroom apartments in Antiguo Country, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in Antiguo Country, Bogotá is COP 5,400,000 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,050,000 to COP 6,912,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 60,012/m² and the median listed size is 90 m². That is 108% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,600,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,000,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 89.5% of listings include parking.
