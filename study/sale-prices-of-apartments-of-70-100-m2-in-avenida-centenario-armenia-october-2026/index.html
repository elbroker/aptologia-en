Sale prices of apartments of 70–100 m² in Avenida Centenario, Armenia — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Avenida Centenario, Armenia is COP 550,000,000, based on 103 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 475,000,000 to COP 630,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,614,458/m² and the median listed size is 83 m². That is 45% above the study's reference median for Armenia (COP 380,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 379,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 97% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
