Sale prices of homes in Lomas de las Águilas, Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in Lomas de las Águilas, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 9,700,000, based on 49 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 8,000,000 to MXN 11,500,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 32,812/m² and the median listed size is 315 m². The median number of bathrooms is 3.
