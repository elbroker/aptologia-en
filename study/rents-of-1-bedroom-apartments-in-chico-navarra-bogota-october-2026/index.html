Rents of 1-bedroom apartments in Chicó Navarra, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in Chicó Navarra, Bogotá is COP 3,400,000 per month, based on 47 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,200,000 to COP 3,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 64,000/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is 39% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,450,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 631,250. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 91.5% of listings include parking.
