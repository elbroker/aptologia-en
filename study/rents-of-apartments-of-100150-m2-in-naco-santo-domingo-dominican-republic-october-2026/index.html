Rents of apartments of 100–150 m² in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 100–150 m² in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,600 per month, based on 88 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,300 to USD 1,800. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 12.8/m² and the median listed size is 124 m². That is 19% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,350). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
