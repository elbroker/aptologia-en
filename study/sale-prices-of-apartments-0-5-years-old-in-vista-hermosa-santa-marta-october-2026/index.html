Sale prices of apartments 0–5 years old in Vista Hermosa, Santa Marta — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments 0–5 years old for sale in Vista Hermosa, Santa Marta is COP 818,640,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 500,000,000 to COP 1,415,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,686,189/m² and the median listed size is 88 m². That is 36% above the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 850,000. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
