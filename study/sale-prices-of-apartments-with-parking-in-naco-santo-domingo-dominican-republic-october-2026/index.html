Sale prices of apartments with parking in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with parking for sale in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 330,000, based on 430 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
