Sale prices of 3+ bedroom houses with 2 bathrooms in Centro, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with 2 bathrooms for sale in Centro, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 152,500, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 119,250 to USD 218,750. The median price per square metre is USD 815/m² and the median listed size is 195 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 160,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
