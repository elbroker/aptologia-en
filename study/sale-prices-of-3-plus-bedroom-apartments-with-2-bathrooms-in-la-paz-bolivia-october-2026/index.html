Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms in La Paz, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in La Paz, Bolivia is USD 118,000, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 105,000 to USD 139,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,004/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 9% below the study's reference median for La Paz (USD 130,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 50. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
