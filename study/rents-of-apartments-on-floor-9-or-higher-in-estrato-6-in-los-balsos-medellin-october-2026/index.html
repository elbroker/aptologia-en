Rents of apartments on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 in Los Balsos, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 in Los Balsos, Medellín is COP 6,850,000 per month, based on 74 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,125,000 to COP 9,967,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 57,090/m² and the median listed size is 136 m². That is 43% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). 97.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
