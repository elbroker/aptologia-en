Rents of apartments with parking in Santiago, Chile — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with parking in Santiago, Chile is CLP 600,000 per month, based on 754 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from CLP 470,000 to CLP 850,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is CLP 10,583/m² and the median listed size is 59 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
