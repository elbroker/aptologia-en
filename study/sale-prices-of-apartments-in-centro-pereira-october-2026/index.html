Sale prices of apartments in Centro, Pereira — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Centro, Pereira is COP 380,000,000, based on 217 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 285,000,000 to COP 500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,628,319/m² and the median listed size is 110 m². That is 13% below the study's reference median for Pereira (COP 435,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 574,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 67.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
