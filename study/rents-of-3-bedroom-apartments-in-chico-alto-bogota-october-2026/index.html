Rents of 3-bedroom apartments in Chicó Alto, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3-bedroom apartments in Chicó Alto, Bogotá is COP 14,250,000 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 10,800,000 to COP 18,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,911/m² and the median listed size is 288 m². That is 119% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 6,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,361,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 97.4% of listings include parking.
