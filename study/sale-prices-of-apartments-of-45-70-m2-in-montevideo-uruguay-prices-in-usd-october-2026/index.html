Sale prices of apartments of 45–70 m² in Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 164,900, based on 1,890 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
