Sale prices of 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more in Manga, Cartagena — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more for sale in Manga, Cartagena is COP 2,350,000,000, based on 90 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,350,000,000 to COP 4,325,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,973,822/m² and the median listed size is 402 m². That is 81% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 1,300,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 85.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
