Rents of homes 6–15 years old in the Suba locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes 6–15 years old in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 4,504,000 per month, based on 495 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,246,000 to COP 10,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 34,067/m² and the median listed size is 112 m². That is 46% above the study's reference median (COP 3,079,400). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 758,500. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 80.6% of listings include parking.
