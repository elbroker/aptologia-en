Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² in Zipaquirá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² in Zipaquirá is COP 1,200,000 per month, based on 131 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,100,000 to COP 1,400,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 21,487/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median for Zipaquirá (COP 1,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 196,250. The most frequent estrato is 3, 72% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
