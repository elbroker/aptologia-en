Rents of houses of 250 m² or more in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses of 250 m² or more in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 6,750 per month, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 4,325 to USD 13,875. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 17.9/m² and the median listed size is 380 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 7,300). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
