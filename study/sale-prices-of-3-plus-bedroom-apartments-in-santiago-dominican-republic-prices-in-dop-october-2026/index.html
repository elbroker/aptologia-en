Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments in Santiago, Dominican Republic (prices in DOP) — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Santiago, Dominican Republic is DOP 7,450,000, based on 64 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from DOP 5,837,500 to DOP 8,500,000. The median price per square metre is DOP 66,098/m² and the median listed size is 110 m². That is 9% above the study's reference median for Santiago (DOP 6,837,500). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
