Rents of homes in estrato 3 in Chapinero Central, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 3 in Chapinero Central, Bogotá is COP 1,800,000 per month, based on 92 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,472,500 to COP 2,300,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 54,412/m² and the median listed size is 31 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median (COP 1,900,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 288,600. 26.1% of listings include parking.
