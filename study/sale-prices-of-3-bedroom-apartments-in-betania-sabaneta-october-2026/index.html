Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments in Betania, Sabaneta — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Betania, Sabaneta is COP 519,000,000, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 367,500,000 to COP 750,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,285,714/m² and the median listed size is 92 m². That is 11% below the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 580,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 260,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 62.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
