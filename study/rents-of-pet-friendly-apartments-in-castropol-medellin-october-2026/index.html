Rents of pet-friendly apartments in Castropol, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for pet-friendly apartments in Castropol, Medellín is COP 5,650,000 per month, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,900,000 to COP 7,250,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 52,500/m² and the median listed size is 100 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
