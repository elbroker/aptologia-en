Rents of homes in estrato 3 in the Bosa locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 3 in the Bosa locality, Bogotá is COP 1,200,000 per month, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,000,000 to COP 1,342,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 27,208/m² and the median listed size is 44.5 m². That is 9% above the study's reference median (COP 1,100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 120,000. 16.1% of listings include parking.
