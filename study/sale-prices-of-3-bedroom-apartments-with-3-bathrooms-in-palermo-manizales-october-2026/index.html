Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Palermo, Manizales — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Palermo, Manizales is COP 650,000,000, based on 161 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 550,000,000 to COP 850,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,571,717/m² and the median listed size is 151 m². That is 44% above the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 450,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
