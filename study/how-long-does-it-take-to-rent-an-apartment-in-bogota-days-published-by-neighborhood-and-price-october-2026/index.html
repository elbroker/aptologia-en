How long does it take to rent an apartment in Bogotá? Days published by neighborhood and price (October 2026)

Machine-assisted English version of the Spanish original by Aptología. All figures are locked to the original (numbers are copied, not re-translated); if in doubt, the Spanish original prevails.

An apartment for rent in Bogotá has been published in the medium 32 days, and 3 of each 10 have been more than 60. The price is heavy: below $1.5 million the median is 25 days and above $7 million, 62 days (half takes more than two months). The 2 rooms are the ones that last the least published (27 days).

Abstract: 11,618 active apartments of Fincaraíz and Cienbícas with date of publication, without duplicates, cut to 12 October of 2026.

Updated: 12 October of 2026 · Next update: monthly.

What it measures and what it doesn't: We measure how many days each ad that is still active (the age of the announcement) has been published, not the exact time until the contract is signed. It is the best indicator available today: our intake began on 9 October of 2026 and still does not have enough closed ads to measure time until leases. We'll update it with that data. The ads that are rented quickly come out before the sample, so these figures exaggerate some real time; they serve to compare neighborhoods and prices to each other.

How to use it to decide: If you are looking, an apartment that has been published for more than 60 days usually has room to negotiate the rent. If you rent yours, compare to your neighborhood and your price range: if you have much more than the median, the price is probably high.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to rent an apartment in Bogotá? The active apartments have been published 32 days in the medium (cut to 12 October of 2026); 29% has been published for more than 60 days. It's the age of active ads, not the exact time until the contract.

Are cheap apartments rented faster? Yeah. Under $1.5 million the median is 25 days; between $4 and $7 million, 44; above $7 million, 62.

Methodology

  • Source: daily intake base of Aptology, with public ads of Metroocuadrado, Fincaraíz, Cienalquís, Rentola and alchem.com. Court as at 12 October of 2026.
  • Filters: live ads that pass quality control, only apartments in Bogotá, rent between $400,000 and $30,000,000. TikTok and Facebook Market are excluded.
  • Duplicate: one ad is left per group of duplicates (the same building on several portals). There are 21,042 unique apartments.
  • Days published: cut-off date minus the publication date shown by the portal (Fincaraíz and Cienquítes; Metroocadrado does not publish it). Discard ages greater than 730 days. If the advertiser republishes, the date is reopened.
  • Limitations: are prices ordered, not closed. The neighborhoods come as each portal writes, so the same sector can appear with two names. Only groups with at least 20 ads are published.
  • Open data: datos.csv, CC BY license 4. 0.
