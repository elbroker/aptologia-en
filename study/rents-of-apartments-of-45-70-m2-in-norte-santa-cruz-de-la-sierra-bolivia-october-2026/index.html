Rents of apartments of 45–70 m² in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 420 per month, based on 65 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 350 to USD 500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 7.98/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is 26% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 570). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 50. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
