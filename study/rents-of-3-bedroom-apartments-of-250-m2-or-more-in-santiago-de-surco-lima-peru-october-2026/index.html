Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments of 250 m² or more in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 250 m² or more in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is PEN 8,740 per month, based on 79 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 6,584 to PEN 10,291. The median monthly rent per square metre is PEN 25.1/m² and the median listed size is 325 m². That is 187% above the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 3,050). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
