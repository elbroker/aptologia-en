Sale prices of 3+ bedroom houses with 2 bathrooms in Villa Urquiza, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with 2 bathrooms for sale in Villa Urquiza, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 329,500, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
