Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments in Cedofeita, Santo Ildefonso, Sé, Miragaia, São Nicolau e Vitória, Oporto, Portugal — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Cedofeita, Santo Ildefonso, Sé, Miragaia, São Nicolau e Vitória, Oporto, Portugal is EUR 490,000, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 409,000 to EUR 592,000. The median price per square metre is EUR 5,647/m² and the median listed size is 85 m². That is 14% above the study's reference median for Oporto (EUR 430,875). 50.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
