Rents of 2-bedroom apartments in Chicó Reservado, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in Chicó Reservado, Bogotá is COP 9,000,000 per month, based on 88 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 7,500,000 to COP 11,125,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 78,261/m² and the median listed size is 110 m². That is 246% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 2,600,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,375,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
