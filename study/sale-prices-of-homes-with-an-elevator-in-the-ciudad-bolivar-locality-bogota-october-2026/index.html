Sale prices of homes with an elevator in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with an elevator for sale in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá is COP 231,000,000, based on 157 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 219,000,000 to COP 257,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,081,144/m² and the median listed size is 47 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median (COP 241,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 151,909. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 21.7% of listings include parking.
