Sale prices of apartments with 2 bathrooms in Surquillo, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Surquillo, Lima, Peru is PEN 480,000, based on 232 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 418,325 to PEN 556,657. The median price per square metre is PEN 7,145/m² and the median listed size is 68 m². That is 23% below the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 621,600). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PEN 1. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
