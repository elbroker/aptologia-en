Sale prices of apartments in Centro Internacional, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Centro Internacional, Bogotá is COP 599,000,000, based on 99 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 459,000,000 to COP 770,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,283,044/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 480,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 69.7% of listings include parking.
