Sale prices of apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is PEN 841,875, based on 136 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 732,900 to PEN 948,125. The median price per square metre is PEN 6,913/m² and the median listed size is 119 m². That is 35% above the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 621,600). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
