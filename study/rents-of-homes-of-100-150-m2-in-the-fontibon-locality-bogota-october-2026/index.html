Rents of homes of 100–150 m² in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes of 100–150 m² in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá is COP 3,794,723 per month, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,100,000 to COP 4,650,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 33,333/m² and the median listed size is 110 m². That is 107% above the study's reference median (COP 1,833,850). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 591,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 74.3% of listings include parking.
