Sale prices of apartments in Ismael Perdomo, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Ismael Perdomo, Bogotá is COP 230,000,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 210,500,000 to COP 250,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,495,455/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 70% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 148,838. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 17.6% of listings include parking.
