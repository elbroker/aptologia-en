Frequently asked questions

What is the best neighborhood to rent in Bogotá for price? Depends on the estrato. For space, parking and rooms by weight: Mirandela (estrato 3), Santa Teresa (estrato 4), La Calleja (estrato 5) and Santa Barbara Central (estrato 6), with cut to 12 October of 2026.

Which estrato neighborhood 3 gives more meters for the rent? Mirandela: 33.6 m² for each million rent, with medium rental of $1,500,000.

Which estrato neighborhood 4 is most suitable? Santa Teresa and Cedritos head the index; in Santa Teresa the 76% of the apartments have a parking lot.

How is the price / quality index calculated? Within each estrato, each neighborhood receives a percentile in three sizes: square meters per million rental (50%), percentage of apartments with parking lot (25%) and rooms per million rental (25%). 100 is the best of the estrato.

Does the index include safety or transport? No. Just measure what the ads bring: price, area, rooms and parking.

Methodology

  • Source: daily intake base of Aptology, with public ads of Metroocuadrado, Fincaraíz, Cienalquís, Rentola and alchem.com. Court as at 12 October of 2026.
  • Filters: live ads that pass quality control, only apartments in Bogotá, rent between $400,000 and $30,000,000. TikTok and Facebook Market are excluded.
  • Duplicate: one ad is left per group of duplicates (the same building on several portals). There are 21,042 unique apartments.
  • Contents: only neighborhoods with at least 30 ads with area (20 to 400 m²), rooms and estrato 2 to 6. The estrato of the neighborhood is the most common among its ads.
  • Limitations: are prices ordered, not closed. The neighborhoods come as each portal writes, so the same sector can appear with two names. Only groups with at least 20 ads are published.
  • Open data: datos.csv, CC BY license 4. 0.
