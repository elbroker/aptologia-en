How many years of minimum wage does an apartment cost? Ranking by city 2026

Machine-assisted English version of the Spanish original by Aptología. All figures are locked to the original (numbers are copied, not re-translated); if in doubt, the Spanish original prevails.

In Bogotá, an apartment of 2 alcobas costs 30.2 years of full minimum wage: the median price requested is $634 million, according to the sales ads of 5 October of 2026, and the minimum wage of 2026 is $1,750,905 (Decree 1469 of 2025), In Cartagena they are 37.1 years old and in Cali, the cheapest of the eight cities measured, 12.6.

Abstract: In none of the eight cities with sufficient data an apartment of 2 rooms costs less than 10 years of minimum wage. The rise of 23% of the minimum by 2026 cut between 2.9 and 8.6 years, according to the city, but did not change the ranking.

Updated: 9 October of 2026.

Prices ordered, not closing. The Aptology figures are medium of the price requested in the announcements published on 5 October of 2026, not what is finally paid or signed.

What the data say

  • No low city of 10 years. The starting hypothesis was "more than 10 years in expensive cities." The data go further: the eight cities exceed 10 years.
  • Cartagena is the most expensive measure in minimum wagesabove Bogotá. Your offer for sale includes a lot of tourist or second-residence housing (see limitations).
  • Measures against the real income of each city, Cartagena gets worse: the average income per person there is $1,107,779 per month (DANE, 2025), so the same apartment costs 58.7 years of that income. In Bogotá, with a higher income ($2,297,080), it is 23 years.
  • The initial rate is also far away: saving 30% of the minimum wage, bringing together an initial rate of 30% of 2 alcobs in Bogotá ($190.2 million) takes 30.2 years.

Methodology

  • Aptology data: average price ordered in apartment sales ads 2, by neighborhood (open Aptology API, 5 October of 2026, file rentabilidad_arriendo_venta_barrio.csv). For each city we take the median of these medium-sized by neighborhood, weighted by the number of ads. Only cities with at least 300 sales ads of 2 alcobs enter: Cartagena, Bogotá, Medellín, Rionegro, Barranquilla, Bucaramanga, Chia and Cali.
  • Public data: minimum wage of 2026 ($1,750,905, Decree 1469 of 2025, ratified by Decree 159 of 2026) and 2025 ($1,423,500). Per capita income of the unit of expenditure 2025 by capital city (DANE, GEIH, monetary poverty annex, published on 12 June 2026).
  • Formula: years = price ¬ (monthly income × 12).
  • Update: update reread the Aptology dataset and the DANE annex and regenerate datos.csv. calculator / prices _ per _ ciud.json and the charts. Every January, the new minimum wage must be put to the hand in the script.

Limitations

  • They are prices ordered in ads, no closing prices or avalanos, and mix new and used housing.
  • The median per city is calculated from the median per neighborhood, not from individual ads. In addition, it only counts neighborhoods that also have rental ads.
  • The offer in oversized portals represents the medium and high segments. The price is not that of a VIS housing: the VIS ceiling is measured in minimum wages and is not compared here.
  • DANE's income is a average per person (not the median) of 2025, while prices are 2026. Rionegro and Chia have no DANE entry data.
  • The calculation does not include interest on credit, notarial expenses or subsidies.
