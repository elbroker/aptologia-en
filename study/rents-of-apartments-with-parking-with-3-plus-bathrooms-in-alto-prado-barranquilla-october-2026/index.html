Rents of apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Alto Prado, Barranquilla — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Alto Prado, Barranquilla is COP 5,203,140 per month, based on 88 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,950,000 to COP 8,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 30,385/m² and the median listed size is 180 m². That is 86% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,075,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
