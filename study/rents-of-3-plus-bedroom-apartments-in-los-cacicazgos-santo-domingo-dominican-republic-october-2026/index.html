Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments in Los Cacicazgos, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Los Cacicazgos, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 1,850 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,450 to USD 3,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 10/m² and the median listed size is 210 m². That is 3% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 1,800). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
