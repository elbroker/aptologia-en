Rents of 3+ bedroom pet-friendly apartments in Madrid — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom pet-friendly apartments in Madrid is COP 1,200,000 per month, based on 71 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,040,000 to COP 1,300,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 20,968/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 1,200,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 183,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
