Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments in Lisboa, Portugal — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Lisboa, Portugal is EUR 2,300 per month, based on 195 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 1,900 to EUR 2,985. The median monthly rent per square metre is EUR 20.4/m² and the median listed size is 119 m². That is 21% above the study's reference median for Lisboa (EUR 1,900). 46.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
