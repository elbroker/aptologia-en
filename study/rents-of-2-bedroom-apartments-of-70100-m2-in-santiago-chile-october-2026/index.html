Rents of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Santiago, Chile — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Santiago, Chile is CLP 950,000 per month, based on 199 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from CLP 600,000 to CLP 1,400,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is CLP 11,765/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². That is 111% above the study's reference median for Santiago (CLP 450,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
