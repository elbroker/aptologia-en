Sale prices of homes with a gym in the Bosa locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with a gym for sale in the Bosa locality, Bogotá is COP 215,000,000, based on 87 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 195,000,000 to COP 255,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,523,810/m² and the median listed size is 48 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median (COP 224,750,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 140,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 32.2% of listings include parking.
