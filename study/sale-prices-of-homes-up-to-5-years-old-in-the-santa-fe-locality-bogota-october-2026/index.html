Sale prices of homes up to 5 years old in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes up to 5 years old for sale in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá is COP 420,000,000, based on 469 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 305,000,000 to COP 719,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,480,432/m² and the median listed size is 46.5 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median (COP 454,982,760). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 300,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 43.7% of listings include parking.
