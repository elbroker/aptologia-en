Sale prices of homes with security or a doorman in Los Patios — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with security or a doorman for sale in Los Patios is COP 390,000,000, based on 66 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 276,250,000 to COP 850,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,799,346/m² and the median listed size is 106 m². That is 3% above the study's reference median (COP 380,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 360,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 89.4% of listings include parking.
