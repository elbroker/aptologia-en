Sale prices of apartments of 150–250 m² in estrato 5 in Barranquilla — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² in estrato 5 for sale in Barranquilla is COP 650,000,000, based on 363 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 520,000,000 to COP 800,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,550,150/m² and the median listed size is 178 m². That is 44% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 862,500. 98.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
