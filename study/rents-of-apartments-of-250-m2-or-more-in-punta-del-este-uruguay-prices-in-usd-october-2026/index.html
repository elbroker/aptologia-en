Rents of apartments of 250 m² or more in Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 250 m² or more in Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 10,000 per month, based on 128 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 6,000 to USD 16,125. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 29.7/m² and the median listed size is 310 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 2,200. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
