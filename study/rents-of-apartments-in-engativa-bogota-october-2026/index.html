Rents of apartments in Engativá, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Engativá, Bogotá is COP 1,650,000 per month, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,300,000 to COP 2,175,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 30,000/m² and the median listed size is 54.5 m². That is 53% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 194,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 28.6% of listings include parking.
