Rents of apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado is COP 6,500,000 per month, based on 184 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,500,000 to COP 8,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 51,250/m² and the median listed size is 131 m². That is 48% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 4,400,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 97.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
