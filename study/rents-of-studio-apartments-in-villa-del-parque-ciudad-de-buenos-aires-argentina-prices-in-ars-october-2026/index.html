Rents of studio apartments in Villa del Parque, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in ARS) — October 2026

The median asking rent for studio apartments in Villa del Parque, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is ARS 600,000 per month, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
