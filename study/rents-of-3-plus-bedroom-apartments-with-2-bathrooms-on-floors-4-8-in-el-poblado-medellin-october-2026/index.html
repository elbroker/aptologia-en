Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms on floors 4–8 in El Poblado, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms on floors 4–8 in El Poblado, Medellín is COP 5,500,000 per month, based on 67 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,290,000 to COP 6,400,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,735/m² and the median listed size is 96 m². That is 15% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 92.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
