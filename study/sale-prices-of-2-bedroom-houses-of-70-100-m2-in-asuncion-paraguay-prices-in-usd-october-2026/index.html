Sale prices of 2-bedroom houses of 70–100 m² in Asunción, Paraguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom houses of 70–100 m² for sale in Asunción, Paraguay is USD 148,950, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 134,612 to USD 178,499. The median price per square metre is USD 1,821/m² and the median listed size is 80.5 m². That is 57% below the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 350,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
