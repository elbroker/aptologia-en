Rents of 1-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom in São Paulo, Brazil — October 2026

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom in São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 5,555 per month, based on 92 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 3,500 to BRL 9,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 74.3/m² and the median listed size is 74.5 m². That is 53% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,250. 90.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
