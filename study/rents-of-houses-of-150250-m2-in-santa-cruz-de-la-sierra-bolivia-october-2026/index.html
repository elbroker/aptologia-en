Rents of houses of 150–250 m² in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses of 150–250 m² in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 900 per month, based on 222 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 653 to USD 1,348. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 4.5/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
