Sale prices of houses with 3+ bathrooms in Parque Batlle, Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Parque Batlle, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 599,496, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 402,500 to USD 812,500. The median price per square metre is USD 2,219/m² and the median listed size is 285 m². That is 123% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 269,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
