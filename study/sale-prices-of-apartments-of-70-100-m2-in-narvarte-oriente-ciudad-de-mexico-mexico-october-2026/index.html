Sale prices of apartments of 70–100 m² in Narvarte Oriente, Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Narvarte Oriente, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 4,800,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 4,295,000 to MXN 5,631,500. The median price per square metre is MXN 62,779/m² and the median listed size is 82 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 4,990,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
