Rents of 3-bedroom apartments in the Suba locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3-bedroom apartments in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 3,390,000 per month, based on 513 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,400,000 to COP 4,504,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 37,884/m² and the median listed size is 88 m². That is 41% above the study's reference median (COP 2,400,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 580,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 86% of listings include parking.
