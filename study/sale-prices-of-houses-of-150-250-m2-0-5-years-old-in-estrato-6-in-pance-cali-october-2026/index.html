Sale prices of houses of 150–250 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 6 in Pance, Cali — October 2026

The median asking price for houses of 150–250 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Pance, Cali is COP 1,485,000,000, based on 74 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
