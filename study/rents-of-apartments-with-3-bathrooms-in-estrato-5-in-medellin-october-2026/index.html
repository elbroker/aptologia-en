Rents of apartments with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 5 in Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 5 in Medellín is COP 5,500,000 per month, based on 1,952 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,400,000 to COP 7,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 42,508/m² and the median listed size is 128 m². That is 15% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 627,000. 86.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
