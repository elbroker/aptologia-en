Sale prices of apartments on floors 1–3 in estrato 6 in Santa Paula, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments on floors 1–3 in estrato 6 for sale in Santa Paula, Bogotá is COP 860,000,000, based on 101 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 700,000,000 to COP 1,290,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,955,513/m² and the median listed size is 122 m². That is 15% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,049,000. 98% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
