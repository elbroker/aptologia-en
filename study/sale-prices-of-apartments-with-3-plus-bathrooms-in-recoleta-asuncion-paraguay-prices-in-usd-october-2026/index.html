Sale prices of apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Recoleta, Asunción, Paraguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Recoleta, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 202,778, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
