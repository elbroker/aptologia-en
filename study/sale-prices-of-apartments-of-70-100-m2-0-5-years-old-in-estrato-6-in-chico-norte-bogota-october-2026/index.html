Sale prices of apartments of 70–100 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 6 in Chicó Norte, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² 0–5 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Chicó Norte, Bogotá is COP 915,000,000, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
