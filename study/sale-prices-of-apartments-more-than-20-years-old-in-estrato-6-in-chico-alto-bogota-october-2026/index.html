Sale prices of apartments more than 20 years old in estrato 6 in Chicó Alto, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments more than 20 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Chicó Alto, Bogotá is COP 1,935,000,000, based on 104 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,497,500,000 to COP 2,723,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,739,410/m² and the median listed size is 271 m². That is 158% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,000,000. 99% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
