Sale prices of homes in Barranquilla — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in Barranquilla is COP 262,000,000, based on 95 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 187,000,000 to COP 436,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,805,714/m² and the median listed size is 64 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 221,147. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 51.6% of listings include parking.
