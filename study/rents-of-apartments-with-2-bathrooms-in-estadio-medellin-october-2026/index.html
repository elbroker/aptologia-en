Rents of apartments with 2 bathrooms in Estadio, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with 2 bathrooms in Estadio, Medellín is COP 3,100,000 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,600,000 to COP 3,650,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 38,877/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². That is 35% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 62.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
