Homes for rent for COP 2.5–4 million a month in the Puente Aranda locality, Bogotá — October 2026

Homes listed for rent for COP 2.5–4 million a month in the Puente Aranda locality, Bogotá have a median asking rent of COP 2,800,000 per month, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,527,300 to COP 3,250,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 42,667/m² and the median listed size is 65 m². That is 56% above the study's reference median (COP 1,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 269,500. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 60.5% of listings include parking.
