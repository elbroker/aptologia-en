Sale prices of houses of 100–150 m² in Villa Devoto, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for houses of 100–150 m² for sale in Villa Devoto, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 277,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 219,675 to USD 306,250. The median price per square metre is USD 2,180/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 290,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
