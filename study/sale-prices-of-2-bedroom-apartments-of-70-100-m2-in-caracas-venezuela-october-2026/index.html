Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Caracas, Venezuela — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Caracas, Venezuela is USD 135,000, based on 114 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 95,000 to USD 165,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,621/m² and the median listed size is 83 m². That is 44% below the study's reference median for Caracas (USD 240,000). 87.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
