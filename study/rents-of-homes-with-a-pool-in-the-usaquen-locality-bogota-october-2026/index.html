Rents of homes with a pool in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes with a pool in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá is COP 9,500,000 per month, based on 353 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,800,000 to COP 16,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 54,545/m² and the median listed size is 160 m². That is 107% above the study's reference median (COP 4,600,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,415,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 96.6% of listings include parking.
