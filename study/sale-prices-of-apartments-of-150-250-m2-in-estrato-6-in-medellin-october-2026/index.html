Sale prices of apartments of 150–250 m² in estrato 6 in Medellín — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² in estrato 6 for sale in Medellín is COP 1,475,000,000, based on 2,843 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,210,000,000 to COP 1,801,587,390. The median price per square metre is COP 7,901,235/m² and the median listed size is 190 m². That is 76% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,100,000. 98.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
