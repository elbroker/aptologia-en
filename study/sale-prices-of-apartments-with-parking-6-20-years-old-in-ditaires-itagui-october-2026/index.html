Sale prices of apartments with parking 6–20 years old in Ditaires, Itagüí — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with parking 6–20 years old for sale in Ditaires, Itagüí is COP 445,000,000, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 365,000,000 to COP 505,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,666,667/m² and the median listed size is 69 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median for Itagüí (COP 470,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 325,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
