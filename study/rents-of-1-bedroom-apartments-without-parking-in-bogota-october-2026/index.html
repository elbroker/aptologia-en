Rents of 1-bedroom apartments without parking in Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments without parking in Bogotá is COP 1,800,000 per month, based on 1,008 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,500,000 to COP 2,300,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 57,353/m² and the median listed size is 31 m². That is 45% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 250,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
