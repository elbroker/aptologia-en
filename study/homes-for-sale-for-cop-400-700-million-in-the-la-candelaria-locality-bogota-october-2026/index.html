Homes for sale for COP 400–700 million in the La Candelaria locality, Bogotá — October 2026

Homes listed for sale for COP 400–700 million in the La Candelaria locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 520,000,000, based on 65 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 455,000,000 to COP 599,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,545,455/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is 10% below the study's reference median (COP 575,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 580,500. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 81.5% of listings include parking.
