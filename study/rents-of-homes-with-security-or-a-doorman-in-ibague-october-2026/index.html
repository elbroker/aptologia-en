Rents of homes with security or a doorman in Ibagué — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes with security or a doorman in Ibagué is COP 995,000 per month, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 740,000 to COP 1,359,150. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 18,446/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 999,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 216,200. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 58.1% of listings include parking.
