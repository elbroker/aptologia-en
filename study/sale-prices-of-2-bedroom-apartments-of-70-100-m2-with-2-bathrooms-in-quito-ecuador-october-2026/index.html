Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms in Quito, Ecuador — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Quito, Ecuador is USD 131,616, based on 237 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 98,000 to USD 165,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,510/m² and the median listed size is 86 m². That is 9% below the study's reference median for Quito (USD 145,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 98. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
