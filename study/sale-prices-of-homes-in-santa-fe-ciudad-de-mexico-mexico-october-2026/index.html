Sale prices of homes in Santa Fe, Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in Santa Fe, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 8,216,000, based on 481 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 5,500,000 to MXN 13,999,999. The median price per square metre is MXN 55,385/m² and the median listed size is 145 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
