Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms in Quito, Ecuador — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Quito, Ecuador is USD 110,000, based on 666 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 77,249 to USD 158,975. The median price per square metre is USD 870/m² and the median listed size is 125 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Quito (USD 145,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 60. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
