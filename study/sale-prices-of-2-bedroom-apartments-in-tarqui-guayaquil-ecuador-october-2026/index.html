Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments in Tarqui, Guayaquil, Ecuador — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Tarqui, Guayaquil, Ecuador is USD 140,000, based on 153 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 108,000 to USD 185,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,406/m² and the median listed size is 95 m². That is 10% below the study's reference median for Guayaquil (USD 155,250). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
