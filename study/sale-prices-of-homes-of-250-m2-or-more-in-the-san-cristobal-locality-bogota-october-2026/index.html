Sale prices of homes of 250 m² or more in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes of 250 m² or more for sale in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá is COP 715,000,000, based on 118 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 449,250,000 to COP 950,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 2,037,037/m² and the median listed size is 330 m². That is 124% above the study's reference median (COP 319,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 10,556. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 53.4% of listings include parking.
