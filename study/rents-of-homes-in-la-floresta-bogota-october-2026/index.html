Rents of homes in La Floresta, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in La Floresta, Bogotá is COP 2,450,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,162,500 to COP 4,975,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 49,510/m² and the median listed size is 52.5 m². That is 30% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,497,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 501,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 63.3% of listings include parking.
