Rents of furnished apartments in estrato 5 in El Poblado, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for furnished apartments in estrato 5 in El Poblado, Medellín is COP 7,125,000 per month, based on 178 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,300,000 to COP 9,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 71,429/m² and the median listed size is 88.5 m². That is 48% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). 75.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
