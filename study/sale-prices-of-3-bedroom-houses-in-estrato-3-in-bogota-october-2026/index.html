Sale prices of 3-bedroom houses in estrato 3 in Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for 3-bedroom houses in estrato 3 for sale in Bogotá is COP 550,000,000, based on 777 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 399,000,000 to COP 750,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,587,219/m² and the median listed size is 118 m². That is 27% below the study's reference median (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 230,000. 64.9% of listings include parking.
