Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms in Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms for sale in Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 390,000, based on 165 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
