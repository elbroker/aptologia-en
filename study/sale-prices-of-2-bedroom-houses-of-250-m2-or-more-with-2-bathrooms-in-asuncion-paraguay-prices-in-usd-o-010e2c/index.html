Sale prices of 2-bedroom houses of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms in Asunción, Paraguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom houses of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms for sale in Asunción, Paraguay is USD 285,000, based on 64 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

That is 19% below the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 350,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
