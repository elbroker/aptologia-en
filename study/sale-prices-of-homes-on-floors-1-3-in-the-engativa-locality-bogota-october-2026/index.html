Sale prices of homes on floors 1–3 in the Engativá locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes on floors 1–3 for sale in the Engativá locality, Bogotá is COP 495,000,000, based on 588 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 325,000,000 to COP 750,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,821,918/m² and the median listed size is 93.5 m². That is 9% below the study's reference median (COP 545,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 210,150. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 67% of listings include parking.
