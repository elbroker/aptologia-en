Homes for rent for less than COP 1.5 million a month in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá — October 2026

Homes listed for rent for less than COP 1.5 million a month in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá have a median asking rent of COP 1,200,000 per month, based on 91 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,064,400 to COP 1,350,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 27,264/m² and the median listed size is 42.5 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median (COP 1,250,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 148,100. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 6.6% of listings include parking.
