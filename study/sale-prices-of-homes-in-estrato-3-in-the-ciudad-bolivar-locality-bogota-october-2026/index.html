Sale prices of homes in estrato 3 in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes in estrato 3 for sale in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá is COP 240,000,000, based on 293 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
