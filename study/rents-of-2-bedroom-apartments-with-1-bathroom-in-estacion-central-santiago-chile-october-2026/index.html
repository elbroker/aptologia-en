Rents of 2-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom in Estación Central, Santiago, Chile — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom in Estación Central, Santiago, Chile is CLP 350,000 per month, based on 99 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
