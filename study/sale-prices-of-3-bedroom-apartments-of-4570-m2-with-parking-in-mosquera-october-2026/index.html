Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking in Mosquera — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking for sale in Mosquera is COP 316,000,000, based on 196 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 266,000,000 to COP 350,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,357,599/m² and the median listed size is 59 m². That is 1% below the study's reference median for Mosquera (COP 320,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 271,250. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
