Rental by municipality in the Comunitat Valenciana: the most expensive and the cheapest according to the contracts of 2025

Machine-assisted English version of the Spanish original by Aptología. All figures are locked to the original (numbers are copied, not re-translated); if in doubt, the Spanish original prevails.

The highest median rental income of the Valencia Community in 2025 was paid in San Antonio de Benagéber (Valencia), with 1,700 euros per month, and the lowest of 119 municipalities with 30 contracts or more in Utiel (Valencia), with 460 euros. In Valencia, the municipality with the most contracts (11,322), the median income was 1,000 euros per month.

Abstract: We order 119 municipalities of the Comunitat Valenciana with 30 rental contracts or more registered in 2025 (33,611 contracts).

Methodology

  • Source: Generalitat Valenciana (lease bonds, 2025). The official tables were taken from the night exportes of Aptology of 18 October of 2026 (is _ rent _ municipality, is _ rent _ neighborhood, is _ purchase _ municipality), which collect them without changing them.
  • Statistical: average monthly income per contract; full year 2025.
  • What is measured: income from registered contracts (bonds) or official valuation values, not prices ordered in portals.
  • Minimum sample: only rows with 30 contracts or valuation or more are published.
  • Limitations: Each source uses its own methodology (mean in Catalonia, median in the Valencia Community), so they should not compare with each other without caution.
  • Open data: datos.csv, CC BY license 4. 0.
