Sale prices of apartments with 2 bathrooms in Renacimiento, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic (prices in DOP) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Renacimiento, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is DOP 9,900,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
