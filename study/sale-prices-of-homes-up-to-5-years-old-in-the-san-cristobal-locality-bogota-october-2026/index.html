Sale prices of homes up to 5 years old in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes up to 5 years old for sale in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá is COP 295,000,000, based on 270 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 218,000,000 to COP 557,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,791,810/m² and the median listed size is 71 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median (COP 319,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 157,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 43% of listings include parking.
