Rents of 2-bedroom apartments in La Blanqueada, Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in UYU) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in La Blanqueada, Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 33,800 per month, based on 92 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 30,975 to UYU 37,225. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 587/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 33,200). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 5,225. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
