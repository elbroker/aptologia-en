Rents of homes in Tarqui, Guayaquil, Ecuador — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in Tarqui, Guayaquil, Ecuador is USD 730 per month, based on 557 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 500 to USD 1,165. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 6.82/m² and the median listed size is 97 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 50. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
