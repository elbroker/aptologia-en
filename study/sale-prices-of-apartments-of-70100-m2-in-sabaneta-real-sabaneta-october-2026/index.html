Sale prices of apartments of 70–100 m² in Sabaneta Real, Sabaneta — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Sabaneta Real, Sabaneta is COP 580,000,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 512,500,000 to COP 668,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,135,208/m² and the median listed size is 83.5 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 550,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 355,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
