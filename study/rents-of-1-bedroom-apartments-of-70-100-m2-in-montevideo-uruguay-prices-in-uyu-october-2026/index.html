Rents of 1-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in UYU) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 29,000 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 25,200 to UYU 36,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 395/m² and the median listed size is 76 m². That is 3% below the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 30,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 3,950. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
