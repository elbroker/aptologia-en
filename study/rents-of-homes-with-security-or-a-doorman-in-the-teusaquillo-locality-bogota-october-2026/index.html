Rents of homes with security or a doorman in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes with security or a doorman in the Teusaquillo locality, Bogotá is COP 2,800,000 per month, based on 446 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,900,000 to COP 3,700,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 57,321/m² and the median listed size is 45 m². That is 1% above the study's reference median (COP 2,769,500). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 364,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 59% of listings include parking.
