Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments more than 20 years old in La Cabrera, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments more than 20 years old for sale in La Cabrera, Bogotá is COP 2,490,000,000, based on 93 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,700,000,000 to COP 3,100,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,573,882/m² and the median listed size is 257 m². That is 232% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,200,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 97.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
