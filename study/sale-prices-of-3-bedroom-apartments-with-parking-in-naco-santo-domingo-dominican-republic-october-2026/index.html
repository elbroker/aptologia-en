Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments with parking in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking for sale in Naco, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 558,500, based on 198 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 420,500 to USD 708,918. The median price per square metre is USD 2,447/m² and the median listed size is 241 m². That is 133% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
