Rents of apartments with 3+ bathrooms in San Isidro, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with 3+ bathrooms in San Isidro, Lima, Peru is PEN 7,650 per month, based on 176 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 4,981 to PEN 11,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is PEN 34.2/m² and the median listed size is 214 m². That is 151% above the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 3,050). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PEN 800. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
