Sale prices of apartments of 150–250 m² 0–5 years old in Ciudad Jardín, Cali — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² 0–5 years old for sale in Ciudad Jardín, Cali is COP 1,180,000,000, based on 88 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 850,000,000 to COP 1,407,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,540,225/m² and the median listed size is 171 m². That is 174% above the study's reference median for Cali (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,050,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
