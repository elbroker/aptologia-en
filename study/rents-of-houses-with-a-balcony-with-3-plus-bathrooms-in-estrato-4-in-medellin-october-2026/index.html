Rents of houses with a balcony with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 4 in Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses with a balcony with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 4 in Medellín is COP 6,080,000 per month, based on 240 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,500,000 to COP 9,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 30,283/m² and the median listed size is 220 m². That is 28% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 8,500,000). 75% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
