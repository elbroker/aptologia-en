Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Pereira — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Pereira is COP 530,000,000, based on 432 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 420,000,000 to COP 640,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,530,556/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 22% above the study's reference median for Pereira (COP 435,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 424,413. The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
