Rents of 4+ bedroom houses in Chapinero, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 4+ bedroom houses in Chapinero, Bogotá is COP 15,500,000 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 11,500,000 to COP 21,625,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 39,533/m² and the median listed size is 401 m². That is 29% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 12,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 4 and 71.9% of listings include parking.
