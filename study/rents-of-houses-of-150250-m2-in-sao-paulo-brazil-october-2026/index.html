Rents of houses of 150–250 m² in São Paulo, Brazil — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses of 150–250 m² in São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 5,500 per month, based on 612 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 3,922 to BRL 7,940. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 29.1/m² and the median listed size is 192 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 392. 97.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
