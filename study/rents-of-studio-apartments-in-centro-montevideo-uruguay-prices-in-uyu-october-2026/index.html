Rents of studio apartments in Centro, Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in UYU) — October 2026

The median asking rent for studio apartments in Centro, Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 21,000 per month, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 18,500 to UYU 24,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 650/m² and the median listed size is 32 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 22,500). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 3,500. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
