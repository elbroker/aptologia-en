Sale prices of 1-bedroom apartments in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments for sale in the Chapinero locality, Bogotá is COP 520,000,000, based on 757 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 359,000,000 to COP 760,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,167,232/m² and the median listed size is 51 m². That is 68% below the study's reference median (COP 1,600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 562,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 76.6% of listings include parking.
