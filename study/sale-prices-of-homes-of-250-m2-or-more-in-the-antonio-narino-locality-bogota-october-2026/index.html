Sale prices of homes of 250 m² or more in the Antonio Nariño locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes of 250 m² or more for sale in the Antonio Nariño locality, Bogotá is COP 980,000,000, based on 143 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 730,000,000 to COP 1,275,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 2,600,000/m² and the median listed size is 353 m². That is 44% above the study's reference median (COP 680,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 10,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 60.1% of listings include parking.
