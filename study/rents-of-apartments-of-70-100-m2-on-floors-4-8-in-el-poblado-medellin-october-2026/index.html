Rents of apartments of 70–100 m² on floors 4–8 in El Poblado, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² on floors 4–8 in El Poblado, Medellín is COP 4,800,000 per month, based on 123 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,875,000 to COP 5,865,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 57,500/m² and the median listed size is 85 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 75.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
