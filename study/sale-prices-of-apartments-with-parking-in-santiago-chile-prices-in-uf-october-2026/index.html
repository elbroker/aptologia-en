Sale prices of apartments with parking in Santiago, Chile (prices in UF) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with parking for sale in Santiago, Chile is UF 8,500, based on 944 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 5,600 to UF 11,300. The median price per square metre is UF 79.8/m² and the median listed size is 101 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
