Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 250 m² or more in Baruta, Caracas, Venezuela — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 250 m² or more for sale in Baruta, Caracas, Venezuela is USD 397,500, based on 92 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 320,000 to USD 600,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,146/m² and the median listed size is 360 m². That is 66% above the study's reference median for Caracas (USD 240,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
