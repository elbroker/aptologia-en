Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² without parking in Medellín — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² without parking for sale in Medellín is COP 300,000,000, based on 408 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 260,000,000 to COP 350,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,359,731/m² and the median listed size is 56.6 m². That is 64% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 225,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
