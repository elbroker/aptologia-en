Rents of apartments on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 in El Tesoro, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments on floor 9 or higher in estrato 6 in El Tesoro, Medellín is COP 9,000,000 per month, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
