Sale prices of homes in Paranhos, Oporto, Portugal — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in Paranhos, Oporto, Portugal is EUR 322,500, based on 124 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 255,000 to EUR 403,675. The median price per square metre is EUR 4,630/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². 58.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.5.
