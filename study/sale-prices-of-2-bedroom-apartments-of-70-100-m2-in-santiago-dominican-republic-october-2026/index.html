Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Santiago, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Santiago, Dominican Republic is USD 163,250, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 130,136 to USD 234,375. The median price per square metre is USD 1,925/m² and the median listed size is 88.5 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median for Santiago (USD 178,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
