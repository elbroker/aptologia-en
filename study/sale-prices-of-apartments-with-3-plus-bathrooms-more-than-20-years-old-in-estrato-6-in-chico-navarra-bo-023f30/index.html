Sale prices of apartments with 3+ bathrooms more than 20 years old in estrato 6 in Chicó Navarra, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms more than 20 years old in estrato 6 for sale in Chicó Navarra, Bogotá is COP 1,200,000,000, based on 155 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,005,000,000 to COP 1,650,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,923,077/m² and the median listed size is 179 m². That is 60% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,480,000. 98.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
