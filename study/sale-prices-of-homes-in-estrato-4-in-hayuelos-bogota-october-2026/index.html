Sale prices of homes in estrato 4 in Hayuelos, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes in estrato 4 for sale in Hayuelos, Bogotá is COP 543,590,000, based on 102 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
