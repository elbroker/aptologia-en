Sale prices of homes in Calderón, Quito, Ecuador — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in Calderón, Quito, Ecuador is USD 77,500, based on 171 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 65,000 to USD 99,000. The median price per square metre is USD 634/m² and the median listed size is 125 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 30. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
