Sale prices of apartments of 100–150 m² in Mirador Sur, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² for sale in Mirador Sur, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 252,500, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 210,000 to USD 292,645. The median price per square metre is USD 1,900/m² and the median listed size is 141 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
