Rents of homes of 45–70 m² in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes of 45–70 m² in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá is COP 2,800,000 per month, based on 176 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,183,362 to COP 3,300,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 53,043/m² and the median listed size is 54 m². That is 29% above the study's reference median (COP 2,162,225). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 428,550. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 55.1% of listings include parking.
