Sale prices of homes with security or a doorman in the Engativá locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with security or a doorman for sale in the Engativá locality, Bogotá is COP 350,000,000, based on 592 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 280,000,000 to COP 485,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,396,825/m² and the median listed size is 64 m². That is 36% below the study's reference median (COP 545,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 250,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 65.4% of listings include parking.
