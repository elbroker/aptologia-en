Sale prices of apartments under 45 m² in estrato 5 in Armenia — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments under 45 m² in estrato 5 for sale in Armenia is COP 262,500,000, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
