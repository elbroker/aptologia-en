Sale prices of apartments with 2 bathrooms in Santa Maria Maior, Lisboa, Portugal — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in Santa Maria Maior, Lisboa, Portugal is EUR 665,000, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 542,625 to EUR 890,000. The median price per square metre is EUR 6,945/m² and the median listed size is 93 m². That is 1% above the study's reference median for Lisboa (EUR 660,000). 17.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
