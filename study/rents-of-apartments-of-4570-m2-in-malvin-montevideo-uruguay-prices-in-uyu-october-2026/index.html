Rents of apartments of 45–70 m² in Malvín, Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in UYU) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in Malvín, Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 33,000 per month, based on 82 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 29,918 to UYU 39,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 630/m² and the median listed size is 52 m². That is 10% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 30,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 5,800. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
