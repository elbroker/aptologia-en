Sale prices of homes in San Borja, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in San Borja, Lima, Peru is PEN 1,217,100, based on 365 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 756,200 to PEN 1,931,000. The median price per square metre is PEN 7,224/m² and the median listed size is 168 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PEN 1. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.5.
