Rents of apartments with a balcony with 3+ bathrooms in Camino Verde, Envigado — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony with 3+ bathrooms in Camino Verde, Envigado is COP 5,000,000 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,462,500 to COP 6,612,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 48,714/m² and the median listed size is 110 m². That is 14% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 4,400,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
