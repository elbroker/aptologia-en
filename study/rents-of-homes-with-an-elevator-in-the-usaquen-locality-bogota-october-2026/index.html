Rents of homes with an elevator in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes with an elevator in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá is COP 4,335,000 per month, based on 2,826 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,900,000 to COP 8,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 55,059/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median (COP 4,600,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 785,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 88.1% of listings include parking.
