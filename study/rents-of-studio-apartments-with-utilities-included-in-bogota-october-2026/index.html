Rents of studio apartments with utilities included in Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for studio apartments with utilities included in Bogotá is COP 1,450,000 per month, based on 121 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,200,000 to COP 1,850,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 51,786/m² and the median listed size is 27.5 m². The most frequent estrato is 3, 31.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
