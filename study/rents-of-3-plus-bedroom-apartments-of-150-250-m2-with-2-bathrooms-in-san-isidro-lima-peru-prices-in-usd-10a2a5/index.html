Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in San Isidro, Lima, Peru (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in San Isidro, Lima, Peru is USD 1,750 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,375 to USD 2,300. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 10/m² and the median listed size is 187 m². That is 40% above the study's reference median for Lima (USD 1,250). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
