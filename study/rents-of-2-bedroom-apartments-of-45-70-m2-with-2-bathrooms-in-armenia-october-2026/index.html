Rents of 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms in Armenia — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms in Armenia is COP 2,095,242 per month, based on 66 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,600,000 to COP 2,575,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 36,046/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 16% below the study's reference median for Armenia (COP 2,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 270,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 93.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
