Homes for sale for COP 250–400 million in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá — October 2026

Homes listed for sale for COP 250–400 million in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 300,000,000, based on 111 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 274,100,000 to COP 350,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,222,222/m² and the median listed size is 79.2 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median (COP 319,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 211,152. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 36.9% of listings include parking.
