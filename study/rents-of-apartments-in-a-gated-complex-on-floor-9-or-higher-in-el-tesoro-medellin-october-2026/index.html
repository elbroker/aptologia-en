Rents of apartments in a gated complex on floor 9 or higher in El Tesoro, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in a gated complex on floor 9 or higher in El Tesoro, Medellín is COP 8,200,000 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,500,000 to COP 13,750,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 58,274/m² and the median listed size is 140 m². That is 71% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 89.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
