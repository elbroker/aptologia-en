Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments of 250 m² or more with 3+ bathrooms in Asunción, Paraguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 250 m² or more with 3+ bathrooms in Asunción, Paraguay is USD 3,000 per month, based on 65 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,500 to USD 3,800. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 9.01/m² and the median listed size is 308 m². That is 122% above the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 1,350). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
