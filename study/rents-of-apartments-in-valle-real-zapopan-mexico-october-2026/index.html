Rents of apartments in Valle Real, Zapopan, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Valle Real, Zapopan, Mexico is MXN 30,000 per month, based on 97 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 27,000 to MXN 38,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 281/m² and the median listed size is 115 m². That is 7% above the study's reference median for Zapopan (MXN 28,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
