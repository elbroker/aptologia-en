Duplex and penthouse for rent in Bogotá: how much more they cost than a normal apartment (2026)

Machine-assisted English version of the Spanish original by Aptología. All figures are locked to the original (numbers are copied, not re-translated); if in doubt, the Spanish original prevails.

In Bogotá, a penthouse for rent costs 0.5% less per m² than a comparable apartment and a duplex 6.3% less. The medium rental is $14,000,000 for a penthouse, $7,500,000 for a duplex and $2,300,000 for the rest.

Abstract: We analyze 18,920 unique apartments for rent in Bogotá with description, 745 duplex and 359 penthouse.

Methodology

  • Source: daily intake base of Aptology, with public ads of Metroocuadrado, Fincaraíz, Cienalquís, Rentola and alchem.com. Court as at 13 October of 2026.
  • Filters: live ads that pass quality control, in Bogotá, rent between $400,000 and $30,000,000. TikTok and Facebook Market are excluded.
  • Duplicate: one ad is left per group of duplicates (the same building on several portals).
  • Matched premium: The price per m² of ads is compared with and without the attribute within the same layer cell × rooms (minimum 8 ads per side) and the weighted median of the differences is reported. So the premium is not confused with estrato or size. The attribute is detected in the text of the ad, so they only have ads with description. Duplex and penthouse are detected in the text of the ad; if an ad mentions both, it counts as penthouse.
  • Limitations: are prices ordered, not closed. The neighborhoods and localities come as each portal writes.
  • Open data: datos.csv, CC BY license 4. 0.
