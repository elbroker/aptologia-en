Rents of houses of 250 m² or more with parking in José Ignacio, Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses of 250 m² or more with parking in José Ignacio, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 17,850 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 12,448 to USD 20,750. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 44.3/m² and the median listed size is 350 m². That is 145% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 7,300). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
