Sale prices of apartments of 45–70 m² in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic (prices in DOP) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is DOP 4,032,250, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
