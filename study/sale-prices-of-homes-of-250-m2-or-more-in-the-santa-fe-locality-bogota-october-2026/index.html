Sale prices of homes of 250 m² or more in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes of 250 m² or more for sale in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá is COP 2,200,000,000, based on 98 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,400,000,000 to COP 3,822,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,255,319/m² and the median listed size is 479 m². That is 384% above the study's reference median (COP 454,982,760). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 720,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 53.1% of listings include parking.
