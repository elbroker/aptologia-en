Price of the square meter of the house in 306 municipalities of Spain according to the official value (second quarter of 2026)

The most expensive square meter of free housing in Spain in the second trimester of 2026 was valued in Santa Eulària des Riu (Illes Balears), with 6,844 euros, and the cheapest of 306 municipalities in Villarobledo (Albacete), with 666 euros. Madrid marks 5,418 euros per m² and Barcelona 4,602 euros. In the median of 113 municipalities, new housing is charged by 16.3% above that used.

Methodology

  • Source: MIVAU, statistics of value of housing (2026 -T2). The official tables were taken from the night exportes of Aptology of 18 October of 2026 (is _ rent _ municipality, is _ rent _ neighborhood, is _ purchase _ municipality), which collect them without changing them.
  • Statistical: average rated value per m² of free, total, new and used housing.
  • What is measured: income from registered contracts (bonds) or official valuation values, not prices ordered in portals.
  • Minimum sample: only rows with 30 contracts or valuation or more are published.
  • Limitations: Each source uses its own methodology (mean in Catalonia, median in the Valencia Community), so they should not compare with each other without caution.
