Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Loma de la Doctora, Sabaneta — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Loma de la Doctora, Sabaneta is COP 569,000,000, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 515,000,000 to COP 637,370,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,971,429/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 3% above the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 550,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 399,840. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
