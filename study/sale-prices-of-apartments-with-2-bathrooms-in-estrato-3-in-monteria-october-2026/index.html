Sale prices of apartments with 2 bathrooms in estrato 3 in Montería — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms in estrato 3 for sale in Montería is COP 290,000,000, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
