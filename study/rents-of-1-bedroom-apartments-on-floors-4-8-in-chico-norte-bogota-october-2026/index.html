Rents of 1-bedroom apartments on floors 4–8 in Chicó Norte, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments on floors 4–8 in Chicó Norte, Bogotá is COP 3,950,000 per month, based on 85 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,300,000 to COP 4,780,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 79,386/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 20% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 611,500. The most frequent estrato is 6, 92.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
