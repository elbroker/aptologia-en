Sale prices of homes with a gym in the Suba locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with a gym for sale in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 970,000,000, based on 3,083 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 598,500,000 to COP 1,477,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,972,973/m² and the median listed size is 137 m². That is 16% below the study's reference median (COP 1,150,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 720,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 92.5% of listings include parking.
