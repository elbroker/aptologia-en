Rents of houses with 3+ bathrooms in La Castellana, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses with 3+ bathrooms in La Castellana, Medellín is COP 7,300,000 per month, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 5,600,000 to COP 10,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 32,051/m² and the median listed size is 234 m². That is 14% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 8,500,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 81.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
