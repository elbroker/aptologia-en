Sale prices of houses 0–5 years old in La Soledad, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for houses 0–5 years old for sale in La Soledad, Bogotá is COP 1,390,000,000, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,150,000,000 to COP 1,700,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,242,424/m² and the median listed size is 321 m². That is 16% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
