Rents of 2-bedroom apartments in Roosevelt, Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in Roosevelt, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 2,350 per month, based on 85 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,800 to USD 5,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 33/m² and the median listed size is 78.5 m². That is 38% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 3,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
