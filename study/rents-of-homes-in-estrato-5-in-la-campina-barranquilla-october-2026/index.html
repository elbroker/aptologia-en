Rents of homes in estrato 5 in La Campiña, Barranquilla — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 5 in La Campiña, Barranquilla is COP 2,750,000 per month, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
