Sale prices of apartments in El Reloj, Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in El Reloj, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 4,479,000, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
