Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments 0–5 years old in Suramérica, Itagüí — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments 0–5 years old for sale in Suramérica, Itagüí is COP 580,000,000, based on 107 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 500,000,000 to COP 651,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,680,412/m² and the median listed size is 73 m². That is 23% above the study's reference median for Itagüí (COP 470,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 400,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 96.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
