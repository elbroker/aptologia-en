Sale prices of apartments of 70–100 m² in Malvín, Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Malvín, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 247,500, based on 120 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 219,000 to USD 286,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,975/m² and the median listed size is 83 m². That is 34% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 185,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
