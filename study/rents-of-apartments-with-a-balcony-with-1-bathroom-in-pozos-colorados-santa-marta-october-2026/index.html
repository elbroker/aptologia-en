Rents of apartments with a balcony with 1 bathroom in Pozos Colorados, Santa Marta — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony with 1 bathroom in Pozos Colorados, Santa Marta is COP 2,325,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,887,500 to COP 2,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 55,263/m² and the median listed size is 40 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 3,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
