Sale prices of apartments in La Samaria, Ibagué — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in La Samaria, Ibagué is COP 390,000,000, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 260,000,000 to COP 540,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,705,882/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Ibagué (COP 330,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 305,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 94.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
