Rents of 3+ bedroom houses of 100–150 m² with parking in Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in UYU) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses of 100–150 m² with parking in Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 53,000 per month, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 39,000 to UYU 66,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 468/m² and the median listed size is 118 m². That is 38% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 38,500). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
