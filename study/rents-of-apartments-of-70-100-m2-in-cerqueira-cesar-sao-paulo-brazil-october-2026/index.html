Rents of apartments of 70–100 m² in Cerqueira César, São Paulo, Brazil — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in Cerqueira César, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 9,000 per month, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 5,900 to BRL 12,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 104/m² and the median listed size is 81 m². That is 149% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,774. 97.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
