Rents of houses with a balcony in Cartagena — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses with a balcony in Cartagena is COP 7,250,000 per month, based on 146 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,562,500 to COP 14,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 26,667/m² and the median listed size is 298 m². The most frequent estrato is 5, 88.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
