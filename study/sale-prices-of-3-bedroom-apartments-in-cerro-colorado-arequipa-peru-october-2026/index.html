Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments in Cerro Colorado, Arequipa, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Cerro Colorado, Arequipa, Peru is PEN 509,411, based on 86 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 407,625 to PEN 651,802. The median price per square metre is PEN 4,356/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median for Arequipa (PEN 533,532). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
