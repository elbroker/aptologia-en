Rents of apartments of 70–100 m² in Ladrillera, Monterrey, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in Ladrillera, Monterrey, Mexico is MXN 35,250 per month, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 30,000 to MXN 38,375. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 438/m² and the median listed size is 79 m². That is 36% above the study's reference median for Monterrey (MXN 26,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
