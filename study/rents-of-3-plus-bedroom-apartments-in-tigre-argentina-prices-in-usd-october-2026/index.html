Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments in Tigre, Argentina (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Tigre, Argentina is USD 2,500 per month, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,600 to USD 4,050. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 18.6/m² and the median listed size is 142 m². That is 92% above the study's reference median for Tigre (USD 1,300). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
