Sale prices of homes in estrato 5 in Santa Paula, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes in estrato 5 for sale in Santa Paula, Bogotá is COP 845,000,000, based on 88 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
