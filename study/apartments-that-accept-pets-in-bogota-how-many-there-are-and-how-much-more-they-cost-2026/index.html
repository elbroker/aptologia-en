Apartments that accept pets in Bogotá: how many there are and how much more they cost (2026)

Machine-assisted English version of the Spanish original by Aptología. All figures are locked to the original (numbers are copied, not re-translated); if in doubt, the Spanish original prevails.

In Bogotá, an apartment for rent that accepts pets costs on average 7.0% more per square meter than a comparable one of the same estrato and number of rooms. It is mentioned by 3.6% of the ads with description; the average rental of which is $2,100,000 compared to $2,500,000 of which is not.

Abstract: We analyze 19,156 unique apartments for rent in Bogotá with description, of which 694 mention that it accepts pets.

Methodology

  • Source: daily intake base of Aptology, with public ads of Metroocuadrado, Fincaraíz, Cienalquís, Rentola and alchem.com. Court as at 13 October of 2026.
  • Filters: live ads that pass quality control, in Bogotá, rent between $400,000 and $30,000,000. TikTok and Facebook Market are excluded.
  • Duplicate: one ad is left per group of duplicates (the same building on several portals).
  • Matched premium: The price per m² of ads is compared with and without the attribute within the same layer cell × rooms (minimum 8 ads per side) and the weighted median of the differences is reported. So the premium is not confused with estrato or size. The attribute is detected in the text of the ad, so they only have ads with description.
  • Limitations: are prices ordered, not closed. The neighborhoods and localities come as each portal writes.
  • Open data: datos.csv, CC BY license 4. 0.
