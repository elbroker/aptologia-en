Methodology

  • Source: daily intake base of Aptology, with public ads of Metroocuadrado, Fincaraíz, Cienalquís, Rentola and alchem.com. Court as at 13 October of 2026.
  • Filters: live ads that pass quality control, in Bogotá, rent between $400,000 and $30,000,000. TikTok and Facebook Market are excluded.
  • Duplicate: one ad is left per group of duplicates (the same building on several portals).
  • Matched premium: compare the price per m² of ads for each type of advertiser against real estate within the same layer cell × rooms (minimum 8 ads per side) and report the weighted median of the differences. So the premium is not confused with estrato or size. The attribute is detected in the text of the ad, so they only have ads with description. Type of advertiser: it is classified by the name of the advertiser in Metroocuadrado, Fincaraíz and Cienaltes. It is a company if the name brings words like real estate, SAS or Ltda.; if it is a person name with 3 ads or less, it is taken as a particular (possible direct owner); with more than 3 ads, as an independent agent. It's an approach.
  • Limitations: are prices ordered, not closed. The neighborhoods and localities come as each portal writes.
