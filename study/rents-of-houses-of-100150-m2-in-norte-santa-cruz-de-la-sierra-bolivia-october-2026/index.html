Rents of houses of 100–150 m² in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses of 100–150 m² in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 500 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 450 to USD 600. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 4.35/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 57% below the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 1,166). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
