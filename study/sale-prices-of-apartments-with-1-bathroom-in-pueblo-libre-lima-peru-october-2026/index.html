Sale prices of apartments with 1 bathroom in Pueblo Libre, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with 1 bathroom for sale in Pueblo Libre, Lima, Peru is PEN 299,000, based on 43 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
