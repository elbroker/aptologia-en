Sale prices of houses with parking 6–20 years old in Alto de las Palmas, Envigado — October 2026

The median asking price for houses with parking 6–20 years old for sale in Alto de las Palmas, Envigado is COP 4,200,000,000, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,500,000,000 to COP 4,887,500,000. The median listed size is 470 m². That is 83% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 2,300,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,100,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
