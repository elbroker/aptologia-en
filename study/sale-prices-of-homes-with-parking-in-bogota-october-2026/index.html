Sale prices of homes with parking in Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with parking for sale in Bogotá is COP 1,050,000,000, based on 43,405 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 635,000,000 to COP 1,800,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,847,826/m² and the median listed size is 170 m². That is 21% above the study's reference median (COP 865,852,573). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 900,000. The most frequent estrato is 6.
