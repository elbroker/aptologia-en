Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Villa Campestre, Barranquilla — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Villa Campestre, Barranquilla is COP 3,050,000 per month, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,700,000 to COP 3,475,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 33,162/m² and the median listed size is 93 m². That is 9% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
