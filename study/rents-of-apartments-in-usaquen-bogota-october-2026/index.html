Rents of apartments in Usaquen, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Usaquen, Bogotá is COP 7,850,000 per month, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,665,000 to COP 14,375,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 53,180/m² and the median listed size is 157 m². That is 124% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,338,200. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 84.8% of listings include parking.
