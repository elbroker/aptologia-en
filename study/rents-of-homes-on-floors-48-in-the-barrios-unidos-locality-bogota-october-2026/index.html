Rents of homes on floors 4–8 in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes on floors 4–8 in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá is COP 3,600,000 per month, based on 219 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,000,000 to COP 7,850,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 68,000/m² and the median listed size is 57 m². That is 33% above the study's reference median (COP 2,700,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 566,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 63% of listings include parking.
