Rents of 2-bedroom apartments in estrato 6 in Cartagena — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in estrato 6 in Cartagena is COP 5,000,000 per month, based on 233 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,800,000 to COP 6,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 52,941/m² and the median listed size is 95 m². That is 35% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 3,700,000).
