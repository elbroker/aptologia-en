Sale prices of homes on floors 4–8 in the Engativá locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes on floors 4–8 for sale in the Engativá locality, Bogotá is COP 330,000,000, based on 360 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 271,750,000 to COP 476,225,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,438,596/m² and the median listed size is 62 m². That is 39% below the study's reference median (COP 545,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 244,797. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 58.1% of listings include parking.
