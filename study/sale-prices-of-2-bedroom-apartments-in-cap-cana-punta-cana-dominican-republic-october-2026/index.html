Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments in Cap Cana, Punta Cana, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Cap Cana, Punta Cana, Dominican Republic is USD 470,000, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 375,000 to USD 533,000. The median listed size is 120 m². That is 85% above the study's reference median for Punta Cana (USD 253,500). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
