Rents of studio apartments in Chapinero Alto, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for studio apartments in Chapinero Alto, Bogotá is COP 2,057,500 per month, based on 76 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,700,000 to COP 2,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 58,065/m² and the median listed size is 37.5 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,900,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 447,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 47.4% of listings include parking.
