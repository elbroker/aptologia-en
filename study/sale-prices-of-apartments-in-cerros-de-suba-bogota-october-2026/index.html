Sale prices of apartments in Cerros de Suba, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Cerros de Suba, Bogotá is COP 1,695,000,000, based on 170 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,260,000,000 to COP 2,500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,131,414/m² and the median listed size is 224 m². That is 122% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,650,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 94.1% of listings include parking.
