Rents of 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² in San Isidro, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² in San Isidro, Lima, Peru is PEN 3,257 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 3,045 to PEN 3,612. The median monthly rent per square metre is PEN 53.2/m² and the median listed size is 62 m². That is 7% above the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 3,050). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
