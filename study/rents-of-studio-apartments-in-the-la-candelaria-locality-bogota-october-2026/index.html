Rents of studio apartments in the La Candelaria locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for studio apartments in the La Candelaria locality, Bogotá is COP 1,600,000 per month, based on 52 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,400,000 to COP 1,862,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,637/m² and the median listed size is 30.7 m². That is 14% below the study's reference median (COP 1,850,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 231,650. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 17.3% of listings include parking.
