Sale prices of homes in El Batán, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in El Batán, Bogotá is COP 850,000,000, based on 279 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 620,000,000 to COP 1,150,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,072,874/m² and the median listed size is 137 m². That is 2% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 661,500. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 97.8% of listings include parking.
