Sale prices of homes with security or a doorman in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with security or a doorman for sale in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá is COP 230,000,000, based on 181 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 213,525,000 to COP 256,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,990,758/m² and the median listed size is 47.6 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median (COP 241,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 150,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 19.9% of listings include parking.
