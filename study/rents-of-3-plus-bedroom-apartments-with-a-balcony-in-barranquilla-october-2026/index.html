Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments with a balcony in Barranquilla — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with a balcony in Barranquilla is COP 3,635,000 per month, based on 1,410 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,500,000 to COP 5,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 34,324/m² and the median listed size is 108 m². That is 30% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 550,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 98.4% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
