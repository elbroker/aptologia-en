Sale prices of apartments of 45–70 m² with 1 bathroom in Ciudad Verde, Soacha — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² with 1 bathroom for sale in Ciudad Verde, Soacha is COP 167,500,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 160,000,000 to COP 193,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,248,038/m² and the median listed size is 52.6 m². That is 1% below the study's reference median for Soacha (COP 170,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 118,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
