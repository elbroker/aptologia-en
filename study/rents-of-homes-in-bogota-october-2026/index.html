Rents of homes in Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in Bogotá is COP 3,495,000 per month, based on 19,947 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,900,000 to COP 8,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 48,571/m² and the median listed size is 70 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 680,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 67% of listings include parking.
