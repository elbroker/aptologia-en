Rents of houses of 250 m² or more in Guadalajara, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses of 250 m² or more in Guadalajara, Mexico is MXN 53,000 per month, based on 75 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 42,000 to MXN 73,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 137/m² and the median listed size is 364 m². The median number of bathrooms is 3.
