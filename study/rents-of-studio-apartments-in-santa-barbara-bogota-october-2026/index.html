Rents of studio apartments in Santa Barbara, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for studio apartments in Santa Barbara, Bogotá is COP 2,775,000 per month, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,212,500 to COP 3,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 74,166/m² and the median listed size is 36 m². That is 46% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,900,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 460,024. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 75.7% of listings include parking.
