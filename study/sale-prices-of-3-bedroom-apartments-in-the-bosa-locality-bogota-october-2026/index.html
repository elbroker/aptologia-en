Sale prices of 3-bedroom apartments in the Bosa locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for 3-bedroom apartments for sale in the Bosa locality, Bogotá is COP 194,875,000, based on 246 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 171,250,000 to COP 216,985,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,911,111/m² and the median listed size is 48 m². That is 3% above the study's reference median (COP 189,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 100,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 11% of listings include parking.
