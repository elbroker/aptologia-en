Rents of apartments with a balcony in estrato 5 in El Cabrero, Cartagena — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony in estrato 5 in El Cabrero, Cartagena is COP 4,376,500 per month, based on 46 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,500,000 to COP 6,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 46,129/m² and the median listed size is 97.5 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 3,700,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 864,900. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
