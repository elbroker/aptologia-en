Sale prices of apartments under 45 m² with 2 bathrooms in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile (prices in UF) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments under 45 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile is UF 2,650, based on 79 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
