Rents of apartments with a balcony in La Carolina, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony in La Carolina, Bogotá is COP 11,750,000 per month, based on 62 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 7,550,007 to COP 14,750,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 54,825/m² and the median listed size is 186 m². That is 256% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,780,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 95.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
