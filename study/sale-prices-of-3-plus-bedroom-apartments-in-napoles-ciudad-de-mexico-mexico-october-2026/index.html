Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments in Nápoles, Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Nápoles, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 7,340,819, based on 205 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 5,295,000 to MXN 8,999,904. The median price per square metre is MXN 50,918/m² and the median listed size is 137 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 7,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
