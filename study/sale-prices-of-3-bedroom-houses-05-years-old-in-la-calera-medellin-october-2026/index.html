Sale prices of 3+ bedroom houses 0–5 years old in La Calera, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses 0–5 years old for sale in La Calera, Medellín is COP 3,150,000,000, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,487,500,000 to COP 3,797,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,545,330/m² and the median listed size is 420 m². That is 127% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 1,390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,500,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 5.
