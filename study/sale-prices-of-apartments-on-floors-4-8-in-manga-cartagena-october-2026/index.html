Sale prices of apartments on floors 4–8 in Manga, Cartagena — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments on floors 4–8 for sale in Manga, Cartagena is COP 600,000,000, based on 69 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 480,000,000 to COP 820,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,692,308/m² and the median listed size is 115 m². That is 25% below the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 800,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 650,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
