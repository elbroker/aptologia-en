Rents of 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² without parking in Cartagena — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² without parking in Cartagena is COP 2,080,000 per month, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,600,000 to COP 2,437,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 35,158/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 44% below the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 3,700,000). The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
