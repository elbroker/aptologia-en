Rents of apartments of 45–70 m² in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 2,100 per month, based on 41 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,500 to USD 3,400. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 36.7/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 56% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 4,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
