Sale prices of homes with a terrace or balcony in the Puente Aranda locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with a terrace or balcony for sale in the Puente Aranda locality, Bogotá is COP 850,000,000, based on 204 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 550,000,000 to COP 1,300,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,746,032/m² and the median listed size is 153 m². That is 31% above the study's reference median (COP 650,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 550,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 79.9% of listings include parking.
