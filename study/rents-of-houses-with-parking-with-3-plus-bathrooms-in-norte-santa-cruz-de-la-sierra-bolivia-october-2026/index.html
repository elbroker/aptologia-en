Rents of houses with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Norte, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 1,220 per month, based on 153 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 785 to USD 2,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 4.67/m² and the median listed size is 250 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 1,166). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
