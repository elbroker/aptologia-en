Rents of studio apartments in Mooca, São Paulo, Brazil — October 2026

The median asking rent for studio apartments in Mooca, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 1,825 per month, based on 40 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 1,690 to BRL 2,255. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 66.3/m² and the median listed size is 28 m². That is 31% below the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 2,650). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 300. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
