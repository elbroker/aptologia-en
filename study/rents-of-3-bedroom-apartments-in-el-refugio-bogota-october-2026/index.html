Rents of 3-bedroom apartments in El Refugio, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3-bedroom apartments in El Refugio, Bogotá is COP 12,850,000 per month, based on 66 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 10,500,000 to COP 20,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 58,449/m² and the median listed size is 239 m². That is 98% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 6,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,442,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
