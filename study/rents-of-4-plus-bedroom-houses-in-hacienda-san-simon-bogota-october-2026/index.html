Rents of 4+ bedroom houses in Hacienda San Simon, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 4+ bedroom houses in Hacienda San Simon, Bogotá is COP 13,500,000 per month, based on 64 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 12,500,000 to COP 15,125,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 29,520/m² and the median listed size is 490 m². That is 12% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 12,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,044,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 100% of listings include parking.
