Rents of homes in Caracas, Venezuela — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in Caracas, Venezuela is USD 1,400 per month, based on 396 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 900 to USD 2,712. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 12/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². 95% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
