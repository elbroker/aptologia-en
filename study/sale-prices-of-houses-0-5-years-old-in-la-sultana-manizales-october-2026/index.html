Sale prices of houses 0–5 years old in La Sultana, Manizales — October 2026

The median asking price for houses 0–5 years old for sale in La Sultana, Manizales is COP 465,000,000, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 427,500,000 to COP 502,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 2,533,333/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 40% below the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 770,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
