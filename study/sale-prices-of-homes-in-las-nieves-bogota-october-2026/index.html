Sale prices of homes in Las Nieves, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in Las Nieves, Bogotá is COP 375,000,000, based on 175 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 288,500,000 to COP 594,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,957,295/m² and the median listed size is 49.9 m². That is 57% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 309,300. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 46.9% of listings include parking.
