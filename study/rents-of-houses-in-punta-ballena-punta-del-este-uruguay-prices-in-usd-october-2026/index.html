Rents of houses in Punta Ballena, Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses in Punta Ballena, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 3,800 per month, based on 69 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,500 to USD 6,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 21.8/m² and the median listed size is 200 m². That is 48% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 7,300). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
