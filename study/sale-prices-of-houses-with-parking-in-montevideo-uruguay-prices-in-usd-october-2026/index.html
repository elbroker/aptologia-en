Sale prices of houses with parking in Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for houses with parking for sale in Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 330,000, based on 2,110 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 179,225 to USD 640,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,738/m² and the median listed size is 216 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
