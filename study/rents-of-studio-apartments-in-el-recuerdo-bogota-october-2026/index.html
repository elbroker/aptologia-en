Rents of studio apartments in El Recuerdo, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for studio apartments in El Recuerdo, Bogotá is COP 2,000,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,712,500 to COP 2,650,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 71,633/m² and the median listed size is 31 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,900,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 269,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 23.3% of listings include parking.
