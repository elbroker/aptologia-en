Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments with a balcony with 1 bathroom in Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with a balcony with 1 bathroom in Bogotá is COP 1,490,000 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,286,500 to COP 2,022,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 28,035/m² and the median listed size is 52 m². That is 55% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The most frequent estrato is 3, 46.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
