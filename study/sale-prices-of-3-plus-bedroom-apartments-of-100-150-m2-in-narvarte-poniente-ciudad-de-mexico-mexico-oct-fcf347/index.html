Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² in Narvarte Poniente, Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² for sale in Narvarte Poniente, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 6,772,501, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 5,500,000 to MXN 8,646,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 56,985/m² and the median listed size is 124 m². That is 36% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 4,990,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
