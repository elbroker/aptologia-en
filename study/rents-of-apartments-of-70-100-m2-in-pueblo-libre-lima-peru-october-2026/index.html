Rents of apartments of 70–100 m² in Pueblo Libre, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in Pueblo Libre, Lima, Peru is PEN 2,300 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 2,100 to PEN 2,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is PEN 27.6/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 25% below the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 3,050). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
