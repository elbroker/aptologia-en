Rents of apartments of 70–100 m² in Las Mercedes, Asunción, Paraguay (prices in PYG) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in Las Mercedes, Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 6,000,000 per month, based on 52 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 5,500,000 to PYG 7,025,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is PYG 77,465/m² and the median listed size is 84 m². That is 15% above the study's reference median for Asunción (PYG 5,200,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
