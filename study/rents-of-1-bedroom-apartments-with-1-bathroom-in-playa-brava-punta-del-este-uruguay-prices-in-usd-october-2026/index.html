Rents of 1-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 3,200 per month, based on 71 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,000 to USD 5,550. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 38.5/m² and the median listed size is 73.5 m². That is 33% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 4,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
