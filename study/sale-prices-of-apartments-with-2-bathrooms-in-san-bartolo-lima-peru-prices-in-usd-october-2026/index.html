Sale prices of apartments with 2 bathrooms in San Bartolo, Lima, Peru (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms for sale in San Bartolo, Lima, Peru is USD 165,000, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 125,000 to USD 189,400. The median price per square metre is USD 1,818/m² and the median listed size is 88 m². That is 36% below the study's reference median for Lima (USD 258,023). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
