Sale prices of apartments in Tunja — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Tunja is COP 325,000,000, based on 82 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 250,000,000 to COP 413,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,477,124/m² and the median listed size is 70.5 m². That is 17% below the study's reference median (COP 390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 250,200. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 72% of listings include parking.
