Sale prices of homes in Pinares, Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in Pinares, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 400,000, based on 458 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 301,250 to USD 620,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,524/m² and the median listed size is 180 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 790. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
