Rents of 3+ bedroom houses of 70–100 m² in Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses of 70–100 m² in Bogotá is COP 2,425,000 per month, based on 56 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,975,000 to COP 2,817,250. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 27,528/m² and the median listed size is 84.3 m². That is 76% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 10,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 332,000. The most frequent estrato is 3, 68.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
