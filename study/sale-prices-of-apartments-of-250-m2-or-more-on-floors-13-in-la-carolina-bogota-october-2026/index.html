Sale prices of apartments of 250 m² or more on floors 1–3 in La Carolina, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 250 m² or more on floors 1–3 for sale in La Carolina, Bogotá is COP 1,724,500,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,550,000,000 to COP 2,300,000,000. The median listed size is 303 m². That is 130% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,944,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
