Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments in Bella Vista Sur, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Bella Vista Sur, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 274,350, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 228,750 to USD 290,764. The median price per square metre is USD 2,332/m² and the median listed size is 116 m². That is 22% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 225,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
