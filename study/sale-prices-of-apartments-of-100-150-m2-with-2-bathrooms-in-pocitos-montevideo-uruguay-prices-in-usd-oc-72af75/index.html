Sale prices of apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Pocitos, Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Pocitos, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 366,090, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 316,804 to USD 402,250. The median price per square metre is USD 3,263/m² and the median listed size is 112 m². That is 98% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (USD 185,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
