Sale prices of homes under 45 m² in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes under 45 m² for sale in the Santa Fe locality, Bogotá is COP 318,500,000, based on 300 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 255,000,000 to COP 370,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,465,368/m² and the median listed size is 30 m². That is 30% below the study's reference median (COP 454,982,760). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 240,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 26.7% of listings include parking.
