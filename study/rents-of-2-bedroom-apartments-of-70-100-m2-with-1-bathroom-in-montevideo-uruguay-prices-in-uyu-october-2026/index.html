Rents of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom in Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in UYU) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom in Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 36,000 per month, based on 184 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 29,800 to UYU 42,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 470/m² and the median listed size is 75.5 m². That is 20% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 30,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 6,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
