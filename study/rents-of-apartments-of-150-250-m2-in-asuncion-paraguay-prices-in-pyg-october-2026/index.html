Rents of apartments of 150–250 m² in Asunción, Paraguay (prices in PYG) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 150–250 m² in Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 8,114,616 per month, based on 134 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 6,000,000 to PYG 10,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is PYG 45,364/m² and the median listed size is 180 m². 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
