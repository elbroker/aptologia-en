Sale prices of houses of 250 m² or more in estrato 4 in Cajicá — October 2026

The median asking price for houses of 250 m² or more in estrato 4 for sale in Cajicá is COP 1,700,000,000, based on 136 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,300,000,000 to COP 2,137,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,824,335/m² and the median listed size is 345 m². That is 31% above the study's reference median for Cajicá (COP 1,300,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,000,000. 99.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
