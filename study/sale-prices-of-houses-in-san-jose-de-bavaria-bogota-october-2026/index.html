Sale prices of houses in San Jose de Bavaria, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for houses for sale in San Jose de Bavaria, Bogotá is COP 1,950,000,000, based on 1,240 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,650,000,000 to COP 2,500,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,736,842/m² and the median listed size is 450 m². That is 62% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 860,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 99.4% of listings include parking.
