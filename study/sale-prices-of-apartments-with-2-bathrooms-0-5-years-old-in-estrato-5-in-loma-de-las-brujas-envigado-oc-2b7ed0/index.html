Sale prices of apartments with 2 bathrooms 0–5 years old in estrato 5 in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms 0–5 years old in estrato 5 for sale in Loma de las Brujas, Envigado is COP 840,000,000, based on 80 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
