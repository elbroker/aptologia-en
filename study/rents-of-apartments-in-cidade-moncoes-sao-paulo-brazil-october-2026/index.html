Rents of apartments in Cidade Monções, São Paulo, Brazil — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Cidade Monções, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 9,900 per month, based on 65 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 5,500 to BRL 14,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 102/m² and the median listed size is 97 m². That is 173% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,909. 96.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
