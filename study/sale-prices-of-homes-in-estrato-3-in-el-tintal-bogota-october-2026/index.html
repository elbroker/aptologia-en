Sale prices of homes in estrato 3 in El Tintal, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes in estrato 3 for sale in El Tintal, Bogotá is COP 262,500,000, based on 62 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
