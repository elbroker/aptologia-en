Sale prices of homes in Punta Gorda, Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in Punta Gorda, Montevideo, Uruguay is USD 580,000, based on 182 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
