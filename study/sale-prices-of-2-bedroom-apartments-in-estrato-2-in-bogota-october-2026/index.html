Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments in estrato 2 in Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments in estrato 2 for sale in Bogotá is COP 190,000,000, based on 557 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 162,000,000 to COP 225,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,404,043/m² and the median listed size is 44 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median (COP 200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 115,000. 11.1% of listings include parking.
