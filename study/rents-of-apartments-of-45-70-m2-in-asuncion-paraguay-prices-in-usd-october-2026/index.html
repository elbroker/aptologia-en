Rents of apartments of 45–70 m² in Asunción, Paraguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in Asunción, Paraguay is USD 998 per month, based on 249 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
