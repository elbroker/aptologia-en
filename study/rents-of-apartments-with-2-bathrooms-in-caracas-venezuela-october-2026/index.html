Rents of apartments with 2 bathrooms in Caracas, Venezuela — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with 2 bathrooms in Caracas, Venezuela is USD 1,000 per month, based on 125 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 800 to USD 1,400. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 10.8/m² and the median listed size is 88 m². 96.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
