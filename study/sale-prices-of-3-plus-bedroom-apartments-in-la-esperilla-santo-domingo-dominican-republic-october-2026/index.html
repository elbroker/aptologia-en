Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments in La Esperilla, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in La Esperilla, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 390,000, based on 109 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 310,000 to USD 550,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,880/m² and the median listed size is 232 m². That is 22% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 320,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
