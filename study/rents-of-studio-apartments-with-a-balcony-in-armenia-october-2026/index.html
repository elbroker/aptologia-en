Rents of studio apartments with a balcony in Armenia — October 2026

The median asking rent for studio apartments with a balcony in Armenia is COP 1,575,000 per month, based on 64 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,300,000 to COP 1,950,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 38,829/m² and the median listed size is 38 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 190,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 83.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
