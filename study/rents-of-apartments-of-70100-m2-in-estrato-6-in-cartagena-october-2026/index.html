Rents of apartments of 70–100 m² in estrato 6 in Cartagena — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in estrato 6 in Cartagena is COP 4,500,000 per month, based on 177 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,600,000 to COP 6,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 51,504/m² and the median listed size is 87 m². That is 22% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 3,700,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 762,000. 98.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
