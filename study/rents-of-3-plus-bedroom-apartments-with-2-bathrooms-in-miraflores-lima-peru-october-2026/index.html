Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms in Miraflores, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms in Miraflores, Lima, Peru is PEN 4,103 per month, based on 190 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 3,348 to PEN 5,980. The median monthly rent per square metre is PEN 33.4/m² and the median listed size is 126 m². That is 35% above the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 3,050). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PEN 412. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
