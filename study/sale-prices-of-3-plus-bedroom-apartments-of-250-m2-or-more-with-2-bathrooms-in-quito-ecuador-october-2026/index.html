Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms in Quito, Ecuador — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms for sale in Quito, Ecuador is USD 200,000, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 169,900 to USD 249,000. The median price per square metre is USD 728/m² and the median listed size is 300 m². That is 38% above the study's reference median for Quito (USD 145,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
