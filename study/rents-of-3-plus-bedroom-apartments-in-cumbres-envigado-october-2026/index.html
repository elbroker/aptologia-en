Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments in Cumbres, Envigado — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Cumbres, Envigado is COP 4,650,000 per month, based on 290 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,950,000 to COP 5,637,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 47,814/m² and the median listed size is 98 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 4,550,000). The most frequent estrato is 5, 98.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
