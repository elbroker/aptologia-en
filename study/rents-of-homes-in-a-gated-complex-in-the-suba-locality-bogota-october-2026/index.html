Rents of homes in a gated complex in the Suba locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in a gated complex in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 3,500,000 per month, based on 867 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,900,000 to COP 9,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 37,255/m² and the median listed size is 88 m². That is 14% above the study's reference median (COP 3,079,400). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 653,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 77.6% of listings include parking.
