Rents of 2-bedroom apartments in the Engativá locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments in the Engativá locality, Bogotá is COP 1,500,000 per month, based on 149 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,200,000 to COP 1,980,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 29,091/m² and the median listed size is 52 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median (COP 1,700,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 188,500. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 24.8% of listings include parking.
