Sale prices of houses more than 20 years old in Ciudad Jardín, Barranquilla — October 2026

The median asking price for houses more than 20 years old for sale in Ciudad Jardín, Barranquilla is COP 950,000,000, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 750,000,000 to COP 1,200,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,019,943/m² and the median listed size is 322 m². That is 6% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 900,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 95.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
