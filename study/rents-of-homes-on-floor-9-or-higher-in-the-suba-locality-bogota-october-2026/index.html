Rents of homes on floor 9 or higher in the Suba locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes on floor 9 or higher in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 2,150,000 per month, based on 231 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,800,000 to COP 3,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 47,500/m² and the median listed size is 45 m². That is 30% below the study's reference median (COP 3,079,400). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 309,400. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 53.7% of listings include parking.
