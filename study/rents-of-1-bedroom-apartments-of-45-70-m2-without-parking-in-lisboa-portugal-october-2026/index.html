Rents of 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² without parking in Lisboa, Portugal — October 2026

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² without parking in Lisboa, Portugal is EUR 1,400 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
