Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in Cartagena — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Cartagena is COP 980,000,000, based on 144 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 780,000,000 to COP 1,350,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,766,968/m² and the median listed size is 169 m². That is 23% above the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 800,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,134,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 94.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
