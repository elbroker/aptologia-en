Rents of houses of 100–150 m² in estrato 5 in Pereira — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses of 100–150 m² in estrato 5 in Pereira is COP 2,400,000 per month, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,162,500 to COP 3,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 20,477/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 63% below the study's reference median for Pereira (COP 6,460,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 290,000. 94.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
