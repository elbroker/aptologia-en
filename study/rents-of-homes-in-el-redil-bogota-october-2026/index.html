Rents of homes in El Redil, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in El Redil, Bogotá is COP 1,443,000 per month, based on 103 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,190,000 to COP 1,700,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 37,864/m² and the median listed size is 40 m². That is 59% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,497,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 225,500. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 23.3% of listings include parking.
