Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments 0–5 years old in Santa María de los Ángeles, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments 0–5 years old for sale in Santa María de los Ángeles, Medellín is COP 1,287,500,000, based on 108 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 950,000,000 to COP 1,527,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,571,429/m² and the median listed size is 153 m². That is 53% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 970,400. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
