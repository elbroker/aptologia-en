Homes for sale for COP 250–400 million in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá — October 2026

Homes listed for sale for COP 250–400 million in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá have a median asking price of COP 310,000,000, based on 345 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 280,000,000 to COP 353,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,603,774/m² and the median listed size is 46 m². That is 42% below the study's reference median (COP 530,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 215,250. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 39.4% of listings include parking.
