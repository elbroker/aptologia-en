HOA fee rate in Bogotá by district and estrato (October 2026)

Machine-assisted English version of the Spanish original by Aptología. All figures are locked to the original (numbers are copied, not re-translated); if in doubt, the Spanish original prevails.

In Bogotá, the rate of HOA fee of an apartment for rent amounts in the median to 15.8% of the rent: $700,000 per month on a medium rental of $4,000,000. It goes from $204,000 in estrato 3 to $1,528,500 in estrato 6, but as a proportion of the rent is almost equal in all estratos (13% to 17%). per square metre, the HOA fee costs $5,000 / m² in estrato 3 and $10,075 / m² in estrato 6.

Abstract: we measure 8,023 apartments for rent in Bogotá that publish the HOA fee apart from the rent, without duplicates, with cut to 12 October of 2026.

Frequently asked questions

How much is HOA fee paid in Bogotá? The median is $700,000 per month, 15.8% of the rent, in 8,023 ads with cut to 12 October of 2026. Half of the apartments pay between $330,000 and $1,530,000.

How much is estrato HOA fee 3? The median is $204,000 per month (13.3% of the rent), with a typical range of $166,000 to $300,000.

How much is estrato HOA fee 4? The median is $400,000 per month (15.9% of the rent).

How much is estrato HOA fee 6? The median is $1,528,500 per month; half pays between $900,000 and $2,355,000.

Does the HOA fee pay for it by the tenant or the owner? In most ads the tenant pays it apart from the rent; therefore this study measures it separately. When the ad says "HOA fee included," it's already inside the rent. What is agreed must be written in the contract.

In which district of Bogotá is the HOA fee higher? Among the neighborhoods with at least 30 ads, Santa Barbara Alta ($2,550,000) and Chico Alto ($2,550,000). The lowest is in Lagos de Torca ($227,500).

Methodology

  • Source: daily intake base of Aptology, with public ads of Metroocuadrado, Fincaraíz, Cienalquís, Rentola and alchem.com. Court as at 12 October of 2026.
  • Filters: live ads that pass quality control, only apartments in Bogotá, rent between $400,000 and $30,000,000. TikTok and Facebook Market are excluded.
  • Duplicate: one ad is left per group of duplicates (the same building on several portals). There are 21,042 unique apartments.
  • HOA fee: only ads that publish the separate rate (not "included"), between $30,000 and $5,000,000 and less than the rent. The value per m² uses areas from 20 to 400 m².
  • Limitations: are prices ordered, not closed. The neighborhoods come as each portal writes, so the same sector can appear with two names. Only groups with at least 20 ads are published.
  • Open data: datos.csv, CC BY license 4. 0.
