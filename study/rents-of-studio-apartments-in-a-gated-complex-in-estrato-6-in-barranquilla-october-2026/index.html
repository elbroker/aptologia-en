Rents of studio apartments in a gated complex in estrato 6 in Barranquilla — October 2026

The median asking rent for studio apartments in a gated complex in estrato 6 in Barranquilla is COP 2,700,000 per month, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,194,000 to COP 3,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 54,717/m² and the median listed size is 49 m². That is 35% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 392,000. 98.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
