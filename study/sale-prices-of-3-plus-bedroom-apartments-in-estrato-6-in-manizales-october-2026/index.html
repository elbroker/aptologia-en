Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments in estrato 6 in Manizales — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments in estrato 6 for sale in Manizales is COP 650,000,000, based on 856 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 530,000,000 to COP 813,090,250. The median price per square metre is COP 5,896,226/m² and the median listed size is 110 m². That is 44% above the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 470,500. 96.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
