Sale prices of homes in the Usme locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in the Usme locality, Bogotá is COP 200,000,000, based on 262 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 140,000,000 to COP 350,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,169,811/m² and the median listed size is 51 m². That is 77% below the study's reference median (COP 865,852,573). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 95,562. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 25.2% of listings include parking.
