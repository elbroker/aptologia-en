Sale prices of apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile (prices in UF) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile is UF 12,700, based on 261 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
