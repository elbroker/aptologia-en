Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Tetelpan, Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Tetelpan, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 4,267,950, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 3,380,600 to MXN 4,801,800. The median price per square metre is MXN 52,336/m² and the median listed size is 77 m². That is 14% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 4,990,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
