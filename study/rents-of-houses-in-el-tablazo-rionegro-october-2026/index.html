Rents of houses in El Tablazo, Rionegro — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses in El Tablazo, Rionegro is COP 13,500,000 per month, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 8,225,000 to COP 18,750,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 41,566/m² and the median listed size is 350 m². That is 29% above the study's reference median for Rionegro (COP 10,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,000,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 98.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
