Sale prices of homes in estrato 5 in the Suba locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes in estrato 5 for sale in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 1,290,000,000, based on 4,233 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 880,000,000 to COP 1,800,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,660,377/m² and the median listed size is 235 m². That is 12% above the study's reference median (COP 1,150,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 761,000. 97.3% of listings include parking.
