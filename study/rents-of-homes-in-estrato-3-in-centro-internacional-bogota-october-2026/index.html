Rents of homes in estrato 3 in Centro Internacional, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 3 in Centro Internacional, Bogotá is COP 2,000,000 per month, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,650,000 to COP 2,600,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 58,824/m² and the median listed size is 37 m². That is 20% below the study's reference median (COP 2,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 267,500. 21.1% of listings include parking.
