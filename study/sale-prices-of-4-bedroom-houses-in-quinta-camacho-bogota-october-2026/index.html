Sale prices of 4+ bedroom houses in Quinta Camacho, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for 4+ bedroom houses for sale in Quinta Camacho, Bogotá is COP 2,700,000,000, based on 119 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,990,000,000 to COP 3,600,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,240,250/m² and the median listed size is 435 m². That is 116% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,250,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 99,999. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 81.5% of listings include parking.
