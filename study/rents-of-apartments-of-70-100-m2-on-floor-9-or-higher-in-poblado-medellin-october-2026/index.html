Rents of apartments of 70–100 m² on floor 9 or higher in Poblado, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² on floor 9 or higher in Poblado, Medellín is COP 5,000,000 per month, based on 65 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,500,000 to COP 6,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 59,861/m² and the median listed size is 84 m². That is 4% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 80% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
