Rents of apartments with parking in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with parking in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 830 per month, based on 208 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 600 to USD 1,200. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 9.07/m² and the median listed size is 92 m². That is 46% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 570). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 10. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
