Rents of apartments of 70–100 m² with parking in estrato 3 in Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² with parking in estrato 3 in Bogotá is COP 2,500,000 per month, based on 126 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,025,000 to COP 3,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 33,225/m² and the median listed size is 77 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 350,500. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
