Homes for sale for COP 400–700 million in Tunja — October 2026

Homes listed for sale for COP 400–700 million in Tunja have a median asking price of COP 495,000,000, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 432,500,000 to COP 582,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,169,014/m² and the median listed size is 170 m². That is 27% above the study's reference median (COP 390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 345,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 84.7% of listings include parking.
