Sale prices of homes under 45 m² in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes under 45 m² for sale in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá is COP 181,440,000, based on 95 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 155,000,000 to COP 220,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,340,909/m² and the median listed size is 38 m². That is 43% below the study's reference median (COP 319,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 130,000. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 10.5% of listings include parking.
