Rents of 3-bedroom apartments in Cúcuta — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3-bedroom apartments in Cúcuta is COP 1,300,000 per month, based on 87 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 900,000 to COP 1,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 19,549/m² and the median listed size is 62 m². That is 4% above the study's reference median (COP 1,250,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 265,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 56.3% of listings include parking.
