Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 375,000, based on 440 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 290,000 to USD 451,250. The median price per square metre is USD 3,947/m² and the median listed size is 92 m². That is 21% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 310,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 700. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
