Rents of 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in Monterrey, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in Monterrey, Mexico is MXN 25,000 per month, based on 99 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 21,000 to MXN 30,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 126/m² and the median listed size is 192 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Monterrey (MXN 33,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
