Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom in Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom in Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 16,400 per month, based on 60 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 13,425 to MXN 23,625. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 205/m² and the median listed size is 81 m². That is 49% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 32,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
