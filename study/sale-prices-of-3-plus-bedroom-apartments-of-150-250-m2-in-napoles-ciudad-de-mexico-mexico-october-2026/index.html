Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² in Nápoles, Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Nápoles, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 8,950,000, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 7,340,811 to MXN 9,960,804. The median price per square metre is MXN 45,313/m² and the median listed size is 192 m². That is 79% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 4,990,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
