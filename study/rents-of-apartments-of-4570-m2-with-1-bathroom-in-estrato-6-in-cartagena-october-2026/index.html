Rents of apartments of 45–70 m² with 1 bathroom in estrato 6 in Cartagena — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² with 1 bathroom in estrato 6 in Cartagena is COP 3,581,011 per month, based on 49 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,950,000 to COP 4,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 59,183/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 3% below the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 3,700,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 500,000. 80% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
