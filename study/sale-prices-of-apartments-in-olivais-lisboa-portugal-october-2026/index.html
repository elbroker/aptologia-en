Sale prices of apartments in Olivais, Lisboa, Portugal — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Olivais, Lisboa, Portugal is EUR 394,900, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 359,900 to EUR 498,000. The median price per square metre is EUR 4,973/m² and the median listed size is 84 m². That is 40% below the study's reference median for Lisboa (EUR 660,000). 11.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
