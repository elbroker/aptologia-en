Sale prices of apartments with 2 bathrooms 6–20 years old in estrato 5 in Bella Suiza, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with 2 bathrooms 6–20 years old in estrato 5 for sale in Bella Suiza, Bogotá is COP 685,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
