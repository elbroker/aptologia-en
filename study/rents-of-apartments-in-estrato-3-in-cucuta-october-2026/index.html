Rents of apartments in estrato 3 in Cúcuta — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in estrato 3 in Cúcuta is COP 1,100,000 per month, based on 67 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 900,000 to COP 1,400,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 20,163/m² and the median listed size is 55.1 m². That is 12% below the study's reference median (COP 1,250,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 230,000. 37.3% of listings include parking.
