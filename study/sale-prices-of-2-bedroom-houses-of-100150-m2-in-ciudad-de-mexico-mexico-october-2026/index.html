Sale prices of 2-bedroom houses of 100–150 m² in Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom houses of 100–150 m² for sale in Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 6,500,000, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 5,200,000 to MXN 7,471,035. The median price per square metre is MXN 53,993/m² and the median listed size is 127 m². That is 56% below the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 14,750,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
