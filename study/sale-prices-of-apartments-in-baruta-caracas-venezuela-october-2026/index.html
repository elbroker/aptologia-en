Sale prices of apartments in Baruta, Caracas, Venezuela — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Baruta, Caracas, Venezuela is USD 265,000, based on 389 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 170,000 to USD 395,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,500/m² and the median listed size is 143 m². That is 10% above the study's reference median for Caracas (USD 240,000). 98.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
