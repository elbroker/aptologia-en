Rents of houses with 2 bathrooms in José Ignacio, Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses with 2 bathrooms in José Ignacio, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 12,215 per month, based on 62 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 6,175 to USD 17,875. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 54.7/m² and the median listed size is 159 m². That is 67% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 7,300). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
