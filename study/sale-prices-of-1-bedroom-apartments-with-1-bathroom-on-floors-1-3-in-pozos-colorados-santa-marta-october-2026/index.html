Sale prices of 1-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom on floors 1–3 in Pozos Colorados, Santa Marta — October 2026

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments with 1 bathroom on floors 1–3 for sale in Pozos Colorados, Santa Marta is COP 405,000,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 356,750,000 to COP 436,925,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,858,453/m² and the median listed size is 38.5 m². That is 32% below the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 360,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
