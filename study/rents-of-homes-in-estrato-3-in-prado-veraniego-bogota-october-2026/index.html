Rents of homes in estrato 3 in Prado Veraniego, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in estrato 3 in Prado Veraniego, Bogotá is COP 2,000,000 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
