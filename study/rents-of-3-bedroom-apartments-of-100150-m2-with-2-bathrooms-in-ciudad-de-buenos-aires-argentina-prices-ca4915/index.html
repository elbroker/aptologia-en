Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in ARS) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is ARS 1,800,000 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from ARS 1,500,000 to ARS 2,175,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is ARS 15,126/m² and the median listed size is 119 m². That is 112% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (ARS 850,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
