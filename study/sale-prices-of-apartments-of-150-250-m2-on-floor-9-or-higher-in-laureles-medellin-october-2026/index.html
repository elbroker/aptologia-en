Sale prices of apartments of 150–250 m² on floor 9 or higher in Laureles, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² on floor 9 or higher for sale in Laureles, Medellín is COP 1,450,000,000, based on 66 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,212,500,000 to COP 1,744,625,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,631,599/m² and the median listed size is 198 m². That is 73% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 920,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 95.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
