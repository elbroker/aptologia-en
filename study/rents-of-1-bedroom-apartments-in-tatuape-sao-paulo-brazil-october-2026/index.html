Rents of 1-bedroom apartments in Tatuapé, São Paulo, Brazil — October 2026

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in Tatuapé, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 2,525 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 1,762 to BRL 3,175. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 58.8/m² and the median listed size is 38 m². That is 16% below the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 570. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
