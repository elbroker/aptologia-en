Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments in Zona Centro, Envigado — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Zona Centro, Envigado is COP 802,500,000, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 646,250,000 to COP 1,195,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,125,000/m² and the median listed size is 111 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 850,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 600,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 96.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
