Sale prices of 3-bedroom apartments in estrato 1 in Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for 3-bedroom apartments in estrato 1 for sale in Bogotá is COP 280,000,000, based on 101 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
