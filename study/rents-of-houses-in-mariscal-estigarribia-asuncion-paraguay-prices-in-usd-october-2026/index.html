Rents of houses in Mariscal Estigarribia, Asunción, Paraguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses in Mariscal Estigarribia, Asunción, Paraguay is USD 2,500 per month, based on 87 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,000 to USD 4,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 6.63/m² and the median listed size is 450 m². That is 11% below the study's reference median for Asunción (USD 2,800). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
