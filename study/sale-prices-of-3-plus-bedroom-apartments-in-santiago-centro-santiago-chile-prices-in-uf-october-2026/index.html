Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile (prices in UF) — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments for sale in Santiago Centro, Santiago, Chile is UF 4,200, based on 753 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
