Sale prices of apartments of 70–100 m² on floors 4–8 in Chicó Norte, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 70–100 m² on floors 4–8 for sale in Chicó Norte, Bogotá is COP 853,000,000, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 682,500,000 to COP 995,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,033,445/m² and the median listed size is 84.3 m². That is 14% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 750,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 840,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
