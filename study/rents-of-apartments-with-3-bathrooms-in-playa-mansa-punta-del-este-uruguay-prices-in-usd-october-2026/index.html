Rents of apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Playa Mansa, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 6,000 per month, based on 307 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 4,050 to USD 11,650. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 39.1/m² and the median listed size is 163 m². That is 25% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 4,800). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 1,200. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
