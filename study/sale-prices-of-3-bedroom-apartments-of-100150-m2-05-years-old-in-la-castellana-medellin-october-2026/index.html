Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² 0–5 years old in La Castellana, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² 0–5 years old for sale in La Castellana, Medellín is COP 885,000,000, based on 75 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 698,000,000 to COP 955,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,896,552/m² and the median listed size is 117 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 417,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 94.6% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
