Sale prices of apartments in Chapinero Central, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Chapinero Central, Bogotá is COP 370,000,000, based on 105 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 278,500,000 to COP 523,980,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,250,000/m² and the median listed size is 44 m². That is 52% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 333,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 49.5% of listings include parking.
