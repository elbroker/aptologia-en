Sale prices of homes of 45–70 m² in the Suba locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes of 45–70 m² for sale in the Suba locality, Bogotá is COP 346,500,000, based on 1,180 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 270,000,000 to COP 450,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,000,000/m² and the median listed size is 57.6 m². That is 70% below the study's reference median (COP 1,150,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 309,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 66% of listings include parking.
