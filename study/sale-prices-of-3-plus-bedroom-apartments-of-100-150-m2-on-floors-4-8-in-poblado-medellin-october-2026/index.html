Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² on floors 4–8 in Poblado, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² on floors 4–8 for sale in Poblado, Medellín is COP 950,000,000, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 868,750,000 to COP 987,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,397,789/m² and the median listed size is 126 m². That is 13% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 842,500. The most frequent estrato is 6, 92.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
