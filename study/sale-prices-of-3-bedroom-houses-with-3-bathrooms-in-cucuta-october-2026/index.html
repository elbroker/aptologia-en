Sale prices of 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms in Cúcuta — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Cúcuta is COP 600,000,000, based on 791 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 400,000,000 to COP 900,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,095,238/m² and the median listed size is 214 m². That is 20% above the study's reference median for Cúcuta (COP 500,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 310,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 94.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
