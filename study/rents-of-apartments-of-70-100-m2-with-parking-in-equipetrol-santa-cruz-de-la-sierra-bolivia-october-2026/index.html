Rents of apartments of 70–100 m² with parking in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² with parking in Equipetrol, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 790 per month, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 600 to USD 963. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 9.3/m² and the median listed size is 81 m². That is 39% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 570). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 1. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
