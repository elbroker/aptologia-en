Sale prices of apartments in Cercado de Lima, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Cercado de Lima, Lima, Peru is PEN 410,000, based on 319 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 328,020 to PEN 483,890. The median price per square metre is PEN 6,502/m² and the median listed size is 64.5 m². That is 34% below the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 621,600). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PEN 1. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
