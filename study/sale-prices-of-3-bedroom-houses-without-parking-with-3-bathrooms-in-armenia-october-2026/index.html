Sale prices of 3+ bedroom houses without parking with 3+ bathrooms in Armenia — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses without parking with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Armenia is COP 700,000,000, based on 66 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 505,000,000 to COP 1,100,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,838,384/m² and the median listed size is 214 m². That is 30% below the study's reference median for Armenia (COP 1,000,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 3 and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
