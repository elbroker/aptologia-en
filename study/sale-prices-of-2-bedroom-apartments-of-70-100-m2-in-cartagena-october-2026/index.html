Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Cartagena — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Cartagena is COP 750,000,000, based on 1,077 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 573,000,000 to COP 950,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,424,658/m² and the median listed size is 86 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median for Cartagena (COP 800,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 673,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 95.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
