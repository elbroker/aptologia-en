Rents of apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 65,000 per month, based on 943 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 45,000 to MXN 91,732. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 296/m² and the median listed size is 220 m². The median number of bathrooms is 3.
