Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Bucaramanga — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Bucaramanga is COP 495,000,000, based on 195 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 400,000,000 to COP 590,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,400,000/m² and the median listed size is 112 m². That is 15% above the study's reference median for Bucaramanga (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 461,500. The most frequent estrato is 4, 95.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
