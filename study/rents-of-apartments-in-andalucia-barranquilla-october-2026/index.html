Rents of apartments in Andalucía, Barranquilla — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Andalucía, Barranquilla is COP 2,600,000 per month, based on 51 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,950,000 to COP 3,221,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 32,585/m² and the median listed size is 87 m². That is 7% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 489,712. The most frequent estrato is 5, 98% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
