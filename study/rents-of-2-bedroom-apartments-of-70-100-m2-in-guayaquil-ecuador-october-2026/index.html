Rents of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Guayaquil, Ecuador — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Guayaquil, Ecuador is USD 850 per month, based on 293 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 600 to USD 1,150. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 10.2/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (USD 850). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
