Sale prices of 1-bedroom apartments under 45 m² in Balvanera, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for 1-bedroom apartments under 45 m² for sale in Balvanera, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 64,450, based on 74 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
