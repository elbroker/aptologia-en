Rents of homes up to 5 years old in the Bosa locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes up to 5 years old in the Bosa locality, Bogotá is COP 1,064,674 per month, based on 104 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 900,000 to COP 1,288,900. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 22,500/m² and the median listed size is 45 m². That is 3% below the study's reference median (COP 1,100,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 111,050. The most frequent estrato is 2 and 13.5% of listings include parking.
