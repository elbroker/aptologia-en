Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² in La Castellana, Armenia — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² for sale in La Castellana, Armenia is COP 795,000,000, based on 45 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 650,000,000 to COP 830,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,640,000/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 109% above the study's reference median for Armenia (COP 380,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 730,500. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
