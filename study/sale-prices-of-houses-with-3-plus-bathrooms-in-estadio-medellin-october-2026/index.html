Sale prices of houses with 3+ bathrooms in Estadio, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking price for houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Estadio, Medellín is COP 1,175,000,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 912,500,000 to COP 1,787,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,250,977/m² and the median listed size is 205 m². That is 15% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 1,390,000,000). The most frequent estrato is 5 and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
