Sale prices of apartments more than 20 years old in Palermo, Manizales — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments more than 20 years old for sale in Palermo, Manizales is COP 560,000,000, based on 33 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 480,000,000 to COP 650,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,500,000/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 24% above the study's reference median for Manizales (COP 450,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 290,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 90.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
