Rents of 2-bedroom apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms in São Paulo, Brazil — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms in São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 5,500 per month, based on 1,437 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 3,500 to BRL 7,999. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 73/m² and the median listed size is 70 m². That is 52% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,202. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
