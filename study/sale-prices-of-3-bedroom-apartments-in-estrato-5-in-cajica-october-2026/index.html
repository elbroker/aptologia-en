Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments in estrato 5 in Cajicá — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments in estrato 5 for sale in Cajicá is COP 660,000,000, based on 181 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 500,000,000 to COP 780,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,637,931/m² and the median listed size is 93 m². That is 69% above the study's reference median for Cajicá (COP 390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 450,000. 98.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
