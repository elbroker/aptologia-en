Sale prices of apartments in Pasadena, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Pasadena, Bogotá is COP 780,000,000, based on 211 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 569,000,000 to COP 950,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 7,796,743/m² and the median listed size is 103 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 765,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 800,000. The most frequent estrato is 5 and 94.8% of listings include parking.
