Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in ARS) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is ARS 1,300,000 per month, based on 92 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from ARS 1,137,500 to ARS 1,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is ARS 16,242/m² and the median listed size is 79 m². That is 53% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (ARS 850,000). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
