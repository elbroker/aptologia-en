Sale prices of apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is USD 249,000, based on 110 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 220,169 to USD 290,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,168/m² and the median listed size is 117 m². That is 3% below the study's reference median for Lima (USD 258,023). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
