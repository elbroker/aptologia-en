How much a parking lot is worth in Bogotá: price difference for sale and for rent per estrato (October 2026)

Machine-assisted English version of the Spanish original by Aptología. All figures are locked to the original (numbers are copied, not re-translated); if in doubt, the Spanish original prevails.

In Bogotá, an apartment for sale with a parking lot asks in the median $40 million more than a comparable one without a parking lot, and one with two parking lots asks for $105 million more than a comparable one. For rent, having a parking lot increases the rent by $200,000 a month in front of an equal apartment without it.

Abstract: We compare 36,813 apartments for sale and 14,931 apartments for rent, unique, with and without parking, within comparable groups.

Methodology

  • Source: live housing ads collected by the intake of Aptology in Metrocuadrado, Fincaraíz, Cienaltes, Wasi, Habi, Houm and Domus, from 11 to 18 October of 2026. Court: 18 October of 2026.
  • Debugging: the same property published several times is counted only once (grouping by portal and, between portals, by price, area, rooms and coordinates). Prices and areas out of range are excluded and, for rent, HOA fee is subtracted when the portal includes it in the rental.
  • What is measured: prices ordered (published), not closing prices.
  • Matched comparison: The difference in the median price between apartments with and without parking lot within groups of the same estrato, locality, number of rooms and area range is calculated (6 ads or more on each side; for 2 vs. 1 also the number of bathrooms), and is summarized with the weighted median of these differences. By estrato only figures are shown with 10 comparable groups or more. It's not an avalano from the parking lot.
  • Limitations: are prices ordered in portals, which may differ from the final value of rental or sale. The coverage is higher in the north of Bogotá and in estratos 4 to 6, because the published offer is concentrated there. The neighborhoods and the town come from what each portal writes; when the town is missing it is assigned by the nearest neighbouring ads.
  • Open data: datos.csv, CC BY license 4. 0.
