Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with parking in Santiago, Chile (prices in UF) — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with parking for sale in Santiago, Chile is UF 9,725, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UF 8,921 to UF 10,775. The median price per square metre is UF 86.1/m² and the median listed size is 111 m². That is 40% above the study's reference median for Santiago (UF 6,950). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
