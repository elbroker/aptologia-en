Sale prices of apartments of 100–150 m² in Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 100–150 m² for sale in Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 6,345,404, based on 1,915 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 5,200,000 to MXN 7,700,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 53,647/m² and the median listed size is 119 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
