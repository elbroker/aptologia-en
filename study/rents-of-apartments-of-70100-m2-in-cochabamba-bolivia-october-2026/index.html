Rents of apartments of 70–100 m² in Cochabamba, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² in Cochabamba, Bolivia is USD 350 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 300 to USD 658. The median listed size is 86 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
