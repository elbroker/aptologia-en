Rents of apartments with parking in Valle del Lili, Cali — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with parking in Valle del Lili, Cali is COP 1,800,000 per month, based on 97 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,550,000 to COP 2,700,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 27,500/m² and the median listed size is 67 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median for Cali (COP 1,900,000). The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
