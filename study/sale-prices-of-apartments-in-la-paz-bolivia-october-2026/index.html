Sale prices of apartments in La Paz, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in La Paz, Bolivia is USD 130,000, based on 481 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 102,000 to USD 175,000. The median price per square metre is USD 1,099/m² and the median listed size is 131 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 50. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
