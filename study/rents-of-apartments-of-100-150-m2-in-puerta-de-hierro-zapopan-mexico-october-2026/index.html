Rents of apartments of 100–150 m² in Puerta de Hierro, Zapopan, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 100–150 m² in Puerta de Hierro, Zapopan, Mexico is MXN 48,999 per month, based on 75 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 38,000 to MXN 55,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 378/m² and the median listed size is 129 m². That is 75% above the study's reference median for Zapopan (MXN 28,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
