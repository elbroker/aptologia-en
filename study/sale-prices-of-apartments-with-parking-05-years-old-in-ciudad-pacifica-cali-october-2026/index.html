Sale prices of apartments with parking 0–5 years old in Ciudad Pacífica, Cali — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments with parking 0–5 years old for sale in Ciudad Pacífica, Cali is COP 290,000,000, based on 69 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 235,000,000 to COP 420,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,310,345/m² and the median listed size is 72 m². That is 33% below the study's reference median for Cali (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 295,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
