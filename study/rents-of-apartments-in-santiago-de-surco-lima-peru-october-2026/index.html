Rents of apartments in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Santiago de Surco, Lima, Peru is PEN 3,500 per month, based on 408 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 2,600 to PEN 5,700. The median monthly rent per square metre is PEN 27/m² and the median listed size is 124 m². That is 15% above the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 3,050). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PEN 250. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
