Rents of apartments with a balcony in Laureles, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony in Laureles, Medellín is COP 4,400,000 per month, based on 1,203 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,500,000 to COP 5,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 40,800/m² and the median listed size is 107 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 414,000. The most frequent estrato is 5, 90.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
