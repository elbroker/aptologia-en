Rents of apartments of 150–250 m² in estrato 6 in Altos de Riomar, Barranquilla — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 150–250 m² in estrato 6 in Altos de Riomar, Barranquilla is COP 6,600,000 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,275,000 to COP 7,819,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 34,905/m² and the median listed size is 167 m². That is 136% above the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
