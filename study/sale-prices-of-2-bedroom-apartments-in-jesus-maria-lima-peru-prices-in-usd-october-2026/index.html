Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments in Jesús María, Lima, Peru (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Jesús María, Lima, Peru is USD 150,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 130,420 to USD 172,084. The median price per square metre is USD 2,251/m² and the median listed size is 63 m². That is 28% below the study's reference median for Lima (USD 209,164). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
