Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments listed by the owner in Zipaquirá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments listed by the owner in Zipaquirá is COP 1,450,000 per month, based on 35 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,110,000 to COP 1,557,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 23,077/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². That is 12% above the study's reference median for Zipaquirá (COP 1,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 233,000. The most frequent estrato is 3, 78.1% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
