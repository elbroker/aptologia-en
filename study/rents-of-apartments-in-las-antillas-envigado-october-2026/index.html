Rents of apartments in Las Antillas, Envigado — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Las Antillas, Envigado is COP 3,200,000 per month, based on 92 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,850,000 to COP 3,600,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 42,308/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². That is 27% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 4,400,000). The most frequent estrato is 4, 90% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
