Sale prices of apartments of 150–250 m² in Lordelo do Ouro e Massarelos, Oporto, Portugal — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Lordelo do Ouro e Massarelos, Oporto, Portugal is EUR 1,190,000, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 855,000 to EUR 1,345,000. The median price per square metre is EUR 6,234/m² and the median listed size is 179 m². That is 180% above the study's reference median for Oporto (EUR 425,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
