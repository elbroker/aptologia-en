Rents of apartments in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in the San Cristóbal locality, Bogotá is COP 1,350,000 per month, based on 68 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,200,000 to COP 1,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 29,583/m² and the median listed size is 45 m². That is in line with the study's reference median (COP 1,350,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 183,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 22.1% of listings include parking.
