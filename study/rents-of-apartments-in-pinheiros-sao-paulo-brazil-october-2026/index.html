Rents of apartments in Pinheiros, São Paulo, Brazil — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Pinheiros, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 6,345 per month, based on 506 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 4,255 to BRL 9,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 104/m² and the median listed size is 68.5 m². That is 75% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 1,450. 90.2% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
