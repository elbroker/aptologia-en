Sale prices of houses in Castilla, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for houses for sale in Castilla, Bogotá is COP 700,000,000, based on 63 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 522,500,000 to COP 895,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,646,018/m² and the median listed size is 163 m². That is 42% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,200,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 220,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 74.6% of listings include parking.
