Rents of houses of 150–250 m² in Guayaquil, Ecuador — October 2026

The median asking rent for houses of 150–250 m² in Guayaquil, Ecuador is USD 1,200 per month, based on 143 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 855 to USD 1,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 6.12/m² and the median listed size is 180 m². The median number of bathrooms is 3.
