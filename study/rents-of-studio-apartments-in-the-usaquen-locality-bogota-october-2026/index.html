Rents of studio apartments in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for studio apartments in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá is COP 2,600,000 per month, based on 665 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,000,000 to COP 3,300,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 68,889/m² and the median listed size is 37.6 m². That is 43% below the study's reference median (COP 4,600,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 400,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 62.9% of listings include parking.
