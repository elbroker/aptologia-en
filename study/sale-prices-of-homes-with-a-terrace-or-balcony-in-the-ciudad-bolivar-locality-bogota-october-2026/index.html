Sale prices of homes with a terrace or balcony in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes with a terrace or balcony for sale in the Ciudad Bolívar locality, Bogotá is COP 250,000,000, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 220,000,000 to COP 385,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,210,526/m² and the median listed size is 53 m². That is 4% above the study's reference median (COP 241,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 160,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 24.6% of listings include parking.
