Sale prices of houses with 2 bathrooms in estrato 6 in Santa Marta — October 2026

The median asking price for houses with 2 bathrooms in estrato 6 for sale in Santa Marta is COP 575,000,000, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
