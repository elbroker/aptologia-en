Sale prices of apartments in Renacimiento, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Renacimiento, Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic is USD 260,000, based on 355 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 201,075 to USD 327,500. The median price per square metre is USD 1,886/m² and the median listed size is 145 m². That is 8% above the study's reference median for Santo Domingo (USD 240,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
