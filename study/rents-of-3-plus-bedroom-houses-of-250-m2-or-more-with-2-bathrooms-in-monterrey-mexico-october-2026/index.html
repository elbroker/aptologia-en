Rents of 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms in Monterrey, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom houses of 250 m² or more with 2 bathrooms in Monterrey, Mexico is MXN 33,500 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 26,250 to MXN 39,375. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 116/m² and the median listed size is 288 m². That is 2% above the study's reference median for Monterrey (MXN 33,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
