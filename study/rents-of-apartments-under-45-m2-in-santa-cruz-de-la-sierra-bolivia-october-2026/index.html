Rents of apartments under 45 m² in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments under 45 m² in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 371 per month, based on 95 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 300 to USD 470. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 10/m² and the median listed size is 40 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 50. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
