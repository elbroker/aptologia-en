Sale prices of apartments on floors 1–3 in Bavaria, Santa Marta — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments on floors 1–3 for sale in Bavaria, Santa Marta is COP 425,000,000, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 367,500,000 to COP 760,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 3,938,776/m² and the median listed size is 121 m². That is 29% below the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 674,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
