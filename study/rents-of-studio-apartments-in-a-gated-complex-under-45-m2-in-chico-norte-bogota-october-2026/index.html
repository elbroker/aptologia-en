Rents of studio apartments in a gated complex under 45 m² in Chicó Norte, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for studio apartments in a gated complex under 45 m² in Chicó Norte, Bogotá is COP 2,825,000 per month, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,380,000 to COP 3,125,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 105,556/m² and the median listed size is 24.9 m². That is 49% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 1,900,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 66.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
