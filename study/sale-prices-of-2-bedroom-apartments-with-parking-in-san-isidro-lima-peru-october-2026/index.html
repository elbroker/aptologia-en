Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments with parking in San Isidro, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with parking for sale in San Isidro, Lima, Peru is PEN 794,000, based on 171 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 579,500 to PEN 1,131,894. The median price per square metre is PEN 8,878/m² and the median listed size is 90 m². That is 28% above the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 621,600). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
