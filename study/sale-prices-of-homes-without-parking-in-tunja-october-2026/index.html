Sale prices of homes without parking in Tunja — October 2026

The median asking price for homes without parking for sale in Tunja is COP 238,000,000, based on 51 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 183,500,000 to COP 395,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,139,669/m² and the median listed size is 58 m². That is 39% below the study's reference median (COP 390,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 115,200. The most frequent estrato is 3.
