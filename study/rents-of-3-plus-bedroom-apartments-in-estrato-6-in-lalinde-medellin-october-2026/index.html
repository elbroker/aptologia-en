Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments in estrato 6 in Lalinde, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in estrato 6 in Lalinde, Medellín is COP 7,900,000 per month, based on 38 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 6,625,000 to COP 11,750,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 59,942/m² and the median listed size is 130 m². That is 65% above the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 4,800,000). 97.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
