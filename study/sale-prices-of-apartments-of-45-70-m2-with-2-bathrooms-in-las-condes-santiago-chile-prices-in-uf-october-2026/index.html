Sale prices of apartments of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile (prices in UF) — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Las Condes, Santiago, Chile is UF 6,900, based on 122 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
