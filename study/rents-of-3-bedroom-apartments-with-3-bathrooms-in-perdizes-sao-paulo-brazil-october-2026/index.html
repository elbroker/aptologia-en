Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Perdizes, São Paulo, Brazil — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Perdizes, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 9,800 per month, based on 80 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 7,000 to BRL 14,388. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 59.2/m² and the median listed size is 170 m². That is 171% above the study's reference median for São Paulo (BRL 3,621). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 2,766. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 4.
