Rents of apartments listed by the owner of 70–100 m² in estrato 4 in Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments listed by the owner of 70–100 m² in estrato 4 in Bogotá is COP 3,400,000 per month, based on 176 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,894,000 to COP 3,800,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 40,846/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 3% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 494,150. 92.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
