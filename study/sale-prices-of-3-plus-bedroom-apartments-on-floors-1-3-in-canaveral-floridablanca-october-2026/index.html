Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments on floors 1–3 in Cañaveral, Floridablanca — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments on floors 1–3 for sale in Cañaveral, Floridablanca is COP 422,500,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 380,000,000 to COP 583,750,000. The median price per square metre is COP 4,794,715/m² and the median listed size is 97.5 m². That is 4% below the study's reference median for Floridablanca (COP 440,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 481,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
