Rents of apartments with a balcony in estrato 6 in Bocagrande, Cartagena — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with a balcony in estrato 6 in Bocagrande, Cartagena is COP 6,000,000 per month, based on 89 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,200,000 to COP 8,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 51,064/m² and the median listed size is 113 m².
