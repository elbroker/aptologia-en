Rents of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with parking in Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in UYU) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with parking in Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 45,000 per month, based on 101 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 39,500 to UYU 55,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 584/m² and the median listed size is 76 m². That is 50% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 30,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 8,000. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
