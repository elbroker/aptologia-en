Sale prices of homes on floors 1–3 in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes on floors 1–3 for sale in the Barrios Unidos locality, Bogotá is COP 850,000,000, based on 371 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 560,000,000 to COP 1,300,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,129,126/m² and the median listed size is 142 m². That is 1% below the study's reference median (COP 860,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 499,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 74.1% of listings include parking.
