Sale prices of apartments in Cabañas, Bello — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Cabañas, Bello is COP 399,900,000, based on 119 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 330,000,000 to COP 457,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,000,595/m² and the median listed size is 79.5 m². That is 18% above the study's reference median for Bello (COP 340,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 280,000. The most frequent estrato is 4, 64.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
