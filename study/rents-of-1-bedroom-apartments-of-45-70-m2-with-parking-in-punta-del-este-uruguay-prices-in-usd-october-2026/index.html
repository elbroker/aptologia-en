Rents of 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking in Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking in Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 2,455 per month, based on 117 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,400 to USD 3,800. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 42.5/m² and the median listed size is 56 m². That is 49% below the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 4,800). The median number of bathrooms is 1.
