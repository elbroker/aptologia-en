Sale prices of apartments on floors 4–8 in estrato 6 in Bello Horizonte, Santa Marta — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments on floors 4–8 in estrato 6 for sale in Bello Horizonte, Santa Marta is COP 799,000,000, based on 136 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 557,500,000 to COP 1,380,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 10,465,116/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 33% above the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 600,000,000).
