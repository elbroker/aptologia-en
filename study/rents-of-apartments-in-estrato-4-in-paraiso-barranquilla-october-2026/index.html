Rents of apartments in estrato 4 in Paraíso, Barranquilla — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in estrato 4 in Paraíso, Barranquilla is COP 2,120,000 per month, based on 72 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,739,844 to COP 2,651,250. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 31,667/m² and the median listed size is 65.5 m². That is 24% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 265,000. 89.3% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
