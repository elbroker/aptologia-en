Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments on floors 4–8 0–5 years old in Belén, Medellín — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments on floors 4–8 0–5 years old for sale in Belén, Medellín is COP 599,000,000, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 462,750,000 to COP 831,250,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,375,676/m² and the median listed size is 95 m². That is 29% below the study's reference median for Medellín (COP 840,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 326,500. The most frequent estrato is 5 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
