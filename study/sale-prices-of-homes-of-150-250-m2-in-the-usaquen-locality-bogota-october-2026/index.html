Sale prices of homes of 150–250 m² in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes of 150–250 m² for sale in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá is COP 1,340,000,000, based on 3,766 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,080,000,000 to COP 1,650,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 6,918,579/m² and the median listed size is 189 m². That is 30% above the study's reference median (COP 1,030,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 1,350,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 98.4% of listings include parking.
