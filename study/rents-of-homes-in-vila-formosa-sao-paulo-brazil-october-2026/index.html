Rents of homes in Vila Formosa, São Paulo, Brazil — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in Vila Formosa, São Paulo, Brazil is BRL 2,700 per month, based on 113 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from BRL 1,900 to BRL 4,416. The median monthly rent per square metre is BRL 40.4/m² and the median listed size is 60 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is BRL 478. 81.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
