Rents of apartments of 100–150 m² in Puerto Madero, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 100–150 m² in Puerto Madero, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 3,050 per month, based on 36 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 2,575 to USD 4,200. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 25.1/m² and the median listed size is 125 m². That is 88% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 1,625). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
