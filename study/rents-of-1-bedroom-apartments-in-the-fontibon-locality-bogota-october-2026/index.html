Rents of 1-bedroom apartments in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom apartments in the Fontibón locality, Bogotá is COP 1,700,000 per month, based on 78 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,554,994 to COP 1,975,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 56,712/m² and the median listed size is 30 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median (COP 1,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 200,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 23.1% of listings include parking.
