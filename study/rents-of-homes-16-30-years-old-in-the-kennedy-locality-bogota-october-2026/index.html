Rents of homes 16–30 years old in the Kennedy locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes 16–30 years old in the Kennedy locality, Bogotá is COP 1,837,500 per month, based on 52 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,400,000 to COP 2,551,625. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 24,495/m² and the median listed size is 71 m². That is 23% above the study's reference median (COP 1,500,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 252,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 40.4% of listings include parking.
