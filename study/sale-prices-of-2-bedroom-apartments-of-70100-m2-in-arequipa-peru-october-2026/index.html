Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Arequipa, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² for sale in Arequipa, Peru is PEN 366,994, based on 39 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 275,124 to PEN 420,000. The median price per square metre is PEN 4,067/m² and the median listed size is 81 m². That is 27% below the study's reference median for Arequipa (PEN 501,350). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
