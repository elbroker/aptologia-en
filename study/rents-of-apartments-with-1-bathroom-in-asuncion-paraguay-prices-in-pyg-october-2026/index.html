Rents of apartments with 1 bathroom in Asunción, Paraguay (prices in PYG) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with 1 bathroom in Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 4,500,000 per month, based on 1,115 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 3,700,000 to PYG 5,200,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is PYG 78,125/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PYG 200,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
