Sale prices of 2-bedroom houses in Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom houses for sale in Lima, Peru is PEN 612,601, based on 57 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 356,250 to PEN 1,050,000. The median price per square metre is PEN 4,590/m² and the median listed size is 125 m². That is 72% below the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 2,173,590). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
