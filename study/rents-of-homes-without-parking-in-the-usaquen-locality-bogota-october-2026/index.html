Rents of homes without parking in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes without parking in the Usaquén locality, Bogotá is COP 2,000,000 per month, based on 408 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,500,000 to COP 3,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,366/m² and the median listed size is 40 m². That is 57% below the study's reference median (COP 4,600,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 300,000. The most frequent estrato is 3.
