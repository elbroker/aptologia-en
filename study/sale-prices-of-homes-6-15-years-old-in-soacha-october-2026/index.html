Sale prices of homes 6–15 years old in Soacha — October 2026

The median asking price for homes 6–15 years old for sale in Soacha is COP 153,000,000, based on 151 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 140,000,000 to COP 169,060,359. The median price per square metre is COP 3,085,106/m² and the median listed size is 50 m². That is 2% below the study's reference median (COP 155,935,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 85,356. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 0.7% of listings include parking.
