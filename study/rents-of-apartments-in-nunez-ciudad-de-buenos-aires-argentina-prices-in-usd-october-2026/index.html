Rents of apartments in Núñez, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Núñez, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 2,200 per month, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 1,150 to USD 4,200. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 23.3/m² and the median listed size is 87 m². That is 35% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 1,625). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
