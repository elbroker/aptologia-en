Rents of apartments of 45–70 m² in Ñuñoa, Santiago, Chile — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in Ñuñoa, Santiago, Chile is CLP 620,000 per month, based on 197 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from CLP 550,000 to CLP 700,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is CLP 11,165/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². That is 38% above the study's reference median for Santiago (CLP 450,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
