Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments in estrato 5 in Bellavista, Cali — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments in estrato 5 for sale in Bellavista, Cali is COP 725,000,000, based on 42 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 600,000,000 to COP 850,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,840,000/m² and the median listed size is 128 m². That is 69% above the study's reference median for Cali (COP 430,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 775,000. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
