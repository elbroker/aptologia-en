Rents of apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 6 in Santa Marta — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 100–150 m² with 3+ bathrooms in estrato 6 in Santa Marta is COP 5,950,000 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,500,000 to COP 7,500,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 46,549/m² and the median listed size is 124 m². That is 98% above the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 3,000,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
