Rents of apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms in Otraparte, Envigado — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms in Otraparte, Envigado is COP 4,150,000 per month, based on 157 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,800,000 to COP 4,650,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 50,000/m² and the median listed size is 85 m². That is 6% below the study's reference median for Envigado (COP 4,400,000). The most frequent estrato is 5 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
