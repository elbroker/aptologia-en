Sale prices of 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms in Asunción, Paraguay (prices in PYG) — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses of 150–250 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Asunción, Paraguay is PYG 850,000,000, based on 109 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PYG 650,000,000 to PYG 1,020,000,000. The median price per square metre is PYG 4,363,636/m² and the median listed size is 190 m². That is 13% below the study's reference median for Asunción (PYG 975,000,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
