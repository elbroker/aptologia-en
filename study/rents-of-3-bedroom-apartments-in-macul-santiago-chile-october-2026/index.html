Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments in Macul, Santiago, Chile — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments in Macul, Santiago, Chile is CLP 600,000 per month, based on 32 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
