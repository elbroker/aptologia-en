Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms in Arequipa, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments of 100–150 m² with 2 bathrooms for sale in Arequipa, Peru is PEN 521,342, based on 114 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 471,562 to PEN 604,425. The median price per square metre is PEN 4,420/m² and the median listed size is 120 m². That is 4% above the study's reference median for Arequipa (PEN 501,350). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
