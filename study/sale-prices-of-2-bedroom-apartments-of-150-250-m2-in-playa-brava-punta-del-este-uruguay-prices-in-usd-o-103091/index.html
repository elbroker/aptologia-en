Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Playa Brava, Punta del Este, Uruguay is USD 605,000, based on 59 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 462,500 to USD 780,000. The median price per square metre is USD 3,613/m² and the median listed size is 178 m². That is 68% above the study's reference median for Punta del Este (USD 360,000). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
