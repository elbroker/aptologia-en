Sale prices of apartments in São Domingos de Benfica, Lisboa, Portugal — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in São Domingos de Benfica, Lisboa, Portugal is EUR 629,900, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 460,000 to EUR 920,000. The median price per square metre is EUR 7,177/m² and the median listed size is 91 m². That is 5% below the study's reference median for Lisboa (EUR 660,000). 56.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
