Sale prices of apartments of 150–250 m² in Cayma, Arequipa, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 150–250 m² for sale in Cayma, Arequipa, Peru is PEN 842,900, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 721,000 to PEN 946,096. The median price per square metre is PEN 4,760/m² and the median listed size is 180 m². That is 68% above the study's reference median for Arequipa (PEN 501,350). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 3.
