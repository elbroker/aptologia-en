Sale prices of homes 6–15 years old in the Antonio Nariño locality, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes 6–15 years old for sale in the Antonio Nariño locality, Bogotá is COP 415,000,000, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 272,500,000 to COP 957,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 5,506,410/m² and the median listed size is 67.8 m². That is 39% below the study's reference median (COP 680,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 180,000. The most frequent estrato is 3 and 52.9% of listings include parking.
