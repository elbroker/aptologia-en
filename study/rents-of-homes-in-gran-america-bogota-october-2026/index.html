Rents of homes in Gran America, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for homes in Gran America, Bogotá is COP 1,840,000 per month, based on 48 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,597,500 to COP 3,037,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 60,952/m² and the median listed size is 29.5 m². That is 47% below the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,497,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 288,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and 33.3% of listings include parking.
