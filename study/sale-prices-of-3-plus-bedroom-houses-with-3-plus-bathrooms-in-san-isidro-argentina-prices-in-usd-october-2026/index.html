Sale prices of 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms in San Isidro, Argentina (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in San Isidro, Argentina is USD 575,000, based on 70 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
