Rents of apartments of 70–100 m² on floors 4–8 in Chicó Norte, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 70–100 m² on floors 4–8 in Chicó Norte, Bogotá is COP 5,850,000 per month, based on 58 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 4,800,500 to COP 7,700,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 72,381/m² and the median listed size is 80 m². That is 77% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 3,300,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 841,000. The most frequent estrato is 6, 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
