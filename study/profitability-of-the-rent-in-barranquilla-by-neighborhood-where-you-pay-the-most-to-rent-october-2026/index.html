Profitability of the rent in Barranquilla by neighborhood: where you pay the most to rent (October 2026)

Machine-assisted English version of the Spanish original by Aptología. All figures are locked to the original (numbers are copied, not re-translated); if in doubt, the Spanish original prevails.

In Barranquilla, buying an apartment to rent it yields in the typical neighborhood 8.4% gross per year: the price requested is equivalent to 11.9 years of rent. The district with the highest gross return is Altos del Prado (10.5%) and the smaller district, Alameda del Río (7.0%). For the whole city, with the median per m², the gross return implied is 8.9%.

Abstract: We compare the price per m² of 5,548 apartments for sale with the rent per m² of 2,858 for rent in 29 Barranquilla neighborhoods.

Methodology

  • Source: night events of the Aptology database (aptologia.com) of 18 October 2026: current housing ads in real estate portals of 13 countries, collected by Aptology and seen online between 11 and 18 October 2026. Court: 18 October of 2026.
  • Debugging: exact duplicates (same city, area, type, price, currency, area, rooms and bathrooms) and prices outside the percentile 1 - 99 of each country, operation, currency and type are removed; the price per m² uses only ads with declared area between 12 and 2,000 m² and is also cut to P1-P99.
  • What is measured: prices ordered (published), not closing prices. For rent, monthly rent without HOA fee, common expenses or condo.
  • Minimum sample: each published figure is based on at least 30 ads; cells with less are omitted.
  • Colombia: Prices in Colombian pesos, no currency conversion.
  • Years of rent to pay for the house: The average sales price (monthly average rental × 12) between the same type of ads and the cell; it is the price / rent ratio (price-to-rent ratio) and is equivalent to 100 - gross profitability. It does not include HOA fee, taxes, vacations, financing or recovery: it is a reference to compare to purchase and rent, not an investment recommendation.
  • Rentability by neighborhood: medium rental per m² × 12 ¬ medium price per m² of apartments in the same neighborhood; only neighborhoods with 30 ads or more for sale and 30 or more for rent with declared area. Using the m² and not the total price prevents the typical size of each neighborhood from bias the comparison.
  • Limitations: The coverage depends on the portals that travel Aptology in each country and is greater in large cities and in the medium-high and high segments; the area is the one that declares the announcement, unverified; the area names come from the portals.
  • Privacy: only aggregates are published; no personal data, link to ads or name of advertiser is published.
  • Open data: datos.csv, CC BY license 4. 0.
