Sale prices of houses with 3+ bathrooms in Baños, Cuenca, Ecuador — October 2026

The median asking price for houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Baños, Cuenca, Ecuador is USD 165,000, based on 37 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 135,000 to USD 290,000. The median price per square metre is USD 805/m² and the median listed size is 208 m². That is 23% below the study's reference median for Cuenca (USD 215,000). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
