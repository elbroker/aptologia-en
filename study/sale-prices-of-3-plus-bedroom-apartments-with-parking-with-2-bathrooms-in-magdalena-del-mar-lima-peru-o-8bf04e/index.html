Sale prices of 3+ bedroom apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms in Magdalena del Mar, Lima, Peru — October 2026

The median asking price for 3+ bedroom apartments with parking with 2 bathrooms for sale in Magdalena del Mar, Lima, Peru is PEN 663,521, based on 99 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from PEN 571,500 to PEN 791,928. The median price per square metre is PEN 7,630/m² and the median listed size is 81 m². That is 7% above the study's reference median for Lima (PEN 621,600). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is PEN 200. The median number of bathrooms is 2.
