Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Monterrey, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments with 3+ bathrooms in Monterrey, Mexico is MXN 50,000 per month, based on 111 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 37,975 to MXN 58,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is MXN 299/m² and the median listed size is 164 m². That is 92% above the study's reference median for Monterrey (MXN 26,000). The median number of bathrooms is 3.
