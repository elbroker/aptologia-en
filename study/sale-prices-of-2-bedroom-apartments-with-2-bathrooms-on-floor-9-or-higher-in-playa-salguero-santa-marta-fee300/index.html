Sale prices of 2-bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms on floor 9 or higher in Playa Salguero, Santa Marta — October 2026

The median asking price for 2-bedroom apartments with 2 bathrooms on floor 9 or higher for sale in Playa Salguero, Santa Marta is COP 730,000,000, based on 44 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 670,000,000 to COP 762,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,663,342/m² and the median listed size is 75 m². That is 22% above the study's reference median for Santa Marta (COP 600,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 622,314. The most frequent estrato is 6, 95.5% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
