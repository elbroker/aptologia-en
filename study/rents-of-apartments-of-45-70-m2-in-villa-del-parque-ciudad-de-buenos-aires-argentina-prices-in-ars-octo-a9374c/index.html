Rents of apartments of 45–70 m² in Villa del Parque, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in ARS) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments of 45–70 m² in Villa del Parque, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is ARS 1,150,000 per month, based on 31 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
