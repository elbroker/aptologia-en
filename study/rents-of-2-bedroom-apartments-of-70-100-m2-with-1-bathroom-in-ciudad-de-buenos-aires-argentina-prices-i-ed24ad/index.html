Rents of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in ARS) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² with 1 bathroom in Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is ARS 1,200,000 per month, based on 122 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
