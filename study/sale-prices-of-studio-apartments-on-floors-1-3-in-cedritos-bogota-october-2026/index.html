Sale prices of studio apartments on floors 1–3 in Cedritos, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for studio apartments on floors 1–3 for sale in Cedritos, Bogotá is COP 370,000,000, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 310,500,000 to COP 442,500,000. The median price per square metre is COP 9,716,304/m² and the median listed size is 40 m². That is 10% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 335,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 300,000. The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
