Rents of apartments with parking in Punta Carretas, Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in UYU) — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with parking in Punta Carretas, Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 46,000 per month, based on 49 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 39,500 to UYU 55,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 700/m² and the median listed size is 66 m². That is 53% above the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 30,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is UYU 6,750. The median number of bathrooms is 1.
