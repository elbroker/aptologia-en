Rents of apartments under 45 m² in estrato 5 in Barranquilla — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments under 45 m² in estrato 5 in Barranquilla is COP 2,200,000 per month, based on 54 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 1,892,500 to COP 2,600,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 57,267/m² and the median listed size is 40 m². That is 21% below the study's reference median for Barranquilla (COP 2,800,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 287,000. 85.7% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
