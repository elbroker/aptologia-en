Sale prices of apartments in Del Valle Norte, Ciudad de México, Mexico — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments for sale in Del Valle Norte, Ciudad de México, Mexico is MXN 6,908,700, based on 91 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from MXN 5,414,043 to MXN 9,495,000. The median price per square metre is MXN 59,602/m² and the median listed size is 125 m². That is 38% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de México (MXN 4,990,000). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
