Rents of 2-bedroom apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments with parking with 3+ bathrooms in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 775 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 648 to USD 1,144. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 7.5/m² and the median listed size is 112 m². That is 36% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 570). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 100. The median number of bathrooms is 3.
