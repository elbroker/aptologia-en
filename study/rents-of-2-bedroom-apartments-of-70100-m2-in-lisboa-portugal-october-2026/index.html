Rents of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Lisboa, Portugal — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Lisboa, Portugal is EUR 1,750 per month, based on 87 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from EUR 1,470 to EUR 2,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is EUR 20/m² and the median listed size is 83 m². That is 8% below the study's reference median for Lisboa (EUR 1,900). 39.8% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
