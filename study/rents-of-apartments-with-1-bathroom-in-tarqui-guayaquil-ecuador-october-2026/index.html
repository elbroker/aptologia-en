Rents of apartments with 1 bathroom in Tarqui, Guayaquil, Ecuador — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments with 1 bathroom in Tarqui, Guayaquil, Ecuador is USD 400 per month, based on 91 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
