Sale prices of houses with 3+ bathrooms in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking price for houses with 3+ bathrooms for sale in Palermo, Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Argentina is USD 890,000, based on 61 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 680,000 to USD 1,600,000. The median price per square metre is USD 2,717/m² and the median listed size is 304 m². That is 207% above the study's reference median for Ciudad de Buenos Aires (USD 290,000). The median number of bathrooms is 4.
