Sale prices of homes in Bosques del Marques, Bogotá — October 2026

The median asking price for homes for sale in Bosques del Marques, Bogotá is COP 3,000,000,000, based on 128 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,167,500,000 to COP 4,200,000,000. The median price per square metre is COP 8,860,759/m² and the median listed size is 338 m². That is 247% above the study's reference median for Bogotá (COP 865,000,000). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is COP 2,500,000. The most frequent estrato is 6 and 97.7% of listings include parking.
