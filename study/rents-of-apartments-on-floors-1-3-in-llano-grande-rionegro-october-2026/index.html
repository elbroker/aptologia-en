Rents of apartments on floors 1–3 in Llano Grande, Rionegro — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments on floors 1–3 in Llano Grande, Rionegro is COP 6,325,000 per month, based on 50 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 3,550,000 to COP 8,000,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 65,000/m² and the median listed size is 99 m². That is 139% above the study's reference median for Rionegro (COP 2,650,000). The most frequent estrato is 6, 97.9% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
