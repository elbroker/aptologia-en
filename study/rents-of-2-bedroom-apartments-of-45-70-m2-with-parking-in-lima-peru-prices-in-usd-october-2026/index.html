Rents of 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking in Lima, Peru (prices in USD) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 45–70 m² with parking in Lima, Peru is USD 980 per month, based on 34 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 831 to USD 1,100. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 15.8/m² and the median listed size is 61 m². That is 22% below the study's reference median for Lima (USD 1,250). The median number of bathrooms is 2.
