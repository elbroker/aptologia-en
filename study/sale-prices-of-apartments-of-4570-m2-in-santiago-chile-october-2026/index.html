Sale prices of apartments of 45–70 m² in Santiago, Chile — October 2026

The median asking price for apartments of 45–70 m² for sale in Santiago, Chile is CLP 105,000,000, based on 239 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from CLP 87,750,000 to CLP 120,000,000. The median price per square metre is CLP 1,885,246/m² and the median listed size is 55 m². The median number of bathrooms is 2.
