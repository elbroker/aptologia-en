Rents of 1-bedroom houses in Montevideo, Uruguay (prices in UYU) — October 2026

The median asking rent for 1-bedroom houses in Montevideo, Uruguay is UYU 22,000 per month, based on 89 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from UYU 18,500 to UYU 29,500. The median monthly rent per square metre is UYU 468/m² and the median listed size is 50 m². That is 43% below the study's reference median for Montevideo (UYU 38,500). 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 1.
