Rents of 3+ bedroom apartments without parking with 2 bathrooms in Sabaneta — October 2026

The median asking rent for 3+ bedroom apartments without parking with 2 bathrooms in Sabaneta is COP 2,700,000 per month, based on 108 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from COP 2,187,500 to COP 3,350,000. The median monthly rent per square metre is COP 37,919/m² and the median listed size is 70 m². That is 13% below the study's reference median for Sabaneta (COP 3,100,000). The most frequent estrato is 4 and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
