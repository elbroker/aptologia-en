Rents of apartments in Las Palmas, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking rent for apartments in Las Palmas, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 1,000 per month, based on 30 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).
