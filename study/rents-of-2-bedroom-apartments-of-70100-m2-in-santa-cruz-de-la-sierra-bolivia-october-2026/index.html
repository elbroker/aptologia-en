Rents of 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia — October 2026

The median asking rent for 2-bedroom apartments of 70–100 m² in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia is USD 600 per month, based on 201 unique active listings (Aptología data as of 18 October 2026).

The middle half of listings ranges from USD 500 to USD 757. The median monthly rent per square metre is USD 7.16/m² and the median listed size is 85 m². That is 5% above the study's reference median for Santa Cruz de la Sierra (USD 570). The median monthly HOA fee (administración) declared in the listings is USD 34. 100% of listings include parking and the median number of bathrooms is 2.
